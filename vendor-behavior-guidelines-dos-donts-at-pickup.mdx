## Purpose and scope

Vendor behavior at pickup directly affects Cashkr's brand, customer trust, SLA, and repeat orders.  
This SOP defines how you must act at every pickup so that:

- Customers feel safe, respected, and in control.
- Orders complete smoothly with zero confusion.
- All rules on inspection, pricing, and OTP payment are followed.

For the full operational flow, read [Order Flow – Unlock → Pickup → Payment](/order-flow-unlock-pickup-payment).

<Callout kind="info">

Customers expect a vendor who arrives on time, identifies themselves clearly, explains what is happening, and completes payment through a secure OTP process without any pressure or extra charges.

</Callout>

## Non‑negotiable compliance rules

These rules are mandatory. Breaking them can lead to SLA strikes, hold, or deactivation as per:

- [SLA Violations & Escalation Rules](/sla-violations-escalation-rules)
- [Vendor Hold & Deactivation Process](/vendor-hold-deactivation-process)

### OTP and payment rules

- Use only the Cashkr app to collect payment.
- Follow the OTP payment flow exactly as shown in [Vendor Payment OTP & Confirmation Flow](/vendor-payment-otp-confirmation-flow).
- Collect the payment OTP only inside the app flow. Do not guess or assume the OTP.
- Hand over the device only after the app shows Payment Successful with a transaction ID.

<Callout kind="alert">

You must never:
- Take any cash or UPI payment outside the Cashkr app.
- Ask the customer to transfer money to any personal account.
- Bypass or guess the payment OTP.
- Take the device before the in‑app payment shows Payment Successful.

These are high‑risk violations under payment and fraud categories.

</Callout>

### Pricing and inspection rules

- Let the app decide the price based on actual inspection results.
- Do not change or manipulate the condition to reduce the price unfairly.
- Do not promise or negotiate any price outside the app.

<Callout kind="alert">

You must never:
- Quote a manual price that is not visible in the app.
- Say you will adjust the price later or outside the system.
- Mark wrong condition, wrong IMEI, or fake damage to reduce the price.

</Callout>

### Behavior and safety rules

- Speak politely in all situations, even if the customer is rude.
- Respect personal space and do not enter the home unless invited.
- Do not threaten, intimidate, or use abusive language.
- Do not smoke, chew tobacco, or appear intoxicated at pickup.

<Callout kind="alert">

You must never:
- Threaten or intimidate the customer for any reason.
- Use abusive words, slurs, or shouting.
- Harass the customer or their family members.

These are zero‑tolerance behaviour violations and can directly lead to hold or deactivation.

</Callout>

### Attendance and SLA rules

- Call the customer shortly after you unlock the lead and again shortly before arrival.
- Go to the actual location shown in the app; do not mark Customer Not Available without visiting.
- If there is a genuine issue (address wrong, safety concern, etc.), follow [Handling Customer Issues During Pickup](/handling-customer-issues-during-pickup) and log notes properly.

## Recommended pickup interaction sequence

Follow this sequence for every pickup. It matches the stages in [Order Flow – Unlock → Pickup → Payment](/order-flow-unlock-pickup-payment).

<Steps>

<Step title="Plan and travel to the location" icon="map">

- Call the customer shortly after unlock to confirm time and address.
- Before starting, check the route in Maps and keep your phone charged and the Cashkr app logged in.
- Travel safely and avoid rash driving. If delayed, inform the customer.

</Step>

<Step title="Call shortly before arrival" icon="phone">

- Call the customer shortly before reaching the building or gate.
- Confirm that they are available and have the device and any accessories ready.
- If the customer says they are not available, use the correct disposition or reschedule as per app options.

</Step>

<Step title="Greet and identify yourself" icon="users">

- At the doorstep, greet politely and confirm the customer name and device.
- Clearly state that you are from Cashkr for inspection and pickup.
- If asked, show the Cashkr app screen with the order details for verification.

</Step>

<Step title="Start inspection with OTP (if applicable)" icon="lock">

- Where required, follow the inspection OTP flow in the app.
- Explain that inspection and pricing are system‑based, not manual.
- Only proceed once the customer shares the correct OTP and the app allows inspection.

</Step>

<Step title="Inspect the device transparently" icon="monitor">

- Ask permission before checking each feature and handle the device carefully.
- Check IMEI or serial number in front of the customer.
- Show and explain any defects that may impact the final price.

</Step>

<Step title="Show and explain the final price" icon="info">

- Show the price on the app screen to the customer.
- Explain calmly if the price has reduced due to actual condition.
- If the customer does not accept, use the correct disposition such as Customer Rejected Price and end the pickup politely.

</Step>

<Step title="Complete payment via OTP" icon="shield">

- Tap Pay Now in the app and explain that an OTP will come to their registered number.
- Ask the customer to read out the OTP and enter it in the payment screen.
- Confirm that both your app and the customer’s app or SMS show Payment Successful and note the transaction ID.

</Step>

<Step title="Collect device and close the order" icon="check-circle">

- After payment success, collect the device and agreed accessories only.
- Pack the device properly and offer help if the customer needs to reset or log out.
- Thank the customer and mark the order as Completed in the app.

</Step>

</Steps>

For detailed call and WhatsApp scripts at each stage, follow [Pickup Communication Best Practices](/pickup-communication-best-practices).

## Professionalism and best‑practice behaviors

Use this section as your daily checklist for how to behave before, during, and after pickup.

### Before you reach the location

- Dress neatly; avoid very casual or untidy appearance.
- Keep your phone fully charged and the Cashkr app logged in.
- Carry basic tools you may need (charger, power bank) and a proper bag for safe device carrying.
- Avoid last‑minute cancellations or delays; if there is a genuine issue, inform the customer and update the app.

### At the doorstep and during conversation

- Remove helmet, cap, or sunglasses before starting the conversation.
- Maintain a calm, friendly tone; do not sound rushed or irritated.
- Keep a reasonable distance from the door and respect privacy.
- Keep personal calls to a minimum; focus on the pickup until completion.

### Inspection etiquette

- Take the device only after the customer clearly hands it to you.
- Do not open the device body or attempt any repair; you are only inspecting and collecting.
- If you use any personal tools, ask permission first.
- Speak clearly about every major defect you see (cracks, spots, battery health, camera issues).

### Explaining price and handling objection

- Always rely on the app price. Use neutral lines like “The app has calculated this price based on the condition we recorded.”
- Avoid blaming Cashkr, customer, or “market rate” for the price.
- If customer disagrees, do not argue or push; follow the correct disposition and refer to [Price Change Explanation Script](/price-change-explanation-script) if needed.

### Payment and closure

- Explain the OTP payment flow in simple language; do not rush the customer.
- Confirm payment success before taking the device.
- Do a quick final check: accessories collected as per app, order marked as Completed, customer has no open doubts.

## Quick reference: Do and Don’t behavior list

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Do" icon="check">

- Arrive around the scheduled time and communicate delays early.
- Call the customer shortly after unlock and shortly before arrival.
- Greet politely and introduce yourself as a Cashkr partner.
- Show the Cashkr app if the customer asks for verification.
- Respect the home, privacy, and personal boundaries of the customer.
- Inspect the device gently and transparently in front of the customer.
- Let the app decide the price and show it clearly on your screen.
- Follow all OTP flows for inspection and payment exactly as in the app.
- Take payment only through the Cashkr app and confirm success.
- Collect only the device and accessories shown in the order.
- Close the order correctly in the app and thank the customer.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Don’t" icon="x">

- Do not go to the location without trying to call the customer.
- Do not demand extra money, tips, or “service charges”.
- Do not ask for the customer’s personal banking PIN or passwords.
- Do not offer or accept cash, direct UPI, or any offline payment.
- Do not shout, argue, or use abusive language, even if provoked.
- Do not enter the customer’s home unless they clearly invite you.
- Do not open the device body, attempt repair, or cause damage.
- Do not mark wrong condition, fake damage, or wrong IMEI.
- Do not guess the payment OTP or enter it without the customer reading it.
- Do not take the device before the app shows Payment Successful.
- Do not falsely mark Customer Not Available without reaching the location.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Difficult situations and how to handle them

Use this as your playbook when something goes wrong at the pickup. For detailed classifications, proofs, and escalations, read [Handling Customer Issues During Pickup](/handling-customer-issues-during-pickup).

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Customer disputes or rejects the price" default-open="true">

- Stay calm and listen to the customer’s concern fully.
- Show the defects on the device and how they appear in the app.
- Explain that the price is system‑calculated based on actual condition.
- If the customer still does not agree, select the correct reason (for example, Customer Rejected Price) and end the pickup politely.
- Do not argue, do not try manual negotiation, and do not attempt offline deals.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Customer is rude or angry">

- Keep your voice low and calm; do not match their anger.
- Focus on facts: order details, app price, OTP payment.
- If you feel unsafe or the argument is escalating, step away from the door or building safely.
- Inform Ops or support as per your local process and document what happened.
- Avoid any physical or verbal confrontation; behaviour violations can impact both parties.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Customer refuses or delays sharing payment OTP">

- Politely explain that payment through OTP inside the app is mandatory for their safety and for Cashkr records.
- Give the customer a few minutes if they are looking for the OTP SMS or confirming with family.
- If they clearly refuse to share or complete the OTP, select the correct reason (for example, OTP Not Provided) and do not take the device.
- Do not suggest cash, personal UPI, or any offline payment as an alternative.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Customer is not available at the location">

- Call the customer from outside the location and wait a reasonable short time.
- If the customer asks to reschedule, use the reschedule option in the app where available.
- If the customer does not answer despite multiple attempts, follow the correct disposition such as Customer Not Available with notes and, if possible, a photo of the location entrance (without revealing any private information).
- Never mark Customer Not Available from far away or without visiting; this can become an SLA violation.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Customer asks to pay in cash or direct UPI">

- Politely explain that Cashkr only allows payment through the app using OTP for safety and record‑keeping.
- Repeat that you cannot accept or send money to any personal account.
- Guide the customer to complete the OTP flow in the app.
- If the customer insists on offline payment and refuses OTP, do not take the device and close with the appropriate reason.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## How violations are handled

Cashkr tracks behaviour, inspection, payment, and SLA violations centrally. Repeated or serious issues can lead to:

- SLA strikes and reduced order allocation, as explained in [SLA Violations & Escalation Rules](/sla-violations-escalation-rules).
- Temporary hold or permanent deactivation in line with [Vendor Hold & Deactivation Process](/vendor-hold-deactivation-process).

Use these guidelines to protect yourself, the customer, and the Cashkr brand. Consistent good behavior leads to:

- Higher customer ratings and more orders.
- Fewer complaints and escalations.
- Stronger long‑term partnership with Cashkr.
