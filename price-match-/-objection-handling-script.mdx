## Overview

- **Team:** B2C Sales  
- **Purpose:** Handle customers who say another platform is offering a better price and convince them to still sell through Cashkr.  
- **Tone:** Confident, trust-building, value-driven (not argumentative).  
- **When to use:** Customer says another platform is offering a higher price for their device.

## Script by language

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English (training reference)" icon="book-open">

<Steps>

<Step title="Customer objection (higher price elsewhere)">

> **Customer:** "Cashify (or another site) is offering me a higher price."

</Step>

<Step title="Acknowledge and explain pricing">

> **Agent:**  
> "I totally understand, and it is good that you are checking all options. Just to clarify — our prices are based on real-time market rates and actual inspection, so they are always accurate and final."

</Step>

<Step title="Ask for final quote and offer to recheck/match">

> **Agent:**  
> "If you can share the other platform's final quote (not just the estimate), we will recheck it and try to match or beat that price."

</Step>

<Step title="Reinforce Cashkr value: pickup, inspection, payment">

> **If customer sounds hesitant:**  
> **Agent:**  
> "Also, remember — with Cashkr, pickup is free, the inspection is transparent, and payment happens instantly during pickup. No waiting, no hidden charges."

</Step>

<Step title="If customer insists on other platform">

> **If customer insists:**  
> **Agent:**  
> "I understand, totally your choice! I will send you your Cashkr quote link again — you can always come back to us anytime. Prices are live and update daily."

</Step>

<Step title="Closing question (offer to recheck now)">

> **Closing line (for conversion):**  
> **Agent:**  
> "Would you like me to recheck your model's price right now to see if we can match that offer?"

</Step>

</Steps>

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish (live calls)" icon="phone">

<Steps>

<Step title="Customer objection (higher price elsewhere)">

> **Customer:** "Mujhe Cashify par zyada price mil raha hai."

</Step>

<Step title="Acknowledge and explain pricing">

> **Agent:**  
> "Bilkul sahi hai sir/ma'am, aap comparison kar rahe ho, that is good. Cashkr ka price actual inspection rate pe based hota hai — real-time market ke hisaab se, jo mostly accurate rehta hai."

</Step>

<Step title="Ask for final quote and offer to recheck/match">

> **Agent:**  
> "Agar aap mujhe dusre platform ka final quote share kar do, main abhi check kar deta hoon ki hum uske barabar ya usse better price de sakte hain ya nahi."

</Step>

<Step title="Reinforce Cashkr value: pickup, inspection, payment">

> **If customer unsure:**  
> **Agent:**  
> "Cashkr mein pickup free hai, inspection doorstep par hota hai, aur payment wahi turant milta hai UPI pe. Kahi aur aapko wait nahi karna padta ya hidden cut nahi hota."

</Step>

<Step title="If customer wants to think about it">

> **If customer says 'thik hai, sochta hoon':**  
> **Agent:**  
> "Koi problem nahi, main link firse bhej deta hoon — jab chahein confirm kar lijiye. Price market ke saath badal sakta hai, isliye best price aaj lock kar lena better rahega."

</Step>

<Step title="Closing question (offer to recheck now)">

> **Agent (optional closing prompt):**  
> "Main abhi aapke model ka price dubara recheck kar doon, taaki agar match ho raha ho to aaj hi lock kar sakein?"

</Step>

</Steps>

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Usage notes

- Stay calm, never challenge the customer's comparison.  
- Focus on **trust, speed, and transparency** — Cashkr's key advantages.  
- Always offer to recheck or match prices if feasible.

<Callout kind="tip">

Stay calm and do not argue with the customer. Keep bringing the conversation back to trust, speed, and transparent pricing as Cashkr's main advantages.

</Callout>
