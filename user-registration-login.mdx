## Overview

The Cashkr customer website lets new users register and existing users log in using their mobile number and a one-time password (OTP).

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Use this SOP to train support, QA, and product teams on how web registration and login work, including the exact on-screen steps and expected field behavior.
</Callout>

## User registration and login flows

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Sign up" icon="plus">
    ### Sign up flow

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open profile from homepage" icon="home" title-type="p">
        - Go to the Cashkr website homepage.

        - Click **Profile** in the top navigation.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Enter mobile number" icon="terminal" title-type="p">
        - Enter a valid 10-digit mobile number in the phone field.

        - Click **Continue** or the equivalent action to request an OTP.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Verify OTP" icon="lock" title-type="p">
        - Check the mobile device for the OTP.

        - Enter the OTP in the OTP field and submit.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Complete profile details" icon="edit" title-type="p">
        - Enter **First name**, **Last name**, and **Email address** when prompted.

        - Click **Complete signup**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Registration success" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
        - The user is now registered and logged in to their Cashkr account.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    ### Field behavior

    - **Phone field** accepts only 10-digit numbers.

    - **OTP retry** is allowed after 60 seconds.

    - Users can **edit the mobile number** before submitting the OTP.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Login" icon="log-in">
    ### Login flow

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open profile from homepage" icon="home" title-type="p">
        - Go to the Cashkr website homepage.

        - Click **Profile** in the top navigation.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Enter registered mobile number" icon="terminal" title-type="p">
        - Enter the mobile number that is already registered on Cashkr.

        - Click **Continue** or the equivalent action to request an OTP.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Verify OTP and log in" icon="lock" title-type="p">
        - Enter the received OTP in the OTP field.

        - Click **Login**.

        - The user is logged in to their Cashkr account.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
