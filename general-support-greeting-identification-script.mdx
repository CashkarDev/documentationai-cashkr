**Used by:** CX executives and support staff

**Purpose:** Standardize how agents greet, verify, and begin every customer conversation (call, chat/WhatsApp, email) while protecting customer data.

---

## Quick checklist before you start

- Greet professionally and introduce yourself and Cashkr.
- Check if it is a good time to talk (for calls).
- Confirm the customer identifier (mobile number and/or order ID).
- Verify identity in the system before sharing any order/payment details.
- Clarify the main issue and map it to a category.
- Set clear expectations on what you will do next and by when.
- Log key details in the ticket or CRM.

<Callout kind="alert">

Data protection rules you must follow on every interaction:

- Always verify the customer before sharing order or payment details.
- Never ask for or share OTP, passwords, or full card/bank details.
- Do not disclose order details to any third party without customer confirmation.
- Do not promise refunds, approvals, or timelines that are not confirmed in policy or system.

</Callout>

## Standard interaction flow

Use this sequence for all conversations: greet, permission/time check (for calls), verify identity, confirm issue category, set expectation, then transition to troubleshooting.

<Steps>

<Step title="Greet and introduce yourself" icon="users">

Use a polite, clear greeting that mentions Cashkr and your name. Adjust slightly for call, chat/WhatsApp, or email.

</Step>

<Step title="Check time and communication channel (calls only)" icon="help-circle">

Confirm that it is a good time to talk and that the customer can speak for a few minutes. If not, schedule a callback.

</Step>

<Step title="Verify customer identity" icon="shield">

Ask for registered mobile number and/or order ID. Confirm in system before discussing any order or payment details.

</Step>

<Step title="Clarify and tag the issue category" icon="settings">

Ask one or two questions to understand the main concern, then map it to an internal category and open the correct training script if needed.

</Step>

<Step title="Set expectation and next step" icon="info">

Tell the customer what you are doing now (for example, checking the order, coordinating with operations) and an expected response or resolution timeline.

</Step>

<Step title="Transition to troubleshooting or resolution" icon="arrow-right">

Use a short, clear line to move from greeting and verification into problem solving.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Scripts by language and channel

Use the templates below as copy-ready scripts. Adapt names and details, but keep structure and tone.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English (recommended)" icon="book-open">

### Call opening and confirmation

**Call opening**

> "Hello, you have reached Cashkr Support. My name is \[Agent Name]. How may I help you today?"

**Time and permission check**

> "Is this a good time to talk for a few minutes so I can understand and resolve your concern?"

If customer says no:

> "No problem. When would be a better time to reach you back today? I will schedule a callback for that time."

### Chat / WhatsApp opening

> "Hi, thank you for contacting Cashkr Support. My name is \[Agent Name]. How can I help you with your order or payment today?"

If customer has already typed the issue:

> "Thank you for sharing the details. I will first verify your account and order, then help you with this."

### Email opening

> "Dear \[Customer Name],  
>  
> Thank you for writing to Cashkr Support. My name is \[Agent Name], and I will be assisting you with your concern regarding your \[order/payment]."

Add a short acknowledgement of their issue:

> "I understand you are facing an issue with \[brief issue summary]. I will review your order details and share the next steps."

### Verification questions and fallbacks

Ask for identity before discussing any order details.

**Primary verification (ask both if possible)**

> "To verify your details, may I confirm your registered mobile number and your Cashkr order ID?"

If customer shares only one:

- If only mobile:

  > "Thank you. I am checking your account with the mobile number \[repeat last 4 digits only if needed]."

- If only order ID:

  > "Thank you. I am checking your order with ID \[order ID] now."

**If customer does not have order ID handy**

> "No problem. Can you share the mobile number used to create the order on Cashkr? I will search using that."

If still not available:

> "In that case, can you share the full name and approximate date when you placed the order so I can try to locate it?"

**After successful verification**

> "Thank you. I have verified your details. Let me now check your order status and help you with this."

### Acknowledgement and empathy lines

Use one or two lines to show you understand the concern.

- "I understand this is important for you, and I will do my best to resolve it as soon as possible."
- "I can see why this would be worrying. Let us check what is happening with your order right away."
- "Thank you for your patience. I will quickly review the details so we can move towards a solution."

### Transition to troubleshooting

Use this once identity and issue type are clear.

> "Got it, I have noted your concern. Give me a moment while I check your order details so I can guide you with the exact next steps."

If you need to place the customer on brief hold (call):

> "May I place you on hold for 1 to 2 minutes while I check this in the system and with the relevant team? I will come back with an update."

## Issue category identification

Once you understand the basic concern, map it to an internal category and follow the relevant script or SOP.

- **Order related (pickup, status, tracking)**
  - First action: Open the order in Admin Panel and check current status and history.
  - If pickup not happening or delayed, follow the guidance in **Pickup Not Available Script** (`/pickup-not-available-script`).

- **City or pincode availability**
  - First action: Check if the customer pincode is serviceable using the latest serviceable area list.
  - For detailed wording and options, see **City and Pincode Availability** (`/city-pincode-availability-serviceable-areas`).

- **Payment or payout related**
  - First action: Check the Cashkr wallet, payout logs, and bank/UPI status if visible.
  - If linked to return flow or confirmation, refer to **Return Flow and Payment Confirmation** (`/return-flow-payment-confirmation`).

- **Price mismatch or change**
  - First action: Compare current app price with order creation price and review device condition notes.
  - Use the approved explanation lines in **Price Change Explanation Script** (`/price-change-explanation-script`).

- **Pickup / return flow issues**
  - First action: Check pickup attempt logs, vendor notes, and reason codes for failure or rejection.
  - Then apply the guidance in **Handling Customer Issues During Pickup** (`/handling-customer-issues-during-pickup`).

- **Complaint or escalation**
  - First action: Listen fully once, acknowledge the experience, and check all key events on the order.
  - Use the wording in **Complaint and Escalation Acknowledgement Script** (`/complaint-/-escalation-acknowledgement-script`) and log clearly for escalation.

## Tricky situations and how to respond

Use these patterns when the standard flow does not work.

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Customer not found in system" default-open="false">

If you cannot find the customer with the given details:

1. Confirm spelling and details:

   > "Let me confirm once more. Can you please spell your full name, and confirm the mobile number digit by digit?"

2. Try alternative search:

   > "Do you have any other mobile number or email that you may have used on Cashkr?"

3. If still not found:

   > "At the moment, I am not able to find any order with these details. You can share a screenshot of your order confirmation or the SMS/email received from Cashkr so I can investigate further."

Set expectation:

> "Once I receive the screenshot or correct details, I will recheck and update you on the next steps."

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Details mismatch with system" default-open="false">

If customer details do not match what is in the system:

1. Softly highlight mismatch:

   > "I am seeing a different name or number in our system for this order. For your security, I need to verify this correctly before sharing more details."

2. Ask for additional verification:

   > "Can you confirm the email address used on Cashkr or the last 4 digits of the mobile number saved in the profile?"

3. If still mismatch:

   > "Because the details are not matching our records, I am not allowed to share order or payment information. You can log in to your Cashkr account with the registered details or have the registered person contact us directly."

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Third-party caller (friend, family, vendor)" default-open="false">

When someone else calls or messages on behalf of the customer:

1. Clarify relationship:

   > "May I know your relationship with the customer who placed the order?"

2. State privacy rule:

   > "For data security, we can share full order and payment details only with the person whose mobile number is registered on the order."

3. Offer safe options:

   - "If the registered customer is with you, they can come on the call for quick verification."
   - "The registered customer can also contact us directly from their mobile or WhatsApp, and we will help immediately."

Do not share OTP or payment information with third parties.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Angry or frustrated customer" default-open="false">

When the customer starts the conversation upset:

1. Calm acknowledgement:

   - "I understand you are upset, and I am here to help you with this."
   - "I am sorry for the trouble you have faced. Let us first look at what went wrong and then we will talk about the solution."

2. Bring structure:

   > "To help you properly, I will first verify your details and check your order history, then I will explain what I can do for you today."

3. If customer talks over you:

   > "I want to make sure I fully understand your concern. May I take 30 seconds to summarize what I heard, and then you can correct me if anything is missing?"

Stay calm, do not argue, and stick to policy.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Customer has multiple issues at once" default-open="false">

When the customer shares many problems together:

1. Prioritize:

   > "You have raised a few important points. To make sure we do not miss anything, can we first solve the most urgent one, then we will take the next?"

2. Confirm order and issue 1:

   > "Let us start with \[issue 1, for example, pickup delay] for order \[ID]. I will check this now."

3. After resolving first:

   > "Now that we have addressed the pickup issue, let us quickly review your second concern about \[issue 2]."

Log each issue as a separate point inside the same ticket if policy requires.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Internal notes and logging template

Every ticket or interaction must show that you greeted, verified, identified the issue, and set expectations.

Use this mini-template as guidance for your internal notes:

- **Customer identifier:** Verified mobile number and/or email.
- **Order ID:** Main order ID discussed, plus any related orders if needed.
- **Issue summary:** One or two lines in plain language (for example, "pickup delayed twice", "payment not credited to bank", "price changed at pickup").
- **Issue category:** Order, payment, city/pincode, pickup/return, price change, complaint/escalation, or other.
- **Actions taken:** Checks you performed (for example, "checked admin panel status", "reviewed payout log", "contacted vendor").
- **Promised next step and timeline:** What you told the customer will happen and by when (for example, "will update by end of day after coordinating with operations").

<Callout kind="alert">

If you commit to a follow-up or timeline in your notes, add a reminder or follow the **Follow-Up and Callback Script** (`/follow-up-/-callback-script`) so the customer actually receives the promised update.

</Callout>
