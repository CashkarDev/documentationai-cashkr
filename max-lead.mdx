## Overview

Max Lead limits how many leads you can have in **InProgress** at the same time. It prevents overloading your team and helps distribute work fairly between vendors.

<Callout kind="info">

Max Lead is a **per-vendor cap** on how many leads can be in the InProgress state. When you hit this cap, you cannot unlock more new leads until you complete or fail some of your existing ones.

</Callout>

## What Max Lead means

Max Lead is a number configured for your vendor account in the Admin Panel. It counts all orders that appear under your [`InProgress Orders`](/inprogress-orders) list.

- Every unlocked order that moves into InProgress consumes **1 Max Lead slot**.
- When your current in-progress count reaches the Max Lead value, your Max Lead is considered **full**.
- While Max Lead is full, the system blocks you from taking on additional new leads, except in the same-customer exception case.

## Where Max Lead applies

Max Lead applies in two main places:

- **Vendor unlocking from Available Orders**

  - When you try to unlock a new lead from [`Available Orders`](/available-orders), the system checks your current in-progress count against your Max Lead cap.
  - If you are at or above your cap, the unlock fails with a Max Lead error (unless it qualifies for the same-customer bypass).

- **Admin assigning orders to you**

  - When an admin assigns an order to your vendor from the Admin Panel, the system performs the same Max Lead check.
  - If you are already at Max Lead, the assignment **does not go through**, unless the order qualifies as a same-customer order for you.

## Step-by-step: what happens when you unlock

This is the standard flow when you try to unlock an order from the Available Orders screen.

<Steps>

<Step title="You try to unlock a lead" icon="play-circle">

You choose an order from [`Available Orders`](/available-orders) and tap **Unlock**. The system identifies which vendor is unlocking and which customer/order this is for.

</Step>

<Step title="System checks your Max Lead usage" icon="settings">

The system counts how many leads you currently have in [`InProgress Orders`](/inprogress-orders) and compares it to your Max Lead limit set in the Admin Panel.

</Step>

<Step title="If under cap, unlock succeeds" icon="check-circle">

If your current in-progress count is **below** your Max Lead:

- The unlock succeeds.
- The order moves to your **InProgress Orders** list.
- Your in-progress count increases by 1.

You should immediately see the unlocked order under InProgress.

</Step>

<Step title="If at cap, system evaluates same-customer exception" icon="help-circle">

If your current in-progress count is **already at or above** your Max Lead, the system checks whether this order qualifies as a **Same Customer Order** for you (see the exception section below).

</Step>

<Step title="Outcome: block or bypass" icon="alert-triangle">

- If the order **does not** meet the Same Customer Order conditions, the system blocks the unlock and shows a Max Lead error message.
- If the order **does** meet the Same Customer Order conditions, the system allows the order to be auto-assigned to you and adds it to InProgress, even though you are at Max Lead.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Exception: Same Customer Order bypass

Some orders from the **same customer** are allowed to bypass the Max Lead cap so you can continue handling all orders for that customer smoothly.

<Callout kind="info">

When an order qualifies as a **Same Customer Order** for your vendor, the system **ignores the Max Lead cap** for that order only and moves it into your InProgress list, even if you are already at Max Lead.

</Callout>

- **Vendor unlocks**

  - If you try to unlock a Same Customer Order, the system auto-assigns it to you and places it under InProgress, regardless of your current Max Lead usage.

- **Admin assignment**

  - If an admin assigns a Same Customer Order to your vendor while you are at Max Lead, the assignment still succeeds and the order moves to your InProgress list.

Read the full Same Customer Order rules and matching logic in [`Same Customer Order`](/same-customer-order).

## Connections

Max Lead interacts with other parts of the Vendor App and Admin Panel.

- **Admin Panel vendor settings**

  - An admin sets and updates your vendor’s Max Lead from the vendor section of the Admin Panel.
  - Admins can **increase or decrease** this value at any time, which immediately changes how many in-progress leads you are allowed to hold.

- **Available Orders and InProgress Orders**

  - The unlock button on [`Available Orders`](/available-orders) uses your current Max Lead usage plus the Same Customer Order rules to decide whether to allow the unlock.
  - [`InProgress Orders`](/inprogress-orders) is the main list that counts toward your Max Lead usage.

- **Credits and other gates**

  - Max Lead is **separate** from your credit balance.
  - Even if your Max Lead is not full, you may still be blocked from unlocking because of **insufficient credits**; see [`Buy Credits`](/buy-credits) and [`Reserved Order`](/reserved-order) for how credit-based limits work.
  - In the same way, even if you have enough credits, Max Lead can still block you when your in-progress cap is full.

## Common states and errors

This section covers what you are likely to see when Max Lead affects your workflow and how to interpret it.

### Max Lead reached when unlocking

When you try to unlock an order and your in-progress count is already at your Max Lead:

- The system blocks the unlock.
- You see an error message indicating that you have reached your **maximum allowed leads** or similar wording.
- The order stays in **Available Orders** and does not appear in **InProgress Orders**.

To continue unlocking new orders:

- Complete or fail some existing InProgress orders so your in-progress count drops below the cap, or
- Ask an admin to **increase your Max Lead** in the Admin Panel.

### Same-customer unlock behaves differently

If the order is a Same Customer Order for you:

- You may **not see a Max Lead error**, even if you are already at cap.
- The order moves into **InProgress** and increases your visible in-progress count beyond the numerical Max Lead value.
- This is expected behavior and matches the Same Customer Order bypass rules.

### Confusion with credit failures

Credit issues can look similar to Max Lead failures but come from a different rule:

- If you have **insufficient credits**, the unlock may fail **even when you are under Max Lead**.
- These failures are tied to credit balance and reserved orders, not to the Max Lead cap.
- For credit-related behavior and messages, refer to [`Buy Credits`](/buy-credits) and [`Reserved Order`](/reserved-order).

## Developer and tester notes

<Callout kind="tip">

Use these checks when you debug or test Max Lead behavior across the Vendor App and Admin Panel.

</Callout>

- **Admin Panel configuration**

  - Confirm the Max Lead value for the vendor in the Admin Panel vendor details.
  - Adjust the value up/down to test behavior at low and high caps.

- **Counting in-progress leads**

  - Treat every order visible in [`InProgress Orders`](/inprogress-orders) as consuming 1 Max Lead slot.
  - Same Customer Orders that bypass the cap should still appear in this list and count in the visible total, even when the total exceeds the configured Max Lead.

- **Unlock flow tests**

  - Test unlocking when:
    - Current in-progress count is **below** Max Lead.
    - Current in-progress count is **exactly equal** to Max Lead:
      - With a regular (non-same-customer) order: expect **block** with Max Lead error.
      - With a Same Customer Order: expect **bypass** and successful assignment.
  - Confirm that unlock behavior is the same whether the action starts from the vendor app (unlock) or the Admin Panel (assign to vendor).

- **Separation from credits**

  - Verify that:
    - When Max Lead is full but credits are sufficient, unlock is blocked due to Max Lead.
    - When Max Lead has room but credits are insufficient, unlock is blocked due to credits, potentially creating reserved orders as described in [`Reserved Order`](/reserved-order).

- **Edge conditions**

  - If an admin reduces Max Lead **below** the current in-progress count, existing InProgress orders should remain assigned, but new unlocks should be blocked until the in-progress count falls back under the new cap, except for Same Customer Orders which still bypass the cap.
