## Overview

**Cashkr BuyBack Days** is a time-boxed promotional event. During the window, eligible devices earn a **bonus on top of the normal best-value price**, and the customer is shown the full arithmetic rather than a vague "extra cash" claim.

The event is abbreviated **CBD** in tight UI spaces, such as device tile labels.

<Callout kind="warning">
**Status: design finalised, mechanics not yet documented.** The surfaces, copy and visual treatment below are confirmed. The pricing, configuration and settlement rules in [Open questions](#open-questions) are still to be filled in by the team that built it — do not implement or support against assumptions.
</Callout>

## What the customer sees

The headline is a quote-style card that spells out the sum:

| Line | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Best Value We Pay | ₹27,500 |
| Cashkr BuyBack Days bonus | ₹500 |
| **You get** | **₹28,000** |

<Callout kind="info">
Showing the breakdown is deliberate. The bonus reads as a real addition to a real price, which is what separates this from a discount-code gimmick.
</Callout>

## Visual rules

The event uses **only Cashkr's existing green and mint**, the same palette already in the app and site.

<Callout kind="alert">
Do not reintroduce gold, orange or yellow, coin imagery, or `BONUS` stickers on tiles. That combination was built and rejected: against a dark green base it read like a betting or loan app, which undermines trust on exactly the screen where the customer is deciding whether Cashkr will actually pay them.
</Callout>

Specifically replaced during the redesign:

- The dark home strip became the white **No Service Charges** card.
- Per-tile `BONUS` stickers became the small green **+ CBD bonus** label.
- The gold coin button in the centre of the navigation became the normal **BUYBACK** tab.

## Open questions

These need answers before the event can be supported or extended. Each one changes behaviour the support and vendor teams will be asked about.

<Expandable title="Is the bonus flat or variable?" default-open="false">
The confirmed example is a flat ₹500. Unknown whether that is global, per category, per model, or a percentage with a cap.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Where is it configured, and by whom?" default-open="false">
Whether there is a dedicated Admin Panel section for events, or it rides on the existing coupon/campaign tables. Also which roles can create and activate an event — Marketing, or admin-only.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="When does the bonus attach to the price?" default-open="false">
At quote generation, or at payment/completion. This decides what happens on **requote** and under **dead price**: if a quote is regenerated after the window closes, does the bonus survive, and does the customer see a drop they were not warned about.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How is the Valid on list built?" default-open="false">
Manual model selection, or a rule over category, price band or class.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Does it stack with coupons and referrals?" default-open="false">
Whether a coupon can be applied on top of the bonus, and how the combination is displayed and capped.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Who absorbs the bonus?" default-open="false">
Whether it comes out of Cashkr's margin or changes vendor deduction, credit cost or unlock economics — and whether the vendor app shows the bonus at all.

Related: the ₹500 in the example reads as part of the **device payout**, not a wallet credit. Cashkr Wallet holds referral earnings only and is never used for device payouts, so this needs confirming explicitly.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Window handling" default-open="false">
Where start and end dates are set, and what happens to in-flight leads and orders at rollover.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="API surface" default-open="false">
Whether any admin or customer endpoints exist for this yet. The ck-admin v1.4.0 spec (662 operations) contains no routes matching an events or buyback-days prefix, so either it is unbuilt, served by CashkrBackend, or implemented through the existing `/api/marketing/campaigns` routes.
</Expandable>
