## Overview

- **Team:** B2C Sales  
- **Purpose:** Convert a lead (someone who checked price on the website/app) into a confirmed order with doorstep pickup and instant payment.  
- **Tone:** Friendly, informative, helpful — not pushy.  
- **When to use:** Call within 5 minutes of lead generation for best conversions.

<Callout kind="tip">

Sound conversational and natural, not robotic. Use your own voice while keeping the core lines and structure the same.

</Callout>

## Script

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English (Training)" icon="book-open">

<Steps>

<Step title="Opening">

> Hi, this is \[Your Name] calling from Cashkr. You had recently checked the price of your \[Device Model] on our website/app. I just wanted to confirm if you’re planning to sell it.

</Step>

<Step title="If yes / maybe">

> Perfect! I can help you complete your order right now so you can get a doorstep pickup and instant payment. It’ll just take 2 minutes.

</Step>

<Step title="Verify details">

> Can I quickly confirm your device details? You selected \[Model Name], \[Storage], and condition as \[Condition]. Is that correct?

> Great! And your pickup location is \[Area/City] — correct?

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm pickup and explain process">

> Perfect, we have pickup available in your area. Once you confirm, our partner will visit, check the device, and you’ll receive payment instantly to your UPI.

</Step>

<Step title="Call to action">

> Shall I help you complete the order now? You’ll get a confirmation SMS right after.

</Step>

<Step title="If later">

> No problem, I’ll send you your price link again. Just tap it anytime to confirm your order — remember, device prices can drop over time, so it’s best to lock it now while the quote is valid.

</Step>

<Step title="If not interested">

> Sure, thank you for checking with Cashkr! In case you decide later, your quote link will stay active — you can place the order anytime.

</Step>

</Steps>

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish (Live calls)" icon="phone">

<Steps>

<Step title="Opening">

> Hello, main \[Name] bol raha/rahi hoon Cashkr se. Aapne apne \[Phone Model] ka price check kiya tha hamare app/website par — kya aap us phone ko bechne ka plan kar rahe ho?

</Step>

<Step title="If yes / maybe">

> Great! Main aapka order abhi confirm karwa deta hoon — sirf 2 minute lagenge. Pickup aapke ghar se hoga aur payment instant milta hai UPI pe.

</Step>

<Step title="Verify details">

> Bas ek baar confirm kar lijiye — aapne \[Model + Storage + Condition] select kiya tha na?

> Pickup address \[Area/City] ka hi hai?

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm pickup and explain process">

> Perfect, wahan pickup available hai. Hamara partner visit karega, phone check karega, aur turant aapko payment mil jaayegi.

</Step>

<Step title="Call to action">

> Main abhi order confirm kar du kya? Message turant mil jaayega confirmation ka.

</Step>

<Step title="If later">

> Thik hai, main link dubara bhej deta hoon. Jab chahein click karke confirm kar lijiye. Bas dhyaan rahe, price time ke saath kam ho sakta hai — best price aaj lock ho jaayega.

</Step>

</Steps>

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Usage notes

- Use this script within 5 minutes of lead generation for best conversions.  
- Always confirm **model, condition, and pincode/pickup area** before pushing for order confirmation.  
- Keep the tone friendly and helpful, not pushy.  
- End with light urgency: remind customers that device prices can drop over time, so it is better to lock the quote while it is valid.
