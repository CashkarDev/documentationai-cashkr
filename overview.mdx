## What the Cashkr Website is

The Cashkr Website is the public-facing entry point where people discover, evaluate, and sell their old devices online. It focuses on acquisition, education, and conversion into trade-in requests.

The website exposes Cashkr pricing, trade-in value propositions, and lead forms, and then hands off deeper engagement to other Cashkr surfaces such as the Customer App, Vendor App, and Admin Panel.

<Callout kind="info">

This page covers the Cashkr Website only: the public marketing and trade-in funnel at `cashkr.com` (or equivalent). For the operational tools that process orders, see the Customer App, Vendor App, and Admin Panel documentation.

</Callout>

## Who the website serves

The website targets three main audiences:

- **Consumers (B2C)** — Individuals looking to sell a personal device quickly and at a transparent price.
- **Businesses and institutions** — Companies, schools, or organizations interested in bulk or corporate buyback programs.
- **SEO-driven visitors** — Users landing on device- or brand-specific pages from search, who may not yet know Cashkr but have strong selling intent.

These audiences share a core need (turn old devices into cash) but arrive with different context and scale, which the website addresses through dedicated flows and messaging.

## Primary user journeys

The website is optimized around a small set of repeatable, measurable journeys.

### 1. Consumer device trade-in

A typical consumer path:

1. **Discover** — Land on the homepage or a search-optimized device/brand page.
2. **Evaluate** — Check indicative pricing, eligibility, and condition guidelines for a specific device model.
3. **Configure** — Select device specs (storage, variant, condition) to see a final quote.
4. **Commit** — Submit a trade-in request or start an order, often creating or linking a Cashkr account.
5. **Handoff** — Continue the journey in the Customer App (tracking, pick-up/drop-off, payment status) once the order is created.

This journey is the primary revenue driver for the website.

### 2. Corporate buyback and bulk leads

A typical business path:

1. **Discover** — Reach dedicated corporate or bulk-sell pages via navigation, campaigns, or search.
2. **Understand offering** — Review eligibility (device types, volumes), service regions, and expected timelines.
3. **Submit lead** — Fill a structured lead form with company details, approximate volumes, and contact information.
4. **Handoff** — Route the lead into internal workflows in the Admin Panel or CRM for qualification and follow-up.

The main outcome here is a qualified lead, not a completed order.

### 3. SEO content to qualified session

A typical SEO-driven path:

1. **Search** — Enter queries like "sell iPhone 12 in Mumbai" or "best price for old laptop".
2. **Land** — Arrive on a device-, brand-, or city-specific landing page.
3. **Engage** — Read concise content that builds trust (pricing transparency, process, FAQs, reviews).
4. **Convert** — Move into the consumer device trade-in or corporate lead flows described above.

The success of this journey is measured by how many SEO visits progress into quote or lead actions.

## Key objectives and KPIs

The Cashkr Website exists to turn attention into opportunities and orders. Common objectives include:

- **Drive device resale orders**
  - KPIs: number of trade-in requests started, completed order submissions, conversion rate from visit to quote/order.

- **Capture corporate and bulk leads**
  - KPIs: number of lead form submissions, lead qualification rate, conversion to signed deals.

- **Grow organic and campaign traffic**
  - KPIs: organic sessions, ranking for target keywords, click-through rates on key entry pages.

- **Support customer account and order interactions**
  - KPIs: login or app-download initiations from the website, click-through to Customer App, self-serve resolution rate for order-status or FAQ visits.

- **Maintain brand and trust signals**
  - KPIs: bounce rate on trust-critical pages (About, FAQs, reviews), time on page, explicit trust indicators (reviews, NPS where surfaced).

Teams should align any new website changes with at least one of these objectives and define measurable impact before shipping.

## Relationship to other Cashkr surfaces

The website does not operate in isolation; it is the front door to the broader Cashkr ecosystem.

### Customer App

The Customer App manages the lifecycle after a consumer commits to a trade-in:

- Receives orders originating on the website.
- Handles tracking, communication, and payout status.
- Surfaces account history, saved details, and repeat-sell prompts.

Success on the website feeds qualified, high-intent users into the Customer App.

### Vendor App

The Vendor App supports partner shops or logistics vendors who execute on the orders:

- Uses order information initially captured via the website flows.
- Coordinates pick-ups, inspections, and local partner actions.
- Ensures devices promised on the site are handled according to operational rules.

Website accuracy (pricing, eligibility, SLAs) directly affects vendor workloads and expectations.

### Admin Panel

The Admin Panel is the internal control plane that configures what the website exposes:

- Manages device catalogs, pricing rules, and eligibility criteria.
- Controls promotional content, banners, and lead routing destinations.
- Provides reporting that links traffic and conversion data back to operational performance.

Product and operations teams use the Admin Panel to keep the public website and downstream systems aligned.
