The Store Leads view shows all store-based leads that are pending follow-up or conversion. Admins use this page to track store walk-ins, update dispositions, and convert qualified leads into confirmed store orders.

## Where this page fits

The Store section in the Admin Panel is divided into three views:

- **Leads**: Store leads that are pending action or conversion.
- **Completed**: Store orders that have been successfully completed.
- **Failed**: Store orders that failed or could not be completed.

Leads move out of the Leads view once you convert them to orders; from there, the orders continue through the main B2C pipeline and end up in either Completed or Failed based on the outcome.

## What you see in the table

Each row in the Leads table represents one pending store lead with the following key fields:

- **Created At**: Date and time the lead was created.
- **Model**: Device model associated with the lead.
- **City**: City where the lead originated.
- **Type**: Device category such as Mobile, Laptop, Tablet, or Mac.
- **Status**: Current status of the lead based on your last action.

When no leads match the active filters, the page displays a `No Orders Found` message.

## Filters and controls

Use filters and search to focus on the leads that need your attention.

- **Status filter**: Narrow leads by their current status.
- **Type filter**: Filter by device category such as Mobile, Laptop, Tablet, or Mac.
- **City filter**: Filter by the city where the lead originated.
- **Date range**: Filter leads by creation date using the From and To date fields.
- **Search**: Search for a specific lead by order details, device name, or customer information.
- **Filters button**: Open additional filtering options when available.
- **Apply filters**: Apply the current filter and search settings.
- **Reset**: Clear all filters and return to the full list of leads.

## Admin actions

From the Leads table, you manage each lead and progress it toward conversion:

- Open a lead row to review full lead details.
- Update the lead status after each follow-up attempt.
- Mark as **Connected** and select a reason such as Callback, Sold Elsewhere, Order Already Placed, Better Price Elsewhere, Higher Expectation, Was Just Checking, Not Selling Anywhere, or Out of Town.
- Mark as **Not Connected** and select a reason such as Switch Off, Not Reachable, Ringing, or Wrong Number.
- Convert a confirmed lead to an order by entering pickup address, payment details, and appointment date and time.
- Schedule follow-up callbacks for leads that require later contact.

## Lead conversion flow

Use this flow to move each store lead from creation to final outcome.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Lead created in store" icon="download">
    A store-based lead is generated and automatically appears in the Store Leads view with a pending status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initial follow-up from admin" icon="settings">
    Open the lead, contact the customer, and update the status as Connected or Not Connected with the correct reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customer confirms order" icon="users">
    Once the customer agrees to proceed, confirm details such as model, price, and pickup expectations during your follow-up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Convert lead to order" icon="rocket">
    Create the order from the lead by adding pickup address, payment details, and appointment date and time; the lead leaves the Leads view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order moves through B2C pipeline" icon="bar-chart">
    The converted order progresses through the main B2C order pipeline and ultimately appears in Store Completed on success or Store Failed on failure.
  </Step>
</Steps>
