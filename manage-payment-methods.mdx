## Overview

Manage how users receive payouts by configuring their preferred payment method during the sell-device flow or from the Profile payment screen.

## Available payment options

Users can choose from the following payment methods:

- **Cash**
- **UPI**
- **Bank transfer**

## Where users can manage payment details

<Steps>

  <Step title="Add or update UPI ID while selling a device">

  - Start the **sell device** flow.
  - When prompted for payment method, select **UPI**.
  - Enter or update the user's UPI ID.
  - Confirm and continue to the next step in the sell flow.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Add or update bank details while selling a device">

  - Start the **sell device** flow.
  - When prompted for payment method, select **Bank**.
  - Enter or update bank details: **account number**, **IFSC**, **bank name**, and **account holder name**.
  - Confirm and continue to the next step in the sell flow.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Add or update UPI ID from Profile → Payment">

  - Go to **My Profile** on the website.
  - Open the **Payment** screen.
  - Select **UPI** and enter or update the user's UPI ID.
  - Save the changes.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Add or update bank details from Profile → Payment">

  - Go to **My Profile** on the website.
  - Open the **Payment** screen.
  - Select **Bank** and enter or update bank details: **account number**, **IFSC**, **bank name**, and **account holder name**.
  - Save the changes.

  </Step>

</Steps>

## Restrictions

<Callout kind="alert">

Payment method details (UPI and bank information) cannot be edited after the order is placed. Ensure all details are correct before confirming the order.

</Callout>
