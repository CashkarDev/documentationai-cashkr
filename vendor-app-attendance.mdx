## Overview

Attendance is recorded through the Vendor App. There is no separate punch-in screen — **logging in with a selfie is the punch-in**.

How the day ends depends on whether you are a vendor or a cashboy.

## If you are a vendor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in with a selfie" icon="camera">
    Open the Vendor App and log in. You are asked for a selfie as part of signing in. Once you are in, your punch-in for the day is recorded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work as normal" icon="truck">
    Attendance does not affect unlocking leads, calling customers, or completing orders. Nothing else changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You are logged out at midnight" icon="moon">
    At 12:00 AM the app logs you out automatically, and that is recorded as your punch-out. There is no manual punch-out for vendors and nothing you need to do at the end of your day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
Because the punch-out is automatic, your recorded end time is always midnight rather than the time you actually stopped. Your attendance shows the days you were active, not the hours you worked.
</Callout>

## If you are a cashboy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in with a selfie" icon="camera">
    Same as the vendor — logging in with a selfie records your punch-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work as normal" icon="package">
    Handle the pickups and orders your vendor assigns to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Punch out with a selfie" icon="log-out">
    At the end of your day, punch out. This also requires a selfie.

    Unlike a vendor, this step is yours to do. Your recorded hours reflect when you actually punched out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
Remember to punch out. A vendor's day is closed automatically at midnight, but a cashboy's is not — forgetting leaves your record open for the day.
</Callout>

## Vendors: reviewing your cashboys

A vendor can see the attendance of the cashboys registered under their own account, so you can check who was present on a given day before assigning work.

You can only see your own cashboys. Attendance for other vendors and their cashboys is not visible to you.

## What admins see

Vendor and cashboy attendance also appear in the Admin Panel, inside each vendor's detail panel alongside devices, pincodes, credits, and tier access.

This means attendance is visible to the operations team as well as to you.
