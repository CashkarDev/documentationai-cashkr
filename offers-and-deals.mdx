# Offers and Deals

Manage the list of special offers and deals that appear on the BigboldTech website, including product details and affiliate links.

## What the Offers and Deals collection shows

The Offers and Deals collection stores every offer that appears on the website.

- List of offers and deals with product details.

- Associated affiliate link URL for each product.

- Integration with the website\`s offers and deals section.

## Key UI elements

Use these controls at the top of the Offers and Deals collection:

- **Search and filter**: Locate offers by product name or by status.

- **Create new entry**: Add a new offer or deal with its product details and affiliate link URL.

## Manage offers and deals

Use the Content Manager to search, add, update, and delete offers in the Offers and Deals collection.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Offers and Deals collection" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    From the Strapi sidebar, open **Content Manager**, then select the **Offers and Deals** collection type. The list shows all existing offers and deals.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Search and filter offers" icon="search" title-type="p">
    Use the search bar to look up offers by product name, and apply filters to narrow results by status. This helps you quickly find an offer to review or edit.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a new offer or deal" icon="plus" title-type="p">
    Click **Create new entry**. Enter the product details such as **name** and **description**, and add the **affiliate link URL**. Save and publish the entry to make the new offer live.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Update an existing offer" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    Click an existing entry in the list, adjust the product details or affiliate link URL as needed, then save and publish. The updated offer appears on the website as soon as you publish.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Delete an offer" icon="trash" title-type="p">
    Open the entry you want to remove and use the delete action. Confirm the deletion when prompted to permanently remove the offer from Strapi and the website.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  New offers go live after you publish them, and any published updates reflect instantly on the website\`s offers and deals section.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  When you confirm deletion, Strapi removes the offer from both the Offers and Deals collection and the live website. Double-check before deleting.
</Callout>
