## What lead creation shows

Lead creation captures users who reach the final device price but do not confirm an order, so Operations can follow up and convert them.

<Callout kind="info">

- When a customer completes the device Q&A flow, reaches the final step, and sees the final price **but does not confirm or place the order**, the system creates a lead.
- Each lead stores the selected device, all question answers, the entered pincode, and customer contact information.
- The system creates leads **only when the customer is logged in**.
- Leads appear in **Admin Panel → Leads** and are used by the Operations team for manual follow-up and conversion.

</Callout>

## Step-by-step user flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Customer completes device Q&A" icon="zap" title-type="p">

  - Customer selects a device on the website.
  - Customer answers all condition and device-related questions.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Final price is shown" icon="monitor" title-type="p">

  - System calculates and displays the final device price on the last step of the flow.
  - Customer is logged in while viewing this final price.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Order is not confirmed" icon="x-circle" title-type="p">

  - Customer does **not** click the confirm/place-order action.
  - Customer leaves the flow, navigates away, or closes the session without confirming.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Lead is created" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">

  - System creates a lead with the device details, Q&A answers, pincode, and customer contact information.
  - Lead is stored in **Admin Panel → Leads** for the Operations team to follow up.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## System logic

- Create a lead when **all** of the following are true:
  - Customer completes the device Q&A and the **final price is shown**.
  - Customer **does not place or confirm the order**.
  - Customer is **logged in**.
- Store the created lead in **Admin Panel → Leads**.
- Include in the lead:
  - Selected device details.
  - All device Q&A answers.
  - Entered pincode.
  - Customer contact information.
- Use created leads for **Operations team follow-up and conversion**.

## Connections

- Lead records appear under **All Leads** in the **Admin Panel**.
- The Operations team uses these leads as a **trigger for manual follow-up** with customers who dropped off after seeing the final price.

## Developer and tester notes

**For developers**

- Ensure the system creates a lead when the customer reaches the **final price screen** but does **not** confirm or place the order.
- Create leads **only when the customer is logged in** at the time the final price is shown.
- Save the lead in **Admin Panel → Leads** with:
  - Customer name and contact information.
  - Selected device and condition details.
  - All Q&A answers and the pincode.

**For testers**

- Verify that a lead is created **only when the user does not place or confirm the order** after seeing the final price.
- Confirm that **no lead** is created when:
  - The user does not reach the final price screen, or
  - The user successfully confirms/places the order.
- Check that leads **sync correctly** to **Admin Panel → Leads** with complete customer and device data.
- Validate that created leads are available for the Operations team and can be used for **follow-up and conversion tracking**.
