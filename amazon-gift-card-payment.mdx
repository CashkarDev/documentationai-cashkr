## Overview

Customers can choose **Amazon Gift Card** as a payment method when creating an order, alongside Cash, UPI, and Bank transfer. If the customer selects this option, the gift card details are shared only after the vendor completes the order. The feature is available on both the Customer mobile app and the Cashkr website.

## Eligibility

<Callout kind="alert">

Amazon Gift Card is available only for orders with a total value less than ₹10,000 at the time of order creation. Orders of ₹10,000 or above will not display the gift card option — customers must choose Cash, UPI, or Bank transfer instead.

</Callout>

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step title="Order creation">

Customer creates a sell order from the app or website. If the order value is below ₹10,000, the gift card option appears alongside Cash, UPI, and Bank on the payment method selection screen.

</Step>
<Step title="Select Gift Card">

Customer selects Amazon Gift Card as the preferred payment method. No gift card details are shared at this point — only the payment preference is recorded on the order.

</Step>
<Step title="Vendor processes and completes" icon="check-circle">

The vendor picks up the device, inspects it, and completes the order in the Vendor App as usual. The vendor does not need to do anything different for gift card orders.

</Step>
<Step title="Gift card delivery">

Once the vendor marks the order as complete, the system automatically issues Amazon Gift Card details, including the gift card code and redemption instructions, to the customer.

</Step>
<Step title="Redeem on Amazon" icon="external-link">

The customer redeems the gift card on Amazon by adding the code to the Amazon account balance. The gift card is redeemable exclusively on Amazon.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Key points

| Point | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Gift card issuance timing** | Gift card details are issued only after the vendor marks the order as complete — not at the time of order placement. |
| **Redemption platform** | The gift card is redeemable exclusively on Amazon. |
| **Eligibility threshold** | The ₹10,000 threshold applies to the order value at the time of creation. Orders ≥ ₹10,000 must use Cash, UPI, or Bank. |
| **Platforms** | Available on both the Customer mobile app and the Cashkr website. |
| **Vendor impact** | Vendors do not need to take any additional action for gift card orders — the system handles delivery automatically upon order completion. |
