## Adding a new tool

When you add a new Business Development tool, update this page so others know what it is and when to use it. For each new tool, capture:

- **Tool name** – The exact product name.
- **Category** – For example: social media, sales outreach, CRM, documentation, or reporting.
- **Primary use case** – One clear sentence on what the team uses it for.
- **Notes** – Any important context such as ownership, access, or key do and do nots.

Keep descriptions concise and practical so new team members can quickly understand the tool and decide if it is relevant to their work.
