## Overview

Complete marks the successful end of the vendor In Progress workflow and moves the order into the payout queue.

When you complete an order, the app captures final inspection details and updates the order status across the Vendor App, Customer App/Website, and Admin Panel.

<Callout kind="info">

When a customer has selected **Amazon Gift Card** as their payment method, completing the order automatically triggers Amazon Gift Card delivery to the customer. You do not need to do anything extra — the system issues gift card details (gift card code and redemption instructions) to the customer upon order completion. The gift card is redeemable exclusively on Amazon.

</Callout>

## When you can complete an order

Completion is only available after you move the order into the final inspection state.

<Callout kind="info">

To see the **Complete Order** action in the Vendor App, you must:

- Mark the order as **Out for Pickup**.
- Reach the customer and start inspection.
- Enter and verify the **lead OTP** shared with the customer.

If inspection does not match the initial offer or the customer disagrees, use **Requote** or the **Fail After Requote** path instead of completion. For details on these flows, see [Out for Pickup or Fail](/out-for-pickup-or-fail) and [Fail After Requote](/fail-after-requote).

</Callout>

<Callout kind="info">

The **inspection timer** must be considered during completion. If the timer has expired before you tap **Complete Order**, you must select a **delay reason** from a predefined list before the app allows you to proceed with completion. See the [Inspection Timer](/inspection-timer) page for full details.

</Callout>

After OTP verification, the app unlocks both **Complete Order** and **Requote**. Use **Complete Order** only when you and the customer agree on the final price and you are closing the deal.

## Step-by-step: complete an order

Use this flow when you have finished inspection, agreed on the price, and are ready to close the order.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the order and tap Complete Order" icon="check-circle">

- Go to **InProgress Orders** in the Vendor App.
- Open the target order that is already marked as **Out for Pickup** with OTP verified.
- Tap **Complete Order** to start the completion form.

</Step>

<Step title="Capture customer signature" icon="edit">

- Ask the customer to sign on the app screen.
- Capture and save the **signature** as required by the app.

</Step>

<Step title="Upload ID card photos (front and back)" icon="upload">

- Take a clear photo of the **front** side of the customer ID card.
- Take a clear photo of the **back** side of the same ID card.
- Upload both photos in the respective front and back fields.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter closing amount" icon="terminal">

- Enter the **closing amount** agreed with the customer.
- Confirm that this amount matches what you will pay out.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm device IMEI" icon="info">

- Review the **IMEI** displayed in the app.
- Confirm that the IMEI matches the device you are buying.

</Step>

<Step title="Upload bill photo or blank document" icon="upload">

- If the customer has a purchase bill, take and upload a **bill photo**.
- If no bill is available, upload a **blank photo** or placeholder image as per the app guidance, so the field is not left empty.

</Step>

<Step title="Review and confirm completion" icon="check">

- Review all captured details: signature, ID photos, closing amount, IMEI, and bill/blank photo.
- Tap **Confirm** to submit and complete the order.
- Wait for the confirmation message and ensure the order moves out of **InProgress Orders**.

</Step>

</Steps>

When completion succeeds, the order no longer appears in In Progress and moves into the Completed lifecycle state.

## Inputs captured

Completion captures a fixed set of data used for compliance, verification, and payouts.

- **Customer signature**: Digital proof that the customer agreed to sell the device.
- **ID card front photo**: Front side of the government ID for KYC and verification.
- **ID card back photo**: Back side of the same ID, completing the KYC record.
- **Closing amount**: Final price you will pay the customer for the device.
- **IMEI confirmation**: Final device identifier confirmation to prevent mismatches.
- **Bill photo or blank photo**:
  - If a purchase bill exists, the app stores a photo of the bill.
  - If no bill exists, the app stores a blank or placeholder image to keep the record complete.
- **Total inspection time taken**: Actual elapsed time from inspection start to order completion.
- **Delay reason**: Selected by the vendor if the inspection timer expired before completion. Not captured if the vendor completed within the configured duration.

These inputs are stored with the order and are visible in the Admin Panel for audit and support.

## System logic and state updates

Completion does more than close the screen; it updates system-level states and triggers payouts.

<Callout kind="info">

Key business rules on completion:

- Completion **triggers the payout queue** for the order.
- The order status changes to **Completed** in:
  - Vendor App
  - Customer App and website
  - Admin Panel
- The In Progress workflow for this order ends at completion.
- If the order's payment mode is **Amazon Gift Card**, completing the order triggers automatic gift card issuance to the customer instead of a standard payout (Bank/UPI) or cash settlement. No additional vendor action is required.

</Callout>

When the vendor submits the completion form:

- The system validates that all required inputs are present.
- The order leaves the **In Progress** list and enters the **Completed** state.
- Internal payout processing services pick up the order from the payout queue.
- Downstream systems reference the completed order for reconciliation and reporting.
- Completion records the **total inspection time taken**. If the inspection timer expired, the selected **delay reason** is also recorded with the order.

## Connections

Completion is connected to multiple surfaces so all parties see a consistent final state.

- **Admin Activity Log**
  - The system creates an **activity log entry** capturing that the vendor completed the order.
  - Admin users can review who completed the order and when.

- **Admin Orders section**
  - The order is marked as **Completed** in the Admin Panel orders section.
  - All completion inputs (signature, ID, IMEI, bill/blank photo, closing amount) are available to admins.

- **Vendor credits deduction**
  - On completion, the system records a **credit deduction event** in the vendor section.
  - The vendor credit balance reflects any deductions linked to the completed order.

- **Customer App and website**
  - The customer sees the order as **Completed** in the Customer App and website.
  - Any subsequent payout or tracking flows reference this completed state.
  - For Amazon Gift Card orders, the customer receives gift card details (gift card code and redemption instructions) after the vendor completes the order. The gift card is redeemable exclusively on Amazon. See [Amazon Gift Card Payment Option](/amazon-gift-card-payment) for full details.

- **Vendor App**
  - The completed order is no longer listed under **InProgress Orders**.
  - The order appears under the relevant **Completed** or history view, depending on app design.

## Common states and errors

This section helps you understand what you will see or need to fix when something blocks completion.

### Typical states

- **Out for Pickup, OTP not verified**
  - **Complete Order** is not available.
  - You must verify the lead OTP before you can proceed.

- **Out for Pickup, OTP verified**
  - **Complete Order** and **Requote** actions are available.
  - Use Complete to close at the agreed price; use Requote if the price changes.

### Common issues

- **Missing required input**
  - The app blocks submission if:
    - Signature is not captured.
    - Either ID front or back photo is missing.
    - Closing amount is empty or invalid.
    - IMEI is not confirmed.
    - Bill/blank photo is missing.
  - Fix: Add the missing input and retry.

- **Upload failures**
  - Network issues can cause image uploads (ID, bill/blank photo) to fail.
  - Fix: Check connectivity, retry the upload, and confirm that the image shows as attached before submitting.

- **OTP-related blocking**
  - If OTP verification fails or times out, the app does not allow completion.
  - Fix: Reattempt OTP verification as per the [Out for Pickup or Fail](/out-for-pickup-or-fail) guidelines.

If errors persist, do not attempt partial workarounds; keep the order In Progress and escalate with full context to support or admin.

## Developer and tester notes

Use this section when you are integrating, testing, or debugging the completion behavior.

<Callout kind="tip">

Treat completion as the **single source of truth** for closing a vendor order. All downstream payout and status flows depend on this event and its captured inputs.

</Callout>

- **Prerequisite enforcement**
  - Ensure APIs and app logic enforce the condition: completion available only when:
    - Order is **Out for Pickup**, and
    - Lead OTP is successfully **verified**.
  - Automated tests should cover that Complete and Requote are hidden or disabled before OTP verification.

- **Data integrity**
  - Verify that all completion inputs are stored and linked to the correct order:
    - Signature file or data.
    - ID front and back images.
    - Closing amount value.
    - IMEI used for final confirmation.
    - Bill or blank image.
  - Check that Admin views and logs surface this data correctly.

- **State propagation**
  - Confirm that when an order is completed:
    - Vendor App removes the order from In Progress.
    - Customer App and website show the order as Completed.
    - Admin Panel orders reflect Completed status.
    - Admin Activity Log includes a completion entry.
    - Vendor credit balance registers a **credit deduction event** related to the order.
    - The order appears in the payout queue for further processing.
    - If the order payment mode is Amazon Gift Card, verify that gift card details are issued to the customer upon completion (no manual payout needed).

- **Negative coverage**
  - Test failure paths:
    - Attempt completion without one or more required fields.
    - Try to complete an order that skipped the Out for Pickup + OTP steps.
    - Validate that **Fail After Requote** and other fail flows do not trigger the payout queue or credit deduction meant for successful completion.

- **Inspection timer integration**
  - Test the timer expiry → delay reason selection → completion flow: start an inspection, let the timer expire, select a delay reason, then complete the order.
  - Verify that total time taken and delay reason are stored and visible in admin order details.
  - Verify that completing within the timer does not require a delay reason.
