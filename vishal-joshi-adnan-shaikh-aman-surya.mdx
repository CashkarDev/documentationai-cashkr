## Role & reporting line

- **Role:** In-House Pickup Associate – Cashkr  
- **Department:** Operations  
- **Applies to:** Adnan Shaikh, Aman Surya, Vishal Joshi  
- **Reports to:** Head of Operations, Pricing & Models – Mohsin Surya  
- **Works closely with:**
  - **CX Team:** For customer coordination and escalations  
  - **Mohsin:** For pricing logic, feedback and special cases (only when system does not cover)  
  - **Kishan:** For CX expectations and common issues  
  - **Umair:** Indirectly, via feedback on SLA/quality  

## Role purpose

Visit customers on behalf of Cashkr, verify device condition, apply system record pricing, and complete pickups smoothly while giving a professional, trustworthy experience—without needing manual price approvals for normal cases.

## Key result areas (KRAs)

- **KRA 1: On-Time & Successful Pickups** – Complete assigned pickups within the expected time (SLA) and convert maximum valid appointments into successful deals.
- **KRA 2: Accurate Device Evaluation & App Flow Completion** – Evaluate devices correctly as per Cashkr’s condition rules and complete all steps in the app (photos, checks, dispositions) without shortcuts.
- **KRA 3: Record Price Adherence (No Off-Book Deals)** – Follow the system record price and authorised deduction/adjustment rules; avoid random discounts or side deals.
- **KRA 4: Customer Experience & Communication** – Maintain polite, clear communication at the customer’s home—explain pricing, process, and payout clearly, represent Cashkr well.
- **KRA 5: On-Ground Feedback to Ops/Pricing** – Share practical feedback with Mohsin / Ops on pricing and conditions (for example, common issues found, patterns not covered by the form), so the system logic keeps improving.

## KPIs (per person)

### Pickup Completion Rate

- **Definition / formula:** Number of pickups completed ÷ number of pickups assigned.

### SLA Compliance

- **Definition / formula:** Percentage of pickups completed within target time (on same day / promised slot).

### Cancellation/Failure Rate (Associate-Side)

- **Definition / formula:** Percentage of appointments failed due to associate-side reasons (no-show, delay, miscommunication) vs customer-side reasons.

### Record Price Adherence

- **Definition / formula:** Percentage of deals closed at or within allowed range of system record price.
- **Notes:** Number of cases where manual override was needed (should be low and justifiable).

### Customer Complaints/Praise

- **Definition / formula:** Number of complaints naming the associate (rude, unprofessional, wrong info) vs number of positive mentions.

### App Flow & Disposition Accuracy

- **Definition / formula:** Percentage of orders where all required fields, photos, and statuses are correctly filled.
- **Notes:** Number of corrections requested by Ops/Quality due to wrong disposition or missing data.

### Feedback Quality

- **Definition:** Useful, specific feedback given to Mohsin/Ops (for example, pricing misalignment in some models, consistent condition pattern in a city).

## SOPs to follow

They do not own SOPs; they follow them as defined in the handbook.

### Pickup Visit SOP

1. Confirm appointment and timing with customer.
2. Reach on time, in proper attire, with ID if required.
3. Introduce self as Cashkr representative and briefly explain what will happen.

### Device Evaluation SOP

1. Follow step-by-step checks in the app:
   - Physical condition (screen, body, back, frame).
   - Functional tests (power, touch, camera, speakers, mic, and so on).
   - Battery health (where applicable).
2. Take required photos in the app.
3. Mark correct condition grade as per the system rules.

### Pricing Application SOP (Record Price)

1. Use the system-generated record price based on model and condition.
2. Apply only allowed adjustments visible in the app (for example, predefined deduction options).
3. If something unusual is outside the system logic:
   - Mark the correct reason.
   - Contact Ops only in rare cases, as per rule (not for every deal).

### Customer Communication SOP

1. Explain how final price is calculated (based on condition and what the customer declared vs actual).
2. If the price is lower than the online quote, clearly explain the reason (damage, missing accessories, and so on).
3. Never argue; if customer refuses:
   - Close politely.
   - Use correct disposition.

### Safety, Compliance & Payout SOP

1. Follow any KYC/verification steps defined by Ops.
2. Confirm payout method and ensure records in the app are accurate.
3. Do not accept or pay any cash outside defined flows if company policy disallows it.

### Feedback & Reporting SOP

1. At the end of day or week, share feedback to Mohsin/Ops:
   - Models where record price feels off (too high/too low vs ground reality).
   - Recurring condition patterns.
   - Any customer confusion that should be fixed in app flows or FAQ.

## Weekly & monthly reporting

### Weekly

- **To:** Mohsin  
- **Includes:**
  - Number of pickups assigned vs completed.
  - SLA on-time percentage (visits).
  - Number of fails/cancellations and main reasons.
  - Any complaints raised in the week.
  - Quick notes: 2–3 observations from the field.

### Monthly

- **To:** Rolled up by Mohsin  
- **Includes:**
  - Completion percentage, SLA, cancellations (by associate).
  - Complaint count vs good feedback.
  - Any patterns (for example, one associate always late, one with best CSAT).
  - Recommendations: coaching, appreciation, role changes if needed.
