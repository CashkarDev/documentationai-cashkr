# LogIn and Forgot Password - BBT

Access the BigBoldTech Strapi admin panel to manage website articles and recover your account if you forget your password.

## Overview

The BigBoldTech Strapi admin login page provides secure access for team members to create and manage article content.  
Any changes you make to articles in Strapi appear on the BigBoldTech website once published.

<Callout kind="info">

Strapi authenticates users against its internal user database. You must have a valid admin account to log in.

</Callout>

## Accessing the Strapi admin login

Open the Strapi admin login page in your browser:

- URL: `https://mktg.stage.articles.cashkr.com/admin/auth/login`

You should see the login screen with fields for email and password, as shown below.

## Login and password reset flow

Use this flow to either log in directly or recover access when you forget your password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Strapi admin" icon="log-in">

  - Go to `https://mktg.stage.articles.cashkr.com/admin/auth/login`.
  - Enter your registered **email address**.
  - Enter your **password**.
  - Click **Login**.

  When the credentials are valid, Strapi redirects you to the admin dashboard, where you can manage BigBoldTech articles and related content.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Start the forgot password flow" icon="help-circle">

  - On the login page, click **Forgot Password**.
  - Enter the **registered email address** associated with your Strapi admin account.
  - Click the button to request a reset link.

  Strapi sends a password reset email to the provided address if it matches an existing admin user.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Reset your password from the email link" icon="mail">

  - Open the password reset email in your inbox.
  - Click the **reset password** link in the email.
  - On the reset page, enter a **new password** and confirm it.
  - Submit the form to save your new password.

  After successful reset, return to the login page and log in using your email and the new password.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">

Password reset emails contain a temporary link that expires after a limited time. If the link no longer works, start the forgot password flow again to receive a fresh email.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert">

Password reset links grant access to your Strapi admin account. Do not forward or share these emails or links with anyone.

</Callout>

## How this affects the BigBoldTech website

Once you log in and update articles or related content types in Strapi, those changes sync to the BigBoldTech website.  
Use your admin access carefully, because edits, new articles, and content updates become visible to site visitors after they are published.
