## Overview

Vendor management and operations agents use this script to handle vendor calls and chats after a hold or suspension is lifted. The goal is to confirm reactivation, set clear expectations for SLAs and behavior, and rebuild trust while guiding vendors on how to stay active.

<Callout kind="info">

Use this script as a reference during live conversations to keep messaging consistent, professional, and aligned with Cashkr performance and compliance standards.

</Callout>

**Used by:** Vendor Management / Operations Team

### Objective

- Reinstate vendor confidence and trust after a hold or deactivation.
- Clarify post-reactivation responsibilities and expectations.
- Encourage sustained SLA compliance and good customer handling.

## Scenario scripts

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title='“Has my account been reactivated?”' default-open="true">

**Agent response:**

> Yes, your account has been successfully reactivated.  
> You can now start receiving and unlocking new orders again.  
> Log out and log back in once so your Vendor App refreshes and your order feed appears correctly.

If the vendor still cannot see orders:

> No problem — I will ask the technical team to refresh your order sync.  
> It usually takes a few minutes after reactivation for new leads to start appearing.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title='“Why was I reactivated now?”'>

**Agent response:**

> After our recent review, we noticed consistent improvement in your performance and communication.  
> The Ops team approved your reactivation after confirming that all pending SLA issues and documents are resolved.  
> We are glad to have you back on board.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title='“What should I do differently this time?”'>

**Agent response:**

> That is a good question, and it shows a proactive attitude.  
> Here are a few things that will help you stay active and receive regular leads:
>
> - Always respond to new leads quickly.  
> - Complete pickups within the committed time.  
> - Do not mark orders as failed without valid proof.  
> - Ensure instant payouts to customers after verification.  
> - Communicate politely with customers and with our support team.  
>
> Following these steps will help you avoid holds or deductions in the future.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title='“Can I get my old leads or orders back?”'>

**Agent response:**

> Leads from the period when your account was on hold are already expired or reassigned to other vendors.  
> New leads will start coming in based on your area and credit balance.  
> Make sure your Wallet and Credits are active so the system can assign new orders to you instantly.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title='“Do I need to do anything before I start again?”'>

**Agent response:**

> Yes, do a few quick checks before you start:
>
> - Make sure your Wallet has enough balance for customer payouts.  
> - Confirm that your Credits are available for unlocking new orders.  
> - Check your app version and update it if there is a newer version on the Play Store.  
> - Turn on location access and notifications for the app.  
>
> Once that is done, you are good to go.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title='“How can I avoid getting suspended again?”'>

**Agent response:**

> Stay active by maintaining a strong SLA record.  
> That means no delayed pickups, no missed payouts, and professional customer interaction.  
> The system automatically rewards consistent vendors — you will see higher lead flow and better-paying orders when your SLA stays clean for 2–3 weeks.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title='“Can I confirm if my Credits or Wallet are safe after reactivation?”'>

**Agent response:**

> Yes, your Wallet and Credits remain safe even during a hold.  
> Reactivation does not affect your balances.  
> You can check both under the Transactions section in your Vendor App.  
> If anything looks missing, tell me and I will confirm it from the backend.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title='“Who approved my reactivation?”'>

**Agent response:**

> The Operations Manager approved your reactivation after a full review of your performance and pending cases.  
> The final approval was recorded in the Admin Panel, and the system automatically restored your lead flow afterward.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title='“Thanks for reactivating me.”'>

**Agent response:**

> You are welcome.  
> We are happy to have you active again on the Cashkr Partner Network.  
> Let us keep service smooth — that helps you get more leads and higher-value devices.  
> If you ever face issues with credits, leads, or payouts, you can reach out to us directly and we will help.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Internal team notes (quick reference)

| Action                     | Responsible             | Checkpoints                        | Timeframe              |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Reactivation request raised | Vendor Management Team  | Verify SLA, documents, and reason  | Same day               |
| Ops Manager approval       | Operations Head         | Confirm eligibility                 | Within 24 hrs          |
| Account reactivated        | Admin Team              | Restore lead flow and credits      | Within 1 hr of approval |
| Vendor follow-up           | Agent                   | Confirm app refresh and readiness  | Within the same day    |

## Key tone phrases

- “Welcome back to the Cashkr Partner Network.”
- “We are glad to see your performance improving.”
- “Keep up the consistency — we will support you all the way.”
- “Your leads will start automatically once the sync is complete.”
- “Our goal is to help you grow with us.”
