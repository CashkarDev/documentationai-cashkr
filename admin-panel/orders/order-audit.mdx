## Overview

Open **Orders → Closed → Order Audit**. *Every completed order paid below the quoted price gets a person's sign-off.* Clear **Needs a look** first.

Audit results also show up as order statuses elsewhere: **Completed · Awaiting audit**, **Completed · Audited** and **Completed · Audit flagged** (available in the All Orders Status filter).

## Queues

The page is split into four queue cards. Each shows a count; click a card to open that queue.

| Queue | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs a look** | An automatic check failed — go through it properly |
| **Looks clean** | Every check passed — compare the screenshot and approve |
| **Flagged** | Waiting on your follow-up |
| **Approved** | Orders approved today |

When a queue is empty the page shows *Nothing waiting here.*

## Filters and search

- **Search** box — find an order by order ID, token or phone.
- **Show test orders** — checkbox to include test-account orders.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with Needs a look" icon="alert-triangle" title-type="p">
    These orders failed an automatic check. Review the price drop, inspection photos and timeline carefully before deciding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear Looks clean" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    All checks passed. Compare the screenshot with the order and approve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up on Flagged" icon="flag" title-type="p">
    Orders you flagged wait here until the follow-up with the vendor or customer is done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  Only completed orders paid **below** the quoted price enter the audit. Orders paid at the quoted price do not need a sign-off.
</Callout>
