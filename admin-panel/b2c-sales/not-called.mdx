## Overview

Open **B2C Sales → To Work → Not Called** (breadcrumb *Dashboard / B2C Sales / Not Called*). This view lists leads that no one has called yet, so the team can make sure every lead gets a first call.

## What the screen shows

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Order Date** | When the lead was created |
| **Model** | Device model |
| **Assigned** | Sales executive the lead is assigned to, or *Unassigned* |
| **Type** | Device category badge |
| **Status** | Lead status, for example **New** |
| **Notes** | Call notes, or *No Notes* |

The list is paginated (25 rows per page).

## Filters and search

- **Mine / All / Unassigned** toggle at the top right.
- **Search leads...** box in the header.
- Click **Filters** to open: **Type** (device type), **All Tiers** (High End, Mid End, Low End), **City**, and a from/to date range. Click **Apply Filters** or **Reset**.

## Actions

Click a row to open the lead drawer — call the customer with **Click to call**, record the outcome under **Update Disposition**, or convert with **Create Lead**. See [New Leads](/new-leads) for the drawer and dispositions.

<Callout kind="info">
  Leads marked Connected - Callback or Not Connected are worked from **Callbacks**. Always save a disposition after every call so the lead leaves this queue.
</Callout>
