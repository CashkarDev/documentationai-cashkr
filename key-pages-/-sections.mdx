## Acquisition and discovery

Pages that attract visitors, capture intent, and route users into trade-in or corporate flows.

<Columns cols={2}>

  <Card title="Search Functionality" href="/search-functionality" icon="search">
    Homepage universal search bar with autosuggestions that lets users quickly find devices. Results come from Admin Panel products, and only active models with valid pricing and class lead into the evaluation Q&A.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Device Price Calculator" href="/device-price-calculator" icon="bar-chart">
    Standalone calculator that estimates resale value without placing an order. Users pick a category and answer condition questions; pricing uses the same logic and data sources as the Sell Device flow.
  </Card>

</Columns>

<Columns cols={2}>

  <Card title="Articles" href="/articles" icon="book-open">
    SEO-focused articles list and detail pages powered by Strapi CMS. Content, thumbnails, and metadata sync in real time from Strapi and support SEO tags for discovery.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Corporate Buyback" href="/corporate-buyback" icon="users">
    Public lead form for corporate device buyback with required contact and location details. Each submission creates a lead in the Admin Panel Buyback module and moves through In Progress, Approved, and Completed statuses.
  </Card>

</Columns>

<Columns cols={2}>

  <Card title="Partner With Us" href="/partner-with-us" icon="handshake">
    Vendor acquisition page that explains how vendors work with Cashkr. The primary call-to-action is to download the Vendor App from Google Play; there is no form or dynamic logic on this page.
  </Card>

</Columns>

## Sell / trade-in journey

Pages and flows involved in selling a device as a consumer, from evaluation to order tracking and drop-off leads.

<Columns cols={2}>

  <Card title="Sell Device Flow" href="/sell-device-flow" icon="shopping-cart">
    Step-based trade-in journey covering device selection, serviceability by area or pincode, and condition questions that define price. Checkout collects coupons, address, pickup slot, and payment method, and connects to Admin Panel modules for product, pricing, areas, coupons, and orders.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Lead Creation" href="/lead-creation" icon="file-plus">
    Drop-off capture for users who reach a final price in the Q&A but do not place an order. The system creates a lead with device details, answers, pincode, and contact info so Operations can follow up from the Admin Panel.
  </Card>

</Columns>

<Columns cols={2}>

  <Card title="Track Orders" href="/track-orders" icon="clock">
    Logged-in view of all orders with statuses Generated, In Progress, Completed, and Failed. Users can reschedule only while the order is Generated and unassigned, and call an agent once a vendor is assigned, with real-time sync across Website, Vendor App, Customer App, and Admin Panel.
  </Card>

</Columns>

## Account and profile

Shared account surfaces that handle login, identity, addresses, payout details, and account lifecycle.

<Columns cols={2}>

  <Card title="Login & Registration" href="/login-registration" icon="lock">
    Phone number plus OTP login shared with the Customer App. New users provide basic profile details after OTP, while existing users log in directly; the phone number is the unique identifier and drives session token creation.
  </Card>

  <Card title="My Profile" href="/my-profile" icon="user">
    Profile screen showing first name, last name, and email as editable fields, with phone number locked. Updates persist to the profile while keeping phone immutable and consistent across Website, Admin Panel, and Customer App.
  </Card>

</Columns>

<Columns cols={2}>

  <Card title="Address" href="/address" icon="map-pin">
    Address management page listing saved pickup addresses and allowing add or edit. Enforces a maximum of three addresses, derives city from pincode, syncs with the Customer App, and snapshots the selected address into each order.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Payment" href="/payment" icon="credit-card">
    Screen for storing payout details such as bank account or UPI on the user profile. Validates inputs and ensures payment details are available for payouts after order completion and kept in sync across all connected systems.
  </Card>

</Columns>

<Columns cols={2}>

  <Card title="Logout and Account deletion" href="/logout-and-account-deletion" icon="shield">
    Describes how logout clears the session token and returns the user to the homepage, blocking restricted pages. Also covers account deletion, which requires phone plus OTP verification, anonymizes the phone, soft-deletes the record, and propagates removal across login and connected systems.
  </Card>

</Columns>

## Growth and referrals

Surfaces that support referral-driven growth and rewards.

<Columns cols={2}>

  <Card title="Refer & Earn" href="/refer-earn" icon="gift">
    Referral program page where each customer has a permanent referral code used once by a new customer at registration. Rewards apply after order completion: the referee receives a discount at checkout, and the referrer reward moves from pending to wallet credit after admin approval, with safeguards against resetting eligibility by re-registering.
  </Card>

</Columns>
