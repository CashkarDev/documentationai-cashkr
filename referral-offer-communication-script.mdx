## Overview

- **Team:** Sales / Vendor Support / Customer Engagement  

- **When used:**
  - Informing customers about referral programs
  - Communicating special offers like exchange bonuses, extra cash, or seasonal deals

- **Goal:** Communicate Cashkr offers and referral programs clearly so customers either sell their device or refer friends and family.

- **Tone:** Friendly, motivating, and action-oriented.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English (Script)" icon="book-open">

<Steps>

<Step title="Opening" icon="play-circle">

> Hi \[Customer Name], this is \[Your Name] from Cashkr.  
> I am calling to share an exciting offer and let you know about our Referral Program.

</Step>

<Step title="Explain referral program" icon="users">

> Cashkr offers a Referral Program where you can earn ₹\_\_\_\_ for every friend or family member you refer to sell their old phone with Cashkr.  
>  
> Here is how it works:  
> 1️⃣ You share your unique referral link (sent via WhatsApp or email).  
> 2️⃣ When your referred person sells their device through Cashkr, you will earn ₹\_\_\_\_ as a reward.

</Step>

<Step title="Explain current offers" icon="tag">

> We also have some limited-time offers running right now, which I think you will like.  
>  
> - Extra ₹2000 on Exchange!  
> - Get an additional ₹500 when you sell 2 devices!  
> - Seasonal Offer: 10% more for old phones when you upgrade to a new model  
>  
> These offers are available for a limited time and only on specific devices, so now is the perfect time to sell and upgrade.

</Step>

<Step title="Create urgency" icon="alert-triangle">

> These offers end on \[offer end date], so you will need to act quickly.  
> You can easily check your phone’s value and schedule a pickup in minutes using the Cashkr App or website.

</Step>

<Step title="Incentive to act" icon="zap">

> Not only do you get the extra bonus through the offer, but you also get to earn money for every referral.  
> You can use the extra value towards buying your next phone, or even get a discount on accessories through Cashkr.

</Step>

<Step title="Encourage referrals" icon="plus">

> Would you like me to send you your referral link so you can start sharing it with your friends and family?  
> The more people you refer, the more you can earn. There is no limit on how many people you can refer.

</Step>

<Step title="Closing (recap + ask)" icon="check-circle">

> So, to recap:  
> - You can earn extra cash by referring friends and getting them to sell with Cashkr.  
> - You also get ₹\_\_\_\_ on your old device plus special bonuses right now.  
>  
> Would you like me to send you the referral link and get started on your phone’s valuation?

</Step>

</Steps>

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish (Live conversations)" icon="users">

<Steps>

<Step title="Opening" icon="play-circle">

>  
> Main aapko referral link bhej doon aur aap apne phone ki valuation start karein?

</Step>

## Internal notes

- Use this script when informing existing or potential customers about offers and referral opportunities.
- Send the referral link and promo code after the call via WhatsApp or SMS.
- Track customer participation in the Referral Program Dashboard.
- Explain offer timelines clearly, including the end date and device eligibility, to avoid confusion.

<Callout kind="info">

Always send the referral link and any active promo code immediately after the call, and clearly mention offer timelines and device eligibility so customers know when and how they can use the offer.

</Callout>
