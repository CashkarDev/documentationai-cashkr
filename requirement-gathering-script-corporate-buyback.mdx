## Overview

Use this script when speaking with corporate customers about selling their company devices to Cashkr. The goal is to collect clear requirements so you can share an Excel format, get complete device details, and trigger a quotation within 24 hours.

- Used by: **B2B Sales Executive**
- Objective: Collect all necessary information on devices, conditions, security, logistics, and expectations.
- Outcome: Device sheet sent, internal records updated, quotation queued, and follow-up planned.

---

## 📞 Call script – requirement gathering

<Steps>

<Step title="Opening" icon="phone">

> “Hi \[Name], this is \[Your Name] again from **Cashkr**. I hope you’re doing well.  
> I wanted to quickly confirm the details for your company’s buyback request so we can prepare an accurate quotation for you.”

**Record:**

- Lead or enquiry reference (if any)
- Decision-maker’s name and role
- Preferred communication channel (WhatsApp or email)

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm company details" icon="building">

> “Before we begin, could I reconfirm your company’s full name and location for the pickup?”

**Record:**

- Company name
- Full address and pincode
- City
- Contact person name and phone

</Step>

<Step title="Understand device details" icon="monitor">

> “Could you share what type of devices you’re planning to sell?”

> “Got it. Could you tell me roughly how many of each?”

**Example follow-ups:**

> “Are these company-owned assets or employee exchange devices?”  
> “Are all the devices in working condition, or do you also have non-working ones?”  
> “Do you still have the chargers and accessories?”

**Record:**

- Device type (phones / laptops / Mac / tablet)
- Brand and model
- Approximate quantity per model
- Ownership type (company-owned or employee exchange)
- High-level condition (working, non-working, mixed)
- Accessories available (chargers, adapters, etc.)

</Step>

<Step title="Data and security" icon="shield">

> “Would you like us to provide **data wipe certificates** for your devices? We can issue certificates per serial number if needed.”

**Record:**

- Whether data erasure is required
- Type of certification needed (per device / per serial number)
- Note: `Data erasure required – certified` if applicable

</Step>

<Step title="Pickup and logistics" icon="truck">

> “Are all the devices located at one office or multiple branches?”  
> “Do you prefer **on-site inspection and pickup**, or do you want to **share the device list first** for an estimated price?”

**Record:**

- Pickup city
- Number of locations (single office or multiple branches)
- Any branch-level notes (large volumes, access restrictions)
- Preferred pickup or inspection date (if mentioned)
- Preference: on-site inspection and pickup vs share device list first

</Step>

<Step title="Expected price and timeline" icon="bar-chart">

> “Do you have an expected price range for the devices?” *(Optional – helps gauge expectation)*  
> “When are you planning to complete this buyback — this week or within the month?”

**Record:**

- Expected price range (if shared)
- Priority and timeline (this week, within the month, later)
- Any internal approval constraints mentioned

</Step>

<Step title="Documents and next steps" icon="file-text">

> “That’s great, thank you for sharing these details.  
> I’ll send you a simple Excel format right after this call — you can fill in your device details (make, model, condition, quantity), and we’ll share a formal **quotation within 24 hours.**”

> “Would you like me to include our **company profile** and **data destruction process document** as well?”

**Record:**

- Email address (and WhatsApp, if they prefer)
- Agreement to fill Excel device sheet
- Whether to attach:
  - Company profile
  - Data destruction process document

</Step>

<Step title="Close professionally" icon="check-circle">

> “Thanks a lot, \[Name].  
> Once you share the filled sheet, I’ll immediately have our pricing team work on your quote.  
> Expect a response from us within a day — and if needed, we can schedule a quick inspection or pickup right after quotation approval.”

> “I’ll WhatsApp or email you the format in the next 5 minutes.”

**Record:**

- Confirmation of how and when you will send the Excel format
- Customer’s confirmation to revert with the filled sheet
- Any agreed follow-up time (if specified)

</Step>

</Steps>
