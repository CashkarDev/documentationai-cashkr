## Overview

Used by: Store Partnership / Channel Expansion Team.

Train onboarding associates on how the Store Vendor App works so they can confidently explain and demonstrate it to new vendors under the B2Ch model.

The goal is for associates to understand the full in-store flow from device selection to payout, and to use consistent language when coaching vendors.

## Quick checklist before a vendor demo

Use this checklist to prepare before you start a live demo with a store vendor.

- Confirm you have:
  - A test or dummy customer profile and sample device details ready.
  - Stable internet on the demo device.
  - Access to the Store Vendor App with a working login.
- Decide which scenario you will demo:
  - Customer accepts the quote and chooses instant bank transfer.
  - Customer accepts the quote and chooses store voucher with bonus.
  - Customer rejects the quote after viewing it.
- Align on key messages to repeat:
  - Entire flow happens inside the app in under 5 minutes.
  - Every transaction is OTP + ID verified for safety.
  - No cancellation needed if customer does not proceed.
  - Voucher option gives extra value and supports the store.
- Open on the app home screen:
  - Keep the device ready on the home screen showing the four categories: Phone, Laptop, Tablet, Mac / iMac.

## Internal training script: end-to-end app flow

Use the following steps as your internal script. Each step explains what the vendor does in the app and what you should say or emphasize while training.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Explain the app purpose" icon="info">

  **What the vendor does in the app**

  - Open the Store Vendor App on the in-store device and stay on the home screen.

  **What you should say/emphasize**

  - The Store Vendor App is designed to make the entire in-store resale process simple, transparent, and instant.
  - It allows the store vendor or associate to:
    - Evaluate a customer’s device in real time.
    - Get an instant quote from Cashkr’s system.
    - Verify the customer with mobile OTP and ID proof.
    - Complete the transaction instantly via Wallet or issue a store voucher with bonus value.
  - Highlight that everything happens in one app and in front of the customer.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Start a new session with correct device type" icon="play-circle">

  **What the vendor does in the app**

  - On the home screen, choose the correct device category:
    - Phone
    - Laptop
    - Tablet
    - Mac / iMac

  **What you should say/emphasize**

  - Tell vendors to always start by selecting the correct device type the customer wants to sell.
  - Explain that choosing the right category ensures the right questions and pricing logic load for that device.
  - Reinforce that one session equals one device for one customer.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select brand, model, and variant" icon="monitor">

  **What the vendor does in the app**

  - After selecting the device type, choose:
    - Brand (for example, Apple, Samsung, Dell).
    - Model and variant (for example, iPhone 13 Pro 128GB, HP Pavilion i5).
  - Confirm the exact device variant before moving ahead.

  **What you should say/emphasize**

  - Stress that accurate brand and model selection is the base of correct pricing.
  - Encourage vendors to double-check the storage, RAM, and exact model name with the customer before proceeding.
  - Explain that once the model is selected, the app automatically loads a condition questionnaire tailored to that device.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Walk through the condition questionnaire" icon="settings">

  **What the vendor does in the app**

  - Answer the on-screen questions about the device condition, such as:
    - Screen condition (cracked or working fine).
    - Body condition (dents, scratches, or damages).
    - Power and performance (device powering on normally or not).
    - Accessories availability (charger, box, etc.).
  - Fill these answers live with the customer present.

  **What you should say/emphasize**

  - Explain that these condition inputs directly affect the final valuation.
  - Emphasize that honest, accurate answers protect both vendor and customer:
    - Honest answers = accurate price.
    - Misreporting can cause disputes later.
  - Coach vendors to show each question to the customer and confirm the answer before selecting it.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify the customer with OTP" icon="shield">

  **What the vendor does in the app**

  - After completing the condition questions, enter the customer’s mobile number.
  - Ask the customer to read out the OTP they receive.
  - Enter the OTP to verify the customer.

  **What you should say/emphasize**

  - Clarify that OTP verification links the quote to a real, verified customer.
  - Highlight that this step:
    - Prevents misuse or fake leads.
    - Avoids duplicate or repeated quote requests from the same number.
  - Remind vendors not to skip OTP or use their own number; it must be the customer’s number.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Show and explain the instant quote" icon="bar-chart">

  **What the vendor does in the app**

  - After OTP verification, view the instant quote shown on the screen.
  - Turn the screen to the customer so they can see the offer clearly.

  **What you should say/emphasize**

  - Explain that this is the final instant quote generated by Cashkr based on:
    - Current market price.
    - Device model and variant.
    - Condition and accessories inputs.
  - Reinforce that the pricing is transparent and calculated by the system, not manually by the vendor.
  - Tell vendors to give the customer a moment to think and decide whether they want to proceed.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Handle if the customer rejects the offer" icon="x-circle">

  **What the vendor does in the app**

  - If the customer does not want to proceed:
    - Tap the back button or go back to the home screen.
    - Start a new session only if there is another device or another customer.
  - Do not create any cancellation or fail entry.

  **What you should say/emphasize**

  - Make it clear that rejected quotes are not treated as failed orders.
  - The session ends automatically once the vendor backs out; nothing stays pending.
  - Train vendors that if the customer disagrees with the price, they should:
    - Thank the customer.
    - Close the session.
    - Move on to the next customer or device without any extra steps.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Proceed if the customer agrees to sell" icon="check-circle">

  **What the vendor does in the app**

  - If the customer accepts the offer, move to the final verification screen.
  - Upload a clear photo of the customer’s ID proof (for example, Aadhaar, PAN).
  - Check that the ID photo and the person in front of them match.

  **What you should say/emphasize**

  - Explain that this ID upload is the KYC step and is mandatory for payout.
  - Emphasize that the ID must belong to the same person whose mobile number was verified via OTP.
  - Highlight that this protects against fraud and ensures payout authenticity for both Cashkr and the store.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose payment or voucher option" icon="credit-card">

  **What the vendor does in the app**

  - After uploading the ID, choose how the customer wants to receive value:
    - **Instant Bank Transfer (default)**:
      - Processed instantly through the vendor’s Wallet.
      - The customer receives money directly in their bank account.
    - **Store Voucher option**:
      - Generate a voucher for the same store.
      - Cashkr adds an extra bonus amount (for example, ₹500 extra) to the voucher value.

  **What you should say/emphasize**

  - Present both options clearly and let the customer choose.
  - For instant bank transfer, emphasize speed and that money moves instantly through the system-backed Wallet.
  - For store voucher, emphasize:
    - Customer gets extra value compared to direct cash.
    - Voucher can be used for purchases in the same store.
    - This supports the store and increases in-store engagement.
  - Coach vendors never to make a selection without confirming with the customer.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the order and confirm" icon="check">

  **What the vendor does in the app**

  - After selecting payment or voucher:
    - Press the `Complete Order` button.
  - The app then:
    - Triggers payment from the vendor’s Wallet (for bank transfer) or issues the store voucher.
    - Marks the order as completed.
    - Generates a digital receipt.
    - Logs the transaction in the `Completed Orders` section.

  **What you should say/emphasize**

  - Reinforce that the entire process from device selection to payout or voucher is completed in under 5 minutes.
  - Mention that both vendor and customer have a digital record of the transaction.
  - Encourage vendors to show the digital receipt on screen and, if available, send or share it with the customer as per process.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Explain what happens after completion" icon="database">

  **What the vendor does in the app and store**

  - After completion:
    - The device appears under the vendor’s `Completed Orders`.
    - At the end of the day, the vendor collects all purchased devices from the store counter.
  - Cashkr’s system reconciles:
    - Orders.
    - Payments.
    - ID records.

  **What you should say/emphasize**

  - Emphasize that every completed device is fully logged and traceable.
  - Highlight that this creates full transparency and audit control for:
    - Cashkr.
    - The store partner.
    - The vendor.
  - Remind vendors to follow the store’s end-of-day process for collecting and storing purchased devices.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Handle errors, glitches, or repeats" icon="alert-triangle">

  **What the vendor does in the app**

  - If the app freezes, a wrong input is made, or something looks incorrect:
    - Return to the home screen.
    - Start a new session with the correct details.
  - Do not worry about incomplete or half-done entries; they do not stay open.

  **What you should say/emphasize**

  - Reassure vendors that the app is built for fast, real-time sessions.
  - Clarify that:
    - Incomplete sessions do not block future orders.
    - They can always restart a fresh session without calling support.
  - Train them to immediately restart rather than trying to fix a half-broken flow during a customer interaction.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Internal notes for associates

Use this table as a quick reference when coaching vendors on the flow and key messages.

| Step | Process stage        | System function        | What to emphasize                         |
| ---- | -------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Device selection     | Starts session         | Choose correct device type first          |
| 2    | Condition Q&A        | Auto valuation         | Honest answers = accurate price           |
| 3    | OTP verification     | Customer validation    | Prevents fraud and ensures real customer  |
| 4    | Quote display        | Price shown            | Transparent, instant value                |
| 5    | ID upload            | KYC step               | Mandatory for payout                      |
| 6    | Payment / voucher    | Transaction execution  | Both options are processed within the app |
| 7    | Order complete       | Auto-closes and logs   | Generates digital receipt instantly       |

## Key talking points to repeat

<Callout kind="tip">

Use these lines repeatedly during training so vendors remember the core value of the Store Vendor App.

- The entire process, from quote to payout, happens inside the app. No manual confirmation is needed.
- Every transaction is OTP plus ID verified for safety and compliance.
- If the customer does not agree with the quote, just start a new session; there is nothing to cancel.
- The store voucher option gives customers extra value and helps the store drive more sales.
- At the end of the day, all confirmed devices are collected, and every transaction is already logged and reconciled in the system.

</Callout>
