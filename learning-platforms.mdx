## Why learning platforms matter at Cashkr

Cashkr grows quickly, so everyone needs to keep learning and upskilling across SEO, marketing, tech, operations, analytics, and leadership.

<Callout kind="info">
  Learning platforms give you structured, on-demand training so freshers ramp up faster and experienced team members stay sharp and aligned with current best practices.
</Callout>

Continuous learning keeps our work quality high, supports promotions and role changes, and helps teams stay ahead of industry trends.

## Platforms we use

<Card title="Udemy" icon="book-open">

  Online learning platform with thousands of short, practical courses across topics like SEO, performance marketing, content, and development.

  **Example:** A fresher in the SEO team takes an `SEO for Beginners` course to understand keyword research, on-page SEO, and basic analytics before working on live pages.

</Card>

<Card title="Coursera" icon="book-open">

  Platform for in-depth, structured courses and professional certificates from universities and industry partners, especially useful for managers and specialists.

  **Example:** A marketing manager completes a `Digital Marketing Analytics` certificate to improve campaign measurement, reporting, and experiment design.

</Card>

## How we use these daily

- **Freshers** → Learn the fundamentals of their role (SEO, ads, content writing, customer support, basic coding).
- **Mid-level employees** → Go deeper into advanced topics and earn relevant certifications.
- **Managers** → Focus on leadership, data analytics, decision-making, and strategy courses.
- **Company-wide** → Everyone is encouraged to spend a few hours each month on structured skill-building.

## Guidelines

Use these simple guidelines to get the most value from Udemy and Coursera:

- **Choose relevant courses** that match your current role, upcoming projects, or skills discussed with your manager.
- **Plan time for learning**, aiming for a few focused hours each month instead of long, irregular stretches.
- **Apply learnings quickly** by using course concepts in ongoing tasks, experiments, or processes you own.
- **Take short notes** while learning so you remember key points and can revisit them before using a concept.
- **Share takeaways with your team** in standups, internal docs, or Slack so others can benefit from what you learned.

## Teams using these platforms

All teams at Cashkr use these platforms, including SEO, Marketing, Tech, and Operations, for both onboarding and ongoing upskilling.
