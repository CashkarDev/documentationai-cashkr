**Team:** B2C Sales  
**Scenario:** Customer wants to sell their device but Cashkr pickup is not yet available in their area.  
**Tone:** Helpful, empathetic, and future-oriented — focus on expansion and future benefit.

---

## Quick checklist

Use this as a live reminder during calls.

- Acknowledge concern and thank the customer for checking.
- Explain that pickup is not yet live in their area and expansion is ongoing.
- Capture **area and pincode** to add to the expansion/notification list.
- Remind them that their **device price quote stays saved**.
- Mention future **free pickup and instant payment** when service becomes active.
- Offer to notify them personally (WhatsApp/SMS/call) when pickup starts.

---

## What not to say

Avoid negative or confusing phrasing that reduces trust.

- Do not say: **"We do not provide service in your area"** (sounds permanent).  
  Say: **"Pickup is not live in your area yet; we are expanding city by city."**
- Do not give **specific dates or guaranteed timelines** (for example, "next week it will start").  
  Say: **"We regularly add new pincodes and will notify you as soon as yours is active."**
- Do not say: **"You cannot sell your device"**.  
  Say: **"You can still check and save your device price; you can confirm once pickup starts."**
- Do not blame the system or use harsh words like **"system error"** or **"cannot do anything"**.  
  Stay future-focused and solution-oriented.

## Call scripts

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="English – Training reference" icon="book-open">

### Acknowledge

**Customer:** “Pickup is not showing available in my area.”

**Agent:**  
“I understand, and thank you for checking. Let me quickly confirm this for your area.”

### Explain

“Currently, our pickup service is not live in your area yet. We are expanding city by city, and your location is already in our upcoming list.”

### Capture details

“I will note down your area and pincode right now so we can notify you as soon as pickup becomes active there. We usually add new locations over time as we expand.”

> Capture: **Customer name, area, pincode, and preferred contact channel (WhatsApp/SMS/call)** in your tool.

### Offer alternatives

“In the meantime, you can still check your device price anytime. Your quote stays saved, and once pickup opens in your area, you will be able to confirm your order on the same price flow, subject to the usual checks.”

### Close

“Thank you for showing interest in Cashkr. We will inform you the moment your area is activated, so you can get free pickup and instant payment.”

## Usage notes

<Callout kind="tip">

- Always **note the customer’s pincode and area** for the expansion list or CRM.
- Keep tone positive and future-focused — say **"coming soon"**, not **"not available"**.
- Clearly mention that **pickup is not live yet** and that expansion is ongoing.
- Offer to notify personally on **WhatsApp/SMS/call** when pickup becomes active.
- Remind the customer that **their quote can be checked/saved** and used once pickup is available.

</Callout>
