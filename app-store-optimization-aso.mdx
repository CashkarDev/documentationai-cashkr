## Why this matters

<Callout kind="info">

App Store Optimization (ASO) makes the Cashkr app easier to discover in the App Store and Play Store, which directly impacts organic installs and customer acquisition costs. When our listing uses the right keywords, visuals, and reviews, more people searching for terms like `sell old phone app` find and trust Cashkr without needing paid ads.

</Callout>

## Tool we use

<Card title="ASOLift" icon="bar-chart">

ASOLift is an AI-based ASO tool we use to improve our app keywords, titles, descriptions, and visuals across the App Store and Play Store.

At Cashkr, the marketing and growth teams use ASOLift to research search behavior in core cities, test better titles and descriptions, and keep our screenshots aligned with current campaigns and offers.

**Concrete example:** If ASOLift shows that people in Hyderabad often search for `sell old phone app`, we add and refine those keywords in our app title and description so Cashkr ranks higher for that query and similar phrases.

</Card>

## How we use this

- **Optimize app title and description** with high-intent, trending keywords that match how people actually search.
- **Improve visuals** (icons, screenshots, preview images) to increase store listing click-through and install conversion.
- **Monitor rankings and reviews** regularly, then adjust keywords, copy, and visuals based on performance and user feedback.

## Typical ASO iteration workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Research keywords and competitors" icon="search">

  Use ASOLift to discover high-intent keywords by city and category, and review how competitor apps rank and position themselves for those terms.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Update store listing content" icon="edit">

  Refine the app title, subtitle, and description to include priority keywords and clearer value propositions, while keeping copy readable and on-brand.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Ship updated assets" icon="upload">

  Coordinate with design to refresh icons and screenshots, then publish updated text and visuals to both the App Store and Play Store.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Monitor rankings and conversion" icon="bar-chart">

  Track keyword rankings, listing views, and install conversion in ASOLift and store dashboards to see which changes improved performance.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Respond to reviews and iterate" icon="refresh-ccw">

  Reply to new reviews, prioritize fixes or feature requests that affect ratings, and feed learnings back into the next round of keyword and creative updates.

  </Step>
</Steps>
