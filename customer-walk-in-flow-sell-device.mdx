## Overview

Store vendors use this flow in the Store app to help walk-in customers sell their old devices. The flow covers device assessment, customer registration or login, price estimation, and final order outcome as either completed or failed.

## Flow

<Steps>

  <Step title="Start with the walk-in customer" icon="users">

  - Customer visits the store with their old device.
  - Vendor logs into the Store app panel.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select and assess the device" icon="monitor">

  - Select **Device Type**, **Brand**, and **Model** (models are searchable).
  - Fill in **device variant**, **age**, **condition** (battery, screen, body, technical), and **included accessories**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Register or log in the customer" icon="phone">

  - Ask the customer to **register or log in** using their phone number.
  - If the customer is new, collect **full name** and **email address**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Review estimated price and device report" icon="bar-chart">

  - System calculates an **estimated price** using admin-defined pricing logic.
  - Customer reviews the generated **device report**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose order outcome" icon="arrow-right">

  - Vendor proceeds with either:
    - **Checkout Flow** to complete the sale, or
    - **Failure Flow** to fail the order.

  </Step>

</Steps>

## Order outcomes (Checkout vs Failure)

Use these outcomes after the customer has reviewed the device report and agreed on the next action.

<Tabs>

  <Tab title="Checkout Flow" icon="check-circle">

  - Click **Checkout** from the device report screen.
  - Enter the device **IMEI number**.
  - Enter the **final sale price** agreed with the customer.
  - Ask the customer to choose a **Payment Mode**:
    - **Cash**: amount paid directly to the customer.
    - **Buyback**: amount used as credit toward a new purchase.
  - Capture **ID proof**, **customer signature**, and **device bill upload**.
  - Confirm and submit to mark the order as **Completed**.

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Failure Flow" icon="x-circle">

  - Click **Fail** instead of Checkout.
  - Select a **failure reason** from the available options.
  - (Optional) Add **remarks** explaining why the order is being failed.
  - Ask the customer to upload **photos of all six sides of the device**.
  - Click **Submit Report** to mark the order as **Failed**.

  </Tab>

</Tabs>

## System logic

- Pull the device price from **Admin Panel → Products + Class + Pricing**.
- Tag each order to the **vendor ID** and **store details**.
- Store all **documents and photos** with the order record.
- Store both **completed** and **failed** orders in the admin panel.

## Admin panel connections

This flow depends on configuration and records in the Admin Panel:

- **Products**: device catalog used for device type, brand, and model selection.
- **Pricing & Class**: rules used for quote and estimated price calculation.
- **Store Orders**: records created for each completed or failed order.
- **Store Vendors**: vendor profiles and credit logic tied to each order.
