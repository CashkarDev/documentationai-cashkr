## What the Careers page shows

The Careers page presents Cashkr's employer story and lists every role currently open for applications. Roles are not hand-coded into the page — they are published from the Admin Panel and appear here automatically.

The page has three parts:

- **Hero section** – static employer branding, an introduction to working at Cashkr, and a **View Open Positions** link that scrolls to the listing.
- **Current openings** – one entry per open role, showing the job title and location with an **Apply Now** link.
- **Supporting sections** – a recruitment contact card, and the Perks & Benefits section grouped into Health & Wellness, Wealth & Equity, Flexibility & Freedom, Learning & Mastery, and Family & Milestones.

<Callout kind="info">

- Only roles with status **Open** appear in Current openings. Closed roles are removed from the list.
- When no roles are open, the listing shows an empty state inviting the visitor to check back or contact the recruitment team.
- Each role has its own URL at `/careers/<slug>`, where the slug is set when the role is created in the Admin Panel.

</Callout>

## Job detail page

Selecting a role opens its own page at `/careers/<slug>`. The page header shows the job title over a hero image, with the workplace type, location and department beneath it. Content is then split across two tabs — **Job details** and **Application**.

The Job details tab renders, in order:

- **About Cashkr** – standard company boilerplate, identical on every role page.
- **About the role** – the description written in the Admin Panel.
- **Job Description** – the responsibilities list.
- **Job requirements** – the requirements list.
- **Skills** – the skills list, shown as a single comma-separated line.
- **Benefits and perks** – the benefits list, under a short standard introduction.
- **Apply** and **Share job** actions.

## Applying for a role

The application form lives on the Application tab, also reachable directly at `/careers/<slug>/apply`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a role from the listing" icon="folder" title-type="p">

  - The candidate selects **Apply Now** against a role in Current openings, or opens the role page and switches to the **Application** tab.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter contact details" icon="user" title-type="p">

  - **Full name** – required.
  - **Email address** – required, and validated for format.
  - **Phone number** – required, with a country code selector offering India (+91), UAE (+971), USA (+1) and UK (+44).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload a CV or resume" icon="upload" title-type="p">

  - Required. Accepts PDF, DOC, DOCX, JPEG and PNG up to 50MB.
  - The file can be selected through the upload link or dragged onto the drop zone.
  - An unsupported file type is rejected with a message naming the accepted formats.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload a cover letter" icon="file-text" title-type="p">

  - Optional, with the same accepted formats and size limit as the CV.
  - Stored separately from the CV, so recruiters can download either one independently.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit the application" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">

  - The candidate selects **Submit Application**.
  - On success the form is replaced by an **Application received** confirmation naming the role.
  - The application appears immediately in the Admin Panel under **Marketing → Careers → Applications**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## System logic

**Role visibility.** The listing is driven by role status. Opening or closing a role in the Admin Panel adds it to or removes it from Current openings without any content change on the website.

**Closed roles.** A closed role is dropped from the listing but its job page stays reachable, so links already shared or indexed by search engines do not break. The page shows *This role is no longer accepting applications* in place of the apply action, and `/careers/<slug>/apply` redirects back to the job page.

**Deleted or unknown roles.** A slug that does not exist returns the standard Cashkr 404 page.

**Duplicate applications.** A candidate can apply to several different roles, but only once per role. A second application to the same role using the same email address is rejected.

**Listing expiry.** Each role carries an expiry date set in the Admin Panel. The date is published as part of the job posting so search engines know when the listing lapses.

## Connections

- **Admin Panel → Marketing → Careers** – the single source for every role and every application shown here.
- **Recruitment team** – the contact card links to the HR contact who handles direct enquiries.
- **Analytics** – application form starts and submissions are tracked as conversion events.

## Developer and tester notes

**For developers**

- Role content is stored as structured lists (responsibilities, requirements, skills, benefits) rather than free-form HTML, so the job page controls all formatting.
- The slug is fixed once a role is published and forms the permanent URL for that posting.
- The public site renders role data server-side; there is no client-side fetch of the careers API from the browser.

**For testers**

- Confirm a newly opened role appears in Current openings and a closed role disappears from it.
- Confirm a closed role's job page still loads and shows the no-longer-accepting message, and that its `/apply` URL redirects to the job page.
- Confirm an unknown slug returns the 404 page.
- Test the apply form with a missing CV, an unsupported file type and a malformed email address, and confirm each is blocked with a message.
- Submit an application with both a CV and a cover letter, then confirm both files download correctly from the Admin Panel.
- Confirm a repeat application to the same role with the same email address is refused.
- Check the listing, job page and apply form at mobile, tablet and desktop widths for layout and horizontal overflow.
