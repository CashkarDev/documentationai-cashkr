## Why this matters

<Callout kind="info">

Productivity and team tools keep Cashkr teams connected, organized, and efficient. They reduce back-and-forth, avoid scheduling conflicts, and make it easy to create training and demo materials without rework.

</Callout>

## Tools we use

<Card title="Slack" icon="message-square">

Slack is our primary communication platform where conversations are organized into channels instead of long email threads. We use it across all teams for daily updates, quick questions, and cross-functional coordination. For example, the `#marketing` channel is used for campaign updates, while `#tech-support` is used to report app issues in real time.

</Card>

<Card title="Tidycal" icon="calendar">

Tidycal is our meeting scheduling tool that generates shareable booking links and prevents double-booking. HR relies on it to streamline interviews, and other teams use it for external and cross-team meetings when scheduling needs to be clear and structured. For example, instead of going back and forth on "What time works for you?", HR shares a Tidycal link with candidates to let them pick a slot.

</Card>

<Card title="Vento" icon="video">

Vento is a screen recording tool used to create explainers, product demos, and training materials. It lets you pause, rewind, and re-record mistakes without starting over, which is useful when building long SOP videos. For example, an Ops team member can record a vendor training SOP and, if they make a mistake halfway, rewind to that point and continue recording the same video.

</Card>

## How we use these daily

- **All teams** → Use Slack for daily communication, quick questions, and status updates.
- **HR team** → Uses Tidycal to schedule interviews and internal HR meetings without time clashes.
- **Marketing, ops, training** → Use Vento for explainers, product demos, training content, and SOP walkthroughs.
- **Tech/support** → Uses Slack channels like tech-support to surface and resolve app issues quickly.

## Typical productivity workflow

<Steps>

  <Step title="Communicate and align in Slack" icon="message-square">

  Start with Slack to discuss work, clarify requirements, and share quick updates across teams. Use relevant channels so conversations stay easy to find and everyone who needs context can see it.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Schedule meetings with Tidycal when needed" icon="calendar">

  When a discussion needs a live call (for example, interviews or cross-team planning), create or share a Tidycal link so participants can pick a time that works, avoiding manual back-and-forth and double-booking.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Record SOPs and demos with Vento" icon="video">

  For repeatable processes or training, record the workflow using Vento instead of explaining it repeatedly in calls. Use the pause and rewind features to fix mistakes without restarting the entire recording.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Share links and keep async updates" icon="link">

  After recording or scheduling, share the meeting or Vento recording links back in relevant Slack channels. This keeps updates asynchronous and ensures that anyone who missed the live discussion can catch up later.

  </Step>

</Steps>
