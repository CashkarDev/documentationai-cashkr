## Overview

**Sidebar path:** Dashboards → OPERATING → **Growth** (`/dashboard/growth`)

Growth is the main acquisition and conversion dashboard. A tab bar at the top switches between six views:

| Tab | What it covers | Documented in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Headline KPIs, health index, conversion, spend, ops health, trends | This page |
| **Scoreboard** | Volume, conversion and operational performance | This page |
| **Funnel** | End-to-end user journey and drop-offs | Funnel page |
| **Sales** | Lead pipeline, revenue and failure analysis | Sales Pipelines page |
| **Geography** | City readiness, vendor demand, coverage | Expansion page |
| **Campaigns** | Leads, orders and completion per UTM campaign | This page |

Most tabs share a **date range** picker (default: last 30 days), a **Refresh** button and an **AI Analyst Insight** panel with **Analyze Data**, which writes an AI summary of the current numbers.

## Overview tab

### KPI cards

Total Leads, Orders, Completed (each with sparkline and % change), L→O Rate, Completion, Fail Rate, Ad Spend, eCPA (attributed).

### Health Index

A gauge from *Critical* to *Optimal* summarising Completion %, Fail %, Cancel % and TAT (days).

### Summary panels

| Panel | Metrics |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversion Rates** | Lead → Order, Completion Rate, Requote Rate, Cancel Rate |
| **Marketing Spend** | Total Ad Spend, CAC (platform — *awaiting sync* until Google/Meta conversions arrive), CAC (our attribution), Cost Per Lead |
| **Operational Health** | Fail Rate, Cancel Rate, Requote Rate, Pickup TAT |
| **Conversion Funnel** | Total Leads, Orders, Completed, Failed, Cancelled |

Each panel has a **View Details** link to a standalone report page: Conversion Rates and Conversion Funnel → `/dashboard/reports/funnel`, Marketing Spend → `/dashboard/reports/campaign-performance`, Operational Health → `/dashboard/reports/vendor-sla`.

<Callout kind="alert">
On the stage server these report pages currently return *This page could not be found*. Use the **Funnel**, **Campaigns** and **Sales** tabs, or **Operations / SLA**, for the same detail.
</Callout>

### Charts and lists

- **Orders vs Completed** — daily trend.
- **Daily Outcomes Mix** — cancelled, completed and failed for the last 14 days.
- **Top Drivers** — switch between **Campaigns**, **Vendors** and **Cities**; shows *Top by completed* and *Worst by CPA*.
- **Recent Completions** — latest pickups, with **View All Orders**.

## Scoreboard tab

Title: **Growth Scoreboard** — *Volume, conversion & operational performance*.

| Block | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Growth Health** | Weak / Moderate / Strong band from Completion, Fail Rate, Cancel Rate and On-Time. |
| **Volume** | Total Leads, Orders, Completed. |
| **Rates** | Lead → Order %, Completion Rate, Fail Rate, Cancel Rate, Requote Rate, Avg Pickup TAT, On-Time Pickup — each with a label such as *Best*, *Low*, *Normal*, *Fast*, *Good*. |
| **Volume Trends** | Daily leads, orders, completed. |
| **Daily Outcomes Mix** | Completed vs failed, last 14 days. |
| **Conversion Funnel** | Leads → Orders Received → Completed / Failed with drop-off % and overall conversion. |
| **Device Breakdown** | Orders by device (Mobile, Tablet, Laptop, Headphone, Mac, Watch) split into completed, failed, cancelled. |
| **City Performance** | Top 15 cities: Leads, Orders, Completed, Completion %, Fail %, Cancel %. |
| **Exceptions** | Cities that need attention, for example high leads with low completion. |

## Campaigns tab

| Block | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **KPI cards** | Total Leads, Orders, Lead → Order, Completed (with completion %). |
| **Top Campaigns by Completion** | Best campaigns with source and completed/orders. |
| **Daily Leads Trend** | Leads per day. |
| **All Campaigns** | Campaign, Source / Medium, Leads, Orders, Conv %, Completed, Comp %. |

<Callout kind="info">
Effective CPA on the Campaigns tab needs ad-spend data. Until Google Ads spend is connected, the tab shows volume and conversion only.
</Callout>

## Actions

All Growth tabs are read-only. Use the date range, tabs and **View Details** links to navigate.
