## Beliefs in detail

### 1. Customers first, always

Every decision we take, from product design to support scripts, must serve the customer better, faster, and more transparently.

### 2. Vendor = partner

Vendors are not just executors. They are the delivery arm of Cashkr trust. Treat them with clarity and respect, and expect the same standard in return.

### 3. Speed over perfection

Launch fast, fix fast, learn faster. A working version in the wild is always better than a perfect plan stuck on paper.

### 4. Default to action

When you see something broken or unclear, fix it, flag it, or improve it. We reward people who move things forward, not those who stay passive.

### 5. Data + gut = decisions

Use data wherever possible, and combine it with strong instincts built on ground reality. Neither data alone nor gut alone is enough for important decisions.

### 6. Disagree, then commit

Challenge ideas, not people. Debate hard while decisions are open. Once a call is made, back it fully, even when it was not your idea.

### 7. No ego in execution

Whether you are the CEO or a new intern, every task matters. Titles do not build trust; consistent execution and willingness to do the work do.

### 8. Document everything

If it is not written, it does not exist. Capture ideas, decisions, and fixes in writing so the team can find, use, and improve them.

### 9. Respect time

Start on time, end on time, and avoid unnecessary meetings. Do not overbook or drag; our speed depends on how we guard each other's time.

### 10. Own outcomes

Do not outsource responsibility. When you commit to something, you own the result end to end, not just your part of the process.
