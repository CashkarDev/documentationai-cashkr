## Overview

Use **Out for Pickup** to move a qualified lead into pickup and inspection, or **Fail** to close a lead that cannot continue. This choice controls whether the order proceeds to on-site inspection or is permanently closed as a failed lead.

<Callout kind="info">

- **Out for Pickup**: Proceed with pickup and inspection. Customer receives an out-for-pickup email and the vendor will complete OTP-based verification on-site.
- **Fail**: Stop the order. The lead flow ends and the system records the failure reason and timestamp.

</Callout>

## Where this step happens

This decision happens **after you record the call outcome** on the Call Disposition screen.

- Complete [Call Disposition](/call-disposition) for an **In Progress** order.
- Return to the order under [InProgress Orders](/inprogress-orders).
- On the order detail, choose **Out for Pickup** or **Fail** based on the outcome of your call.

## Out for Pickup flow

Use Out for Pickup when the customer agrees to sell and you will proceed with pickup and inspection.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Mark order as Out for Pickup" icon="arrow-right">

  - On the In Progress order detail, tap **Out for Pickup**.
  - Confirm that you are proceeding with pickup.

  After this, the system updates the order to out-for-pickup state.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Customer receives Out for Pickup email" icon="mail">

  - The customer receives an **Out for Pickup** email with pickup details.
  - The website and Customer App reflect the updated status so the customer can track progress.

  Use this as confirmation that the order has moved from call stage to pickup stage.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Start inspection and enter lead OTP" icon="terminal">

  - On the vendor app, the order now shows an **Inspection Started** option.
  - Visit the customer, start inspection, and tap **Inspection Started**.
  - Ask the customer for the **lead OTP** they received.
  - Enter the lead OTP into the app to verify the customer and the lead.

  After successful OTP verification, the app unlocks final order actions.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete order or requote after OTP verification" icon="check-circle">

  - Once the OTP is verified, the app shows **Complete Order** and **Requote** buttons.
  - Tap **Complete Order** when you finish inspection and agree on the initial price.
  - Tap **Requote** if the final price must change based on actual device condition. For failing after a changed quote, follow the dedicated [Fail After Requote](/fail-after-requote) flow.

  These actions move the order from pickup/inspection into final resolution.

  </Step>

</Steps>

## Fail flow

Use Fail when the order **cannot be continued** after speaking with the customer.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Choose Fail from the order" icon="x-circle">

  - Open the In Progress order detail.
  - Tap **Fail** when the lead will not proceed (for example, customer no longer wants to sell).

  This opens the failure reason screen.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select a failure reason" icon="settings">

  - Pick the most accurate **failure reason** from the list, such as:
    - **Not interested in selling**
    - **Was just checking**
  - Add any additional notes if your build supports it.

  Make sure the selected reason clearly reflects why the lead is not continuing.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit and close the lead" icon="check">

  - Tap **Submit** to confirm the failure.
  - The system ends the lead flow and marks the order as **Failed**.

  The order moves out of your active In Progress list and appears in Admin as a failed order.

  </Step>

</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">

Failing an order is **final for this lead flow**. To re-open or re-create a lead after failure, follow your internal lead creation or reassignment process.

</Callout>

## Inputs captured

The system records specific inputs at this step for tracking and audit.

- **Fail action**
  - **Failure reason** selected from the predefined list (for example, Not interested in selling, Was just checking).
  - **Failure timestamp** when you submit the fail action.
- **Out for Pickup and inspection**
  - **Out for Pickup timestamp** when you mark the order as Out for Pickup.
  - **Inspection Started timestamp** when you start inspection.
  - **Lead OTP value** entered by the vendor.
  - **OTP verification outcome** (success or failure) including time of verification.

These inputs feed into reporting, Admin views, and customer-visible status.

## System logic and state updates

The app drives different system behavior depending on the action you choose.

- **Fail**
  - Ends the **lead flow** for that order.
  - Saves the **failure reason** and **timestamp**.
  - Moves the order into **Failed Orders** in the Admin Panel.
  - Updates the order status on the **Customer App** and **website** so the customer sees that the order is no longer active.

- **Out for Pickup**
  - Updates the order status to **Out for Pickup**.
  - Triggers the **Out for Pickup email** to the customer.
  - Makes **Inspection Started** available in the vendor app.
  - After OTP verification, unlocks **Complete Order** and **Requote** actions.
  - Syncs status updates to the **Customer App** and **website** for tracking.

## Connections

Out for Pickup and Fail are connected to multiple systems and views.

- **Admin Panel**
  - Failed orders appear under **Failed Orders** with reason and timestamp.
  - Out-for-pickup and inspection events are available for support and analytics.

- **Customer App and Website**
  - Order status updates in real time for:
    - Out for Pickup
    - Inspection in progress (if surfaced)
    - Failed
  - Customers can track their order from the **Order Confirmation & Tracking** and **Track Orders** views.

- **Email notifications**
  - Out for Pickup triggers a **vendor out-for-pickup email** to the customer.
  - OTP and other messages follow your existing notification configuration.

## Common states and errors

Use this section to resolve typical issues around this step.

### Wrong or expired OTP

- **Symptom**: OTP entry fails or shows an invalid/expired message.
- **What to check**
  - Confirm the customer is reading the **latest OTP** they received.
  - Verify that you are entering the OTP for the **correct lead**.
  - Retry once, then escalate if OTP continues to fail.
- **Impact**
  - Inspection cannot proceed to **Complete Order** or **Requote** until OTP verification succeeds.

### Status not syncing to Customer App or website

- **Symptom**: Vendor app shows Out for Pickup or Failed, but the customer still sees an older status.
- **What to check**
  - Confirm you submitted the **Fail** or **Out for Pickup** action (no pending confirmation modals).
  - Wait a short period for sync and then refresh the Customer App or website view.
- **Next step**
  - If the status still does not update, capture:
    - Order ID
    - Current vendor app status
    - Screenshot of the customer view
  - Share with support or engineering for API sync investigation.

### Out-for-pickup email not received

- **Symptom**: Customer reports they did not receive the Out for Pickup email.
- **What to check**
  - Verify the order is marked **Out for Pickup** in the vendor app.
  - Confirm the customer email in the order details is correct.
  - Ask customer to check spam/junk folders.
- **Next step**
  - If still missing, raise a ticket with the order ID for email service and event-log review.

### Missing failure reason

- **Symptom**: System allows Fail without a clear reason, or Admin cannot see the reason.
- **What to check**
  - Ensure the **failure reason dropdown** is mandatory in the latest build.
  - Confirm that the selected reason appears in **Admin → Failed Orders**.
- **Next step**
  - For any failed orders missing a reason, log examples for data-migration or validation fixes.

## Developer and tester notes

<Callout kind="tip">

- **State transitions**
  - Verify the transition from **In Progress → Out for Pickup → Inspection Started → Complete/Requote** and **In Progress → Failed**.
  - Confirm that failing an order **removes it from active In Progress lists** and surfaces it in Admin **Failed Orders** with reason and timestamp.

- **Audit fields**
  - Ensure API payloads capture:
    - Failure reason and failure timestamp.
    - Out-for-pickup timestamp.
    - Inspection started timestamp.
    - Lead OTP value (if stored) and verification result.
  - Validate that these fields are available for reporting.

- **Cross-system sync**
  - Test that status and timestamps match across:
    - Vendor App
    - Customer App
    - Website
    - Admin Panel
  - Inspect event logs when OTP fails to confirm proper error codes and messages.

- **Requote integration**
  - After OTP verification, confirm that **Requote** routes into the [Fail After Requote](/fail-after-requote) logic when the requoted offer is rejected.
  - Make sure requote-related failures do not overwrite the original **Fail** reasons for non-requote cases.

</Callout>
