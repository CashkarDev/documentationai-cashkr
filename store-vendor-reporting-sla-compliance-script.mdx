## Overview

Use this script to train new B2Ch store vendors on daily reporting hygiene, SLA commitments, and transparent performance tracking inside the Cashkr Vendor App.

<Callout kind="info">

Store vendors must keep their Wallet funded before store opening, close every order on the same day, collect all paid devices before store closing, and submit the End-of-Day Summary report by 7:30 PM.

</Callout>

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="1. Purpose of reporting and SLA tracking" default-open="true">

“In the B2Ch model, vendors operate directly inside partner stores — so every transaction is time-stamped and monitored for reliability.

Reporting and SLA adherence are what separate Cashkr’s service network from unorganized buyers.

Associates must ensure vendors know their daily routine.”

<Steps>

<Step title="Start of day" icon="play-circle">

- Log in to the Vendor App before store opening.
- Check Wallet balance and ensure it is sufficient for the day.

</Step>

<Step title="During the day" icon="settings">

- Serve customers promptly.
- Complete inspection and payment steps for each customer without delay.
- Close every order in the app before moving to the next customer.

</Step>

<Step title="End of day" icon="check-circle">

- Reconcile orders and payments.
- Collect all paid devices from the store.
- Submit the End-of-Day Summary before 7:30 PM.
- Log out only after confirming no pending payments or open orders.

</Step>

</Steps>

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="2. Daily reporting routine">

“Each vendor must complete a quick 3-step report every evening before logging out.”

Daily report includes:

- Total orders handled today
- Completed and collected devices count
- Pending voucher redemptions
- Wallet balance status

The vendor updates this through the `End-of-Day Summary` screen in the Vendor App, which the Cashkr Ops Team reviews automatically.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open End-of-Day Summary" icon="monitor">

- Navigate to the `Reports` or `End-of-Day Summary` screen in the Vendor App.
- Confirm that all orders from the day are visible.

</Step>

<Step title="Fill in required details" icon="edit">

- Enter the total orders handled.
- Confirm the number of devices completed and collected.
- Mention any pending voucher redemptions and verify Wallet balance.

</Step>

<Step title="Submit and log out" icon="check-circle">

- Submit the End-of-Day Summary before 7:30 PM.
- Log out only after verifying there are no pending payments or open orders.

</Step>

</Steps>

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="3. SLA timelines defined">

“SLA (Service Level Agreement) for Store Vendors is stricter than for home pickups:

- Order Processing SLA: Every customer interaction (inspection → payment) must finish within 20 minutes of starting a session.
- End-of-Day SLA: All paid devices must be collected from stores before closing hours.
- Payment SLA: No payment should remain pending after order completion in the app.
- Report SLA: End-of-Day Summary must be submitted by 7:30 PM.

Non-compliance to any SLA is auto-flagged in the vendor’s performance dashboard.”

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="4. Performance Dashboard in the app">

“Every vendor has a built-in Performance Dashboard under the `Reports` tab.

It shows daily and weekly metrics such as:

- Total Orders Completed
- Average Handling Time
- SLA Score (percentage)
- Wallet Health Status
- Customer Experience Rating (stars)

Associates must teach vendors to monitor these numbers themselves. High SLA scores improve future store allocation priority.”

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="5. How SLA scores are calculated">

“SLA equals Timeliness plus Completeness plus Accuracy.”

The SLA score components are:

| Component                                  | Weight | Example                               |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Order closure on same day                  | 40%    | All orders updated by store close     |
| Payment on time                            | 30%    | Instant transfer or voucher issuance  |
| End-of-Day reporting                       | 20%    | Report submitted by 7:30 PM           |
| Professional conduct and customer feedback | 10%    | No complaints or violations recorded  |

“Overall SLA below 80% for two consecutive weeks triggers a performance review.”

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="6. Escalation and penalty process">

<Callout kind="alert">

“If a vendor misses multiple SLAs or fails to report devices:

1. First instance: Verbal warning and reminder through support ticket.
2. Second instance: Wallet hold and temporary pause on new orders.
3. Third instance: Vendor placed on `Hold` status until Ops Manager review.
4. Persistent violations: De-allocation from store clusters.”

</Callout>

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="7. End-of-day checklist for associates to train">

“Ensure vendors follow this daily routine.”

<Steps>

<Step title="Before store opening" icon="play-circle">

- Log in before store opening.
- Check Wallet balance sufficiency.
- Keep ID scanner or verification method ready for customers.

</Step>

<Step title="During store hours" icon="settings">

- Close every order in the app before attending the next customer.
- Maintain professional conduct with every customer interaction.

</Step>

<Step title="Store closing and sign-off" icon="check-circle">

- Submit the End-of-Day Summary.
- Collect all devices from the store that were paid for.
- Confirm that no pending payment exists.
- Log out from the app after all checks are complete.

</Step>

</Steps>

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="8. Ops team monitoring and support">

“Cashkr’s Ops Dashboard tracks every vendor’s daily report, collection status, and Wallet health.

Associates should assure vendors that support is available if they face technical issues or delays — but repeated negligence will affect store allocations and performance scores.”

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="9. Recognition for good performance">

“Vendors with 90% plus SLA scores for four weeks receive extra benefits:

- Priority store mapping
- Monthly incentive bonus
- Feature in the Cashkr internal leaderboard

Associates should motivate vendors to view SLA not as a penalty but as a growth metric.”

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Internal team notes (quick reference)

Use this table as a quick reference when coaching or reviewing vendor performance.

| Stage           | Vendor responsibility           | Verification by Ops      | Action on failure   |
| --------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------ | ------------------- |
| Daily reporting | Submit summary by 7:30 PM      | App auto-flag            | Warning             |
| Collection      | Collect all devices same day   | Store handover logs      | Wallet hold or hold |
| Payment         | Make instant Wallet transfer   | Transaction ID audit     | Escalate to Ops     |
| SLA score       | Maintain above 80%             | Weekly performance review| Pause allocation    |

## Key phrases associates must reinforce

Use these phrases consistently while training vendors:

- “All orders must be closed same day — no carry forwards.”
- “Wallet balance equals service readiness.”
- “Daily reporting is non-negotiable; it is how you stay visible in the system.”
- “High SLA means more orders and better incentives.”
- “Transparency and punctuality protect both the vendor and Cashkr.”
