## Overview

**Sidebar:** B2C Sales → Support → **AI Health** (`/support/ai-health`)

"What the bot could not handle, and what customers keep asking about." Use it to decide what to add or fix in the CX Genie bot and whether the Ops/Sales tagger can be trusted.

## Period

Buttons at the top right: **7d** (default), **30d**, **90d**.

## Sections

### How much the bot is handling

Volume of chats the bot handled on its own. Until the CX Genie webhook **"Bot receives a new message"** is ticked under the bot's Webhook settings, this shows "Not being counted yet" — failed chats are visible but successful ones are not, so totals cannot be judged.

### Is the Ops / Sales tag right?

Every chat is tagged Ops or Sales when it opens. A person checks the tag by moving the case or closing it with a reason that belongs to a desk.

| Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Tagged by the rules | Chats tagged automatically, with how many were marked unsure (those want a person first). |
| Checked by a person | Moved to the other desk, or closed with a reason that names one. |
| Rules were right | Share of checked tags the rules got right ("—" until something is checked). |

### Why chats reach a person

Two problems, kept apart because they need opposite fixes:

- **The bot did not know the answer** — count of chats. The answer is missing; add it in CX Genie.
- **The bot answered but it did not help** — *not measured*; CX Genie does not report this. Empty means unmeasured, not zero.

### Questions that keep escalating

Questions ranked by how often they escalated in the selected period.

### Technical details

Collapsed by default, for developers: CX Genie events the panel did not recognise, kept as they arrived (values shortened, since they carry customer messages).
