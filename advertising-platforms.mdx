## Why this matters

<Callout kind="info">

Paid advertising is one of the fastest ways to bring in high-intent leads and app installs at Cashkr. It lets us target specific cities, devices, and customer segments so our campaigns reach the right people at the right time.

</Callout>

## Tools we use

<Card title="Google Ads" icon="zap">

We use Google Ads for search, display, YouTube, and Performance Max campaigns focused on high-intent traffic and app installs.

**Typical use at Cashkr:** Capture users who are actively searching for ways to sell their phones or devices in specific cities.

**Example:** When someone searches `sell old phone in Mumbai`, a Google Search ad appears at the top of results and directs them to the Cashkr sell-device page for Mumbai.

</Card>

<Card title="Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram)" icon="play-circle">

Meta Ads power our visual performance campaigns across Facebook and Instagram, including reels, stories, and carousel formats.

**Typical use at Cashkr:** Show short, engaging creatives that explain how Cashkr works and drive app installs or repeat visits.

**Example:** A 15-second Instagram Reel demonstrates how easy it is to sell a phone on Cashkr, targeted to smartphone users in Pune who have shown interest in recommerce or used devices.

</Card>

<Card title="LinkedIn Ads" icon="briefcase">

LinkedIn Ads support our B2B and corporate buyback acquisition, targeting decision makers in companies and IT teams.

**Typical use at Cashkr:** Promote bulk device buyback and upgrade programs to corporate accounts in target cities.

**Example:** A LinkedIn Sponsored Content campaign targets IT managers in Bangalore with a message about Cashkr bulk device buyback for old office phones and laptops.

</Card>

## How we use these daily

- **Performance marketing team** → Sets up and manages city-specific campaigns (for example, Mumbai, Pune) across Google and Meta, aligned with monthly lead and install targets.
- **Retargeting and lifecycle** → Runs YouTube and Instagram retargeting to users who visited the website or app but did not complete checkout or lead submission.
- **Corporate and partnerships** → Uses LinkedIn Ads to reach businesses, IT managers, and procurement leads for corporate device buyback and bulk deals.
- **Marketing leadership and stakeholders** → Review spend, CPI/CPL, and city-level performance to decide budget allocation and scale-up or pause decisions.

## Typical advertising workflow

<Steps>

  <Step title="Define goal and audience" icon="target">

  - Align on the primary goal: app installs, lead submissions, or corporate buyback enquiries.
  - Choose target cities, device types, and key audience segments (for example, Android users in Mumbai searching to sell phones).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Create creatives and landing experience" icon="edit">

  - Draft ad copy, visuals, and video creatives tailored to the platform and audience.
  - Map each campaign to the right landing page or app deep link, ensuring tracking parameters and events are in place.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch and monitor early performance" icon="play-circle">

  - Launch campaigns with conservative budgets and standard bidding strategies.
  - Monitor first 24–72 hours for impressions, CTR, CPC, install or lead volumes, and basic quality signals.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Optimize and retarget" icon="settings">

  - Pause or adjust underperforming keywords, audiences, and creatives.
  - Shift budget to top-performing ad sets and set up retargeting for site visitors, add-to-cart users, and partial leads.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Reporting and learnings" icon="bar-chart">

  - Summarize key metrics by city, platform, and campaign (CPI, CPL, lead-to-order rate).
  - Capture learnings on messaging, formats, and audiences and feed them into the next cycle of campaign planning.

  </Step>

</Steps>
