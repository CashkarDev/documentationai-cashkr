## Purpose

Equip agents with a simple, repeatable playbook to handle price, pickup delay, and competitor offer objections using approved scripts, without over-promising on price or pickup timing, and with the correct next action and disposition on every call.

## Principles and golden rules

Always follow these principles across all objections.

- **No over-promising**
  - Do not commit exact pickup time or guarantee price changes.
  - Use system- and inspection-based language from the [Price Change Explanation Script](/price-change-explanation-script).
- **Inspection-based pricing**
  - Explain that prices depend on market plus physical inspection.
  - Never say system glitch or blame the tool.
- **Instant, transparent payment**
  - Reinforce that payment is processed instantly via UPI or bank transfer at pickup, after inspection and OTP confirmation.
- **No attacking competitors**
  - Never say others are fake or cheating.
  - Focus on final payout, transparent inspection, and instant payment.
- **Always log notes and disposition correctly**
  - Use one clear disposition at the end of every call.
  - Follow the structure in [Understanding Dispositions](/understanding-dispositions-connected-not-connected-create-order).

<Callout kind="tip">

Mindset: understand first, reassure second, convert last.

</Callout>

## Universal objection-handling flow

Use this flow for any objection before going into the specific objection playbook.

<Steps>

<Step title="Pause and listen" icon="help-circle">

Let the customer finish. Do not interrupt or argue while they explain the issue.

</Step>

<Step title="Acknowledge and empathise" icon="info">

Start with a short acknowledgment so the customer feels heard, for example:  
"Thank you for sharing that, I understand why you are concerned."

</Step>

<Step title="Identify the objection type" icon="settings">

Quickly identify if the core issue is:

- Price or quote difference
- Pickup delay or coordination
- Competitor offering higher price
- A combination of these

</Step>

<Step title="Use the relevant objection card" icon="book-open">

Open the matching objection card below and follow:

- What not to say
- Approved script (English or Hinglish)
- What information to collect
- Next action and correct disposition

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm next step with customer" icon="check-circle">

Before ending the call, confirm what will happen next in simple words and make sure the customer agrees, for example:  
"I will now raise a recheck and update you" or "I will help you place the order now."

</Step>

<Step title="Close with correct disposition and notes" icon="terminal">

Update CRM with:

- Summary of the objection
- Any competitor quote details
- Agreed next action  
Then select the correct disposition as per the cheat sheet at the end of this page.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Expandable title="1) Price objection">

### Customer says

Common statements:

- "Price is too low."
- "I was expecting more."
- "Other apps are showing higher."

### Why it happens

- Customer selected stricter or incorrect condition options.
- Customer is comparing marketing prices, not inspection-based final price.
- Expectation mismatch due to market volatility and dynamic pricing.
- Customer does not understand inspection and re-quote logic.

### What NOT to say

- "This is the final price, nothing can change."
- "Other platforms are fake or cheating."
- "You will not get more anywhere."
- "System glitch ho gaya" or any statement blaming the system.

<Callout kind="alert">

Do not commit that price will increase or that Cashkr will definitely match any competitor. You may **attempt** a price review or match only as per the [Price Match / Objection Handling Script](/price-match-/-objection-handling-script).

</Callout>

### Approved response

Use these short scripts as your core talk-track. Adapt tone but keep meaning the same.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English" icon="book-open">

**Step 1: Acknowledge**

"I understand, thanks for sharing this. The price you see is based on the condition options selected right now."

**Step 2: Explain inspection-based pricing**

"Our final price is always confirmed after physical inspection of the device. We do not deduct anything without a clear reason, and we explain it during inspection."

**Step 3: Give positive possibility (without promising)**

"If your device condition is better than what was selected, the price can improve after inspection. If it is different or has more issues, the price may also change accordingly."

**Step 4: Reinforce instant and transparent payment**

"We do inspection in front of you and once you are comfortable, payment is done instantly by UPI or bank transfer, with no hidden charges."

**If customer quotes a higher number elsewhere**

"May I know what price you are getting on other platforms and whether it is a final inspected quote or just an online estimate?

If you share the details, I can check if we can review or match it as per our policy."

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish" icon="users">

**Step 1: Acknowledge**

"Samajh sakta hoon, aapka concern valid hai. Jo price aap dekh rahe ho woh abhi jo condition options select kiye gaye hain, uske basis par hai."

**Step 2: Explain inspection-based pricing**

"Hamara final price hamesha physical inspection ke baad confirm hota hai. Bina reason ke hum kuch deduct nahi karte, sab kuch aapke saamne explain kiya jata hai."

**Step 3: Positive possibility (no guarantee)**

"Agar aapke phone ki condition selected se better nikli, toh price improve ho sakta hai. Aur agar extra issues mile, toh price accordingly change ho sakta hai."

**Step 4: Instant and transparent payment**

"Inspection aapke saamne hoti hai, aur jab aap comfortable ho jate ho, usi time UPI ya bank transfer se instant payment ho jata hai, koi hidden charge nahi hota."

**If customer quotes a higher number elsewhere**

"Jo dusri jagah aapko price mil raha hai, woh final inspection ke baad ka hai ya sirf online estimate?

Agar aap details share kar do, main dekh sakta hoon ki hum usko review ya match kar sakte hain ya nahi, policy ke hisaab se."

</Tab>

</Tabs>

### What to collect

- Exact price the customer expected or is getting elsewhere.
- Screenshot or details of competitor offer, if available (online estimate vs inspected quote).
- Any specific issues they believe are incorrect in the current Cashkr quote.

### Next action

- If customer disputes price due to inspection findings:
  - Use the language from the [Price Change Explanation Script](/price-change-explanation-script).
  - Raise a recheck or clarification request if the customer has valid points.
- If customer shares a clear, higher **final** quote from a competitor:
  - Follow the [Price Match / Objection Handling Script](/price-match-/-objection-handling-script) to request proof and check if review or match is possible.
- If customer understands and agrees with the current logic:
  - Guide them to confirm or place the order (website or assisted) using the [Lead to Order Conversion Script](/lead-to-order-conversion-script).

### Correct disposition

- **Connected → Waiting for Quote**
  - Customer is thinking, comparing, or waiting for price review / match decision.
- **Connected → Customer Will Place Order**
  - Customer agrees with pricing logic and says they will place the order themself.
- **Create Order**
  - Only if you actually helped the customer place the order on the call.
- Always follow the detailed rules in [Understanding Dispositions](/understanding-dispositions-connected-not-connected-create-order).

<Expandable title="2) Pickup delay objection (order vs lead)">

### Customer says

Common statements:

- "Vendor did not come on time."
- "Pickup is getting delayed."
- "No one has called me yet for pickup."

### Why it happens

- Vendor availability issues or area-specific shortage.
- Operational delays or SLA breaches.
- Customer expectation mismatch on exact pickup timing.
- For leads: customer assumes order is confirmed but no order is placed yet.

### What NOT to say

- "Vendor will definitely come in 10 minutes" or any exact time guarantee.
- "System problem, nothing we can do."
- Openly blaming vendors or internal teams.
- Committing reschedule or reassignment that is not supported by the current order status.

<Callout kind="info">

Always align your communication with live order status from the tracking tools:

- Website: [Track Orders](/track-orders)
- Customer app: [Order Confirmation and Tracking](/order-confirmation-tracking)  
Use the pickup language from [Pickup Communication Best Practices](/pickup-communication-best-practices).

</Callout>

### Approved response (order already placed)

Use this when the order exists in the system.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English" icon="book-open">

**Step 1: Acknowledge**

"I am really sorry for the delay. Thank you for informing us."

**Step 2: Check status**

"Let me quickly check your order status and coordinate with our pickup team."

(Actually check in the admin panel or tracking tool.)

**Step 3: Respond as per status**

- If order is in generated stage with no vendor assigned:  
  "I can see your order is generated but a pickup partner is not yet assigned. I will share this with our operations team so that a partner can be assigned and contact you."
- If vendor assigned or out for pickup:  
  "I can see a pickup partner is already assigned. I will request them to connect with you and we will try to get this completed as early as possible."

**Step 4: Set realistic expectation**

"Our team coordinates based on your pincode and partner availability, so exact timing can vary, but I have highlighted your case now."

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish" icon="users">

**Step 1: Acknowledge**

"Delay ke liye maafi chahunga, aapne bataya uske liye thanks."

**Step 2: Check status**

"Main abhi aapka order status check karta hoon aur pickup team se coordinate karta hoon."

(Actual system me status dekhna hai.)

**Step 3: Respond as per status**

- Agar order generated hai, vendor assign nahi hua:  
  "Mujhe dikh raha hai ki aapka order generated hai, lekin abhi tak pickup partner assign nahi hua. Main ye request operations team ko forward kar raha hoon taaki partner assign ho aur aapse contact kare."
- Agar vendor assigned ya out for pickup hai:  
  "Yaha dikh raha hai ki pickup partner assign hai. Main unko request karunga ki aapse jaldi contact karein, taaki pickup jald se jald ho sake."

**Step 4: Realistic expectation**

"Hamari team pincode aur partner availability ke basis par coordinate karti hai, isliye exact time fix nahi bol sakte, lekin maine aapka case highlight kar diya hai."

</Tab>

</Tabs>

### Approved response (still a lead, no order yet)

Use this when the customer has only checked price or created a lead, but order is not placed.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English" icon="book-open">

**Step 1: Clarify status**

"I checked your details. Right now, the order is not yet confirmed in the system, which is why pickup has not started."

**Step 2: Explain flow**

"Pickup is assigned only after the order is confirmed. Once we confirm the order, the system assigns a nearby partner based on your pincode."

**Step 3: Offer to help create the order**

"If you are ready, I can help you place the order now so that we can move your request forward."

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish" icon="users">

**Step 1: Clarify status**

"Maine aapke details check kiye, abhi system me order confirm nahi hua hai, isi liye pickup start nahi hua."

**Step 2: Explain flow**

"Pickup tabhi assign hota hai jab order confirm ho jata hai. Order confirm hote hi system aapke pincode ke hisaab se nearest partner assign karta hai."

**Step 3: Offer help**

"Agar aap ready ho, toh main abhi call par hi aapka order place karne me help kar sakta hoon, taaki pickup process aage badhe."

</Tab>

</Tabs>

### What to collect

- Order ID or registered phone number.
- Current status from system: Generated, Assigned, Out for Pickup, In Progress.
- Customer preference: proceed with current order, reschedule, or cancel.
- For leads: confirmation that the customer wants to proceed to order creation.

### Next action

- **If order exists:**
  - Check status using the tools linked above.
  - If still in generated stage and within allowed rules, follow [Reschedule Pickup SOPs](/reschedule-pickup---sops) and [Pickup Communication Best Practices](/pickup-communication-best-practices).
  - If vendor already assigned or out for pickup, coordinate through the internal process and update customer without promising an exact time.
- **If still lead (no order):**
  - Use the [Lead to Order Conversion Script](/lead-to-order-conversion-script) to guide the customer and create the order.
- Escalate only if it matches escalation rules in your SLA and operations guidelines.

### Correct disposition

- **Connected → Callback**
  - You or another team member will update customer after checking with operations or vendor.
- **Connected → Customer Will Place Order**
  - Customer says they will place order themself later due to timing or schedule.
- **Create Order**
  - You created the order during the call for a lead.
- **Connected → Technical Issue**
  - Tracking or status is clearly not syncing and you need support, as per [Order Tracking and Customer Notifications](/order-tracking-customer-notifications).
- For not answered calls, use **Not Connected** dispositions as defined in [Understanding Dispositions](/understanding-dispositions-connected-not-connected-create-order).

<Expandable title="3) Competitor offer objection">

### Customer says

Common statements:

- "Cashify is giving more."
- "Another app is paying higher."
- "I got a better deal elsewhere."

### Why it happens

- Competitors show high pre-inspection prices that reduce at pickup.
- Different condition selection or missing issues in online questionnaire.
- Delayed payment models vs instant payouts.
- Customer is comparing ads or banners, not final inspected payout.

### What NOT to say

- "They are cheating customers" or "Their price is fake."
- "We are always better than everyone."
- Any direct attack on competitor brand.
- Any guarantee that Cashkr will definitely beat any competitor offer.

<Callout kind="info">

Stay neutral and factual. Focus on **final payout after inspection** and **instant transparent payment**, as in the [Price Match / Objection Handling Script](/price-match-/-objection-handling-script).

</Callout>

### Approved response

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English" icon="book-open">

**Step 1: Acknowledge**

"That is completely fair, many customers compare multiple platforms, and you should choose what feels best for you."

**Step 2: Explain inspection vs ad price**

"Many platforms show a higher online price at first, but the final amount often changes during the inspection at pickup."

"At Cashkr, we do the inspection in front of you, explain any difference in price, and then complete instant payment before closing the pickup."

**Step 3: Ask for final quote proof (without pressure)**

"If you already have a final inspected quote from them, you may share a screenshot or details. I can check if we can review or match this as per our policy."

**Step 4: Keep door open if they still want to compare**

"No problem if you want to compare peacefully. You can come back anytime; your price check with us remains valid for now, and we will still be happy to help you later."

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish" icon="users">

**Step 1: Acknowledge**

"Bilkul theek hai, zyada customers multiple apps compare karte hain, aapko jo best lage woh choose karna chahiye."

**Step 2: Explain inspection vs ad price**

"Kayi platforms pe online price starting me zyada dikhaya jata hai, lekin pickup ke time inspection ke baad final amount change ho jata hai."

"Ham Cashkr me inspection aapke saamne karte hain, agar price me koi difference hota hai toh clearly reason batate hain, phir OTP ke baad turant payment kar dete hain."

**Step 3: Ask for final quote proof**

"Agar aapke paas unka final inspected quote hai, toh aap screenshot ya details share kar sakte ho. Main check kar sakta hoon ki hum usko review ya match kar sakte hain ya nahi, policy ke hisaab se."

**Step 4: Keep door open**

"Agar aap aaram se compare karna chahte ho toh bilkul theek hai. Aap kabhi bhi wapas aa sakte ho, aapka price check abhi valid hai, aur hum baad me bhi help karne ke liye ready rahenge."

</Tab>

</Tabs>

### What to collect

- Name of competitor platform.
- Shown price and whether it is online estimate or final inspected quote.
- Screenshot or any written proof of competitor quote.
- Customer preference: want us to try to match or only informing.

### Next action

- If the customer wants a match and shares proof:
  - Follow [Price Match / Objection Handling Script](/price-match-/-objection-handling-script) to handle comparison and request approval.
- If customer prefers to try competitor first but is open to returning:
  - Clarify that they can come back while the quote is valid.
  - Document details for future follow-up using [Follow-Up / Callback Script](/follow-up/-callback-script) if needed.
- If customer decides to go ahead now with Cashkr:
  - Guide them through order creation using [Lead to Order Conversion Script](/lead-to-order-conversion-script).

### Correct disposition

- **Connected → Waiting for Quote**
  - Customer is actively comparing or waiting for our price match/review.
- **Connected → Customer Will Place Order**
  - Customer chooses Cashkr and says they will place the order themself.
- **Connected → Already Placed an Order**
  - Customer confirms they have already sold the device elsewhere.
- **Callback**
  - You or team will revert after internal price match review.
- Follow detailed behavior in [Understanding Dispositions](/understanding-dispositions-connected-not-connected-create-order).

<Expandable title="4) Combo objection (price + delay + competitor)">

### Customer says

Typical combined objection:

- "Price is low and pickup is also slow, others are giving more and faster."

### Why it happens

- Customer has faced or fears pickup delay.
- Customer has seen higher competitor prices.
- Price expectation and trust both are low.
- Customer feels frustrated and wants a simple, reliable solution.

### What NOT to say

- "You will not get better service anywhere else."
- "Our vendor will definitely come on time today" (no exact time commitment).
- "Other platforms are fake, only we are genuine."
- Any emotional reaction or argument to customer frustration.

<Callout kind="tip">

With combo objections, **break the problem into parts**: first price understanding, then pickup coordination, then competitor comparison. Handle each calmly instead of defending everything at once.

</Callout>

### Approved response

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English" icon="book-open">

**Step 1: Acknowledge fully**

"I understand, you are worried about both the price and the pickup experience, especially when you see other platforms offering more. Your concern is completely valid."

**Step 2: Separate the issues**

"Let us solve this step by step: first about the price, then about the pickup, and then about any competitor offers you have."

**Step 3: Address price (short)**

"For price, our system works on market rates plus the condition selected, and final price is confirmed after inspection in front of you. We do not reduce anything without showing the reason."

**Step 4: Address pickup (short)**

"For pickup, our team assigns a nearby partner based on your pincode and availability. Sometimes there can be delays, but whenever you face an issue, we coordinate with the pickup team and try to complete it as early as possible."

**Step 5: Address competitor (short)**

"Regarding other platforms, many show higher prices online that change after inspection. At Cashkr, inspection and any change in price are fully transparent, and payment is instant once you agree."

**Step 6: Offer practical next step**

"If you share your current quote or expectation, I can check if any review is possible. And if you are ready, I can also help you confirm the order so that we can move your pickup forward."

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish" icon="users">

**Step 1: Acknowledge fully**

"Main samajh sakta hoon, aapko price bhi low lag raha hai aur pickup ke experience ka bhi doubt hai, upar se doosre apps zyada dikhate hain. Aapka concern bilkul sahi hai."

**Step 2: Separate issues**

"Chaliye isko ek-ek karke solve karte hain: pehle price ki baat, phir pickup, phir jo competitor offers aap dekh rahe ho."

**Step 3: Price (short)**

"Price hamare system me market rate plus aapne jo condition select ki hai uske basis par aata hai. Final amount physical inspection ke baad hi confirm hota hai, aur jo bhi difference hota hai woh aapke saamne clearly explain kiya jata hai."

**Step 4: Pickup (short)**

"Pickup ke liye ham nearby partner aapke pincode ke hisaab se assign karte hain. Kabhi-kabhi delay ho sakta hai, lekin jab bhi issue hota hai hum pickup team se coordinate karke jald se jald complete karne ki koshish karte hain."

**Step 5: Competitor (short)**

"Jo doosre platforms hain, unka online price aksar inspection ke baad change ho jata hai. Cashkr me inspection transparent rehta hai, aur aap agree karte hi UPI ya bank se instant payment ho jata hai."

**Step 6: Practical next step**

"Aap jo bhi current quote dekh rahe ho ya expect kar rahe ho, agar aap share karo, main dekh sakta hoon koi review possible hai ya nahi. Aur agar aap ready ho, toh main abhi order confirm karne me bhi help kar sakta hoon, taaki pickup process aage badhe."

</Tab>

</Tabs>

### What to collect

- Customer expectation on price and any competitor quote details.
- Current Cashkr quote and inspection status.
- Order status: lead vs confirmed order, and current pickup status from system.
- Whether the customer is still open to proceed with Cashkr if concerns are clarified.

### Next action

- Handle each part using the relevant playbooks:
  - For price and competitor:  
    - [Price Change Explanation Script](/price-change-explanation-script)  
    - [Price Match / Objection Handling Script](/price-match-/-objection-handling-script)

- If customer agrees after clarification:
  - Assist in order creation or confirmation using [Lead to Order Conversion Script](/lead-to-order-conversion-script).
- If customer still prefers competitor or wants to drop:
  - Close politely and log details to help future learning.

### Correct disposition

- **Connected → Waiting for Quote**
  - Customer is thinking, wants some time, or waiting for price review.
- **Connected → Callback**
  - You need to coordinate with operations or management and call back.
- **Connected → Customer Will Place Order**
  - Customer will place the order later after comparing.
- **Create Order**
  - You created the order on the call and confirmed pickup process.

## When to stop pitching

Know when to pause and protect the relationship instead of pushing for conversion.

Stop pitching and move to closure when:

- Customer clearly says "Not interested" or equivalent.
- Customer sounds irritated, angry, or repeatedly asks you to stop.
- Customer requests: "Do not call me again" or "I will contact you if needed."

Use this polite close script.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English" icon="book-open">

"I understand, thank you for your time.

I will not take more of your time now. If you ever wish to sell your device later, Cashkr will be happy to help you. Have a good day."

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish" icon="users">

"Bilkul theek hai, aapka time dene ke liye thank you.

Main abhi aapka zyada time nahi loonga. Agar aap future me phone bechna chaho, toh Cashkr hamesha help ke liye available rahega. Have a nice day."

</Tab>

</Tabs>

Set the disposition according to the actual outcome, for example **Connected → Not Interested** or **Connected → Customer Will Place Order** (if they prefer to do it themselves later and clearly say so).

## Disposition cheat sheet

Use this as a quick guide after handling any objection. Always log **one** final disposition per call with clear notes.

<Callout kind="info">

This is only a summary. For full definitions and edge cases, follow [Understanding Dispositions](/understanding-dispositions-connected-not-connected-create-order).

</Callout>

### Connected dispositions (call answered)

- **Customer Will Place Order**
  - Customer understands pricing and process.
  - They say they will place the order themself later (website or app).
- **Callback**
  - You or another agent will call back with an update on:
  - Price review / price match decision.
  - Pickup coordination or escalation.
- **Waiting for Quote**
  - Customer is comparing prices, thinking, or waiting for:
  - Updated quote after inspection.
  - Decision on price match.
- **Already Placed an Order**
  - Customer confirms they have already sold with Cashkr earlier or sold elsewhere.
- **Technical Issue**
  - You identified an app/website or tracking issue that needs tech or admin support before customer can proceed.
- **Not Interested**
  - Customer clearly says they are not interested or does not want further calls.
- **Create Order**
  - You successfully created an order on the call and confirmed details with the customer.  
  - Only use if the order actually exists in the system after the call.

### Not connected dispositions

Use these when you could not speak to the customer at all:

- Ringtone but no answer.
- Phone switched off or unreachable.
- Number invalid.

Choose the appropriate **Not Connected** subtype based on your CRM options and follow retry rules in your internal calling SOP.
