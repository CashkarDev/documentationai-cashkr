## Overview

Logout in the Store app behaves differently for customers and vendors to balance security with convenience.

## Customer session behavior

Customers stay signed in only for the duration of their order flow.

<Callout kind="info">

Customers are automatically logged out once an order is **completed** or **failed**. This ends the session lifecycle for security and prevents unintended reuse of the session.

</Callout>

After logout, customers need to sign in again to start a new order or access any customer-specific details.

## Vendor logout

Vendors stay signed in while they work and end their session manually from the dashboard.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the dashboard" icon="monitor">

From the Store app, go to the **vendor dashboard** where you manage orders and customer interactions.

</Step>

<Step title="Locate the Logout button" icon="search">

Look for the **Logout** button in the dashboard UI, typically in the header or menu section.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm logout" icon="log-out">

Tap the **Logout** button. The app ends the current vendor session and returns to the login screen.

</Step>

</Steps>

After logging out, vendors must enter their credentials again to resume work.

## System logic

Customer and vendor sessions follow different lifecycle rules in the Store app:

- **Customer sessions are temporary** and tied to an active order. The app ends the session automatically once the order is completed or failed.
- **Vendor sessions persist until manual logout**, allowing vendors to stay signed in while handling multiple customers across the day.
