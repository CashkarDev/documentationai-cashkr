## Overview

Share your thoughts and questions on BigboldTech articles by adding a comment at the bottom of the page. Commenting helps you engage with authors and other readers.

Scroll to the end of any article to find the comments section, below the main content.

## How to post a comment

Use the comment form at the bottom of the article to submit your message.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the comments section">

- Scroll to the bottom of the article.
- Locate the comment area below the article content.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter your details">

- Enter your **Name**.
- Enter your **Email** address.

</Step>

<Step title="Write your comment">

- Type your comment in the text box.
- Keep your message clear, relevant to the article, and respectful to others.

</Step>

<Step title="Submit your comment">

- Review your comment for clarity and tone.
- Click **Submit** to send your comment for review.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Comment guidelines

Follow these guidelines to help your comment get approved quickly:

- Stay on topic and relate your comment to the article content.
- Use respectful, inclusive language.
- Avoid spam, promotions, or repetitive duplicate posts.
- Do not use offensive or abusive language.

## What happens after you submit

Submitted comments may be reviewed before they appear under the article. The review process checks that comments follow the community guidelines and are appropriate for publication.

If your comment is approved, it appears below the article in the comments list. If it does not appear, it may still be under review or may not meet the guidelines.
