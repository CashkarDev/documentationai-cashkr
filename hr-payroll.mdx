## Why this matters

<Callout kind="info">

Payroll accuracy and on-time payouts directly impact employee trust and morale. A single, consistent system for attendance, leave, and salary calculation reduces errors and keeps ownership clear across HR, finance, and management.

</Callout>

## Tools we use

<Card title="Petpooja Payroll" icon="users">

Petpooja Payroll is the system of record for attendance, leave tracking, and monthly salary processing, including Loss of Pay (LOP) calculations.

HR uses Petpooja to process salaries on the 10th of every month based on attendance and approved leave data.

</Card>

## How we use these daily

- **HR team** → Reviews attendance, approves or rejects leave requests, updates employee records, and runs monthly payroll in Petpooja.
- **Finance team** → Validates the payroll reports from Petpooja, checks totals against budgets, and prepares payout files.
- **Management** → Monitors overall payroll trends, headcount costs, and ensures fair and consistent policy application.

## Typical workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture attendance and leaves" icon="clipboard-list">

  HR captures attendance and leave data in Petpooja throughout the month, based on shift-wise presence and approved time off.

  </Step>

  <Step title="HR review and approvals" icon="check-circle">

  Before payroll, HR reviews pending leave requests, regularises missed punches if needed, and confirms that attendance records are complete.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Payroll calculation with LOP" icon="settings">

  HR runs the payroll calculation in Petpooja. The system applies LOP where attendance or leave balances are insufficient, generating a draft salary sheet.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Finance review and sign-off" icon="bar-chart">

  Finance reviews the draft payroll output, checks summary reports, and confirms totals and deductions before approving for payout.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Payout and record keeping" icon="database">

  After approval, payouts are initiated based on the final payroll, and HR/finance keep Petpooja reports as the reference for future audits and employee queries.

  </Step>
</Steps>
