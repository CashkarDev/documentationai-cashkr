## Roles & responsibilities

| Role                       | Responsibility                                     |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Copywriter**             | Writes and formats captions for all content types  |
| **Marketing Manager**      | Reviews for tone, clarity, compliance              |
| **Social Media Executive** | Uses final captions during scheduling              |

## Caption framework (for all content types)

| Section         | Description                                                 |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1️⃣ Hook        | First 125 characters → emotional, curiosity, or offer-based |
| 2️⃣ Value/Story | Adds context, educates, entertains, or supports CTA         |
| 3️⃣ CTA         | “Tap link in bio”, “DM us now”, “Comment ‘Bonus’”           |
| 4️⃣ Hashtags    | 10–20 mix: branded + contextual + geo                       |

## Platform formats & examples

### Instagram

| Field      | Value                                 |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Style      | Casual, witty, emoji-rich             |
| Max length | ~2,200 characters                     |
| CTA        | “Tap link in bio”, “Comment BONUS”    |

**Examples:**

**A. Service-Oriented (Doorstep Pickup):**

> Your old phone still chilling in a drawer? Let’s change that 📦  
> Get instant quotes, doorstep pickup, and cash in your account — all from the Cashkr app.  
> Tap the link in bio to sell now!  
> #Cashkr #SellOldPhone #InstantCash

**B. E-Waste Awareness:**

> That old gadget? It won’t recycle itself. ♻️  
> Every device you sell with Cashkr stays out of landfills and gets repurposed.  
> Sell smart. Live clean.  
> #RecycleTech #EcoSmart #Cashkr

**C. Industry News/Trends:**

> Did you know? India generates over 1.6 million tonnes of e-waste yearly.  
> Cashkr is building a sustainable future — one device at a time 🌍  
> Join us: Sell your unused tech today.  
> #EwasteIndia #SustainableLiving #Cashkr

### LinkedIn

| Field  | Value                                      |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------ |
