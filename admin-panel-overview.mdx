## Overview

The Admin Panel is Cashkr's central control system for managing orders, vendors, customers, payments, pricing, and overall business operations. It is primarily used by Operations, CX, Management, Business Development, Vendor Operations, and Finance teams.

Goals of the Admin Panel:

- Smooth, coordinated daily operations
- Accurate tracking of orders, vendors, and customers
- Faster issue resolution for customers and vendors
- Reliable visibility into business performance
- Improved vendor and customer experience

<Callout kind="info">

Treat the Admin Panel as the single source of truth for operational data. Add clear internal notes for every meaningful change so other teams can understand what happened and why.

</Callout>

## Who uses the Admin Panel

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Operations Team" icon="settings">

The Operations team is responsible for managing the end-to-end order flow and ensuring SLAs are met.

- Vendor assignment and reassignment
- Pickup issue resolution
- Order flow monitoring
- SLA compliance and escalation
- Tracking and resolving stuck orders

</Tab>

<Tab title="Customer Experience (CX)" icon="users">

The CX team handles customer queries and order-level support, using the Admin Panel to investigate and resolve issues.

- Tracking order status for customers
- Editing customer details such as address, phone, and timeslot
- Managing failure reasons
- Logging and resolving complaints
- Coordinating with vendor and operations teams

</Tab>

<Tab title="Management" icon="bar-chart">

The Management team uses Admin Panel insights to monitor performance and guide business decisions.

- Reviewing daily and weekly performance dashboards
- Monitoring city-level operations
- Evaluating vendor performance
- Reviewing business health and growth metrics

</Tab>

<Tab title="BD / Area Management" icon="map">

The Business Development and Area Management teams use the Admin Panel to manage serviceability coverage and expansion.

- Managing serviceable pincodes and areas
- Identifying coverage gaps
- Coordinating with Vendor Operations for onboarding in uncovered areas
- Monitoring area-wise order trends
- Preventing ad spend in non-serviceable areas

</Tab>

<Tab title="Vendor Operations" icon="briefcase">

The Vendor Operations team actively uses the Admin Panel to manage vendor lifecycle and discipline.

- Vendor onboarding and verification
- Tracking vendor SLA performance
- Placing vendors on hold or deactivating when required
- Maintaining quality control and discipline logs

</Tab>

<Tab title="Finance" icon="database">

The Finance team depends on Admin Panel data for accurate payments and reconciliations.

- Verifying payments
- Managing refunds
- Handling wallet and credit adjustments
- Reviewing payment and transaction logs

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Navigating the panel

The left sidebar holds every module, grouped into collapsible sections. Inside each section, small uppercase sub-headings (for example **LIVE**, **NEEDS ATTENTION**, **ROSTER**) group related screens.

| Element | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Search menu…** | Top of the sidebar | Type to find any screen by name. |
| **Collapse** (‹) | Next to the logo | Collapses the sidebar to icons. |
| **Count badges** | Next to some items | Live counts, for example orders in *Assigned · No Action* or *Delayed*, and *Hot Leads*. |
| **Sidekick (AI)** | Bottom of the sidebar | Opens the Sidekick AI assistant. |
| **Theme** | Bottom of the sidebar | Switches between light and dark theme. |
| **Logout** | Bottom of the sidebar | Ends your session. |

## Key rules when using the Admin Panel

<Callout kind="alert">

High-risk actions: never change order statuses without authorization, always document any manual data edits, and follow the defined approval hierarchy for refunds, deactivation, and reassignment.

</Callout>

- Add an internal note for every change that affects an order, customer, vendor, payment, or pricing.
- Follow the approval hierarchy for refunds, vendor hold or deactivation, and vendor reassignment.
- Do not perform unauthorized status changes on orders.
- Do not make manual data edits without proper documentation and internal notes.
- Maintain SLA commitments and follow escalation rules when SLAs are at risk or breached.
- Business Development must update pincodes regularly to reflect actual serviceable areas.
- CX must log every complaint properly (B2C Sales → Support → CX Desk).
- Vendor Operations must maintain vendor discipline and quality control logs.
