## Get started with Cashkr

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Cashkr operates in India and focuses on buying used smartphones, laptops, Macs, tablets, and other electronics. Use the Website track if you are a customer selling a device. Use the API track if you are a developer evaluating integrations.
</Callout>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Sell on Website" icon="monitor">
    Cashkr Website is the public entry point for customers who want to sell used devices online, from instant quotes to order creation....

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Cashkr Website" icon="home" title-type="p">
        Go to the Cashkr Website and choose the route that fits your need: consumer trade-in for individual devices or corporate buyback if you are representing a business.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Select your device" icon="smartphone" title-type="p">
        Pick the correct category such as smartphone, laptop, Mac, or tablet, then choose the exact brand and model you want to sell so Cashkr can price it accurately.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Answer condition questions" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
        Fill in basic details about the device condition, including physical state, functionality, and accessories, to generate an instant quote or indicative value.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Log in with phone and OTP" icon="lock" title-type="p">
        Enter your mobile number and complete the one-time password login so Cashkr can create your trade-in order, track history, and allow rescheduling or account actions.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Confirm pickup or drop-off" icon="truck" title-type="p">
        Review the offer, select a pickup slot or available drop-off option where supported, and submit the order to complete your initial journey on the Cashkr Website.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Explore the API" icon="code">
    Use the Cashkr API to integrate trade-in flows, pricing, or lead capture from your own apps or systems.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Review API capabilities" icon="book-open" title-type="p">
        Skim the API overview and sample endpoints to understand what you can do programmatically, such as creating trade-in requests or syncing device data from your systems.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Set up authentication" icon="key" title-type="p">
        Read the authentication guide to see how to send secure requests, including supported auth methods and any required headers or tokens for calling Cashkr APIs.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Call a sample endpoint" icon="terminal" title-type="p">
        Use your preferred HTTP client or API tool to hit a sample endpoint from the reference, verify that you receive a valid response, and confirm network access from your environment.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Map Cashkr flows to your app" icon="settings" title-type="p">
        Decide where Cashkr fits into your product: for example, embedding pricing before checkout, capturing corporate buyback leads, or automating order creation from your internal tools.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
