<Callout kind="info">

**Used by:** B2B Sales Executive  

**Objective:** Professionally follow up after sending a quotation or proposal, confirm receipt, address questions, and move the client closer to approval or scheduling inspection.

**Purpose overview:**

- Confirm that the client has received and reviewed the quotation.
- Reiterate value and trust factors: instant payment, data security, and easy pickup.
- Handle hesitation or negotiation in a structured, professional way.
- Move the lead toward confirmation or inspection booking.

</Callout>

## Scripts and workflows

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Call script">

**Opening**

> "Hi \[Name], this is \[Your Name] from Cashkr.  
> I am following up on the buyback quotation we shared with you for your company’s devices.  
> I just wanted to check if you’ve had a chance to review it?"

<ExpandableGroup>

  <Expandable title="1. They have seen it" default-open="true">

> "That’s great. Did everything look clear to you in the proposal — pricing, pickup terms, and payment process?"

*(Pause to let them respond.)*

If they say **yes, but still deciding**:

> "Sure, I understand. Just to add, our quotation includes instant payment at pickup, and our team can also handle data wiping with certificates if needed.  
> We can even schedule a quick inspection before final pickup to make it easier for your internal team."

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="2. They haven’t seen it yet">

> "No worries, I’ll resend the proposal right now for your reference. It should be from my email ID – please check your spam or promotions folder once.  
> Would you like me to send it again or share it on WhatsApp as well?"

*(Resend immediately and update sheet as "Re-shared".)*

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="3. They want negotiation">

> "Sure, I completely understand where you’re coming from.  
> Our quotes are based on current market resale rates and device condition assumptions.  
> If you’d like, you can share a counter price or a sample device photo, and we can recheck the valuation with our pricing team."

*(If large deal – offer inspection.)*

> "For bulk lots, we also offer an on-site inspection. That usually helps us adjust pricing more accurately and finalize faster."

<Callout kind="alert">

Do not promise a price increase on the call. Position any change as **subject to inspection** and pricing team approval.

</Callout>

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="4. Move toward action">

> "If everything looks good, we can confirm a pickup date and time that suits your team.  
> We’ll handle packaging, inspection, and payment all on-site — completely hassle-free."

<Callout kind="alert">

Always confirm:

- That the decision-maker has approved the proposal.
- That the **date and time you offer are actually available** in the pickup calendar.

</Callout>

  </Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

**Closing**

> "I’ll wait for your confirmation on the final quantity or pickup schedule, \[Name].  
> Once you give the go-ahead, we can close this within 24–48 hours.  
> I’ll also keep a priority slot ready for your pickup window."

### Email follow-up template (formal)

**Subject:** Follow-up on your corporate buyback quotation – Cashkr  

**Body:**

> Hi \[Name],
>
> I hope you’re doing well. Following up on the buyback quotation shared with you for \[Company Name]’s IT devices.
>
> We’d be happy to assist with:
>
> - **Instant payment** on pickup  
> - **Certified data wipe** (if required)  
> - **Bulk inspection and logistics support**
>
> If there are any **revisions** or **counter-offers**, please share, and we’ll review them promptly.  
> Once confirmed, we can schedule your pickup as early as \[insert available date].
>
> Looking forward to your response.
>
> Best regards,  
> \[Your Full Name]  
> Corporate Buyback | Cashkr  
> 📞 \[Phone] | ✉️ \[Email]

<Callout kind="alert">

Do not commit to dates that operations cannot support. Check pickup capacity before mentioning an "earliest" date in the email.

</Callout>

### Internal follow-up and CRM workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set disposition after sending quotation" icon="settings">

  - Update CRM status to **"Quotation Sent – Awaiting Confirmation"**.
  - Attach or link the quotation PDF in the CRM entry for quick resend.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Plan first follow-up" icon="clock">

  - Schedule the **first follow-up call or email 24 hours** after sending the quotation.
  - Use the call script or email template above to confirm receipt.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Plan second follow-up" icon="clock">

  - If there is no response after the first follow-up, schedule a **second follow-up 3 days later**.
  - Reconfirm value points: instant payment, certified data wipe, and logistics support.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Escalate warm leads" icon="arrow-right">

  - After **2 follow-ups with no clear decision**, update status to **"Warm Lead / Requires Revisit"**.
  - Add clear notes on:
    - Decision-maker details
    - Key objections (if any)
    - Expected internal approval timelines

  </Step>

  <Step title="Maintain accurate records" icon="database">

  - Keep all communication history (calls, emails, WhatsApp shares) logged in CRM.
  - Ensure the latest quotation version is attached so any team member can quickly follow up.

  </Step>
</Steps>
