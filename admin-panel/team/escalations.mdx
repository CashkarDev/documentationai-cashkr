## Overview

**Sidebar:** B2C Sales → Team → **Escalations** (`/team/escalations`)

Escalations collects issues raised by executives against a lead or order — for example an abusive customer — so a team lead can route each one to the function that owns it and close it out.

## Filters

Tabs at the top:

- **Open** — escalations not yet resolved (with count).
- **Resolved today** — resolved since the start of the day (with count).
- **All** — every escalation.

At the right, a summary of recent resolutions (for example "No resolutions in the last 7 days").

## What each escalation card shows

- **Type badge** — the escalation category, e.g. *Abusive customer*.
- **Age and alert rule** — how long ago it was raised and how it alerts (e.g. "alerts immediately").
- **Note** — what the executive wrote.
- **Context line** — who raised it, the order / lead ID, the customer and the device.

## Actions

Each open card has:

| Button | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Route to Quality | Hand the escalation to the Quality team. |
| Route to Ops | Hand it to Operations. |
| Route to Pricing | Hand it to Pricing. |
| Route to Tech | Hand it to Tech. |
| Route to Finance | Hand it to Finance. |
| Resolve | Mark it resolved. |

<Callout kind="info">

Open escalations from any day also count in **Escalations still open** on [Day Close](/admin-panel/team/day-close).

</Callout>
