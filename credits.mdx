## Overview

Use the Credits page in the Finance module to approve or reject vendor credit purchase requests and audit all historical credit transactions.

<Callout kind="info">

Treat Credits as the financial source of truth for vendor credit inflows. Every approval adds credits to a vendor account and impacts Cash In Hand and finance reports.

</Callout>

---

## Tabs

<ParamField body="credits-purchase" param-type="tab" show-location="false">

</ParamField>

<ParamField body="purchase-logs" param-type="tab" show-location="false">

## Credits Purchase (Pending approvals)

The Credits Purchase tab lists all **pending** credit purchase requests submitted by vendors and waiting for admin action.

<Callout kind="tip">

Work through Credits Purchase from newest to oldest requests, verifying each payment before approving. Every approved request immediately credits the vendor wallet.

</Callout>

### What you see

Each request card shows the key details you need to verify the payment and decide whether to approve or reject.

<ParamField body="vendor-name" param-type="text" show-location="false">
Display name of the vendor who submitted the credit purchase request.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="vendor-id" param-type="text" show-location="false">
Unique system ID of the vendor, usually shown in brackets next to the vendor name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="payment-id" param-type="text" show-location="false">
Internal payment reference number recorded for this transaction.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="date-time" param-type="datetime" show-location="false">
Timestamp of when the vendor submitted the credit purchase request.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="amount" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
INR amount paid by the vendor for this credit purchase.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="credits" param-type="number" show-location="false">
Number of credits that will be added to the vendor account if you approve the request.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="payment-screenshot" param-type="file" show-location="false">
Image proof of payment uploaded by the vendor (for example UPI, bank transfer, or payment gateway screenshot).
</ParamField>

### Approve a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the request" icon="eye" title-type="p">

Check the vendor name and vendor ID, the INR amount, and the credits to be issued. Review the **Payment Screenshot** carefully to confirm that the payment reference, amount, and payer match the request.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Approve (enter TXN ID)" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">

Click the **Approve (enter TXN ID)** button on the request card when you are confident the payment is valid.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the transaction ID" icon="edit" title-type="p">

In the popup, enter the **Transaction ID** from the payment screenshot or from your bank or payment gateway records so that finance and audit can cross-check the transaction later.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm approval" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">

Submit the TXN ID to finalize approval. Credits are immediately added to the vendor account, and the request moves to **Purchase Logs** with status **Approved**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">

Always cross-check the TXN ID and amount against bank or payment gateway records before approving. Incorrect approvals directly affect company cash and vendor credit balances.

</Callout>

### Reject a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the request" icon="eye" title-type="p">

Inspect the Payment Screenshot, amount, vendor details, and any visible discrepancies. If the screenshot is missing, unclear, incorrect, or appears fraudulent, move to rejection.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Reject" icon="x-circle" title-type="p">

Click the **Reject** button on the request card when you decide not to honor the payment.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm rejection" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">

Confirm the rejection in the popup. The request moves to **Purchase Logs** with status **Rejected**, and no credits are added to the vendor account.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">

Rejected requests stay visible in Purchase Logs for audit and reconciliation. You can use them to investigate disputes or suspicious payment attempts.

</Callout>

## Purchase Logs

The Purchase Logs tab shows a complete record of all credit purchase requests, including both approved and rejected transactions.

### What each log entry shows

Use the fields in each row to reconcile bank statements, investigate disputes, and review vendor credit history.

<ParamField body="vendor-name-id" param-type="text" show-location="false">
Vendor name and vendor ID for the credit purchase transaction.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="payment-id" param-type="text" show-location="false">
Internal payment reference number tied to the original request.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="request-date-time" param-type="datetime" show-location="false">
Date and time when the vendor originally submitted the credit purchase request.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="txn-id" param-type="text" show-location="false">
Transaction ID entered by the admin at the time of approval; use this to match against bank or payment gateway records.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="approved-date" param-type="datetime" show-location="false">
Date and time when the admin approved or rejected the request.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="amount" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
Final INR amount recorded for the credit purchase transaction.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="credits" param-type="number" show-location="false">
Number of credits issued for the transaction if approved, or zero if rejected.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="receipt-icon" param-type="action" show-location="false">
Action icon to view or download the receipt for this transaction.
</ParamField>

## Business logic

### How credits enter the system

```plaintext
Vendor submits Credit Purchase Request
        ↓
Request appears in Credits Purchase tab (Pending)
        ↓
Admin reviews Payment Screenshot
        ↓
Admin Approves (enters TXN ID) or Rejects
        ↓
If Approved → Credits added to Vendor's account
If Rejected → No credits added
        ↓
Request logged in Purchase Logs with final status
```

### Credit to INR conversion

Each credit purchase converts INR to credits at a fixed platform rate. The **Credits** column on each card or log row shows the exact number of credits the vendor receives for the INR amount recorded.

## Admin responsibilities

<ParamField body="approve-request" param-type="action" show-location="false">
Approve a request only when the payment screenshot is valid and the INR amount matches your bank or payment gateway records.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="reject-request" param-type="action" show-location="false">
Reject requests with missing, blurry, mismatched, or suspicious payment screenshots or amounts.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="cross-check-txn-id" param-type="action" show-location="false">
Always verify the TXN ID against official bank or payment gateway entries before confirming approval.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="use-purchase-logs" param-type="action" show-location="false">
Use Purchase Logs for monthly reconciliation, audits, dispute handling, and reviewing vendor credit history.
</ParamField>
