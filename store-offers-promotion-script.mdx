**Team:** Store Onboarding / Channel Operations (B2CH)

**Purpose:** Inform store partners about ongoing Cashkr promotional offers for customer exchanges and encourage them to actively promote these offers at their outlets.

**Tone:** Energetic, friendly, partnership-based — always highlight benefits for both the **customer** and the **store**.

## Quick checklist for caller

Use this checklist before and during the call to keep your pitch sharp and consistent.

- Confirm offer details: type, bonus amount, eligibility, and end date.
- Check store status in tracking sheet and last interaction notes.
- Have the digital posters and communication pack ready to share on WhatsApp.
- Personalize the opening with store owner name and city.
- Clearly mention that Cashkr handles pickup and payment.
- Ask for explicit confirmation to activate the offer.
- Update the store tracking sheet immediately after the call.

<Callout kind="info">

Use these placeholders and always customize:

- `Store Owner Name`
- `Your Name`
- `Date`
- `City`

</Callout>

## Opening

“Hi `Store Owner Name`, this is `Your Name` from Cashkr. I am calling to inform you about a new Cashkr offer that can help you attract more customers and close more sales this week.”

## Offer explanation

“Cashkr is running a limited-time **Exchange Boost Offer** where customers who sell their old phones through your store using the Cashkr App get **extra value on their old device** instantly.”

## Examples

Use one or more of these examples, depending on the live campaign. Do not change the amounts or conditions.

1. 📱 **Exchange Boost Offer:** “Buy a new phone and get ₹2,000 extra on your old phone value.”

2. 💸 **Festival Offer:** “Get ₹1,500 instant bonus on old phone buyback if the new phone is above ₹25,000.”

3. 🎁 **Weekend Exchange Camp:** “Flat ₹1,000 bonus on every buyback order placed between Friday–Sunday.”

4. 🛍️ **Cashkr Loyalty Offer:** “Extra ₹500 on 3rd order from same customer (repeat exchange).”

5. 🔋 **Battery Bonus Offer:** “Extra ₹1,000 if the phone is fully functional and charges properly.”

## Store benefits

“These offers help you:

- Convert more walk-in customers.
- Increase new phone sales easily.
- Earn more commission as exchange volume grows.”

## Urgency

“It is a limited-time campaign, valid till `Date`. We already have stores in `City` promoting this offer and getting great response.”

## Call to action

“Would you like to activate this offer for your store? I will just need to confirm your details and send you the new offer poster and communication pack for your counter.”

## Handling responses

### If store says yes

“Perfect! You will receive the digital poster and app notification today. Just display the poster at your counter and inform every customer who is buying a new phone that Cashkr is giving an instant buyback bonus.”

### If store says they will think about it

“No worries sir, I will send you the offer details on WhatsApp so you can go through it. It is already helping other stores increase footfall.”

## Opening

“Hello sir, main `Your Name` bol raha/rahi hoon Cashkr se. Aaj ek special offer ke baare me batana tha jo aapke store ke liye kaafi useful hai — isse aapke naya phone sales aur customer exchange dono badhenge.”

## Offer explanation

“Abhi Cashkr par chal raha hai **Exchange Boost Offer** jisme customer ko milta hai **extra value apne old phone par**, jab wo aapke store se naya phone leta hai.”

## Examples

Ye ready-made lines use karo, campaign ke hisaab se mix and match kar sakte ho. Amounts ya conditions change mat karo.

1. “Buy new phone aur paaiye ₹2,000 extra apne old phone ke price par.”

2. “Festive bonus ₹1,500 on any old phone if new phone above ₹25,000.”

3. “Weekend offer — har buyback par ₹1,000 extra, Friday se Sunday tak.”

4. “Repeat customer ke liye ₹500 loyalty bonus.”

5. “Working battery phones par ₹1,000 extra bonus.”

## Store benefits

“Isse aapke store par customers khud aayenge, aapko phone exchange karwana easy ho jaayega, aur Cashkr end of day me payment aur pickup handle karega.”

## Urgency

“Offer limited time ke liye hai sir, `Date` tak valid rahega. `City` ke bahut saare stores ye hi offer chala rahe hain aur unko kaafi acha response mil raha hai.”

## Call to action

“Kya aap chahte hain ki main aapke store ke liye ye offer activate kar du? Main WhatsApp par aapko poster aur details bhej deta hoon, aap counter par display kar sakte hain.”

## Handling responses

### Agar store haan bole

“Great! Offer aaj se active ho jaayega. Main abhi aapko poster bhej raha hoon — aap bas counter par lagaiye aur har customer ko batayiye ki Cashkr ke through extra value mil raha hai.”

### Agar store bole soch ke batayenge

“Koi problem nahi sir, main aapko WhatsApp par saari details bhej deta hoon. Aap araam se dekh lijiye, baaki `City` ke stores ko bhi isse footfall aur sales badhne me help mil rahi hai.”

<Callout kind="tip">

**Usage notes for associates**

- Call stores **1 day before offer launch**.
- Send digital posters (JPEG or A4 print) on WhatsApp immediately after the call.
- Clearly mention **validity dates** on every call.
- Update the store tracking sheet with status `Offer Active` as soon as the store agrees.
- Do a mid-offer follow-up to check if the store has displayed the poster and is pitching the offer to every eligible customer.

</Callout>
