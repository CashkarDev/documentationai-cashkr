## Overview

**Sidebar path:** Dashboards → MARKETING → **Push Notifications** (`/dashboard/push-performance`)

Push Notifications measures what happens after a push goes out: *delivery, taps, leads and orders for every notification — by campaign, audience and time*. Notifications themselves are composed and sent from **Marketing → Push Notifications** (the push hub); this dashboard only reports on them.

<Callout kind="info">
The app reports a **tap**, not an impression, so "opened" and "tapped" are the same figure on this page. **Uninstalled** means the app was removed or the device token expired — the nearest thing to an opt-out that Firebase reports.
</Callout>

## Filters

| Control | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Date range** | Defaults to the last 30 days. Presets: Today, Yesterday, This Week, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This Month, Last Month, All Time. |
| **App** | All apps, Customer app or Vendor app. |
| **Audience** | All audiences, Everyone on the app, Topic / segment, Individual users. |
| **Campaign** | All campaigns or one campaign by name. |
| **Show test runs** | Switch, off by default. Turn it on to include test sends in the numbers. |
| **Notifications CSV** | Downloads one row per notification for the current filters. |
| **Daily CSV** | Downloads the day-by-day series for the current filters. |
| **Refresh** | Reloads the numbers. |

## What the screen shows

### KPI cards

| Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Notifications** | Notifications sent in the period, with how many are scheduled ahead. |
| **Sent** | Device tokens targeted. |
| **Delivered** | Tokens reached, with delivery %, failed count and uninstalled count. |
| **Tapped** | Taps, with tap rate (taps ÷ delivered). |
| **Leads** | Leads attributed to a notification, as % of tappers. |
| **Orders** | Orders from those leads, with completed count and % of leads. |

<Callout kind="info">
A lead counts for a notification in two ways: the lead names the notification directly, or the customer tapped the notification and created a lead within **24 hours**. The note under the KPI cards shows how many leads came each way.
</Callout>

### Day by day

Sent and delivered per day, with taps drawn on top. Leads and orders are placed on the day they were created, which can be the day after the notification went out.

### Delivery status

Every notification created in the period, including ones that never went out:

| Status | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Sent** | Notifications sent, with delivered and failed device counts. |
| **Scheduled** | Notifications scheduled, with how many are still to go out. |
| **Test** | Test sends and how many devices they targeted. |

### Why sends failed

Firebase's reason for every device that was not reached in the period (for example *App uninstalled or token expired*), with count and share. **Retry from History** opens the push hub's History tab.

### By app

Customer app vs vendor app, with columns **Segment, Sends, Delivered, Uninstalled, Tapped, Tap %, Leads, Orders**. Each segment also shows how many notifications it covers.

### Every notification

One row per notification in the period (for example *25 of 25 in this period*). A search box filters by title, campaign, sender or deep link. Click a column header to sort.

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Notification** | Title, send time and the admin who sent it (when recorded). |
| **Audience** | App (Customer / Vendor) and audience type (for example *individual*). |
| **Sent / Delivered / Deliv %** | Tokens targeted, reached, and delivery rate. |
| **Uninstalled** | Tokens that failed because the app was removed or the token expired. |
| **Tapped / Tap %** | Taps and tap rate. |
| **Leads / Orders / Completed** | Downstream results attributed to the notification. |

**Open the push hub** at the bottom opens **Marketing → Push Notifications** on its History tab.

## Actions

The dashboard is read-only apart from the two CSV downloads. To send, schedule or retry a notification, use the push hub (**Marketing → ACQUISITION → Push Notifications**).

<Callout kind="tip">
Use **Day by day** and the tap rates in **Every notification** to pick the time slot for the next campaign, and **Why sends failed** to see how much of your audience has uninstalled — a high uninstalled share means the targeted list needs cleaning, not a better message.
</Callout>
