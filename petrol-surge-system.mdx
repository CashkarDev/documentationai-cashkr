## Overview

Quick-commerce platforms operate on 1.5–3 km radii, so their distance pay is a minor component. A Cashkr pickup can be 15 km out, and the agent spends 45 to 75 minutes on site running guided inspection, requote, payment and signature. On a low-value device that trip does not pay for itself, so it gets refused.

The Petrol Surge System pays a bonus on qualifying orders so far and low-value pickups still get accepted. It is called a **petrol bonus** to partners and **trip compensation** internally — the names differ deliberately.

## It is not petrol, it is time

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Mumbai petrol, Sep 2026 | ₹111.21/litre |
| Realistic two-wheeler economy | 45 km/l on a 110cc commuter |
| Fuel cost | ~₹2.47/km |
| 16 km round trip | ~₹40 of petrol |
| Agent's real cost on that trip | ~₹120–170 |

Petrol is roughly a quarter of what the trip actually costs the agent. The rest is time. The bonus is named after petrol because *petrol ka kharcha* is the language agents use and it reads as transparent rather than algorithmic — but what it compensates is the whole trip.

## Cost model

| Component | Mumbai, Sep 2026 | Basis | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Petrol | ₹111.21/l | Pump price, 22 Sep | Verified |
| Two-wheeler fuel | ₹2.22–2.78/km | 40–50 km/l | Verified |
| Diesel — car pickups, laptops | ₹97.83/l → ₹6.52/km | 15 km/l | Verified |
| Wear, tyres, service | ~₹1.00/km | Estimate, unsourced | Inferred |
| Agent time | ~₹2.50/km-equivalent | From ₹100/hr opportunity cost | Inferred |
| **All-in, two-wheeler** | **~₹6/km** | Sum of the above | Inferred |

₹6/km also matches the ₹5–10/km that food and quick-commerce platforms pay beyond a free radius. Convergent from two directions, which makes it a defensible starting rate even though half its components are estimates.

<Callout kind="info">

**A regulatory anchor worth knowing.** The Motor Vehicles Aggregator Guidelines 2025 cap surge at 2× base fare, set a floor of 50% of base, and require drivers to receive at least 80% of the fare. It does not bind Cashkr, but it is the Indian regulator's stated view of a defensible surge and a sensible self-imposed ceiling.

</Callout>

## How the bonus is built

Two layers, deliberately separated so one is predictable and one is reactive.

```js
// Layer 1 — deterministic trip allowance (published, builds trust)
trip_allowance = round_to_5(
      max(0, round_trip_km - FREE_KM) * RATE_PER_KM
    + DWELL_ALLOWANCE[device_category]
)

// Layer 2 — dynamic surge (reactive, capped, never shown as a ladder)
surge = min(
      SURGE_CAP,
      STEP * escalation_level * zone_multiplier * weather_multiplier
)

pickup_bonus = min(
      trip_allowance + surge,
      CAP_ABS,                    // e.g. ₹250
      CAP_PCT * quote_value       // e.g. 12% of the customer quote
)
```

**Both caps matter.** Without the percentage cap the system will one day pay ₹180 of bonus on a ₹900 device, and nobody finds out until month-end.

**Surge needs an exit.** If the bonus reaches its cap and the order is still unclaimed, the system must stop bidding and route elsewhere — batch it with another pickup in the pincode, push the customer to a later slot, or offer a store drop-off. Most surge systems have no exit condition and quietly become an uncapped subsidy.

## Three answers gate the whole thing

| Question | Why it blocks | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Are per-transition order timestamps stored, or is the status column overwritten? | The system is fed by one event stream — offer shown → accepted, rejected or expired. If status is overwritten, that history does not exist and nothing here is buildable. | Engineering |
| What is gross margin per completed order, by device band? | Every cap is anchored to what other platforms pay, not to what a Cashkr pickup earns. | Finance |
| Does a travel bonus enter the 5% gig-worker contribution base? | The Social Security (Central) Rules 2026, effective 8 May 2026, require aggregators to contribute a notified percentage of turnover or 5% of amounts paid to gig workers. | Yogesh |

<Callout kind="warning">

**The gap is not all refusals.** Roughly 270 orders/day arrive against about 39/day completing. A large share of that is customers dropping off at quote stage (`isCompleted=0`), not partners refusing trips. Sizing the partner-refusal slice is what Phase 0 exists to do, and it must happen before any money is spent.
