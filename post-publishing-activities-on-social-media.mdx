## Objective

Ensure that once content is published on Cashkr social media channels, post-engagement best practices are followed to maximize reach, trigger platform algorithms, and strengthen audience and creator relationships.

## Roles and responsibilities

| Role                       | Responsibility                                                |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Social Media Executive** | Executes all engagement steps after every post                |
| **Marketing Manager**      | Monitors post-performance and engagement quality periodically |

## Step-by-step process (to be followed immediately after posting)

<Callout kind="info">
  Follow the timing mentioned in each step to get the strongest algorithm and engagement boost from every post.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1: Share your post to story (within 5 minutes)" icon="upload">

    - Share every reel, carousel, or feed post to Instagram and Facebook stories.
    - Add interactive elements such as:
      - Emoji slider
      - Countdown (for offers)
      - "New Post" sticker or tap-through overlay

  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 2: Engage with your previous post (right after posting)" icon="message-square">

    - Go to your last published post.
    - Like and reply to all comments.
    - If there are many comments, respond to half now and the rest the next day to re-trigger engagement.
    - Use this to revive older content and boost performance with returning viewers.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 3: Engage with your own community (first 1 hour)" icon="users">

    - Reply to new comments, even if it is just emojis or a short thank you.
    - Answer all DMs, including product questions, referrals, or appreciation.
    - Add an engagement hook by using a question in the caption or pinning a comment such as:
      - "What phone would you sell first?"
      - "Tag a friend who needs this!"
    - React to mentions or story tags, especially when others reshare the post.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 4: Engage with other creators (right after posting)" icon="heart">

    - Like, reply to, or comment on 5–7 other creators recent posts to boost visibility and trigger mutual algorithm exposure.
    - Share a story reply or emoji reaction to a peer creator to build goodwill and future collaborations.
    - Follow these 5 rules when engaging with other creators:
      1. **Be authentic** – Do not fake interest.
      2. **Offer value** – Share relevant responses, not just emojis.
      3. **Have fun** – Witty or warm tones work better.
      4. **Avoid repetition** – Do not leave the same comment everywhere.
      5. **Follow through** – If you offer to collab or exchange DMs, complete the loop.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 5: Stay active and signal the algorithm (30–60 minutes)" icon="activity">

    - Avoid ghosting after posting.
    - Stay online for 30–60 minutes after publishing.
    - Continue liking, commenting, replying to stories, or scrolling in-feed.
    - Use this activity to show platforms such as Instagram and X that you are active, which boosts post distribution.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Daily posting checklist (post-publish)

- [x] Shared post to story (5 minutes after post)
- [x] Engaged with previous post (immediately after new post)
- [x] Replied to comments and DMs (within 1 hour)
- [x] Commented on 5 creators posts (within 1 hour)
- [x] Stayed active for 30–60 minutes after posting
