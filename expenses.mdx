title: Expenses
description: |-

  Track and manage all business expenses
---
The Expenses page is part of the Finance module. It allows admins to record, track, and manage all business-related expenses in one place. Expenses can be added manually one by one or uploaded in bulk via an Excel file.

---

**Summary Cards**

Four summary cards at the top provide a quick snapshot of all expense activity based on the active filter:

- **Total Expenses** — Total INR value of all recorded expenses

- **Total Entries** — Total number of individual expense records

- **Top Category** — The category with the highest total spend

- **Categories** — Total number of expense categories in use

Summary card values update dynamically based on the active date range, category, and payment method filters.

---

**Expenses Table**

Each row in the expenses table represents a single business expense entry:

- **Date** — Date the expense was incurred

- **Title** — Name or short description of the expense

- **Category** — The category the expense belongs to (e.g. Travel, Operations, Marketing)

- **Amount** — INR value of the expense

- **Payment** — Payment method used to pay the expense

- **Created By** — Admin who logged the expense entry

- **Recurring** — Whether the expense is a recurring entry or a one-time record

- **Actions** — Edit or delete the expense entry

---

**Filters & Controls**

Date Range Filter Click Pick a date range to filter expenses between specific dates. Useful for monthly expense reporting, budget reviews, and finance audits.

Search Search for a specific expense by title or description. Press Enter after typing to apply the search.

Category Filter Filter expenses by category using the All Categories dropdown. This helps isolate spending in a specific area such as logistics, salaries, or office costs.

Payment Method Filter Filter expenses by how they were paid using the All Methods dropdown. Useful for reconciling cash payments vs. bank transfers vs. UPI.

Export Click Export to download the current filtered expense list for reporting, audits, or sharing with the finance team.

---

**Adding an Expense**

Click the green Add Expense button (top right) to manually log a new business expense. Fill in the title, category, amount, payment method, date, and whether it is a recurring expense. Confirm to save the entry.

---

**Uploading Expenses via Excel**

Click Upload Excel (top right) to bulk import multiple expense entries at once. This is useful for uploading monthly expense sheets or migrating existing expense records into the system.

Ensure the Excel file follows the required format before uploading — incorrect formats may cause import errors.

---

**Recurring Expenses**

Expenses marked as Recurring are regular, repeating costs such as monthly rent, salaries, or subscription fees. These are flagged separately in the Recurring column so finance teams can distinguish fixed ongoing costs from one-time expenses.

---

**Admin Responsibilities**

- Log all business expenses promptly to keep records up to date

- Assign the correct category to every expense for accurate reporting

- Use the date filter regularly to review monthly and quarterly spending

- Upload bulk expenses via Excel to save time for large volumes

- Review the Top Category card to identify the highest areas of spend

- Export expense data monthly for finance reconciliation

---

**Connections to Other Modules**

- **Cash In Hand** — Cash payment expenses are reflected in the cash flow tracker

- **Reports** — All expense data feeds into the finance reports module

- **Credits** — Expense records help reconcile overall business costs against revenue

---

**Important Notes**

Expenses added here are for internal business cost tracking only. They are not linked to vendor orders or device transactions.

Recurring expenses should be reviewed periodically to ensure they are still active and the amounts are current.

Deleted expenses are permanently removed and will not appear in the Logs or Reports.
