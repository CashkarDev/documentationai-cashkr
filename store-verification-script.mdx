## Overview

<Callout kind="info">

**Team:** Store Onboarding / Channel Operations (B2CH)  
**Purpose:** Verify a newly onboarded store's details (documents, address, and setup readiness) before activation on the Cashkr Store App.  
**Tone:** Professional, polite, supportive — sound like a partner, not an inspector.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip">

Use a calm, conversational pace. Smile while speaking, avoid sounding like an audit or inspection, and keep the call short and respectful of the store owner's time.

</Callout>

## Store verification call scripts

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="English Version">

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Opening and context" icon="play-circle">

    “Hi \[Store Owner Name], this is \[Your Name] from Cashkr. I am just calling to verify your store details before activating your Cashkr Store App account.”

    “Can I confirm a few details with you?”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Verification checklist" icon="list">

    Read each point calmly and confirm with the store owner. Update your tracker while speaking, not after the call.

    - **Store name:** “Can you confirm your store name once? I have it as \[Store Name].”
    - **Store address and pincode:** “Your store address I have is \[Store Address] and pincode \[Pincode]. Is this correct for pickup mapping?”
    - **Owner's name and contact number:** “Your name I have is \[Owner Name] and mobile number \[Contact Number]. Is that correct?”
    - **Store type:** “What type of store is this? Mobile store, multi-brand retailer, or franchise?”
    - **Cashkr app installed:** “Have you already installed the Cashkr Store App on your phone?”
    - **Training done or required:** “Has our associate already visited for training, or do you want us to schedule a training visit?”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Outcome – all details verified" icon="check-circle">

    Use this if documents and details are complete and correct.

    “Perfect, all your details are verified. We will activate your store on the Cashkr Store App today, and you will start seeing order access from tomorrow.”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Outcome – documents missing" icon="alert-triangle">

    Use this only for missing documents. Mention the exact item and channel.

    “I just noticed we are missing \[specific document, for example GST or ID proof]. Can you share it on WhatsApp at \[official number or email] so we can complete your activation?”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Closing the call" icon="arrow-right">

    “Thank you for partnering with Cashkr. Once live, our associate will visit regularly for inspection and collection support. You will also get a confirmation message once activation is done.”

    </Step>
  </Steps>

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Hinglish Version">

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Opening and context" icon="play-circle">

    “Hello \[Store Owner Name], main \[Name] bol raha or rahi hoon Cashkr se. Yeh call aapke store ke verification ke liye hai — activation se pehle kuch basic details confirm karni hoti hain.”

    “Bas aapke sath kuch details confirm karni thi, theek hai?”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Verification checklist" icon="list">

    Keep the tone friendly and clear. Confirm each point and update your tracker side by side.

    - “Store ka naam kya hai? Mere paas \[Store Name] likha hai, sahi hai?”
    - “Store ka address aur pincode \[Pincode] sahi hai na pickup ke liye?”
    - “Owner ka naam \[Owner Name] aur contact number \[Contact Number] sahi hai?”
    - “Store type kya hai – mobile shop, multi-brand outlet ya franchise?”
    - “Cashkr Store App aapne install kar liya hai kya?”
    - “Hamare associate ne aapko training di thi ya abhi training schedule karni hai?”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Outcome – sab details sahi" icon="check-circle">

    Use this when all details and documents are in place.

    “Perfect sir, sab details verify ho gayi. Aapka store aaj hi activate kar diya jaayega, aur kal se aapko order access milna shuru ho jaayega.”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Outcome – document pending" icon="alert-triangle">

    Use this only when some document is missing. Be specific and helpful.

    “Sir bas ek document pending hai — \[mention item]. Aap WhatsApp kar dijiye \[official number] par, taki activation complete ho jaaye.”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Closing the call" icon="arrow-right">

    “Thank you for joining Cashkr Partner Program. Hamara associate aapke store par regular visit karega collection ke liye. Activation confirmation aapko message ke through mil jaayega.”

    </Step>
  </Steps>

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Operational requirements

<Callout kind="alert">

- Make the verification call **within 24 hours of registration**.  
- Log verification status in **Store Tracker** immediately after the call.  
- Approve activation only after all three are completed: documents verified, app setup done, and store location confirmed.

</Callout>
