## Adding a new tool

When you add a new operations tool to this page, capture the same three fields used in the table:

- **Tool**: The exact name of the tool as used in the team and in login credentials.
- **Primary use case**: One short phrase that explains what the tool is mainly used for.
- **Example usage**: A concrete example of how the operations or marketing team uses the tool in practice.

Keep descriptions short and consistent so team members can scan the table quickly and choose the right tool.
