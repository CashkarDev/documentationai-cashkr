## Overview

Use BigBoldTech's search tool to quickly find articles and related products by entering a few keywords in the search bar.

## Where to find search

You always access search from the main website header:

- On the homepage, the search bar appears at the top of the page.
- On other pages, access it from the navigation menu at the top.

## How to search

Follow these steps to run a search and open a result.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the search bar">

- Go to the BigBoldTech homepage or any page with the main navigation.
- Locate the search bar at the top of the page or within the navigation menu.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter your keywords">

- Click inside the search bar.
- Type the topic or product you are interested in, such as `identify fake chargers` or `Best Gaming Laptop`.

</Step>

<Step title="Review search results">

- After you enter your keywords, view the list of matching results that appears directly below the search bar.
- Scan the titles to find the article or related product you need.

</Step>

<Step title="Open an article or product">

- Click any result in the list.
- The full article or product details page opens in the same window.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Tips for better results

Use these simple patterns to help the search tool show more relevant results:

- Use a few specific words instead of full sentences.
- Include the device or product type, such as `laptop`, `charger`, or `phone`.
- Try another keyword if you do not see what you expect on the first try.
