## Overview

**Sidebar:** B2C Sales → Team → **Reviews** (`/team/reviews`)

Reviews supports a weekly one-to-one between a team lead and a sales executive: the executive's numbers for the week, where they are losing leads, and a single coaching action that carries into the next week.

## Filters

- **Choose an executive…** — pick the executive to review. Until one is chosen the screen shows "Pick an executive."
- **Date** — any date in the week; the label beside it shows the week (e.g. "Week of 12 Oct").

## What the screen shows

### This week

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Leads worked | Leads the executive actually worked this week. |
| Calls logged | Calls recorded. |
| Conversion | Wins ÷ leads **worked**. |
| Orders placed | Orders created from their leads. |
| Closed as lost | Leads closed as lost. |
| First-call speed | How quickly new leads got their first call. |
| Same-day call rate | Share of leads called on the day they arrived. |

<Callout kind="info">

Conversion is wins over leads **worked**, not over leads assigned — an executive is not accountable for a queue nobody could get to.

</Callout>

### Top loss reasons

"Where to point the coaching" — the most common reasons this executive's leads were closed as lost this week ("No closures this week" when empty).

### Coaching

"One agreed action per week, carried forward."

- **Last week's agreed action** — shown for follow-up ("Nothing was agreed last week…" if none).
- **This week's action** — a text box ("One thing to work on this week") with **Set this week's action**.

<Callout kind="tip">

One action per week, replaced rather than added to. A week with two agreed actions is a week with none.

</Callout>
