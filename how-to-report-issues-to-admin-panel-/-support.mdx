## Purpose of this SOP

Use this SOP whenever you see a live problem in Cashkr (customer/vendor complaint, app bug, data mismatch, payment issue) and need to report it so Ops and Tech can act quickly.

Your goals:

- Classify the **severity** correctly.
- Log a **clean trail** in the Admin Panel.
- Raise a **reproducible ticket** in the ticket tool or support channel.
- Escalate to the **right team** without breaking any risk or data rules.

---

## Before you report: quick checklist

Confirm these basics before you open a ticket or ping any channel.

- You understand **what exactly is wrong**, in simple language.
- You know **who is impacted** (customer, vendor, store, internal).
- You have opened the **right record in Admin Panel**:
  - Orders module for order/flow issues.
  - Payments module for wallet/credits/refunds.
  - Support and Complaints for existing complaints.
  - Settings for configuration issues (pincode, serviceability, pricing toggles).
- You have collected:
  - Order ID or lead ID (if any).
  - User ID and/or vendor ID.
  - Pincode, city, and device details if relevant.
  - Screenshots or screen recordings if possible.
  - Exact timestamps and steps to reproduce.

<Callout kind="tip">

If you are in doubt between multiple issues, start with **one clean ticket per user or per order**. Do not mix unrelated problems in a single report.

</Callout>

## Severity levels (P0–P3)

Use these guidelines to choose the right priority. If two levels seem possible, pick the **higher** one and justify it in the ticket.

### P0 – Critical / system down

Entire flows are blocked or money is at risk right now.

Examples:

- All or most orders are **stuck in one status** (for example, cannot move from created to out for pickup).
- OTP for vendor payment or order confirmation **fails for majority of users/vendors**.
- Payment success on gateway but **no order created** or **wallet/credits not updated** for many users.
- Serviceability logic broken: **non‑serviceable pincodes showing as serviceable** or vice versa for large areas (city‑wide).
- Any live issue that can cause **large financial loss** or **legal/compliance exposure** if not fixed immediately.

### P1 – High impact but not system‑wide

Many users/vendors are affected, but core flows still work for others.

Examples:

- Orders from certain **pincodes or cities** are failing or stuck.
- Specific **device category** or brand not moving ahead in the flow.
- Multiple vendors reporting **OTP delays** or occasional failures.
- Repeated **payment mismatch** for a specific payment mode or bank.
- Customer or vendor complaints are increasing on the same bug in one region.

### P2 – Medium impact / recurring bug

Single‑user or low volume issues that recur and need a fix, but not urgent fire.

Examples:

- Individual order stuck due to **edge‑case status** or rare combination.
- One vendor’s **credit balance** incorrect compared to transactions.
- UI bug in app or website that **does not block** the flow but confuses users.
- Minor serviceability mismatch for few pincodes.

### P3 – Low impact / improvement request

Nice‑to‑have changes, content issues, or one‑off cases that can be handled manually.

Examples:

- Text changes, spelling errors, or unclear labels.
- Request to adjust **copy, tooltips, or help text**.
- Single historical data correction with no pattern.
- Suggestions to improve a flow based on feedback.

<Callout kind="info">

Severity is about **impact and urgency**, not about who reported it. Even a founder‑reported issue can be P2 if it affects one case only.

</Callout>

## Where to log in the Admin Panel

Always log the issue first in the Admin Panel. Admin Panel is the **source of truth** for what happened and when.

### Core modules to use

- **Orders**  
  For anything related to order creation, status, pickup, reschedule, pricing, vendor assignment.

- **Payments (Wallet and Credits)**  
  For wallet balance mismatches, vendor credits, refunds, reversals, payout issues.

- **Support and Complaints**  
  For formal customer/vendor complaints, SLA breaches, and escalations.

- **Logs and Audit Trail**  
  For checking who changed what and when. Use this to reconstruct timeline and support your ticket.

- **Settings**  
  For pincode serviceability, pricing configs, toggles that may cause or solve the issue. Do not change settings without approval.

### How to add internal notes correctly

1. Open the **exact record**:
   - Order detail page in Orders.
   - Wallet/credit transaction or vendor account in Payments.
   - Complaint ticket in Support and Complaints.

2. Add an **internal note** that is:
   - Time‑bound: mention when the issue was reported and when it started (if known).
   - Clear: one note per topic; avoid long story‑telling.
   - Actionable: state what you observed and what you did (if anything).

<Callout kind="alert">

Always add an internal note for every **meaningful change** or escalation. Do not adjust order status, payments, refunds, deactivation, or reassignment without following the **approval hierarchy**.

</Callout>

**Example internal note:**

> 2024‑06‑21 15:32 – Customer says payment success but order not visible in app. Razorpay shows success for Rs 7,500 at 15:20. Checked Admin Panel → no order created for this mobile. Marking as P1 payment mismatch and raising ticket. No manual refund triggered yet.

## Standard issue reporting workflow

Follow these steps every time, from first observation to final ticket.

<Steps>

<Step title="Stabilize the user/vendor conversation" icon="help-circle">

- Acknowledge the issue and stay calm.
- Do **not** promise a specific outcome (refund, extra payment, incentive) until Ops or Finance confirms.
- Give a realistic expectation: for example, "I am logging this for our Ops/Tech team right now. We will update you as soon as we identify the issue or need more details."

</Step>

<Step title="Collect minimum required information" icon="list-ordered">

Capture these data points while you still have the user/vendor in contact:

- Order ID or lead ID (if created).
- User ID and phone number (and email if relevant).
- Vendor ID and store name (for vendor issues).
- Pincode and city.
- Device details (brand, model, storage, condition) if order‑related.
- Timestamps:
  - When the issue happened (time and timezone).
  - When the user/vendor tried each action (for example, clicked Pay Now at 15:20).
- Technical context:
  - Whether they used app or website, and which OS (Android/iOS) if known.
  - Network type (Wi‑Fi or mobile data) if mentioned.

Ask for:

- Screenshots of error messages.
- Screen recording if the issue is in the flow (optional but very useful).

</Step>

<Step title="Check Admin Panel and logs" icon="monitor">

- Open the **Orders** module using the Order ID or lead ID.
- Confirm current **order status** and recent actions.
- Check **Payments (Wallet and Credits)** for:
  - Wallet or credit deductions.
  - Refunds or reversals.
- Use **Logs and Audit Trail** to:
  - See which user or system changed statuses and when.
  - Confirm if any manual override already happened.

Add a short internal note summarizing what you found.

</Step>

<Step title="Decide severity and owner" icon="alert-triangle">

- Use the P0–P3 rules above to set **severity**.
- Identify **primary owner**:
  - Ops (order flow, vendor assignment, SLA).
  - Finance (wallet/credits/payout/refund mismatch).
  - Tech (bugs, errors, system behaviour, OTP not working).
  - Compliance (fraud, suspicious patterns, legal risk).

Mention both in your note and in the ticket: `Severity: P1, Owner: Tech + Ops`.

</Step>

<Step title="Log a clean Admin Panel note" icon="edit">

- Go back to the relevant record (order, complaint, payment).
- Add an internal note that covers:

  - Summary in one line.
  - Severity and owner.
  - Facts (what happened, when, for whom).
  - Any manual workaround you already did or will do.

Success signal: the note is clear enough that **another team member can understand the case without calling you**.

</Step>

<Step title="Create or update the support ticket" icon="package">

- Go to the agreed **ticket tool or support channel**.
- Use the **ticket template** below and fill every required field.
- Attach screenshots, links to Admin Panel record, and logs if available.
- Link the ticket ID back in the **Admin Panel note** so the loop is clear.

Success signal: the ticket has:

- Clear title and severity.
- Exact steps to reproduce.
- One or more specific examples (Order IDs, timestamps).
- Admin Panel link and screenshot.

</Step>

<Step title="Monitor and escalate if needed" icon="bar-chart">

- Track the ticket until you get a response or fix.
- If the issue is P0 or P1 and **no update** within the expected SLA, follow the escalation guidelines below.
- Once the issue is resolved, add a **closing note** in the Admin Panel:
  - What fix was applied (config change, code fix, manual adjustment).
  - Whether any **refunds or adjustments** were done.
  - Any follow‑up to prevent repeat.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Common issue categories and required evidence

Use these expandable sections to know what to collect for typical Cashkr issues.

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Orders stuck or incorrect status" default-open="true">

Applies when orders are stuck (for example, in created, out for pickup, in progress) or show unexpected status transitions.

Collect:

- Order ID(s) and user ID(s).
- Current status and **expected next status**.
- Timestamps of last status change.
- Vendor ID(s) involved (if any).
- Screenshots of:
  - Order tracking screen (customer app or website).
  - Order view in Admin Panel Orders module.
- Logs and Audit Trail entries for:
  - Status changes.
  - Vendor assignment or reassignment.
  - Manual overrides by agents.

Key questions to answer:

- Is this affecting one order, multiple orders, or a pattern (for example, specific city/vendor)?
- Was there any **manual status edit** before it got stuck?
- Any recent config change in **Settings** (for example, SLA or flow rules)?

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="OTP failures (customer or vendor)" default-open="false">

Covers OTP issues for login, order confirmation, or vendor payment OTP.

Collect:

- Phone number and user ID or vendor ID.
- Which OTP flow failed:
  - Customer login/registration.
  - Order confirmation.
  - Vendor login.
  - Vendor payment OTP.
- Timestamps of OTP attempts (with timezone).
- Exact message on screen:
  - Error text.
  - Whether "OTP sent" message appeared.
- Screenshots from device:
  - OTP screen.
  - Error messages or loading states.
- Check Admin Panel:
  - Any failed login or attempt logs in Logs and Audit Trail.
  - Any account block or restriction for that user/vendor.

Key questions to answer:

- Is OTP not received at all, or received late, or marked invalid?
- Does this happen across multiple carriers/networks or just one?
- Can you reproduce on your own test device?

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Payment and refund mismatches" default-open="false">

Includes wallet/credits not updating, vendor payouts, double charges, or refunds not reflected.

Collect:

- Order ID, user ID, and vendor ID if vendor is involved.
- Payment reference (transaction ID from gateway or bank).
- Amount, payment mode, and bank/wallet details (without full card numbers).
- Timestamps of payment, refund, or reversal attempts.
- Screenshots:
  - Payment success/failure pages.
  - Wallet or credits balance screen.
  - Bank SMS or app snapshot (mask sensitive details).
- Check Admin Panel:
  - Payments (Wallet and Credits) for all related entries.
  - Orders module for order status vs payment status.
  - Logs and Audit Trail for:
    - Manual refunds or reversals.
    - Changes in wallet/credit balances.

Key questions to answer:

- Did system **deduct money but not create order**?
- Did system **create order but not deduct or credit money correctly**?
- Has any manual refund already been processed?

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Pincode serviceability mismatches" default-open="false">

For cases where customer sees wrong serviceability info (serviceable vs non‑serviceable) or cannot proceed due to pincode rules.

Collect:

- Pincode(s) and city.
- User ID and device type being sold.
- Flow where issue appears:
  - Address entry.
  - Pincode check on homepage.
  - During order scheduling.
- Screenshots of:
  - Error message or serviceable/non‑serviceable state.
  - Any conflicting information (for example, website says serviceable, app says not).
- Check Admin Panel:
  - **Settings** module for current pincode config.
  - Any recent changes logged in Logs and Audit Trail.

Key questions to answer:

- Is pincode incorrectly marked **non‑serviceable** when it should be serviceable, or opposite?
- Is this issue limited to specific devices or all devices?
- Has any Ops or BD member recently requested a change for this area?

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Vendor ops issues (credits, access, SLA)" default-open="false">

For vendor credit balance issues, access problems, or SLA-related disputes.

Collect:

- Vendor ID and store name.
- Type of issue:
  - Credit balance mismatch.
  - Unable to unlock leads or accept orders.
  - SLA breach or penalty dispute.
- Screenshots:
  - Vendor app screens.
  - Vendor summary and credit details in Admin Panel.
- Check Admin Panel:
  - Payments (Wallet and Credits) for vendor entries.
  - Orders module for vendor’s recent orders and statuses.
  - Logs and Audit Trail for:
    - Vendor hold/deactivation/reactivation.
    - Manual credit additions or deductions.

Key questions to answer:

- Is this a **single mistake** or a pattern across multiple orders?
- Has vendor been **recently held or deactivated**?
- Are there any **existing tickets** or complaints linked to this vendor?

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Minimum information to include in every ticket

Even for small issues, include at least:

- **Identifiers**
  - Order ID or lead ID (if applies).
  - User ID and phone number.
  - Vendor ID and store name (if vendor‑related).
- **Context**
  - Device details (brand/model/storage) if order‑related.
  - Pincode and city.
  - App vs website, Android vs iOS (if known).
- **Evidence**
  - Screenshots or screen recordings.
  - Error message text (copy exact wording).
  - Logs and Audit Trail summary.
- **Timeline**
  - When the user/vendor started the action.
  - When each issue step occurred (for example, OTP requested at 10:05, OTP screen error at 10:06).
- **Reproduction steps**
  - Step‑by‑step actions someone else can follow.
  - Mention if you reproduced it yourself or if it only happens to specific users.

If any of these are missing, mention why (for example, "Customer disconnected before sharing screenshot").

## Escalation and do/don’t rules

### Who to escalate to

- **Ops**  
  - Order flow issues, SLA breaches, vendor assignment, reschedules, stuck orders.
- **Finance**  
  - Wallet and credits mismatches, vendor payouts, refunds, reversals, double charges.
- **Tech**  
  - Bugs, crashes, UI/UX glitches, OTP delivery or validation problems, performance issues.
- **Compliance/Risk**  
  - Suspected fraud, KYC issues, repeated chargebacks, legal notices, misuse of referral or coupon systems.

When in doubt, tag **Ops + Tech** first and mention that Finance/Compliance may be required.

### Strict do/don’t rules

<Callout kind="alert">

- Never **share OTPs**, passwords, or full card/bank details in tickets or Admin Panel notes.
- Never **promise refunds, extra payments, or incentives** without approval from Finance or the designated approver.
- Never **edit order statuses, payments, refunds, deactivations, or vendor reassignment** without following the defined approval hierarchy in Admin Panel.
- Avoid sharing **PII** (full card numbers, full bank account numbers, Aadhaar, PAN, etc.) in screenshots or text. Mask sensitive parts if you must share.
- Do not open multiple tickets for the **exact same root cause**; link all related examples to one master ticket.

</Callout>

### Escalation timing

- **P0**  
  - Immediately escalate through the fastest support channel plus a ticket.
  - Keep updating Admin Panel notes every time there is a new development.

- **P1**  
  - Raise ticket within **30 minutes** of confirmation.
  - Escalate in support channel if no response within agreed SLA (for example, a few working hours).

- **P2**  
  - Create ticket on the same day with 1–2 strong examples.
  - Bundle similar cases together (do not spam with one ticket per identical bug).

- **P3**  
  - Log as part of backlog or improvement list.
  - Attach at least one use case or feedback quote to justify the change.

## What good reporting looks like

A good issue report:

- Is **specific**: one problem per ticket.
- Is **traceable**: Admin Panel notes, logs, and ticket are linked.
- Is **reproducible**: someone else can follow steps and see the same issue.
- Is **safe**: no risky promises or sensitive data mishandling.
- Respects **approval hierarchy** for high‑risk actions like refunds and deactivation.

Use this page as your checklist whenever you are about to say, "I will raise this with our team."
