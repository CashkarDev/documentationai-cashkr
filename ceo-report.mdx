## Overview

**Sidebar path:** Dashboards → LEADERSHIP → **Executive** (`/dashboard/executive`)

The Executive dashboard is the full CXO report. A tab bar at the top switches between four consoles:

| Tab | Console | Documented in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CEO** | CEO Command Center | This page |
| **CFO** | CFO Console (P&L, GST, TDS, cash, compliance) | Finance dashboard page |
| **CMO** | Marketing Report (channels, campaigns, funnel, geo) | Marketing Report page |
| **COO** | Operations Console | This page (below) |

<Callout kind="info">
The **Leadership** dashboard's "Full CEO report" and "CFO report" links open this page. **Dashboards → Finance** opens the CFO tab directly.
</Callout>

## CEO Command Center

### Header and controls

| Control | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Date range** | Defaults to the last 30 days (for example *Sep 12, 2026 – Oct 12, 2026*). Scopes all period metrics. |
| **Refresh** | Reloads the data. |
| **AI Analyst Insight → Analyze Data** | Generates an AI-written summary of the current numbers. |

### Business health and key inputs

| Block | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Business Health** | A score out of 100 with a band (*At Risk* → *Strong*) on a colour bar. |
| **Key Inputs** | Margin %, Completion % and Runway (months). |
| **Order Activity** | Daily line chart of Orders Received, Completed and Failed. |

### KPI cards

Revenue, Net Profit, Cash in Bank (with change since baseline), GCV (avg per order), Orders Done, Burn Rate (per month), Expenses, Inventory Value (units split by B2B / Excess / Walk-in), Net Take Rate (Commission ÷ GMV), Runway (at current burn) and Repeat Customers (% of total users). Most cards show % change vs the previous period.

### Report sections

| Section | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Revenue Engine Breakdown** | Share of B2C Commission, B2C Buyback and B2B Buyback; Commission Revenue with lifetime total; Credits Earned, Orders Completed, Avg Credit Value, Conversion Rate, Failure Rate. |
| **Revenue vs Expenses Trend** | Net profit for the period, margin, and a previous-vs-current chart. **View details** opens the finance reports. |
| **Order Outcomes** | Completed vs failed vs in progress per device (Phone, Laptop, Mac, Tablet). |
| **Acquisition Funnel** | Leads → Orders Placed → Completed with step and overall conversion. |
| **Burn Rate & Cash Runway** | Burn rate, runway, burn/revenue, runway vs 12-month target, and **Where we burn** by expense category. |
| **Marketing Efficiency** | ROAS, Cost / Order, Cost / Completed, with ad-data sync age. |
| **Vendor Fulfillment** | Completed ÷ vendor visits, on an *At Risk* → *Strong* scale. |
| **Order Pipeline** | Total Leads, Orders Placed, In Progress, Completed, Failed, Reserved, Completion Rate, Failure Rate, and a by-device split. |
| **Operations & SLA** | Scheduled / Completed / Failed Pickups Today, Pickup Delayed, Requote Rate, failure breakdown (By Vendor / By CashKr / By Customer) and top cities. |
| **Vendor Health** | Active Vendors, Onboarded (period), Completion, Acceptance and Requote rates, Credit Pool, Avg Credits/Vendor, top vendors. |
| **Marketing & Customers** | Spend, orders, conversion, CAC (platform and our attribution), CPL, paid vs organic leads, total and new users, avg rating, reviews, open complaints, repeat rate. |
| **Inventory & Capital Risk** | Total value and units, turnover, dead stock, and aging buckets (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days). |
| **Cash Flow & Finance** | Cash balance, incoming, outgoing, net cash flow, vendor payouts due, top expenses. |
| **B2B & Store Ops** | B2B leads new/converted, active and closed deals; active stores, store orders, pending settlements, active tokens. |
| **Top / Worst Performer** | Best and worst vendor in the window. |
| **Active Alerts** | Critical and total alert count, for example dead stock, high vendor failures, pending vendor payouts, pickup delays. |
| **Metrics Legend** | Definitions of the metrics used on the page. |

## COO — Operations Console

Subtitle: *Throughput · Queues · SLA · Failures · Coverage · Vendor performance · Customer health*. Uses a 7-day **Trend period** picker.

| # | Section | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Today vs yesterday** | Orders Received, Completed, Failed, Vendor Utilization, Active Vendors (took 1+ order today), Live Vendors. Refreshes every 30 seconds. |
| 2 | **Live queues** | Pending Pickup, Unassigned, No Vendor Action, Overdue Pickup, Awaiting Payment — "work them down to zero". |
| 3 | **Order outcomes over time** | Daily Received / Completed / Failed chart. |
| 4 | **SLA performance** | Avg TAT (with median), On-time rate (within 24h), Completion rate, Requote rate, and a TAT distribution (under 2h, 2–6h, 6–24h, over 24h). |
| 5 | **Failure analysis** | Failures by source (Vendor / CashKr / Customer) and top failure reasons. |
| 6 | **Coverage gaps** | Cities that received orders but have zero live external vendors. |
| 7 | **Vendor performance** | Live, Active this period, Idle vendors; top vendors by completed orders. |
| 8 | **Customer health** | Avg rating, open complaints, top cancellation reasons. |
