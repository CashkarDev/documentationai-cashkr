## How Cashkr fits together

Cashkr connects customers, vendors, and internal teams across four main product surfaces. Each one focuses on a different part of the trade-in journey but they all share the same backend services for identity, pricing, and orders.

Shared backend services include:

- Phone-number and one-time password authentication for login and registration
- Device catalog and pricing rules that drive quotes across all surfaces
- Order, payment, and vendor management that keep requests in sync end to end

## Cashkr Website

The Cashkr Website is the public-facing entry point where people discover, evaluate, and sell their old devices online. It focuses on acquisition, education, and conversion into trade-in requests.

**Who uses it**

- New and returning customers who are not yet in any app
- Visitors arriving from marketing campaigns, search, or referrals
- Support agents who guide customers through the website flow during calls

**Primary responsibilities**

- Explain how Cashkr works, eligibility, and supported device categories
- Let customers check indicative prices for devices before committing
- Start trade-in flows that capture basic contact and device details
- Hand off authenticated customers to the Customer App where needed

**Main data it creates and consumes**

- Reads: device catalog, pricing rules, available pickup or drop-off areas
- Creates: leads, initial trade-in requests, and pre-authenticated sessions
- Updates: contact details tied to a phone number after OTP verification

Authentication on the Website uses a phone-number plus one-time password flow that is shared with the Customer App, so the same identity and sessions carry across channels.

## Customer App

The Customer App gives individuals a focused environment to manage the full lifecycle of their device trade-ins after they first engage on the Website or directly in the app.

**Who uses it**

- Customers who have created or want to create trade-in requests
- Returning users who want to track status, reschedule pickups, or add devices

**Primary responsibilities**

- Authenticate customers using the shared phone-number plus OTP flow
- Capture detailed device information, photos, and condition checks
- Show live status for each trade-in from request to payout
- Coordinate customer actions such as confirming pickup times or bank details

**Main data it creates and consumes**

- Reads: device catalog and pricing, live order status, vendor assignment, payout status
- Creates: new trade-in orders, device condition reports, customer preferences
- Updates: pickup slots, contact and payout information, cancellation or change requests

Because the Customer App sits on top of the same backend as the Website, any change to a trade-in is reflected in the Admin Panel and Vendor App in near real time.

## Vendor App

The Vendor App is for agents, store partners, and logistics vendors who turn online trade-in requests into completed pickups and verified devices.

**Who uses it**

- Field agents who travel to customers for pickups
- Store partners who accept in-person drop-offs
- Vendor managers who oversee on-the-ground operations

**Primary responsibilities**

- Receive assigned orders and routing details from the central system
- Validate device condition on-site against what the customer declared
- Capture final device checklists, photos, and acceptance or rejection decisions
- Confirm pickups, handovers, and cash or digital payouts where applicable

**Main data it creates and consumes**

- Reads: assigned orders, customer contact and address, expected device details and quoted price
- Creates: verification results, condition adjustments, pickup completion events
- Updates: order status, final approved price, payout confirmation, and vendor performance metrics

Data from the Vendor App feeds directly into the Admin Panel for quality checks, exception handling, and financial reconciliation.

## Admin Panel

The Admin Panel is the internal command center that coordinates leads, orders, vendors, pricing, and financial flows across Cashkr.

**Who uses it**

- Operations teams that manage order queues and exceptions
- Vendor and partner managers who oversee capacity and quality
- Finance teams that track payouts, settlements, and margins
- Support teams who respond to customer and vendor queries

**Primary responsibilities**

- Monitor and prioritize incoming leads and active orders
- Configure vendor coverage, assignment rules, and capacity
- Manage pricing strategies, device catalogs, and promotional programs
- Oversee payments to customers and vendors, and handle disputes

**Main data it creates and consumes**

- Reads: all orders, device and pricing data, vendor performance, payment history
- Creates: pricing updates, vendor records, manual adjustments or overrides
- Updates: order routing, escalations, refunds, and payout statuses

The Admin Panel is the main interface to backend services such as order management, catalog configuration, and financial reporting, ensuring that changes apply consistently to the Website, Customer App, and Vendor App.
