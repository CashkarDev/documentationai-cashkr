## Audience and purpose

For **Inbound Sales**, **Follow-Up**, **CX**, and **BD teams**.

Use this SOP to **professionally close a lead** when Cashkr **cannot service the customer’s pincode or device**, without damaging trust or brand perception, and to **capture demand for future expansion**.

---

## Core workflow: from check to closure

Use this standard flow whenever pickup is not available.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify serviceability in Admin Panel" icon="search">

  Go to **Admin Panel → Serviceable Areas / Order Assignment** and confirm:

  - You entered the **correct pincode**.
  - You selected the **correct device category**.
  - There is **no nearby pincode** where pickup is genuinely possible.

  <Callout kind="info">

  Do not assume unavailability. Always confirm in the Admin Panel before telling the customer pickup is not available.

  </Callout>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Communicate clearly with the customer" icon="users">

  Use the **approved closing script** to explain that pickup is not available, without giving false commitments or blaming the system. If a nearby pincode is serviceable and practical, offer that as an option.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the correct disposition in Admin Panel" icon="settings">

  In the lead disposition, select:

  - **Connected → Non-Serviceable Area**, or
  - **Connected → Waiting for Serviceability** (if this option exists and is appropriate).

  Add notes using the **mandatory notes format** so future teams understand what happened and that demand was flagged.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Log demand for BD and adjust marketing if needed" icon="bar-chart">

  Share the pincode, city, device type, and demand pattern with **BD / Area Management** so they can plan expansion. If there are multiple similar leads, inform **Marketing** so they can adjust or pause ads for that area.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Situations when pickup is not available

Pickup may not be available when:

- The **pincode is not serviceable**.
- There is **partial serviceability** (for example, some device types not supported).
- There is a **temporary vendor shortage**.
- The **device is not supported** or is **too old**.
- There are **safety or operational restrictions** in the area.

---

## Mandatory verification before closing

Before telling the customer that pickup is not available, always verify serviceability.

**Path:** Admin Panel → **Serviceable Areas / Order Assignment**

Confirm:

- The **pincode** is correct.
- The **device category** is correct.
- No **nearby serviceable pincode** is realistically possible for the customer.

<Callout kind="info">

Do not skip this check. Wrong pincode or device selection can lead to lost business and poor customer experience.

</Callout>

## Approved customer-facing scripts

Use these exact scripts or stay very close to the wording. Keep your tone calm and polite.

### Primary closing script (recommended)

> **Agent:**  
> "I have checked this for you. Currently, we do not have pickup service available in your area for this device."  
>  
> *Pause*  
>  
> "We do not want to give you false commitments. As soon as service opens in your area, we will be happy to help."

### If the customer asks “When will it be available?”

> **Agent:**  
> "Right now, I cannot share an exact timeline, but I will make sure your request is shared with our expansion team."

### If a nearby pincode is serviceable

Use this **only if genuinely possible** and practical for the customer.

> **Agent:**  
> "If you have access to a nearby serviceable pincode, we can proceed with pickup there."

---

## What not to say (strictly prohibited)

<Callout kind="alert">

Do not give false hope, vague promises, or misleading reasons. The following are **strictly prohibited**:

- "Vendor will be arranged somehow"  
- "Try placing the order, we will see later"  
- "Pickup might happen, not sure"  
- "System issue, call later"

**Rule:** No false hope. No vague promises.

</Callout>

## Admin Panel disposition and notes

### Correct disposition

Set the disposition to:

- **Connected → Non-Serviceable Area**, or  
- **Connected → Waiting for Serviceability** (if this option exists and fits the situation).

### Mandatory notes format

Use this format for notes so that CX, BD, and Ops can understand the situation later:

```text
Pickup unavailable – pincode not serviceable for [device type].
Customer informed politely.
Demand flagged for BD.
```

Replace `[device type]` with the actual device category (for example, "iPhone", "Android phone", "laptop").

## Internal escalation and demand logging

### For BD / Area Management team

Sales/CX must log:

- **Pincode**
- **City**
- **Device type**
- **Lead volume pattern** (for example, "3–5 similar leads per week")

BD uses this information to:

- Identify **expansion opportunities**.
- **Onboard vendors** in high-demand areas.
- Prioritize **city/pincode activation**.

### For Marketing team

When you see multiple leads from non-serviceable areas:

- Inform **Marketing** so they can **pause or adjust ads** for that location and avoid misleading users.

## Handling customer pushback

Stay calm, acknowledge the concern once, and then close politely.

### Objection: “Why are you running ads then?”

> **Agent:**  
> "Thanks for pointing that out. We are continuously updating our coverage. I will make sure this feedback is shared internally so we do not mislead users."

### Objection: “Other apps are coming here.”

> **Agent:**  
> "That is possible. At Cashkr, we only open areas when we are confident about quality and payment safety."

## When to use callback vs closure

Use these rules to decide whether to **close the lead** or **schedule further action**.

- **Area permanently not serviceable**  
  - **Action:** Close the lead.  
  - Set disposition to **Connected → Non-Serviceable Area** and add mandatory notes.  
  - Log demand for BD if there is repeated interest from that pincode.

- **Temporary vendor shortage**  
  - **Action:** Use **callback / CX follow-up**.  
  - Tell the customer there is a short-term operational issue and that you will follow up.  
  - Set the appropriate waiting or callback disposition as per your calling SOP.

- **Partial serviceability** (for example, some devices or variants supported, others not)  
  - **Action:** Explain clearly which devices or variants are supported.  
  - Offer **available alternatives** if any (for example, different device category that is serviceable).  
  - If their specific need cannot be met, close the lead with correct disposition and notes.

- **High-demand non-serviceable area**  
  - **Action:** Close the lead **and** flag strongly to BD.  
  - Highlight that there is consistent demand from this pincode or city so it can be prioritized for expansion.

## CX involvement when the customer is upset

If the customer is unhappy:

- **Apologize once** for the inconvenience.
- **Explain honestly** that the area or device is currently not serviceable.
- **Do not** escalate as a complaint unless there is a separate service issue.
- **Close politely** using the approved scripts.

If the situation feels sensitive or the customer is highly escalated, follow your standard **CX escalation flow** for additional support while keeping the same message: pickup is not available today.

## One-page quick summary (training card)

Use this as a quick mental checklist during calls.

**When pickup is not available:**

1. Verify serviceability in the **Admin Panel**.  
2. Inform the customer **honestly** using the approved script.  
3. Do **not** over-promise or give vague timelines.  
4. Close the lead with the **correct disposition** and **mandatory notes**.  
5. **Log demand** for BD and inform Marketing if many such leads are coming from the same area.

**Goal:**  
Protect **trust today** → Enable **expansion tomorrow**.
