## Overview

Customer notifications keep users informed at every stage of the Cashkr journey, from order creation to payout and account updates. Use this page as the single reference for message content across WhatsApp, push, SMS, and email.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Each notification section below includes tabs for each delivery channel:

  - **WhatsApp**: Message copy sent via WhatsApp.

  - **Push**: Title, body, and CTA used for in-app push notifications.

  - **SMS**: Text message content, including OTPs and critical alerts.

  - **Email**: Subject/body where applicable or a link to the full template.

  Dynamic placeholders such as `\{name}`, `\{device_name}`, and `\{otp_code}` must be substituted by your notification service at send time. Keep the placeholder formats exactly as shown so they match your implementation.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The **Status** label on each notification indicates rollout state:

  - **live**: Currently active in production and wired to the corresponding event.

  - **Not live**: Draft or planned template that is not yet triggered automatically. Use these only for testing or after coordinating activation.
</Callout>
