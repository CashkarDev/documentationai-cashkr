## Overview

Open **B2C Sales → Outcomes → Lost Leads**. The page lists everything closed as lost in the selected period and explains why, so pricing and operations can act on the reasons.

## Filters

| Control | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Reason** dropdown | **All reasons**, then reasons grouped by owning function: **Pricing** (Better Price Elsewhere, Higher Expectations, Condition Deduction Disputed), **Trust and Process** (Doesn't Trust Online Buyback), **Availability** (Area Not Serviceable), **Customer Side** (Not Selling Anymore, Sold Elsewhere, Was Just Checking, Unreachable After 3 Attempts, Wrong Number), **Product and Tech** (App Issue During Sell Flow), **Retention** (Declined Revised Price, Already Sold Elsewhere After Failure, Lost Trust After Failed Pickup, Refused To Re-book, Unreachable After 3 Attempts, Do Not Contact, Device Not Eligible) |
| **Source** chips | **All**, **Retention** (failed orders), **Leads** |
| **Date range** | From / to date (defaults to the last 7 days) |

The count of lost records is shown next to a **CSV** export button.

## What the screen shows

### Lost list

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Lost On** | When it was closed as lost |
| **Customer** | Customer |
| **Device** | Device model |
| **Quoted** | Quoted price |
| **Reason** | Lost reason |
| **Closed By** | Who closed it |

When nothing was lost the table shows *No lost leads in this period.*

### Ringing — still being called, not lost

Shows how many **leads** and **calls that rang out** were marked *Not Connected → Ringing* in the period. These leads are **not** closed — they stay in **Callbacks** and are not part of the lost count or the reasons.

### Reason breakdown

How many were closed in the period, grouped by reason, with the owning function shown against each reason. When nothing was closed it shows *Nothing to break down yet.*

### Competitor gap

Built from closures where the competitor platform and their price were captured. *This is what goes to pricing.* When there are none it shows *No priced competitor losses in this period.*

<Callout kind="info">
  Capture the competitor platform and price whenever a customer says they got a better price elsewhere — it feeds the Competitor gap panel.
</Callout>
