## Overview

Use the Strapi Homepage to quickly find and open recently edited or published articles for the Cashkr website.

## What it shows

The dashboard displays recently edited and published articles so you can access active content without searching through the full list.

## Common tasks

Use these steps to work with the Strapi homepage as an admin or editor.

<Steps>
<Step title="Log in and open the Strapi homepage">

Sign in to the Strapi admin panel to access the Homepage dashboard for Cashkr web articles.

</Step>
<Step title="Review Last Edits and Published Articles">

Review the "Last Edits" section to see content that was updated recently and the "Published Articles" section to see the most recently live content.

</Step>
<Step title="Open an article in the Content Manager">

Click an entry in either section to open it in the Content Manager and review or edit the article.

</Step>
</Steps>

## System logic

The homepage list updates in real time based on the most recent content changes and publication activity.

## Connections

The Homepage is integrated with the Content Manager to show article data and is linked to the website article API to reflect published content.
