## Overview

After you complete an order, you can upload a photo of the customer's review to earn a 5% credit reward. Your submission goes through admin verification before credit is granted.

## When you can upload

The **Upload Review** option appears only after the order moves to **Completed**.

<Callout kind="info">

If the order is still **In Progress** or **Out for Pickup**, you will not see **Upload Review**.

Complete the order first, then return to the order to upload the review photo. For the completion flow, see [Complete](/complete).

</Callout>

## Step-by-step: upload a review

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the completed order in your order history" icon="camera">

- Go to your completed orders or order history in the Vendor App.
- Open the order where you want to claim the review reward.
- Confirm the order is already marked **Completed**.

</Step>

<Step title="Tap Upload Review" icon="upload">

- Open the order details screen.
- Tap **Upload Review**.
- If the option does not appear, check that the order is completed.

</Step>

<Step title="Capture or select a clear review photo" icon="image">

- Take a new photo or choose one from your device.
- Make sure the customer's review is readable and fully visible.
- Use a supported image format such as **jpg** or **png**.

</Step>

<Step title="Submit the photo" icon="check">

- Review the image before submitting.
- Tap submit to send the review photo for verification.
- After submission, the order status changes to **SUBMITTED**.

</Step>

<Step title="Wait for admin verification" icon="clock">

- Admin reviews the photo in the background.
- Return to the same order view to track the current review status.
- If approved, your reward is added to your available credit.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">

Upload a clear, well-lit photo with the full review visible. Clear images are less likely to be rejected during verification.

</Callout>

## Review states and what they mean

Your review submission moves through a small set of states until admin makes a final decision.

| State | What it means | What you see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SUBMITTED | Awaiting admin review | Your photo is uploaded and pending |
| APPROVED | Verified valid, 5% credit granted | Credit added to your balance, status shows approved with amount |
| REJECTED | Verified invalid, no credit | Status shows rejected with reason from admin |

## How the 5% credit works

Approved review refunds add credit to your account based on the device's credit value. The system calculates your reward as device credit value × 5% and adds that amount to your total available credit.

If a device has a credit value of `30`, your review reward is `1.5` credit. After approval, you can check the updated amount in your credit section or on the [Buy Credits](/buy-credits) page.

## Common states and errors

Use this table to quickly identify why a review reward is blocked or delayed.

| Issue | Cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upload Review not visible | Order not yet completed | Complete the order first |
| Photo upload fails | Network or file size issue | Check your connection and make sure the photo is within the size limit in jpg or png format |
| Submission already exists | You already uploaded for this order | One submission is allowed per order; check the current status instead |
| Rejected — can I retry? | Depends on admin policy | If re-upload is allowed, you will see the option again |
| Credit not showing after approval | Credit may take a moment to reflect | Check the Buy Credits section; if it still does not appear, contact support |
