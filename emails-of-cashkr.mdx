## Quick chooser

Pick the right address for the scenario you are working on:

- **Order updates, OTPs, or password resets** → `noreply@cashkr.com`
- **New user onboarding or product education series** → `hello@mail.cashkr.com`
- **Discount campaigns, festive promotions, or win-back flows** → `offers@promo.cashkr.com`
- **Customer replies from ticketing tools or direct support emails** → `support@cashkr.com` (TBD: confirm final setup)
- **DNS, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC policy monitoring** → `dmarc@cashkr.com` (reports only, never visible to customers)
