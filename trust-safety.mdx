## Overview

**Sidebar path:** Dashboards → OPERATING → Vendors → **Trust** tab (`/dashboard/vendors-analytics?tab=trust`)

**Trust & Safety** covers fraud detection, vendor compliance and an action center for suspicious orders.

## Filters

| Control | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Date range** | Defaults to the last 30 days. |
| **Refresh** | Reloads the data. |
| **AI Analyst Insight → Analyze Data** | Generates an AI summary. |

## What the screen shows

### Trust Health

A score with a *Critical* → *Healthy* band, based on resolution rate, suspicious rate and vendor health. Shows % resolved, % suspicious, unresolved count and vendor flags.

### KPI cards

| Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Lead Flags** | Flags raised on leads (with resolved count). |
| **Suspicious** | Suspicious completed orders (count and % of completed). |
| **Vendor Flags** | Flags raised on vendors. |
| **Avg Resolution** | Average time to resolve a flag. |
| **Resolution Rate** | Share of flags resolved. |
| **Unresolved** | Open flags. |
| **Vendor Health** | Share of vendors that are live. |
| **Suspicious Rate** | Suspicious orders ÷ completed. |

### Panels

| Panel | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Suspicious Breakdown** | New Phone Drops, Fake Requotes, Condition Jumps, Drastic Drops, Screen Issues. |
| **Problem Types** | Lead flag types. |
| **Vendor Health** | Vendors by status: Live, On Hold, Pending, Internal and others. |
| **Flag Reasons** | Vendor flag reasons. |
| **Daily Safety Trend** | Lead flags and vendor flags per day. |

### Action tables

Switch between **Suspicious Orders**, **Risky Vendors**, **Unresolved Flags** and **Compliance Issues**.

The **Suspicious Orders** table lists flagged orders: Order ID, Model, Vendor, Generated price, Final price, Drop %, Flags (for example *Fake Requote*, *New Phone Drop*, *Condition Jump*), City, Date and Actions.

<Callout kind="info">
A negative **Drop** means the final price was higher than the generated quote — a common sign of a fake requote.
</Callout>

## Actions

On a suspicious order, click the **Order ID** or the icon in the **Actions** column to open it for review. The tab itself does not change any data. Fraud records themselves are managed under **System → Admin → Stolen Devices** and **Fraud & Trust Audit**.
