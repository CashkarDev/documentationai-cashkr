## Overview

This ensures consistent communication, faster resolutions, and better conversions.

## Customer journey stages

A customer goes through 8 major stages:

- Landing on website/app
- Choosing device type
- Selecting Brand → Model → Variant
- Picking device condition options
- Getting the price
- Entering details and checkout
- Order confirmation
- Pickup and payment process

<Steps>

  <Step title="Stage 1 — Landing on the Cashkr website/app" icon="home">

  Customers typically arrive through:

  - Google Ads and SEO
  - Social media
  - Direct type-in
  - Referral link
  - App Store or Play Store

  **What the customer sees on landing:**

  - Search bar with primary action like: Sell your device
  - Device categories: Phone, Laptop, Tablet, MacBook
  - Popular brands and top models
  - FAQs and trust elements such as instant payment and doorstep pickup

  **Key CX/Sales notes:**

  - Ensure customers land on the correct device category.
  - If a user is confused, guide them to start by selecting device type.
  - Explain Cashkr in simple language: "You get instant payment at home after inspection."

  </Step>

  <Step title="Stage 2 — Selecting device type" icon="monitor">

  The customer chooses a broad device category:

  - Phones
  - Laptops
  - MacBooks
  - Tablets

  **What the customer does:**

  - Clicks on the relevant device type before searching for brand or model.

  **Marketing role:**

  - Ensure device categories are clearly visible and easy to tap or click.

  **Sales/CX guidance:**

  - When a customer asks "Where do I enter my model?", direct them first to the device type page.
  - Confirm that they have selected the right category before moving ahead.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Stage 3 — Choose Brand → Model → Variant" icon="chevron-right">

  The journey is funnel-based:

  - Customer selects **Brand** (for example, Apple, Samsung, OnePlus, Dell, HP).
  - Customer selects **Model** (for example, iPhone 13, Dell Inspiron).
  - Customer selects **Variant** (for example, 128GB, 256GB, i5, 8GB RAM).

  **What the customer sees/does:**

  - A stepwise flow where each selection filters the next list.
  - Clear lists or tiles of brands, then models, then variants.

  <Callout kind="alert">

  **Missing model handling — Sales/CX rule:**

  - If device is newly launched and expected to be supported → raise ticket to pricing team.
  - If device is very old or obsolete → treat as not supported and explain to customer.

  </Callout>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Stage 4 — Customer selects condition options" icon="settings">

  This step affects pricing directly.

  **For phones, customer selects:**

  - Screen condition
  - Body condition
  - Battery health
  - Camera working
  - Face ID or Touch ID
  - Physical and functional issues

  **For laptops and MacBooks, customer selects:**

  - Display condition
  - Keyboard and trackpad
  - Battery backup
  - Hinges
  - Body dents
  - Charger availability

  **CX/Sales role:**

  - Guide customers to choose honestly, because price changes after inspection.
  - Reinforce that accurate condition leads to smoother pickup and fewer disputes.

  **Marketing role:**

  - Ensure the UI is clear with tooltips and helper text like:
    - "Select the condition closest to your actual device."

  </Step>

  <Step title="Stage 5 — Customer gets the price" icon="bar-chart">

  After selecting all conditions, the system:

  - Calculates the price instantly.
  - Shows it clearly as **Estimated Price**.
  - Sets the amount the customer expects to receive.

  **What the customer sees:**

  - A prominent estimated price for their device.
  - A clear call-to-action to proceed to checkout or continue.

  <Callout kind="alert">

  **Important pricing rules:**

  - Price is not locked as per Cashkr policy.
  - Vendor inspection may change the final price based on actual condition.
  - If a customer says "XYZ offers more", Sales asks for their quote screenshot to try price match.

  </Callout>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Stage 6 — Checkout and customer details" icon="edit">

  The customer proceeds to the checkout form and enters:

  - Name
  - Phone number
  - Pickup address
  - Pincode
  - Date and time preference

  **Marketing role:**

  - Optimize the form for higher conversion.
  - Reduce friction by keeping fields clear and necessary.

  **CX/Sales role:**

  - Help customers complete the form if they are stuck.
  - If a customer says "pickup not available", follow the pincode resolution SOP.
  - Confirm the correct pincode to reduce vendor issues later.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Stage 7 — Order confirmation" icon="check-circle">

  After submission:

  - The customer sees an order confirmation screen.
  - The system sends SMS or WhatsApp messages with order details.
  - Vendor receives a notification in the vendor app.

  **Internal order states to know:**

  - Order Created
  - Assigned or Unassigned
  - Vendor Accepted
  - Vendor Enroute

  **Sales/CX responsibilities:**

  - May call the customer for confirmation based on internal flow.
  - Inform the customer that they can:
    - Edit the address before the vendor reaches.
    - Reschedule if required.
    - Cancel the order anytime.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Stage 8 — Pickup and payment journey (vendor side)" icon="truck">

  From the customer point of view, the vendor handles:

  **A. Vendor calls customer**

  - Confirms address and timing.
  - Shares estimated arrival.

  **B. Vendor reaches home**

  - Checks device physically.
  - App recalculates the final price based on inspection.

  **C. Customer confirms**

  - If customer agrees → payment happens.
  - If not → vendor marks fail reason and customer is not charged.

  **D. OTP-based instant payment**

  - Vendor taps "Pay Now" in the app.
  - Customer receives an OTP.
  - Payment happens instantly to the customer bank account after OTP verification.
  - Vendor collects the device.

  **E. Order completed**

  - Order status moves to completed and device is logged as collected.

  **CX role during this stage:**

  - Handle escalations such as:
    - Vendor delayed.
    - Price mismatch confusion.
    - Payment SMS delay.
    - Customer safety concerns during pickup.

  </Step>

</Steps>

## Common customer questions — quick answer guide

<ExpandableGroup>

  <Expandable title="Is price fixed?">

  A: Price may change after inspection.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="When will vendor come?">

  A: Same-day or next-day depending on availability.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How will I get paid?">

  A: Instant bank transfer after OTP verification.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Is Cashkr safe?">

  A: Yes, Cashkr uses OTP-secured payments.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I sell without charger or box?">

  A: Yes, price adjusts accordingly.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Is doorstep pickup free?">

  A: Yes, there are no hidden charges.

  </Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

Marketing, Sales, and CX must all give consistent answers.

## Role of each team in the customer journey

<Tabs>

  <Tab title="Marketing Team" icon="megaphone">

  - Increase awareness of Cashkr and the sell device flow.
  - Reduce drop-offs across landing, selection, and checkout.
  - Create trust through testimonials, badges, and reviews.
  - Improve landing page and app home performance.

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Sales Team" icon="users">

  - Convert leads into orders.
  - Handle objections and price comparisons.
  - Explain pricing clearly, including estimated vs final price.
  - Assist customers who are stuck anywhere in the flow.

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="CX Team" icon="help-circle">

  - Solve customer problems at any journey stage.
  - Assist during order status changes (creation, assignment, pickup, completion).
  - Handle complaints, escalations, and refund-related queries.
  - Coordinate with vendors and internal teams for resolution.

  </Tab>

</Tabs>

All three teams together create a frictionless experience.

Website/App → Device selection → Condition selection → Get price → Fill details → Create order → Vendor assigned → Pickup → Inspection → OTP payment → Completion
