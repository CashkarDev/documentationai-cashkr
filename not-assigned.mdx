## Overview

The Not Assigned page in **Admin Panel → Orders - B2C → Not Assigned** shows all orders that customers created or that were converted from leads but are not yet picked up or assigned to any vendor. Admins use this view to monitor unassigned orders and act before they become further delayed.

<Callout kind="info">
  An order qualifies as **Not Assigned** when it is visible in the vendor app for the relevant pincode and device type, but **no vendor has accepted, picked up, or been manually assigned to the order yet**.
</Callout>

## What the Not Assigned page shows

Each row in the Not Assigned list represents one order that is currently waiting to be picked up by a vendor. The key columns are:

- **Created At** — Date and time when the order was created.
- **Model** — Device model the customer wants to sell.
- **City** — City where the pickup needs to happen.
- **Type** — Device category: Mobile, Laptop, Tablet, or Mac.
- **Status** — Current status of the order: **Generated** or **Pickup Delayed**.

### Order statuses on this page

- **Generated** — The order has been created and is visible to vendors in the vendor app based on matching pincode and device type. No vendor has accepted or acted on it yet.
- **Pickup Delayed** — The scheduled pickup date has passed and no vendor has picked up the order. For example, if a customer created an order on 20 March with a pickup date of 24 March and no vendor completed the pickup by 24 March, the order status automatically changes to Pickup Delayed after that date. The order remains available for vendors to pick up or for admin to manually assign.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Treat **Pickup Delayed** orders as priority. These customers have already passed their expected pickup date, so delays here directly impact service experience.
</Callout>

## Why orders appear in Not Assigned

An order appears in the Not Assigned page when:

- No vendor in the customer's pincode has accepted the order yet.
- There are no live vendors covering the customer's pincode and device type.
- Vendors in the area saw the order but skipped or ignored it.
- The pickup date has passed without any vendor action, resulting in **Pickup Delayed** status.

## Filters and controls

Use the filters and controls at the top of the Not Assigned page to narrow down which orders you see:

- **Device Type Filter** — Filter orders by device category (Mobile, Laptop, Tablet, or Mac).
- **City Filter** — Filter orders by the city where the pickup is required.
- **From Date / To Date** — Filter orders by their creation date range.
- **Show Orders with Test Accounts** — Toggle to include test account orders in the view. Turn this **off** during normal operations to see only real customer orders.
- **Search** — Search for a specific order using order details, device name, or customer information.
- **Apply Filters** — Apply the selected filter values to update the order list.
- **Reset** — Clear all filters and return to the default view.
- **Pagination Controls** — Navigate across pages of results using Previous and Next. The total result count appears below the table.

<Callout kind="alert">
  During day-to-day operations, keep **Show Orders with Test Accounts** turned off so you do not mix real customer orders with internal test data.
</Callout>

## Admin actions on Not Assigned orders

For orders sitting in Not Assigned, especially those with **Pickup Delayed** status, admins should:

- **Manually assign vendors** — Open the order detail and assign it to a specific vendor.
- **Nudge existing vendors** — Contact vendors in the matching pincode and prompt them to accept the order.
- **Reassign to nearby areas** — Assign the order to a vendor from a nearby pincode if none are available in the exact pincode.
- **Reschedule with customers** — Contact the customer to reschedule the pickup appointment when needed.
- **Escalate operational gaps** — Escalate to the operations team for cities or pincodes with consistently unassigned orders.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Always inform the customer when an order becomes **Pickup Delayed** or needs rescheduling. Proactive communication helps maintain trust even when pickups are late.
</Callout>

## Order flow for Not Assigned orders

These steps show how an order moves through the Not Assigned state before it becomes In Progress.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Customer places order" icon="download">
    The customer creates an order on the website or app and selects a preferred pickup date and time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order becomes visible to vendors" icon="monitor">
    The order appears in the vendor app for vendors whose pincode and device type settings match the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order waits for vendor action" icon="users">
    If no vendor accepts or picks up the order before the scheduled pickup date, it remains unassigned in the Not Assigned view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Status changes to Pickup Delayed" icon="alert-triangle">
    When the scheduled pickup date passes without completion, the order status automatically updates to **Pickup Delayed** but stays available for vendors or manual assignment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vendor pickup or manual assignment" icon="settings">
    A vendor eventually accepts the order from the vendor app, or an admin manually assigns the order to a vendor from the admin panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order moves to In Progress" icon="rocket">
    As soon as a vendor is assigned (via app or admin action), the order exits Not Assigned and moves to the **In Progress** queue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Admin responsibilities

Use the Not Assigned view as a daily operational queue:

- Check the Not Assigned page every day to spot orders without a vendor.
- Prioritise **Pickup Delayed** orders, as these have already crossed the customer's expected pickup date.
- Manually assign delayed orders to available vendors to prevent further dissatisfaction.
- Monitor the count badge on **Not Assigned** in the sidebar; a rising count suggests a coverage gap in a specific city or pincode.
- Cross-check with the **Expansion → Area** page to confirm vendor coverage for affected pincodes.

## Connections to other modules

Understanding how Not Assigned links to other modules helps you resolve root causes, not just symptoms:

- **In Progress** — Once a vendor accepts or is manually assigned to the order, it moves from Not Assigned to **In Progress**.
- **Vendors - B2C** — Vendor pincode and device type assignments control which orders each vendor can see and accept.
- **Expansion → Area** — Pincode serviceability and vendor coverage here directly affect whether orders get assigned or stay in Not Assigned.
- **Leads** — Orders converted from leads appear in Not Assigned if no vendor has been assigned yet.
- **Quality - B2C** — Prolonged unassigned or Pickup Delayed orders can negatively impact overall service quality metrics.

## Important notes

An order with **Pickup Delayed** status is still active and visible in the vendor app. The delayed status is informational, not a cancellation, and vendors can pick it up at any time.

If an order remains unassigned for a long period, investigate whether there is a vendor coverage gap in that pincode and work with the Expansion team to onboard new vendors. Combine this with customer communication to keep expectations clear when delays occur.
