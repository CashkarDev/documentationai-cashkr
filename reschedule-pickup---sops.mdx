## Overview

Reschedule Pickup lets customers change the scheduled pickup date and time for an order directly from the Track Orders page when the order is still unassigned to a vendor.

## Availability

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Rescheduling is available only while the order is **unassigned** to any vendor.\
  Once a **vendor is assigned**, the Reschedule button is disabled and the customer cannot change the pickup slot.
</Callout>

## Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Track Orders page" icon="list-ordered" title-type="p">
    Go to the **Track Orders** page on the website and locate the order that the customer wants to reschedule.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open order details" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    Click on the **Details** of that order to open the order details view.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click the Reschedule button" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    In the order details view, click the **Reschedule** button.\
    The button is visible only if the order is still unassigned to any vendor.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select new date and time" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    Choose the new **pickup date** and **pickup time** from the reschedule interface.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the updated slot" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    Click **Save** to confirm the new pickup date and time.\
    The order updates with the new schedule and the rescheduled slot appears in the order details.
  </Step>
</Steps>
