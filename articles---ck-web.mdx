## Overview

Manage all Cashkr website articles in the Strapi admin panel. From here you search, filter, create, edit, publish, and delete articles that appear on the website.

## What it shows

The Articles collection in Strapi displays:

- A **list of all articles**, including drafts and published content.
- Article **metadata**, such as titles and categories.
- A **search and filter** section to find specific articles.
- An **Add new article** button to create new entries.

## Common tasks

Use these steps for the typical flow of managing articles in Strapi.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the Articles collection" icon="folder">

- Log in to the Strapi admin panel.
- Navigate to the **Content Manager**.
- Select the **Articles** collection type from the sidebar.

</Step>

<Step title="Search and filter articles" icon="search">

- Use the **search bar** to find articles by title or other visible fields.
- Apply **filters** to narrow the list by status (draft/published), category, or other available fields.

</Step>

<Step title="Create a new article" icon="plus">

- Click the **Create new entry** or **Add new article** button.
- Fill in the article fields, such as title, content, and category.
- Save the entry as a **draft** or **publish** it based on your needs.

</Step>

<Step title="Edit an existing article" icon="edit">

- Click an article in the list to open it.
- Update the required fields.
- Save or publish the changes so they reflect on the website.

</Step>

<Step title="Delete an article" icon="trash">

- Select the article you want to remove.
- Confirm the deletion when prompted.
- After confirmation, the article is removed from both Strapi and the website.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Drafts vs published

<Callout kind="info">

Articles remain in **draft** until you publish them. Once you save and publish an article, updates are live on the website after saving.

</Callout>

- Use **draft** status while creating or reviewing content.
- Switch to **published** when the article is ready for the website.

## Deleting articles

<Callout kind="alert">

When you delete an article and confirm the action, Strapi removes it and it no longer appears on the website. Deletion requires confirmation to avoid accidental removal.

</Callout>

- Delete only when you are sure the article is no longer needed.
- If you want to temporarily hide content, keep the article and switch it back to **draft** instead of deleting.

## Connections

- The Articles collection is **integrated with the website article API**, so changes made to any article are directly reflected in the website article section.
- Articles are **linked to the Media Library** for embedded media such as images and other assets.
