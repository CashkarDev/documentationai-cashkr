## Overview

Refer & Earn lets you earn rewards by inviting your friends to use Cashkr from the customer app.  
When your friend registers using your referral code and completes an order, both of you earn rewards.

You find Refer & Earn in the **Customer App** menu. From there, you generate your referral code, share it, and track your referrals and rewards.

<Callout kind="info">

Key program rules:

- Each customer has one **permanent referral code** generated from the Refer & Earn section.
- A **new customer can use a referral code only once**, and the code is **locked to their account**.
- Rewards for both users trigger **only after order completion** and when all program conditions are met.
- The **referrer reward is pending** after order completion and is credited to the wallet **after admin approval**.

</Callout>

## How Refer & Earn works

Use these steps as the standard referral flow for the customer app.

<Steps>

<Step title="Generate your referral code" icon="plus">

- Open the Cashkr **Customer App**.
- Go to the **Refer & Earn** section.
- Tap **Generate code**.

The app generates your **unique referral code** instantly. This code is permanent and works for all active referral programs.

</Step>

<Step title="Share your referral code" icon="share-2">

- From the Refer & Earn screen, share your code using:

  - WhatsApp  
  - SMS  
  - Social media  
  - Copy and share link

Your code is the same across all channels, and every friend you invite should register using this code.

</Step>

<Step title="Friend registers using your code" icon="users">

- Your friend installs or opens the Cashkr customer app.
- During registration, they **enter your referral code**.
- After successful registration:

  - The referral code is **locked to their account**.
  - They cannot change it or apply another referral code later.

A referral code is valid **only once per new customer**. If the friend has already used any referral code in the past, they cannot apply another.

</Step>

<Step title="Order completion and reward creation" icon="check-circle">

- Your friend completes a **sell device** order in the Cashkr app.
- Once the vendor completes the order at pickup, Cashkr:

  - Applies the referee reward (discount/benefit) **automatically at checkout** based on the active referral offer and the **device generated price**.
  - Generates your referrer reward based on the **final price on which the order is completed by the vendor**.

From this point:

- The **referee reward** is approved instantly.
- The **referrer reward** appears as **pending** until admin approval.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Reward behavior

### Referee reward (your friend)

The referee reward is the benefit your friend gets when they use a referral code and complete an eligible order.

- The app applies the reward **automatically at checkout** when the friend places an order using the account that has your referral code.
- The **discount or benefit depends on the active referral offer** at that time.
- The reward is **calculated based on the device generated price**.
- The **vendor pays the final amount**, reflecting any referee discount.
- The referee reward is **approved instantly** once the order is confirmed with the vendor.

If the order is cancelled or fails, no referee reward is applied.

### Referrer reward (you)

The referrer reward is what you earn when your friend, who registered with your code, completes an eligible order.

- After your friend completes the order:

  - The system **generates your reward**.
  - The reward appears in the Refer & Earn tracking view as **pending**.

- After **admin approval**:

  - The reward is **credited to your Cashkr Wallet**.
  - The amount is **calculated based on the final price** on which the vendor completes the order, not just the initial device estimate.

If the order is not completed or does not meet program conditions, your reward does not move to approved or wallet credit.

## Where to see referral earnings

### Refer & Earn section in the customer app

Use the Refer & Earn screen to track referral-level details:

- See your **referral code**.
- View the list of **referred users**.
- Check **reward status** for each referral (for example, pending or completed).

### Wallet section in the customer app

Use the Wallet to see your overall balances, including earnings from referrals and other eligible sources.

- **Total balance**: Amount currently available to withdraw.
- **Total earned**: Lifetime earnings, including all approved referral rewards.
- **Total withdrawn**: Amount you have already withdrawn from your wallet.

Referral rewards that are still **pending** do not appear in total balance but may be visible in detailed referral tracking.

## Important Refer & Earn rules

These rules are enforced across the customer app experience:

- Each customer has **one permanent referral code**, generated once from the Refer & Earn section.
- A **new customer can use a referral code only once** when they register.
- After registration, the code is **locked to that account**; they cannot change or reuse another code.
- **Deleting and re-registering with the same number or device does not reset referral eligibility**. If the user already used a referral code once, they cannot use another code even after reinstalling or reregistering.
- Cashkr gives rewards **only after order completion** and when all **program conditions** are satisfied.
- Referrer rewards **always require admin approval** before they are credited to the wallet.

If any of these rules are not met, the system should not grant new referral benefits.

## Common states and errors

This section describes how common situations should appear in the customer app and what they mean.

### Referral code already used

Symptoms:

- The friend tries to enter a referral code during registration and sees a message that the code cannot be applied.

Behavior:

- The system detects that:

  - The friend has already used a referral code before, or  
  - The current flow does not allow applying a referral code.

- In both cases, **do not attach a new referral** to this customer, and do not create any new rewards.

### Friend not eligible for referral

Symptoms:

- Friend does not see the referral input field at signup, or the app blocks code entry.

Behavior:

- Treat the friend as **not eligible** for referral benefits, for example:

  - Existing user trying to re-register to gain referral benefits.
  - User whose number or device is already tagged against a previous referral.

- Do not generate referrer or referee rewards, even if a code is typed somewhere else in the flow.

### Order not completed yet (pending rewards)

Symptoms:

- In Refer & Earn, you see referrals with **pending** status.
- In the Wallet, the total balance is unchanged.

Behavior:

- The **friend has registered with your code and placed an order**, but the vendor has not completed it yet or admin has not approved the reward.
- Keep the referrer reward in **pending** state until:

  - The vendor completes the order at the final price, and  
  - Admin approves the reward.

Once approved, mark the reward as completed and **credit the amount to the wallet**. Only then should the Wallet total balance increase.

## Developer and tester notes

<Callout kind="tip">

Use this section to align implementation and QA behavior across website and customer app flows.

</Callout>

- **Referral code generation**: Generate the code only from the **Refer & Earn** section in the customer app and store it as a **permanent identifier** against the customer.
- **Code locking**: When a new customer registers with a referral code, **lock the code to that customer** and prevent any future changes, even after reinstall or re-registration with the same mobile number.
- **Single-use rule**: Enforce that **each new customer consumes exactly one referral code at most once**, and block any additional referral attempts.
- **Reward calculation**:

  - For the referee, calculate the benefit based on the **device generated price** and the **active referral offer** at checkout.
  - For the referrer, calculate the reward based on the **final order completion price recorded by the vendor**, not on the initial quote.

- **State transitions**:

  - Referee reward: `eligible → applied → approved instantly`.
  - Referrer reward: `eligible → generated (pending) → approved by admin → credited to wallet`.

- **Wallet aggregates**:

  - Include only **approved and credited** referral rewards in **Total balance** and **Total earned**.
  - Exclude **pending** rewards from wallet totals but make them visible in referral-level tracking where required.

- **Cross-channel consistency**: Match the underlying rules and calculations used on the Cashkr website Refer & Earn feature so that referral behavior is consistent across web and customer app experiences.
