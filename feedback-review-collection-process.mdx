## Overview

Use this SOP to collect, route, and act on customer feedback and reviews after every completed order. It aligns Customer Experience, Marketing, Sales, Support, and Operations on when to ask for feedback, how to respond to negative experiences, and how to convert positive feedback into public reviews and vendor insights.

Consistent execution here improves service quality, builds trust across platforms, and keeps internal teams working from the same feedback pipeline.

## When we collect feedback

Collect feedback at three fixed touchpoints after payment is successful:

- **Immediately after order completion (primary)**  
  Shown or sent as soon as the order is marked complete and payment is successful.  
  Channels: in-app pop-up, WhatsApp message, SMS link.

- **2 hours after completion (reminder)**  
  Automated reminder asking the customer to share a rating or review if they skipped the first prompt.

- **24 hours after completion (final follow-up)**  
  Last attempt to collect internal feedback or a public review for that order.

## What we collect

Use these feedback types to separate internal CX signals, public reviews, and escalations.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Internal (CX)" icon="users">

Internal experience feedback is for CX and internal improvement only. It is not published directly.

- **Overall rating**: 1–5 stars.
- **Comments**: open text from the customer about their experience.
- **Vendor behaviour rating**: how the vendor interacted with the customer.
- **Pickup experience**: punctuality, politeness, and clarity during pickup.
- **Payment speed rating**: how fast and smooth the payment felt.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Public reviews (Marketing)" icon="zap">

Public reviews help build trust and social proof on external platforms.

- **Google My Business reviews**
- **Play Store reviews**
- **App Store reviews**
- **Website testimonials**
- **Social media reviews or shout-outs**

</Tab>

<Tab title="Negative feedback (Ops/CX)" icon="alert-triangle">

Negative feedback triggers structured follow-up and, when needed, vendor or process changes.

- **Service delay**
- **Vendor behaviour issues**
- **Wrong inspection or mis-evaluation**
- **Payment issues**
- **Missed pickup or no-show**

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Automated feedback flow

This flow runs automatically after order completion and routes customers based on their rating.

<Steps>

<Step title="Order completion and trigger" icon="check-circle">

Once payment is successful and the order is completed, the system triggers a **Feedback Request** for that order.

You should see the customer move into the post-order feedback journey at this moment.

</Step>

<Step title="Customer receives message" icon="message-square">

The customer receives an in-app pop-up and/or WhatsApp/SMS message such as:

> Thank you for selling your device on Cashkr. Please rate your experience.

Typical action buttons:

- **Rate experience**
- **Write a review**
- **Need help? Contact support**

</Step>

<Step title="Customer submits rating" icon="star">

The customer chooses one of the standard rating options:

- **5 stars**
- **4 stars**
- **3 stars**
- **1–2 stars**

</Step>

<Step title="Routing logic based on rating" icon="settings">

Use this logic to understand what happens next for each rating band:

| Rating band | Action taken                            |
| ----------: | ---------------------------------------- |
| 1–2 stars   | Escalate to CX team for immediate review |
| 3 stars     | Ask for more detailed internal feedback  |
| 4 stars     | Ask for a public review                  |
| 5 stars     | Redirect to Google Play / GMB for review |

Customers with higher ratings are guided toward public platforms, while lower ratings stay internal and trigger follow-up.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Manual feedback collection

For some customers, automated messages are not enough. CX or Sales must **call** and collect feedback manually in these cases:

- **High-value transactions**
- **Corporate customers**
- **Complaints that have been resolved**
- **Repeat customers**

Use a short, consistent script:

> We would love to know how your experience was with Cashkr today. Your feedback helps us improve our service.

Capture the same fields as internal feedback: rating, comments, pickup, vendor behaviour, and payment speed.

## Public review collection

Convert positive experiences into public reviews without changing the underlying routing logic.

### After a positive rating (4–5 stars)

When a customer rates **4 or 5 stars**, send a short thank-you message with direct links:

- **WhatsApp/SMS text example**  
  > Thank you. Would you like to support us with a quick review on Google?

- **Links to include**
  - Google My Business review link
  - Play Store and/or App Store review links (for app users)

### Marketing team actions

Marketing uses the collected public reviews for brand and growth activities:

- Collects **screenshots of reviews** from GMB, app stores, website, and social media.
- Performs **basic sentiment checks** to understand themes.
- Prepares a **weekly review summary** highlighting wins and key feedback trends.
- Selects and formats the best reviews into social media posts, stories, and website sections.

## Negative feedback handling (1–2 stars)

Route all **1–2 star ratings** into a strict CX/Ops follow-up process.

<Callout kind="info">

Always attempt first contact for a 1–2 star rating **within 15 minutes** of receiving the alert.

</Callout>

<Steps>

<Step title="CX receives alert" icon="alert-triangle">

The system flags the negative review or low rating and assigns it to the CX team for investigation and response.

Ensure someone on CX is actively watching these alerts during working hours.

</Step>

<Step title="Contact customer within 15 minutes" icon="phone">

Reach out to the customer by call or message using a calm, solution-focused tone:

> Hi, we noticed you had an issue. We are here to resolve it immediately.

Confirm the order details before discussing the issue so the customer feels heard and understood.

</Step>

<Step title="Understand the issue clearly" icon="help-circle">

Ask open questions and classify the issue into one or more categories:

- Vendor behaviour
- Pricing issues
- Payment problem
- Delay
- Wrong inspection
- Miscommunication

Document the category and a short summary so Ops and other teams can act on it later.

</Step>

<Step title="Resolve or escalate to the right team" icon="arrow-right">

Depending on the category, route and follow up with:

- **Vendor Ops** for vendor-related issues, behaviour, or repeated SLA problems.
- **Finance** for payment delays, mismatches, or failures.
- **Tech** for app or website issues affecting the customer journey.

Stay with the case until you confirm the customer’s issue is resolved, even if another team performs the fix.

</Step>

<Step title="Request updated feedback after resolution" icon="check-circle">

Once you resolve the issue, ask the customer if the experience has improved:

> Your issue is resolved now. If you feel your experience has improved, we would appreciate an updated rating.

If they update their rating, capture it in the same place as the original feedback and, if appropriate, nudge them toward a public review.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Review publishing workflow (Marketing)

Marketing turns raw reviews into branded assets and proof points.

<Steps>

<Step title="Collect reviews from all sources" icon="download">

Gather reviews and feedback regularly from:

- Google My Business
- Play Store
- App Store
- Internal feedback tools and summaries
- Social media direct messages or comments

Centralize these in your existing tracking or storage location.

</Step>

<Step title="Shortlist valid reviews" icon="check-circle">

Filter the collected reviews using the following criteria:

- Review appears **genuine** and specific.
- **Positive or clearly constructive** sentiment.
- Mentions **Cashkr service** directly.
- Relevant to **phones, laptops, tablets, or MacBooks**.
- Contains **clear value** or a strong story worth sharing.

</Step>

<Step title="Add shortlist to creative templates" icon="edit">

For each shortlisted review, prepare it for publishing:

- Add the **reviewer name** (or initials, as per existing practice).
- Copy the **review text**.
- Include the **star rating**.
- Apply **highlight graphics or layouts** using the standard creative templates.

This makes it easy to reuse reviews across campaigns.

</Step>

<Step title="Publish on key platforms" icon="upload">

Use the formatted reviews on:

- Instagram stories
- Instagram feed posts
- Website testimonial sections
- Carousel ads or creatives
- Trust badges or proof sections on the homepage

Schedule and space posts to maintain a consistent flow of social proof.

</Step>

<Step title="Store assets and replies" icon="folder">

Save final assets and any standard reply text in the **Review Replies** folder, following existing company practice.

This keeps future campaigns fast and ensures consistent messaging when responding to reviews.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Vendor feedback collection (internal)

Each completed order also collects **vendor-specific ratings** that feed into Vendor Ops decisions:

- **Vendor behaviour rating**
- **Vendor timeliness score**
- **Vendor professionalism rating**

Vendor Ops uses these fields to:

- Take action on **SLA violations** or repeated complaints.
- Identify **top-performing vendors** for more orders or recognition.
- Trigger **vendor hold or deactivation** when issues repeat across orders.

Consistent data entry here directly supports vendor quality management.

## Question bank by touchpoint

Use these standard questions at each stage so feedback remains consistent and comparable.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="After pickup" icon="truck">

Ask:

> How was the pickup experience?

Focus on punctuality, politeness, and clarity during the pickup.

</Tab>

<Tab title="After payment" icon="credit-card">

Ask:

> Was the payment smooth and instant?

Confirm whether the promised payment experience matched reality.

</Tab>

<Tab title="After issue resolution" icon="check-circle">

Ask:

> Did our team resolve your concern properly?

Check if the customer feels fully resolved, not just updated.

</Tab>

<Tab title="After vendor replacement" icon="repeat">

Ask:

> How was the new vendor experience?

Compare the replacement vendor to the original, especially when the first vendor caused issues.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Weekly feedback analytics

CX and Marketing teams should review aggregated feedback weekly to stay aligned.

**CX team tracks:**

- Total feedback count
- Percentage of positive reviews
- Percentage of negative reviews
- Most common complaint type
- Vendor issues flagged for Ops

**Marketing team tracks:**

- Google My Business review growth trend
- App rating trends on Play Store and App Store
- Sentiment heatmap or themes across reviews
- Weekly social proof assets created or published

Share key highlights in your regular reporting rhythm so actions stay tied to real customer voices.

## Quick summary card

<Callout kind="tip">

Use this as a one-page mental model for the feedback and review process:

- **Touchpoints:** Order completion, 2-hour reminder, and 24-hour final reminder.  
- **Positive feedback:** For 4–5 star ratings, thank the customer and guide them to public review links.  
- **Negative feedback:** For 1–2 star ratings, trigger immediate CX follow-up, understand the issue, escalate to the right team, and request an updated rating after resolution.  
- **Feedback types:** Pickup experience, vendor behaviour, payment speed, and overall satisfaction, plus vendor-specific scores for internal Ops.  
- **Outputs:** Better customer experience, stronger vendor management, and richer marketing content and social proof.

</Callout>
