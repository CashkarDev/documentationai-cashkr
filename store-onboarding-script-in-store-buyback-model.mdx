## Overview

Use this script to onboard mobile stores as official Cashkr partners, where the store helps customers exchange their old phones instantly through the Cashkr system.

- **Team:** Store Onboarding / Channel Expansion (B2CH)  
- **Tone:** Friendly, business-to-business, focused on trust, simplicity, and daily benefit.

## Quick checklist for the call

Follow this flow on every onboarding or demo call:

- **Opening:** Introduce yourself, Cashkr, and the instant buyback service.
- **Explain the model:** Walk through how the Cashkr Store App and associate work in-store.
- **Benefits:** Emphasise customer voucher, no resale headache, and end-of-day settlement.
- **Trust / proof:** Mention that Cashkr already works with multiple stores in the area.
- **Close with action:** Ask to register the store and mention quick setup and training.
- **Handle objections:** Address questions on cost or margin (no joining cost, more sales).
- **Next steps:** Share Store App / onboarding link and log details in CRM / Admin Panel.

## Call script by language

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="English">

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Opening">

    “Hi, this is \[Your Name] from Cashkr. We help mobile stores like yours offer instant buyback service to customers who are purchasing new phones.”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Explain the model">

    “Here’s how it works — whenever a customer visits your store to buy a new phone and wants to sell their old one, your staff can open the **Cashkr Store App** and create an order.

    Our Cashkr associate will be present at your store to inspect the device, provide a **fair buyback value**, and complete the transaction on the spot.”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Benefits">

    - “Your customer gets an instant voucher that they can use toward their new phone purchase.”
    - “You don’t need to negotiate or handle resale — we handle the entire process.”
    - “At the end of the day, Cashkr settles the payment and collects all the devices directly from your store.”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Trust / reassurance">

    “Cashkr already works with multiple stores in \[city/region], helping them close more new-phone sales every day by offering customers a smooth exchange experience.”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Close with action">

    “Would you like to register your store today? The process is quick — we just need your basic details, and we’ll assign a Cashkr associate for setup and training.”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Objection handling">

    **If the store asks about cost or margin:**

    “There’s no joining cost at all. You earn more sales because customers prefer stores that take their old phones instantly. Cashkr manages all logistics, payment, and inspection.”

    </Step>
  </Steps>

## Usage notes

<Callout kind="tip">

- Use this script for onboarding or demo calls with store owners.
- Always mention **Cashkr associate presence in-store** to build confidence.
- Keep focus on **store benefit → easier new phone sales and happy customers**.
- Share the **Store App link or onboarding form** immediately after the call.
- Log store details in the **CRM / Admin Panel** so the team can assign an associate and track status.

</Callout>
