## Why documentation matters at Cashkr

Organized documentation keeps every SOP, policy, project, and knowledge resource in one place so teams do not repeat work or ask the same questions.

<Callout kind="info">

Strong document and knowledge management makes onboarding faster, reduces handover gaps, and helps new joiners become productive quickly.

</Callout>

### BreezeDOC

BreezeDOC manages all formal documents that need signatures or consistent templates, such as vendor agreements, NDAs, and key policies. Use it when you need a signed record or a document that follows an approved format.

### Astravue

Astravue keeps project work structured and visible across teams. Use it to break work into tasks, assign owners, add deadlines, and track status for campaigns, feature work, and operations improvements.

### Eniston

Eniston is the single source of truth for reusable knowledge at Cashkr. Store onboarding guides, SOPs, troubleshooting checklists, and FAQs so anyone can search and find accurate, up-to-date information.

## How we use these daily

- **Marketing team**
  - Tracks campaign plans, tasks, and deadlines in Astravue.
  - Stores creative brief templates, brand guidelines, and campaign checklists in Eniston.
- **Operations team**
  - Maintains vendor SOPs, pickup guidelines, and escalation flows in Eniston.
  - Uses Astravue to track operational projects and process improvements.
- **Tech team**
  - Uploads API documentation, architecture notes, and deployment runbooks in Eniston.
  - Manages feature delivery tasks, bug fixes, and tech debt items in Astravue.
- **HR and management**
  - Keeps policies, onboarding checklists, and role-specific guides in Eniston.
  - Uses BreezeDOC for offer letters, contracts, and other documents that require signatures.

## Guidelines

Follow these guidelines so documentation stays consistent and easy to find:

- **Choose the right tool**
  - Use **BreezeDOC** for any document that requires signatures or legally relevant approval.
  - Use **Astravue** for tasks, timelines, and ownership of work.
  - Use **Eniston** for reusable knowledge, SOPs, and anything people will search for later.
- **Use clear, consistent names**
  - Start titles with team and topic, for example: `Ops - Pickup SOP - Metro Cities` or `Marketing - July 2024 Campaign Plan`.
  - Avoid personal or unclear titles like `New doc` or `Temp notes`.
- **Version important documents**
  - Add version or date to critical docs, for example: `Vendor Policy v2` or `Refund SOP - 2024-06`.
  - When you update a process, either archive the old version or clearly mark it as outdated.
- **Make content searchable**
  - Include common keywords and key terms in titles and headings so teams can find docs by search.
  - Add short summaries at the top of longer documents describing who the doc is for and when to use it.
- **Keep ownership clear**
  - Add an owner team or role at the top of each document, and review critical SOPs and policies on a regular schedule.
