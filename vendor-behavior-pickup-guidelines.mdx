## Overview

Professional behavior at pickup keeps customers safe and protects your Cashkr partnership. Use this page to train new vendors and agents on how to speak and act during every visit.

<Callout kind="info">

**Used by**

- Vendor Operations
- Vendor Onboarding trainers
- Field vendors and their agents

**When to use**

- After vendor app training, before first live pickups
- During refresher training or when behavior issues are reported

**Goal**

- Standardize how vendors and agents interact with customers at the doorstep
- Ensure every pickup follows OTP-based payment rules and professional conduct

**Tone**

- Clear, respectful, firm on non-negotiable rules

</Callout>

## Non-negotiables at pickup

These rules are zero-tolerance. Breaking them can lead to holds, deactivation, or other actions as per policy.

<Callout kind="alert">

- **No offline deals**  
  Never offer or accept deals outside the Cashkr app. Do not share personal buying offers or bypass the app price.

- **Payment must follow OTP flow in the app**  
  Take payment only through the Cashkr app flow: wallet → OTP verification → bank transfer.  
  No cash, no external UPI apps, no direct bank transfers, no manual pricing.

- **Do not take the device before payment success**  
  Inspect the device first, agree on final app price, complete OTP payment in app, wait for `Payment Successful`, and only then collect the device.

- **Always be respectful**  
  No shouting, threats, abuse, arguments, or pressure. If there is a problem, pause and follow the escalation process instead of fighting with the customer.

</Callout>

## Recommended pickup interaction sequence

Follow this sequence for every pickup. Trainers can walk vendors through each step using the script section below.

<Steps>

<Step title="First contact call" icon="phone">

- Introduce yourself with name and that you are calling from Cashkr.
- Mention that you are calling about their Cashkr sell order.
- Confirm address, preferred time, and any building access details.
- Speak slowly, clearly, and politely; do not sound rushed or annoyed.

</Step>

<Step title="Before-arrival confirmation" icon="check">

- Call again before leaving or when you are nearby.
- Reconfirm time and landmark so the customer is prepared.
- If you will be late, inform the customer and offer a revised time.
- Update call disposition in the app as per training.

</Step>

<Step title="Doorstep greeting and identification" icon="home">

- Greet the customer respectfully when they open the door.
- State your name and that you are from Cashkr for the pickup.
- Keep your phone in hand with the Cashkr app open on the order.
- Stand at a respectful distance and avoid entering the house without permission.

</Step>

<Step title="Inspection etiquette" icon="monitor">

- Ask for permission before touching or checking the device.
- Confirm the model and basic condition from the app with the customer.
- Do the inspection where the customer can clearly see what you are doing.
- Explain any major issues you find in simple, non-technical language.

</Step>

<Step title="Price explanation (system-based)" icon="bar-chart-2">

- Use only the price shown in the Cashkr app; never quote personal prices.
- If price changes after inspection, explain calmly that it is system-based.
- Connect the reason to inspection (for example, battery, screen, or body condition).
- If the customer disagrees strongly, stop and follow the objection-handling and escalation guidance.

</Step>

<Step title="OTP payment and confirmation" icon="lock">

- Once the customer agrees to the final app price, start the payment in the app.
- Ask the customer to read out or enter the OTP on their own phone; do not enter it yourself.
- Wait until the app shows `Payment Successful` and show this screen to the customer.
- Confirm that the customer has received the confirmation SMS or notification.

</Step>

<Step title="Collect device and close order" icon="package">

- After payment success, collect the device and available accessories (box, charger, etc.).
- Handle the device carefully so the customer sees you are responsible.
- Mark the order with the correct status in the app immediately; do not delay.
- Thank the customer and remind them they will get a message and can rate the service.

</Step>

<Step title="Notes, ratings, and agent accountability" icon="users">

- Add any important notes in the app (for example, missing accessories, minor issues aligned with policy).
- Maintain polite behavior throughout; this directly affects your ratings.
- If you send an agent, make sure they follow this exact sequence.
- Vendors are fully responsible for their agents’ behavior and any violations.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">

Use this sequence as a checklist during role-plays. Trainers can pause after each step and ask the vendor to repeat the key line in their own words.

</Callout>

## Onboarding script (English and Hinglish)

Use these scripts during training and live calls. Vendors can adapt small words to their style, but must keep the meaning, respect, and OTP rules.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English Script" icon="book-open">

**Opening: explain purpose of training**

"Hi \[Vendor Name], this is \[Your Name] from Cashkr.

Your account is active and you know how to use the app. Now I will explain how to behave with customers during pickup visits so you build trust and keep strong ratings."

**Step 1 – First contact call**

"Hi sir/ma’am, this is \[Your Name] calling from Cashkr regarding your sell order.

I am calling to confirm your pickup address and a convenient time for today. Is this address and time comfortable for you, or would you like to reschedule?"

**Step 2 – Before-arrival confirmation**

"Hello sir/ma’am, this is \[Your Name] from Cashkr again.

I am on the way for your device pickup and will reach in around \[X] minutes. Are you available at the same address, or should I come at a different time?"

**Step 3 – Doorstep greeting and identification**

"Hello sir/ma’am, good \[morning/afternoon/evening]. I am \[Your Name] from Cashkr, here for your device pickup.

I have your order open in my app so we will follow the same details and price that you saw on Cashkr."

**Step 4 – Inspection etiquette**

"Before I check the device, may I take your permission to inspect it?

I will check the screen, body condition, battery, and other basic functions. I will do everything in front of you and if I find anything different from the app answers, I will explain it."

**Step 5 – Price explanation (system-based)**

"Based on the actual condition, the Cashkr system has updated the final price to ₹\[Amount].

This price is fully system-based according to the device model and condition. I do not decide the price personally, I only follow what is shown in the app."

If the customer agrees:

"Shall I proceed with payment at this price?"

**Step 6 – OTP payment and confirmation**

"Great. I will now complete the payment through the Cashkr app.

You will receive an OTP on your phone. Please read it out or enter it so that we can confirm the payment in the system.

Once I submit the OTP, the app will show `Payment Successful` and the amount will be processed to your selected method as per Cashkr policy."

After success:

"Here, you can see on my screen that the payment is successful. You will also receive a confirmation message from Cashkr."

**Step 7 – Collect device and close order**

"Now that payment is done, I will collect your device. If you have the box or charger and want to give it, I can take that as well.

I will now mark your order as completed in the app."

**Step 8 – Ratings and closing**

"After this visit, Cashkr may ask you to rate my service.

If you liked the experience, you can give a good rating. Your feedback helps me continue getting orders.

Thank you for selling your device with Cashkr."

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish Script" icon="book-open">

**Opening: training purpose**

"Hello \[Vendor Name], main \[Your Name] bol raha/rahi hoon Cashkr se.

Ab aapka account active hai aur aap app use karna jaante ho. Ab main aapko bataunga/bataungi ki pickup ke time customer se kaise baat karni hai, taaki unka trust bane aur aapko achhi rating mile."

**Step 1 – Pehli call (first contact)**

"Hello sir/ma’am, main \[Your Name] bol raha/rahi hoon Cashkr se. Aapne ek sell order create kiya tha.

Main aapka pickup address aur aapke liye convenient time confirm karna chahunga/chahungi. Kya yeh address aur time theek hai, ya aapko reschedule karna hai?"

**Step 2 – Pickup se pehle confirmation**

"Hello sir/ma’am, main \[Your Name] Cashkr se bol raha/rahi hoon.

Main ab aapke pickup ke liye nikal raha/rahi hoon aur lagbhag \[X] minute me pahunch jaunga/jaungi. Kya aap isi time pe available rahenge, ya aapko doosra time chahiye?"

**Step 3 – Doorstep greeting aur introduction**

"Namaste sir/ma’am, good \[morning/afternoon/evening]. Main \[Your Name] hoon Cashkr se, aapke device pickup ke liye aaya/aayi hoon.

Mere phone me Cashkr app me aapka order open hai, toh hum wahi details aur price follow karenge jo aapne app me dekha hai."

**Step 4 – Device inspection se pehle permission**

"Device check karne se pehle, kya main aapse permission le sakta/sakti hoon?

Main screen, body condition, battery aur basic functions check karunga/karungi. Sab kuch aapke saamne hoga, aur agar app me diye gaye answers se koi difference hoga toh main aapko clearly explain karunga/karungi."

**Step 5 – Price explanation (system-based)**

"Actual condition ke hisaab se, Cashkr system ne final price ₹\[Amount] show kiya hai.

Yeh price poora system-based hai, main khud se price decide nahi karta/karti. Main sirf app me jo amount aata hai wahi follow karta/karti hoon."

Agar customer agree kare:

"Kya main is price pe payment proceed kar sakta/sakti hoon?"

**Step 6 – OTP payment aur confirmation**

"Thik hai sir/ma’am, ab main Cashkr app se payment complete karunga/karungi.

Aapke phone par ek OTP aayega. Kripya OTP read karke bataiye ya khud enter kijiye, taaki system me payment confirm ho jaye.

Jaise hi main OTP submit karunga/karungi, app me `Payment Successful` show hoga aur amount aapke selected method ke hisaab se process ho jayega."

Success ke baad:

"Yahan aap dekh sakte hain, mere phone me payment successful show ho raha hai. Aapko bhi Cashkr ki taraf se confirmation message mil jayega."

**Step 7 – Device collect karke order close karna**

"Ab payment ho chuka hai, main aapka device collect karunga/karungi. Agar aapke paas box ya charger hai aur aap dena chahte hain, toh main woh bhi le sakta/sakti hoon.

Main abhi app me aapka order completed mark kar raha/rahi hoon."

**Step 8 – Rating aur closing**

"Pickup ke baad Cashkr aapse service ke baare me rating maang sakta hai.

Agar aapko experience accha laga ho toh aap acchi rating de sakte hain. Aapki rating se mujhe future me aur orders milne me madad milti hai.

Dhanyavaad, aapne Cashkr ke through apna device sell kiya."

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Behavior Do and Don’t

Use this section as a quick behavior checklist during training.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Do" icon="check-circle">

- Speak politely on every call and at the doorstep, even if the customer is angry or confused.
- Keep the Cashkr app open and follow whatever price and flow the system shows.
- Ask permission before checking the device and keep the inspection visible to the customer.
- Explain any price change calmly with a clear reason linked to the app answers.
- Complete OTP payment in the app and show `Payment Successful` before taking the device.
- Mark the correct status in the app immediately after each action.
- Inform the customer if you will be late or need to reschedule, and use the right app disposition.
- Escalate issues through Cashkr support or as per SOP instead of arguing.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Don’t" icon="x-circle">

- Do not offer to buy the device personally or make cash/UPI deals outside the app.
- Do not change or fix a price that is different from the app, even if the customer asks.
- Do not raise your voice, use slang, taunt, or threaten the customer.
- Do not take the device to another room or leave the house with it before payment success.
- Do not enter the customer’s home without being invited or stand too close.
- Do not enter or ask for the OTP on the customer’s phone; let them share or enter it.
- Do not delay marking orders in the app or keep orders stuck without action.
- Do not ignore calls or messages if you have already committed to a pickup time.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## If you use agents

Many vendors use agents to complete pickups. The same rules apply to them.

<Callout kind="info">

- You are fully responsible for **every pickup done on your account**, whether you go or your agent goes.
- Cashkr will treat your agent’s behavior, payment flow, and communication as your own.
- Train agents using this exact script and sequence before sending them for live pickups.

</Callout>

When assigning agents:

- Share these guidelines and make them acknowledge the non-negotiable rules.
- Monitor early pickups closely through calls and app status updates.
- If an agent misbehaves or breaks OTP/payment rules, stop sending them immediately and inform Cashkr support as per escalation SOPs.

For more on explaining agent usage to vendors, see [Agent Assignment Script (Explaining Pickup Agent Flow)](/agent-assignment-script-explaining-pickup-agent-flow).

## What happens if guidelines are violated

If a vendor or agent breaks these behavior or payment rules, Cashkr may take action according to standard SLAs and processes.

- For how violations are tracked and escalated, see [SLA Violations and Escalation Rules](/sla-violations-escalation-rules).
- For holds, suspensions, or deactivation decisions, see [Vendor Hold and Deactivation Process](/vendor-hold-deactivation-process).

Trainers should remind vendors that keeping all pickups within app rules and maintaining respectful communication protects both the vendor and the customer.
