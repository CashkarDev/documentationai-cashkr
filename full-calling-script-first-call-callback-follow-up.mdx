## Purpose and when to use this script

<Callout kind="info">

Use this script when you call **high-intent buyback leads** who checked a device price on Cashkr but did not complete the order.  
Tone: **polite, confident, helpful, and non-pushy**.

This page focuses on **what to say on the call**. For how leads move from **Lead → Order** and exact priority rules, refer to [Lead Handling in Admin Panel (Lead → Order Flow)](/lead-handling-in-admin-panel-lead-order-flow).

</Callout>

- Goal of every call: **help the customer decide the next step** and **end with the correct disposition and notes**.  
- Do not promise anything that conflicts with [Lead to Order Conversion Script](/lead-to-order-conversion-script) or [Price Match / Objection Handling Script](/price-match-/-objection-handling-script).  
- For deeper objection-handling lines in Hinglish and more scenarios, see [Follow-Up / Callback Script](/follow-up-/-callback-script).

## Standard call structure

Follow this structure on **every connected call**, regardless of whether it is a first call, callback, or follow-up.

<Steps>

<Step title="Opening and greeting" icon="phone">

- Confirm you are speaking to the right person.
- Introduce yourself and Cashkr.
- Remind them of the **price check** they did.

**Sample lines:**

> Agent:  
> "Hi, am I speaking with [Customer Name]?  
> This is [Your Name] calling from Cashkr.  
> You recently checked the price for your [Device Name] on our app or website."

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm context and intent" icon="help-circle">

- Confirm they remember the price check.
- Check if they are still interested in selling.

**Sample lines:**

> Agent:  
> "I am calling to see if you need any help completing the pickup for your device or if you had any questions before selling."

If they say they are interested, move towards **creating the order** using [Lead to Order Conversion Script](/lead-to-order-conversion-script).

</Step>

<Step title="Ask discovery question" icon="info">

- Understand what blocked them from completing the order.

**Always ask:**

> Agent:  
> "May I know what stopped you from completing the order earlier?"

Use their answer to pick the right objection handling path in the **Objection handling** section below.

</Step>

<Step title="Handle objection or question" icon="message-square">

- Listen fully, do not interrupt.
- Use the relevant objection block from **Objection handling**.
- Focus on clarity: pickup process, pricing, safety, or availability.

After addressing the concern, gently check if they want to proceed now:

> Agent:  
> "If your questions are clear now, would you like me to help you book the pickup now?"

</Step>

<Step title="Close with clear next step" icon="check-circle">

Based on their response, close with one clear action:

- **Create Order now** (and actually create it in the system).
- **Schedule a callback** at a specific time.
- **Mark that the customer will place the order later** from the app or website.
- **Capture that they are comparing or waiting for a quote**.
- **Note that they are not interested or have already placed an order somewhere else**.

Do not push if the customer clearly says they are not interested. Keep the brand impression positive.

</Step>

<Step title="Set disposition and add notes" icon="edit">

- Choose the correct disposition as per [Understanding Dispositions (Connected, Not Connected, Create Order)](/understanding-dispositions-connected-not-connected-create-order).
- Add **clear notes** on what happened and what was agreed.

Examples of good notes:

- "Connected, customer comparing with other website, will share screenshot on WhatsApp if better quote."  
- "Connected, requested callback tomorrow after 7:30 pm, said will confirm with family."  
- "Not reachable, first attempt, mobile ringing, will try later evening."

Notes help the next agent understand the history and keep promises to the customer.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Script variants by call type

Use these variants to adjust your lines depending on whether it is a first call, scheduled callback, follow-up, or when the customer did not pick up.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="First call (new lead)" icon="phone">

**When to use:** First call to a new price-check lead marked as **Not Called** or **Fresh** in the system.

**Suggested flow:**

> Agent:  
> "Hi [Customer Name], this is [Your Name] from Cashkr.  
> You recently checked the price for your [Device Name] on our app or website, is that right?"

If customer confirms:

> Agent:  
> "Great, I am calling to see if you need any help completing the pickup for your device."

Then ask the discovery question:

> Agent:  
> "May I know what stopped you from completing the order earlier?"

Use the **Objection handling** section to respond, then:

- If customer is ready to sell now:

> Agent:  
> "Perfect, I can help you book the pickup right now. It usually takes about 2–3 minutes. Shall we complete it together?"

Use [Lead to Order Conversion Script](/lead-to-order-conversion-script) for detailed order-creation steps and wording.

- If customer is not ready, select the correct path from **Objection handling** and close with the right **disposition and notes**.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Callback (scheduled)" icon="clock">

**When to use:** You or another agent scheduled a callback and the lead disposition is **Connected → Callback**.

**Respect the timing:** Call as close as possible to the scheduled time mentioned in the notes.

**Suggested opening:**

> Agent:  
> "Hi [Customer Name], this is [Your Name] from Cashkr.  
> I am calling you back as discussed regarding your [Device Name]."

Bring back the previous context from notes:

> Agent:  
> "Last time, you wanted to [reason from notes: check with family / think about price / confirm pickup time]. Are you available to proceed now?"

If customer says **yes** and is ready:

> Agent:  
> "Great, I will help you place the order now so that we can arrange the pickup and process your payment at pickup."

Follow [Lead to Order Conversion Script](/lead-to-order-conversion-script) for the detailed flow.

If customer is still unsure:

> Agent:  
> "No problem. Just to remind you, the inspection happens in front of you and payment is done instantly before pickup is closed. Would you like to keep a time for a final callback, or would you prefer to place the order yourself later from the app?"

- If they want another callback: schedule a **clear time** and set **Connected → Callback** again with specific notes.  
- If they want to place order later: set **Connected → Customer Will Place Order** and record what they said.

For more callback variations and objection lines, refer to [Follow-Up / Callback Script](/follow-up-/-callback-script).

</Tab>

<Tab title="Follow-up (2nd / 3rd attempt)" icon="repeat">

**When to use:** You have already spoken to the customer before, or this is a follow-up on an earlier attempt, but no order is created yet.

**Suggested opening:**

> Agent:  
> "Hi [Customer Name], this is [Your Name] from Cashkr.  
> I am just following up regarding the price you checked for your [Device Name]."

**Soft nudge line:**

> Agent:  
> "I wanted to check if you would like to go ahead with the pickup now, or if you still have any questions or need help from our side."

If customer delays again:

> Agent:  
> "That is completely fine. You can place the order anytime directly from the Cashkr app or website whenever you are ready. If you would like, I can also help you on call now."

**Disposition examples:**

- If they say they will place the order themselves later: **Connected → Customer Will Place Order**, with notes on approximate timing.  
- If they are waiting to compare or share a quote: **Connected → Waiting for Quote**, with notes.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Not connected (attempt notes)" icon="x-circle">

**When to use:** The call is not answered, phone is switched off, wrong number, or otherwise **Not Connected**.

You still need to:

- Select a **Not Connected** disposition option as per [Understanding Dispositions](/understanding-dispositions-connected-not-connected-create-order).  
- Add a clear internal note for each attempt.

**Examples of internal notes:**

- "Not connected, first attempt at 2:15 pm, mobile ringing, will try again in evening."  
- "Not connected, second attempt, switched off, will retry tomorrow afternoon."  
- "Wrong number, person does not know customer name, marked as wrong contact."

Make attempts at **different times of day** where it makes sense, without committing to a fixed number of attempts in this script. Follow any specific attempt rules described in [Lead Handling in Admin Panel (Lead → Order Flow)](/lead-handling-in-admin-panel-lead-order-flow).

</Tab>

<Tab title="High-end devices (iPhone / MacBook / premium)" icon="star">

**When to use:** High-value devices (for example, newer iPhones, MacBooks, premium phones) where customers may be more price-sensitive or more concerned about safety.

Use the usual flow but add **extra confidence and value**.

After confirming intent and understanding their concern, you can add:

> Agent:  
> "For this model, you are getting one of the better prices available right now on Cashkr, and we arrange a dedicated pickup partner for high-value devices."

Reinforce the process:

> Agent:  
> "The inspection happens in front of you, and once everything matches, we send the payment instantly to your UPI or bank account before pickup is marked complete."

Closing line:

> Agent:  
> "If everything sounds good, would you like me to book the pickup today, or would you prefer another day that works better for you?"

Then follow the same **disposition and notes** rules as other calls.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Objection handling (what to say for common responses)

Use these blocks when the customer shares a reason for not completing the order earlier. Always adapt to what they actually say instead of reading lines mechanically.

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Price comparison or better quote somewhere else">

**Customer example:** "I am checking on other websites" or "I got a better price somewhere else."

**Key points:**

- Acknowledge that comparing is normal.  
- Highlight Cashkr benefits: instant payment, transparent pricing, doorstep pickup.  
- Do not promise fixed price lock or guaranteed match beyond allowed guidelines.

**Sample lines (align with Price Match / Objection Handling Script):**

> Agent:  
> "That makes sense, many customers compare prices before deciding.  
> With Cashkr, you get doorstep pickup, inspection in front of you, and instant payment to your UPI or bank before the pickup is closed."

If they mention a specific higher quote:

> Agent:  
> "If you have a higher quote, you can share the details with me. I will see what is possible within our system and let you know. Prices can depend on exact model, condition, and current market."

If they are still deciding:

> Agent:  
> "No problem. I will mark that you are comparing prices. If you want, I can call you back after you finish checking, or you can place the order directly from the Cashkr app whenever you are ready."

**Common dispositions:**

- **Connected → Waiting for Quote** if they will share a quote or get back after comparing.  
- **Connected → Customer Will Place Order** if they say they will book themselves later.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Trust or safety concern">

**Customer example:** "Is this safe?" or "How do I know I will get the money?"

**Key points:**

- Focus on verified pickup partners.  
- Inspection is done in front of the customer.  
- Payment is instant before pickup is completed.

**Sample lines:**

> Agent:  
> "That is a fair question. Our pickup partners are verified, and the entire inspection happens in front of you at your address.  
> Once everything matches the details you entered, we send the payment instantly to your UPI or bank account. Only after you receive the payment does the pickup get completed."

Reassure without overpromising:

> Agent:  
> "If you want, I can also explain each step before we book the pickup so you know exactly what to expect."

Disposition depends on whether they proceed now or later:

- If they agree to book now: create the order and set **Create Order** only after the order is actually made.  
- If they want more time: **Connected → Callback** or **Connected → Customer Will Place Order**, with notes.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Customer busy or asks to call later">

**Customer example:** "I am busy now, call me later" or "I am in office, call at night."

**Key points:**

- Respect their time.  
- Confirm an exact callback window.  
- Honour the callback as per the note.

**Sample lines:**

> Agent:  
> "No problem, I understand. When would be a good time for me to call you back?"

After they share a time:

> Agent:  
> "Sure, I will call you around [their preferred time]. I will also keep a note so that we do not disturb you at the wrong time."

**Disposition:**

- **Connected → Callback** with clear notes: "Callback requested after 7:30 pm, customer in office, prefers evenings."

On the next call, start with the **Callback (scheduled)** script above and reference the reason from your note.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Not interested or wants to decide later without a clear time">

**Customer example:** "Not interested" or "I will think about it, not now" without wanting a callback.

**Key points:**

- Do not push.  
- Confirm if they want any information before you close.  
- Keep the door open for future.

**Sample lines (if they say not interested):**

> Agent:  
> "That is completely fine. If you change your mind later and want to sell your [Device Name], you can always check the price again or place an order on the Cashkr app or website."

If they say they will think but refuse a callback:

> Agent:  
> "No problem, you can decide at your convenience. If you need any help in the future, our support is available through the app and website."

**Disposition:**

- Use a **Connected** disposition that reflects the actual outcome (for example, a variant for not interested or similar as defined in your disposition list).  
- Add a brief note on why they are not interested or what they said.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Customer already placed an order (with Cashkr or elsewhere)">

**Customer example:** "I already sold it" or "I already placed an order."

**Key points:**

- Clarify whether the order is with Cashkr or another platform.  
- If with Cashkr, confirm basic details and stop further selling attempts.  
- If with another platform, respect the decision.

**Sample lines:**

> Agent:  
> "Thank you for letting me know. Is the order already completed, or is the pickup still pending?"

If order is already placed with Cashkr:

> Agent:  
> "Great, in that case you can track your order status in the Cashkr app or website. If you face any issue during pickup, you can reach out to our support team."

If they sold elsewhere:

> Agent:  
> "Understood. I will update this lead so you do not receive more sales calls for this device. If you have any other devices to sell in the future, you can always check prices on Cashkr again."

**Disposition:**

- Follow the rules in [Understanding Dispositions (Connected, Not Connected, Create Order)](/understanding-dispositions-connected-not-connected-create-order), typically **Connected → Already Placed an Order** or equivalent, with notes on where they sold.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Pickup not available in their area or for their device">

**Customer example:** "Pickup is not available in my area" or "Your app shows not serviceable."

**Key points:**

- Acknowledge the limitation.  
- Do not promise future availability dates unless documented.  
- Guide them to re-check later or stay updated.

**Sample lines (align with Pickup Not Available Script):**

> Agent:  
> "I checked, and currently we are not servicing pickups in your area or for this specific combination. I understand that is disappointing."

If there is a documented way to stay updated (for example, checking again after some time):

> Agent:  
> "You can check again on the Cashkr app or website after some time, as we keep expanding our service areas. As soon as pickup becomes available, it will show there first."

**Disposition:**

- Use an appropriate **Connected** disposition with notes: "Pickup not available for pincode 400099 currently, informed customer."

For detailed wording and more examples, see [Pickup Not Available Script](/pickup-not-available-script).

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Disposition and next action

Every call must end with **one correct disposition** and a **clear note**, as per the golden rule in [Understanding Dispositions (Connected, Not Connected, Create Order)](/understanding-dispositions-connected-not-connected-create-order).

Key points to remember:

- Mark the call as **Connected** only if you actually spoke to the customer.  
- Mark as **Not Connected** if you did not speak to them (ringing, switched off, no answer, wrong number, etc.).  
- Use **Create Order** only when you have **actually created the order in the system** during or immediately after the call. Do not select **Create Order** just because the customer said they will sell.  
- For callbacks, once you promise a time, **honour that callback** and record the outcome after each attempt.  
- Always write **specific notes**: what the customer said, any objections, what you explained, and what was agreed as the next step.

For a full list of dispositions, definitions, and examples, read:
