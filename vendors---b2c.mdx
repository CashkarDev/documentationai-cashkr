## Overview

Use the **All Inventory** module in the Admin Panel to see all devices that have been completed by internal vendors, received into stock, and are available for resale.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Only devices completed through internal vendor orders appear in All Inventory. Externally completed vendor orders do not show in this module.
</Callout>

---

## Inventory sources

Devices in All Inventory come from three stock sources. Use the source to understand how the device entered your inventory and which team owns it.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="B2C In-house" icon="home">
    **B2C In-house** covers devices completed by in-house B2C vendors.\
    These are devices purchased from individual customers through B2C operations and processed by your internal vendor network.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="B2C Excess" icon="package">
    **B2C Excess** tracks excess stock from B2C operations.\
    These are devices that remain in stock after B2C purchase and processing.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="B2B Buybacks" icon="briefcase">
    **B2B Buybacks** includes devices received through corporate B2B buyback orders.\
    These devices typically arrive in bulk from business clients.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Filters and controls

Use filters and controls at the top of the All Inventory page to narrow the list, view older stock, or export data.

<ParamField body="search" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Search bar that lets you find devices by:

  - **Device name** (for example, `Oppo F31`, `Asus ROG`)

  - **Serial number**

  Type your term and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to apply the search.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="sourceFilter" param-type="dropdown" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Source filter that limits inventory to a specific stock source:

  - **All Sources** — shows the full inventory

  - **B2C In-house** — only in-house completed devices

  - **B2C Excess** — only excess B2C stock

  - **B2B Buybacks** — only corporate buyback devices
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="showOldInventory" param-type="checkbox" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Toggle that includes older inventory entries that are no longer in active circulation. Use this to trace historical stock or reconcile past sales.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="export" param-type="button" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Action that downloads the current filtered inventory list as a file. Use it for reporting, audits, or sharing with finance and operations.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="refresh" param-type="button" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Action that reloads the latest inventory data so new internal vendor completions and recent changes appear in the table.
</ParamField>

## Inventory table columns

Each row in the All Inventory table represents a single device and includes the following columns.

<ParamField body="date" param-type="date" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Date when the device was added to inventory after internal vendor completion.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="device" param-type="string" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Device name and model, such as `iPhone 12` or `Samsung Galaxy S21`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="specCondition" param-type="string" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Combined specification and condition details, including RAM, storage, and condition grade.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="serial" param-type="string" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Unique device serial number.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="source" param-type="enum" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Stock source label for the device: `B2C In-house`, `B2C Excess`, or `B2B Buybacks`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="price" param-type="currency" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Cost price of the device in your base currency.
</ParamField>

## How devices enter inventory

Devices move into All Inventory automatically when an internal vendor completes an order. No manual inventory entry is required.

```plaintext
Internal vendor completes order
        ↓
Device added to All Inventory
        ↓
Source tagged (B2C In-house / B2C Excess / B2B Buybacks)
```

## Punch sale

Use **Punch Sale** to sell one or more devices directly from All Inventory to a dealer.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Before you punch a sale, confirm that:

  - The device details and source are correct

  - The target customer exists in the **Customers (Dealers)** module or you are ready to add a new dealer

  - You know the selling price and payment method
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select device rows" title-type="p">
    Select the checkbox on the left of each device you want to sell.\
    You can select a single device or multiple devices for a bulk sale.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Punch Sale" title-type="p">
    After selecting the devices, click the **Punch Sale** button that appears above the table.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the customer (dealer)" title-type="p">
    In the popup, select an existing dealer from the customer list.\
    If the dealer is not yet created, click **Add New Customer** and complete the dealer details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter selling price per device" title-type="p">
    For each selected device, enter the **Selling Price**.\
    The system calculates the **Total Sale Value** automatically based on your entries.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select payment method" title-type="p">
    Choose the payment method used for this sale, such as **Cash** or any other available payment option.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm and finalize the sale" title-type="p">
    Review the devices, dealer, prices, and payment method, then click **Confirm Sale**.\
    On confirmation, the devices are removed from All Inventory and a sale record is created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Once you confirm a sale, the device is permanently removed from All Inventory and cannot be sold again from this view.\
  Use the Device Sales module to review or audit confirmed sales.
</Callout>

## After a sale

After you complete the Punch Sale flow, the system updates inventory and finance records automatically.

```plaintext
Device selected → Punch Sale → Dealer selected → Sale confirmed
        ↓
Device removed from All Inventory
        ↓
New entry created in Device Sales page
```

The corresponding entry in the **Device Sales** module includes:

- Device name and model

- Serial number

- Selling price

- Customer (dealer) name

- Payment method

- Sale date

---

## View device details

Click any device row in All Inventory to open its detail view.

The detail view shows:

- Device model, variant, and specifications

- Source type (`B2C In-house`, `B2C Excess`, or `B2B Buybacks`)

- Cost price

- Serial number

- Completion date from the internal vendor

Use this view to verify information before punching a sale or during reconciliations.

---

## Related modules

All Inventory works closely with other Admin Panel modules:

- **Order Completion module** — pushes completed internal vendor orders into All Inventory as new stock.

- **Credit deduction process** — adjusts vendor or partner credits at the point of order completion.

- **Device Sales** — receives devices after a Punch Sale; use it for sales tracking and reporting.

- **Customers (Dealers)** — stores dealer records that you select during the Punch Sale flow.
