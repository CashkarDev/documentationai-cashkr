## Overview

Unlock Lead lets you take ownership of a customer lead in your service area so you can move it into your pickup workflow.

The screen shows a list of **available leads** within your configured service area, each with an option to **Unlock** the lead for pickup. Leads shown here come from the [Available Orders](/available-orders) list.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  - The system checks your **available credits** and **max lead cap** before every unlock.

  - On successful unlock, the order moves from **Available Orders** to your **InProgress Orders** list.

  - The system **deducts credits per lead** only when the unlock succeeds.
</Callout>

## Unlock flow for vendors

Use this flow whenever you want to take a new customer lead.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Available Leads" icon="list-ordered" title-type="p">
    Open the **Available Leads** tab in the Vendor App. This shows all leads in your service area that are not yet assigned to any vendor.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review lead details" icon="eye" title-type="p">
    Tap a lead to review key information such as customer location, device details, offered price, and preferred pickup slot. Confirm that the lead fits your service area and capacity.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Attempt to unlock" icon="lock" title-type="p">
    Tap **Unlock** on the selected lead. The system records your unlock attempt and starts the gating checks.
  </Step>

  <Step title="System runs gating checks" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    Behind the scenes, the system validates:

    - **Available credits**: Your credit balance must cover the configured **per lead** cost.

    - **Max lead cap**: You must be below your configured **Max Lead** limit for active orders.

    If both checks pass, the unlock continues. If any check fails, the unlock is blocked and you see an error message.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Lead moves to In Progress on success" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    When the unlock succeeds:

    - The order disappears from **Available Orders** and is no longer visible to other vendors.

    - The order appears in your **InProgress Orders** list as an active pickup.

    - The system deducts the relevant **credits per lead** from your balance.

    You can now manage the order through [InProgress Orders](/inprogress-orders) and continue with call disposition, reschedule, or pickup actions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## System behavior and logic

This section explains what the system does when you unlock a lead.

### Credit deduction

The platform charges you only for successful unlocks.

- The system **deducts credits per lead** immediately after a successful unlock.

- If the unlock fails for any reason, the system **does not deduct credits**.

- You can review and purchase more credits from the [Buy Credits](/buy-credits) section. Credits become usable only after admin approval, then they are available for unlocks.

### Max lead and reservation rules

The Max Lead limit controls how many active leads you can handle at the same time.

- If unlocking this lead would **exceed your Max Lead cap**, the system blocks the unlock and may treat the order as **reserved** based on configuration.

- Reserved behavior is explained in more detail in [Reserved Order](/reserved-order).

- When you complete or fail existing orders and drop below the Max Lead cap, you become eligible to unlock new leads again.

### Same customer auto-assignment

When you unlock a lead for a customer, the system tries to keep all that customer's related orders with you.

- If the same customer creates **additional orders**, the system can **auto-assign** those orders to you instead of exposing them to all vendors.

- This reduces duplicate visits and improves coordination with the customer.

- For the full rules on how related orders are linked and assigned, see [Same Customer Order](/same-customer-order).

## Where the order goes after unlock

After a successful unlock:

- The order leaves the shared [Available Orders](/available-orders) pool.

- The order appears under **In Progress** on the **InProgress Orders** screen.

- Only **unlocked orders** appear in InProgress; you will not see locked/available orders there.

Refer to [InProgress Orders](/inprogress-orders) for how to manage orders after unlock.

## Common outcomes and error states

### Unlock success

The unlock succeeds when:

- You have **enough approved credits** for at least one lead.

- You are **below the Max Lead cap**.

- The lead is still **available** and not taken by another vendor.

You see the lead move into **In Progress**, and your credit balance decreases by the configured per-lead cost.

### Insufficient credits

If your **credit balance is too low**, the system blocks the unlock:

- The unlock button action fails with an error related to **insufficient credits**.

- The order remains in **Available Leads** for other eligible vendors.

- You do not lose any credits in this case.

To fix this, go to [Buy Credits](/buy-credits), add credits, wait for admin approval, then try unlocking again.

### Max lead reached

If you have already reached your **Max Lead** limit:

- The system prevents the unlock and shows an error related to **Max Lead reached** or capacity limits.

- In some configurations, the order may be **reserved** and kept from other vendors until your capacity frees up.

- You must complete, fail, or close existing In Progress orders before unlocking more leads.

See [Max Lead](/max-lead) and [Reserved Order](/reserved-order) for detailed rules.

### Lead already taken

Because multiple vendors can see the same available lead, another vendor might unlock it first:

- If another vendor unlocks the lead before your attempt is processed, your unlock fails with a **lead already taken** message.

- The lead disappears from your **Available Leads** list.

- No credits are deducted from your account for a failed unlock of this type.

In this case, select another available lead and try again.

## Admin connections

Unlock behavior depends on admin-side configuration and monitoring.

- **Vendor credits**: Admins manage your credit purchases, approvals, and adjustments from the **Vendor Credits** section. Only approved credits count toward unlock eligibility.

- **Max Lead config**: Admins configure your **Max Lead** limit, which controls how many active leads you can hold in In Progress at once.

- **Order status in Admin Panel**: In the **Orders** section of the Admin Panel, admins see when an order is **locked by a vendor**, which vendor owns it, and how it progresses through the lifecycle.

These admin settings explain why an unlock may be allowed or blocked for your account.

## Developer and tester notes

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  - When testing unlocks, vary **credit balance** and **Max Lead** values to verify all outcomes: success, insufficient credits, max lead reached, and lead taken by another vendor.

  - Confirm that **credits are only deducted** on successful unlocks, not on failed attempts.

  - Validate **state transitions** in both Vendor App and Admin Panel: Available Orders → Unlock Lead → InProgress Orders, and verify the locked-by-vendor flag in the Admin order view.

  - For regression testing of related-order logic, include scenarios where the same customer creates multiple orders and confirm **auto-assignment** rules match [Same Customer Order](/same-customer-order).
