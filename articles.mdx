## What the Articles page shows

The Articles page shows a scrollable or paginated list of promotional articles published by the Marketing team via Strapi CMS.

<Callout kind="info">

- The page shows a scrollable or paginated list or grid of articles with thumbnail, title, and short preview.
- When a user clicks an article card, the full article detail page opens.
- The website fetches articles dynamically from Strapi CMS; publish or update in Strapi reflects on the site in real time.
- The page and each article support SEO visibility using meta tags so marketing content can rank in search.

</Callout>

## Step-by-step user flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Articles page" icon="folder" title-type="p">

  - User navigates to the Articles section from the homepage navbar or footer.
  - The browser loads the Articles list view.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Browse available articles" icon="book-open" title-type="p">

  - User scrolls through the list or paginated view, or scans a grid of article cards.
  - Each card shows a thumbnail image, article title, and a short preview or summary.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Open an article" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">

  - User clicks an article card from the list or grid.
  - The website opens the article detail view with full content.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## System logic

Articles on this page always come from Strapi CMS and update in real time when marketing changes content.

- Fetch articles dynamically from Strapi CMS, rather than hardcoding content in the website.
- Reflect newly published or updated articles immediately on the Articles list and detail pages.
- Apply SEO meta tags to the Articles list and individual article pages to support organic search visibility.

## Connections

The Articles page connects the marketing content workflow in Strapi to the public website.

- Integrates with Strapi CMS where the marketing team uploads and updates article content.
- Surfaces that content on the website as a scrollable or paginated list or grid view.
- Uses SEO meta tags per article to help drive organic traffic from search engines.

## Developer and tester notes

**For developers**

- Ensure dynamic fetching of articles from Strapi CMS, including thumbnails, titles, previews, and full content.
- Implement and verify real-time reflection of newly published or edited articles from Strapi on the website.
- Add and maintain SEO meta tags for the Articles list and each article detail page (title, description, and any required structured data).

**For testers**

- Verify that newly published articles in Strapi appear on the Articles page without delay.
- Check article linking and content: each card should open the correct detail page with complete, readable content and no broken links.
- Confirm that SEO meta tags exist and are correct on the Articles list page and each article detail page.
