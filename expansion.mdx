## Overview

**Sidebar path:** Dashboards → OPERATING → Growth → **Geography** tab (`/dashboard/growth?tab=geography`)

**Geographic Expansion** shows city readiness, vendor demand and coverage, so BD and Vendor Ops can decide where to add vendors or expand.

## Filters

| Control | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Date range** | Defaults to the last 30 days. |
| **AI Analyst Insight → Analyze Data** | Generates an AI summary. |

## What the screen shows

### KPI cards

Cities, Total Leads, Orders, Completed, Failed, Comp %, Uncovered, Pressure.

### Health

A *Weak* → *Strong* band with Cities, Gaps, Ready and Demand (leads per vendor), for example *Gaps Found*.

### Panels

| Panel | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Coverage Stats** | Active Cities, Covered Cities %, No Vendors, Uncovered Leads, Uncovered Pincodes. |
| **Expansion Readiness** | Ready Cities, Completion Rate, Avg Demand/Vendor, High Performers, Low Completion. |
| **Vendor Demand** | Pressure Zones, Avg Demand Ratio, and the cities with the most leads per vendor. |
| **Risk Zones** | Coverage Gaps, Failed Orders, and at-risk cities with vendor count and completion. |
| **Daily Expansion & Order Trend** | Leads, orders and completed per day. |
| **Expansion Readiness (top cities)** | Readiness score per city with Leads, Comp % and Vendors. |
| **Vendor Demand Heatmap** | Leads per vendor, Leads, Vendors and avg Pickup time per city. |

### City Master Table

Switch between **City Master Table** and **Coverage Gaps**.

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **City** | City and main device category |
| **Leads / Orders / Completed / Failed** | Volume in the period |
| **Comp %** | Completion rate |
| **Vendors** | Live vendors serving the city |
| **Demand** | Leads per vendor |
| **Readiness** | Expansion readiness score |

A footer shows how many cities are **Covered** and how many have **No Vendors**.

## Actions

The tab is read-only.

<Callout kind="tip">
A city with high **Demand** (many leads per vendor) and a good readiness score is a candidate for onboarding more vendors. Cities in **Coverage Gaps** should go to Vendor Ops first.
</Callout>
