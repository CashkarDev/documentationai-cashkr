---

## Objective

To write compelling, pre-approved captions for all festive and occasion-based social media posts — aligned with visuals and brand tone — and managed entirely within Canva.

## Tools Required

- **Canva (Pro Workspace)** – For visual design, caption writing, versioning, and approvals
- **Slack / WhatsApp / Email** – For coordination onl

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **Graphic Designer:** Completes all festive creatives first in Canva

- **Social Media Executive / Content Writer:** Drafts captions directly within Canva after visuals are ready

- **Marketing Manager:** Reviews and approves captions within Canva

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1: Access Final Creative Design">
    - Open the relevant festive post design in **Canva**

    - Review the creative for:

      - Theme/tone

      - Visual hook

      - Product or service emphasis

      - Space availability for text alignment
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 2: Draft Caption Using Cashkr Format">
    All captions must follow this 4-part structure:

    **FESTIVE / EVENT CAPTION FORMAT:**

    **1️⃣ Hook (First 125 characters)**

    - Catchy, emotion-triggering, curiosity-based or offer-driven

    - Should tie in with the festival or event

    - Avoid plain "Wishing you a Happy..." unless stylized creatively

    ✅ *Examples:*

    - “Still storing your old phone during Diwali? Let’s change that! 🎇”

    - “This Holi, colour your wallet with cashback! 💸🎨”

    **2️⃣ Value / Story Section**

    - Tie Cashkr’s value proposition with the event

    - Emphasize ease, speed, environment, or gifting

    ✅ *Examples:*

    - “Festivals mean upgrades. Sell your old devices for instant cash with Cashkr.”

    - “Each device you sell avoids landfills — make this Earth Day count 🌍.”

    **3️⃣ CTA (Call-to-Action)**

    - Tell the user exactly what to do

    - Platform-specific CTA: "Link in bio", "DM us", "Comment BONUS" etc.

    ✅ *Examples:*

    - “Tap the link in bio to get a quote now”

    - “Comment BONUS to unlock ₹200 extra this Diwali!”

    **4️⃣ Hashtags (10–20 max)**\
    Rotate across the 4 types to ensure reach and relevance:

| Type                 | Examples                                        |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Branded**          | #Cashkr #SellWithCashkr #CashkrFestive          |
| **Offer-Based**      | #SellOldPhone #InstantCash #TradeInPhone        |
| **Geo-Targeted**     | #MumbaiDeals #DelhiOffers #HyderabadTech        |
| **Festive/Seasonal** | #DiwaliBonus #RecycleTech #HoliSale #RakhiGifts |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 3: Add Captions in Canva Directly">
    - Use **Canva's comment box** or add a caption text box inside the file for each format
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 4: Internal Review & Approval">
    - Tag the **Marketing Manager** inside Canva for caption approval

    - Use Canva comments for revisions

    - Limit to **2 rounds of changes**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 5: Lock Approved Version">
    - Once approved:

      - Confirm caption placement is final inside Canva

      - Add “✅ Approved” label or tag to the design

      - Mark visually as **Ready for Scheduling**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 6: Handoff for Scheduling">
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Notes

- Captions should support, not repeat, the creative visual

- Emojis are optional — use them to enhance tone, not distract

- Avoid generic festive language unless repackaged creatively

- Keep tone aligned with Cashkr’s voice: direct, friendly, trustworthy
