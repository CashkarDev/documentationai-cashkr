## Why this matters

Consistent, clear communication during pickup builds customer trust, reduces complaints and SLA issues, and keeps you compliant with Cashkr rules.

Use this page as your standard script set for all pickups. Combine it with:

- [Order Tracking and Customer Notifications](/order-tracking-customer-notifications)
- [Handling Customer Issues During Pickup](/handling-customer-issues-during-pickup)

## Quick checklist for every pickup

You are ready for pickup communication when you:

- Understand the 4 touchpoints: after unlock, before arrival, during inspection, payment and completion.
- Use respectful, professional language in all calls and messages.
- Explain that prices and changes are system-based, not your personal decision.
- Follow the OTP flow correctly and never bypass it.
- Know how to respond calmly if the customer disagrees with price, is not available, or refuses to sell.

## End-to-end communication timeline

Use this sequence for every pickup. Timings are guidance; follow local conditions and SLAs.

<Steps>

<Step title="After order unlock" icon="phone">

- Call the customer as soon as possible (ideally within 10 minutes of unlocking the order).
- Introduce yourself, confirm the device and address, and agree on a convenient pickup time.
- Mark the correct disposition and status in the vendor app after the call. See [Out for Pickup or Fail](/out-for-pickup-or-fail).

</Step>

<Step title="Before reaching the customer" icon="arrow-right">

- Call again shortly before you arrive (ideally 10–20 minutes before).
- Confirm the customer is available and share your estimated time of arrival.
- If you need to reschedule, align on a new time and update the order using [Reschedule](/reschedule) or [Reschedule Pickup - SOPs](/reschedule-pickup---sops).

</Step>

<Step title="During pickup and inspection" icon="monitor">

- Greet the customer politely, clearly connect yourself with Cashkr, and request permission to start inspection.
- Explain what you are checking and that the final price is calculated by the app based on actual condition.
- If the final price changes from the initial quote, use the standard language from [Price Change Explanation Script](/price-change-explanation-script).

</Step>

<Step title="Payment, OTP, and completion" icon="check-circle">

- Explain the OTP-based payment flow before starting payment.
- Ask for the OTP only after the customer confirms they are ready to proceed.
- Complete the payment in the app, confirm success on both sides, and close the order correctly (complete, reschedule, or failure based on the approved flow such as [Fail After Requote](/fail-after-requote)).

</Step>

</Steps>

## The 4 communication touchpoints

These touchpoints match the customer-visible stages described in [Order Tracking and Customer Notifications](/order-tracking-customer-notifications).

1. After order unlock (Vendor Assigned / En Route planned)
2. Before arrival (En Route, confirming availability)
3. During inspection (Arrived / Inspection in Progress / Final Price Generated)
4. Payment and completion (OTP Payment / Completed or Failed)

Each touchpoint below includes call scripts and WhatsApp or SMS templates.

## Stage 1: After order unlock

Contact the customer quickly so they know who you are and what will happen next.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Call scripts" icon="phone">

**Goal:** Introduce yourself, confirm details, and agree on pickup time.

**Script 1 – First contact after unlock**

"Hello [Customer Name], I am [Your Name] calling from Cashkr regarding your order to sell your [Device]. I see your pickup address as [Area / Landmark]. Is this correct?"

(If yes)

"Great, I would like to schedule the inspection and payment at your location. What time today or tomorrow works best for you?"

(If time agreed)

"Thank you. I will plan to reach around [Agreed Time]. I will call you again before I start so you know I am on the way."

**Script 2 – Customer not reachable (voicemail / short message on call)**

"Hello [Customer Name], this is [Your Name] from Cashkr regarding your order to sell your [Device]. I tried reaching you to schedule the pickup. You can call me back on this number, or I will try again later."

Remember to log the correct disposition in the app. See [Understanding Dispositions (Connected, Not Connected, Create Order)](/understanding-dispositions-connected-not-connected-create-order).

</Tab>

<Tab title="WhatsApp/SMS templates" icon="message-square">

Use these only as support to calls, not as a replacement for mandatory call attempts.

**Template 1 – After missed call / unable to connect**

"Hi [Customer Name], this is [Your Name] from Cashkr regarding your order to sell your [Device]. I tried calling you to schedule the pickup. Please call or reply with a suitable time for inspection and payment at your address."

**Template 2 – Pickup time confirmation**

"Hi [Customer Name], your Cashkr pickup for [Device] is scheduled for approximately [Date] at [Time] at [Short Address / Area]. I will call you before I start so you know I am on the way."

Align your communication with the statuses used in [Track Order and Order Stages](/track-order-order-stages) so the customer message and app status stay consistent.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Stage 2: Before reaching the customer

Reconnect shortly before you arrive so the customer is ready and feels informed.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Call scripts" icon="phone">

**Goal:** Confirm availability and share a clear ETA.

**Script 1 – On the way**

"Hello [Customer Name], this is [Your Name] from Cashkr. I am on the way for your [Device] pickup and inspection. I expect to reach in about [ETA, for example 20 minutes]. Will you be available at [Address / Landmark] at that time?"

(If customer asks you to wait or adjust slightly)

"No problem, I can adjust by about [X] minutes. I will plan for [Updated Time] and call you when I reach the building or gate."

**Script 2 – Running late**

"Hello [Customer Name], this is [Your Name] from Cashkr. I am getting delayed due to [short neutral reason, for example traffic on main road], and I will now reach around [New ETA]. Is that still convenient for you, or would you like to reschedule to another time today or tomorrow?"

If the customer wants to change the slot, follow [Reschedule](/reschedule) and [Reschedule Pickup - SOPs](/reschedule-pickup---sops).

</Tab>

<Tab title="WhatsApp/SMS templates" icon="message-square">

**Template 1 – On the way update**

"Hi [Customer Name], this is [Your Name] from Cashkr. I am on the way for your [Device] pickup and expect to reach in about [ETA]. Please keep the device and any required accessories ready for inspection."

**Template 2 – Delay information**

"Hi [Customer Name], due to traffic I will now reach for your Cashkr [Device] pickup at approximately [New ETA]. If this does not work, reply with a better time and I will reschedule accordingly."

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Stage 3: During pickup and inspection

Your behavior during inspection strongly affects trust and price acceptance.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Call scripts" icon="phone">

These lines are mostly spoken in person at the door, but you may use them on call if needed (for example, asking the customer to come to the gate).

**Greeting and identification**

"Hello [Customer Name], I am [Your Name] from Cashkr, here for the pickup and inspection of your [Device]. May I come in and check the device now?"

**Explaining the inspection**

"During this inspection, I will check the body condition, screen, battery health, and basic functions of the [Device]. The Cashkr app will calculate the final price based on the actual condition."

(While noticing an issue)

"I can see that the [Issue, for example screen has a visible crack / battery health is lower]. Based on this, the system may adjust the final price compared to the initial quote shown in the app."

**Explaining system-based pricing**

"The final price is fully system-defined in the Cashkr app. I do not have permission to manually increase or decrease it. If the condition is different from what was selected earlier, the app updates the price automatically."

For more detailed phrases and objections, follow [Price Change Explanation Script](/price-change-explanation-script).

</Tab>

<Tab title="WhatsApp/SMS templates" icon="message-square">

Use messages here only if the customer is not physically present and you need written confirmation to proceed or to wait.

**Template 1 – Customer not at exact location**

"Hi [Customer Name], I have reached near [Landmark] for your Cashkr [Device] pickup. Please confirm if you are nearby or share any specific directions."

**Template 2 – Cannot proceed without customer**

"Hi [Customer Name], I am at [Location] for your Cashkr [Device] pickup but I am unable to connect with you. Please call or message within [short time window, for example 10–15 minutes] so we can complete today; otherwise I may need to reschedule or mark the pickup as not completed."

When considering fail or reschedule, follow the rules in [Handling Customer Issues During Pickup](/handling-customer-issues-during-pickup) and [Out for Pickup or Fail](/out-for-pickup-or-fail).

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Stage 4: Payment, OTP, and completion

OTP-based payment protects both customer and Cashkr. Always seek clear consent before completing payment.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Call scripts" icon="phone">

**Explaining OTP-based payment**

"Now I will initiate the payment through the Cashkr app. You will receive a one-time password (OTP) on your registered mobile number. Once you share that OTP with me, I will confirm the payment and the amount will be processed to your account."

(If customer asks why OTP is required)

"The OTP confirms that you agree to the final price and that the payment is going to the correct person. Without your OTP, the payment cannot be completed."

**Checking payment success**

"I have completed the payment in the Cashkr app. You should receive an SMS or bank notification shortly. Could you please check your messages or bank app and confirm that you have received the payment?"

**Closing the interaction**

"Thank you for selling your [Device] through Cashkr. If you face any issue with this transaction, you can reach Cashkr support from the app."

If the customer refuses to proceed at this stage, follow the appropriate flow from [Handling Customer Issues During Pickup](/handling-customer-issues-during-pickup), including [Fail After Requote](/fail-after-requote) if applicable.

</Tab>

<Tab title="WhatsApp/SMS templates" icon="message-square">

**Template 1 – Payment initiation confirmation**

"Hi [Customer Name], I have initiated the Cashkr payment for your [Device] at the agreed final price of [Amount]. You will receive an OTP on your registered mobile number. Share this OTP only with me for this payment."

**Template 2 – Payment success confirmation**

"Hi [Customer Name], your Cashkr payment for [Device] has been completed. Please check your SMS or bank app for confirmation. Thank you for using Cashkr."

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Difficult situations and how to handle them

Use these patterns when interaction becomes challenging. Always combine with the detailed rules in [Handling Customer Issues During Pickup](/handling-customer-issues-during-pickup).

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Customer disputes the price or says &quot;price reduced&quot;" default-open="true">

**Goal:** Stay calm, explain system logic, and then follow the correct action (requote, reschedule, or fail).

**Suggested response**

"I understand this price is lower than what you saw earlier. The app has updated the price because during inspection we found [Condition Difference, for example lower battery health / screen damage]. The price is decided by the Cashkr system based on these actual conditions, and I do not have permission to change it manually."

(If customer still disagrees)

"No problem. If you are not comfortable with this final price, we do not have to complete the pickup. I can close this order for now, or we can see if it can be requoted as per the Cashkr policy."

Then choose the correct action according to policy:

- For requote scenarios, follow [Price Change Explanation Script](/price-change-explanation-script) and [Fail After Requote](/fail-after-requote).
- For refusal or non-availability, follow the classification and actions in [Handling Customer Issues During Pickup](/handling-customer-issues-during-pickup).

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Customer not available at address or asks to reschedule">

**Goal:** Avoid arguments, capture the correct reason, and reschedule or fail according to policy.

**Suggested response**

"I am currently at [Location / Landmark] for your Cashkr pickup. I understand you are not available right now. We can reschedule to a time that works better for you."

(If customer wants to reschedule)

"Which day and time is convenient for you? I will update the Cashkr system and you will see the new timing in your order details."

Then:

- Use the reschedule options in the vendor app as per [Reschedule](/reschedule) and [Reschedule Pickup - SOPs](/reschedule-pickup---sops).
- If policy requires failing the order (for example, repeated no-shows), follow [Out for Pickup or Fail](/out-for-pickup-or-fail) and [Handling Customer Issues During Pickup](/handling-customer-issues-during-pickup).

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="OTP not received or technical issue with payment">

**Goal:** Explain why OTP is needed, troubleshoot calmly, and avoid any workarounds.

**Suggested response**

"The OTP is required to confirm that you approve the final price and the payment. Without the OTP, the Cashkr system will not allow me to complete the transaction."

Steps to communicate:

- "Let us first confirm that your network is working and the SIM with this number is in the phone."
- "Sometimes the OTP can take a few minutes. Let us wait briefly and try again."

If OTP still not received or app has an issue:

- "It looks like there is a technical issue with receiving the OTP or completing the payment right now. I will not be able to complete the pickup without this. I will report this to Cashkr support and we can either wait for a fix or reschedule, depending on what support advises."

Do not take any alternate payment or bypass the app. Follow the escalation guidance in [Handling Customer Issues During Pickup](/handling-customer-issues-during-pickup).

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Customer has safety or data-privacy concerns">

**Goal:** Reassure the customer, especially about data and payment security.

**Suggested response**

"For your safety, Cashkr uses OTP-based payment so that the payment is made only after you approve the final price. Without your OTP, I cannot take the device or complete the payment."

(If customer asks about data on the device)

"It is best to back up your data and reset your [Device] before handing it over. If you want, I can guide you step by step on how to log out and reset from the device settings."

If the customer still feels unsafe or unsure:

"No problem, your comfort is important. If you are not comfortable right now, we can stop here and you can decide later. I can request to close or reschedule this order as per your preference."

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Customer refuses to sell after inspection or at payment stage">

**Goal:** Respect the decision, avoid pressure, and close correctly.

**Suggested response**

"That is completely fine. If you are not comfortable with the final price or with completing the sale today, we will not proceed."

(Then add as needed)

"I will update the Cashkr system that the pickup did not happen because you did not wish to proceed at the final price. You can always create a new order later if you change your mind."

Then follow the correct disposition and failure or reschedule flow described in [Handling Customer Issues During Pickup](/handling-customer-issues-during-pickup) and [Out for Pickup or Fail](/out-for-pickup-or-fail).

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Do and don’t guidelines

These guidelines apply at every stage of the pickup.

### Do

- Use a calm, respectful, and professional tone in all calls and messages.
- Introduce yourself with your name and mention Cashkr clearly.
- Confirm key details: customer name, device model, address, and approximate time.
- Explain that the Cashkr app calculates the final price based on actual condition.
- Keep the customer informed if you are running early or late.
- Listen fully to customer concerns before responding.
- Follow the approved flows for reschedule, requote, and failure using the correct SOP pages.

### Don’t

- Do not argue with the customer about price, condition, or policy.
- Do not promise anything outside Cashkr policy or beyond what the app shows.
- Do not complain about traffic, distance, or your personal issues.
- Do not use slang, harsh language, sarcasm, or personal comments.
- Do not pressure the customer to sell if they are uncomfortable or not ready.
- Do not replace required calls with only WhatsApp or SMS messages.

## Red-flag behaviors (strictly prohibited)

Certain behaviors create serious risk for customers and for Cashkr. These are strictly prohibited and may lead to SLA strikes, temporary hold, or permanent deactivation as per [SLA Violations and Escalation Rules](/sla-violations-escalation-rules) and [Vendor Hold and Deactivation Process](/vendor-hold-deactivation-process).

<Callout kind="alert">

The following actions are not allowed under any circumstances:

- Arguing, shouting, or using disrespectful language with customers or their family members.
- Threatening, intimidating, or pressuring the customer to accept a price or complete the sale.
- Asking the customer for any extra money beyond what is shown in the Cashkr app.
- Blaming Cashkr or making negative statements about the company or the app to justify price or policy.
- Taking payment in any form outside the Cashkr system or attempting to settle directly with the customer.
- Bypassing the OTP flow, guessing OTPs, or asking the customer to share screenshots of OTP messages instead of reading the code.
- Editing order details, address, or condition in a way that does not match reality in order to influence price.
- Sharing customer data, photos, or device information with anyone for non-Cashkr purposes.

</Callout>

If you are unsure how to handle a difficult situation, stop, stay calm, and follow [Handling Customer Issues During Pickup](/handling-customer-issues-during-pickup) or contact the designated support channel instead of improvising.

## Safety, OTP, and compliance reminders

<Callout kind="alert">

- Always complete payment only through the Cashkr app using the official OTP flow described in [Vendor Payment OTP and Confirmation Flow](/vendor-payment-otp-confirmation-flow).
- Never suggest or accept direct cash, UPI, or other off-app payment arrangements for Cashkr orders.
- Never proceed with pickup if the customer clearly says they do not agree with the final price or are not comfortable sharing the OTP.

</Callout>

Following these communication practices keeps you aligned with Cashkr policies, protects customers, and helps you maintain a strong vendor record with fewer disputes and escalations.
