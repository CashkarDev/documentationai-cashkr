## Overview and when to use this script

<Callout kind="info">

Use this script **after** the corporate client has accepted the quotation and shared basic requirements in the [Requirement Gathering Script (Corporate Buyback)](/requirement-gathering-script-corporate-buyback).

Your objective now is to:

- Confirm final commercial and logistics details
- Align on payment mode and timing
- Reinforce that pricing is subject to physical verification
- Hand off cleanly to operations and finance

</Callout>

**Used by:** B2B Sales Executive  
**Outcome:** Deal marked as confirmed, pickup scheduled, payment expectations aligned, and internal teams informed.

---

## Closure call flow (high level)

<Steps>

<Step title="Set the context and confirm acceptance" icon="phone">

Open the call, thank the client for confirming the quotation, and state that you are calling to lock final pickup and payment details.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm final deal details" icon="check-circle">

Read back device type, quantity, pickup location, agreed pricing structure, chosen payment mode, and tentative pickup date and time. Pause and let the client correct anything.

</Step>

<Step title="Reassure process, inspection and payment" icon="info">

Explain how the pickup, physical verification, invoice, and payment will flow. Clearly mention that pricing is subject to physical verification and that payment timing follows the agreed payment mode.

</Step>

<Step title="Prepare them for pickup" icon="settings">

Share what you need ready on pickup day: devices accessible, access permissions, point of contact, and any asset tags or lists.

</Step>

<Step title="Close professionally and confirm next communication" icon="arrow-right">

Summarize next steps, confirm they will receive a written email summary, and mention the point of contact from the operations team.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Scripts and templates

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Call script (closure confirmation)" icon="phone">

### Opening

> Hi \[Name], this is \[Your Name] from Cashkr.  
> Thank you for confirming the buyback quotation. I am calling to quickly review the final details and align on pickup and payment so we can move this to operations and finance.

---

### 1. Confirm final deal details

> Just to reconfirm, you will be selling:
>
> - Device type: \[for example, laptops, phones, tablets]  
> - Total quantity: \[XX units]  
> - Pickup location: \[full address]  
> - Pricing: \[total amount or per-unit price range], subject to physical verification at pickup  
> - Payment mode: \[NEFT / RTGS / UPI transfer / bank transfer after invoice / immediate transfer at pickup]  
> - Tentative pickup date and time: \[agreed date and time]
>
> Does this look accurate, or is there anything you would like to adjust?

Pause and let them confirm or correct details.

---

### 2. Reassure process, inspection and payment

> On the pickup day, our assigned team will visit your office for inspection.  
> They will count the devices, match them with your shared list, and do a basic physical check.

> Our quotation and final value are always subject to physical verification. If we notice any major mismatch in quantity or condition, we will first discuss and agree with you before proceeding.

> Based on the payment mode we have agreed:
>
> - If we have aligned on **immediate transfer at pickup**, our team will initiate the transfer once the inspection is completed and you confirm the final device list on-site.  
> - If we have aligned on **post-pickup payment**, you can raise the invoice to Cashkr Technologies Pvt. Ltd. after pickup and verification, and our finance team will process the payment within the agreed timeline.

If the client has asked for data wipe certificates:

> For data wiping, we will issue digital data wipe certificates within 48 hours of pickup for all serial numbers that you share and that our team verifies.

---

### 3. Prepare for pickup

> To make pickup smooth, it would help if:
>
> - All devices are kept ready and easily accessible for inspection  
> - Any devices stored in different locations are clearly marked or informed to us  
> - Necessary access permissions for security or IT areas are pre-arranged

> One day before pickup, we will share the pickup team details and contact numbers with you, so your team can coordinate directly on timing and access.

---

### 4. Close the loop professionally

> Thank you for choosing Cashkr for your IT asset liquidation. Our focus is to keep the process clear, documented, and predictable for your team.

> After the pickup and payment are completed as per our agreed mode, you will receive a payment acknowledgment and pickup confirmation for your internal records.

> If this works well for your organization, we would be happy to support future refresh cycles or recurring buyback requirements as well.

<Tab title="Internal handoff checklist" icon="settings">

Use this checklist after the closure call and email to move the case from sales to operations and finance.

### Sales to operations

- Log the deal as **Confirmed – Pickup scheduled** in the tracker, with:
  - Company name and contact person
  - Final agreed quantity and device types
  - Pickup address and access notes
  - Agreed pickup date and time window
  - Agreed payment mode and expected payment timeline
  - Whether data wipe certificates are required

- Share closure email copy or summary with the operations team.

- Confirm operations has:
  - Assigned a pickup team or vendor
  - Contact details for the on-ground representative
  - Any special instructions from the client (security, parking, asset tags, packaging)

### Operations to finance

- Operations team to update:
  - Actual picked quantity and any deviations
  - Final approved value after verification
  - Confirmation that pickup is complete

- Once the client shares the invoice:
  - Verify that invoice details match the final agreed value and company details
  - Forward invoice and pickup confirmation to finance with clear subject and internal notes

### Finance and closure

- Finance to process payment as per:
  - Agreed payment mode (NEFT / RTGS / UPI / bank transfer)
  - Agreed payment timeline (for example, same day, next working day, within X working days)

- After payment:
  - Share payment acknowledgment slip with the client
  - Sales to update status as **Closed – Payment acknowledged** in the tracker

## Common questions and objections

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Can we raise the invoice before pickup?" default-open="true">

Many corporate clients prefer to raise the invoice early.

> You can say:  
> "For most buyback cases, we recommend raising the invoice after pickup and final physical verification so that the invoice exactly matches the final picked quantity and value. If your internal process needs a pre-pickup document, we can share a proforma or confirmation email, and then you can raise the final invoice post-verification."

Keep the principle clear: **final invoice should match verified quantity and final agreed value**.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="When exactly will we receive the payment?">

Align payment timing to the mode confirmed earlier.

> You can say:  
> "Payment timing will follow the mode we have agreed together. If we have agreed on immediate transfer at pickup, our team will initiate the transfer after completing inspection and final confirmation on-site. If we have agreed on post-pickup payment, finance will process the transfer within the agreed timeline after pickup, verification, and invoice receipt."

Avoid promising "instant" payment without linking it to the chosen mode and standard processing timelines.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Are data wipe certificates included?">

Some clients need certificates for compliance.

> You can say:  
> "Yes, if your team requires data wipe certificates, we will provide them digitally. Once pickup is completed and our team verifies the serial numbers, we will share the certificates within 48 hours. Please ensure your asset list has clear serial numbers so the certificates match your records."

If they do not need certificates, still record their preference in your notes.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Is the quoted price final, or can it change at pickup?">

Reinforce the subject-to-inspection caveat without sounding uncertain.

> You can say:  
> "The quotation is based on the details shared and is subject to physical verification at pickup. As long as the actual devices and condition match what we have discussed, the value remains the same. If there is any major difference in quantity or condition, we will discuss it with you on the spot and agree on the revised value before proceeding."

Always document any change agreed at pickup in writing after the visit.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Best practices for B2B closure calls

- Confirm all key fields verbally and again in writing: quantity, condition summary, address, pickup date, payment mode, and timeline.
- Always mention that the final value is **subject to physical verification** and align on how you will handle deviations.
- Avoid absolute language like "payment instantly" without context. Tie payment wording to the **agreed mode and timeline**.
- Keep tone professional, clear, and solution-oriented. Focus on predictability and documentation for the client.
- Update internal trackers immediately after the call so operations and finance do not work on outdated information.
