## Overview

Use the Cash In Hand page in the Finance module to track physical cash, bank balances, and all related transactions across accounts. The page consolidates balances and movements so you always know how much money is available in cash and in bank at any time.

## At a glance

The top of the page shows three summary cards with your current financial position. These update in real time as you add accounts or record transactions.

<Columns cols="3">
  <Card title="Cash in Hand" href="#" icon="wallet" cta="View cash accounts" horizontal="false">
    Total physical cash currently available across all cash accounts, such as store drawers or office cash boxes.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Bank Balance" href="#" icon="banknote" cta="View bank accounts" horizontal="false">
    Total balance available across all linked bank accounts or payment wallets tracked in the system.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Total Available" href="#" icon="arrow-up-down" cta="View all balances" horizontal="false">
    Combined total of Cash in Hand and Bank Balance, showing overall funds available across all accounts.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Accounts

The Accounts section lists every cash and bank account configured in the system. Each account maintains its own running balance and contributes to the summary cards at the top.

Typical accounts include:

- Individual cash drawers at stores

- Main office cash accounts

- Bank current or savings accounts

- Payment wallets used by the business

After you add accounts, they appear in this section with:

- The account name

- Current balance

- Access to detailed transaction history for that account

Add all active cash and bank accounts before you start recording transactions so balances stay accurate and complete.

## Recent transactions

The Recent Transactions section shows a table of all financial movements across every account. Each row represents a single transaction entry with the following fields:

- **Date** — When the transaction was recorded

- **Account** — Which cash or bank account the transaction belongs to

- **Category** — The type or purpose of the transaction

- **Description** — A short note describing what the transaction is for

- **Amount** — The value of the transaction in INR

Use this table to:

- Review recent inflows and outflows

- Confirm that cash from device sales and other activities has been logged

- Cross-check balances against external statements or physical cash counts

Click **Refresh** (top right) to reload the latest account balances and transaction list.

## Common workflows

Use these workflows daily to keep Cash in Hand accurate.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add an account" icon="plus" title-type="p">
    Add each cash drawer, bank account, or payment wallet so balances roll up correctly into the summary cards.

    - Click **Add Account** in the top right of the Accounts section.

    - Enter the account name and select whether it is a cash or bank type, based on how your team defines accounts.

    - Save the account to add it to the Accounts list.

    After you add an account, it appears in the Accounts section and becomes available to select when you record transactions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Record a transaction" icon="table" title-type="p">
    Log every inflow and outflow so Cash in Hand and Bank Balances always match reality.

    - Click **Record Transaction** in the top right of the Recent Transactions section.

    - Select the correct **Account** so the right balance is updated.

    - Choose a **Category** that matches the purpose of the transaction.

    - Add a clear **Description** for future reference.

    - Enter the **Amount** in INR and confirm to save.

    Use this flow to record cash received from device sales, cash payments for expenses, bank deposits and withdrawals, and any other movement of funds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Admin responsibilities

Keep Cash in Hand reliable by following these operational practices:

- Add all active cash, bank accounts, and payment wallets before recording any transactions.

- Record every cash and bank inflow or outflow as soon as possible to keep balances up to date.

- Always assign the correct account and the most accurate category for each transaction.

- Review the **Total Available** balance regularly against expected cash flow and external statements.

- Use this page together with **Expenses**, **Device Sales**, and other Finance pages for full financial visibility.

- Click **Refresh** on the page when you need to see the latest balances and recent transactions.

## Connections

Changes in Cash in Hand are linked to activity in other Finance and operations modules:

- **Expenses** — Cash payment expenses reduce the Cash in Hand balance.

- **Device Sales** — Cash-mode sales from Punch Sale increase Cash in Hand.

- **B2B Payments** — Cash-mode B2B payments update the cash balance for the relevant account.

- **Reports** — Transaction data from all accounts flows into Finance reports for reconciliation and analysis.

## Important notes

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Transactions recorded in Cash in Hand are permanent entries and directly affect account balances. Always select the correct account and double-check the amount and details before confirming any transaction.
</Callout>

Keep in mind:

- Each account tracks its balance independently, so mis-assigning an account will create discrepancies.

- The **Total Available** card shows combined cash and bank balances; use the individual cards to see how much is in physical cash versus bank accounts.
