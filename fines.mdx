## Overview

The Fines page in Quality - B2C lets admins manually add or deduct credits from any vendor account and review a complete history of all credit operations.

<Callout kind="info">
  Use the Fines page to apply manual credit adjustments (rewards, penalties, corrections) and to access an auditable log of every credit operation across all vendors.
</Callout>

The page is typically used to:

- Reward vendors for good performance
- Penalise vendors for order failures, policy violations, or poor service
- Correct any credit discrepancies when reconciling vendor balances

## Summary cards

Three summary cards at the top provide an overview of all credit operations across the platform for the selected period:

- **Total Fines Processed** — Total number of credit operations (additions and deductions) performed in the selected period.
- **Total Added Credits** — Total credits added across all vendors in the selected period.
- **Total Deducted Credits** — Total credits deducted across all vendors in the selected period.

Use these totals to monitor aggregate credit activity and support monthly finance reconciliation.

## Select vendor and vendor information panel

The top section of the page allows you to search for and select a specific vendor to perform a credit operation on.

- **Search inputs** — Search by vendor name, email address, or vendor ID to find the correct vendor.
- **Vendor name and status** — Shows the vendor name and whether the vendor is currently live or on hold.
- **Email** — Registered email address of the vendor.
- **Phone number** — Primary contact number for the vendor.
- **Shop name** — Name of the vendor shop or business.
- **City** — City where the vendor operates.
- **Current Credits** — Vendor's live credit balance at the time of the operation.

Always confirm that you have selected the correct vendor before applying any credit changes.

## Perform a credit operation

After you select a vendor, the Fines section appears where you configure and confirm the credit adjustment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Operation Type and Performed By" icon="settings">

  - **Operation Type** — Select the type of credit operation to perform:
    - **Deduct Credits** — Remove a specified number of credits from the vendor's balance. Use for fines, penalties, or corrections.
    - **Add Credits** — Add a specified number of credits to the vendor's balance. Use for rewards, corrections, or compensations.
  - **Performed By** — Select who is performing the operation. This defaults to Admin and is stored with the transaction for audit purposes.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Credit Amount and Reason" icon="edit">

  - **Credit Amount** — Enter the number of credits to add or deduct from the vendor's account. The field shows `0` by default; update it to the correct value before confirming.
  - **Reason** — Enter a clear, detailed reason for the credit operation. This field is mandatory and is visible in the Fines History for anyone reviewing the transaction later.
  - **Voice Recording Notes** — Instead of — or in addition to — typing a reason, admins can record a voice note directly from the admin panel. The voice note is saved with the credit operation record and provides richer context for why the credit adjustment was made. This is especially useful when the reason is complex or involves multiple factors.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and confirm the operation" icon="check-circle">

  - **Verify vendor and Current Credits** — Confirm you are working on the correct vendor and review the Current Credits value.
  - **Check all fields** — Confirm Operation Type, Performed By, Credit Amount, and Reason are accurate.
  - **Confirm the operation** — Submit the form to apply the credit change. The vendor's credit balance updates immediately, and the transaction appears in the Fines History.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fines history

The Fines History section below the vendor selector shows a complete log of all credit operations across all vendors on the platform.

Each row in the Fines History represents one credit operation, with the following columns:

- **Date** — Date and time the operation was performed.
- **Operation** — Credit amount added or deducted, shown with a green plus for additions and a red minus for deductions.
- **Vendor** — Name and email of the vendor whose credits were affected.
- **Reason** — The detailed reason entered by the admin at the time of the operation.
- **Amount** — Credit value of the operation — positive for additions, negative for deductions.
- **Previous Balance** — Vendor's credit balance before the operation.
- **New Balance** — Vendor's credit balance after the operation.
- **Performed By** — Name of the admin who performed the operation.

### Filters and controls in Fines history

Use the available filters and controls above the table to focus on the records you need:

- **Date Range Filter** — Select a date range to view credit operations within a specific period, such as for monthly reconciliation or audit reviews.
- **Operation Type Filter** — Filter by type:
  - **All Fines** — Shows all credit operations.
  - **Deductions only** — Shows only credit deduction operations.
  - **Additions only** — Shows only credit addition operations.
- **Vendor Filter** — Filter the history by a specific vendor to review that vendor's complete credit history.
- **Results Per Page** — Choose how many records to display per page (for example, 10 or more), depending on the volume of data.
- **Export CSV** — Download the filtered Fines History as a CSV file for finance reporting, audits, or sharing with the management team.

## Common use cases

Use the Fines page for the following typical scenarios.

**Credit deductions (Deduct Credits):**

- Vendor failed an order without a valid reason.
- Vendor did not show up for a confirmed pickup.
- Vendor quoted a wrong price to the customer.
- Vendor violated platform policies.
- Correction for an over-credited vendor account.

**Credit additions (Add Credits):**

- Vendor performed exceptionally well on a difficult order.
- Compensation for a system error that incorrectly deducted credits.
- Incentive for achieving a performance milestone.
- Manual correction for an under-credited vendor account.

## Admin responsibilities and best practices

Admins using the Fines page are responsible for keeping credit operations accurate, consistent, and auditable.

- **Always document reasons clearly** — Enter a detailed and accurate Reason before confirming any credit operation. Vague or generic reasons create audit problems later.
- **Double-check before confirming** — Review Operation Type, Credit Amount, vendor details, and Reason before you confirm. Because changes take effect immediately, you cannot rely on an automatic reversal.
- **Verify application in Fines History** — After confirming, quickly check the new entry in Fines History to ensure the Amount, Previous Balance, and New Balance are correct.
- **Monitor platform-level credit activity** — Review the Total Deducted Credits and Total Added Credits summary cards regularly to spot unusual patterns across the vendor network.
- **Support finance processes** — Export the Fines History monthly for finance reconciliation and management reporting.
- **Align with failure decisions** — Cross-reference deductions with the To Be Failed page to ensure fines are applied consistently for accepted failures.

## Connections to other modules

The Fines page interacts with several other admin modules:

- **Quality - B2C — To Be Failed** — Accepted vendor failures typically result in credit deductions processed through the Fines page.
- **Credits (Finance)** — Credit additions through Fines complement the credit purchase flow managed in the Finance Credits module.
- **Vendors - B2C** — The credit balance shown in a vendor's profile reflects all additions and deductions processed on the Fines page.
- **Reports** — Credit operation data feeds into finance and operations reports for overall platform credit flow analysis.
