---
title: Hot Leads
description: High-intent leads that said yes but have not booked, raised a price objection, or had questions — sorted by how long they have been waiting.
---

## Overview

Open **B2C Sales → To Work → Hot Leads** (the sidebar shows a count badge). Hot Leads are customers who showed strong intent but have not completed a booking. They are the highest-value calls in the queue.

## Tabs

| Tab | Who is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Yes — Not Booked** | Customer said yes to selling but has not booked the pickup |
| **Price Objection** | Customer pushed back on the quoted price |
| **Questions** | Customer had questions that need answering before booking |

## What the table shows

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting** | How long the lead has been waiting (for example `2d 5h`) |
| **Customer** | Customer name and lead token (with copy button) |
| **Device** | Device model |
| **Quoted** | Quoted price (₹). When a bonus is included in the quote, a note under the amount shows it, for example *incl. ₹500 IPHONEEXTRA* |

The list is paginated with Previous, page numbers and Next.

## Actions

Click a row to open the lead drawer:

- **Device Specifications** and **Quoted Price**
- **Update Disposition** — **Select Status** (Connected / Not Connected), reason, **Add remarks...** and the **Update Disposition** button
- **Create Lead** — convert the lead into an order
- **Customer Information** with **Click to call**
- **Activity Logs**

<Callout kind="tip">
  Work the longest-waiting leads first. For **Price Objection**, use the price-match / objection-handling script before offering to requote.
</Callout>
