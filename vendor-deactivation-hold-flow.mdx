## Overview

**Used by:** Cashkr Ops, Vendor Management, CX, and Support teams  
**Purpose:** Standardize when to place a vendor on Hold vs permanently Deactivate, how to investigate cases, and how to document and communicate decisions.

This SOP protects customer safety, prevents fraud, and enforces SLA and policy compliance across all vendor operations.

## Key definitions

<Callout kind="info">

**Hold (temporary block)**

- Vendor cannot unlock new orders but can complete existing ones.  
- Use Hold for moderate, correctable violations or when retraining is required.  

**Deactivation (permanent block)**

- Vendor is fully removed from the Cashkr system and cannot access orders.  
- Use Deactivation for severe misconduct, fraud, customer safety issues, or repeated violations after Hold and training.

</Callout>

## When to put a vendor on Hold

Use Hold for correctable issues where fraud is not yet proven, and the vendor can potentially be retrained.

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Performance-related holds" default-open="true">

Use a Hold when performance breaches cross thresholds or show clear negative patterns, including:

- More than 5 SLA violations in a week.
- Frequent delayed pickups.
- Too many To Be Failed orders due to vendor actions.
- Low completion rate over a meaningful sample.
- Wrong inspection steps repeated across orders.
- High number of customer complaints linked to the same vendor.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Behaviour-related holds">

Use a Hold when vendor behaviour is unacceptable but not yet at the level of permanent removal:

- Rude or disrespectful behaviour with customers.  
- Arguments or aggressive communication on calls or at pickup.  
- Not following pickup etiquette (dress, language, basic conduct).  
- Unprofessional conduct during visits.  
- Complaining about or abusing Cashkr policies in front of customers.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Process violations">

Use a Hold when vendors do not follow defined Cashkr processes:

- Attempting offline payment (for example, UPI, cash) instead of following Cashkr payment flow.  
- Entering payment OTP without explicit customer permission.  
- Not following app inspection instructions or skipping mandatory checks.  
- Not calling the customer after unlocking the order when required.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Suspicious activity (not yet proven fraud)">

Use a Hold while Ops investigates suspicious or inconsistent activity:

- Customer reports discomfort with vendor conduct, presence, or behaviour.  
- Vendor marking fake statuses in the app.  
- False Customer Not Available updates.  
- Device discrepancies between customer description, inspection, and final status that require verification.

Keep the vendor on Hold until Ops completes verification and decides on reactivation or deactivation.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Hold flow (how Hold works)

<Steps>

<Step title="Identify the issue" icon="alert-triangle">

Identify the trigger through one or more of:

- SLA tracking dashboards.  
- Customer complaint or escalation.  
- Routine Ops review or audits.  
- Payment irregularities.  
- App or system audit logs.

Success signal: You have at least one clear incident or pattern linked to a specific vendor and order IDs.

</Step>

<Step title="Review vendor activity" icon="search">

Ops reviews all available data for the affected orders:

- Order timeline and status history.  
- Vendor behaviour notes.  
- App logs and action history.  
- Call recordings or call logs.  
- GPS logs where available.  
- Complaint details and supporting evidence.

Success signal: You have a documented view of what happened and can classify the issue under performance, behaviour, process, or suspicious activity.

</Step>

<Step title="Apply Hold in Admin Panel" icon="settings">

- In the Admin Panel, select the relevant vendor profile.  
- Apply Hold status using the Hold control.  
- Confirm that the vendor no longer receives new orders but can complete existing ones.

Success signal: Vendor shows as on Hold in Admin Panel and stops receiving new unlocks.

</Step>

<Step title="Notify the vendor" icon="send">

Send a Hold notification via WhatsApp or SMS using the standard template in the Communication section.

Include:

- Reason category (SLA, behaviour, process, or policy violation).  
- Instruction to contact support for mandatory retraining.

Success signal: Vendor has received confirmation that the account is on Hold and next steps are clear.

</Step>

<Step title="Complete mandatory retraining" icon="book-open">

Before considering reactivation:

- Explain the exact violation to the vendor.  
- Assign and track completion of the relevant retraining module.  
- Capture explicit acknowledgement from the vendor that they understand the policy and consequences of repeat issues.  
- Monitor short-term behaviour where relevant.

Success signal: Vendor has completed training and acknowledged the violation in a traceable way.

</Step>

<Step title="Decide on reactivation vs escalation" icon="check-circle">

Based on severity, frequency, and training outcome:

- If the issue is resolved and behaviour/performance is acceptable, reactivate the vendor.  
- If issues continue or evidence escalates, extend the Hold or escalate to Deactivation following the rules in this SOP.

Success signal: Admin Panel reflects the final decision (Hold extended, removed, or vendor deactivated), and notes are updated.

</Step>

</Steps>

## When to permanently deactivate a vendor

Use Deactivation for zero tolerance violations and for repeated serious issues after Hold and training.

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Fraud or criminal behaviour" default-open="true">

<Callout kind="alert">

Treat all fraud and criminal scenarios as zero tolerance. Apply permanent deactivation and consider legal escalation as required.

</Callout>

Fraud and criminal triggers include:

- Taking any customer device without completing valid payment through Cashkr systems.  
- Tampering with the device to alter condition or value.  
- Manipulating IMEI or device identifiers.  
- Deliberately faking inspection results to change the quoted value.  
- Forging screenshots, payment proofs, IDs, or any supporting documents.  
- Any attempted theft or scam involving customer property, money, or Cashkr systems.

These cases require immediate review for permanent deactivation.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Financial misconduct">

Financial misconduct directly affects customer trust and Cashkr revenue. Triggers include:

- Intentionally collecting offline payment instead of following the Cashkr payment process.  
- Asking the customer to pay any extra charges beyond what the app shows.  
- Refusing to complete payment through the OTP-based system.  
- Taking any payment outside the Cashkr system flow.

Where intent and pattern are clear, move to permanent deactivation.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Customer safety risks">

<Callout kind="alert">

Customer safety risks are zero tolerance. Prioritize customer safety over vendor retention in all such cases.

</Callout>

Deactivation triggers related to customer safety include:

- Threatening or intimidating the customer in any way.  
- Physical misbehaviour or contact beyond what is necessary for pickup.  
- Harassment, abusive language, or intimidation at any point.  
- Entering the customer’s home or private premises without explicit permission.  
- Any unsafe action reported by the customer that makes them feel at risk.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Repeated high violations after Hold">

Deactivate vendors who continue to violate policies despite Holds and training:

- More than 3 Holds in 30 days.  
- More than 10 SLA violations per week on a consistent basis.  
- Poor performance for 4 consecutive weeks with no sustained improvement.  
- Repeated behaviour issues even after documented training and warnings.

In these cases, permanent deactivation protects platform quality and customer experience.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Deactivation flow

<Steps>

<Step title="Flag a major violation" icon="alert-triangle">

Identify a serious violation through:

- Customer complaint or escalation.  
- Ops audit or case review.  
- Payment discrepancy detected by systems or finance.  
- Safety flags raised by CX or on-ground teams.  
- Fraud detection alerts from internal checks.

Success signal: The case is clearly classified under fraud/criminal, financial misconduct, customer safety, or repeated high violations.

</Step>

<Step title="Ops Manager review" icon="search">

Ops Manager performs a high-level but thorough investigation:

- Check all relevant order and vendor logs.  
- Verify customer reports and match with system data.  
- Review vendor history, including prior Holds, SLAs, and complaints.  
- Look for repeat patterns or prior warnings.

Success signal: Ops Manager has a documented recommendation on whether to proceed with immediate deactivation or Hold plus further investigation.

</Step>

<Step title="Decide immediate block vs Hold plus deeper check" icon="settings">

- For severe fraud, criminal behaviour, or clear safety risk, move to immediate permanent block.  
- For medium severity or where more evidence is required, place the vendor on Hold while running a deeper check.

Success signal: Decision is recorded in internal notes, including rationale for immediate block or temporary Hold.

</Step>

<Step title="Deactivate vendor in Admin Panel" icon="x-circle">

- In the Admin Panel, select the vendor profile.  
- Apply Deactivate status using the Deactivate control.  
- Confirm that the vendor can no longer access orders or unlock new leads, including existing ones if policy requires.

Success signal: Vendor shows as Deactivated in Admin Panel and loses access to operational functions.

</Step>

<Step title="Notify the vendor" icon="send">

Send a deactivation message using the standard template in the Communication section:

- Clearly state that the account has been permanently deactivated.  
- Reference serious violation of Cashkr policy without sharing internal investigation details.  
- Make it explicit that the decision is final.

Success signal: Vendor has been formally informed, and a record of the message is stored.

</Step>

<Step title="Add vendor to fraud and block lists" icon="shield">

- Flag the vendor in the fraud or blocked database to prevent re-onboarding.  
- Ensure linked IDs, numbers, and documents are tagged as blocked, as per internal tools and processes.

Success signal: Vendor details appear in fraud or block listings, preventing future sign-ups or activation.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Reactivation rules (after Hold only)

<Callout kind="tip">

Reactivation is only possible after a Hold and only when all reactivation conditions below are met. Deactivated vendors remain permanently blocked.

</Callout>

A vendor can be reactivated only when:

- The issue is minor and does not involve fraud, criminal behaviour, or major safety risk.  
- The vendor completes mandatory training relevant to the violation.  
- The vendor explicitly accepts the mistake and agrees to corrective behaviour going forward.  
- There is no fraud or confirmed malicious intent in the case.  
- Ops Manager reviews the case and approves reactivation.

<Callout kind="alert">

No reactivation is allowed after Deactivation. All deactivated vendors remain permanently blocked and are recorded in the fraud or blocked database.

</Callout>

## Approval hierarchy

Maintain the following approval levels for Hold and Deactivation actions:

- **Who can put a vendor on Hold:**  
  - Ops Associate  
  - Vendor Support Team  
  - Ops Manager  

- **Who can remove Hold:**  
  - Ops Manager only  

- **Who can deactivate a vendor:**  
  - Ops Manager with CEO approval  

- **High severity fraud cases:**  
  - CEO direct approval required for deactivation and any related escalations.

## Vendor communication templates

Use these templates for vendor-facing communication. Customize only the specific reason or case references; keep the structure and tone consistent.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Hold notification" icon="info">

> Your Cashkr vendor account has been placed on HOLD due to SLA or behaviour issues.  
> Contact support to complete the required retraining and review your account.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Pre-deactivation warning" icon="alert-triangle">

> Your recent performance and/or behaviour shows repeated violations of Cashkr policies.  
> If this continues, your account may be permanently deactivated and you will lose access to Cashkr orders.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Deactivation message" icon="x-circle">

> Your Cashkr vendor account has been permanently deactivated due to serious violation of company policies.  
> This decision is final and you will not be reactivated on the platform.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Admin Panel notes and audit checklist

When placing a vendor on Hold or permanently Deactivating, record complete notes so every action is auditable:

- Violation type (performance, behaviour, process, suspicious, fraud, safety, financial misconduct, repeated violations).  
- Order ID or multiple order IDs linked to the case.  
- Evidence references (screenshots, app logs, call recordings, GPS logs, complaint tickets).  
- Short explanation of what happened and why the chosen action (Hold or Deactivate) is appropriate.  
- Any training modules assigned or completed.  
- Final decision and status (Hold applied, Hold removed, Deactivated).  
- Reviewer name and role.  
- Date and time of action.

## Quick summary (for vendor training)

Use this section as a high-level card during vendor or internal training.

- **Hold = Temporary pause**  
  - Used for moderate violations and correctable issues.  
  - Requires retraining and acknowledgement.  
  - Can be reactivated if behaviour and performance improve.

- **Deactivation = Permanent block**  
  - Used for fraud, threats, offline payments, device theft, or repeated serious violations.  
  - No reactivation after deactivation.  
  - Vendor is logged in the fraud or blocked database.

- **Threshold cues:**  
  - 5 or more weekly SLA violations from the same vendor → move to Hold and training.  
  - Any confirmed fraud or serious safety issue → immediate deactivation review and permanent block once confirmed.
