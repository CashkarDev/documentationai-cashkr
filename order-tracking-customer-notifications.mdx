## Overview

Use this SOP to keep order tracking and customer notifications consistent across Sales, CX, Vendor Ops, Marketing, and Support.

- **Audience:** Sales, CX, Vendor Ops, Marketing, Support, and Training teams.
- **Purpose:**
  - Define how customers track their order and what they see.
  - Map internal statuses to customer-facing stages and notifications.
  - Standardize CX responses, manual updates, and escalations.
- **Goals:**
  - Zero confusion on order status.
  - Transparent order flow.
  - Consistent messaging across channels.
  - Better customer trust.
  - Improved SLA handling.

<Callout kind="info">

Transparent, consistent messaging at every stage of the order journey reduces customer confusion, builds trust, and helps teams meet SLAs.

</Callout>

## Customer-visible order flow

Customers see the following main order stages in their tracking view.

<Steps>

<Step title="Order Created" icon="check">

The customer places an order and receives confirmation that their request is recorded.

</Step>

<Step title="Vendor Assigned" icon="users">

A pickup partner is assigned and will contact the customer to confirm timings.

</Step>

<Step title="Vendor En Route" icon="truck">

The vendor starts the trip and is on the way to the customer location.

</Step>

<Step title="Vendor Arrived" icon="check-circle">

The pickup partner reaches the customer address.

</Step>

<Step title="Inspection in Progress" icon="search">

The vendor inspects the device and evaluates its condition.

</Step>

<Step title="Final Price Generated" icon="bar-chart">

The system or vendor generates the final price for the device.

</Step>

<Step title="Payment Sent (OTP Generated)" icon="lock">

The vendor initiates payment, and the customer receives an OTP to confirm.

</Step>

<Step title="Order Completed – Payment Successful" icon="zap">

Payment is completed successfully and the order is closed.

</Step>

<Step title="Order Failed / Cancelled / Customer Rejected Price" icon="x-circle">

If payment fails, the order is cancelled, the vendor fails, or the customer rejects the price, the order ends in a failure status.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Internal status mapping (Admin Panel)

The Admin Panel uses more granular internal statuses that drive customer-facing statuses and notifications.

- Created
- Pending Vendor Assignment
- Assigned
- Vendor Accepted
- Vendor En Route
- At Location
- Inspection Started
- Inspection Completed
- Payment Initiated
- Payment Successful
- Completed
- Customer Rejected / Vendor Failed / Unable to Reach

These internal statuses trigger the automated messages and help CX understand where the order is stuck.

## Automated customer notifications

Customers receive SMS, WhatsApp, and App notifications at key events.

| Event                               | Trigger                        | Message                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Order Creation                      | Customer places order          | Your order has been created! Your vendor partner will contact you soon for pickup.      |
| Vendor Assigned                     | Vendor unlocks order           | A pickup partner has been assigned. They will call you shortly to confirm timings.      |
| Vendor En Route                     | Vendor taps `Start Trip`       | Your pickup partner is on the way.                                                      |
| Vendor Arrival                      | Vendor taps `Arrived`          | Your pickup partner has arrived at your location.                                       |
| Final Price Ready                   | Vendor completes inspection    | Final price for your device is ready. Please confirm to proceed with payment.           |
| OTP Payment                         | Vendor initiates payment       | Your payment OTP is XXXXXX. Share it with the pickup partner to complete payment.       |
| Payment Success / Order Completion  | Payment processed              | Payment successful! Thank you for selling your device on Cashkr.                        |
| Order Failed / Customer Rejected    | Vendor selects failure option  | Your order could not be completed. No worries—create another order anytime.             |

## Customer tracking options

### SMS / WhatsApp links

- Customers receive a direct link to the order tracking page in SMS and WhatsApp.
- The link shows the live status and key details.

### App order history

- Customers go to **My Orders** in the app.
- They see:
  - Live order status.
  - Vendor details and contact (after assignment).
  - Any important notes or next steps.

### Customer Support (CX team)

- CX can fetch and share:
  - Vendor name.
  - Vendor contact.
  - Current status.
  - ETA.
  - Payment verification details.

## CX handling: common tracking questions

Use these patterns when customers ask about their order status or vendor.

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Where is my vendor?">

Check the current status:

- If **Assigned**: Vendor has the order but has not called yet.
- If **En Route**: Share the ETA.
- If **Accepted but not en route**: Call the vendor and update the customer.

Recommended script:

> I have checked your order—your pickup partner will reach you shortly. I will keep you updated.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Why is my order still waiting for vendor?">

Possible reasons:

- Low vendor availability.
- High rush period.
- Remote or hard-to-serve area.

CX steps:

- Tag Vendor Ops.
- Try assigning an alternative vendor.
- Provide a realistic time estimate to the customer.

Recommended script:

> We are assigning the best available partner. Please allow us a short time—we will update you soon.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Vendor is late">

Steps:

- Call the vendor.
- Confirm the updated ETA.
- Reassign the order if the vendor is unavailable.

Recommended script:

> I apologize for the delay. I am coordinating with your pickup partner right now.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Payment not received but vendor left">

Steps:

- Check payment logs.
- Verify the transaction status.
- Escalate to Finance if needed.

Recommended script:

> I am checking the transaction right now. You will receive confirmation shortly.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Manual notifications (CX)

CX should send manual messages when automation does not fully explain the situation.

Typical cases:

- Vendor reassignment.
- Vendor failure.
- Delay due to traffic or operational issues.
- Price dispute review.
- Return flow.
- Payment verification or investigation.

Use this template and adapt details as needed:

```text
We’re updating your order status. Our team is reviewing the situation and will update you shortly.
```

## Notification SLAs

Use these SLAs to ensure customers are notified on time.

| Stage            | SLA to notify customer |
| ---------------- | ---------------------- |
| Vendor Assigned  | Within 5 minutes       |
| Vendor Delayed   | 10–20 minutes          |
| Vendor Reassigned| Immediate              |
| Final Price Ready| Instant                |
| Payment Completed| Instant                |
| Order Failed     | Within 15 minutes      |

## Escalation rules

Escalate quickly when an issue cannot be resolved at CX level.

### Escalate to Vendor Ops when

- Vendor is unreachable.
- Vendor delays are more than 30 minutes.
- Customer reports feeling unsafe.
- Vendor misbehaviour is reported.
- Wrong inspection or evaluation is suspected.

### Escalate to Finance when

- Payment is stuck or pending for too long.
- Double deduction is reported.
- Payment not received in the customer bank account.

### Escalate to Tech when

- Status is not updating in the app or Admin Panel.
- App shows errors during tracking or payment.
- Wrong notifications are sent or triggers are misfiring.

## Common issues and resolutions

Use these troubleshooting flows for frequent problems.

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Customer did not receive notifications">

Ask the customer to:

- Check SMS spam or blocked senders.
- Check WhatsApp blocked status.
- Confirm mobile network coverage.
- Reinstall or update the Cashkr app.

If the issue continues after these checks, create a Tech escalation.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Vendor assigned but did not call">

- Call the vendor directly.
- Confirm the ETA.
- Reassign to another vendor if needed.

Keep the customer informed about any change or delay.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Order stuck on a status">

- Open the Admin Panel and refresh.
- Check if the vendor has updated the status from their app.
- If status is still stuck:
  - Ask the vendor to update from the app.
  - Raise a Tech escalation if the status still does not move.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Customer wants live tracking">

- Share the vendor’s contact number and ETA.
- Only share these details **after** the order is assigned to a vendor.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## One-page summary (training card)

### Order flow

Created → Assigned → En Route → Arrived → Inspection → Final Price → OTP Payment → Completed

### Automatic notifications sent at

- Order Created.
- Vendor Assigned.
- Vendor En Route.
- Arrival.
- Final Price.
- OTP Payment.
- Completion or Failure.

### CX should

- Provide clear ETA and set expectations.
- Coordinate with the vendor when there are delays or issues.
- Assist customers with payment and OTP verification.
- Proactively update customers when there is any delay or change.
- Escalate to Vendor Ops, Finance, or Tech as per the rules above.
