## Overview

**Sidebar path:** Marketing → Push Notifications (`/notification`)

The **Push Notification Hub** lets admins send, schedule, and manage push notifications for the **Customer App** (Cashkr users), the **Vendor App** (partner vendors), or **both apps** at once. Each tab is reachable directly through the URL, for example `/notification?tab=history`.

## Tabs

| Tab | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Compose** | Write a one-off notification, choose app and audience, send a test, send now or schedule. |
| **Analytics** | Delivery performance and outcomes (taps, leads, orders) for a selected period. |
| **History** | Log of every notification sent, with status and delivery counts. |
| **Scheduled** | Notifications queued for a future date and time, with edit, reschedule and cancel. |

<Callout kind="info">
The hub no longer shows separate **Campaigns**, **Templates** or **Segments** tabs. Existing campaigns, segments and active templates are still counted on the **Analytics** tab.
</Callout>

## Compose tab

The left side holds the **Compose Notification** form; the right side shows a **Live Preview**.

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** \* | Counter shows `0/50` — keep it under 50 characters so it isn't cut off. |
| **Body** \* | Counter shows `0/120` — keep it under 120 characters so it isn't cut off. |
| **Personalise** | Click a variable to insert it: `{first_name}`, `{device}`, `{quote_amount}`. Example: *Hi \{first\_name\}, your \{device\} is worth \{quote\_amount\}.* becomes *Hi Rahul, your iPhone 13 is worth ₹24,500.* |
| **Banner Image** (optional) | Upload an image or paste its URL. JPG or PNG under 1 MB, roughly 2:1. Shown large only when the reader expands the notification. |
| **Target App** \* | **Customer App** (Cashkr users), **Vendor App** (partner vendors), or **Both apps** (one announcement to both). |
| **Audience** \* | **All Users** or **Specific Users**. Specific Users opens a picker where you search by name, phone or email and tick recipients; people without a registered device are marked **no device**. |
| **Schedule for later** | Toggle on to set a **Date** and **Time (IST)** to send. |

Two buttons sit under the form:

- **Send test** — sends the notification as a test (test sends appear in History with status **Test** and the name of the admin who sent them).
- **Review & send** (or **Review & schedule** when scheduling is on) — opens a review step before anything is sent.

### Live preview

- Switch between **Android** and **iPhone** mock-ups.
- Switch between **Collapsed** (how it first appears — truncation depends on the phone) and **Expanded** (shows the banner image).
- A light/dark toggle shows the notification on a dark or light phone theme.
- A summary below shows the selected **Target App** and **Audience** (e.g. *Not Chosen Yet*, or the number of customers picked).

<Callout kind="alert">
Sent notifications cannot be recalled. Double-check the **Target App** and **Audience** before confirming — sending a customer promotion to the Vendor App (or vice versa) is the most common mistake.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step title="Write the message" icon="pencil" title-type="p">
Enter **Title** and **Body** within the character guidance, add personalisation variables if needed, and optionally add a **Banner Image**.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick app and audience" icon="users" title-type="p">
Choose **Customer App**, **Vendor App** or **Both apps**, then **All Users** or **Specific Users**.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the preview and test" icon="eye" title-type="p">
Review Android/iPhone and Collapsed/Expanded views, and use **Send test** to check it on a device.
</Step>
<Step title="Send or schedule" icon="send" title-type="p">
Leave **Schedule for later** off and click **Review & send**, or turn it on, set date and time (IST), and click **Review & schedule**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Analytics tab

Filters: period (e.g. **Last 30 days**) and **All Apps**.

- **KPI cards:** Total Sent, Delivered, Failed, Delivery Rate.
- **Summary counts:** Active campaigns, Campaigns this period, Segments, Active templates.
- **Charts:** Notifications Over Time (Sent / Delivered / Failed); Campaigns by Type.
- **Recent Campaigns** table: Campaign, Type, Status, App, Sent, Delivered, Rate, Date.
- **What Notifications Produced:** a funnel of **Delivered** (of sent), **Uninstalled** (app removed or token expired), **Tapped** (tap rate), **Leads** (% of tappers), **Orders** (% of leads) and **Completed** (pickups done), followed by a per-notification table with columns **Notification**, **Delivered**, **Uninstalled**, **Tapped**, **Leads**, **Orders**, **Completed**, **Tap %**.

## History tab

Table columns: **Notification** (title and body), **Target** (app + audience type such as *Individual*), **Status**, **Delivery** (delivered / targeted, with the failed count and reason such as *App uninstalled or token expired*), **Date** (test sends also show who sent them).

**Filters:** search, **Status** (All Status, Sent, Sending, Scheduled, Failed, Cancelled, Test), **All Apps**, **Refresh**. A total count is shown, with pagination.

Click a row to expand it and see the **Full Title**, **Full Body**, **Image** URL and **Target Value**.

## Scheduled tab

Shows the number of queued notifications with a **Refresh** button. Each card shows the app, audience type, title, body, the scheduled time in IST (or **Due now**), and actions **Edit**, **Reschedule** and **Cancel**. When empty, **Go to Compose** takes you back to schedule one.

## Best practices

- **Preview and test before sending** — check both Collapsed and Expanded views on Android and iPhone, and use **Send test**.
- **Prefer targeted audiences** — pick specific users instead of All Users to avoid notification fatigue.
- **Check Scheduled** before creating new sends to avoid duplicates.
- **Review Analytics and History** after each send to confirm delivery and catch failures.
