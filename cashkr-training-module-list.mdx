Use this catalog to assign and complete Cashkr training by function, team, and role.

## Best practices

- Complete all assigned training modules within the first 5 working days of joining.
- Department heads assign mandatory modules based on role and responsibilities.
- HR maintains a central training tracker sheet to monitor progress.
- Schedule refresher training quarterly or after major product or process updates.
