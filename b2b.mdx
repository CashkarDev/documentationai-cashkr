## Overview

The B2B Payments page in the Finance module shows all payment records linked to B2B corporate buyback deals, including pending, completed, and total paid amounts across active clients.

Records appear only after a B2B deal is confirmed in the sales pipeline and the client accepts the quotation, so deals still in negotiation or lead stage do not show here.

---

## How Finance uses this page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review summary cards" icon="chart-bar" title-type="p">
    Check the four summary cards at the top to see total pending payments, outstanding amount, completed deals, and total paid value for the selected period.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Filter by date and status" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    Apply a date range to focus on payments for a specific period, and use the status filter to view Pending, Completed, or All B2B payment records.

    Scan the payments table by deal number and client to compare quoted value vs amount paid, payment mode, status, and payment date for each B2B deal.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify payment and invoice" icon="shield" title-type="p">
    Confirm that funds have been received in the bank account or payment gateway and verify that the client invoice is received and accurate before updating any record.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Mark payments as Completed" icon="circle-check" title-type="p">
    Update the payment status from Pending to Completed only after full payment is received against the quoted amount and internal verification is done.

  <Step title="Export for reconciliation and audits" icon="download" title-type="p">

    Click Refresh after processing payments or updating deals so that summary cards and table rows reflect the latest payment statuses.

## What you see on the page

### Summary cards

- Pending payments: number of B2B buyback deals with payments not yet received.

- Pending amount: total INR value of all outstanding payments across active B2B deals.

- Completed deals: number of B2B deals where full payment has been received.

- Total paid: total INR value of all completed B2B payments received.

### Payments table

- Deal number: unique reference number linked to the B2B sales pipeline.

- Client: name of the corporate client for the buyback deal.

- Quote: quoted buyback value agreed with the client.

- Paid: amount actually received against the quoted value.

- Mode: payment mode recorded, such as NEFT, RTGS, UPI, or Cash.

- Status: current payment status, either Pending or Completed.

- Date: date when the payment was recorded or marked as completed.

- Action: available admin actions on the record, such as updating status.

### Filters & controls

- Date range filter: restricts payment records to a selected start and end date for reconciliation or audit periods.

- Status filter: shows only Pending, Completed, or All payments.

- Export: downloads the payment list based on current filters for reporting or documentation.

- Refresh: reloads the latest payment data and reflects recent deal or status updates.

## Payment lifecycle

```plaintext
B2B Deal Created in Sales Pipeline
  ↓
Quotation Accepted by Client
  ↓
Payment Record Created in B2B Payments (Status: Pending)
  ↓
Pickup Completed and Devices Verified
  ↓
Client Raises Invoice to Cashkr
  ↓
Finance Processes Payment
  ↓
Admin Marks Payment as Completed
  ↓
Payment Acknowledgment Issued to Client
```

---

## Payment modes

- NEFT: National Electronic Funds Transfer used for standard bank transfers.

- RTGS: Real Time Gross Settlement used for high-value same-day transfers.

- UPI: Unified Payments Interface used for instant digital transfers.

- Cash: physical cash collected at the time of pickup.

For B2B corporate deals, prefer NEFT or RTGS for payments above ₹50,000 and ensure the client has raised a valid invoice to Cashkr Technologies Pvt. Ltd. before processing any payment.

---

## Admin responsibilities

- Monitor Pending payments daily and follow up with clients on overdue B2B deals.

- Mark a payment as Completed only after confirming receipt in the bank account or payment gateway.

- Verify that the client invoice is received and accurate before releasing any payment.

- Export B2B payment records for monthly finance reconciliation, reporting, and audit checks.

- Share a Payment Acknowledgment Slip with the client once a payment is marked Completed and issue data wipe certificates within 48 hours of pickup completion when requested.

---

## Connections to other modules

- B2B Sales Pipeline: confirmed deals here automatically generate payment records in B2B Payments.

- All Inventory: completed B2B buybacks add verified devices into inventory.

- Finance Reports: reports in the Finance section use B2B payment data for revenue and cash flow tracking.

- Cash In Hand: cash-mode B2B payments appear in overall cash flow tracking.
