## Why this matters

<Callout kind="info">

Link and engagement tools help Cashkr track every click, understand which campaigns work, and collect clear feedback on creatives. When teams use the same short links and comment tools, reporting stays accurate and collaboration is faster and less confusing.

</Callout>

## Tools we use

<Card title="Linke" icon="link-engagement-utilities">

Linke creates short links, bio links, and QR codes with tracking, so marketing can see which campaigns, posts, and CTAs drive leads. It also supports tracking pixels for retargeting.

**How we use it at Cashkr:**

- Create short links for ad campaigns with UTMs to measure traffic and leads.
- Maintain bio links (for example, Instagram) that route to multiple CTAs.

**Example:** Marketing sets up a Linke bio link for Cashkr's Instagram profile with CTAs like `Sell Phone`, `Track Pickup`, and `Contact Support`, then adds UTMs so link performance appears clearly in analytics.

</Card>

<Card title="Simple Commentor" icon="message-square">

Simple Commentor is a lightweight feedback tool that lets reviewers click directly on designs or assets and leave precise comments. This replaces long, hard-to-parse email threads.

**How we use it at Cashkr:**

- Collect specific comments on creatives, landing pages, and visuals.
- Keep all design feedback in one place for designers and marketers.

**Example:** Instead of replying with a long email, a reviewer clicks on the banner creative and adds a note like "Move the button higher" so the design team can adjust quickly.

</Card>

## How we use these daily

- **Marketing team**
  - Creates short links for ad campaigns with UTMs to track leads and conversions.
  - Updates Instagram and other social bio links with the latest offers and priority CTAs using Linke.
- **Design team**
  - Shares design previews and gathers visual feedback via Simple Commentor instead of email.
  - Uses comment threads to prioritize edits and confirm when changes are complete.
- **Support team**
  - Shares tracked links (for example, `Track Pickup` or `Contact Support`) with customers so clicks are measurable.
  - Captures quick client notes on support-facing creatives or guides using Simple Commentor.

## Typical link and feedback workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define the tracking and feedback goal" icon="target">

  Start by agreeing on what you want to measure or learn: for example, clicks from a specific Instagram post, lead form submissions from an ad, or clarity of a new banner design. Align marketing, design, and support on the primary CTA and expected user action.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the short or bio link in Linke" icon="link">

  In Linke, set up a short link or bio link with clear naming, add UTMs that match the campaign, and enable any tracking pixels needed. For social bios, include all relevant CTAs (Sell Phone, Track Pickup, Contact Support) in a single, well-structured link.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish, share, and monitor performance" icon="upload">

  Add the Linke URL to ads, social captions, or support responses, then go live. Monitor clicks and basic performance inside Linke and your analytics tools to confirm traffic is routing as expected and that each CTA is being used.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Iterate and collect feedback" icon="edit">

  Use Simple Commentor on related assets (banners, landing pages, creatives) to gather specific comments from stakeholders and clients. Based on link performance and feedback, refine copy, design, or placement, then update the Linke destination or CTAs as needed.

  </Step>
</Steps>
