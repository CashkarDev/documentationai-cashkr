**Used by:** Store Partnership / Channel Expansion Team  

**Purpose:** Train onboarding associates to coach store vendors on professional, transparent in-store customer communication for Cashkr device buyback.

## Objective

To help onboarding associates:

- Understand Cashkr communication expectations for store-level customer conversations.
- Teach vendors how to guide customers through each step, not just operate the app.
- Reduce disputes, rejections, and miscommunication during quoting and payment.
- Maintain a consistent, trust-building experience across all partner stores.

<Callout kind="info">

Treat this script as a coaching guide. Associates should first understand the flow end to end, then role-play with vendors using the sample lines and objections.

</Callout>

## Standard interaction flow (training view)

Use these steps to train vendors on how to handle a full walk-in interaction, from greeting to closure.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Set the context and importance of communication" icon="info">

  Explain to vendors that at partner stores, the Cashkr vendor or associate is the face of the brand.

  > "At partner stores, you represent Cashkr. The way you speak and guide customers directly impacts whether they trust us with their device."

  Reinforce that good communication:

  - Builds trust so customers feel safe handing over their device.
  - Makes the process feel like a guided consultation, not a hard sell.
  - Increases conversions and reduces complaints.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Greet the customer and start the interaction" icon="users">

  Coach vendors to greet every walk-in promptly and confidently when the customer comes to sell a device.

  > "Namaste sir/ma’am, welcome to our Cashkr Partner Store. I will help you check the exact resale value of your device. It will take only a few minutes."

  Emphasize:

  - Keep tone warm, calm, and professional, not rushed or salesy.
  - Make it clear that the customer will get an instant quote with clear next steps.

  <Callout kind="tip">

  Encourage vendors to stand up, make eye contact, and keep their phone aside while greeting. Body language matters as much as words.

  </Callout>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Explain while asking device condition questions" icon="terminal">

  Vendors should never silently tap through questions in the app. Train them to speak out what they are checking and why.

  > "I am going to check a few quick points about your device: screen condition, body condition, and whether you have the original accessories. This helps us give you the most accurate price."

  Clarify for associates:

  - Customers must understand why each question matters.
  - This reduces suspicion that the vendor is manipulating the price.
  - Vendors should pause briefly and let customers see the screen if needed.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Present the system-generated quote clearly" icon="bar-chart">

  Once the instant price appears in the app, vendors should present it clearly and factually.

  > "Based on your device condition and current market value, the Cashkr system is offering ₹14,700 right now."

  Coaching points:

  - Say the number once, confidently, then pause.
  - Do not immediately start justifying or pushing. Let the customer react first.
  - Avoid adding personal comments like "this is low" or "this is the best you will get."

  <Callout kind="info">

  Remind vendors: the quote is system-driven. Their job is to explain how it is calculated and what benefits come with it (instant payment, verified process), not to emotionally sell the number.

  </Callout>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Handle common objections calmly and factually" icon="help-circle">

  When customers question the quote, vendors must stay calm and use facts instead of arguing.

  Explain to vendors that objections are normal, not a sign of failure. Their goal is to:

  - Acknowledge the concern.
  - Reiterate benefits and process.
  - Offer valid options (like small manual top-up or voucher) within policy.

  Use the objection scripts in the next section to role-play with vendors.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm when customer accepts the offer" icon="check-circle">

  Once the customer agrees, vendors should clearly confirm the decision and next steps before touching ID or payment.

  > "Perfect, sir. I will now verify your ID and process the payment. You will receive the amount in your account instantly after verification."

  Emphasize for associates:

  - Transparency at this stage prevents disputes.
  - Vendors should re-state that the amount is system-generated and now locked in.
  - If payment method options exist, flag that they will confirm it before processing.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Explain ID verification and payment method" icon="shield">

  Vendors must explain why they are asking for ID and what will happen with payment.

  > "We need your Aadhaar or PAN to confirm that you are the owner of the device. This is required for instant transfer and to prevent misuse."

  Then, train them to reconfirm payment method:

  > "Would you like to receive the amount in your bank account or through the voucher option? I will confirm this once more before completing the order."

  Key coaching points:

  - Never grab the phone or ID without explaining.
  - Always confirm the name and account details in front of the customer.
  - Reinforce that all payments go through the Cashkr system, not by hand.

  <Callout kind="alert">

  Instruct vendors: never offer or accept direct cash for the device. All payments must go through the app and approved methods only.

  </Callout>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Offer voucher option with transparent benefits" icon="package">

  When a voucher option is available and the customer is interested, coach vendors to explain the benefit honestly.

  > "If you prefer, we can issue a store voucher instead of direct payment. In that case, you will receive ₹14,700 plus a ₹500 bonus. You can use this voucher to buy anything at this store, and it does not expire."

  Training notes:

  - Highlight the extra value (bonus), but avoid overselling.
  - Do not make false promises about additional discounts or resale.
  - Make sure the customer understands that the voucher replaces cash transfer, not adds on top of it.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Reinforce professional conduct expectations" icon="settings">

  Summarize the conduct standards you expect vendors to follow in every interaction.

  Share these points as a checklist:

  - Be present in neat, clean attire.
  - Avoid slang, arguments, or raising your voice.
  - Never offer cash directly; all payments must go through the Cashkr system.
  - Maintain a respectful tone even if the customer is frustrated or disagrees.
  - Stay engaged and guide the customer; do not sit idle while they answer questions.

  <Callout kind="alert">

  Never discuss Cashkr margins, commissions, or internal payouts with customers. If asked, vendors should redirect by focusing on the system price and benefits, not internal economics.

  </Callout>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Close the interaction with gratitude and invitation" icon="check">

  After completing the order and payment (or voucher), vendors should always close with a clear confirmation and thanks.

  > "Thank you for using Cashkr. You will receive an SMS confirmation shortly. If you want to sell another device in the future, you are always welcome to visit us again. The process is quick and safe."

  Coaching notes:

  - A strong closing line increases repeat visits and referrals.
  - Encourage vendors to smile, hand back any documents, and ensure the customer leaves with no open questions.

  </Step>

</Steps>

## Common customer objections and recommended responses

Use this section to coach vendors on typical pushbacks. Read each objection aloud, then practice the recommended response and tone.

<ExpandableGroup>

  <Expandable title="Customer: Another platform is giving me a higher price." default-open="true">

  Recommended vendor response:

  > "I understand, sir. Different platforms show different prices. Our price includes instant payment and a verified pickup process today itself, not after 2–3 days. You also get full documentation and support from Cashkr."

  Coaching notes for associates:

  - Train vendors to acknowledge, not argue.
  - Ask them to underline speed, safety, and reliability as key differentiators.
  - If applicable, they can mention that market prices change daily and compare on the same day.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Customer: My phone is almost new. Why is the price so low?">

  Recommended vendor response:

  > "I understand it feels new for you, sir. The price depends on current resale demand and how much this model has depreciated in the market, not only on age. The good thing is that you are getting an instant, guaranteed quote with no listing or waiting. If you want, I can check if a small bonus or voucher option is available to increase your value slightly."

  Coaching notes:

  - Vendors should educate, not defend.
  - Remind them to use simple language around depreciation and demand.
  - If no bonus is available, they should still explain the system logic clearly.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Customer: Can you increase it by ₹500?">

  Recommended vendor response (if top-up is allowed):

  > "Let me check what flexibility we have. The system price is fixed, but in some cases we can add a small manual top-up. For your device, I can add ₹500 and make your final offer ₹15,200. This will still go through Cashkr, with instant payment and full verification."

  If top-up is not possible:

  > "I checked, sir. For this model and condition, the system is not allowing extra top-up. The quote you see is the best available right now with instant payment and our full service support."

  Coaching notes:

  - Train vendors to never promise a top-up before checking.
  - They must not manually adjust prices outside the allowed system rules.
  - Tone should remain calm even if the customer feels disappointed.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Customer: I will sell it later.">

  Recommended vendor response:

  > "No problem, sir. Prices depend on daily market rates and can go up or down, so you can always check again anytime. Getting a quote is free, and you are always welcome to come back whenever you decide."

  Coaching notes:

  - Respect the customer’s decision without pressure.
  - Vendors should keep the door open for the future and leave a positive impression.
  - Remind vendors that a polite, pressure-free exit often leads to repeat visits.

  </Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Internal quick reference for associates

Use these cues while coaching or auditing store interactions.

- **Greeting:** Focus on warm welcome and clear explanation of the process. Goal: build trust, avoid pushy tone.
- **Quoting:** Present system price once, clearly. Goal: explain logic of price and benefits, avoid false promises.
- **Objection handling:** Stay polite and factual. Goal: educate the customer, not win an argument.
- **Payment and ID:** Explain why ID is required and how payment works. Goal: avoid surprises and confirm details before paying.
- **Closure:** End with gratitude and invitation to return. Goal: leave a positive impression and encourage repeat visits.

## Key phrases to reinforce during training

Use these as coaching anchors when practicing with vendors.

- "The price is system-generated based on your device details. Our role is to explain it clearly and process your order honestly."
- "Explain every step to the customer so they feel guided, not confused."
- "Stay calm and respectful even if the customer disagrees. Your tone represents the Cashkr brand."
- "Always inform the customer before taking their ID or processing any payment."
- "Do not discuss Cashkr margins or commissions with customers. Focus on the service and system price."
- "Use the bonus voucher option as a genuine value-add, not as a trick or pressure tactic."
