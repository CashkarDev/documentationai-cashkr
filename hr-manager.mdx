## Role & reporting line

- **Role:** HR Manager – Cashkr  
- **Department:** Human Resources / People and Culture  
- **Reports to:** CEO  
- **Works closely with:**  
  - All Department Heads (hiring, performance, PIP, policies)  
  - Finance (payroll, CTC, full and final, budgets)  
  - Operations and CX Leads (staffing, shifts, performance issues)  
  - Marketing, Technology and Product Leads (role-specific hiring, org structure)  
  - Admin and IT (assets, access, infrastructure for employees)  

## Role purpose

Own the entire employee lifecycle at Cashkr, from hiring and onboarding to performance, engagement and exit, so that:

- The company has the right people in the right roles, on time.  
- Salaries, attendance, leaves and HR policies are handled accurately and fairly.  
- Employees experience a safe, engaging and compliant workplace.  

## Key result areas (KRAs)

- **KRA 1 – Hiring, onboarding and headcount fulfilment**  
  Ensure all approved roles are hired within agreed timelines, with complete documentation and smooth onboarding.

- **KRA 2 – Attendance, leave and payroll readiness**  
  Maintain accurate attendance and leave records and prepare error-free inputs for monthly payroll.

- **KRA 3 – Performance, PIP and exit management**  
  Support managers on performance issues (including PIP) and manage clean, timely exits including full and final and relieving.

- **KRA 4 – Employee experience, culture and engagement**  
  Drive engagement activities, rituals and feedback mechanisms to keep morale healthy and surface issues early.

- **KRA 5 – Compliance, policies and grievance handling**  
  Maintain HR policies, ensure awareness and adherence, and act as the first safe point of contact for grievances and sensitive cases.

- **KRA 6 – HR data, records and reporting**  
  Maintain clean employee records and provide clear HR reports on headcount, hiring, attendance, PIPs and exits to leadership.

## KPIs

### 1. Time-to-fill and hiring SLA

- **Purpose:** Measure how quickly open roles are filled after an approved hiring request.  
- **Metrics:**  
  - Average time from approved hiring request to offer accepted  
  - Percentage of roles filled within agreed SLA (for example, 30 or 45 days based on level)  
- **Target:** Define SLAs by role type (critical or frontline vs specialist vs leadership) and aim to consistently meet or beat them.  
- **Data source:** Hiring tracker (sheets or ATS) and approval logs.  
- **Review frequency:** Monthly.  
- **Linked KRAs:** KRA 1.  

### 2. New hire joining and early retention

- **Purpose:** Check whether hiring quality and onboarding are effective.  
- **Metrics:**  
  - Offer to joining conversion percentage  
  - Ninety-day new hire retention percentage  
- **Target:** High offer acceptance and early retention rates with low early attrition.  
- **Data source:** Joining records and resignation records.  
- **Review frequency:** Monthly or quarterly.  
- **Linked KRAs:** KRA 1, KRA 3, KRA 4.  

### 3. Payroll accuracy and timely closure

- **Purpose:** Ensure payroll inputs and handover to Finance are accurate and on time.  
- **Metrics:**  
  - Number of payroll error cases per month (wrong salary, loss of pay, leave)  
  - Percentage of payroll inputs shared with Finance on or before the agreed date  
- **Target:** Near-zero payroll errors and 100 percent on-time handover.  
- **Data source:** Payroll issue log, Finance feedback and HRMS reports.  
- **Review frequency:** Monthly.  
- **Linked KRAs:** KRA 2, KRA 6.  

### 4. Attendance and leave process SLA

- **Purpose:** Track quality and timeliness of attendance and leave management.  
- **Metrics (examples):**  
  - Percentage of attendance reconciled by the defined day each month  
  - Percentage of leave requests processed (approved or rejected) within SLA  
  - Number of disputes about attendance or loss of pay per month  
- **Target:** High SLA compliance with minimal escalations or disputes.  
- **Data source:** HRMS (for example, Petpooja) logs, leave records and dispute log.  
- **Review frequency:** Monthly.  
- **Linked KRAs:** KRA 2.  

### 5. Engagement and HR ritual completion

- **Purpose:** Confirm that basic HR rituals and engagement activities happen consistently.  
- **Metrics:**  
  - Percentage of birthdays and work anniversaries celebrated on time  
  - Number of planned engagement activities executed vs planned per quarter  
  - Percentage of quarterly feedback surveys executed as planned  
- **Target:** Near 100 percent completion of planned rituals and surveys.  
- **Data source:** Engagement calendar, event logs and survey records.  
- **Review frequency:** Monthly or quarterly.  
- **Linked KRAs:** KRA 4.  

### 6. PIP, discipline and exit SLA

- **Purpose:** Ensure performance issues and exits are handled in a structured and timely way.  
- **Metrics (examples):**  
  - Number of employees on PIP with documented plans and review dates  
  - Percentage of resignations where:  
    - Exit interviews are conducted  
    - Final settlement and relieving letter are completed within SLA (for example, 5 working days)  
- **Target:** All PIPs documented and all exits completed within defined SLAs.  
- **Data source:** PIP records, exit process tracker and email logs.  
- **Review frequency:** Monthly.  
- **Linked KRAs:** KRA 3, KRA 5, KRA 6.  

### 7. Grievance and sensitive case handling SLA

- **Purpose:** Measure how effectively HR responds to grievances and sensitive issues.  
- **Metrics:**  
  - Average response time to a grievance (first contact)  
  - Average time to resolution or closure  
  - Number of unresolved or lingering cases beyond tolerance  
- **Target:** Fast response and timely closure with proper documentation.  
- **Data source:** Confidential grievance log, email and meeting records.  
- **Review frequency:** Monthly or quarterly.  
- **Linked KRAs:** KRA 4, KRA 5.  

### 8. HR data and records hygiene

- **Purpose:** Track the health and completeness of HR records and access revocation.  
- **Signals and metrics:**  
  - Percentage of employees with complete HR files (ID proof, offer, NDA, policy acknowledgment and other required documents)  
  - Percentage of employees whose access was revoked on time on their last working day  
- **Target:** Close to 100 percent for active employees and recent exits.  
- **Data source:** HR files, access revocation log and IT or Admin confirmations.  
- **Review frequency:** Quarterly.  
- **Linked KRAs:** KRA 5, KRA 6.  

## Core processes / SOPs owned

The HR Manager is owner or co-owner for these SOPs.

### Hiring and recruitment SOP

- How hiring requests are raised by Department Heads or CEO.  
- Difference between backfill and new role.  
- Approval flow involving HR, CEO and Finance where required.  
- JD creation, job posting, screening, interviews and offer process.  
- Maintenance of hiring trackers (status, stage, feedback, CTC).  

### Onboarding and joining SOP

- Pre-joining checklist (documents, background information, system access planning).  
- First-day process (documentation, HR induction, team introduction).  
- Adding new employee to HRMS, payroll, communication tools, email and other systems.  
- Probation and confirmation tracking.  

### Attendance, leave and HRMS SOP

- How employees mark attendance (biometric vs app).  
- Rules for leave application and approvals (earned, casual, sick, uninformed).  
- Loss of pay calculation rules and monthly reconciliation.  
- HR responsibility for keeping HRMS accurate and audit-ready.  

### Payroll preparation SOP

- Monthly payroll calendar (cut-off dates, reconciliation dates).  
- Required inputs:  
  - Attendance and loss of pay  
  - New joiners and exits  
  - Salary revisions (if any)  
- Verification steps before sending inputs to Finance.  
- Handling payroll issues and employee queries.  

### Performance and PIP SOP

- Criteria for when a PIP is triggered.  
- How PIP is documented (goals, timeline, review checkpoints).  
- HR role in reviewing fairness and due process.  
- Final decision flow (confirmation, extension or separation).  

### Engagement, culture and communication SOP

- Birthdays and work anniversaries process (calendar, celebrations, communication).  
- Quarterly or monthly engagement activities (planning, budget, execution, storage of photos).  
- Internal HR communications (policy changes, announcements).  

### Feedback, surveys and HR insights SOP

- How anonymous feedback surveys are planned, sent and followed up.  
- Handling and summarising survey results (themes and action points).  
- Working with leadership on agreed action items.  

### Exit, F&F and access revocation SOP

- Resignation process (email format and notice period).  
- Exit interviews (questions and documentation).  
- Final settlement steps:  
  - Payroll and Finance coordination  
  - No dues from IT, Admin and team leads  
  - Relieving letter and related documents  
- Revoking all accesses (email, HRMS, tools, shared logins).  

### HR policy and compliance SOP

- How HR policies are drafted, reviewed and updated.  
- Storage and location of official policy documents.  
- How changes are communicated to employees.  
- Handling harassment and grievance cases with confidentiality and sensitivity.  

## Weekly & monthly reporting

### Weekly reporting

**Report:** HR and People Weekly Snapshot  

- **To:** CEO; relevant Department Heads (high-level points).  
- **Format:** Short summary via Slack, email or shared document.  
- **Includes:**  
  - **Hiring and movement:** Roles actively hiring and candidates in final stages.  
  - **Attendance and leave notes (if relevant):** Any major patterns, kept very short.  
  - **Engagement and culture:** Activities completed this week and planned for next week.  
  - **Issues and alerts:** Serious HR risks such as high attrition, multiple resignations or major grievances (only high-level, no sensitive detail in group communication).  

### Monthly reporting

**Report:** HR and People section in Monthly Review  

- **To:** CEO, Department Heads and Finance (where needed).  
- **Format:** Slides or document plus relevant sheets.  
- **Includes:**  
  - **Headcount overview:** Total employees by department, joiners vs exits.  
  - **Hiring and talent:** Roles opened, closed and in pipeline; time-to-fill and offer-to-join ratio.  
  - **Attendance and payroll:** Summary of loss of pay cases (if significant) and any recurring patterns (late logins, absenteeism clusters).  
  - **Engagement and feedback:** Activities executed and key survey or feedback themes.  
  - **PIP and exit summary:** Number of employees on PIP, outcomes, and exit reasons summary (broad categories, not personal details).  
  - **Grievance and compliance:** Number of grievances raised and closed (no personal information) and policy changes or updates.  
  - **Next month priorities:** Planned hires, process fixes, engagement focus areas and any policy work.
