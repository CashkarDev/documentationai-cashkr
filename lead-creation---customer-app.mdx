## Overview

Lead creation in the Customer App captures customers who reach the final device price in the sell-device flow but do not confirm an order. Leads are then available in the Admin Panel for follow-up and conversion by the Operations team.

## When a lead is created

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The Customer App creates a lead when all of these conditions are true:

  - The customer is **logged in**.

  - The customer completes the **device Q\&A** so that the **final device price** is shown.

  - The customer **does not tap Confirm Order** on the final price confirmation screen.

  When triggered, the system stores the selected device, Q\&A answers, pincode, and customer contact details as a lead in the Admin Panel.
</Callout>

## Step-by-step customer journey (Customer App)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from HomeScreen or search" icon="home" title-type="p">
    - Customer opens the Customer App.

    - Customer selects a device from the **HomeScreen** categories or uses **search** to find a specific brand and model.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm device variant" icon="monitor" title-type="p">
    - Customer chooses the correct **brand**, **model**, and **variant** for the device they want to sell.

    - The app uses this selection as the base for pricing and condition questions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check serviceability" icon="map" title-type="p">
    - Customer enters a **pincode** or area.

    - If the pincode is **serviceable**, the app allows the customer to proceed.

    - If the pincode is **not serviceable**, the flow stops and **no lead is created**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Answer condition Q&A" icon="help-circle" title-type="p">
    - Customer answers the device **condition questions** that influence pricing (screen, body, functionality, accessories, etc.).

    - The app calculates the device quote based on these answers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select payment method" icon="credit-card" title-type="p">
    - Customer selects a **payment method** (for example, UPI or bank transfer) as part of the sell-device flow.

    - The app prepares the final quote summary using this choice.
  </Step>

  <Step title="View final price confirmation screen" icon="bar-chart" title-type="p">
    - The app shows the **final device price** and summary of the selected device and condition.

    - The screen includes the **Confirm Order** action to place the order.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Customer does not confirm order" icon="x-circle" title-type="p">
    - Customer exits or navigates away **without tapping Confirm Order**.

    - The backend saves this session as a **lead** because the customer reached pricing but did not place an order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## System logic

The system uses the same core rules as lead creation on the website but applies them to the Customer App entry points and journey.

### Conditions for lead creation

- **User authentication**

  - Customer must be **logged in** to the Customer App.

  - If the customer is not logged in, do not create a lead.

- **Quote visibility**

  - Customer has completed the **device Q\&A** and the **final price** is shown on the confirmation screen.

  - If the customer leaves before the final price is calculated, do not create a lead.

- **Order not confirmed**

  - Customer **does not tap Confirm Order**.

  - If the customer confirms the order, treat it as a **normal order**, not a lead.

### Data captured in the lead

The lead snapshot captures enough information for Ops to contact the customer and understand their intent:

- **Selected device details**

  - Brand, model, and variant chosen in the Customer App.

- **Condition inputs (Q\&A)**

  - All answers from the sell-device condition questions that drive pricing.

- **Pincode / serviceability info**

  - The **pincode** entered during the serviceability step that allowed the user to proceed.

- **Customer details**

  - Customer name and contact details from the logged-in profile.

- **Timestamp and context**

  - When the lead was created.

  - Context that this was a **Customer App sell-device flow** where the quote was shown but order not confirmed.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Keep the lead schema between Website and Customer App as aligned as possible. This makes reporting, analytics, and Ops workflows consistent across both channels.
</Callout>

## Connections

Leads created from the Customer App plug into the same operational pipeline as website leads.

- **Admin Panel → Leads**

  - All Customer App leads appear in the **Admin Panel** under the **Leads** section.

  - You can filter or tag leads by **source** (for example, Customer App vs Website) as supported by your implementation.

- **Operations follow-up**

  - Operations teams use these leads to:

    - Call or message the customer.

    - Clarify any questions about pricing or device condition.

    - Encourage the customer to complete the order.

  - Since the quote and device details are already available, Ops can resume the conversation from where the customer dropped off.

## Common scenarios to verify

Use these scenarios to confirm that lead creation is wired correctly for the Customer App.

### Leads should be created

- **Customer reaches final price and backs out**

  - Customer is logged in.

  - Starts from HomeScreen or search, passes serviceability, completes all Q\&A, selects payment method.

  - Sees the final price confirmation screen.

  - Closes the app, navigates away, or goes back **without tapping Confirm Order**.

  - **Expected:** A lead is created and visible in **Admin Panel → Leads** with correct device, Q\&A answers, pincode, and customer details.

- **Customer changes device but still does not confirm**

  - Customer is logged in and changes model or variant mid-flow, then completes Q\&A again.

  - Final price screen shows the updated device and quote.

  - Customer does not confirm the order.

  - **Expected:** The lead reflects the **latest** selected device and Q\&A answers that were visible on the final price screen.

### Leads should not be created

- **Not logged in**

  - Customer browses devices or starts the flow without being logged in and reaches or leaves a partial state.

  - **Expected:** No lead is created because the customer is not authenticated.

- **Pincode not serviceable**

  - Customer selects a device but enters a pincode that is not serviceable.

  - Flow blocks before condition questions or pricing.

  - **Expected:** No lead is created because the final price is never shown.

- **Order confirmed**

  - Customer is logged in and completes the full flow, reaches final price, and **taps Confirm Order**.

  - **Expected:** This becomes a **confirmed order** only, not a lead. Do not create a duplicate lead.

## Developer and tester notes

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Use consistent logging and test accounts to confirm when leads are created or skipped. This makes it easier to validate all edge cases without polluting production analytics.
</Callout>

### Developer checklist

- Trigger lead creation in the backend when:

  - `user.isLoggedIn === true`.

  - Final device price has been calculated and displayed.

  - The session ends or user exits **without** a successful order confirmation event.

- Ensure the payload includes:

  - Selected device (brand, model, variant).

  - All condition Q\&A answers used to compute the quote.

  - Pincode and any serviceability flags.

  - Customer identifiers and contact details from the profile.

  - Timestamp and a flag or field indicating **Customer App** as the source.

- Expose leads to:

  - The shared **Admin Panel → Leads** list.

  - Any analytics or CRM integration that uses leads for follow-up.

### Tester checklist

- Verify **positive cases**:

  - Follow the full sell-device journey in the Customer App as a logged-in test user.

  - Reach the final price screen and exit without confirming.

  - Check **Admin Panel → Leads** to ensure:

    - A new lead exists.

    - Device details, Q\&A answers, pincode, and customer profile data are correct.

    - Source is correctly marked as Customer App if configured.

- Verify **negative cases**:

  - Repeat flows where:

    - The user is not logged in.

    - Pincode is not serviceable.

    - The user leaves before finishing Q\&A.

    - The user confirms the order.

  - Confirm **no unexpected leads** appear in the Admin Panel for these scenarios.

- Cross-verify **field mapping**:

  - Compare what is shown on the final price screen with what appears in the lead record.

  - Ensure all answers and device details match exactly.
