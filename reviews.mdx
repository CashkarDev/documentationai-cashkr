## Overview

**Sidebar path:** Marketing → Platform Reviews (`/platform-reviews`)

The Platform Reviews page aggregates all customer reviews from Google My Business, Google Play Store, and Apple App Store into a single view. Admins use this page to track sentiment by platform, search and filter reviews, and control which reviews appear in Cashkr public-facing widgets.

<Callout kind="info">

Use the Reviews page to monitor sentiment across platforms, quickly find specific feedback with search and filters, and curate which reviews are visible in widgets that appear on the Cashkr website and apps.

</Callout>

## What the Reviews page shows

The top-right corner of the page displays the total number of reviews collected across all connected platforms (e.g. *Total: 633*). The list is paginated, with a "Page X of Y (N reviews)" indicator at the bottom.

Each row in the reviews list represents a single customer review and includes:

- **Platform**: Source badge — Google, Play Store, or App Store.
- **Reviewer**: Reviewer name and profile picture from the original platform.
- **Rating**: Star rating from 1 to 5.
- **Review**: Review text from the customer. Long reviews are truncated with a `Show More` link to expand the full text.
- **Images**: Image icon for reviews with attached images.
- **Date**: Date the review was posted on the original platform.
- **Visibility**: Eye icon that controls whether the review appears in Cashkr public-facing review displays.

## Filters and search

Use search and filters together to quickly focus on a specific set of reviews.

### Search

The search box ("Search by reviewer name or review text...") matches:

- Reviewer name.
- Keywords contained in the review text.

### Platform filter

Filter reviews by the platform where they were posted:

- **All Platforms**: Show reviews from every connected source.
- **Google**: Show only Google My Business reviews.
- **Play Store**: Show only Google Play Store reviews.
- **App Store**: Show only Apple App Store reviews.

### Rating filter

Focus on reviews by star rating:

- **All Ratings**: Show reviews with any rating.
- **Specific ratings**: Filter to 5, 4, 3, 2, or 1-star reviews.

### Visibility filter

Segment reviews based on their current visibility setting:

- **All**: Show all reviews, regardless of visibility.
- **Visible**: Show only reviews currently set to visible.
- **Hidden**: Show only reviews that have been hidden.

### Reset filters

- Use **Reset** to clear all filters and search terms and return to the full reviews list.

## Visibility toggle

Each review row includes a Visibility eye icon on the right side. It controls whether the review appears on Cashkr public-facing review sections on the website or apps.

- **Green eye**: The review is visible.
- **Eye off**: The review is hidden and will not appear publicly.

Use the visibility toggle to:

- Hide reviews that are spam, off-topic, or not representative of real customer experience.
- Feature high-quality positive reviews in public widgets.
- Suppress reviews that contain sensitive, personal, or inappropriate content.

## Core workflow: Curate review visibility

Use this workflow regularly to keep public-facing reviews aligned with Cashkr brand and quality standards.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter and search for relevant reviews" icon="search">

  - Open Marketing → Platform Reviews.
  - Use search, platform, rating, or visibility filters to narrow down to the reviews you want to curate.
  - For example, filter by low ratings (1 and 2 stars) to triage issues or by 5 stars to identify reviews to feature.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Review content and context" icon="star">

  - Scan the rating, review text, images indicator, and date for each review.
  - Prioritize recent reviews and those that mention specific issues, locations, or team members.
  - Note any reviews that are clearly spam, irrelevant, or contain sensitive information.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Adjust visibility for widgets" icon="eye">

  - For reviews you want to show publicly, make sure the eye icon is on (green).
  - For spam, irrelevant, or sensitive reviews, switch the eye off to hide them.
  - Use the **Visible** / **Hidden** filter to double-check the curated set.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Share and follow up where needed" icon="share-2">

  - Share standout positive reviews internally with marketing and operations for recognition.
  - For negative but constructive reviews, coordinate with the team to respond directly on Google My Business, Play Console, or App Store Connect.
  - Revisit filtered views (such as low ratings) regularly to track whether issues are improving over time.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
The current admin panel has no separate Widgets tab or screen under Platform Reviews — visibility is managed directly from this list.
</Callout>

## Admin responsibilities

Admins and marketing owners use the Reviews page to keep Cashkr’s public reputation accurate and up to date:

- Check the Reviews page regularly to monitor customer sentiment across all platforms.
- Filter by low ratings (1 and 2 stars) to spot operational or service issues that need action.
- Respond to negative reviews through the original platform (Google, Play Store, App Store) outside the admin panel.
- Use the visibility toggle to curate which reviews appear in public-facing widgets and on the website/app.
- Use the Platform filter to compare performance and sentiment by channel (Google vs Play Store vs App Store).
- Share notable positive reviews with marketing and operations teams for recognition, training, and morale.

## Connections to other modules

Reviews in this section connect to several other parts of the Cashkr system:

- **Customer app and website**: Visible reviews can appear on customer-facing pages to build trust and improve conversion.
- **Quality - B2C**: Patterns in negative reviews can reveal vendor quality or service issues that the Quality module should address.
- **Push Notifications**: Marketing can use push notifications to encourage satisfied customers to leave reviews on Google or the app stores, which will then appear here once collected.

## Important notes

Reviews shown on this page are synchronized automatically from connected external platforms. You manage visibility inside Cashkr, but ownership and control of the underlying review content stays with Google, Play Store, and App Store.

<Callout kind="alert">

You cannot edit or delete reviews from the admin panel. Hiding a review only removes it from Cashkr public displays; it does not remove or change the review on the original platform. Always publish responses or replies directly on Google My Business, Play Console, or App Store Connect — the admin panel is for monitoring and curation only.

</Callout>
