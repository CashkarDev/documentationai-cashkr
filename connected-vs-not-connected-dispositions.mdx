## Purpose and quick meta

Use the correct disposition on every call so reporting, follow-ups, and conversion decisions stay accurate and fair.

**Used by:** Inbound Sales and Follow-Up teams  
**When to use:** After every call attempt on any Cashkr lead  
**Outcome:** One clear disposition per attempt that matches what actually happened on the call

<Callout kind="info">

**Golden rule:** Disposition must reflect what actually happened on the call, not what you hoped would happen.

</Callout>

---

## Quick decision flow

<Steps>

<Step title="1. Did you speak to the customer?" icon="help-circle">

If you had any two-way conversation (even 2–3 words), select **Connected**.  
If you never heard the customer speak, select **Not Connected**.

</Step>

<Step title="2. If Connected, what is the next action?" icon="arrow-right">

Within **Connected**, pick the sub-disposition that best matches the outcome, such as:

- Callback
- Customer Will Place Order
- Waiting for Quote
- Already Placed an Order
- Technical Issue
- Not Interested

</Step>

<Step title="3. If customer is ready now and you create the order" icon="check-circle">

If the customer agrees to sell now, accepts the price, needs help to complete the flow, and you actually create the order during the call, select **Create Order** as the disposition.

Do **not** use Create Order as a Connected sub-option. It is a separate disposition and applies **only when an order is actually created**.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">

If customer spoke → Connected.  
If customer did not speak → Not Connected.  
If you created the order on that call → Create Order.

</Callout>

## Disposition details by type

Use these tabs while you are on calls to pick the correct option and write compliant notes.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Connected" icon="phone">

### Definition

You **successfully spoke** to the customer and exchanged at least a few meaningful words. The customer picked up, acknowledged the call, and there was two-way communication.

Any real conversation, even if short or negative, is **Connected**.

### When to select Connected

Select **Connected** when:

- Customer answered and spoke
- Customer said they are busy and asked you to call later
- Customer rejected the offer or is not interested
- Customer said they will place the order themselves
- Customer already placed the order
- Customer requested a callback or has questions or objections
- Customer raised a technical issue related to Cashkr

After selecting **Connected**, you **must** choose one of the sub-dispositions:

- Customer Will Place Order
- Callback
- Waiting for Quote
- Already Placed an Order
- Technical Issue
- Not Interested (with reason)

<Callout kind="alert">

Never use **Create Order** as a Connected sub-option. Treat **Create Order** as its own disposition and use it only when you actually create the order on that call.

</Callout>

### Mandatory notes for Connected

Notes for Connected calls must include:

- Customer intent (sell now, compare, delay, reject, etc.)
- Any objection or concern
- Clear next step (who will do what and when)

Example notes you can copy and adjust:

```text
Customer comparing prices with 2 other sites.
Requested detailed quote on WhatsApp. Callback tomorrow after 6 PM.
```

```text
Customer will place order on website tonight.
Explained pickup and payment steps. No objections.
```

```text
Customer not interested due to low price.
Said will wait for a better offer. No follow-up planned.
```

### Example scenarios (Connected)

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Customer said: 'Call me at 7 PM'">

**What happened:** Customer picked up, said they are busy, and requested a specific callback time.

**Select:** Connected → Callback

**Note example:**

```text
Customer at work, asked for callback today at 7 PM.
Will decide after talking to family.
```

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Customer said: 'I will place the order myself'">

**What happened:** Customer is positive, wants to sell, and will complete the flow themselves.

**Select:** Connected → Customer Will Place Order

**Note example:**

```text
Customer agreed on price and understands flow.
Will place order on website tonight after 9 PM.
```

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Customer is comparing offers / waiting for better quote">

**What happened:** Customer is interested but still evaluating or needs a quote.

**Select:** Connected → Waiting for Quote

**Note example:**

```text
Customer comparing offers with 2 other platforms.
Shared expected price range. Will confirm after receiving updated quote.
```

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Customer already placed order with Cashkr">

**What happened:** Customer has already booked a pickup/order in Cashkr.

**Select:** Connected → Already Placed an Order

**Note example:**

```text
Customer already placed order yesterday.
Confirmed pickup schedule and payment mode. No further follow-up needed.
```

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Customer not interested">

**What happened:** Customer clearly declined.

**Select:** Connected → Not Interested

**Note example:**

```text
Customer declined due to low price compared to local shop.
Said will not proceed with Cashkr. No follow-up planned.
```

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Customer reported technical issue with app/website">

**What happened:** Customer wants to sell but is blocked by a technical problem.

**Select:** Connected → Technical Issue

**Note example:**

```text
Customer cannot complete order; app crashes on payment screen.
Shared steps to retry and escalated issue to support team.
```

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

</Tab>

<Tab title="Not Connected" icon="x-circle">

### Definition

You **did not speak** to the customer at all. There was no two-way communication and you never heard the customer’s voice.

Ringing, silence, network errors, or call drop **before** the customer speaks are all **Not Connected**.

### When to select Not Connected

Select **Not Connected** when:

- Phone is switched off
- Number is not reachable or out of network
- No answer (ringing, but nobody picks up)
- Call drops before the customer says anything
- Call goes to voicemail or disconnects without customer speaking

At Cashkr, **Switched Off** and **Not Reachable** are both treated as **Not Connected**.

<Callout kind="info">

For Not Connected leads, make at least **3 call attempts** at **different time slots** (same day or next day) before you stop trying.

</Callout>

### Mandatory notes for Not Connected

Notes for Not Connected must include:

- Attempt number
- Time (and optionally, time window)
- Short status description

Example notes you can copy and adjust:

```text
No answer – 1st attempt at 11:30 AM.
Ringing for 25 seconds, call not picked.
```

```text
Number not reachable – 2nd attempt at 4:10 PM.
Will retry after 7 PM.
```

```text
Phone switched off – 3rd attempt at 9:05 AM.
Marked as Not Connected after 3 attempts.
```

### Example scenarios (Not Connected)

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Phone switched off">

**What happened:** Call did not reach the customer because the phone was off.

**Select:** Not Connected

**Note example:**

```text
Phone switched off – 1st attempt at 2:15 PM.
Will retry in evening slot.
```

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Ringing, no pickup">

**What happened:** Phone rang but customer did not answer.

**Select:** Not Connected

**Note example:**

```text
No answer – 2nd attempt at 7:20 PM.
Rang for 30 seconds, call not picked.
```

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Call dropped before 'hello'">

**What happened:** Network issue or disconnect before the customer spoke.

**Select:** Not Connected

**Note example:**

```text
Call disconnected before customer picked – 3rd attempt at 5:40 PM.
Network issue, will not retry further.
```

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Not reachable / out of coverage">

**What happened:** Network message that number is not reachable.

**Select:** Not Connected

**Note example:**

```text
Number not reachable – 1st attempt at 10:05 AM.
Will retry after 3 PM.
```

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

</Tab>

<Tab title="Create Order" icon="check-circle">

### Definition

**Create Order** is a **separate disposition**, not a sub-option under Connected.

Use **Create Order** only when, during this call:

- Customer is ready to sell now
- Customer agrees on the price and details
- Customer needs or accepts your help to complete the flow
- You actually create the order for the customer (web, app, or admin flow)

If you do not create the order on that call, you must use **Connected** with the correct sub-disposition instead.

### When to select Create Order

Select **Create Order** when all of these are true:

- You spoke to the customer (it is a Connected call)
- Customer confirms they want to proceed immediately
- You walk the customer through the full flow and complete the order
- The order is successfully created and visible in the system

If the customer says they will place the order **later**, or they are still deciding, use:

- Connected → Customer Will Place Order, or
- Connected → Waiting for Quote

### Mandatory notes for Create Order

Notes for Create Order must include:

- Device and key details confirmed
- Final price agreed
- Order ID or reference (if visible)
- Any special instruction (pickup time, address, etc.)

Example notes you can copy and adjust:

```text
Order created on call for iPhone 12 128 GB.
Customer agreed on ₹23,500. Pickup scheduled tomorrow 11 AM.
Order ID: ORD_78K39P.
```

```text
Helped customer complete order in app.
Verified address and payment mode (bank transfer). Pickup day after tomorrow evening slot.
Order visible in system.
```

<Callout kind="alert">

Never mark **Create Order** if the order was **not** created.  
If customer plans to place it later, it is **Connected → Customer Will Place Order**, not Create Order.

</Callout>

## Common mistakes to avoid

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Marking Connected when customer never spoke">

**Symptom:** You select Connected because the phone rang or someone else picked up but the customer never spoke.

**Correct behavior:** If you did not hear the customer speak, select **Not Connected** and log attempt number and time.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Marking Not Connected when customer said 'call later'">

**Symptom:** Customer picked up, said they are busy, asked you to call later, but you mark Not Connected.

**Correct behavior:** Customer spoke, so this is **Connected → Callback** with clear next-step notes.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Using Not Connected to avoid logging a difficult conversation">

**Symptom:** You had a hard or negative call and mark Not Connected to save time.

**Correct behavior:** Always log reality. Use **Connected** with the right sub-disposition and capture objection/reason.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Using Create Order without actually creating an order">

**Symptom:** Customer sounds positive or says they will order later, and you mark Create Order to show progress.

**Correct behavior:** Only use **Create Order** if you actually created the order on that call. Otherwise use the correct Connected sub-disposition.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Skipping notes or writing one-word notes">

**Symptom:** Notes like 'callback', 'no answer', or blank notes.

**Correct behavior:** Follow the mandatory notes policy for each disposition so follow-ups and reporting stay reliable.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Why accurate dispositions matter

Accurate dispositions protect the quality of your sales data and the fairness of agent evaluation.

- **If Connected is over-reported:**  
  Conversion percentage looks worse than reality, and management may think pricing or sales skills are the problem when the real issue is reach.

- **If Not Connected is over-reported:**  
  Lead quality and marketing look weak, and sales effort appears low even when interest is there.

- **If Create Order is misused:**  
  Lead-to-order funnel becomes unreliable, and planning for staffing, pickups, and payouts becomes harder.

Correct dispositions lead to correct strategy, training, and resource allocation.

## KPIs impacted

These dispositions directly affect:

- Connected percentage
- Callback success percentage
- Lead to order conversion percentage
- Agent productivity and follow-up quality
- City-wise and channel-wise performance analysis

## One-page quick summary

<Callout kind="info">

**Connected**

- You spoke to the customer  
- Must choose a sub-disposition (Callback, Waiting for Quote, etc.)  
- Notes: intent, objection, next step

**Not Connected**

- No conversation; customer never spoke  
- Make minimum 3 attempts at different time slots  
- Notes: attempt number and time

**Create Order**

- Customer ready now, agrees on price  
- You actually created the order on that call  
- Notes: device, agreed price, order ID, pickup details

**Golden rule:**  
If customer spoke → Connected.  
If customer did not speak → Not Connected.  
If you created the order on that call → Create Order.

</Callout>
