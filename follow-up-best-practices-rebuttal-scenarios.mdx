## Purpose and scope

<Callout kind="info">

This SOP guides inbound sales and follow-up teams on how, when, and how often to follow up with leads, and which rebuttals to use to move conversations forward without pressure or false promises.

**Purpose**

- Keep follow-ups timely, respectful, and consistent across agents
- Focus on conversion while protecting brand trust
- Ensure all attempts are properly logged for analytics and quality review

**Principle**

Follow-ups should help, not harass.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip">

Use this SOP when you are handling leads created from Cashkr website or app and you are responsible for callbacks, follow-ups, and order conversions for those leads.

</Callout>

## Follow-up timing and cadence

<Steps>
  <Step title="1st follow-up – same day" icon="download">

  Contact the customer by **call** on the same day as lead creation, ideally within **6–8 hours**.

  - If connected: use the opening script and update disposition and notes.
  - If not connected: mark disposition correctly and schedule the next attempt.

  </Step>

  <Step title="2nd follow-up – next day" icon="download">

  Make a **call** on the **next day** if the lead is not yet converted or clearly closed.

  - Reuse the opening script with a short reminder of the earlier interaction.
  - Check previous notes before calling.

  </Step>

  <Step title="3rd follow-up – day 3" icon="download">

  Attempt a **call** again on **day 3**. If calls are repeatedly not answered, you may add **WhatsApp** as a support channel.

  - Keep tone helpful and short.
  - Avoid repeating the exact same pitch.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Final touch – day 5" icon="download">

  Use **WhatsApp** on **day 5** as a final gentle nudge if the customer has not responded or has asked for details on WhatsApp.

  - Use the approved WhatsApp template.
  - Close the loop with correct disposition.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">

Do not exceed **4–5 follow-up attempts** per lead across all channels unless the customer specifically requests callbacks.

</Callout>

## Call best practices

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Before the call" icon="settings">

  - Read previous notes.
  - Check the last disposition.
  - Verify price and serviceability.
  - Know the objection raised earlier.

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="During the call" icon="phone">

  - Use the customer’s name.
  - Acknowledge the previous discussion.
  - Keep your explanation concise.
  - Ask one clear question to move the call forward.

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="After the call" icon="edit">

  - Update disposition correctly.
  - Add clear, short notes with key points and next steps.
  - Schedule the next action (callback, WhatsApp, or close) if needed.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">

- Focus on being helpful, not pushy.
- Respect the customer’s time by being clear and brief.
- Ask one clear question instead of many small questions.

</Callout>

## Universal follow-up opening script

Use this opening for most follow-up calls unless another script is specifically required.

> **Agent:**  
> “Hi \[Name], this is \[Your Name] from Cashkr.  
> I’m just following up regarding the price you checked for your \[Device].”
>
> ⏸ Pause  
> “Is now a good time to talk?”

If the customer says **no**, confirm a better time and set a **callback** disposition with the time in notes.

## Rebuttal scenarios and approved responses

<ExpandableGroup>

  <Expandable title="Scenario 1: “I’m still thinking”" default-open="true">

  **What it means**

  Customer is unsure or needs more time, but is not rejecting the offer.

  **Approved response**

  > “That’s absolutely fine.  
  > Just wanted to check if you need any help or clarification from my side.”

  Guide the customer by asking what is stopping them from placing the order today and address that specific concern.

  **Recommended disposition**

  - Connected → Customer Will Place Order  
  - Or Callback (if they ask for a specific time to think and decide)

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Scenario 2: “Call me later” (repeated)">

  **What it means**

  Customer is not ready to talk now or may be trying to avoid the conversation.

  **Approved response**

  > “Sure. To respect your time, when would be the best time to call you?”

  If the customer gives a clear time, repeat it back and **log it in notes**.

  If the customer is vague again and keeps saying “later” or “some time”:

  - Reduce call frequency.
  - Move to a single WhatsApp follow-up as a gentle reminder.

  **Recommended disposition**

  - Callback (when a time is agreed)
  - Or Customer Will Place Order (if they say they will place it themselves)
  - Add notes about repeated “call later” requests.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Scenario 3: “Price is still low”">

  **What it means**

  Customer feels the quoted price is less than expected or has seen a higher offer elsewhere.

  **Approved response**

  > “Understood. The price is based on the condition selected.  
  > If your device condition is better, the final price after inspection can improve.”

  Avoid promising a higher price. Explain that the inspection team checks the actual condition and updates the price accordingly.

  **Recommended disposition**

  - Waiting for Quote

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Scenario 4: “I’m comparing with others”">

  **What it means**

  Customer is checking multiple platforms and has not yet decided where to sell.

  **Approved response**

  > “That’s fair.  
  > Just keep in mind—Cashkr does inspection in front of you and payment is instant at pickup.”

  Add one short benefit and then stop. Do not speak negatively about competitors.

  **Recommended disposition**

  - Waiting for Quote  
  - Or Customer Will Place Order (if they confirm they will likely choose Cashkr)

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Scenario 5: “I’ll place it myself”">

  **What it means**

  Customer prefers to complete the order journey on their own, without agent support.

  **Approved response**

  > “Perfect.  
  > If you need any help, you can always reach out.”

  Ask if they plan to place it today or later and note this in the system.

  **Recommended disposition**

  - Connected → Customer Will Place Order

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Scenario 6: Customer sounds irritated">

  **What it means**

  Customer feels disturbed by the call frequency, timing, or is not interested.

  **Approved response**

  > “I’m sorry to bother you. I’ll stop calling.  
  > If you ever need help, Cashkr is here.”

  Do not push further once irritation is clear.

  **Recommended disposition**

  - Connected + note (for not interested or do not call)  
  - Stop further follow-ups for this lead.

  </Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## WhatsApp follow-up guidelines

Use WhatsApp as a **supporting** channel, not a spam channel.

Use WhatsApp when:

- Calls go unanswered multiple times.
- Customer asked for details on WhatsApp.
- You need a final gentle nudge after previous attempts.

### Approved WhatsApp template

> “Hi \[Name], this is \[Your Name] from Cashkr.  
> Just checking if you’d like help with selling your \[Device].  
> You’ll get instant payment at pickup.  
> Reply anytime.”

<Callout kind="alert">

Send **only one** WhatsApp follow-up per lead as a nudge. Do not send repeated WhatsApp messages if the customer does not reply.

</Callout>

## When to stop following up

Stop follow-ups in these cases and mark the lead correctly:

- Customer clearly says **not interested**.
- Customer requests **no more calls** or **do not disturb**.
- Area is not serviceable.
- Device is not supported.
- You have completed **4–5 total attempts** with no response.

<Callout kind="alert">

Once you stop follow-ups for any of the above reasons, do not restart calling the same lead unless the customer reaches out again or a supervisor specifically instructs you.

</Callout>

## Disposition mapping for follow-ups

Use these mappings so reporting and next actions are accurate:

- **Ready now** → Create Order  
  Customer agrees to proceed and is ready to place or confirm the order on the call.

- **Thinking** → Customer Will Place Order  
  Customer needs time but shows interest and may place the order themselves or after a callback.

- **Wants call later** → Callback  
  Customer gives a specific time or asks for a later call.

- **Comparing options** → Waiting for Quote  
  Customer is checking other offers and has not decided yet.

- **No response** → Not Connected  
  No call pick up or no meaningful interaction.

- **Not interested** → Connected + note  
  Customer declines clearly or asks you to stop calling.

For more detailed definitions, refer to **Understanding Dispositions (Connected, Not Connected, Create Order)** in the Training section.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Avoid these behaviours during follow-ups:

- Repeating the exact same pitch every call.
- Ignoring previous notes or dispositions.
- Sounding impatient, irritated, or desperate for the order.
- Over-calling beyond the approved cadence.
- Using the wrong disposition that does not match the conversation.

<Callout kind="tip">

Golden rule: be helpful, specific, and respectful in every interaction. Your goal is to **convert with respect, not pressure**.

</Callout>

## Quick training summary

- **Follow-up cadence**: Day 0 (within 6–8 hours) → Day 1 → Day 3 → Day 5 (final touch on WhatsApp).  
- **Tone**: Helpful, calm, and respectful of the customer’s time.  
- **Golden line**: “Do you need any help or clarification from my side?”  
- **Hard limits**: Maximum 4–5 attempts per lead, one WhatsApp follow-up, and stop when the customer says not interested or no calls.
