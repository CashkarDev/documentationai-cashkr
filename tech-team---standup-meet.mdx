## Daily tech standup format

Daily standup keeps the Cashkr tech team aligned on build priorities and highlights blockers before they slow delivery.

<Callout kind="info">

- **Time:** 15–30 minutes (recommended: 10:30 AM)
- **Participants:** Flutter dev, backend dev, web dev, UI/UX, QA
- **Goal:** Ensure everyone is unblocked and aligned on build priorities

</Callout>

## Agenda and discussion flow

Use this shared structure every day so updates stay concise and actionable.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Today" icon="check-circle">

  Focus on what actually moved forward since the last standup.

  - What did you complete today?
  - Did you face any issues or unexpected scope changes?

  </Step>

  <Step title="Tomorrow" icon="arrow-right">

  Align on what you will move next so dependencies are clear.

  - What are you working on tomorrow?
  - Do you foresee any dependencies or blockers?

  </Step>

  <Step title="Blockers / needs" icon="alert-triangle">

  Call out anything that might slow progress so it can be handled after standup.

  - Are you waiting on feedback or approvals?
  - Are any APIs or integrations not working?
  - Are UI changes or design clarifications pending?
  - Do you need QA support or new test cases?

  </Step>

</Steps>

## Role-specific prompts

Use these prompts to keep your update focused on what the team needs to know.

<ExpandableGroup>

  <Expandable title="Shahid (Flutter app dev)" default-open="true">

  - What module are you building tomorrow?
  - Are there any crashes, SDK issues, or integration blockers?

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Roshan (backend dev)">

  - Are there any API changes planned or in progress?
  - Is any deployment pending?
  - Which bugs are you resolving tomorrow?

  </Expandable>

  - Are there any alignment or contract issues with backend APIs?

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Bikesh (UI/UX)">

  - Do you have feedback loops open with QA or devs?

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Nirajan (QA/tester)">

  - What is ready for production push?

  </Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Best practices

- Keep the standup under **30 minutes**.
- Focus on **progress and blockers**, not detailed task breakdowns.
- Keep tickets and updates **maintained and current in Astravue**.
- Highlight **urgent escalations** and handle deep dives **after** the standup.
