## Details

### G1. Growth

Growth focuses on consistently increasing top-of-funnel demand and converting it into completed orders. Marketing, sales, product, and operations should all contribute to meeting these volume targets.

- 6000+ leads / month  
- 1,200+ completed orders / month

### G2. Profitability

Profitability keeps growth sustainable by controlling acquisition costs and improving funnel efficiency. Performance marketing, sales, and conversion teams should anchor their metrics here.

- Target CPA ≤ ₹50 across all channels  
- 40% Completion Rate

### G3. Experience

Experience measures how reliably we deliver a fast and high-quality journey for customers. Product, CX, operations, and vendor teams should track their work against these experience benchmarks.

- CSAT ≥ 4.8/5  
- Average TAT from order to pickup ≤ 24 hours

### G4. Vendor Performance

Vendor performance keeps the partner network reliable and predictable. Vendor management, operations, and training teams should use this as the primary SLA benchmark.

- SLA compliance ≥ 95%

### G5. People & Execution

People and execution ensure clarity, accountability, and consistent follow-through. HR, leadership, and all managers should use this to structure role definitions and reporting.

- 100% roles with defined KRA + KPI + weekly reporting
