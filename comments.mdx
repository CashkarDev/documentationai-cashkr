## Overview

Manage all article comments submitted on the Cashkr website from a single place in Strapi. Use the Comments collection type to review, approve, reject, edit, or delete feedback linked to specific articles.

<Callout kind="info">

Comments require approval before they appear publicly on the website.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert">

Rejected comments are archived and do not appear on the website.

</Callout>

## What it shows

The Comments collection type shows a list of comments linked to articles, including their statuses:

- Pending
- Approved
- Rejected

## Common tasks

Use these steps to handle the full moderation flow for article comments.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Content Manager">

Open Strapi, then go to the Content Manager section in the left sidebar.

</Step>
<Step title="Select the Comments collection type">

In Content Manager, select the Comments collection type to view the list of article comments.

</Step>
<Step title="Search or filter comments by article or status">

Use search and filters to narrow comments by related article or by status (pending, approved, rejected).

</Step>
<Step title="Add a new comment manually">

From the Comments list, add a new entry to create a comment manually and link it to the relevant article.

</Step>
<Step title="Approve or reject pending comments in the list view">

From the list view, change the status of pending comments to approved or rejected as needed.

</Step>
<Step title="Update or delete existing comments">

Open an existing comment to update it, or delete it if it should be removed.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Statuses

Comments use three main statuses:

- **Pending** – New comments waiting for approval.
- **Approved** – Comments that can display publicly on the website.
- **Rejected** – Comments that are archived and not shown publicly.

## System logic

Strapi only shows approved comments on the website. After you update a comment and save and publish it, the change is reflected on the live site based on its status.

## Connections

The Comments collection is linked to the article comment section on the Cashkr website. Each comment entry is associated with a specific article so that updates in Strapi reflect on the correct article page.
