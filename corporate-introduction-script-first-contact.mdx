title: Corporate Introduction Script (First Contact)
description: Phone script and email template for introducing Cashkr corporate buyback, gathering basic requirements, and setting a clear next step.
---

**Used by:** B2B Sales Executive / Corporate buyback calling team  

**Objective:** Introduce Cashkr’s corporate buyback solution, understand the lead’s requirement at a high level, and set up a clear next step (detailed discussion or quotation request).

<Callout kind="info">

Maintain a professional, concise, and confident tone. Focus on value (instant payment, bulk handling, secure process) rather than a hard sales pitch, and always end with a clear next step.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert">

Do not overpromise timelines or services. Only commit to what is written here: introduction email plus device list format, quotation within 24 hours after details, and response within 3 working hours for website leads.

</Callout>

## Call and email flow

Use this flow live during calls. Move through each step, then send the email template on the same day.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Call script" icon="phone">

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Opening and context" icon="play-circle">

    Use this to greet the right contact and state why you are calling.

    > "Hi, this is \[Your Name] from Cashkr – India’s Instant Buyback Platform. Am I speaking with \[Contact Name] from \[Company Name]?"

    If yes:

    > "Great. I am calling regarding your recent inquiry about selling old IT devices in bulk. We handle corporate device buyback and recycling programs for companies across India, and I wanted to briefly introduce how we can help."

    </Step>

    <Step title="Short value proposition" icon="info">

    Keep this part crisp and focused on benefits.

    > "At Cashkr, we help organizations **liquidate their used IT assets** – phones, laptops, and other devices – through a **secure, hassle-free process**."

    You can highlight:

    - Instant payment after pickup  
    - Data wipe certificates, if required  
    - Pan-India pickup coverage  
    - Transparent pricing based on current resale value

    A full version you can read as a block:

    > "We offer instant payment after pickup, data wipe certificates if required, pan-India pickup coverage, and transparent pricing based on current resale value."

    </Step>

    <Step title="Understand high-level requirement" icon="help-circle">

    Ask for a quick overview so you can plan the quotation and next steps.

    > "To suggest the right buyback quote, could you share a quick overview of the devices you are looking to sell – for example, the quantity, brands, and types such as phones or laptops?"

    Aim to collect at least:

    - Approximate **quantity** of devices  
    - **Device types and brands** (phones, laptops, desktops, tablets, etc.)  
    - Expected **timeline** for disposal or pickup  
    - **Pickup locations** or cities involved  
    - Whether they **need data wipe certificates**

    If they are not ready to share details yet:

    > "No problem. I will share a short company overview and a simple format where you can list the devices whenever convenient."

    </Step>

    <Step title="Confirm next step and close call" icon="check-circle">

    Use this to lock in the follow-up and set expectations.

    > "Thank you for your time today. I will send you an official introduction email with our company profile and a simple device list format. Once we receive that, our team will prepare a **custom quote within 24 hours**."

    <Callout kind="tip">

    Use the optional add-on below for leads that came through the corporate buyback website form.

    </Callout>

    Optional add-on for website form leads:

    > "I can see your submission came from our website. We usually respond to corporate leads within **3 working hours**, and I will personally ensure your proposal is prioritized."

    Close the call politely:

    > "Appreciate your time, \[Name]. I will email you the device list format right after this call, and we will take it from there. If you have any specific timeline or target date for pickup, do let me know so we can align accordingly."

    </Step>
  </Steps>

## Internal notes for team

Use these reminders while working on corporate buyback leads.

- If you do not receive the completed device list within 48 hours, move to **Follow-Up Script #2 (Requirement Gathering)**.  
- For large companies or those with a dedicated IT team, ask for the **IT Asset Manager** or **Procurement Head** contact details.  
- Log every lead in the **Corporate Lead Sheet** with status **Intro Call Done – Awaiting Device Details** after you finish the first call.
