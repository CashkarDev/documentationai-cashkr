## Overview

Vendors call customers from inside an in-progress order in the Vendor App. The call is placed through Cashkr's telephony provider using the vendor's **dedicated DID number**, so the customer sees that number rather than the vendor's personal phone.

<Callout kind="info">
The vendor does not need to know or dial the customer's number. The app initiates the call and the platform connects both parties.
</Callout>

## What the customer sees

The customer's phone rings showing the vendor's dedicated DID number as the caller ID. They do not see the vendor's personal mobile number at any point.

Because the same DID is used for every call that vendor makes, a customer who is called more than once during an order sees a consistent number.

## Placing a call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the in-progress order" icon="play-circle">
    Go to **In Progress** in the Vendor App and select the order you are working on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the call action" icon="phone">
    Use the call option on the order. The app requests the call through the platform rather than opening your native dialler with the customer's number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Speak to the customer" icon="message-square">
    The call connects. The customer sees your dedicated DID number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the call disposition" icon="check-circle">
    Record the outcome — Connected, Not Reachable, Reschedule Requested, or another configured option.

    This is still mandatory. The app blocks further action on the lead until a disposition is submitted. See [Call Disposition](/call-disposition).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rules to be aware of

- **You cannot choose or change your DID number.** It is assigned by an admin, in the same way your vendor type and tier access are.
- **Do not share your personal number with customers.** The DID exists so customers reach you through the platform. Giving out a personal number bypasses call traceability and breaks the privacy protection.
- **The call action lives on the order.** There is no general dialler in the app — calls are always placed in the context of a specific in-progress order.
- **Disposition is unchanged.** Nothing about the DID feature removes or alters the requirement to record what happened on the call.

<Callout kind="alert">
If a customer asks for a direct number to call you back, direct them to the number that called them. Do not give out your personal mobile.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

<Expandable title="The call action is not working" default-open="false">
Check the basics first: network connectivity, and whether the order is genuinely in **In Progress** status. The call action lives on in-progress orders.

If the action is present but calls do not connect, report it with your vendor ID, the order ID, and the time of the attempt. See [Reporting a Bug](/help-center/guides/reporting-a-bug).
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="The customer says they saw an unknown number" default-open="false">
That is expected. The number shown is your dedicated DID, not your personal mobile. Explain that it is a Cashkr number assigned to you.

If the customer reports seeing a *personal* mobile number, that is worth escalating — it suggests the call did not route through the platform.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="The customer called the number back" default-open="false">
Inbound behaviour on the DID is **not currently documented**. If a customer tells you they called back and reached someone unexpected, or reached nobody, report it so the behaviour can be confirmed and documented.
</Expandable>
