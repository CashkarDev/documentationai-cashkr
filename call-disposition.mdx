## Overview

Call disposition records what happened when the vendor contacted the customer about an in-progress lead or order.

It exists so operations and Admin teams can see, for each lead, whether the customer was connected, not reachable, or requested a reschedule or other outcome.

## Where call disposition appears

Call disposition appears inside an **In Progress** lead or order in the Vendor App.

When a vendor opens an in-progress lead, the app shows the call disposition action as the first thing to complete before moving ahead in the flow.

<Callout kind="info">

Call disposition is part of the In Progress orders workflow. When the vendor calls the customer, they update the call disposition to record what happened on the call.

</Callout>

## Step-by-step: submit call disposition

<Steps>

<Step title="Open an in-progress lead" icon="play-circle">

- Open the Vendor App.
- Go to your **In Progress** orders.
- Select the specific lead or order you are working on.

Your screen shows the lead or order details with call-related actions.

</Step>

<Step title="Call the customer" icon="phone">

- Use the call option from the in-progress lead to contact the customer.
- Complete the call attempt.

You should now be ready to record what happened on this call.

</Step>

<Step title="Submit call disposition" icon="check-circle">

- Look for the **Call Disposition** section on the in-progress lead.
- Select the appropriate outcome, for example **Connected**, **Not Reachable**, or **Reschedule Requested**.
- Submit the disposition.

Once you submit, the system saves the disposition and unlocks the next actions for this lead.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">

Vendor opens an in-progress lead. The first mandatory step is **Submit Call Disposition** (for example, Connected, Not Reachable, Reschedule Requested). Only after disposition is updated, next steps become available.

</Callout>

## Common disposition outcomes

Use call disposition to capture the main outcome of the call. Typical examples include:

- **Connected** — Vendor spoke to the customer and progressed the discussion.
- **Not Reachable** — Vendor could not reach the customer after the call attempt.
- **Reschedule Requested** — Customer asked to reschedule the pickup or call.
- **Customer asked to cancel** — Customer indicated they do not want to continue with the order.

These examples are not an exhaustive list; the exact options depend on the configured Vendor App UI.

## Key rules and behaviors

<Callout kind="info">

Call disposition is required once per lead before any other action. The app blocks further actions on that lead until you submit a disposition.

</Callout>

After you submit a valid call disposition:

- The app unlocks next steps for the lead or order.
- The system saves the disposition to the Admin Panel for tracking and reporting.
- The disposition appears in the Admin activity logs so teams can see what happened on the call.

The system expects one required disposition per lead; if you call the customer multiple times, any additional logic should be defined at product level before adding to this flow.

## System logic

When the vendor submits a call disposition, the system must store at least the following data:

- **Disposition value** — For example, `Connected`, `Not Reachable`, `Reschedule Requested`, or another configured label.
- **Timestamp** — Exact date and time when the vendor submitted the disposition.
- **Lead or order ID** — The identifier of the in-progress lead or order the disposition belongs to.
- **Vendor or agent identity** — The vendor or agent who submitted the disposition.

The system then syncs this record into the Admin Panel:

- Add an **activity log entry** under the corresponding lead or order.
- Make the disposition value and timestamp available for **tracking and reporting** in Admin views.

<Callout kind="tip">

For development, ensure the same disposition payload is used for both the Vendor App request and the Admin Panel activity log, so reporting and per-lead history always align.

</Callout>

## Connections

Call disposition is closely tied to other parts of the Vendor App and Admin flows:

- **In Progress orders workflow** — Vendors open an in-progress lead, call the customer, and then update the call disposition to record what happened before continuing.
- **Operations monitoring** — The operations team uses Admin activity logs to monitor orders, check if customers were reachable, and see if reschedules or cancellations were requested.
- **Reporting and analytics** — Saved dispositions feed Admin Panel reports for reachability rates, reschedule trends, and cancellation patterns per vendor or region.

These connections rely on call disposition being consistently captured once per lead.

## Common states and errors

In the Vendor App and API, handle these common states and error scenarios:

- **Missing required field**  
  The vendor tries to submit disposition without selecting a value. Show a clear validation message and keep the lead locked until a value is selected.

- **User tries to proceed without disposition**  
  The vendor attempts another action on the lead (for example, progressing the order) before submitting disposition. Block the action and prompt them to submit call disposition first.

- **Network or sync failure**  
  The vendor selects a disposition but the network request fails. Keep the lead in a state where disposition is still required, show an error message, and prompt the vendor to retry when connectivity is restored.

<Callout kind="info">

Never mark the lead as having a completed disposition if the submission to the backend or Admin activity log fails. The lead must remain blocked until the system confirms a successful save.

</Callout>

## Developer and tester notes

<Callout kind="tip">

Use these notes to align Vendor App, API, and Admin Panel behavior during implementation and QA.

</Callout>

- **Single required disposition per lead**  
  Enforce the rule "required once per lead before any other action" at the backend level, not only in the UI. Attempts to progress a lead without a saved disposition should return a clear error.

- **Consistent data model**  
  Ensure the disposition record always includes disposition value, timestamp, lead or order ID, and vendor or agent identity, and that the same identifiers are used in Admin activity logs.

- **Activity log verification**  
  In tests, after submitting a disposition from the Vendor App, verify that:
  - The corresponding Admin Panel lead or order shows a new activity log entry.
  - The value, timestamp, and vendor match the Vendor App submission.

- **Retry and idempotency**  
  For flaky networks, treat repeated submissions of the same disposition for the same lead as idempotent where possible, so vendors do not accidentally create conflicting records.

- **Alignment with In Progress orders docs**  
  When updating the In Progress orders flow, keep the call disposition behavior and wording consistent with this page so vendors see the same concepts across screens and documentation.
