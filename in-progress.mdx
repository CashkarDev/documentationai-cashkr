## Overview

The In Progress page (**Orders → Active → In Progress**, breadcrumb *Dashboard / Orders / In Progress*) displays all orders that have been assigned to a vendor and are currently active in the field. Admins use this page to monitor order progress, track pickup statuses, and take action on delayed or stalled orders.

<Callout kind="info">
  An order appears in In Progress only after an admin or system process assigns it to a vendor. Unassigned orders appear in the Not Assigned section and must be actioned separately.
</Callout>

## Related Orders views

In the sidebar, the **Orders** menu groups views into **Active** (All Orders, Live Order Map, Upcoming, Reserved, In Progress), **Needs Attention** (Not Assigned, Assigned · No Action, Delayed, Flagged, To Be Failed, Coverage Gaps) and **Closed** (Order Audit, Failed After Requote, Failed (No Requote)). See [All Orders](/all-orders) for the full list.

The count badges shown next to **Assigned · No Action** and **Delayed** in the sidebar give a real-time indicator of how many orders need immediate attention.

## What the In Progress table shows

The In Progress page lists all orders currently assigned to vendors. Each row in the table represents one active order and includes:

- **Created At** — Date and time the order was created.
- **Model** — Device model the customer wants to sell (for example, Apple iPhone 14 Pro Max, Dell Inspiron Series).
- **City** — City where the pickup is scheduled.
- **Type** — Device category such as Mobile, Laptop, Tablet, or Mac.
- **Status** — Current status of the order in the pickup pipeline.
- **SLA** — How late the order is (or how much time is left) and the SLA stage, for example `2d 4h late · PICKUP`.

Click any row to open the order detail drawer. For open orders the drawer offers **Flag this order for escalation**, **Send a reminder to the vendor or customer**, **Reschedule Pickup**, **Reassign Vendor** and **Cancel Order**. See [All Orders → Order detail drawer](/all-orders#order-detail-drawer).

## Order statuses in this view

The Status column uses specific labels to show where an order sits in the pickup pipeline:

- **Assigned** — Order has been assigned to a vendor but pickup has not started yet.
- **Out For Pickup / Inspection Started** — Vendor is on the way or has started the inspection.
- **Pickup Delayed** — Order has passed its scheduled pickup time without completion and requires admin attention.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Pickup Delayed orders are a priority. If a vendor does not complete a pickup within the scheduled window, the admin team should intervene — reassign the order or contact the customer to reschedule.
</Callout>

## Filters and controls

Use filters and controls at the top of the page to narrow the In Progress list to the orders you care about:

- **Device Type Filter** — Filter orders by device category such as Mobile, Laptop, Tablet, or Mac.
- **City Filter** — Filter orders by the city where the pickup is scheduled.
- **From Date / To Date** — Filter orders by their creation date range to view orders placed within a specific period.
- **All Pickup Dates** — Filter orders by scheduled pickup date: All pickup dates, Today, Tomorrow or Day After.
- **Show Orders with Test Accounts** — Include or exclude orders created by test accounts to control whether test data appears in the list.
- **Search** — Use the **Search orders...** box in the header to find a specific order.
- The filter panel opens when you click **Filters** in the header.
- **Reset and Apply Filters** — Click **Apply Filters** to apply the selected filters. Click **Reset** to clear all filters and return to the full In Progress order list.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Enabling Show Orders with Test Accounts is useful during testing or QA but should be disabled during normal operations to avoid cluttering the view with dummy data.
</Callout>

## Order status pipeline

The In Progress view tracks orders through a specific sequence from assignment to completion.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Order created" icon="download">
    Order is created by a customer or converted from a lead in the Orders - B2C module.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order assigned to vendor" icon="users">
    The system assigns the order to a vendor based on matching pincode and device type assignments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order appears in In Progress" icon="monitor">
    The order appears in the In Progress list with status Assigned so admins can start tracking it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vendor initiates pickup" icon="play-circle">
    The vendor contacts the customer and initiates the pickup process; the status updates to Out For Pickup and then Inspection Started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pickup completion or delay" icon="alert-triangle">
    If the vendor completes pickup and inspection on time, the order moves out of In Progress to the next stage. If the vendor does not act within the scheduled pickup window, the status changes to Pickup Delayed and the order requires admin follow-up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Admin responsibilities on this page

Admins use the In Progress view to keep active orders moving toward completion:

- Monitor the In Progress list daily to track active order movement.
- Pay close attention to orders with Pickup Delayed status and follow up with the assigned vendor.
- Use the Delayed sub-section for a dedicated view of all overdue orders.
- Use the Assigned · No Action view to identify orders where vendors have gone silent.
- Use the Live Order Map to see where in-play pickups are and which are late.
- Filter by city to manage regional operations and assign local follow-ups to the relevant team.
- Use the Flagged section to review orders with disputes or quality concerns.
- Check the Not Assigned section to ensure no orders are left unassigned for long.

## Connections to other modules

The In Progress view links to several other modules across the admin panel:

- **Leads** — Converted leads from the New Leads page flow into Orders and appear here once assigned.
- **Vendors - B2C** — Orders are assigned to vendors based on their pincode coverage and device type assignments.
- **Quality - B2C** — Completed orders are reviewed for quality and linked back to vendor performance.
- **All Inventory** — Once a vendor completes a pickup and the order is marked done, the device enters All Inventory.
- **Expansion — Area** — Order assignment depends on the serviceable pincodes configured in the Area module.
