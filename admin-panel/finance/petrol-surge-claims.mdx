## Overview

**Sidebar path:** Finance → Petrol Surge Claims (`/finance/petrol-surge`)

Vendors and cashboys submit photos of their petrol bills as reimbursement claims. On this screen Finance reviews each bill and either **approves** it — setting the reimbursement amount — or **rejects** it.

<Callout kind="info">
This is the live claims-review screen. The rule-based trip bonus described in [Petrol Surge (Proposed)](/petrol-surge---admin-panel) is a separate design that is not built.
</Callout>

## What the screen shows

### Status tabs

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending (n)** | Claims waiting for review (default tab) |
| **Approved (n)** | Claims approved, with the approved amount |
| **Rejected (n)** | Claims rejected |
| **All** | Every claim |

### Claims table

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Bill** | Thumbnail of the uploaded bill photo — click to open the bill details |
| **Claimant** | Vendor or cashboy name, with role (e.g. **Vendor**) and phone |
| **Bill Date/Time** | Date and time printed on / entered for the bill |
| **Bill Amount** | Amount claimed by the vendor |
| **Approved** | Amount approved for reimbursement (— until approved) |
| **Status** | Pending, Approved or Rejected |
| **Submitted** | Date the claim was submitted |
| **Action** | **Approve** / **Reject** buttons on pending claims (— once decided) |

## Actions

### View a bill

Click the bill thumbnail to open **Petrol Bill — {claimant}**. It shows the claimant (role, shop and phone), the bill photo, **Bill date/time**, **Bill amount (vendor)**, **Approved amount** and, for decided claims, the reviewer's **Comment**. Pending claims also show **Approve** and **Reject** buttons here.

### Approve

Click **Approve** (in the row or the bill dialog) to accept the claim and set the reimbursement amount. The approved amount can differ from the bill amount — for example a lower amount if only part of the bill is eligible.

### Reject

Click **Reject** to decline the claim. Add a clear comment (e.g. "bill photo is not clear") so the claimant knows why.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pending" icon="clock" title-type="p">
    Start on the **Pending** tab and work oldest submissions first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the bill" icon="receipt" title-type="p">
    Open the bill photo and check that the date/time and amount are readable and match the claim.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    **Approve** with the correct reimbursement amount, or **Reject** with a reason.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
Watch for unusual bill amounts (very large values or ₹0) — verify these with the claimant before approving.
</Callout>
