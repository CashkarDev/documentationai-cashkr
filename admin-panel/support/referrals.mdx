## Overview

**Sidebar:** B2C Sales → Support → **Referrals** (`/support/referrals`)

"Cases parked with another function. You stay the customer's point of contact while someone else holds the answer."

A case lands here the moment its Case state is set to **Waiting on another team** on the [CX Desk](/admin-panel/support/cx-desk).

<Callout kind="info">

This is not the customer referral programme. For Refer & Earn, see [Cashkr Referral System](/cashkr-referral-system).

</Callout>

## KPI cards

| Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Open | Cases waiting on another team. |
| Past expected by | The other team is late. |
| Customer not updated | We owe the customer a word. |

## Tabs

- **Open** — cases currently parked. Empty state: "Nothing parked with anyone."
- **Closed** — referrals that have been closed.

<Callout kind="tip">

The default time by which the customer must be updated on a parked case is set on [CX Settings](/admin-panel/support/cx-settings) (Customer update window).

</Callout>
