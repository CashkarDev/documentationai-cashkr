## Overview

Update your basic account information from the My Profile section while keeping the registered phone number locked for security.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step title="Open profile page" icon="user" titleType="h3">

Go to the **My Profile** section of the Cashkr website to access your account details.

</Step>

<Step title="Edit name and email" icon="edit" titleType="h3">

Update the **Name** and **Email** fields as needed, then save your changes.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Field behavior

- **Name**: editable
- **Email**: editable
- **Phone number**: read-only (locked, not editable)
