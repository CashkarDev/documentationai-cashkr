## Overview

The Cashkr Users page under Data → Users lists every customer registered via the Cashkr website or customer app. Admins use this page to review customer history, monitor referrals and reviews, and block or unblock accounts when needed.

<Callout kind="info">

Use the Cashkr Users page to search for any customer, open their full profile and journey, review feedback and referral activity, and take account actions such as blocking or unblocking.

</Callout>

## Summary cards

Summary cards at the top give a quick view of the overall customer base:

- **Total Users** — Count of all customers registered on the Cashkr platform.
- **Active Users** — Count of customers whose accounts are currently active.
- **Blocked Users** — Count of customers whose accounts have been blocked.

Use these counts to monitor overall growth and the number of customers currently restricted.

## All users list

The All Users table lists every registered customer. Each row represents one customer and includes:

- **Name** — Full name of the customer.
- **Contact** — Registered phone number (with quick call option) and email address.
- **City** — City from the customer's saved address.
- **Orders** — Total number of orders the customer has placed.
- **Rating** — Average rating the customer has given across their completed orders.
- **Status** — Current account status, either Active or Blocked.

Use this list to scan customer activity at a glance and to open the detail panel for deeper investigation.

## Search and refresh

Search and refresh controls help you quickly find the right customer and ensure you are working with current data:

- **Search** — Enter a name, phone number, or email address to find a specific customer, then click Search to apply.
- **Refresh** — Click the refresh button to reload the latest user data and table state.

Search is especially useful when resolving complaints, disputes, or fraud alerts tied to a specific customer.

## Customer detail panel

Click any customer row in the table to open a detail panel on the right. The panel gives a complete view of that customer's relationship with Cashkr across three tabs: Details, Journey, and Reviews.

The panel header shows:

- Customer name.
- Email address.
- Phone number.
- Current status (Active or Blocked).
- Member since date.
- A **Block** toggle that you use to block or unblock the account.

## Details tab

The Details tab combines profile information with key performance and activity metrics for the selected customer.

**Contact information**

- Phone number with a quick call option.
- Email address.
- Full address including city and pincode.

**Performance summary**

- **Avg Rating** — Average rating the customer has given across all completed orders.
- **Total Sale Value** — Total INR value of all devices the customer has sold through Cashkr.

**Order activity**

- **Total** — Total number of orders placed by the customer.
- **Completed** — Number of successfully completed orders.
- **In Progress** — Number of orders currently active and in the pipeline.
- **Failed** — Number of orders marked as failed.

**Referrals**

- **Total** — Total number of referrals made by this customer.
- **Completed** — Number of referrals that resulted in a completed order.
- **Value** — Total INR value of rewards earned through referrals.

Use this tab before taking any action to understand how important the customer is to the platform and how they have behaved over time.

## Journey tab

The Journey tab shows a chronological timeline of the customer's activity on the platform, including:

- Order creation and status changes.
- Key milestones in the customer's lifecycle.
- Other notable events in their engagement history.

Review the journey to understand context behind complaints, disputes, or suspicious patterns before deciding whether to block or escalate.

## Reviews tab

The Reviews tab lists all reviews and ratings the customer has submitted for their completed orders. Use this view to:

- Gauge the customer's satisfaction levels over time.
- Identify patterns in their feedback (for example, repeated complaints about a vendor or a process).
- Cross-check reviews with order and journey data when resolving issues.

## Block and unblock workflow

Use the Block toggle in the customer detail panel to control login access for that customer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer profile" icon="user">

  - From the Cashkr Users table, click the customer row you want to review.
  - Confirm you have the right customer by checking their name, contact details, and member since date in the panel header.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Review history before blocking" icon="search">

  - In the **Details** tab, review order activity, ratings, total sale value, and referral data.
  - In the **Journey** tab, scan the timeline for recent issues, suspicious behavior, or escalations.
  - In the **Reviews** tab, check their feedback history and tone.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Block the customer" icon="ban">

  - In the panel header, turn on the **Block** toggle.
  - Confirm the block action when prompted.
  - The customer's status switches to Blocked and they lose access to the website and app immediately.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Unblock the customer" icon="toggle-right">

  - Reopen the same customer from the table or search results.
  - Turn off the **Block** toggle in the header.
  - The customer's status returns to Active and they can log in again right away.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">

Blocking is immediate and ends all active sessions. The customer is logged out of the website and app and typically does not receive any automatic notification, so ensure you are confident before applying a block.

</Callout>

## When to block a customer

Block a customer when their behavior or a confirmed request requires restricting access to Cashkr:

- **Fraud or fake activity** — Placing fake orders, misrepresenting devices, or otherwise misusing the platform.
- **Coupon and referral abuse** — Repeatedly exploiting coupons or referral programs beyond platform rules.
- **Bad faith interactions** — Persistent abusive, dishonest, or disruptive behavior with vendors or operations.
- **Suspicious behavior flags** — Cases flagged by the Fraud and Safety module that merit a temporary or permanent block.
- **Customer request** — Customer explicitly asking to permanently deactivate or freeze their Cashkr account.

Document the reason clearly so support and operations teams can justify the decision if the customer contacts them later.

## Admin responsibilities

Admins managing the Cashkr Users page are responsible for using the data available to make fair, informed decisions:

- Use search to quickly find the right customer when handling complaints, disputes, or alerts.
- Review the **Details** tab before blocking to understand order history, value, and referral impact.
- Check the **Journey** tab for full context and timing around issues or suspicious events.
- Block customers promptly when fraudulent, abusive, or clearly non-compliant behavior is confirmed.
- Unblock customers as soon as you confirm that a block was applied in error or the issue is resolved.
- Monitor the **Blocked Users** count regularly and periodically review blocked accounts to ensure each block is still justified.

## Connections to other modules

Information on the Cashkr Users page is tightly linked with other admin modules:

- **Orders - B2C** — Orders shown in the Details tab link to full order records in the Orders - B2C section.
- **Referrals** — Referral metrics in the Details tab come from the Referrals module.
- **Fraud and Safety** — Customers flagged for suspicious behavior may require blocks initiated from this page.
- **Coupons** — Investigate coupon misuse by cross-checking orders and coupon usage history here.
- **Platform Reviews** — Reviews and ratings in the Reviews tab are also managed in the Platform Reviews section.

Use these connections to dig deeper into specific issues before blocking or unblocking an account.

## Important notes

<Callout kind="alert">

Blocking a customer is immediate, terminates all active sessions, and does not send an automatic notification by default. Once blocked, the customer cannot log in or place orders until you manually unblock them.

</Callout>

Keep the following in mind when managing blocked accounts:

- Blocking takes effect the moment you confirm the action; there is no grace period.
- Blocked customers cannot place new orders, view their order history, or access referrals in the website or app.
- Review any orders already in progress at the time of blocking separately to decide how operations should handle them.
- Always record the reason for blocking in your internal system before or immediately after applying the block.
- Blocking does not delete the customer's account or data. All profiles, orders, and history remain visible in the admin panel for future reference.
