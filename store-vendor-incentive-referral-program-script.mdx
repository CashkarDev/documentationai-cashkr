## Purpose

Equip onboarding associates to confidently explain incentive, bonus, and referral structures to B2Ch (Store) Vendors so vendors stay target-driven, compliant, and aligned with Cashkr’s long-term growth model.

---

## Key compliance notes

<Callout kind="alert">

- Incentives and bonuses follow approved policy and system rules. Do not promise anything outside the documented program.
- All incentive payouts credit into the **vendor wallet balance** in the Vendor / Store app. The wallet is used for **customer payouts via OTP**, not for buying or unlocking credits.
- **Credits and wallet are separate**. Credits are virtual commission points for unlocking orders and are not cash. Do not say that wallet money converts into credits or that credits can be withdrawn.
- Never suggest off-system transfers, cash adjustments, or manual payouts. Always follow the in-app OTP and payment flow.
- When in doubt, refer to [Understanding Vendor Credits and Wallet System](/understanding-vendor-credits-and-wallet-system), [Vendor Credits, Refunds & Reversals Explanation](/vendor-credits-refunds-reversals-explanation), and [Pickup Communication Best Practices](/pickup-communication-best-practices).

</Callout>

## Program overview

Store vendor incentives reward:

- Consistent order volume  
- Strong SLA and reporting discipline  
- Long-term loyalty and compliance  
- Referrals that bring reliable new partners  
- Promotion of voucher-based transactions  
- Top performance at a city level

All incentives are **system-calculated** and **auto-credited** into the vendor wallet when criteria are met. No manual application is required.

Payout timing:

- **Performance bonuses:** By the **10th of the next month**  
- **Referral bonuses:** Within **7 days** after referred vendor completes the milestone  
- **Loyalty and leaderboard bonuses:** **Quarterly**, at month-end for that quarter

## Standard associate call flow

Use this flow whenever you introduce incentives to a store vendor.

<Steps>

<Step title="Introduce yourself and context" icon="phone">

- Greet the vendor and confirm you are speaking to the decision-maker.
- Set context: onboarding / performance review / incentive briefing.
- Example:  
  “Main Cashkr se bol raha hoon, aaj aapko store vendor incentives aur referral benefits explain karunga, taaki aapko pata ho ki extra income kahan se aa sakti hai.”

</Step>

<Step title="Set expectations" icon="info">

- Mention that incentives are **automatic and policy-based**.
- Clarify that all payouts go to the **vendor wallet** and are visible in the app.
- Example:  
  “Aapko alag se apply nahi karna hota, system aapke orders aur SLA dekh ke incentives auto-credit karta hai wallet me.”

</Step>

<Step title="Explain incentive types" icon="list">

- Briefly list all active categories:
  - Performance incentives (weekly / monthly)
  - Loyalty incentives (quarterly)
  - Referral incentives (one-time per referral)
  - Voucher promotion bonus (weekly)
  - City leaderboard bonus (monthly / quarterly payout)
- Tell the vendor you will explain each in simple language, focusing on how they can earn more.

</Step>

<Step title="Explain referral and growth angle" icon="users">

- Emphasize that referrals give **extra income without extra marketing cost**.
- Explain the referral flow and ₹1,000 wallet credit for each successful referral.
- Reinforce that referrals must follow proper onboarding and order completion.

</Step>

<Step title="Set next steps and motivate" icon="check-circle">

- Confirm the vendor has understood the key targets (orders, SLA, reporting, referrals).
- Set a simple target for the coming month (e.g., 50+ orders and 90%+ SLA).
- Close with motivation and a reminder that performance is rewarded and visible on the leaderboard.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Incentive structures (internal reference + scripts)

Use these expandables as your internal cheat sheet during training and refreshers.

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Performance incentives (weekly / monthly)" default-open="true">

**What it is**

Performance incentives are **short-term bonuses** based on a vendor’s completed store order volume, SLA score, and reporting discipline. They are calculated weekly / monthly and credited at month-end.

**Eligibility (as per current draft)**

- **Order volume bonus**

  - Threshold: **50+ completed store orders in a month**  
  - Bonus: **₹500 flat**  
  - Example: Vendor completes **60** store orders in a month → eligible for ₹500 bonus.

- **SLA score bonus**

  - Threshold: **SLA score above 90%**  
  - Bonus: **₹1,000**  
  - Example: Vendor maintains punctual updates and strong SLA metrics.

- **Zero missed reports bonus**

  - Threshold: **Zero missed reports for 4 weeks in a row**  
  - Bonus: **₹750**  
  - Example: Vendor submits daily / weekly reports consistently.

- **Payout timeline**

  - All performance bonuses are **credited to the vendor wallet by the 10th of the next month**.

**How to explain to vendor (script)**

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English" icon="book-open">

“On top of your normal earnings, we give extra bonuses for performance.

If you complete **50 or more store orders in a month**, you get a **₹500 flat bonus**.

If your **SLA score stays above 90%**, you get an **extra ₹1,000 bonus**.

And if you maintain **zero missed reports for 4 weeks continuously**, you get an **additional ₹750 bonus**.

All these incentives are **auto-calculated by the system** and **credited into your wallet by the 10th of the next month**. You don’t need to apply; the app tracks your orders and SLA.”

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish" icon="users">

“Aapki regular earning ke upar hum **performance ke basis par extra bonus** dete hain.

Agar aap **ek month me 50 ya usse zyada store orders complete karte ho**, to aapko **₹500 ka flat bonus** milta hai.

Agar aapka **SLA score 90% se upar rehta hai**, to aapko **₹1,000 ka extra bonus** milta hai.

Aur agar aap **4 hafton tak ek bhi report miss nahi karte**, proper reporting karte ho, to aapko **₹750 ka extra bonus** milta hai.

Yeh saare incentives **system auto-calculate karta hai** aur **next month ki 10 tareekh tak aapke wallet me credit ho jate hain**. Aapko alag se request nahi daalni hoti.”

</Tab>

</Tabs>

**What to avoid saying**

- Do **not** promise performance incentives for partial completion (e.g., 30–40 orders) unless policy is updated.
- Do not say incentives are “guaranteed” — link them to **actual SLA and reporting**.
- Do not say vendors can **withdraw bonuses as cash without following the OTP payout flow**.
- Do not connect performance incentives to **credits** or unlocking; they are **wallet-only payouts**.

**Where vendor can see it**

- Vendor / Store app → **Wallet** → **Incentive History** (or similar section under wallet where incentives appear as separate entries).
- Performance metrics (orders, SLA, reporting) are visible in the vendor’s **app dashboard**.

**Internal notes**

- Use examples from the vendor’s recent performance during calls (e.g., “Last month you did 48 orders; if you push to 50+, you unlock ₹500 next month.”).
- Cross-check current SLA definitions and reporting rules with:
  - **Store Vendor SLA & Violation Policy Script**
  - **Store Vendor Reporting & SLA Compliance Script**
- If rules are updated, update this script and re-brief the team.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Loyalty incentives (quarterly)">

**What it is**

Loyalty incentives reward **long-term, compliant vendors** who maintain high SLA and zero violations over multiple months. These are **quarterly bonuses** on top of monthly performance payouts.

**Eligibility (as per current draft)**

- Maintain **90%+ SLA** and **zero violations** for **3 consecutive months**.
- Loyalty bonus levels:
  - **₹1,500** for consistent performers meeting the above criteria.
  - **₹2,500** for the **top 10 vendors in the city** based on loyalty and performance metrics.
- **Loyalty scores** are generated automatically from the app’s performance dashboard.
- **Payout timeline:** Loyalty bonuses are **credited quarterly at month-end** for that quarter.

**How to explain to vendor (script)**

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English" icon="book-open">

“We also reward long-term consistency.

If you maintain **90% or higher SLA** and **no violations for 3 continuous months**, you automatically qualify for a **Loyalty Bonus**.

Usually, consistent performers get around **₹1,500**, and the **top 10 vendors in the city** can get up to **₹2,500** as a quarterly bonus.

All this is based on your performance data in the app. You don’t need to apply — the system calculates it and credits the bonus into your wallet at the **end of the quarter**.”

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish" icon="users">

“Hum sirf short-term nahi, **long-term consistent vendors** ko bhi reward karte hain.

Agar aap **3 mahine lagataar 90% se upar SLA rakhte ho** aur **koi violation nahi hota**, to aap **Loyalty Bonus ke liye auto-qualify** ho jaate ho.

Generally, jo vendors consistently acha kaam karte hain unko **₹1,500 tak ka bonus** milta hai, aur **city ke top 10 vendors** ko **₹2,500 tak ka quarterly bonus** mil sakta hai.

Yeh sab aapke app ke performance data se calculate hota hai, aapko kuch apply nahi karna, **quarter ke end par bonus aapke wallet me credit ho jata hai**.”

</Tab>

</Tabs>

**What to avoid saying**

- Do not guarantee that **every vendor** with 90% SLA will get **₹2,500** — that is only for **top 10 in city**.
- Do not promise that loyalty rules will **never change**. Say “as per current policy”.
- Do not say loyalty incentives are paid **monthly**; they are **quarterly**.

**Where vendor can see it**

- Loyalty bonus entries appear in the **wallet incentive history** when credited.
- SLA and violation status are visible under the **SLA / performance section** of the Vendor / Store app.

**Internal notes**

- Use loyalty incentives to **retain high-performing vendors** and push them to avoid violations.
- If a vendor is close (e.g., 2 clean months), highlight what they need to maintain in month 3.
- Align language with **Vendor SLA & Violation Policy Script** for what counts as a violation.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Referral incentives">

**What it is**

Referral incentives reward store vendors who help Cashkr **expand the service network** by referring reliable new service vendors or area partners.

**Eligibility (as per current draft)**

- The existing vendor shares a **referral contact** with the onboarding associate.
- The referred person:
  - Is **successfully onboarded** as a vendor / partner, and  
  - **Completes 10 verified store orders**.
- Once the milestone is reached:
  - The **referring vendor gets ₹1,000 referral credit in their wallet**.
- **No limit** on number of referrals — each successful referral can earn a separate ₹1,000.
- **Payout timeline:** Referral bonus is **credited within 7 days** of the referred vendor completing 10 verified orders.

**How to explain to vendor (script)**

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English" icon="book-open">

“We also have a **Refer & Earn** model for store vendors.

If you refer a **reliable service vendor or area partner**, and we successfully onboard them, and they complete **10 verified store orders**, you get a **₹1,000 referral bonus** in your wallet.

There is **no limit** — if you refer more good partners who complete 10 orders, you keep earning **₹1,000 per successful referral**.

Once your referred vendor completes the 10-order milestone, the system credits your bonus into your wallet **within 7 days**.”

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish" icon="users">

“Store vendors ke liye **Refer & Earn** option bhi hai.

Agar aap koi **achha service vendor ya area partner refer karte ho**, hum usko onboard karte hain, aur wo **10 verified store orders complete kar leta hai**, to aapko **₹1,000 ka referral bonus** aapke wallet me milta hai.

Isme **koi limit nahi hai** — jitne ache log refer karoge, aur jo 10 orders complete karenge, **har ek successful referral par aapko ₹1,000** mil sakta hai.

Jaise hi aapka referred vendor 10 orders complete karta hai, **7 din ke andar andar aapka referral bonus wallet me credit ho jata hai**.”

</Tab>

</Tabs>

**What to avoid saying**

- Do not say referrals are paid **immediately** on onboarding; the condition is **10 verified store orders**.
- Do not allow or suggest **fake referrals** or self-referrals to game the system.
- Do not suggest any **cash-under-the-table** or offline arrangements — everything must pass through official onboarding and wallet crediting.
- Do not promise that rejection of a referral can be overridden manually.

**Where vendor can see it**

- Referral bonus entries show in **Wallet → Incentive / Referral history**.
- Many vendors will also see referral communication in **city WhatsApp groups** or training materials, but the **source of truth is the app wallet history**.

**Internal notes**

- Tag referrals properly in CRM / onboarding tools so the correct referrer gets credit.
- Coordinate with the **Channel Team** to track referred vendors and confirm when the 10-order milestone is crossed.
- If there is a dispute, cross-check with:
  - [Vendor Credits, Refunds & Reversals Explanation](/vendor-credits-refunds-reversals-explanation)  
  - Relevant onboarding logs and order data.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Voucher promotion bonus">

**What it is**

Voucher promotion incentives reward vendors who push **voucher-based payouts** (store vouchers) instead of only cash. This helps improve **store loyalty** and overall vendor share of orders.

**Eligibility (as per current draft)**

- Vendor promotes **voucher-based transactions** where customers take **store vouchers instead of cash**.
- If the vendor reaches **10 voucher transactions per week**, they qualify for an **extra ₹500 weekly bonus**.
- This is a **weekly incentive**, on top of normal earnings.

**How to explain to vendor (script)**

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English" icon="book-open">

“We also give a bonus to vendors who promote **voucher payouts**.

Whenever a customer chooses to take a **store voucher instead of cash**, it counts as a **voucher transaction**.

If you do **10 or more voucher-based transactions in a week**, you get an **extra ₹500 bonus** for that week, on top of your regular earnings.

This encourages more customers to come back to your store and increases your overall order share.”

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish" icon="users">

“Hum un vendors ko bhi bonus dete hain jo **voucher payouts** promote karte hain.

Jab customer **cash ki jagah store voucher leta hai**, wo **voucher transaction** count hota hai.

Agar aap ek hafte me **10 ya usse zyada voucher transactions** karwate ho, to aapko **us week ke liye ₹500 ka extra bonus** milta hai, jo aapki normal earning ke upar hota hai.

Isse customer baar-baar aapke store par aata hai aur aapka **order share badhta hai**.”

</Tab>

</Tabs>

**What to avoid saying**

- Do not force or pressure customers into vouchers against policy; only **offer and explain** the option.
- Do not say voucher bonuses are **guaranteed** if tracking data is incomplete; stay aligned with system-recorded transactions.
- Do not link voucher bonus to **credits**; it is a **wallet incentive**.

**Where vendor can see it**

- Voucher-related incentives show as separate line items in the **wallet incentive history**.
- Voucher transactions themselves appear in **order history** or relevant transaction views.

**Internal notes**

- Align messaging with **Store Vendor Payment & Pickup Process Script** so that voucher explanations to customers stay compliant.
- Remind associates to never promise specific voucher denominations beyond what the app / system offers.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="City leaderboard program">

**What it is**

The city leaderboard ranks vendors based on multiple quality and performance metrics and rewards the **top performers** with recognition and quarterly bonus payouts.

**Eligibility (as per current draft)**

- Each city has a **Vendor Leaderboard** visible to internal teams.
- Vendors are ranked based on:
  - Order completion count  
  - SLA score  
  - Reporting consistency  
  - Customer satisfaction rating (CSAT)
- **Top 3 vendors each month** receive:
  - Special recognition in the **internal Ops bulletin**, and  
  - A **bonus payout of ₹1,000–₹3,000**.
- **Payout timeline:** Leaderboard bonuses are credited **quarterly at month-end** along with other quarterly incentives.

**How to explain to vendor (script)**

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English" icon="book-open">

“In every city, we maintain a **Vendor Leaderboard**.

We rank vendors based on **order completion, SLA score, reporting discipline, and customer satisfaction**.

Every month, the **top 3 vendors** in the city get special recognition in our internal Ops bulletin and a **bonus between ₹1,000 and ₹3,000**.

This is our way of highlighting and rewarding the best-performing partners in each city and encouraging healthy competition.”

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish" icon="users">

“Har city me hum ek **Vendor Leaderboard** maintain karte hain.

Isme vendors ko **order completion, SLA score, reporting consistency aur customer satisfaction** ke basis par rank kiya jata hai.

Har month **city ke top 3 vendors** ko hum **Ops bulletin me highlight karte hain** aur unko **₹1,000 se ₹3,000 tak ka bonus** mil sakta hai.

Isse healthy competition banta hai aur achha kaam karne wale vendors ko proper recognition milta hai.”

</Tab>

</Tabs>

**What to avoid saying**

- Do not promise a fixed **₹3,000** to every top vendor; the range is **₹1,000–₹3,000**.
- Do not share internal ranking details or data beyond what policy allows.
- Do not say that leaderboard rank can be **manually adjusted**; it is based on system metrics.

**Where vendor can see it**

- Leaderboard may not always be vendor-facing; recognition is:
  - Communicated via **Ops calls / reviews**, and  
  - Reflected in **wallet incentive entries** when the bonus is credited.

**Internal notes**

- Use leaderboard examples during reviews to motivate vendors:
  - “You are currently in top 10; with better SLA and reporting, you can reach top 3.”
- Cross-check CSAT and SLA data with **Order Tracking & Customer Notifications** and **Pickup Communication Best Practices** to coach vendors on improvement.

## Payout cycle and wallet vs credits (how to explain safely)

When vendors ask **how and when they get the money**, keep the explanation consistent and policy-aligned.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English" icon="book-open">

“All your **incentives and bonuses** are added to your **vendor wallet** in the app.

- **Performance bonuses** are credited by the **10th of the next month** after we close the month.
- **Referral bonuses** are credited **within 7 days** after your referred vendor hits **10 verified store orders**.
- **Loyalty and leaderboard bonuses** are credited **quarterly at month-end**.

From the wallet, payouts to customers always go through the **OTP payment flow**. The wallet is for managing payouts, and is **separate from credits**.

**Credits are different** — they are like **points** used to unlock orders in the Vendor App. Credits are **not cash** and **cannot be withdrawn or transferred**.”

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish" icon="users">

“Jo bhi aapke **incentives aur bonuses** hain, sabse pehle **aapke vendor wallet me credit hote hain**.

- **Performance bonus** hum **next month ki 10 tareekh tak** credit kar dete hain.
- **Referral bonus** aapko tab milta hai jab aapka referred vendor **10 verified store orders complete** kar leta hai — ye bonus **7 din ke andar** wallet me aa jata hai.
- **Loyalty aur leaderboard bonuses** hum **quarter ke end par** wallet me credit karte hain.

Wallet se aap customers ko payment **OTP flow ke through** hi kar sakte ho. Wallet **credits se alag hai**.

**Credits alag cheez hain** — ye **points** hote hain jo aap orders unlock karne ke liye use karte ho. Credits **cash nahi hote**, na hi withdraw ya transfer ho sakte hain.”

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Common vendor queries (Q&A for associates)

Use these as quick responses during calls. Always keep answers aligned with system behavior.

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Do I need to apply for incentives or bonuses?">

**Recommended response (English)**

“No, you don’t need to apply. The system automatically calculates your incentives based on your **orders, SLA, reporting, and referrals**. Once you meet the criteria, the bonus is auto-credited into your **wallet** as per the payout cycle.”

**Recommended response (Hinglish)**

“**Nahi, aapko alag se apply nahi karna hota.** System aapke **orders, SLA, reporting aur referrals** ke basis par incentives khud calculate karta hai. Jaise hi aap criteria complete karte ho, bonus **payout cycle ke hisaab se aapke wallet me auto-credit ho jata hai**.”

**Internal note**

- If the vendor claims a missing incentive, first **verify data and criteria** before escalating.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Where can I check my incentive and bonus history?">

**Recommended response (English)**

“You can check all your incentives and bonuses inside the Vendor / Store app in the **Wallet section**, under **Incentive or Bonus History**. Each entry will show the **amount, date, and type of incentive**.”

**Recommended response (Hinglish)**

“**Aap apne saare incentives aur bonuses Vendor / Store app ke Wallet section me dekh sakte ho**, waha **Incentive ya Bonus History** me saari entries dikhengi. Waha aapko **amount, date aur kaunsa incentive hai** sab mention hota hai.”

**Internal note**

- If the UI label changes, update the wordings in this script and re-brief associates.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="If a referred vendor quits early, do I lose my bonus?">

**Recommended response (English)**

“Yes. For referral incentives, we count only **active, verified vendors** who complete **10 store orders**. If the referred vendor quits or stops before reaching 10 verified orders, the referral bonus is **not applicable**.”

**Recommended response (Hinglish)**

“**Haan. Referral bonus tabhi milta hai jab aapka referred vendor active, verified ho** aur **10 store orders complete kare**. Agar wo beech me quit kar deta hai ya 10 verified orders complete nahi karta, to **referral bonus applicable nahi hota**.”

**Internal note**

- Do not promise exceptions. Escalate only if there is a **data mismatch**, not because the vendor is upset.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I transfer my incentive balance or use it as credits?">

**Recommended response (English)**

“No. **Wallet balance is non-transferable** and is used only for payouts through the official OTP flow. It cannot be transferred to another person or converted directly into credits.

Also, **credits are different from wallet money**. Credits are internal points used to unlock orders, and they are **not cash and not withdrawable**.”

**Recommended response (Hinglish)**

“**Nahi. Aapka wallet balance non-transferable hai.** Ye sirf system ke through, **OTP flow se payout ke liye use hota hai**. Aap isko kisi aur ke account me transfer nahi kar sakte, na hi isko directly credits me convert kar sakte ho.

**Credits alag cheez hai** — ye **internal points hote hain jo orders unlock karne ke liye use hote hain**, ye **cash nahi hote aur withdraw bhi nahi hote**.”

**Internal note**

- If vendors ask for exceptions or manual transfers, politely decline and refer to:
  - [Understanding Vendor Credits and Wallet System](/understanding-vendor-credits-and-wallet-system)  
  - [Vendor Credits, Refunds & Reversals Explanation](/vendor-credits-refunds-reversals-explanation)

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I get incentives even if my SLA or reporting is weak?">

**Recommended response (English)**

“Incentives are linked to **performance, SLA, and reporting discipline**. If your SLA score is low or reports are frequently missed, your incentives will be affected. To earn bonuses, you need to **improve SLA, complete more orders, and maintain consistent reporting**.”

**Recommended response (Hinglish)**

“Incentives **performance, SLA aur reporting discipline** se linked hote hain. Agar aapka SLA low hai ya aap bar-bar reporting miss karte ho, to **incentives kam ya zero ho sakte hain**. Bonus lene ke liye aapko **SLA improve karna, zyada orders complete karna aur reporting regular rakhni padegi**.”

**Internal note**

- Use this as an opportunity to cross-sell:
  - **Store Vendor Reporting & SLA Compliance Script**
  - [Pickup Communication Best Practices](/pickup-communication-best-practices)

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>
