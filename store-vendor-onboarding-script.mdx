## Overview

Used by the Store Partnership and Channel Expansion team, this script helps Cashkr Onboarding Associates explain, verify, and onboard B2Ch store service vendors who will manage in-store inspections and payments for partner stores.

<Callout kind="info">

Store service vendors handle **store-based orders only** (no home pickups), manage **in-person inspections and customer interactions at partner stores**, and make **instant customer payouts via the Cashkr Wallet**.

</Callout>

### Objective

- Explain the B2Ch store-based vendor role clearly and consistently.
- Verify that vendors meet minimum requirements for in-store servicing.
- Align expectations on wallet usage, credits, SLAs, and activation timelines.

## Training script for onboarding associates

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="1. Introducing the role">

**Associate script**

"Hello, I am from Cashkr, India&apos;s trusted platform for selling old devices.

We are expanding our Store Service Vendor Network — verified partners who handle all inspection and payment activities for Cashkr channel stores.

Your role will not involve visiting customers&apos; homes like in B2C. Instead, you will manage store-based orders throughout the day at fixed store locations."

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="2. When vendor asks: What exactly will I have to do?">

**Associate response**

"You will manage all store-based customer orders in your assigned area. Your daily responsibilities include:"

- Having yourself or your associate present at the partner store during working hours.
- Inspecting devices brought by walk-in customers (phones, laptops, tablets, and similar devices).
- Verifying IMEI, serial number, and device condition based on the Cashkr App flow.
- Providing the final offer price to customers after inspection.
- Completing payment instantly from your Cashkr Wallet when the customer accepts the offer.
- Collecting all confirmed devices from the store at the end of the day for handover or central dispatch.

"So you will manage steady daily volume at fixed store locations, not travel for pickups."

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="3. When vendor asks: How is this different from normal pickup work?">

**Associate response**

"In the B2C model, vendors get single-customer orders from the app and travel for every pickup.

In this B2Ch model, you are linked with specific stores. They generate multiple customer leads daily, and you handle all of them directly from one point.

It is more stable, organized, and higher in volume compared to individual customer pickups."

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="4. Explaining requirements for onboarding">

**Associate response**

"To activate your vendor account, we need the following:"

- Aadhaar Card or PAN Card for KYC.
- Bank account or UPI for Wallet setup.
- Active smartphone with the Cashkr Vendor App installed.
- Sufficient Wallet balance to make same-day payouts to customers.
- Availability of a representative if you cannot personally stay at the store full-time.

"Once we verify these, we will map your profile to specific stores in your operational area."

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="5. When vendor asks: How do I get orders from stores?">

**Associate response**

"You do not need to unlock or accept leads manually.

Orders created by store staff will automatically route to you or your assigned associate based on store mapping.

You will receive instant notifications in the Cashkr Vendor App whenever a new order is created in your store.

You then inspect the device, confirm pricing, and proceed with payment, with all actions recorded in the app."

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="6. Explaining earnings and commission">

**Associate response**

"You earn a per-device commission for every completed store order.

The commission depends on device category — phone, laptop, tablet, or Mac.

We credit commissions to your Wallet weekly, and consistent performance unlocks monthly incentive tiers.

Because stores generate daily volume, your earnings stay more stable even when B2C demand fluctuates."

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="7. When vendor asks: How do I use the app during order handling?">

**Associate step-by-step guide**

"Here is how you use the Cashkr Vendor App in-store:"

<Steps>

<Step title="Store staff creates the order" icon="terminal">

The store staff creates a customer order in the Cashkr Store App for the walk-in customer and their device.

</Step>

<Step title="Order appears in Vendor App" icon="monitor">

You receive the order instantly in your Cashkr Vendor App as a new in-store order notification.

</Step>

<Step title="Verify details and inspect device" icon="search">

Open the order, then verify the device model, condition, and IMEI or serial number as guided by the app.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm final price" icon="check-circle">

After completing the inspection, confirm the final offer price in the Vendor App and discuss it with the customer.

</Step>

<Step title="Complete order and pay via Wallet" icon="credit-card">

When the customer agrees, tap `Mark as Completed` in the app and pay the customer instantly from your Cashkr Wallet.

</Step>

<Step title="Secure device at the store" icon="shield">

Keep the purchased device securely at the store for the rest of the day, following store safety norms.

</Step>

<Step title="End-of-day collection and dispatch" icon="package">

At day end, you or your associate collect all paid devices from the store and prepare them for dispatch or central delivery as per the operations plan.

</Step>

</Steps>

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="8. Explaining Wallet and credit usage">

**Associate response**

"The Wallet in your app is used to make customer payments and should always have enough balance to cover the day&apos;s transactions.

Credits are used to unlock or maintain your active vendor status. They act as your working commission deposit.

You do not need to unlock store orders, but your account must have active credits to stay visible for store allocations."

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="9. Explaining SLA and performance expectations">

**Associate response**

"Because you represent Cashkr directly at partner stores, maintaining performance and professionalism is very important.

Your SLAs include:"

- Staying available or having an associate present at the store during working hours.
- Ensuring all customers are attended promptly.
- Completing all same-day payments.
- Collecting all devices before store closure.
- Uploading proper device photos and order updates in real time.

"Repeatedly missing these may lead to lead pause or a performance review."

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="10. Final confirmation before activation">

**Associate script**

"Before we activate your profile, let us confirm once more:"

- You or your representative will be available at the assigned store daily.
- You understand that all payments are made through your Wallet.
- You will maintain daily end-of-day collection and payment updates.

"Once verification is complete, you will start receiving store orders automatically within 24 hours."

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="11. After successful onboarding">

**Associate script**

"Congratulations, you are now officially onboarded as a Store Service Vendor (B2Ch) with Cashkr.

You will soon receive:"

- Your Vendor App login credentials.
- A short training video on store order handling.
- Details of your assigned stores and store contact persons.

"Keep your Wallet active and stay in touch with the Ops team for daily updates and performance tracking."

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Key tone and communication phrases

- "You will handle store-based orders — no need to travel for pickups."
- "Either you or your associate must be present at the store during the day."
- "You will make customer payments directly from your Wallet once the order is confirmed."
- "All devices are collected at day end — your daily routine stays fixed."
- "This role gives you steady order volume and regular earnings."
