## Overview

Categories organize Cashkr web articles so editors and visitors can browse content by topic. Updates you make here control how articles appear and can be filtered in the website article section.

## What it shows

The Categories collection type in the Content Manager shows:

- **Categories list** with each category name and its associated content.
- **Search and filter controls** for finding categories by name or other filters.
- **Create new entry** action for adding new categories.
- **Edit and delete actions** for updating category details or removing categories.

## Common tasks

Use these steps for typical category management actions.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Categories in Content Manager">

Open the Content Manager and select the `Categories` collection type to see the full list of categories.

</Step>
<Step title="Search or filter categories by name">

Use the search box or filter controls to find categories by name or other available filters.

</Step>
<Step title="Create a new category">

Click `Create new entry` to add a new category, then fill in the required details and save.

</Step>
<Step title="Edit category details">

Select a category from the list, update its details as needed, and save your changes.

</Step>
<Step title="Delete a category">

Choose a category to remove, click the delete action, and confirm the removal to delete it.

</Step>
</Steps>

## System logic

Category changes apply directly to how articles are grouped and filtered.

<Callout kind="info">

Category updates reflect in article filtering immediately, and deleted categories are removed from articles.

</Callout>

## Connections

Categories are integrated with the website article section so visitors can browse and filter articles by category.

They are connected to the Content Manager for article categorization, and any category changes you make here are reflected in the website article section.
