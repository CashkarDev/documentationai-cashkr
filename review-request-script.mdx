---

## Overview

- **Team:** B2C Sales and CX
- **Goal:** Ask satisfied customers to leave a quick review after successful order completion.
- **Tone:** Warm, thankful, short, and human — not corporate.

<Callout kind="tip">

Use this script only when the customer confirms a good experience, keep your ask very short, and send the review link immediately after the call via WhatsApp or SMS.

</Callout>

## Review request scripts

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English" icon="book-open">

<Steps>

<Step title="Opening" icon="play-circle">

“Hi \[Customer Name], this is \[Your Name] from Cashkr. Thank you for selling your \[Device Model] with us. I hope everything went smoothly with your order.”

</Step>

<Step title="If customer sounds happy" icon="help-circle">

“That is wonderful to hear. It would mean a lot if you could share your feedback on the Cashkr app or on Google, because it helps other customers trust our service as well.”

</Step>

<Step title="Closing" icon="check-circle">

“I will send you the review link right after this call. It takes only about 10 seconds to rate us. Thank you so much for choosing Cashkr.”

</Step>

</Steps>

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish" icon="book-open">

<Steps>

<Step title="Opening" icon="play-circle">

“Hello \[Name], main \[Agent Name] bol raha/rahi hoon Cashkr se. Aapne apna \[Phone Model] hamare platform par becha tha. Ummeed hai ki aapka pura process smooth raha?”

</Step>

<Step title="If customer sounds happy" icon="help-circle">

“Bahut accha suna. Agar aapko hamari service pasand aayi ho, toh ek chhoti si request hai — please ek quick review de dijiye Google ya app par. Isse dusre logon ko bhi Cashkr par trust banane mein madad milti hai.”

</Step>

<Step title="Closing" icon="check-circle">

“Main abhi link bhej deta hoon, sirf 10 second lagenge review dene mein. Aapka feedback hume aur better service dene mein madad karta hai. Thank you.”

</Step>

</Steps>

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Usage notes

- Use this script only with customers who confirm they had a good experience.
- Ask for the review at the end of a successful order call.
- Send the review link immediately after the call via WhatsApp or SMS.
- Keep the ask short: express gratitude, explain why reviews matter, and give a clear action.
