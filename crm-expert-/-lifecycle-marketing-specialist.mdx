## Role & reporting line

- **Role title**
  - CRM Expert / Lifecycle Marketing Specialist – Cashkr
- **Department**
  - Marketing & Growth (with strong cross-functional responsibilities)
- **Reports to (primary)**
  - Marketing Head
- **Functional / dotted-line**
  - Data Analyst (for events, cohorts, dashboards and impact measurement)
  - Head of Technology / IT (for integrations, events and API connections)
  - CX / Operations Lead (for service messages, SLAs, pickup and order status flows)
- **Works closely with**
  - Performance Marketing Specialist (align offers, creatives, audiences and campaigns)
  - SEO and Content / Copywriting (for message scripts, subject lines, WhatsApp/Email tone)
  - Product / App and Web Team (in-app messaging, journeys, UX states)
  - Pricing and Buyback team (pricing-based triggers, high-value segments, offers)
  - CX / Support (feedback flows, review collection, communication rules)

## Role purpose

Design, implement and optimise end-to-end lifecycle journeys across WhatsApp, Email, SMS, Push and In-App so that:

- More users move from Lead → Price View → Order → Pickup → Completion → Feedback → Repeat
- Drop-offs in the funnel are reduced and order completion improves
- Existing users are retained, reactivated and nurtured into repeat sellers and advocates

This role sits at the intersection of Marketing + Tech + Data + CX and is highly automation and analytics driven.

## Key result areas (KRAs)

- **KRA 1 – Lifecycle strategy and funnel ownership**  
  Define the customer lifecycle from first contact to repeat sale and map journeys and automations for each key stage and segment.

- **KRA 2 – Automation and journey building across channels**  
  Build and maintain automated flows (WhatsApp, Email, SMS, Push, In-App) for price view, order not completed, pickup reminders, documentation, feedback, win-back, festive offers and more.

- **KRA 3 – Segmentation and personalisation**  
  Create and maintain smart segments by device type, behaviour, lead stage, order status, geography and value; use dynamic data (name, device, quote value, city, etc.) to personalise communication.

- **KRA 4 – Channel management, deliverability and compliance**  
  Ensure messages are delivered reliably (DLT, WhatsApp templates, sender reputation) and channels are used optimally (right volume, right timing, right mix of transactional vs promotional).

- **KRA 5 – Analytics, reporting and continuous optimisation**  
  Measure lifecycle performance (open, click, conversion, completion, retention, CRM revenue) and run A/B tests to continuously improve flows.

- **KRA 6 – Tool and data integration with Tech and Data teams**  
  Work with Tech and Data to keep CRM tools integrated with website, app, admin panel, GA4 and data warehouse so that events, attributes and triggers stay in sync.

- **KRA 7 – Retention, win-back and long-term value**  
  Design retention and re-engagement programs to bring back inactive users, recover cancelled/failed orders, and increase repeat selling and referral behaviour.

## KPIs

### No. 1 – Lifecycle conversion & completion uplift

**Description**

Impact of CRM flows on order completion and key funnel conversions for users who receive lifecycle communication vs those who do not (where measurable).

**Examples of metrics**

- Order completion rate for “Order Not Completed” segment before vs after CRM flows
- Completion rate for users who received pickup/verification reminders vs those who did not

**Target**

Clear uplift trend; specific percentage targets can be set once baseline is known.

**Data source**

GA4, CRM platform, Admin Panel, Looker and dashboards (via Data Analyst).

**Review frequency**

Monthly.

**Linked KRAs**

KRA 1, KRA 2, KRA 5, KRA 7.

### No. 2 – Revenue / orders from CRM flows

**Description**

Direct contribution of CRM automations and campaigns to orders and revenue.

**Metrics**

- Number of orders attributed to CRM flows (for example, clicked from message → completed order)
- Revenue or payout volume associated with CRM-triggered orders (where trackable)

**Target**

Grow CRM-attributed orders and revenue share over time.

**Data source**

CRM platform attribution, GA4, Admin Panel, Looker dashboards.

**Review frequency**

Monthly.

**Linked KRAs**

KRA 1, KRA 2, KRA 5, KRA 7.

### No. 3 – Engagement metrics (open, click, reply)

**Description**

Health of CRM channels in terms of engagement.

**Metrics**

- Open rate (Email, Push where applicable)
- Click-through rate (CTR)
- Reply or interaction rate for WhatsApp flows (where relevant)

**Target**

Channel-standard benchmarks (improve with testing; avoid engagement drop-off caused by spammy sending).

**Data source**

CRM platform and channel analytics, GA4.

**Review frequency**

Weekly / Monthly.

**Linked KRAs**

KRA 3, KRA 4, KRA 5.

### No. 4 – Drop-off reduction in key journeys

**Description**

Impact on specific bottlenecks in the funnel.

**Examples of metrics**

- Percentage of users who viewed price but did not place order → reduced after “Abandoned Price View” flow
- Percentage of orders stuck in “Order Placed / Not Paid / Not Confirmed / Not Verified” statuses → reduced after reminder flows
- Percentage of scheduled pickups that failed due to no-docs / no-answer → reduced after communication flows

**Target**

Define targets per stage (for example, reduce price-view drop-off by X percent, pickup failure rate by Y percent).

**Data source**

Admin Panel, GA4, CRM journey analytics.

**Review frequency**

Monthly.

**Linked KRAs**

KRA 1, KRA 2, KRA 7.

### No. 5 – Retention & re-engagement metrics

**Description**

Measures repeat behaviour and impact of win-back programs.

**Metrics**

- Repeat sell rate (users placing more than 1 order)
- Re-activation rate for inactive users messaged via CRM
- Percentage of cancelled/failed orders recovered via CRM campaigns

**Target**

Increase repeat and retained users and re-activated users quarter-on-quarter.

**Data source**

Admin Panel (user-level order history), CRM segments, dashboards.

**Review frequency**

Monthly / Quarterly.

**Linked KRAs**

KRA 1, KRA 7.

### No. 6 – Journey & segment coverage

**Description**

How comprehensive CRM is across the lifecycle.

**Metrics**

- Number of live automated journeys (for example, Abandoned Price View, Order Not Completed, Pickup Reminder)
- Percentage of total active users and leads that are part of at least one relevant journey or segment

**Target**

Cover all critical stages (lead → price view → order → pickup → completion → feedback → repeat) with at least one automation; grow coverage over time.

**Data source**

CRM platform configuration, journey list, audience coverage metrics.

**Review frequency**

Monthly.

**Linked KRAs**

KRA 1, KRA 2, KRA 3, KRA 6.

### No. 7 – Deliverability, compliance & unsubscribe/spam rate

**Description**

Health and compliance of direct communication channels.

**Metrics**

- Email bounce and spam complaint rate
- WhatsApp template approval rate and block/report rate
- SMS DLT compliance, delivery success rate
- Unsubscribe rate for Email and SMS (where applicable)

**Target**

Keep bounces, complaints and unsubscribes within safe norms; no DLT or WhatsApp violations.

**Data source**

Email platform stats, SMS delivery reports, WhatsApp Business API metrics, CRM dashboards.

**Review frequency**

Monthly.

**Linked KRAs**

KRA 4, KRA 5.

## Core processes / SOPs owned

The CRM Expert / Lifecycle Marketing Specialist is owner or co-owner for these SOPs.

### Customer lifecycle & journey mapping SOP

- **What it covers**
  - Definition and documentation of the lifecycle for Cashkr.
- **Lifecycle definition**
  - Lead → Price View → Order → Pickup → Completion → Feedback → Re-engagement / Repeat
- **Key items**
  - Documents key journeys and states for:
    - Web events and statuses
    - App events and statuses
    - Admin panel events and statuses
  - Identifies which stages must have automation (critical flows) vs nice-to-have.

### Automation & journey build SOP

- **What it covers**
  - How to design and implement lifecycle flows.
- **Key journeys and flows**
  - Abandoned Price View
  - Order Not Completed
  - Pickup Reminder (pre and post scheduled time)
  - Verification / Documentation Reminder
  - Feedback and Review Collection
  - Win-back and Re-engagement sequences
  - Festive and offer-based automations
- **Standards**
  - Triggers, conditions, delays and exit rules
  - Failsafe rules to avoid over-messaging or duplicate journeys.

### Segmentation & personalisation SOP

- **What it covers**
  - Primary segmentation logic and safe use of dynamic fields.
- **Primary segmentation rules**
  - Device type (phone, Mac, laptop, tablet)
  - Lead stage and user behaviour (new lead, cold lead, hot lead)
  - Order status (pending, in-progress, completed, failed)
  - Geography (city clusters, high-priority zones)
  - High-value vs low-value users and orders
- **Dynamic fields and personalisation**
  - How to safely use dynamic fields:
    - Name
    - Device name and model
    - Quote value
    - City
    - Link to resume journey

### Channel & template management SOP

- **What it covers**
  - Governance and rules for WhatsApp, SMS, Email, Push and In-App messages.
- **WhatsApp**
  - Template creation, approval and usage rules
  - Opt-in and opt-out handling and compliance
- **SMS**
  - DLT template management and sender ID usage
  - Allowed vs disallowed content
- **Email**
  - Template library (transactional vs promotional)
  - Basic design and layout rules
- **Push & In-App messages**
  - Placement, frequency and UX rules

### CRM analytics, A/B testing & reporting SOP

- **What it covers**
  - Standard metrics, experimentation, and reporting workflow.
- **Metrics and testing**
  - Standard metrics per journey and channel
  - How to run A/B tests on:
    - Subject lines
    - Message scripts
    - Call-to-action
    - Timing and frequency
- **Documentation and collaboration**
  - How to log experiments, learnings and decisions (which variation becomes the new default)
  - How to work with the Data Analyst to get funnels and cohort reports

### Tool & data integration SOP (with Tech & Data)

- **What it covers**
  - Required data for CRM platform and integration workflow.
- **Events and attributes**
  - Required events and attributes for the CRM platform:
    - User ID
    - Device type
    - Quote value
    - Statuses
    - Timestamps
- **Integration process**
  - How integration requests are raised with Tech:
    - New event mapping
    - New attribute required
    - Webhooks, APIs, or SDK configurations
  - How to validate data sync:
    - Test users
    - Sandbox vs production
    - GA4 comparison

### Retention & win-back playbook SOP

- **What it covers**
  - Retention and re-engagement journeys and alignment with future programs.
- **Key journeys**
  - Inactive users (no recent activity)
  - Failed or cancelled orders
  - Users with bad experience who need recovery or apology flows (aligned with CX)
  - High-value users with special treatment and loyalty
- **Future alignment**
  - Alignment with:
    - Loyalty programs
    - Referral campaigns
    - Special segments for valuable customers

## Weekly & monthly reporting

### Weekly – “CRM & Lifecycle Weekly Snapshot”

**To**

- Marketing Head
- Performance Marketing Specialist
- Data Analyst
- Head of Technology / IT (for integration and tracking issues)
- CX / Operations Lead (for impact on pickups, cancellations, reviews)

**Format**

- Short summary (Slack, Email or Doc) plus dashboards and screenshots.

**Includes**

- **High-level metrics**
  - Number of users touched by CRM flows
  - Orders and revenue influenced by CRM (where visible)
  - Top journeys by volume and conversions (for example, Order Not Completed → Completed)
- **Channel health**
  - Open rates, CTR, reply rates (key campaigns and journeys)
  - Any deliverability issues or blocks
- **Experiments and changes**
  - A/B tests launched or completed and early learnings
  - Any new journeys or segments activated this week
- **Issues and dependencies**
  - Data or tech issues impacting journeys
  - Dependencies on content, CX or Tech
- **Next week focus**
  - Specific flows or segments to improve
  - New automations or experiments planned

### Monthly – “CRM & Lifecycle Section in Monthly Review”

**To**

- CEO
- Marketing Head
- CX and Operations Lead
- Data Analyst
- Tech Lead
- Finance (if revenue reporting is relevant)

**Format**

- Slides or Doc with clear charts and tables.

**Includes**

- **Total CRM impact**
  - Orders and revenue influenced by CRM flows
  - Summary of uplift in completion and retention for key journeys
- **Funnel and drop-off insights**
  - Before vs after metrics for flows like Abandoned Price View, Order Not Completed, Pickup Reminder
  - New bottlenecks discovered
- **Channel performance overview**
  - WhatsApp, Email, SMS, Push, In-App engagement
  - Unsubscribe/spam trends and deliverability health
- **Segments and journeys coverage**
  - New journeys launched
  - Coverage improvements (more users in meaningful automations)
- **Key experiments and learnings**
  - Top wins (for example, a new reminder flow that boosted completions)
  - Fails and what was learned
- **Top 3–5 priorities for next month**
  - New journeys to build or rebuild
  - Key segment or stage to improve (for example, recover failed pickups, reactivate users 90+ days inactive)
  - Integration or data improvements required
