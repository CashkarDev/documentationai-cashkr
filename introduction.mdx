## 💡 Our Story

Founded in 2020, Cashkr started with a simple but powerful mission: to transform the tedious process of selling used electronics into a seamless, rewarding experience. What began as a mobile phone buyback service has today evolved into one of India’s most trusted platforms for selling all kinds of electronics — from smartphones and laptops to macs, tablets, and more.

## 💡 What We Do

Cashkr connects customers, vendors, and businesses through:

- **Customer App & Website** → Easy way for individuals to sell devices instantly
- **Vendor App** → For agents/vendors to manage pickups and fulfill orders
- **Store Portal** → For store partners to onboard devices
- **Admin Panel** → Internal command center for leads, vendors, orders, and payments

## 💡 Our Mission

To make device resale as simple, transparent, and instant as ordering food online — while also promoting sustainable recycling by extending the life of electronics.
