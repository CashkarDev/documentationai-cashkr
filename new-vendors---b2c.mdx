## Overview

**Sidebar:** Vendors → Roster → **New Applications** (`/vendors/application`)

New Applications lists vendor registration requests submitted through the vendor (partner) app that are waiting for review. Use it to verify vendor details and KYC documents, then **Accept** or **Reject** each application.

## List view: what the page shows

Each row is one vendor who has submitted an application. Breadcrumb: Dashboard / Vendors / Application.

### List columns

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Vendor ID | Short system identifier assigned at registration. |
| Vendor Name | Applicant name and shop / business name. |
| Contact | Registered phone number with a quick-call button. |
| City | City entered by the vendor. |
| Health Score | Starts at grade A, 0% for new applicants. |
| Avg. Drop | Blank ("—") for new vendors with no order history. |
| Status | **Pending** for applications awaiting review. |
| Pickup Rate, Trend, City Rank, Errors, Top Devices | Performance columns shared with All Vendors; mostly empty for new applicants. |

## Filters and search

- **Search vendors** — search box at the top right.
- **New Vendors** — checkbox to show only recent registrations.
- **Filters** — opens **Status**, **City** and **From / To date** filters, with **Reset** and **Apply Filters**.

## Application detail panel

Click any row to open the **Application** side panel. The top bar shows **Current Status** (Pending) with the **Accept** and **Reject** buttons.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Details" icon="info">

- **Vendor name and shop name**, with a status badge.
- **Status note** — "Hold: pincodes kept, max leads 0, auto-release on schedule. Barr: pincodes cleared, manual reactivation. Going live requires at least 1 pincode and max leads > 0."
- **Interested in** — device types the vendor selected in the app (for example Mobile, Tablet, Laptop, Mac, Console, TV).
- **Internal Vendor** — toggle to mark the vendor as internal.
- **Phone, Email, Address** — with **Edit details**.
- **GSTIN** — or "Not provided".
- **Created on** — date the application was submitted.
- **Activity History** — log of admin actions on this application.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Documents" icon="folder">

**Vendor Documents** uploaded during registration:

- GSTIN Certificate (optional)
- Gumasta Certificate (optional)
- Aadhar Udhyog
- Shop Photo
- PAN Card
- Aadhar Front and Aadhar Back
- Vendor Photo

Missing optional documents show an **Optional** placeholder.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Review and decision steps

<Steps>

<Step title="Receive vendor application" icon="users">

- The vendor registers through the vendor app.
- The application appears in New Applications with **Pending** status.

</Step>

<Step title="Find and open the application" icon="search">

- Use search, the New Vendors checkbox and Filters (status, city, date range) to locate it.
- Click the row to open the Application panel.

</Step>

<Step title="Review details and history" icon="info">

- On **Details**, confirm name, shop, contact, address, devices of interest and GSTIN.
- Check **Activity History** for earlier admin actions.
- Set the **Internal Vendor** toggle if required.

</Step>

<Step title="Verify documents" icon="shield">

- On **Documents**, confirm the KYC documents are complete, clear and match the application.

</Step>

<Step title="Accept the application" icon="check-circle">

- Click **Accept** when the vendor meets the onboarding criteria.
- The vendor then appears in [All Vendors](/b2c-vendors). To go Live they need at least one pincode and Max Leads greater than 0 — configure devices, pincodes / zones and Max Leads from the vendor panel.
- Track their ramp-up (first orders, first pickup) on the Activation Tracker.

</Step>

<Step title="Reject the application" icon="x-circle">

- Click **Reject** if documents are invalid, details are incorrect or the vendor does not meet the criteria.
- The application is closed and the vendor cannot use the vendor app.
- The applicant's **phone number** becomes available for internal vendors to add as an agent. Their email does not. See below.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What rejection means downstream

Rejecting an application closes it, but the applicant's phone number does not disappear from the system.

<Callout kind="info">

**Internal vendors can add a rejected applicant's phone number as one of their agents.** External vendors cannot. This lets a rejected applicant still work in the field as an agent under an internal vendor's account, without holding a vendor account of their own.

See [Add Agents](/add-agents) for the vendor-side flow and the rules that apply.

</Callout>

Things to be clear about when rejecting:

- **Only the phone number is freed.** The email address the applicant registered with stays tied to the closed application and cannot be reused on the agent record.
- **Rejection is not reversed by this.** Someone added as an agent does not regain vendor access, and their application stays closed.
- **Only internal vendors are eligible.** The Internal Vendor toggle — set here, or later on [Vendors - B2C](/b2c-vendors) — gates the capability.
- **The applicant is not notified.** There is no notification or consent step when their number is added as an agent. Knowledge of this capability is limited to internal vendors, so do not reference it when communicating a rejection.

<Callout kind="alert">

Some behaviours around this are **not yet confirmed**, including whether the closed application is linked to the resulting agent record for audit purposes, whether two internal vendors can claim the same number, whether the person can later re-apply as a vendor, and whether any time limit applies after rejection. The full list is on [Add Agents](/add-agents).

</Callout>

## Connections to other modules

- **Vendor app** — all applications originate from vendor registration in the app.
- **All Vendors** — accepted vendors are managed from [Vendors - B2C](/b2c-vendors).
- **Activation Tracker** — shows ramp progress for recently approved vendors and what is blocking them.
- **Onboarding** — vendor leads worked in [Onboarding B2C](/onboarding-b2c) register in the app and then appear here.
- **Add Agents** — rejected applications are the source of phone numbers internal vendors can add as agents.

## Important notes

<Callout kind="info">

- Verify all documents before accepting; do not accept applications with missing or unclear documents.
- After accepting, configure pincodes / zones, devices and Max Leads on All Vendors — a vendor cannot go Live without at least one pincode and Max Leads greater than 0.
- Rejected applications cannot be reversed from this page; contact the system administrator if a rejection was made in error.
- Pending vendors cannot receive leads or orders.
- A rejected applicant's phone number can be added as an agent by an internal vendor. This does not reopen the rejection, and the applicant is not notified.

</Callout>
