## Overview

The Finance Reports page in the Finance module is your central hub for all financial reporting across the business. It consolidates credits, money movement, profit and loss, and GST compliance into four dedicated report views, each with its own filters and data.

## How to use finance reports

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the required report" icon="monitor">

  - Go to the Finance module in the Admin Panel.
  - Select the **Reports** section and click **Credit Report**, **Money Report**, **Profit &amp; Loss**, or **GST Report** based on your requirement.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply a date range filter" icon="calendar">

  - Use the date range filter at the top of the report view.
  - Select the specific period you want to analyse instead of loading all historical data.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Review key metrics and transactions" icon="list-filter">

  - Scan the summary metrics and totals for a quick health check.
  - Drill into the transaction-level data to reconcile payments, credits, expenses, and margins.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Export GST data for monthly filing" icon="receipt-text">

  - Open the **GST Report** and apply the monthly date range you plan to file.
  - Export the report data each month and share it with your accounts or CA team for GST return filing.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Report modules

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Credit Report" icon="credit-card">

  The Credit Report provides a complete view of all credit-related activity across all vendors on the platform.

  Use this report to:

  - Track how many credits each vendor has purchased over a period.
  - Monitor credit usage against purchases made.
  - View outstanding credit balances for all vendors.
  - Identify vendors with unused or excess credits.
  - Reconcile credit purchases with payments received.

  This report pulls data from the Credits module and reflects all approved credit purchase transactions.

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Money Report" icon="banknote">

  The Money Report gives a full picture of all cash and bank transactions flowing through the business in one consolidated view.

  This report covers:

  - Device purchase payments made to vendors.
  - Device sale receipts from customers and dealers.
  - Business expenses recorded in the Expenses module.
  - B2B payment receipts from corporate clients.
  - Cash and bank account transactions from Cash In Hand.

  Use this report to:

  - Monitor total inflows and outflows over a selected period.
  - Reconcile cash received against cash spent.
  - Identify payment gaps or unrecorded transactions.
  - Share a complete money movement summary with management.

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Profit &amp; Loss" icon="trending-up">

  The Profit &amp; Loss report tracks the overall financial performance of the business across different time periods.

  This report includes:

  - Daily P&amp;L: profit or loss recorded for each individual day.
  - Weekly P&amp;L: aggregated performance across each week.
  - Monthly P&amp;L: high-level monthly revenue versus cost breakdown.

  Key metrics tracked:

  - Total revenue from device sales and B2B deals.
  - Total cost of devices purchased.
  - Gross margin earned.
  - Operating expenses deducted.
  - Net profit or loss for the period.

  Use this report to:

  - Present financial performance to management or investors.
  - Identify high-performing and low-performing periods.
  - Track whether the business is growing month over month.
  - Make pricing and operational decisions based on margin trends.

  <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-39cfb2dd-42b8-41e4-a035-d7210b5839fe/doc-1aebdaec-9f45-458a-9429-3234288b0c70/1774349770259-ita62vamtjg-profit---loss-report.profit & loss report?q=85&fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1915" height="814" alt="Profit and Loss report showing daily, weekly, and monthly profit metrics and trends" />

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="GST Report" icon="receipt-text">

  The GST Report is designed for tax compliance under the Margin Scheme applicable to refurbished and second-hand device trading.

  This report provides:

  - Total GST liability for the selected period.
  - CGST breakdown: Central GST component on margin.
  - SGST breakdown: State GST component on margin.
  - Monthly summaries for filing preparation.
  - Per-transaction GST details based on sale margins.

  Use this report to:

  - Prepare monthly GST returns accurately.
  - Verify GST on margin calculations for each sale.
  - Share GST summaries with the accounts or CA team.
  - Ensure compliance with Margin Scheme GST rules.

  Important: GST is calculated on the margin (Sale Price minus Cost Price) and not on the full sale value. This is specific to the Margin Scheme for used goods. Ensure all device cost prices are correctly recorded in inventory for accurate GST calculations.

## Admin responsibilities

Admins and finance owners use these reports to maintain financial hygiene and support decision-making.

- Review the Money Report monthly to reconcile all inflows and outflows.
- Use the Profit &amp; Loss report for management reviews and business planning.
- Export the GST Report monthly before filing GST returns.
- Monitor the Credit Report to identify vendors with large unused credit balances.
- Use date range filters within each report for period-specific analysis.

## Connections

Each Finance report is tightly integrated with other modules in the Admin Panel.

- **Credits**: Credit Report pulls data from all approved credit purchase transactions.
- **Device Sales**: Sale revenue and margin data feeds into Money Report, Profit &amp; Loss, and GST Report.
- **Device Purchases**: Purchase costs feed into Profit &amp; Loss and Money Report.
- **Expenses**: Business expenses are included in the Money Report and Profit &amp; Loss.
- **B2B Payments**: B2B revenue is reflected in Money Report and Profit &amp; Loss.
- **Cash In Hand**: All cash and bank transactions appear in the Money Report.
- **Returns**: Refunds and returns are accounted for in Profit &amp; Loss and Money Report.

## Important notes

<Callout kind="alert">

- All reports are read-only views. Data is not editable from the Reports page, so make corrections in the respective source modules.
- Always apply a date range filter when opening a report to avoid loading all historical data at once, which may slow down the page.
- The GST Report follows the Margin Scheme calculation where GST is applied on the margin (sale price minus cost price). If you do not record device cost prices correctly in inventory, GST figures in the report will be inaccurate.

</Callout>
