## Overview

Manage the list of article authors for Cashkr Web Articles in the Strapi Content Manager. Changes you make here control which authors appear in the website article section.

## What it shows

- A list of all authors stored in Strapi with their details.
- A search and filter bar so you can quickly find specific authors.
- A **Create new entry** button to add new authors.
- Edit and delete actions for each existing author.
- A settings icon that lets you configure how the authors list is displayed.

## Common tasks

Use these steps for the most common actions in the Authors collection type.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Authors in Content Manager">

- Open the Strapi Content Manager.
- Select the **Authors** collection type to view the list of authors.

</Step>

<Step title="Search and filter authors">

- Use the search bar to look up authors by name.
- Apply filters to narrow down the list to specific author subsets.

</Step>

<Step title="Create a new author entry">

- Click **Create new entry** in the Authors view.
- Fill in the author details and save to add the author to the list.

</Step>

<Step title="Edit an existing author">

- Click an author in the list to open the edit view.
- Update the author details and save your changes.

</Step>

<Step title="Delete an author">

- Select the author you want to remove.
- Use the delete action and confirm the removal to delete the author.

</Step>

<Step title="Configure the authors view">

- Click the settings icon in the Authors list.
- Adjust the view configuration to control which fields and columns are shown.

</Step>

</Steps>

## System logic

<Callout kind="info">

Author changes update article relations instantly, and deleted authors are dissociated from articles.

</Callout>

## Connections

The Authors collection type connects directly to how authors appear on the website and in related content:

- It links authors to articles through the article-author relations in the Content Manager.
- Any changes you make to authors here are reflected directly in the website article section.
