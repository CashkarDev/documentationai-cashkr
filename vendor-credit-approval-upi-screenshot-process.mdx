## Purpose and scope

Use this module to approve or reject vendor-uploaded UPI payment screenshots before credits are added to a vendor Cashkr account.

This SOP applies to Vendor Operations, Finance, Operations, and Management teams.

The goals are:

- Correct vendor credit management
- Identify and block fraudulent credit uploads
- Maintain clear audit trails
- Ensure vendors receive credits only after genuine payments
- Support accurate vendor financial records

This SOP governs the approval process for vendor-uploaded UPI payment screenshots used to add credits to a Cashkr account.

## When is this process used?

Vendors upload UPI payment screenshots in these situations:

- They add credits (commission balance)
- They refill their wallet (if allowed in future updates)
- They claim pending credit from Cashkr Operations
- They need adjustment after an order reversal

Use the following Admin Panel section:

- `Vendors` → `Payments` → `Credit Approval Panel`

## Quick checklist (before you review a request)

Use this quick checklist before approving any vendor credit based on a UPI screenshot.

### Vendor and account eligibility

- Vendor status is `ACTIVE`
- Vendor has valid KYC
- Vendor is not on `HOLD` or `DEACTIVATED`
- Vendor balance matches the expected transaction

### Screenshot fields required

Every UPI screenshot must clearly show:

- Transaction ID or UTR
- Amount paid
- Time and date
- Sender UPI ID (must match the vendor registered UPI)
- Receiver UPI ID (must match the Cashkr bank account UPI ID)
- Paid to / Paid from details
- Success confirmation (for example, `Payment Successful` or `Completed`)

If any of the above is missing, reject the request immediately.

<Callout kind="info">

Finance must verify the payment in the bank statement before you approve any credit. Do not approve based only on the screenshot.

</Callout>

## Eligibility for credit approval

Confirm all of these before detailed review:

- Vendor is `ACTIVE`
- Vendor has valid KYC
- Vendor is not on `HOLD` or `DEACTIVATED`
- Vendor balance aligns with the claimed transaction
- Screenshot follows the required format and contains all required data

If any eligibility condition fails, reject the request and record the reason.

## Required data on UPI screenshots

Check that the uploaded screenshot includes:

- Transaction ID or UTR
- Amount paid
- Time and date of payment
- Sender UPI ID (must match the vendor registered UPI)
- Receiver UPI ID (must match the Cashkr bank account UPI ID)
- Paid to / Paid from details
- Payment status showing success (for example, `Payment Successful` or `Completed`)

If any of these are missing, unclear, or unreadable, reject immediately and instruct the vendor to upload a correct screenshot if payment was genuine.

## Step-by-step approval workflow

Use this workflow for every vendor UPI screenshot request.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Admin Panel" icon="monitor">

  - Log in to the Admin Panel.
  - Navigate to `Vendors` → `Payments` → `UPI Screenshot Approvals`.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the vendor&apos;s pending request" icon="users">

  - Locate the relevant vendor entry in the UPI Screenshot Approvals list.
  - Review the summary details:
    - Vendor name
    - UPI ID
    - Amount claimed
    - Screenshot preview
    - Timestamp of upload

  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify screenshot authenticity" icon="search">

  Review the screenshot in three parts:

  **A. Transaction details**

  - Match the screenshot amount with the vendor claimed amount.
  - Confirm the UTR is present and readable.
  - Check that the payment timestamp is within the last 24–48 hours (as per internal guidelines).

  **B. UPI ID validation**

  - Confirm the sender UPI matches the vendor registered UPI in the system.
  - Confirm the receiver UPI matches the Cashkr bank UPI.

  **C. Image verification**

  - Check the image for visible editing or manipulation.
  - Ensure there are no cropped edges hiding important information.
  - Confirm the screenshot is not reused from past uploads.

  If anything looks suspicious at this stage, escalate to the Vendor Operations Manager before proceeding.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Cross-check payment in bank statement (Finance)" icon="database">

  The Finance team must verify the payment details in the bank statement:

  - Confirm the amount is received.
  - Match the UTR with the screenshot.
  - Match the payment timestamp.
  - Confirm the vendor UPI ID is correct.

  If everything matches, Finance can confirm the payment and you can proceed to the next step.

  If there is any mismatch or the payment is not found, reject the request.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Approve or reject the request" icon="check-circle">

  **Approve**

  - Approve only if:
    - Vendor eligibility checks are passed.
    - Screenshot contains all required data.
    - Screenshot authenticity checks are clean.
    - Finance has confirmed receipt in the bank statement.
  - Click `Approve`.
  - Confirm that:
    - Credits are added to the vendor account automatically.
    - The system logs an audit entry.
    - The vendor receives an SMS or in-app notification that credits are added.

  **Reject**

  - Reject the request if any of the following are true:
    - Amount on screenshot and claimed amount do not match.
    - UTR is missing or unreadable.
    - Sender or receiver UPI ID is wrong.
    - Screenshot is a duplicate of a previous upload.
    - Screenshot appears edited or tampered.
    - Payment is not found in the bank records.
  - Enter a clear rejection reason in the system when prompted.
  - Confirm that the vendor receives a notification: `Your credit request was rejected due to incorrect or invalid payment proof.`

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">

Do not skip Finance verification. No credit approval is allowed based only on screenshots, even if the vendor is trusted.

</Callout>

## Fraud detection rules (zero tolerance)

<Callout kind="alert">

Apply these zero-tolerance rules to protect Cashkr from fraud. Reject such requests immediately and follow escalation rules.

</Callout>

Reject immediately if you see any of the following:

- Screenshot is blurry or intentionally cropped to hide details.
- Image is repeated from previous uploads by the same vendor.
- Sender UPI ID does not match the vendor registered UPI.
- Screenshot shows signs of editing or manipulation.
- Device time does not match the screenshot timestamp in a plausible way.
- Transaction ID or UTR is invalid, fake, or not found in bank records.

Apply internal consequences:

- If detected 2 or more times for a vendor: put the vendor account on `HOLD`.
- If detected 3 or more times: deactivate and permanently block the vendor.

Vendor Operations must add each confirmed case to the Fraud Log inside the Admin Panel.

## Internal approval flow (who does what)

Use this flow to coordinate between teams for credit approvals.

1. **Vendor Operations**

   - Perform the initial screenshot review.
   - Check eligibility, required fields, and basic authenticity.
   - Escalate suspicious or high-value cases to the Vendor Operations Manager.

2. **Finance**

   - Cross-check the payment in the bank statement.
   - Confirm amount, UTR, timestamp, and UPI IDs.
   - Communicate confirmation or mismatch back to Vendor Operations.

3. **Vendor Operations Manager (for high-value uploads)**

   - Review high-value or flagged uploads.
   - Decide to approve or reject after Finance confirmation.
   - Ensure fraud rules are applied consistently.

4. **System**

   - Add credits automatically after approval.
   - Log audit entries.
   - Trigger vendor notifications for approval or rejection.

## Communication flow

### Vendor notifications after approval

Vendors receive a confirmation message, for example:

- `Your credits have been successfully added.`

### Vendor notifications after rejection

Vendors receive a rejection message, for example:

- `Your credit request was rejected due to invalid proof. Please upload the correct screenshot.`

### Vendor Operations internal notes

Always document internal notes for each decision:

- Why the request was approved or rejected
- Whether UTR and key fields were validated
- Whether Finance cross-check was completed and confirmed

## BD and area management team involvement

Business Development and Area Management teams use approved credits as a signal, but they do not approve credits.

They use credit activity to:

- Monitor vendor activity across areas
- Track which areas have active versus inactive vendors
- Identify high-performing vendors versus low-engagement vendors
- Coordinate with Vendor Operations so vendors in high-demand areas keep credits active

BD and Area Management teams should not interfere with the approval process or override fraud rules.

## Common error scenarios and how to handle them

Use these patterns to handle frequent issues:

- **Vendor uploaded wrong screenshot**

  - Reject the request.
  - Ask the vendor to upload the correct screenshot with all required details.

- **Vendor paid but screenshot is missing UTR**

  - Reject the request.
  - Ask the vendor to resend a screenshot where the UTR is clearly visible.

- **Payment not received in bank**

  - Reject the request.
  - Notify the vendor to check with their bank and upload proof again after resolution.

- **Vendor uploaded the same screenshot twice**

  - Reject the duplicate request.
  - Mark it internally as a duplicate attempt.

- **Vendor added credits but account is on HOLD**

  - Inform the vendor that credits cannot be added while the account is on `HOLD`.
  - Redirect the issue to Vendor Operations to review and resolve the account status.

## One-page quick summary (training card)

Use this as a quick reference during training or live operations.

**Credit approval checklist**

- UTR is visible and readable.
- Amount on screenshot matches the claimed amount.
- Payment timestamp is reasonable and matches expectations.
- Sender UPI equals the registered vendor UPI.
- Receiver UPI equals the Cashkr UPI.
- Finance has confirmed the payment in the bank statement.
- Screenshot shows no signs of editing or cropping.

**Outcome rules**

- **Approve** → System adds credits automatically and logs an audit entry; vendor is notified.
- **Reject** → Enter a clear rejection reason; vendor is notified about invalid or incorrect proof.

<Callout kind="alert">

Do not approve any credit without Finance verification, and do not ignore repeated fake or suspicious screenshots. Apply zero-tolerance fraud rules every time.

</Callout>
