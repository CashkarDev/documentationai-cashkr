**Used by:** Store Partnerships / Vendor Management Team

**Purpose:** Train internal teams on how to handle credit refund and reversal cases for B2Ch Store Vendors where adjustments are needed for cancelled, failed, or wrongly charged orders.

**Objective:** Ensure the Vendor Management Team can:
- Identify valid reasons for credit reversals
- Process and verify reversal requests properly
- Maintain fairness and documentation
- Prevent misuse or manipulation of the daily credit system

## 1. Credit system overview (training script)

Use this script to explain the credit logic to vendors or new team members.

> In the B2Ch Store Vendor model, every vendor pays Cashkr Credits for each completed store order.  
> These credits act as Cashkr commission or service fee.

The vendor wallet has two separate flows:

1. Customer payments → direct transfer to customer via wallet  
2. Credits to Cashkr → deducted once daily based on all successfully completed store orders

**Example script:**

> If a vendor completes 8 store orders today and each order costs 5 credits, a total of 40 credits will be deducted at the end of the day from the wallet balance.

## 2. When credit reversals apply

Use this script to define what a credit reversal means.

> Reversal means returning credits back to a vendor when an order is later found to be cancelled, invalid, duplicate, or wrongly deducted.  
> This ensures vendors do not lose credits for transactions that did not actually qualify as valid store orders.

### Eligible vs not eligible scenarios

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Eligible scenarios">

  - **Cancelled orders**  
    Order cancelled by store or Cashkr after completion. These orders are eligible for credit reversal.

  - **Duplicate order entry**  
    Same customer or order counted twice and credits charged twice. Eligible for reversal on the duplicate entry.

  - **Failed verification**  
    ID proof or system verification failed after payment. The underlying order is not valid, so credits are eligible for reversal.

  - **Technical error**  
    Order was marked as completed but later failed in system sync or processing. Credits charged due to this technical issue are eligible for reversal.

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Not eligible scenarios">

  - **SLA violation or late completion**  
    Order invalidated due to SLA breach. Credits are not eligible for reversal in SLA violation cases.

  - **Customer dispute or fraud linked to vendor**  
    Order reversed due to vendor fault or confirmed issue from vendor side. Credits are not eligible for reversal.

  - **App misuse or fake entries**  
    Any manipulation attempt, fake orders, or misuse of the system. Credits are not eligible for reversal.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="alert">

Treat eligibility rules as hard policy:
- Reverse credits only for cancelled, duplicate, invalid, or technically failed orders.
- Do not approve reversals for SLA violations, vendor fault, fraud cases, or misuse.

</Callout>

## 3. How vendors can raise a credit refund request

Use this script to guide vendors to the correct channel.

> Refund or reversal requests must be raised only through the Vendor App, under Support → Credit Reversal Request.  
> Vendors cannot raise these requests verbally or on WhatsApp.

Vendors must submit:

- **Order ID or IDs in question**
- **Reason for reversal**
- **Proof, if applicable** such as screenshots or store confirmation

Once submitted, the request appears on the Vendor Management Dashboard for verification.

<Callout kind="alert">

Do not process or commit on any reversal requests received via calls, chats, or WhatsApp.  
Ask the vendor to raise a formal request through the app with the correct order ID and proof.

</Callout>

## 4. Credit reversal verification workflow

Follow this standard workflow for every reversal request.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Vendor raises reversal request" icon="download">

  - **Action:** Vendor submits a Credit Reversal Request through the Vendor App with order ID, reason, and proof.  
  - **Owner:** Vendor

  </Step>

  <Step title="Check basic eligibility" icon="info">

  - **Action:** Verify the order ID, order status, and credit deduction log in the system. Confirm that credits were actually deducted and that the order is within allowed time limits.  
  - **Owner:** Ops Associate

  </Step>

  <Step title="Validate the reason and proof" icon="search">

  - **Action:** Cross-check the vendor reason against store logs, order timeline, and any attached documentation or screenshots. Confirm if the case matches an eligible scenario.  
  - **Owner:** Vendor Manager

  </Step>

  <Step title="Take decision on approval or rejection" icon="check-circle">

  - **Action:** Decide whether to approve or reject the reversal based on eligibility criteria and verification outcome. Document the reason for decision in the tracker or dashboard.  
  - **Owner:** Ops Manager

  </Step>

  <Step title="Execute credit adjustment" icon="zap">

  - **Action:** On approval, restore credits to the vendor credit balance. The system posts a `+ Credit Adjustment` entry in the Credits tab within 24 hours.  
  - **Owner:** System auto credit (post approval)

  </Step>
</Steps>

**Success signal:** Approved reversals show in the vendor Credits tab as `+ Credit Adjustment` within 24 hours of approval.

<Callout kind="alert">

Do not leave any approved reversal unapplied in the system.  
If the `+ Credit Adjustment` does not appear within 24 hours, escalate to the Ops Manager and log the case in the tracker.

</Callout>

## 6. Turnaround time (TAT) expectations

Align case handling to these timelines.

- **Simple cases (cancelled or duplicate orders)**  
  - TAT: 24 hours  
  - Handling: Auto reversal after basic checks

- **Verification pending (store logs or extra checks)**  
  - TAT: 48 hours  
  - Handling: Manual review by Ops and Vendor Manager

- **Escalated or fraud-linked cases**  
  - TAT: 72 hours  
  - Handling: Ops Manager review and deeper audit

<Callout kind="alert">

No reversal should remain pending beyond 72 hours from request submission.  
If a case is still open at 72 hours, escalate to Ops Head with reasons and next steps.

</Callout>

## 7. Reopening policy

Use this script when explaining or handling reopened cases.

> If a vendor disagrees with a reversal rejection, they can reopen the case once only, within 48 hours.  
> They must attach new supporting proof such as store communication, duplicate order evidence, or Cashkr backend confirmation.

Cases reopened without any new proof are automatically closed and should not be reconsidered again.

## 8. Fraud and misuse handling

Use this script to set expectations with vendors and internally.

> Cashkr strictly monitors misuse of credit reversal requests. Vendors submitting 3 or more false or repetitive reversal requests in a 30-day period will be added to the Reversal Watchlist.

Penalties can include:

- Temporary hold on new orders
- Suspension of reversal rights
- Escalation to Ops Head for audit

<Callout kind="alert">

Track repeat false or abusive reversal requests and add such vendors to the Reversal Watchlist when they hit the threshold.  
Always document reasons, evidence, and actions in the Credit Reversal Tracker Sheet.

</Callout>

## 9. Daily and weekly reporting cadence

Ensure every case is traceable and reported.

- **Daily reversal log**  
  - Owner: Ops Associate  
  - Frequency: Daily  
  - Focus: All pending and resolved reversal cases

- **Weekly summary**  
  - Owner: Vendor Manager  
  - Frequency: Weekly  
  - Focus: Total reversals, approval or rejection rate, key reasons

- **Monthly review**  
  - Owner: Ops Manager  
  - Frequency: Monthly  
  - Focus: Fraud analysis, Reversal Watchlist vendors, process improvements

All reversal cases must be logged in the Credit Reversal Tracker Sheet and supported by relevant system screenshots.

## 10. Tone and communication guidelines

Maintain a consistent, neutral tone with vendors.

- Always acknowledge vendor concerns before verification.
- Never promise approval without backend validation.
- Keep tone neutral, factual, and non-argumentative.
- Avoid long back-and-forth conversations; once status is shared, close the loop clearly.
- Include the relevant **Order ID or ticket ID** in every message for reference.

## 11. Quick reference summary

Use this section as a one-glance refresher during calls.

- **Vendor pays daily credits**  
  Credits are the fee for all completed store orders and are deducted from the vendor wallet once per day.

- **Refund or reversal applies to**  
  Invalid, cancelled, duplicate, or technically failed orders where credits were already deducted.

- **Request method**  
  Vendor App → Support → Credit Reversal Request, with order ID and proof.

- **Processing time**  
  Typically 24–72 hours depending on case complexity and verification needs.

- **Non-eligible cases**  
  SLA breaches, fraud or vendor fault, app misuse, and cases without required proof.

- **Outcome on approval**  
  `+ Credit Adjustment` entry in the Credits tab and credits restored to vendor credit balance.

## 12. Key talking points for vendor management team

Highlight these points in training and vendor conversations.

- **Reversals only apply to credits already paid by the vendor.**
- **Cashkr restores credits only for cancelled, duplicate, or invalid orders.**
- **All reversals go directly to the vendor credit balance, not to the wallet balance.**
- **Requests must come through the app, not calls or chats.**
- **Transparency and a complete audit trail are mandatory for every reversal.**
