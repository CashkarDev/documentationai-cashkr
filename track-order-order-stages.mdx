## Overview

Track Order on the Cashkr website lets logged-in customers view all their orders, see detailed information for each order, and check the current order stage. Customers also see a lead OTP that they must share with the vendor at the time of device inspection.

## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The customer must be logged in to their Cashkr account to access the Track Order page and view their orders.
</Callout>

## Flow

Use this flow to validate or implement how customers track their orders on the website.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Customer logs in" icon="lock" title-type="p">
    The customer signs in to their Cashkr account using the standard login flow.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Customer navigates to Track Order" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    After login, the customer goes to the Track Order section on the website.
  </Step>

  <Step title="System shows all customer orders" icon="list-ordered" title-type="p">
    The Track Order page lists all orders associated with the logged-in customer and shows relevant details and the current order stage for each order.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Customer reviews order details and status" icon="info" title-type="p">
    For every order row or card, display:

    - Order details for that specific order

    - Current status in the stage flow `Generated → Inprogress → Completed / Failed`

    - The lead OTP for that order
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The lead OTP must be visible on the Track Order detail view. Customers share this OTP with the vendor when the vendor starts the inspection of the device.
</Callout>

## Order stages

Order stages on the Track Order page should follow this flow: `Generated → Inprogress → Completed / Failed`.

### Generated

Generated indicates that the customer has created an order and the system has recorded it.

### Inprogress

Inprogress indicates that activity on the order has started.

### Completed

Completed indicates that the order flow has finished successfully.

### Failed

Failed indicates that the order flow did not finish successfully.
