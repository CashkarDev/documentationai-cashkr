## Overview

Use the Device Purchases page in Finance to track all device buying costs and maintain a clean audit trail for inventory and accounting.

This includes completed internal vendor orders, manual offline purchases, B2C and B2B deals, and excess stock buys, all in a single table.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Use Device Purchases alongside **Device Sales**, **B2B**, and **Reports - Finance** to understand margins, reconcile payments, and validate inventory intake against vendor activity.
</Callout>

---

## What it shows

The page combines summary metrics, purchase types, and a detailed table so you can audit every device-level buy.

### Summary metrics

- The top summary section aggregates the key finance metrics for the current filter selection.

- Sum of the **Purchase Price** for all visible device purchase records. Use this as the total buying cost for the selected period or filters.

- Count of device purchase rows currently visible in the table. Each row represents one purchased device.

### Purchase types

- Use purchase types to split reporting by channel or purpose while keeping all entries in one place.

- Standard customer-facing purchases completed via internal vendors. These typically originate from B2C orders.

- Purchases tied to B2B deals or corporate buyback agreements, often involving bulk quantities or negotiated prices.

- Excess stock purchases made to build inventory beyond immediate order requirements.

### Purchase table columns

Each row in the table represents a single device purchase entry with the following columns.

- Purchase date of the device. For automatic entries, this is the date the internal vendor completed the order.

- Purchase type for tracking: **B2C**, **B2B**, or **Excess**. Use this to split reporting by channel or purpose.

- Name or model of the purchased device. This should match the device record used in inventory.

- Key specifications or description of the device, used for quick identification and quality checks.

- Unique serial number of the purchased device for tracking, warranty, and reconciliation.

- Vendor from whom the device was purchased. This includes internal vendors and direct vendor deals.

- Buying cost of the device. This value feeds into **Total Purchase Value** and downstream finance reports.

---

## Filters available

- Use the filters and controls at the top of the page to narrow down purchases and manage data exports.

- Filter purchases by a custom date range. Updates both the table rows and the summary metrics.

- Filter by purchase type: **All Types**, **B2C**, **B2B**, or **Excess**.

- Filter by vendor: **All Vendors** or a specific vendor name for focused vendor analysis.

- Search within the table by **Device Name** or **Serial Number** to quickly locate a specific purchase.

- Export the current purchase data set for accounting or external analysis. Respects the active filters.

- Reload the latest purchase data, including new automatic entries from internal vendor completions and any new manual entries.

## Add Purchase (manual entry)

Use **+ Add Purchase** when you need to record a device purchase that did not come via the standard internal vendor completion flow.

### When to use it

- Device bought offline or outside the platform workflow, but needs to be reflected in finance and inventory.

- Direct deal done with a vendor (for example, bulk or negotiated purchase) that must be captured as a purchase record.

- Correction or adjustment needed in finance records (for example, missing earlier entry).

- Stock correction when inventory count needs to be aligned with actual physical devices purchased.

### Fields required

- The manual purchase form mirrors the columns in the table so that the entry behaves like any other purchase.

- Date of the purchase you are recording. Use the actual buying date, not the entry date.

- Select **B2C**, **B2B**, or **Excess**, based on how this device will be used and reported.

- Device name or model as it should appear in the Device Purchases table and inventory.

- Key specs or description to uniquely identify the device configuration.

- Serial number used for device-level tracking and reconciliation.

- Name of the vendor or party you purchased from.

- Buying cost of this device. This amount contributes to **Total Purchase Value** and finance reports.

### Steps

Use the following flow whenever you create a manual purchase entry.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Purchase form" icon="plus" title-type="p">
    - Go to **Finance → Device Purchases**.

    - Click the **+ Add Purchase** button at the top of the page to open the manual entry form.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter purchase details" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    - Set the **Purchase Date** and **Type** (B2C, B2B, or Excess).

    - Fill in **Device Name**, **Specs / Description**, and **Serial Number**.

    - Add the **Vendor Name** and **Purchase Price** exactly as per the invoice or deal.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and verify the entry" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    - Click **Save** to create the purchase record.

    - Confirm the new row appears in the Device Purchases table with correct values.

    - Check that **Total Purchase Value** and **Total Items** reflect the new entry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Manual purchase entries behave like any other row: they update the summary metrics and, where applicable, enable the device to move into Inventory.
</Callout>

## Business logic

### Automatic purchase entry

Automatic entries are created when internal vendors complete qualifying orders.

```plaintext
Order Completed (Internal Vendor)
        ↓
Purchase Record Created
        ↓
Added to Device Purchases
        ↓
Added to Inventory
```

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Only eligible internal vendor order completions flow into Device Purchases automatically. Use this page to verify that all completed internal orders have corresponding purchase entries.
</Callout>

### Manual purchase entry

Manual entries follow a similar path once saved by an admin.

```plaintext
Admin → Add Purchase
        ↓
Enter Details
        ↓
Save
        ↓
Purchase Record Created
        ↓
Visible in Device Purchases
```

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Treat edits and deletions on this page as finance-impacting actions. Always confirm with finance before adjusting historical purchase entries, especially for closed accounting periods.
</Callout>

## Roles and responsibilities

- The Device Purchases page is used mainly by finance and operations roles that manage buying costs and vendor activity.

- Tracks device buying cost, reconciles purchase values with payments, and exports data for accounting.

- Monitors vendor purchases, checks stock intake, and validates that internal vendor completions are recorded.

- Creates and adjusts manual purchase entries for offline buys, direct deals, and stock corrections.

- Manual purchase entries are fully controlled by admins. Finance and operations should align on who can create or edit these entries.

---

## Connections to other modules

- Device Purchases is tightly connected to order completion flows, inventory, finance reports, and vendor records.

- Automatic source of purchase records when internal vendors complete customer orders. Each eligible completion generates a purchase entry.

- Admin-created manual entries via **+ Add Purchase** for offline or corrective purchases.

- Receives devices from purchase records so that purchased stock becomes available inventory.

- Uses purchase data and **Total Purchase Value** to build profit, loss, and cost reports.

- Links vendor-level purchase history to vendor profiles for performance and reconciliation.

- The **Price** field stores the buying cost, not selling price. This value feeds directly into purchase value and margin calculations.

- **Total Purchase Value** and **Total Items** auto-calculate from the visible rows based on active filters.

- Any edit or deletion of purchase entries should immediately reflect in the summary totals and downstream finance reports.
