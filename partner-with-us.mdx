## What the Partner With Us page shows

The Partner With Us page presents Cashkr's vendor partnership opportunity and highlights the benefits of becoming a vendor partner. The content is static and focuses on encouraging vendors to join via the vendor app.

<Callout kind="info">

- The page is static informational content describing the vendor partnership opportunity.
- The main call-to-action is the **Download Vendor App** button, which links to the Google Play Store.
- The primary purpose is vendor awareness and acquisition; there is no form submission or interactive data entry on this page.

</Callout>

## Step-by-step user flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Partner With Us page" icon="folder" title-type="p">

  - The user navigates to the Partner With Us page from the footer, navigation bar, or a promotional banner.

  </Step>

  - The user reads about the benefits and details of becoming a Cashkr vendor partner.

  </Step>

  - The user clicks the **Download Vendor App** button, which opens the corresponding Cashkr vendor app listing in the Google Play Store.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## System logic

The Partner With Us page does not include any dynamic behavior or server-side processing. All content is static, and there are no forms or fields to submit.

The page exists primarily to promote the vendor app and drive potential vendors into the vendor onboarding flow through marketing and navigation entry points.

## Connections

- Promotes the **Vendor App** as the primary entry into the vendor onboarding process.
- Supports vendor acquisition campaigns from the marketing site, navigation, banners, or other promotional placements.
