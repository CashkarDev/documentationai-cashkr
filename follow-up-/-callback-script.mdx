**Team:** B2C Sales  
**Purpose:** Re-engage leads who checked a price or were contacted earlier but have not placed their order yet.  
**Tone:** Polite, helpful, and conversational — never pushy.

## Quick checklist

Use this checklist while making follow-up or callback calls.

- Confirm you are speaking to the right customer and mention Cashkr and their device.
- Remind them they checked the price and that the quote is still active but can change with the market.
- Highlight convenience: free doorstep pickup and instant payment (where available).
- Handle the most common responses: forgot/later, comparing prices, pickup not available, ready to sell.
- Offer to help place/confirm the order now and send the order link again.
- Keep the tone friendly, calm, and non-pressuring throughout the call.

## Call script

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="English (Training reference)">

### Opening

"Hi \[Customer Name], this is \[Your Name] from Cashkr. You had checked the price for your \[Device Model] recently — just wanted to check if you are still planning to sell it?"

### Confirm intent

If the customer sounds unsure or distracted, gently confirm:

"Is this a good time to talk for a minute about your \[Device Model] quote?"

If they say yes, move ahead. If not, ask for a better time and note it down.

### Objection handling

#### If customer says they forgot or will do later

"No worries. Just a reminder — your price quote is still active, but prices can change anytime based on the market. I can help you confirm your order now so you lock today’s price."

If they still want to do it later:

"That is fine. I will send your order link again so you can confirm it whenever you are ready."

#### If customer says they were comparing prices

"That is totally fine. Many customers compare before deciding. Just to let you know — we offer free doorstep pickup and instant payment. If you are getting a better quote somewhere, I can recheck the current rate for you and see what is possible."

If they share another quote, handle as per the price match guidelines.

#### If customer says pickup is not available in their area

"Got it. In some areas pickup is not active yet. We are adding new pincodes regularly. I will mark your area for update and we will notify you once pickup becomes available there."

If relevant, confirm their pincode before ending the call.

### If customer agrees to sell

If at any point the customer says they want to sell:

"Perfect. Let me guide you through placing the order now — it will just take a minute. After you confirm, you will receive a confirmation message with your order details."

If you are creating or confirming the order for them:

"I am updating your order with the same device details and quote. You will see a message from Cashkr shortly with your order link."

### Closing

For customers who are ready or almost ready:

"Thank you for choosing Cashkr. I have sent your direct order link again — you can confirm it anytime today when it is convenient. If you have any questions while confirming, you can reach out to us."

For customers who still want time or are only exploring:

"Thank you for your time. I have shared the link again, so if you decide to sell your \[Device Model], you can use it to confirm your order in a few clicks."

### Opening

"Hello \[Name], main \[Agent Name] bol raha/rahi hoon Cashkr se. Aapne apne \[Device Model] ka price check kiya tha — bas yahi check karna tha ki aap abhi bechne ka plan kar rahe ho?"

### Confirm intent

If customer seems busy or unsure:

"Abhi baat karna theek time hai kya? Sirf ek minute lagega aapke \[Device Model] ke quote ke baare mein."

Agar woh haan bolen, aage badho. Agar na bolen, unse better time le lo.

### Objection handling

#### Agar customer bole ‘baad mein’ ya ‘bhool gaya/gayi’

"Koi baat nahi. Aapka price quote abhi active hai, lekin price kabhi bhi market ke hisaab se change ho sakta hai. Chahein toh main abhi help kar sakta hoon order confirm karne mein, taaki aaj ka jo rate hai woh hi apply ho."

Agar woh phir bhi baad mein bolen:

"Thik hai, main aapko link dubara bhej deta hoon. Jab aapko time mile, us par click karke order confirm kar sakte ho."

#### Agar customer prices compare kar raha ho

"Bilkul thik, compare karna zaroori hota hai. Bas ek cheez dhyaan rakhna — hamara pickup free hai aur payment turant milti hai. Agar kahin aur se better quote mil raha hai, toh main aapke liye latest price check kar sakta hoon aur dekh sakte hain kya possible hai."

Agar woh doosri jagah ka quote bataye, toh price match rules ke hisaab se handle karo.

#### Agar pickup unke area mein available nahi hai

"Koi problem nahi, kuch areas mein pickup abhi tak start nahi hua hai. Hum naye pincodes add kar rahe hain. Main aapka area note kar leta hoon, jaise hi waha pickup active hoga hum aapko inform kar denge."

Zarurat ho toh unse pincode confirm kar lo.

### Agar customer bole ‘haan bechna hai’

"Great. Main abhi help karta hoon order confirm karne mein — sirf ek minute lagega. Jaise hi aap confirm karoge, aapko confirmation message turant mil jaayega."

Agar tum unke liye order create ya confirm kar rahe ho:

"Main aapka order same device details aur quote ke saath update kar raha hoon. Thodi der mein aapko Cashkr ka ek message aayega jisme order link hoga."

### Closing

Jo customer bechne ke liye ready hai:

"Thank you. Main link dubara bhej deta hoon, aap jab chahein us par click karke confirm kar sakte ho. Agar process mein koi doubt ho, toh humein bata dena."

Jo customer abhi sirf soch raha hai ya time maang raha hai:

"Thik hai, thanks call pick karne ke liye. Maine aapko link bhej diya hai, agar aap decide karte ho bechne ka, toh usi link se aap asaani se order confirm kar sakte ho."

## What not to say

Avoid these types of statements so you do not over-promise or sound pushy.

- Do not promise a fixed price lock for any specific number of days or hours.
- Do not guarantee that Cashkr will always match or beat any other price.
- Do not give exact dates for when pickup will start in a new area.
- Do not pressure customers with lines like "You have to confirm right now" or "This is your last chance."
- Do not argue with the customer about other platforms; stay respectful and focus on our service.

## Usage notes

<Callout kind="tip">

- Best used for follow-ups within 24–48 hours after a lead checks a price.
- Keep the tone helpful and friendly, never aggressive or pushy.
- Focus on three things: remind the quote is active, reassure about service, and re-trigger intent to sell.
- Always confirm you have resent the order link before ending the call.
