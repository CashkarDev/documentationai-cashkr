## Offers and deals section for affiliation

Use Special offers and deals sections in BigboldTech articles to quickly find curated products related to what you are reading and jump directly to retailer offers.

<Steps>

<Step title="Find the Special offers and deals section">

Look for the Special offers and deals block within an article. It usually appears near relevant content and shows a list of products with brief details and pricing highlights.

</Step>

<Step title="Open a deal on the retailer site">

Click the **View deals** button for the product you are interested in. This takes you to the retailer's website using an affiliate link for that specific product.

</Step>

<Step title="Review details and complete your purchase">

On the retailer's site, review the full product description, pricing, and customer reviews, then complete your purchase directly with the retailer if it meets your needs.

</Step>

</Steps>

## How affiliate links work

Affiliate links in Special offers and deals sections help support BigboldTech at no extra cost to you.

When you click a **View deals** button and then buy a product on the retailer's site, the retailer may pay BigboldTech a small commission for that purchase. This commission helps fund content creation and site maintenance and does not change the price you pay to the retailer.

## Leaving BigboldTech and retailer responsibility

When you click **View deals**, you leave the BigboldTech website and move to a third party retailer site.

<Callout kind="info">

The retailer controls product pricing, availability, shipping options, and return policies. Always check the latest details on the retailer's page before completing your purchase.

</Callout>
