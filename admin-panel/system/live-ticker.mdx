## Overview

Live Ticker is a full-screen display designed for an office TV or monitor. It shows headline platform numbers in large flip-style digits and a scrolling ticker of live metrics.

Open it from **System → Admin → Live Ticker** in the left sidebar (route `/ticker`). The page opens without the admin sidebar.

## What the screen shows

| Area | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Header | Cashkr logo, current date, live clock (IST) and an **Enter fullscreen** button |
| Total Users | Total registered users, in large flip digits |
| Received | Orders received today |
| Completed | Orders completed today |
| Converted | Leads converted to orders today |
| LIVE ticker strip | A scrolling bar at the bottom, marked with a red **LIVE** badge, with USERS, ORD·RCVD, ORD·CMPL, ORD·CNV, PHONE, TABLET, LAPTOP, MAC, NEW·VENDORS, CMPL%, CNV% and MARKET (market status, e.g. OPEN) |

The numbers update automatically; there are no filters or actions.

<Callout kind="tip">
Open the page on the display device and click **Enter fullscreen** (top right) to hide the browser chrome.
</Callout>
