## Overview

Coupon Dashboard under Admin Panel → Marketing → Coupons provides a single view of how all coupons are performing across app and web for a selected date range.

<Callout kind="info">

Use this dashboard to monitor overall coupon health, track campaign impact, and understand how discounts influence orders, revenue, and abandoned cart recovery.

</Callout>

The header shows:

- **Title** — `Coupon Dashboard`
- **Date range selector** — For example `Feb 25, 2026 – Mar 27, 2026`
- **Refresh** — Reloads all KPIs, charts, and tables for the selected period

Use the date range and Refresh together whenever you run reviews, daily standups, or campaign post-mortems.

## How to use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the analysis period" icon="calendar">

Set the date range in the top-right selector to match the campaign or review period you care about, such as week-on-week or month-on-month.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Refresh the dashboard" icon="refresh-ccw">

Click **Refresh** after changing the date range so that all KPIs, charts, and tables recalculate using the new period.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Scan key metrics and breakdowns" icon="bar-chart">

Review the top summary cards, period metrics row, and abandoned cart recovery panel to understand overall coupon usage, impact on revenue, and user engagement.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Drill into top coupons" icon="list">

Use the **Top Coupons** table to identify the best performing codes, channels where they work, and whether any coupon is close to its usage limits or underperforming.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Filters

The Coupon Dashboard uses a single global filter:

- **Date range** — Controls which orders, redemptions, and users are counted in all period-based metrics, charts, and the Top Coupons table.

After changing the date range:

- Click **Refresh** to apply the new filter across the dashboard.
- Confirm that KPI cards and graphs update to the new period before using numbers in reports.

## Key metrics

The top part of the dashboard is split into two sections: a summary metrics row and a period metrics row.

### Summary metrics row

These cards give an overall view of your coupon setup and long-term usage:

- **Total Coupons** — Total number of coupons currently configured in the system.
- **All-Time Usage** — Total number of coupon redemptions across all time.
- **Avg Discount/Use** — Average discount amount per coupon redemption in INR.
- **First-Customer Coupons** — Count of coupons restricted to first-time customers, shown with a usage progress bar and an `All coupons` reference.

Use these metrics to understand how heavily you rely on coupons overall and how aggressively you discount.

### Period metrics row

These cards recalculate based on the selected date range:

- **Usage (Period)** — Total coupon redemptions during the selected period, often with a percentage change vs the previous period.
- **New Coupons** — Number of coupons created within the selected period.
- **Unique Users** — Number of distinct customers who used at least one coupon in the period.
- **Revenue** — Revenue from orders where a coupon was applied during the period.
- **Completed Orders** — Count of orders completed with a coupon in the selected period.
- **Total Discount** — Total discount amount given via coupons in INR during the period.
- **Cart Tokens** — Number of cart tokens generated for abandoned cart recovery.
- **Cart Redeemed** — Percentage of generated cart tokens that were actually redeemed.

Compare **Usage (Period)**, **Revenue**, and **Total Discount** to judge whether coupon campaigns are efficient or too costly for the results they generate.

### Abandoned Cart Recovery

The **Abandoned Cart Recovery** panel tracks how well your coupon-based recovery flow is working:

- **Recovery %** — Share of abandoned carts recovered using tokens and coupons.
- **Total tokens** — Total cart tokens generated in the selected period.
- **Redeemed** — Number of tokens successfully used by customers.
- **Unused** — Number of tokens that expired or were never used.

Use this panel to decide whether to adjust incentive levels, validity windows, or follow-up messaging for abandoned cart campaigns.

## Charts and breakdowns

The middle section of the dashboard visualizes coupon performance by status, platform, device, accessibility, discount range, and time.

### Coupon Status

A donut chart shows how all coupons are distributed by status, such as:

- **Active**
- **Expired**
- **Upcoming**

Watch the share of **Expired** vs **Active** coupons to avoid running out of valid offers during live campaigns.

### Platform Breakdown

This donut chart shows where coupons are being used:

- **APP**
- **WEB**
- **BOTH**

Use it to:

- Check if app-only campaigns are driving redemptions.
- Confirm that web banners and landing pages are actually leading to coupon usage.

### Device Breakdown

The Device Breakdown chart shows coupon usage split by device type:

- Phone
- Tablet
- Laptop
- Mac

When there is no data for the selected period, the chart can show **No data**. Use this as a signal to:

- Tune campaigns for device categories where you want more orders.
- Verify whether device-specific coupons are actually being redeemed.

### Accessibility

This breakdown compares:

- **FirstCustomer** — Coupons restricted to first-time users.
- **ALL** — Coupons open to all customers.

Check this chart when you:

- Evaluate the balance between acquisition-focused offers vs retention or reactivation offers.
- Decide whether to convert some first-time-only coupons into all-customer coupons.

### Discount Distribution

A bar chart shows how coupons are distributed across discount value ranges, for example:

- `₹101–200`
- `₹201–500`
- Higher ranges as configured

Use the shape of this distribution to:

- Spot if most offers are clustered in a single low or high range.
- Plan new coupon tiers for better experimentation (e.g., multiple discount levels per campaign).

### Daily Usage Trend

A line chart displays daily coupon redemptions within the selected date range.

Typical uses:

- Identify peak promotion days and weekends.
- See how fast a new coupon starts getting traction after launch.
- Check whether redemptions drop sharply after a campaign ends.

Overlay this view with campaign calendars to understand which promotions truly moved numbers.

## Top coupons table

At the bottom of the dashboard, the **Top Coupons** table lists the most used coupons for the selected date range.

Each row shows:

- **Code** — Coupon code used by customers.
- **Title** — Short name or description of the coupon.
- **Discount** — Discount value or structure configured for the coupon.
- **Platform** — Platform where the coupon is valid: App, Web, or Both.
- **Period Uses** — Number of times the coupon was used within the selected date range.
- **Total Uses** — All-time total usage of the coupon.
- **Max Uses** — Maximum number of times each user can redeem this coupon.
- **Status** — Current coupon status, such as Active or Inactive.

Use this table to:

- Identify your top-performing coupons by **Period Uses** and **Revenue**.
- Spot coupons close to usage limits using **Max Uses** and **Total Uses**.
- Find underperforming coupons that may need better promotion or updated discount values.
