## Overview

- **Team:** Store Onboarding / Channel Operations (B2CH)
- **Purpose:** Train store owners and staff on how to use the Cashkr Store App, understand the buyback process, and manage daily device handovers.
- **Tone:** Friendly, patient, clear — explain step-by-step in simple language.

## Script

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English">

```text
“Hi [Store Owner Name], this is [Your Name] from Cashkr. I’m calling to give you a short training on how to use the Cashkr Store App and manage customer device buyback smoothly.”
```

<Steps>

<Step title="Step 1: Login & Access">

- “You can log in using your registered mobile number.”
- “Once you log in, you’ll see the main dashboard where you can create new buyback orders.”

</Step>

<Step title="Step 2: Creating an Order">

- “Whenever a customer comes to buy a new phone and wants to sell their old one, open the Cashkr Store App.”
- “Tap on Create Order → Enter the device details (brand, model, storage, and condition).”
- “You’ll instantly see the buyback price on the screen.”

</Step>

<Step title="Step 3: Inspection Process">

- “Our Cashkr associate stationed at your store will inspect the device and confirm the condition.”
- “Once inspection is done, the final value will be confirmed instantly.”

</Step>

<Step title="Step 4: Customer Voucher Process">

- “After the price is confirmed, the customer receives a Cashkr voucher of that amount.”
- “The customer can use the voucher while buying a new phone from your store — they just pay the remaining difference.”

</Step>

<Step title="Step 5: End-of-Day Settlement">

- “At the end of each day, Cashkr will collect all buyback devices from your store.”
- “You’ll receive your store’s total payment summary, and the amount will be settled the same day.”

</Step>

<Step title="Step 6: Support & Tracking">

- “You can view all completed and pending orders in the app.”
- “For any issue or delay, you can contact your assigned Cashkr associate or our support team.”

</Step>

</Steps>

```text
“That’s all you need to do — simple and transparent! We’ll also send you a short training video for your team to refer to anytime. Once you start getting customers, your Cashkr associate will be there to assist on spot.”
```

</Tab>

<Tab title="Hinglish">

## Usage notes

<Callout kind="tip">

Run this training live (in person or on video call) and always complete at least one full order demo in the Cashkr Store App during the session.

</Callout>

- Conduct this training physically or via video call at store.
- Ensure app login and first order demo are completed live.
- Share training video and SOP PDF after session.
- Confirm store readiness in tracker (status: Trained & Active).
