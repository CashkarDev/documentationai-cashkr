## Overview

**Sidebar path:** Dashboards → LIVE → **Overview** (`/dashboard`)

The Overview is the landing page of the admin panel. It gives a quick, live snapshot of platform health for a selected date range: user base, order flow, completion and requote rates, device mix, B2B buybacks and vendor onboarding.

<Callout kind="info">
Overview defaults to **today**. Change the date range to compare a week or a month; every card except **Total Cashkr Users Till Date** follows the selected range.
</Callout>

## What the screen shows

### Header

| Element | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Title + range line** | "Overview" with the active range (for example *Oct 12 – Oct 12, 2026*). |
| **Date picker** | Top-right button showing the selected date. Opens the range picker (see Filters). |

### Users hero card

| Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Cashkr Users Till Date** | All active customers in the system (all-time, not date-filtered). |
| **New Users in Selected Period** | Customer accounts created in the selected range. |

### KPI cards

Each card shows a value and a change indicator against the previous period.

| Card | Sub-label | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Completion %** | Overall Completion % | Share of orders that completed. |
| **Requote %** | Overall Requote % | Share of orders where the vendor revised the price. |
| **Orders Received** | Created | Orders created in the period. |
| **Orders Completed** | Updated | Orders that reached Completed in the period. |
| **Orders Converted** | Leads converted to orders | Leads that became orders. |
| **Failed After Requote** | By vendor | Orders that failed after a vendor requote (highlighted in red). |

### Device orders received

One card per category with count and % change: **Smartphone, Tablet, Laptop, Mac, Console, Watch, Camera, Headphone** Orders Received.

### Device orders completed

Completed counts per category: **Smartphone, Tablet, Laptop, Mac, Console, Watch, Camera, Headphone** Completed.

### Business cards

| Card | Sub-label |
| --- | --- |
| **B2B Buybacks Closed** | No. of successful buybacks |
| **Vendors Onboarded** | No. of vendors onboarded & approved |

## Filters

The date picker opens a panel with:

- **Presets:** Today, Yesterday, This Week, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This Month, Last Month, All Time
- **Start Date / End Date** fields and a month calendar
- **Reset**, **Cancel** and **Apply** buttons

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the date picker" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
Click the date button in the top-right corner.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a range" icon="mouse-pointer-click" title-type="p">
Choose a preset or select start and end dates on the calendar.
</Step>
<Step title="Apply" icon="check" title-type="p">
Click **Apply**. All period-based cards recalculate.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Actions

The Overview is read-only. There are no row or bulk actions. For deeper analysis, use **Leadership**, **Executive** or **Growth** under Dashboards.

<Callout kind="tip">
Use **System → Admin → Live Ticker** to show the same headline counters full-screen on an office TV.
</Callout>
