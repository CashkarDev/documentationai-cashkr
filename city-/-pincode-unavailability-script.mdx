description: Standard CX script for handling unserviceable city or pincode queries, keeping conversations empathetic while capturing leads for future expansion.

<Callout kind="info">

**Used by:** CX Executives / Support Staff  

**Purpose:** Guide the CX team on how to respond when a customer’s city or pincode is not yet serviceable by Cashkr, while maintaining a positive experience and capturing the lead.  

**Objective:** Politely inform the customer about unavailability, reassure them about expansion, and log their details in the Unserviceable Pincode Tracker or Expansion Tracker Sheet for future follow-up.

</Callout>

## Main situations and scripts

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="In-app pincode not available">

**Scenario:** Customer tries to place an order and the app shows `Pincode not available`.

**Suggested response:**

> “I completely understand your concern — it looks like Cashkr hasn’t started pickups in your area yet. We’re expanding city by city, and your location will be covered soon.”

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Customer calls or messages">

**Scenario:** Customer calls or messages to ask why their pincode or city is not available.

**Step 1: Acknowledge and collect pincode**

> “Thank you for checking with us! Right now, Cashkr is operational in selected cities.  
> Could you please share your complete pincode? I’ll check if it’s in our current service list.”

**If the pincode is supported (technical glitch):**

> “Your area is serviceable! It might be a temporary system issue. Please try again after clearing the app cache or share your exact address — I’ll raise this to our technical team.”

**If the pincode is not supported:**

> “Thank you for waiting. At the moment, Cashkr pickups aren’t available in your area.  
> But we’re expanding rapidly — I’ll mark your pincode for priority access once it goes live.”

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Lead capture and ops handoff">

**Lead capture message (CRM input):**

> “Can I please note your name, pincode, and device details? Once your area gets added to our coverage list, you’ll receive an update immediately via SMS or WhatsApp.”

*(CX Executive adds to the “Unserviceable Pincode Tracker” sheet.)*

**Customer assurance (tone control):**

> “We don’t want you to miss out — as soon as service starts in your city, you’ll be among the first to get notified.”  

> “We’re continuously expanding to reach every area in India — your feedback helps us prioritize!”

**If customer asks: “Why not available in my city?”**

> “Our pickup network is expanding phase-wise. To ensure the best service, we onboard verified partners city by city.  
> Your area will be active as soon as a verified Cashkr vendor is onboarded there.”

**Internal SOP (CX → Operations handoff)**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive customer request" icon="users">

Note the customer’s city, pincode, and device type as shared in the conversation.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check live coverage sheet" icon="search">

Check the latest coverage or serviceable areas sheet to confirm if the city or pincode is currently available.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Log unserviceable pincodes" icon="edit">

If the city or pincode is not available, add an entry to the “Unserviceable Pincode Tracker” with customer and device details.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Share with Operations" icon="arrow-right">

Ensure the tracker is shared with the Operations or Vendor Management team weekly for cluster expansion reference.

  </Step>
</Steps>

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Ready-to-use replies">

### WhatsApp / chat reply

> “Hi 👋 Unfortunately, Cashkr pickups aren’t available in your area yet. But we’re expanding quickly, and your pincode \[#######] has been added to our priority list. You’ll be notified once it’s live!”

### Email reply

> “Dear \[Customer Name],  
>  
> Thank you for your interest in Cashkr. Currently, our pickup services are not active in your area \[Pincode: ######].  
>  
> However, your location has been added to our expansion list, and we’ll notify you once we go live in your region.  
>  
> Best regards,  
> Cashkr Support Team”

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Tone and guidelines

<Callout kind="tip">

Use empathetic and positive phrasing:

- Prefer: “Cashkr hasn’t started pickups in your area yet” instead of “We don’t serve your area.”  
- Offer assurance with phrases like “soon expanding”, “priority list”, “you’ll be among the first to know.”  
- Reinforce that their feedback helps Cashkr prioritize new locations.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert">

Follow these rules in every “pincode unavailable” interaction:

- Do not give a fixed go-live date unless Operations has confirmed it.  
- Always log the pincode and customer details in the Unserviceable Pincode Tracker or Expansion Tracker Sheet.  
- Make sure the conversation ends on a positive note with clear reassurance that they will be notified.

</Callout>

## CX team note

All “pincode unavailable” chats or calls must:

- End positively with reassurance about future service.
- Capture customer details in the **Unserviceable Pincode Tracker** or **Expansion Tracker Sheet** so marketing and operations can re-target when new cities go live.
