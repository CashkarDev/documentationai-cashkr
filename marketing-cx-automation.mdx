## Why this matters

<Callout kind="info">

Manually sending every email, social post, or customer reply does not scale. Marketing and CX automation tools let Cashkr run coordinated campaigns, outreach, and support flows at volume while keeping messages relevant and timely for each customer.

</Callout>

## Tools we use

<Card title="Robin Reach" icon="zap">

All-in-one social media management tool with AI-assisted content suggestions and scheduling across platforms.  
**Example:** Schedule Instagram Reels and posts in advance so Robin Reach publishes them automatically at peak engagement times.

</Card>

<Card title="Engine Mailer" icon="mail">

Email platform for marketing campaigns and transactional messages.  
**Example:** Send newsletters to all active customers, and trigger instant order confirmations or payment receipts after successful transactions.

</Card>

<Card title="PushLap Growth" icon="users">

Affiliate and referral tracking software for Cashkr referral programs.  
**Example:** Track when a customer refers a friend, credit the correct account, and calculate referral commissions automatically.

</Card>

<Card title="CXGenie" icon="help-circle">

AI chatbot builder for automated FAQs and basic support queries.  
**Example:** When a customer asks "How can I track my pickup?", the CXGenie bot replies instantly with tracking steps and links, without waiting for a human agent.

</Card>

<Card title="Aimfox" icon="linkedin">

LinkedIn automation tool for lead generation and outreach.  
**Example:** Automate sending connection requests and follow-up messages to corporate prospects for the corporate buyback program.

</Card>

<Card title="PowerIn" icon="message-square">

AI-powered auto-commenting on LinkedIn and Twitter to increase brand visibility.  
**Example:** Add relevant comments on industry posts from target audiences so Cashkr appears consistently in relevant conversations.

</Card>

<Card title="LancePilot" icon="phone">

WhatsApp outreach and follow-up tool for sales campaigns.  
**Example:** Run WhatsApp follow-up sequences to business prospects who showed interest in corporate buyback or paused mid-way in the sales process.

</Card>

<Card title="Satzilio Telecom" icon="phone-call">

Enterprise communication provider for smart voice calls and bulk SMS.  
**Example:** Send OTPs, pickup reminders, and promotional SMS campaigns at scale using templated messages and sender IDs.

</Card>

<Card title="Taku" icon="bar-chart">

Mobile advertising aggregation and optimization platform.  
**Example:** Run and optimize mobile ad campaigns across networks, and measure ROI on spend for different acquisition or remarketing campaigns.

</Card>

<Card title="Acefone" icon="headphones">

Cloud telephony for IVR flows, call routing, and call recording.  
**Example:** When a customer calls the Cashkr helpline, Acefone IVR routes them to the correct support queue and records calls for training and quality checks.

</Card>

<Card title="2Factor.in" icon="shield">

OTP SMS delivery platform for secure account actions.  
**Example:** When a customer logs in or confirms a high-value action, 2Factor.in delivers a one-time password code instantly via SMS.

</Card>

## How we use these daily

- **Marketing team**
  - Plans and schedules social content via Robin Reach.
  - Runs email campaigns and newsletters via Engine Mailer.
  - Manages referral and affiliate programs through PushLap Growth.
  - Optimizes mobile ads and spend using Taku.

- **Sales and outreach team**
  - Drives LinkedIn outreach and follow-ups using Aimfox.
  - Runs auto-commenting and engagement via PowerIn.
  - Sends WhatsApp follow-up campaigns using LancePilot.
  - Uses Acefone and Satzilio Telecom for call and SMS campaigns.

- **CX and support team**
  - Handles FAQs and simple queries with CXGenie chatbots.
  - Manages inbound calls, IVR flows, and recordings through Acefone.
  - Uses 2Factor.in and Satzilio Telecom for OTPs and important notifications.

## Typical marketing and CX automation workflow

<Steps>

  <Step title="Plan campaigns and schedule content" icon="edit">

  Define campaign objectives, target segments, and key messages. Draft and schedule social posts in Robin Reach and set up supporting email journeys in Engine Mailer for the same time window.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Run outbound outreach and referrals" icon="arrow-right">

  Activate outbound outreach via Aimfox and LancePilot for priority segments, and configure referral or affiliate flows in PushLap Growth so new customers are tracked and attributed correctly.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Automate customer support touchpoints" icon="help-circle">

  Configure CXGenie chatbots for common questions, and align Acefone IVR flows and Satzilio Telecom SMS templates with active campaigns so customers get consistent information across channels.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Measure performance and iterate" icon="bar-chart">

  Review campaign performance across email, social, WhatsApp, calls, SMS, and referrals. Use Taku for ad ROI and channel-level metrics, then refine messaging, timing, and targeting for the next cycle.

  </Step>

</Steps>
