---

## Objective

To consistently create engaging, light-hearted office culture reels that showcase the human side of Cashkr — boosting relatability, employer branding, and organic reach across Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube Shorts.

## Roles & Responsibilities

| Role                       | Responsibility                                 |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Social Media Executive** | Plans, records, and edits reels                |
| **Marketing Manager**      | Reviews and approves final reel before posting |

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1: Concept Planning">
    - Social Media Executive creates a content idea bank:
      - Office fun moments (games, pranks, jokes)
      - Employee spotlight / birthday / work anniversaries
      - Reaction trends / team memes
      - Day-in-the-life or team intros

    - Optional formats:
      - POV shots
      - Lip-sync with trending audio
      - Quick Q&amp;A or reaction clips
      - "Expectation vs. Reality" fun formats

    - Approval for unusual/experimental concepts to be taken from Marketing Manager
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 2: Shoot Raw Footage">
    - Use a decent phone camera (stabilized)
    - Record in vertical format (9:16)
    - Keep clips short: 5–10 sec shots
    - Ensure:
      - Good lighting
      - No personal/confidential content
      - Team is comfortable being on camera (take verbal OK)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 3: Edit Reel (Canva or Instagram)">
    | Option                 | Notes                                                 |
    | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
    | **Canva Pro**          | Use branded templates, add captions, trim clips       |
    | **Instagram (in-app)** | Add trending audio, use native transitions & stickers |

    - Add:
      - Subtitles or captions (if people are speaking)
      - On-screen emojis/text stickers (light tone only)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 4: Review & Approval">
    - Share draft (via Canva or upload on google drive)
    - Marketing Manager reviews and provides feedback (1 round max)
    - Make final edits and prepare for posting
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 5: Platform-Specific Posting">
    | Platform            | Posting Notes                                            |
    | ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
    | **Instagram Reels** | Add 10–15 relevant hashtags (#OfficeLife #CashkrCulture) |
    | **Facebook Reels**  | Same video; tweak caption to be more descriptive         |
    | **YouTube Shorts**  | Upload via mobile; use 1-line title + hashtags           |

    Post Times: Match with engagement windows — 12 PM or 6 PM recommended.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for Consistency

| ✅ DO                                           | ❌ DON’T                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Keep reels under 30 seconds                    | Use private company info or desks with personal data |
| Add light music / trending audio               | Use filters that distract from message               |
| Involve different teams                        | Repeat same people in every video                    |
| Include CTA: “Tag your teammate”, “Relatable?” | Post without subtitles if there’s dialogue           |
