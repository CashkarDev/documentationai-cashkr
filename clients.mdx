## Overview

**Sidebar path:** Channels → Buyback B2B → Clients (`/buyback-b2b/clients`). The page header reads **B2B Clients — Manage corporate clients and their buyback agreements**.

The Clients page in the Buyback B2B section is the primary workspace for the Sales Team to manage ongoing relationships with corporate accounts that have been converted from leads.

Use this page to see all active corporate clients, maintain communication history, create new deals when clients want to sell devices, and request payments once deals are approved.

## Summary cards

Four summary cards at the top of the page give a snapshot of the current corporate client base:

- **Total Clients** — Total number of registered corporate clients.
- **VIP Clients** — Clients marked as high-value or high-priority accounts.
- **At Risk** — Clients flagged as at risk of disengagement and needing immediate relationship attention.
- **Follow-ups Due** — Clients with a follow-up scheduled for today or overdue.

## Client table

Each row in the clients table represents one corporate client and shows the key details needed for daily sales work:

- **Company** — Name of the corporate company, with its GSTIN below.
- **Contact** — Primary contact person and phone.
- **Relationship** — Relationship status badge: Active, VIP, Dormant or At Risk.
- **Assigned To** — Sales executive responsible for managing this client.
- **Follow-up** — Scheduled date for the next client interaction.
- **Deals** — Number of deals associated with this client.
- **Action** — **View** opens the client detail page.

## Search & filters

- **Search clients...** — search by company or contact.
- **Status filter** — **All Statuses**, **Active**, **VIP**, **Dormant**, **At Risk**.

## Client detail page

Click **View** to open `/buyback-b2b/clients/<client-id>`:

- **Header** — company name, relationship badge, city and **Total Buybacks**.
- **Activity composer** — tabs **Call**, **Email**, **Meeting**, **Note**; type the details and click **Log**. Entries appear in the **Activity Timeline**.
- **Buyback Deals** — counts of **Active**, **Completed** and **Total Value**, with tabs **Active**, **Completed**, **Cancelled** listing the client's deals. **+ New Deal** opens **Create Buyback Request** with **Subject** (e.g. `Q1 Office Renewal`), **Description** and **Notes**; click **Create Request** to create the deal.
- **Contact** — contact name, phone, email and designation.
- **Company** — GST number and address.
- **CRM** — **Assigned To**, **Next Follow-up**, **Last Contact**. Click **Edit** to change **Assigned To**, **Next Follow-up** and **Relationship Status**, then **Save** (or **Cancel**).
- **Documents** — upload KYC documents: **Authorization Letter** (Board Resolution or Letter of Authority on company letterhead), **GST / PAN Certificate**, **Bank Account Proof** (cancelled cheque or bank-attested letter).

## How clients are added

Clients are created automatically when a lead is converted in the Leads page of the Buyback B2B module.

The new client record preserves all lead history and notes so the full context of the relationship is available to the Sales Team.

A client record is also created automatically when you record a **Direct Buyback** from the Deals page, so one-off sellers appear in the Clients list. There is no manual "Add Client" button on this page.

## Sales workflow on the Clients page

Once a lead is converted and a client record exists, the Sales Team manages the entire ongoing relationship from the Clients page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log all client communications" icon="edit">
    Record every interaction with the client, including calls, emails, and meetings, directly on the client record. This builds a complete communication history that any team member can reference before the next touchpoint.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a deal when the client wants to sell devices" icon="plus">
    When an existing client wants to sell a batch of devices, create a new deal from the client record. The deal appears in the Deals page, where the pricing team reviews and manages pricing and approval.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Request payment after deal approval" icon="upload">
    After the client approves the final quote and submits their GST invoice, payment is requested from Finance (logged on the deal as **Sent to Finance**). The payment then appears in **Finance → B2B Buyback** for the finance team to process.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Manage and complete follow-ups" icon="check-circle">
    Schedule follow-up dates on client records to ensure no client goes uncontacted for too long. Use the Follow-ups Due summary card to see which clients need calls or emails today and to clear overdue follow-ups.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Client status management

Use client status controls to keep the portfolio accurate and highlight accounts that need extra attention:

Use **CRM → Edit** on the client detail page to:

- Set **Relationship Status** to **VIP** for high-volume or strategically important accounts.
- Set it to **At Risk** when the client shows signs of disengagement, or **Dormant** when there has been no activity for a long time.
- Change **Assigned To** when accounts are redistributed, and set the **Next Follow-up** date.

## Connections to other modules

The Clients page is tightly connected to other Buyback and Finance modules:

- **Buyback B2B — Leads** — All clients originate from converted leads in the Leads pipeline.
- **Buyback B2B — Deals** — Deals created from the client record appear in the Deals page for the pricing team to manage.
- **Finance — B2B Payments** — Payment requests made from the Clients page create payment records in the B2B Payments page for the finance team.
- **Buyback B2B — Inspection** — Deals that reach the inspection stage are tracked in the Inspection page.

## Important notes and best practices

<Callout kind="info">
  Use the Clients page as the single source of truth for all ongoing B2B relationships. Log every communication, create all deals, and trigger every payment request from this page so history and ownership are always clear.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert">
  Review At Risk clients immediately. Assign a senior sales executive if needed to reconnect with the client and understand the reason for the risk before the relationship breaks down and future deals are lost.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Check the Follow-ups Due summary card every morning and clear overdue follow-ups the same day. Missed or delayed follow-ups directly impact conversion, client satisfaction, and repeat deal volume.
</Callout>
