## How we show up as a team

The Team Manifesto outlines how we treat each other, how we show up, and what we believe makes a world-class team.

<Callout kind="info">

These principles apply to everyone at Cashkr — from founder to intern. Use them to guide day-to-day decisions, feedback, hiring, and how we work together.

</Callout>

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="1. Act like owners" default-open="true">

We do not wait to be told what to do. If we see something broken, we fix it. If we make a mistake, we own it.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="2. Prioritize the mission">

Every decision — big or small — must move us closer to becoming India’s most trusted buyback infrastructure.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="3. Move fast with context">

Speed is power. But we never rush blindly — we move fast because we are informed and aligned.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="4. Respect each other’s time">

We come prepared. We communicate clearly. We minimize meetings. We use async tools well. No time-wasters.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="5. Be direct, but kind">

No politics, no sugarcoating. We give feedback honestly, respectfully, and with the intent to grow.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="6. Learn loudly">

We share what we learn — wins, mistakes, insights — so others can learn too. Documentation > memory.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="7. Celebrate progress, not just results">

We recognize effort, not just success. Even small steps forward are acknowledged and shared.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="8. Do not hide problems">

If something is going wrong — raise it. Hiding it or hoping it goes away only makes it worse.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="9. Trust until proven otherwise">

We assume good intent. Trust is the default — but we also hold each other accountable.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="10. Win together">

No silos. No heroes. If one team fails, we all fail. If one team wins, we all win.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>
