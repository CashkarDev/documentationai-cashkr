## Purpose and audience

Use this SOP to improve Lead to Order Conversion % using data, not assumptions. It aligns Inbound Sales, Follow-Up, CX, BD, Ops, and Management on one measurable funnel.

Teams use lead, call, disposition, order, and city insights from the Admin Panel and reports to find drop-offs, fix root causes, and track impact week over week.

## Quick summary

- Focus on Lead to Order Conversion % as the single primary metric.
- Use Admin Panel data, call notes, exports, and BD coverage data as the source of truth.
- Break the funnel into stages and fix the biggest drop-off first.
- For Sales, improve Connected %, Callback Success %, objection handling, and High-End conversions.
- Use CX and Ops data to remove post-order friction that damages future conversions.
- Run a weekly review to track metrics, decide actions, and assign clear owners.

## Primary conversion metric

<Callout kind="info">

**Primary metric: Lead to Order Conversion %**

Use this as the main indicator of funnel health and process discipline.

**Formula**

```text
Lead to Order Conversion % = (Number of orders created ÷ Total leads) × 100
```

</Callout>

Supporting metrics that help explain conversion:

- Connected %
- Callback Success %
- High-End Conversion %
- City-wise Conversion %
- Device-wise Conversion %

## Core data sources

Rely on these data sources instead of intuition:

### Admin Panel

- Leads: all, not called, callbacks, high-ends.
- Dispositions.
- Orders created.
- SLA and pickup data.

### Call notes and dispositions

- Objection reasons.
- Callback patterns.
- Drop-off reasons.

### Reports and exports

- City-wise performance.
- Device-type performance.
- Non-serviceable leads.
- Pickup failures.

### BD and coverage data

- Pincode availability.
- Vendor shortage areas.

## Conversion funnel breakdown

Track every lead through the same funnel and measure drop-offs at each stage:

```text
1. Lead created
2. Contacted
3. Connected
4. Intent confirmed
5. Order created
```

Each stage has a specific data signal and a corresponding operational fix.

## Role-based responsibilities and levers

Use these role views to know what data to watch and what actions to take.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Sales" icon="bar-chart">

  Sales focuses on contact discipline, callbacks, objection handling, and high-intent leads, using Admin Panel and call data.

  ### Improve Connected %

  **Data to track**

  - Not Connected %.
  - Time to first call after lead creation.
  - Call attempt count per lead per day.

  **Actions**

  - Call the lead on the same day it is created.
  - Use proven best-time calling slots for each city.
  - Make a minimum of 3 call attempts per day before marking as not reachable.

  ### Improve Callback Success %

  **Data to track**

  - Callbacks scheduled vs callbacks completed.
  - Missed callback rate.
  - Leads with repeated callback dispositions.

  **Actions**

  - Call at the exact promised callback time.
  - Avoid vague callback timings; agree on a specific slot.
  - Use WhatsApp reminders or messages before high-value callbacks.

  ### Fix price objection drop-offs

  **Data to track**

  - Leads in "Waiting for quote" or similar dispositions.
  - Price objection notes in call logs.
  - Conversion rate of leads with price-related objections.

  **Actions**

  - Explain how pricing and inspection logic works in clear language.
  - Match competitor quotes where policy allows.
  - Use standard objection-handling scripts, not ad-hoc promises.

  ### Improve High-End Conversion

  **Data to track**

  - Count of High-End leads vs High-End orders.
  - Agent-wise performance on High-End leads.
  - Time from lead creation to first contact for High-End leads.

  **Actions**

  - Route High-End leads to senior or specialized agents.
  - Prioritize faster callbacks for High-End and high-intent leads.
  - Pitch priority pickup and service benefits clearly.

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="CX" icon="users">

  CX uses post-order data to remove friction that hurts trust and future conversions.

  ### Data used

  - Failed orders and failure reasons.
  - Customer complaints and CSAT feedback.
  - Pickup delay and reschedule patterns.

  ### Impact on conversion

  - Fewer failures and complaints increase customer trust.
  - Better experiences drive repeat orders and referrals.
  - Clean communication reduces cancellations and rework that waste leads.

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="BD" icon="briefcase">

  BD uses coverage and demand data to ensure leads can actually convert into orders.

  ### Key BD data

  - Non-serviceable leads by city and pincode.
  - City-wise and pincode-wise demand.
  - Vendor shortage zones and SLA breaches.

  ### Actions

  - Expand into high-demand pincodes and cities.
  - Strengthen vendor supply where pickup failures are frequent.
  - Reduce "Pickup not available" and "Non-serviceable" closures.

  ✔ More coverage and reliable vendors directly increase conversion potential.

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Ops" icon="settings">

  Ops ensures pickups and field operations support the promises made during sales.

  ### Data used

  - Pickup SLA adherence (on-time vs delayed).
  - Pickup failures and reasons.
  - Reschedule and no-show patterns.

  ### Responsibilities

  - Maintain high on-time pickup rates.
  - Reduce operational failures that cause order cancellations.
  - Coordinate with BD for capacity in high-demand or high-failure areas.

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Management" icon="monitor">

  Management uses aggregated trends to guide training, coverage, and process changes.

  ### Data reviewed

  - Daily and weekly Lead to Order Conversion %.
  - City-wise and device-wise performance heatmaps.
  - Agent-wise performance and consistency.
  - Week-on-week improvement on core sub-metrics.

  ### Decisions made

  - Training topics and coaching focus by team or agent.
  - Process or script changes to reduce repeated issues.
  - Coverage expansion or vendor changes by geography.
  - Campaign and channel optimization based on quality and conversion.

  Management focuses on trend correction and enablement, not micromanaging individual leads.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Weekly conversion improvement review

Review conversion data every week across Sales, CX, BD, Ops, and Management.

### Metrics and questions

| Metric       | Question to ask                |
| ------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Connected %  | Are we calling fast enough?    |
| Callback %   | Are callbacks respected?       |
| Conversion % | Where are we losing intent?    |
| City-wise    | Coverage issue or sales issue? |
| Device-wise  | Pricing or trust issue?        |

### How to run the weekly review

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare data" icon="database">

  - Export weekly data for leads, calls, dispositions, and orders from the Admin Panel.
  - Pull city-wise, device-wise, and High-End performance reports.
  - Share a single dashboard or sheet with all stakeholders before the meeting.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Review funnel drop-offs" icon="bar-chart">

  - Check conversion at each funnel stage: created, contacted, connected, intent confirmed, order created.
  - Identify the largest drop-offs by city, device type, and lead segment.
  - Highlight repeat issues from previous weeks that are still open.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Decide actions" icon="settings">

  - Agree on 2–3 focused actions for the coming week (for example, calling discipline, callback handling, specific objection scripts, coverage fixes).
  - Decide which levers belong to Sales, CX, BD, Ops, or Management.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign owners and timelines" icon="users">

  - Assign a clear owner and due date for each action.
  - Define how success will be measured (metric and expected movement).
  - Add actions to the team's tracking sheet and review status in the next weekly meeting.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common conversion killers

<Callout kind="alert">

These patterns repeatedly reduce Lead to Order Conversion % and are visible in the data.

- Delayed first call after lead creation.
- Missed or repeatedly rescheduled callbacks.
- Wrong or incomplete dispositions.
- Over-promising pickup times or prices that cannot be honored.
- Large volume of non-serviceable area leads without BD follow-up.
- Poor or missing call notes that block follow-up quality.

Each issue has a clear data signal. When you ignore the data, you lose conversions.

</Callout>

## One-page training card summary

Use this as a quick reference during training and reviews.

**Conversion % improves when:**

- Calls happen fast, especially the first contact.
- Callbacks are scheduled clearly and always respected.
- Coverage and vendor availability match demand.
- Objections and price concerns are recorded and handled using standard scripts.
- Data from Admin Panel, reports, and CX is reviewed every week.
- Actions from the weekly review have clear owners and timelines.

**Golden rule**

📊 What gets tracked gets improved.
