## Overview

**Sidebar:** Vendors → Roster → **Onboarding** (`/onboarding-b2c`). Page title: **Vendor Onboarding B2C** — "Manage vendor leads from scraped data".

The page holds the pipeline of potential B2C vendors sourced from scraped or uploaded business data. The team calls vendor leads, records outcomes, schedules follow-ups and moves interested businesses towards registering in the vendor app.

It works together with [New Vendors - B2C](/new-vendors---b2c) and [Vendors - B2C](/b2c-vendors) to take a lead from raw data to a live vendor account.

## Scope switch

At the top right, choose whose leads to show:

- **Mine** — leads assigned to you.
- **All** — every lead (default).
- **Unassigned** — leads not yet assigned to anyone.

## What the lead list shows

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Business Name | Business name and lead source (for example **Manual Upload**). |
| Contact | Business phone number. |
| City | City of the business. |
| Device Type | Device type from the upload ("-" if not provided). |
| Address | Business address (truncated). |
| Status | Current lead status. |
| Calls | Number of call attempts. |
| Follow-up | Scheduled follow-up date, if any. |
| Added | Date the lead was added. |
| Actions | Call button and a **⋯** menu. |

## Lead statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| New | Added, not yet contacted. |
| Contacted | Reached at least once, outcome not yet final. |
| Follow-up | Asked to be called back later. |
| Interested | Wants to onboard as a vendor. |
| Not Interested | Declined to join. |
| Converted | Registered and onboarded as a vendor. |
| Invalid | Wrong / unreachable contact details or closed business. |
| Duplicate | Same business already exists as another lead. |

## Search and filters

- **Search** — by business name, phone or city; click **Search** to apply.
- **All Statuses** — filter by any status above.
- **All Cities** — filter by city.
- **All Devices** — Mobile, Tablet, Laptop, Mac, Camera, Headphone, Console, Drone, TV, AC, Desktop, Watch.

## Row actions

- **Call** — phone icon to call the lead.
- **⋯ menu**:
  - **Update Status** — opens **Update Lead** with **Status**, **Disposition** and **Add Note** ("Add a quick note about this call…"), then Save.
  - **View Details**
  - **Mark Interested**
  - **Schedule Follow-up**
  - **Mark Not Interested**

### Dispositions

Available in the Update Lead dialog:

- Interested - Will share documents
- Interested - Needs more info
- Call back later
- Not interested - Already has partner
- Not interested - Not selling phones
- Wrong number
- Not reachable
- Busy - Call again
- Out of service area
- Closed business

## Uploading vendor leads

Click **Upload Vendor List** (top right) to bulk-add leads.

- Upload a **CSV** with columns: **Business Name, Phone Number, Address, City, Device Type**.
- **Sample CSV** downloads a template ("Need the format? Download and fill in the sample CSV").
- **Data Source** — defaults to **Manual Upload**; this becomes the source tag on each lead.
- City and Device Type are read directly from the CSV columns.
- Drag and drop the file or browse, then click **Upload**.

Uploaded leads appear in **New** and **All Leads** with the source tag.

## Vendor onboarding funnel flow

<Steps>

<Step title="Upload and create leads" icon="upload">

Upload a CSV with **Upload Vendor List**. Leads land in **New** with a Manual Upload tag.

</Step>

<Step title="Call new leads and update status" icon="phone">

Call each lead from the row, then use **Update Status** to set the status, a disposition and a note.

</Step>

<Step title="Manage follow-ups" icon="clock">

Use **Schedule Follow-up** for leads who ask to be called later and work the **Follow-ups** tab daily.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm interest and share app link" icon="arrow-right">

Use **Mark Interested** when a lead agrees, then share the vendor app link and guide them through registration.

</Step>

<Step title="Review in New Applications" icon="info">

After registering in the app, the vendor appears in [New Vendors - B2C](/new-vendors---b2c) with Pending status for review.

</Step>

<Step title="Accept and go live" icon="check-circle">

Once accepted and configured, the vendor is managed in [Vendors - B2C](/b2c-vendors) and the lead counts toward **Converted**.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Important notes

<Callout kind="info">

- Interest alone does not convert a lead — the vendor must register in the app and be accepted in New Applications.
- Not Interested, Invalid and Duplicate leads stay visible in **All Leads**.
- Use the **Calls** count to avoid over-calling the same business.
- Always pick a disposition and add a short note when updating a lead so the next caller has context.

</Callout>
