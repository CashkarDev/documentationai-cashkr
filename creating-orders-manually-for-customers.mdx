## Overview

Manual order creation helps Inbound Sales, Follow-Up, and CX teams assist customers who cannot complete the flow themselves. This SOP defines when manual orders are allowed, the exact Admin Panel flow to follow, and the validations required to avoid failures, SLA issues, and customer complaints.

<Callout kind="info">

**Rule:** Manual creation is a support tool to assist customers, not a shortcut to push orders.

</Callout>

---

## 1. When manual order creation is allowed or blocked

<Callout kind="tip">

**When manual order creation is allowed**

Create an order manually only when:

- The customer explicitly asks for help.
- The customer faces technical issues in the app or website.
- The customer is not comfortable using the app.
- A high-end device (for example, iPhone or MacBook) needs assisted conversion.
- The customer is on a call and clearly confirms all details verbally.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert">

**When manual order creation is not allowed**

Do not create an order when:

- The pincode is not serviceable.
- The device is not supported.
- The customer is unsure or only "just checking".
- Price or condition is not confirmed.
- The customer has not given clear consent.

**Golden Rule:**  
If the customer has not said "Yes, please place the order", do not create it.

</Callout>

---

## 2. Mandatory pre-checks before creating an order

Confirm all of the following before you touch the Admin Panel.

### Serviceability

- Verify the customer's city and pincode are correct.
- Confirm the device type is serviceable for that location.

### Device details

- Confirm the brand, model, and variant with the customer.
- Select the correct storage or configuration.
- Select the correct condition based on the customer's responses.
- Note any accessories mentioned (if applicable).

### Price confirmation

- Explain inspection-based pricing and that the final price depends on physical inspection.
- Confirm the customer agrees to the estimated price range shown.

### Pickup details

- Capture the full pickup address.
- Record a clear landmark.
- Confirm the preferred pickup time slot.

### Payment method

- Confirm the customer has a working UPI or bank account ready for payment at pickup.

## 3. Manual order creation flow in the Admin Panel

<Steps>

<Step title="Open lead or customer profile" icon="folder">

- Go to **Admin Panel → Leads → Select lead**.
- If no lead exists, create one from the Customer section before proceeding.

</Step>

<Step title="Click Create Order" icon="play-circle">

- Click **Create Order** on the lead profile.
- This converts the lead into an order record.

</Step>

<Step title="Fill device details" icon="settings">

- Select device type (Phone, Laptop, Tablet, Mac).
- Enter brand and model.
- Choose the correct storage or variant.
- Select the appropriate condition fields.

<Callout kind="alert">

Accuracy in device details and condition is critical. Wrong entries cause pricing issues, pickup failures, and disputes later.

</Callout>

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm estimated price" icon="info">

- Let the system auto-calculate the estimated price.
- Read out the price and confirm verbally with the customer.

Use this script:

> "The estimated price showing is ₹_____. Final price depends on inspection. Are you okay to proceed?"

Proceed only after a clear "yes" from the customer.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter pickup details" icon="home">

- Enter the full address.
- Confirm and enter the correct pincode.
- Add a clear landmark.
- Select the customer's preferred pickup slot.

</Step>

<Step title="Review order summary with customer" icon="search">

Quickly recap the key details with the customer:

- Device (brand, model, variant, condition).
- Estimated price range.
- Pickup location and time slot.
- Payment mode (UPI or bank transfer).

Get final confirmation before you create the order.

</Step>

<Step title="Create order" icon="check-circle">

- Click **Confirm** or **Create Order**.
- Wait for the system to generate the Order ID.
- Note the Order ID for the next steps.

</Step>

</Steps>

---

## 4. After order creation

After the order is successfully created, complete all of these actions.

- Inform the customer that their order has been placed.
- Share the Order ID verbally and, where possible, via SMS or WhatsApp.
- Explain the next steps:
  - Vendor assignment.
  - Pickup and on-spot inspection.
  - Instant payment at pickup via UPI or bank transfer.
- Update the lead disposition to **Create Order** in the system.

---

## 5. Common errors to avoid

<Callout kind="alert">

**Avoid these mistakes when creating manual orders**

- Selecting the wrong device model or variant.
- Choosing the wrong condition based on customer inputs.
- Entering an incorrect pincode or address.
- Creating an order without clear customer consent.
- Over-promising the final price instead of explaining inspection-based pricing.
- Missing key address details or landmarks.

</Callout>

These errors lead to:

- Pickup failures and wasted vendor trips.
- Price disputes at pickup.
- SLA violations and escalations.
- Complaints from vendors and customers.

---

## 6. Handling price disputes

If the customer later says:

> "I did not agree to this price"

Follow this action flow:

- Check call notes and previous communication.
- Re-explain the inspection-based pricing logic calmly.
- Offer cancellation if the customer is not comfortable with the pricing outcome.

<Callout kind="info">

Do not argue with the customer. Focus on clarifying, resolving confusion, and offering a clear next step.

</Callout>

---

## 7. Accountability

- The Sales or CX agent who creates the manual order is responsible for the accuracy of all entered data.
- Repeated wrong or disputed orders may lead to coaching, retraining, or restrictions on manual order creation access.

## 8. Quick summary card

<Callout kind="tip">

**When to use manual orders**

- Customer asks for help or faces technical issues.
- Customer is not comfortable completing the flow alone.
- High-intent or high-value device needs assisted conversion.

**Before creating**

- Serviceability checked.
- Device details and condition confirmed.
- Estimated price explained and agreed.
- Full address, pincode, and pickup slot captured.
- Payment method confirmed.

**After creating**

- Share Order ID with the customer.
- Explain pickup, inspection, and instant payment.
- Update lead disposition to **Create Order**.

**Goal**

Assist customers who want to sell, never force or push orders.

</Callout>
