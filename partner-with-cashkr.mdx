## Overview

The Partner with Cashkr page explains the vendor program, lets potential vendors access the partner app on the Play Store, and guides them into the onboarding process to become verified vendors.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Vendor accounts become verified only after an admin reviews and approves the onboarding form submitted from the vendor app.
</Callout>

## Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="User lands on Partner with Cashkr page" icon="home" title-type="p">
    The user opens the Partner with Cashkr page from the website header or footer navigation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="User reads vendor program details" icon="book-open" title-type="p">
    The page shows a short explanation of the vendor program so the user understands who should apply and what to expect.
  </Step>

  <Step title="User downloads the partner app" icon="download" title-type="p">
    The page links to the partner app on the Play Store so the user can install it on their device.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Vendor completes onboarding in the app" icon="terminal" title-type="p">
    After installing the partner app, the vendor fills out the vendor onboarding form inside the app and submits it for review.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Admin reviews and verifies vendor" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    An admin reviews the submitted onboarding details. If the admin approves the request, the vendor becomes a verified Cashkr vendor.
  </Step>
</Steps>
