## Overview

Vendor Operations and Support teams work with two separate balances in the Cashkr Vendor App: a **wallet** with real money for customer payments and **credits** as virtual commission points for unlocking orders.

Both systems work together so that vendors can pay customers at pickup while Cashkr collects commission upfront through credits. Unlocking an order always depends on both balances.

<Callout kind="info">

**Who is this for:** Vendor Operations, Vendor Support, Finance, and Product teams handling vendor queries, training, or refunds related to wallet and credits.

</Callout>

---

## Wallet: real money balance

### What the wallet is

The wallet is a real-money balance linked to a virtual bank account in Cashkr’s banking setup.

- Each vendor has a mapped **virtual account**.
- When the vendor deposits money, the bank sends an **auto-update**.
- The wallet balance in the Vendor App updates almost instantly based on this bank notification.

### How the wallet is funded

Vendors add money to the wallet by depositing into their assigned virtual account.

<Callout kind="tip">

Treat the wallet as **pre-loaded payout money**. Vendors must keep enough balance here to pay customers when orders complete successfully.

</Callout>

Key points about funding:

- Top-ups happen via **bank deposit into the virtual account**.
- There is **no manual admin action** needed to reflect the top-up once the bank reports it.
- Cashkr does **not charge extra fees** for adding money to the wallet.

If you need to refer to any minimum or recommended top-up levels in training, align with the latest internal policy rather than quoting fixed numbers here.

### What the wallet is used for

The wallet is used only for **paying customers** when an order is successfully picked up and closed.

The system checks or uses wallet balance in three main moments:

- When the vendor tries to **unlock an order** (to confirm they can afford the payout).
- When the vendor **pays the customer** at pickup via OTP.
- When **admin processes a wallet refund or adjustment** after order failures or corrections.

### Key wallet rules

<Callout kind="alert">

- Wallet balance is **real money** belonging to the vendor.
- Vendors **cannot withdraw** wallet balance themselves. Withdrawal is allowed **only during account closure** and is handled by admin.
- Use wallet **only** to pay customers for Cashkr orders, not for any other transfers.

</Callout>

## Credits: virtual commission points

### What credits are

Credits are **non-money digital points** that vendors spend to unlock order details in the Cashkr Partner Portal.

- Credits represent the **commission paid by the vendor to Cashkr** for taking that order.
- Credits are **deducted**, not earned. Vendors spend them to unlock orders.

### How credits are added

Credits are added to the vendor account through the usual **Buy Credits** flow in the Vendor App.

At any point:

- The current credit balance is **stored on the vendor account**.
- Vendors can **view credits in the app**.
- Internal teams can review balances through the admin panel.

### What credits are used for

Vendors use credits to **unlock order details** such as:

- Customer name and contact details.
- Device model and configuration.
- Offered price and inspection checklist.
- Pickup address and time window.

Once an order is unlocked:

- Credits are **deducted instantly**.
- Only **one vendor** gets the unlock (first come, first served).

### Key credit rules

<Callout kind="info">

- Vendors **do not earn** credits from completing orders; they **only spend** credits to unlock them.
- Credits **cannot be transferred** between vendor accounts.
- Credits are **not convertible to cash or wallet balance**.

</Callout>

Credits are typically tied to order type and value. For example:

- Higher-value devices (e.g., recent iPhones or MacBooks) generally require **more credits**.
- Lower-value or budget devices require **fewer credits**.

Exact credit values per device are managed by the **Pricing team** and may change.

## How unlock eligibility is checked

When a vendor taps **Unlock** on an order, the system validates both credits and wallet before granting access.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Check credits" icon="info">

  - System checks whether the vendor has **enough credits** required for that specific order.
  - If credits are **insufficient**, the unlock fails and the vendor must **buy more credits** or choose a different order.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Deduct credits" icon="minus">

  - If credits are sufficient, the system **immediately deducts** the required credits from the vendor account.
  - This deduction happens **before** vendor sees full customer details.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Check wallet balance" icon="info">

  - After credit deduction, the system checks the vendor’s **wallet balance**.
  - Wallet must be high enough to cover the **expected customer payout** after inspection.
  - If wallet is **insufficient**, the unlock attempt fails, and the system does **not** proceed to full unlock.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Unlock successful" icon="check-circle">

  - If both credits and wallet pass checks, the order is **unlocked** for that vendor.
  - Vendor now sees **customer details, device model, quoted price, address, and inspection checklist**.
  - Only **one vendor** can unlock a given order. Once unlocked, it is no longer available to others.

  </Step>

</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">

When a vendor cannot unlock, always check **both**: credit balance and wallet balance, and whether the vendor is on hold or blocked.

</Callout>

## Customer payment flow (wallet → OTP)

Customer payments flow from the vendor wallet to the customer’s bank account using an OTP verification step.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Inspect device and finalize price" icon="info">

  - Vendor visits the customer and **inspects the device** using the app checklist.
  - The app shows the **final price** after any requote or condition updates.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Initiate payment from wallet" icon="play-circle">

  - Vendor taps **Pay Now** in the Vendor App.
  - The system prepares a **wallet → customer bank** transfer for the final price amount.

  </Step>

  <Step title="OTP sent to customer" icon="terminal">

  - Customer receives an **OTP via SMS** on the registered mobile number.
  - Vendor must ask the customer to **share the OTP** only after confirming the amount shown.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Vendor enters OTP and confirms" icon="keyboard">

  - Vendor enters the OTP into the app.
  - On correct OTP entry, the system triggers the **wallet debit** and payment to the customer’s bank account.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Payment completion and traceability" icon="check-circle">

  - Payment is processed instantly **from wallet to customer bank**.
  - The transaction is **logged in vendor app, admin panel, and bank virtual account**.
  - Support and finance teams can use these logs to investigate any disputes.

  </Step>

</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">

- **No manual approval** is needed once OTP is verified.
- Always confirm that the OTP is **received by and shared from the customer** only.
- If the order still shows pending after payment, use logs in the **admin panel** and bank statements to confirm the transaction.

</Callout>

## Refunds and reversals (admin-handled)

Refunds and balance corrections for vendors are handled **inside the Cashkr admin panel**, not by sending money back from the bank to the vendor directly.

Two main components can be adjusted:

- **Credits** that were deducted to unlock the order.
- **Wallet** amounts that were debited to pay the customer.

### Wallet refunds (vendor wallet refunds)

Vendor wallet refunds apply when **vendors paid customers from their wallet** but the order is later **reversed or corrected** and the vendor balance must be restored.

Typical triggers for wallet refunds:

- Order failed after payment due to **quality issues, documentation problems, or system errors**.
- Payment went through, but the order needed to be **cancelled or rolled back** by Cashkr.
- Any mischarge or duplicate debit that needs correction.

When you process a wallet refund from admin:

- Cashkr adjusts the **wallet balance back up**.
- There is **no direct bank transfer** back to the vendor for this; it is an in-system wallet correction.

### Credit reversals (vendor credit reversals)

Vendor credit reversals apply when **credits were deducted to unlock an order** and those credits must be returned.

Typical triggers for credit reversals:

- Order could not proceed **for reasons not under vendor control** (system issue, wrong listing, customer unavailability at the start).
- Order was mistakenly unlocked due to **internal error** and quickly cancelled from the Cashkr side.
- Any training or test case where vendor credits should not be consumed.

When you process a credit reversal from admin:

- Credits are **added back** to the vendor’s credit balance.
- No wallet or bank movement is involved.

### Combined refunds (credits + wallet)

In many failure or cancellation scenarios, you may need to restore **both** credits and wallet.

Typical combined case:

- Vendor unlocked an order (**credits deducted**).
- Vendor visited and paid the customer (**wallet debited**).
- Order was later **failed or cancelled** due to process, pricing, or system reasons where vendor should not lose either commission or payout.

In such cases, an admin should:

1. Review order timeline and logs (unlock, OTP payment, status changes).
2. Decide whether to apply:
   - **Only credit reversal**, or
   - **Only wallet refund**, or
   - **Both** credit reversal and wallet refund.
3. Apply the necessary adjustments in the **admin panel** for that order.

<Callout kind="info">

Always document the **reason** for any refund or reversal in the admin notes. This helps with audits and handling future vendor disputes.

</Callout>

## Common scenarios (support checklist)

Use this section as a quick checklist when handling vendor tickets or calls.

### Scenario 1: Vendor says “Credits deducted but order failed”

- Check the **order history and status** in admin.
- Confirm whether the vendor **unlocked only**, or also **paid the customer**.
- Decide refund type:
  - If only unlock happened → **credit reversal**.
  - If unlock + payment happened and order was reversed → **credit reversal + wallet refund** as per policy.
- Process the necessary refund(s) in admin.
- Update the vendor with:
  - What was reversed (credits, wallet, or both).
  - Approximate time for balances to reflect in their app.

### Scenario 2: Vendor cannot unlock an order

- Check **credit balance**.
- Check **wallet balance** against expected customer payout.
- Check if vendor is **blocked, on hold, or limited** in any way.
- If balances are low:
  - Ask vendor to **buy credits** if credits are insufficient.
  - Ask vendor to **top up wallet** if wallet is insufficient.
- If balances and status look fine, flag to tech/product as a potential **system issue**.

### Scenario 3: Vendor paid customer but order still shows pending

- Ask vendor to confirm:
  - **OTP was received by the customer**.
  - **Correct OTP** was entered.
  - Any **error message** seen on the app.
- In admin panel:
  - Check **wallet transaction logs** for that order.
  - Confirm whether the **wallet debit** occurred.
- If wallet was debited and customer received money but order status did not update:
  - Manually **update order status** as per policy.
  - Ensure future issues are flagged to tech for **status sync checks**.

### Scenario 4: Vendor wants to withdraw wallet money

- Explain that:
  - Wallet money is meant for **customer payouts only**.
  - Vendors **cannot withdraw** wallet balance on demand.
- If the vendor wants to stop working with Cashkr:
  - Inform them that withdrawal or settlement can happen **only during account closure**.
  - Escalate to **Vendor Operations / Finance** to manage the closure and settlement.

## Quick recap for training

Use this as a summary slide or closing script during vendor or internal training.

- **Wallet (real money):**
  - Used to **pay customers**.
  - Funded via **bank deposit into virtual account**.
  - **No self-withdrawal**; settlement only at account closure.
  - Checked during **unlock eligibility** and at **payment via OTP**.

- **Credits (virtual points):**
  - Used to **unlock orders**.
  - **Deducted instantly** when unlocking.
  - **Not transferable**, not convertible to cash.
  - Can be **reversed by admin** when orders fail for valid reasons.

- **Unlock requirement:**
  - Vendor needs **enough credits + enough wallet** to unlock.

- **Payment flow:**
  - Vendor inspects device → taps Pay Now → customer receives OTP → vendor enters OTP → **wallet → customer bank**.

- **Refund logic:**
  - Use **credit reversals** when unlock credits should be returned.
  - Use **wallet refunds** when payout from wallet must be restored.
  - Use **both** when vendor should not lose either credits or payout for a failed/cancelled order.
