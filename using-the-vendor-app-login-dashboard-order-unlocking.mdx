## Overview

Use this SOP to log in to the Cashkr Partner App, understand the dashboard, unlock orders, complete pickups, and process payments via wallet OTP.

End-to-end flow: **Login → Dashboard → Available Orders → Unlock → Customer Details → Pickup → Inspect → Pay (OTP) → Complete Order.**

## Key concepts

Understand Wallet vs Credits before unlocking any order:

- **Wallet balance (₹)** — Real money used to pay customers. Maintain enough balance to cover final device price.
- **Credits balance** — Virtual points required to unlock orders. The app deducts credits immediately when an order is unlocked.
- **Active orders** — Orders you have already unlocked and are currently handling.
- **Available orders** — Orders you can unlock based on your area and pincode access.

<Callout kind="alert">

Credits get deducted from your balance immediately when you unlock an order, even if the order later fails or is cancelled.

</Callout>

## Dashboard overview

After login, the Dashboard shows the key sections vendors and ops must monitor.

### Main dashboard sections

- **Wallet balance (₹)**  
  Shows real money available to pay customers. Keep this sufficient for expected payouts.

- **Credits balance**  
  Shows available credits for unlocking orders. The system deducts credits instantly when you unlock.

- **Active orders**  
  Lists all orders you are currently handling.

- **Available orders**  
  Lists orders you can unlock based on your location, pincode, and access rules.

- **Completed orders**  
  History of all successfully closed orders.

- **Failed / returned orders**  
  Orders cancelled due to:
  - Wrong inspection
  - Device mismatch
  - Customer denial
  - Vendor error
  - Order reversal

## Login process

Only registered and approved vendors can access the Cashkr Vendor App.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app and request OTP" icon="play-circle">

  - Open the Cashkr Vendor App on your phone.
  - Enter your **registered mobile number**.
  - Tap **Get OTP**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter OTP and access dashboard" icon="check-circle">

  - Enter the **6-digit OTP** received via SMS.
  - The app verifies the OTP and redirects you to the **Dashboard** after successful verification.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Login rules to follow" icon="info">

  - Log in only with the **registered and approved** vendor mobile number.
  - Ensure the OTP matches the registered phone number.
  - If OTP is not received:
    - Check network connectivity.
    - Retry after 30 seconds.
    - Contact Vendor Support if it still fails.

  </Step>
</Steps>

### When login fails

Use this table to diagnose and resolve common login failures.

| Issue          | Reason                    | Solution                |
| -------------- | ------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| OTP not coming | Network or SIM issue      | Retry / restart phone   |
| Account not found | Vendor not approved yet | Contact Ops Team        |
| App crash      | Old app version           | Update the app          |
| Incorrect OTP  | Wrong OTP entered         | Re-enter and retry      |

## How order unlocking works

Order unlocking controls which vendor gets customer details and the right to process the order.

### Unlock flow summary

1. Vendor sees an **Available Order**.
2. Vendor checks **credits required**.
3. System checks vendor **credits** and **wallet balance**, pincode access, hold status, and daily limits.
4. If all checks pass, unlock is allowed and credits are deducted at once.
5. Vendor receives full customer details.
6. Vendor calls customer and schedules pickup.
7. Vendor inspects the device at customer location.
8. Vendor pays customer via wallet OTP.
9. Vendor collects device and marks order as **Completed**.

<Callout kind="alert">

Unlock happens only when credits and wallet balance are sufficient and access checks pass. Credits are **deducted immediately** at unlock and are not dependent on order completion.

</Callout>

## Unlocking an order

Use these steps every time you unlock an order from the Vendor App.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Available Orders" icon="monitor">

  - Go to the **Available Orders** tab on the Dashboard.
  - Review the list of orders shown for your area and pincode.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Review order details" icon="info">

  For each order card, check:

  - Device model
  - Expected device condition
  - Expected price
  - Credits required to unlock
  - Pickup pincode

  </Step>

  <Step title="Tap Unlock Order" icon="play-circle">

  - Choose a suitable order.
  - Tap **Unlock Order** to request access.

  </Step>

  <Step title="System auto-checks before unlock" icon="settings">

  The system runs these checks automatically:

  - Credits balance is **greater than or equal to** required credits.
  - Wallet balance is **enough to pay** the customer.
  - Vendor has access for the **pickup pincode**.
  - Vendor is **not on temporary hold**.
  - Vendor has **not exceeded daily limit** for unlocks.

  If all checks pass, unlock is successful and **credits are deducted immediately**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

### What vendor gets after unlocking

Once an order is successfully unlocked, the app shows:

- Customer full name
- Address and Google Map location
- Selected condition
- Expected price
- Order ID
- Device details

Vendor must **call the customer within 10 minutes** of unlocking to schedule pickup.

<Callout kind="alert">

If you delay contacting the customer after unlocking, it increases the chance of order cancellation, disputes, or SLA issues for the vendor.

</Callout>

## Pickup, inspection, and payment flow

Follow this standard sequence at customer location for every unlocked order.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Call, reach, and inspect" icon="phone">

  - Call the customer and confirm **address and time**.
  - Reach the customer location on time.
  - Inspect the device **physically** in front of the customer.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Update condition and confirm price" icon="edit">

  - Update the **device condition** in the app as per actual inspection.
  - The app calculates the **final price** based on updated condition.
  - Confirm the final price with the customer before proceeding.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Pay customer using wallet OTP" icon="wallet">

  - Tap **Pay to Customer** or **Pay Now** in the app.
  - The final price appears on screen.
  - Customer receives an **OTP on their phone**.
  - Ask the customer to **read the OTP**.
  - Enter the OTP in the app to complete payment.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Collect device and complete order" icon="check-circle">

  - Once OTP is verified, payment is sent from your Wallet to the customer bank account.
  - Collect the device from the customer.
  - Mark the order as **Completed** in the app.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">

- Use **only Wallet balance** for payments. **No cash** is allowed.  
- OTP must **come from the customer's phone**; do not create or guess it.  
- Never take the device **before OTP is confirmed and payment is successful**.

</Callout>

## Wallet payment rules

Keep these points in mind whenever you pay a customer via the Vendor App:

- Payment uses only **Wallet balance**, not cash.
- Customer receives an OTP on their mobile number.
- Vendor enters OTP in the app to trigger payment from Wallet to customer bank account.
- Vendor must **never ask for customer PIN or banking credentials**.
- Vendor must not collect extra charges from the customer outside the app.

## Common issues during order unlocking

Use this table to troubleshoot unlock failures and guide vendors.

| Issue                        | Cause                         | Solution                        |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Cannot unlock order         | Low credits                   | Add credits or contact support  |
| Cannot unlock order         | Low wallet balance            | Refill wallet                   |
| Order already unlocked      | Another vendor was faster     | Move to the next available order |
| You are on hold message     | SLA issues or manual hold     | Contact Ops                     |
| Pickup pincode blocked      | No coverage for that vendor   | Request new coverage approval   |

## Vendor best practices

Use these as training checkpoints for vendors and as review criteria for internal ops.

<Callout kind="tip">

- Call customers quickly after unlock (within 10 minutes).  
- Maintain polite, professional communication at all times.  
- Reach pickup location on time.  
- Pay instantly via wallet once inspection is done.  
- Upload correct, honest inspection results in the app.  
- Avoid any fraud such as IMEI mismatch or price manipulation.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert">

- Do not collect extra charges from customers.  
- Do not delay payment after agreeing on the final price.  
- Do not force customers to accept a price.  
- Do not take the device before OTP confirmation and successful payment.

</Callout>

  - Take action on SLA breaches and vendor errors.

- **Vendor Support**
  - Train vendors on wallet and credits usage.
  - Solve unlock errors and login issues.
  - Guide vendors on inspection rules and correct app usage.

- **CX Team**
  - Handle customer complaints and disputes.
  - Resolve payment-related doubts.
  - Educate customers about OTP and payment flow to reduce confusion.
