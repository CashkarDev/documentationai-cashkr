## Overview

The Laptop page in the Devices section is the master catalog for all laptop models available for buyback on the Cashkr platform. Laptop management uses six tabs: Models, Brands, Classes, Pricing, Credits, and Components. Credits and Components are laptop-specific tabs that handle vendor credit algorithms and component-based pricing for processors, RAM, storage, and graphics cards. Together, these controls keep laptop valuations accurate, consistent, and aligned with your pricing logic.

## Tabs

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Models">

### What you see

The Models tab lists all configured laptop models in the system. Each row shows:

- Model name
- Brand and series
- Device image
- Unique system code
- Base buyback price
- Assigned class
- Available actions

The Laptop Models tab does not show a Variants column because laptop pricing is driven more by component specifications (processor, RAM, storage) managed in the Components tab than by fixed variants.

<Tab title="Credits">

### What you see

The Credits tab is unique to laptop management and controls vendor credit earnings for laptop buyback orders. It shows the Laptop Credit Management section with a table of all configured credit algorithms.

Each row in the table shows:

- **Device Type** — Laptop
- **Price Range** — INR price range the algorithm applies to, such as ₹1 to ₹10,000 or ₹10,001 to ₹15,000
- **Credit Amount** — Number of credits awarded to the vendor for completing an order in that price range
- **Type Credits** — Additional credit type details if applicable
- **Created** — Date the algorithm was created
- **Actions** — Edit or delete the algorithm

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-39cfb2dd-42b8-41e4-a035-d7210b5839fe/doc-1aebdaec-9f45-458a-9429-3234288b0c70/1774769352632-65dd9lm6dbt-laptop-credits.laptop credits?q=85&fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1919" height="830" alt="Laptop Credits tab showing credit algorithms by price range and credit amount" />

This configuration allows the platform to automatically award more credits to vendors for higher-value laptop buybacks and fewer credits for lower-value ones.

### Search & filters

Search for a specific credit algorithm and filter by Device Type, set to Laptop, to focus on laptop credit rules.

### Add

Use the Add Credit Algorithm action to create new laptop credit brackets.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the add credit algorithm form" icon="plus">

  Click **Add Credit Algorithm** in the top-right corner.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the price range">

  Set the **Price Range** by choosing the minimum and maximum INR values for the bracket.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Set credit amount">

  Enter the **Credit Amount** for completing a laptop order in this price range.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the algorithm">

  Save the algorithm so it applies to future laptop buyback orders.

  </Step>
</Steps>

### Edit

Update existing credit brackets when you need to tweak how many credits vendors earn.

- Click the **edit** icon on the relevant row.
- Update the **Price Range** and/or **Credit Amount** as required.
- Save your changes; updates apply immediately to new orders.

### Delete

Remove outdated or incorrect credit brackets using the delete action on the corresponding row.

<Callout kind="alert">

Deleting a credit algorithm can leave uncovered price ranges where vendors receive no credits or incorrect credits. After deletion, verify that all price ranges remain fully covered and that laptop orders map cleanly to a single credit rule.

</Callout>

### Example credit algorithms

Typical laptop credit brackets might look like:

- ₹1 to ₹10,000 — 30 credits
- ₹10,001 to ₹15,000 — 45 credits
- ₹15,001 to ₹20,000 — 50 credits
- ₹20,001 to ₹30,000 — 70 credits
- ₹30,001 to ₹40,000 — 100 credits
- ₹40,001 to ₹200,000 — 100 credits

### How components affect laptop pricing

Laptop buyback pricing combines base model price, condition class, and component values to arrive at the final offer shown to the customer.

During evaluation:

- The system identifies the laptop's processor, RAM, storage, and graphics card.
- It looks up the INR values for each component from the Components tab.
- These component values are added to the base model price and adjusted by the assigned class.

Keeping processor, RAM, storage, and graphics card prices accurate and up to date ensures customers receive fair and competitive buyback offers for their laptops.
