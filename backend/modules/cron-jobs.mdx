## Purpose

All scheduled work. Every job is a cron CronJob started in src/server.ts only on the primary PM2 instance (isPrimaryInstance() from src/utils/cluster.ts), all with timezone Asia/Kolkata, all start: true (run on schedule, not on boot).

## Folder / Files

src/jobs/\*.job.ts (13 files), src/server.ts, src/utils/cluster.ts.

## Core Business Logic

Each job wraps its service call in try/catch and logs; none persists run history.

## Validations & Rules

Kill switches and schedule overrides via env vars listed above; two jobs (PhonePe reconcile, vendor credit notify) have an in-memory overlap guard.

## Dependencies

Orders, Lead & Nurture, Vendor Payments & Credits, Gift Cards (Woohoo), Notifications, Attendance, Inspection Code, Model Request, Careers, Admin (customer activity logs), Messaging Utilities.

## Who Uses It

Internal.
