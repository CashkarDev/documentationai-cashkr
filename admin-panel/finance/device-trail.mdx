## Overview

**Sidebar path:** Finance → Reports → Device Trail (`/finance/device-trail`)

**Device Trail** answers "where did this device come from and where did it go?". Search one device and see its full lifecycle on one page: who sold it to Cashkr, what was paid, its inventory and grading status, where it was sold, and a timeline of every event.

---

## Search

Enter one of the following in the search box and click **Search** (or press **Enter**):

| You can search by | Format |
| --- | --- |
| **IMEI** | 14–17 digits |
| **Serial number** | As recorded on the device |
| **Lead ID** | UUID of the B2C lead |
| **B2B deal number** | e.g. `BD-123` |

The result header shows the device name and type (e.g. *Galaxy Z Fold7 (Mobile)*), a source badge (e.g. **B2C Lead**) and what it **Matched on** (for example `imei (handover)`).

---

## What the result shows

### Source — where it came from

Lead ID, **Seller** name and phone, **Collected by** (vendor), **Device**, **Device type**, **IMEI**, **Serial**, **Pickup date** and **Pickup address**.

### Payment to seller

**Quoted price**, **Requote price**, **Final price**, **Paid at**, **Method** (e.g. Cash), and payout details when paid online — **UPI**, **Beneficiary**, **Bank**, **Account**, **IFSC**.

### Inventory & grading

Inventory and grading details once the device is in stock, otherwise *Not yet in inventory.*

### Outbound — where it went

Sale details once the device is sold, otherwise *Not sold yet.*

### Event timeline

A chronological list of every event for the device and its lead, each with date/time, a description and an event type tag, for example:

| Event type | Example |
| --- | --- |
| `LEAD_CREATED` | Lead created for the device |
| `LEAD_LOG` | Lead converted to order, order locked by vendor, status changes (Out For Pickup, Inspection Started), order completed |
| `CALL` | Vendor called the customer |

<Callout kind="tip">
Use Device Trail for disputes and audits — for example to confirm what a seller was paid, or to trace a returned device back to its original purchase.
</Callout>
