## Overview

**Sidebar path:** Finance → Reports → All Reports (`/finance/reports`). Each report also has its own sidebar entry: **Profit & Loss**, **GST**, **Money Flow** and **Credits**.

The **Finance Reports** landing page (*Comprehensive financial analysis and tracking*) shows four tiles. Click a tile to open the report:

| Tile | Opens | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Credit Report** | `/finance/reports/credits` | Credit purchases, usage and outstanding balances with vendors |
| **Money Report** | `/finance/reports/money` | All cash and bank transactions — purchases, sales, expenses, payments |
| **Profit & Loss** | `/finance/reports/profit-loss` | Daily, weekly and monthly P&L analysis |
| **GST Report** | `/finance/reports/gst` | Margin Scheme GST liability — CGST/SGST breakdown and monthly summaries |

Every report page has the same header controls:

- **←** back arrow to return to the Finance Reports landing page
- **From** / **to** date pickers to set the period
- **Refresh** icon to reload the data

<Callout kind="info">
Reports are read-only. To fix a number, correct the source record (Device Sales, Device Purchases, Expenses, Credits, etc.).
</Callout>

  **GST Report** — *Margin Scheme GST on device resales (18% — CGST 9% + SGST 9%)* (`/finance/reports/gst`)

  **Controls:** date range, Refresh.

  **KPI cards**

  | Card | Meaning |
  | --- | --- |
  | **Total Sales** | Total sale value, with the number of devices sold |
  | **Total GST** | Total GST liability, with the number of taxable items |
  | **CGST (9%)** | Central GST component |
  | **SGST (9%)** | State GST component |

  **Monthly GST Breakdown** — **Month**, **Devices**, **Sales**, **Margin**, **CGST**, **SGST**, **Total GST**.

  **Top Devices by GST Liability** — **Device**, **Purchase**, **Sale**, **Margin**, **GST**, **Date**.

  <Callout kind="alert">
  Under the Margin Scheme, GST is charged on the margin (sale price − purchase price), not on the full sale value. Devices sold at or below cost carry no GST, which is why taxable items can be fewer than devices sold. Make sure purchase prices are recorded correctly, otherwise GST figures will be wrong.
  </Callout>

## How to use finance reports

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report" icon="monitor" title-type="p">
  Go to **Finance → All Reports** and click a tile, or open **Profit & Loss**, **GST**, **Money Flow** or **Credits** directly from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date range" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
  Pick a **from** and **to** date to analyse a specific period instead of all history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and drill down" icon="list-filter" title-type="p">
  Check the KPI cards first, then use the breakdown tables (or Money Flow tabs) to reconcile individual transactions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare GST filing" icon="receipt-text" title-type="p">
  Each month, set the GST Report to that month and share the Monthly GST Breakdown with the accounts / CA team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Admin responsibilities

- Review Money Flow monthly to reconcile inflows and outflows.
- Use Profit & Loss for management reviews and planning.
- Review the GST Report monthly before filing.
- Monitor the Credit Report for vendors with large unused balances.

## Connections

- **Credits** → Credit Report and Money Flow (Credit Revenue).
- **Device Sales** → Money Flow, Profit & Loss, GST Report.
- **Device Purchases** → Money Flow, Profit & Loss, GST Report (purchase price).
- **Expenses** → Money Flow and Profit & Loss (Expenses by Category).
- **B2B Payments** → Profit & Loss (B2B Revenue).
- **Completed B2C orders** → Money Flow (B2C Revenue) and Profit & Loss (B2C Commission).
- **Device Trail** (Finance → Reports) — look up the full lifecycle of a single device.
