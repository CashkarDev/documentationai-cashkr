## Overview and purpose

<Callout kind="info">

Audience: Sales, CX, Operations, and Management teams.

The Lead Management module ensures that:

- Every customer who starts the process is followed up
- No high-value opportunities are missed
- Sales and CX teams have a structured follow-up process
- Better conversions lead to more orders for Cashkr
- Customers receive a consistent, high-quality experience

</Callout>

The Lead Management module in the Admin Panel helps teams monitor, call, follow up, and convert leads into completed orders.

## What is a lead?

A lead is a customer who:

- Selected a device
- Checked the price
- Entered their details
- Did not complete checkout (did not place the order)

These users have high intent but need follow-up.

## Lead categories and workflows

The Admin Panel breaks leads into four actionable categories:

- All Leads
- Not Called
- Callbacks
- High Ends

Each category has a different purpose, workflow, and SLA.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="All Leads">

  ### Meaning

  All Leads shows all uncompleted leads from all customers. This includes:

  - New leads
  - Called leads
  - Follow-up leads
  - High-value leads
  - Callbacks

  ### Used by

  - Sales
  - CX
  - Management

  ### What to do in All Leads

  - Filter by date, city, or device
  - Review notes left by agents
  - Check lead sources and patterns
  - Monitor team activity and follow-up
  - Identify where drop-offs happen in the funnel

  **Key actions:**

  - Call the lead
  - Add notes after every interaction
  - Update disposition correctly
  - Send checkout link if needed
  - Follow up again if required

  ### SLA for All Leads

  - Follow up within 24 hours

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Not Called">

  ### Meaning

  Not Called shows leads where no action has been taken yet:

  - No calls
  - No SMS
  - No notes

  These are priority leads.

  ### Used by

  - Sales
  - CX

  ### SOP action flow for Not Called

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Call immediately" icon="phone">

    Call the lead as soon as possible, ideally within 15 minutes of lead creation.

    </Step>
    <Step title="Use the approved calling script" icon="book-open">

    Use the standard first-call script for Not Called leads to introduce Cashkr and confirm intent.

    </Step>
    <Step title="Collect reason for non-completion" icon="help-circle">

    Ask why the customer did not complete the order and listen for objections or blockers.

    </Step>
    <Step title="Offer assistance" icon="settings">

    Help with price clarifications, coupon usage, pickup details, or any confusion about the process.

    </Step>
    <Step title="Update disposition" icon="edit">

    Mark the correct disposition based on the call outcome, such as Connected, Callback, or Create Order.

    </Step>
    <Step title="Add internal note (mandatory)" icon="file-text">

    Write a clear note for every call attempt and outcome. If the customer needs follow-up, move them to Callbacks.

    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Callout kind="alert">

  CX and Sales SLA for Not Called:

  - Every Not Called lead must be attempted within the same day.
  - Once any action is taken, update disposition and remove the lead from Not Called.

  </Callout>

  ### Key actions for Not Called

  - Call immediately
  - Use the approved script
  - Capture the reason for non-completion
  - Offer assistance and send the checkout link if helpful
  - Move to Callbacks if the customer requests a later call

  ### SLA for Not Called

  - Attempt every Not Called lead within the same day (priority)

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Callbacks">

  ### Meaning

  Callbacks are leads where the customer has asked for a scheduled follow-up, for example:

  - "Call me after work"
  - "I will check and confirm in the evening"
  - "I want to re-verify price later"
  - "I am busy right now"
  - "I need to talk to someone at home"

  ### Used by

  - Sales
  - CX

  ### Callback handling flow

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check the callback time" icon="clock">

    Review the scheduled date and time in the notes before calling.

    </Step>
    <Step title="Call at the exact noted time" icon="phone">

    Call as per the promised time. If needed, allow a short buffer of a few minutes, but do not delay to the next day.

    </Step>
    <Step title="Use the callback-specific script" icon="book-open">

    Start the call by referring to the previous conversation and confirming that you are calling as scheduled.

    </Step>
    <Step title="Update disposition after the call" icon="edit">

    Record whether the customer confirmed, requested another callback, or is not interested. Select the correct disposition.

    </Step>
    <Step title="Retrigger or share link if needed" icon="upload">

    Send or retrigger the checkout link if the customer is ready or wants the link again.

    </Step>
    <Step title="Plan the next follow-up if still uncertain" icon="repeat">

    If the customer is still uncertain, schedule a follow-up for the next day and note the exact time.

    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Callout kind="alert">

  Never miss a callback. Missing a scheduled callback shows poor customer experience and directly hurts conversions.

  </Callout>

  ### Purpose of Callbacks

  - Maximize conversions by respecting user timing and commitments
  - Build trust by calling exactly when promised

  ### SLA for Callbacks

  - Call at the exact scheduled time recorded in the notes

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="High Ends">

  ### Meaning

  High Ends are leads that relate to high-value devices with high commission potential, such as:

  - iPhone 13, 14, 15 series
  - MacBooks
  - Flagship Samsung models
  - High-end laptops
  - iPad Pro series

  These leads can significantly increase revenue.

  ### Used by

  - Sales
  - CX
  - Management

  ### How to handle High Ends

  - Prioritize calling High Ends ahead of normal leads
  - Assign or route to a senior sales representative when possible
  - Give a detailed explanation of Cashkr benefits and the process
  - Offer additional support such as WhatsApp follow-up and clarification on pricing or pickup
  - Ask if the customer wants a home pickup
  - Push towards creating an order immediately during the call
  - If the customer is unsure, mark as Callback with a clear time

  <Callout kind="alert">

  High Ends are priority leads:

  - Call within 15 minutes.
  - Management expects daily review of High-End leads and follow-up quality.
  - High Ends must be converted at a higher rate than normal leads.

  </Callout>

  ### Key goal for High Ends

  - Achieve a higher conversion rate compared to regular leads

  ### SLA for High Ends

  - Call within 15 minutes of lead creation

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Lead disposition options

Every lead must end with a correct disposition. Use these standard options:

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="1. Connected" default-open="true">

  The customer picked the call.

  Under Connected, select one of:

  - **Create Order**: Customer is ready to book pickup now, and Sales assists in placing the order.
  - **Callback**: Customer wants a scheduled follow-up at a specific time.
  - **Waiting for Quote**: Customer is comparing or waiting for a revised or confirmed quote.
  - **Customer Will Place Order**: Customer says they will place the order themselves later.
  - **Already Placed an Order**: Customer has already completed the order.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="2. Not Connected">

  The customer did not pick the call.

  - Use **Not Reachable / Switched Off** (merged category) when the number does not connect.
  - Try up to three times and log all attempts in notes.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="3. Create Order">

  The customer is ready to book pickup now.

  - Use Create Order when you assist the customer in placing the order directly from the Admin Panel flow.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

For more conceptual detail on dispositions, also refer to the separate module Understanding Dispositions (Connected, Not Connected, Create Order).

## Lead handling SLA summary

Align your actions with the SLA inside each category:

- **Not Called**: Call within the same day (priority).
- **High Ends**: Call within 15 minutes.
- **Callbacks**: Call at the exact scheduled time.
- **All Leads**: Follow up within 24 hours.

<Callout kind="alert">

SLA violations for Not Called, Callbacks, and High Ends directly impact revenue and customer experience. Treat these timelines as non-negotiable.

</Callout>

## Notes entry rules

<Callout kind="alert">

Every call must have a note. If it is not written → it did not happen.

</Callout>

When you handle any lead:

- Add a clear, short note after every call attempt.
- Include time, outcome, and next action.

**Example notes:**

- "Customer busy, callback at 7 PM"
- "Customer negotiating price — asked for quote comparison"
- "Customer ready — shared checkout link"
- "Switched off — will retry at 3 PM"

Management and other team members rely on these notes to understand context and avoid repeating questions for the customer.

- Number of leads generated

- High-end conversions and quality of handling
- Not Called backlog and SLA adherence

- City-wise lead quality and patterns
- Lead-to-order conversion rate

These insights help optimize:

- Sales staffing and training
- CX coverage and scripts
- Marketing budgets and channel performance
- Overall lead-to-order potential

## One-page training summary

### Lead categories

- **All Leads**: Complete list of all uncompleted leads.
- **Not Called**: Highest priority, no action taken yet.
- **Callbacks**: Scheduled follow-ups that must be honored on time.
- **High Ends**: High-value devices that must convert at a higher rate.

### Team responsibilities

- **Sales**: Call and convert leads to orders.
- **CX**: Support follow-up, clarify doubts, and correct lead details.
- **Operations / Management**: Monitor quality, volume, and SLA adherence.
- **BD Team**: Ensure city and pincode coverage, reduce lead wastage, and improve lead-to-order potential.

### SLA recap

- **High Ends**: 15 minutes
- **Not Called**: Same day
- **Callbacks**: Exact scheduled time
- **All Leads**: 24 hours

Outcome: Higher conversions lead to more orders and better revenue for Cashkr.
