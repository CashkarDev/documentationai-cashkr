## Why this matters

<Callout kind="info">

Social media is where people see, trust, and interact with the Cashkr brand. A consistent presence builds credibility, creates conversations, and connects us with both individual customers and businesses.

</Callout>

## Platforms we use

<Card title="Instagram" icon="camera">

Use Instagram for reels, stories, and carousels that show real customer experiences and build trust. Focus on short, visual content that explains how Cashkr works and highlights successful sales.

**Example:** Share a reel showing a customer selling their phone in under 2 hours, with before/after shots and a quick walkthrough of the pickup experience.

</Card>

<Card title="Facebook" icon="users">

Use Facebook to reach wider age groups and local communities. Run city-specific campaigns, promote offers, and share educational posts about selling used devices safely.

**Example:** Run a "Sell your old laptop" campaign for Pune users in local Facebook groups, with a clear call to action to start the flow on Cashkr.

</Card>

<Card title="YouTube" icon="play-circle">

Use YouTube for longer-form content that explains the product, answers common questions, and builds trust through storytelling. Prioritize explainers, how-to videos, and testimonial-style content.

**Example:** Publish a video ad explaining how Cashkr pickup works, then use the same video as a retargeting asset for people who visited the website but did not complete their order.

</Card>

<Card title="LinkedIn" icon="briefcase">

Use LinkedIn for B2B and corporate positioning. Talk to IT managers, admin teams, and founders about bulk device buyback, compliance, and process reliability.

**Example:** Post about Cashkr’s bulk device buyback solution, highlighting how HR and IT teams can safely dispose of old laptops and phones while recovering value.

</Card>

<Card title="X (Twitter)" icon="message-square">

Use X for quick updates, real-time engagement, and participating in relevant conversations. Stay present in tech and resale discussions and respond to customers who mention Cashkr.

**Example:** Reply to a trending tech hashtag about used phones with a concise, witty Cashkr comment and a link to the sell flow.

</Card>

<Card title="Reddit" icon="terminal">

Use Reddit to engage with niche tech and resale communities. Add helpful, non-spammy answers in relevant threads and build trust by being transparent about how Cashkr works.

**Example:** Answer a question in r/IndiaTech about the best way to sell old devices, explaining the Cashkr process and when it is safer than listing on classifieds.

</Card>

<Card title="Quora" icon="help-circle">

Use Quora to build authority by answering customer questions around safety, pricing, and process. Focus on detailed, honest explanations rather than direct promotion.

**Example:** Answer a question like "What is the safest way to sell my old iPhone?" and walk through the Cashkr flow, including pickup, data safety, and payment.

</Card>

## How we use these daily

- **Instagram and Facebook** → Reels, posts, and stories that show real customers, reviews, and city-specific campaigns.
- **YouTube** → How-to guides, detailed explainers, and testimonial-style videos used in organic and paid campaigns.
- **LinkedIn** → Posts about corporate device buyback, partner stories, and updates that build B2B credibility.
- **X, Reddit, and Quora** → Ongoing conversations, FAQs, and soft branding through helpful answers and timely replies.

## Typical social media workflow

<Steps>

  <Step title="1. Plan content by platform and goal" icon="settings">

  Decide weekly or monthly themes for each platform: trust-building (reviews, stories), education (how Cashkr works), offers (city or device specific), and B2B positioning. Map each idea to the right channel based on audience fit.

  </Step>

  <Step title="2. Create and publish content" icon="upload">

  Prepare creatives and copy that match the format of each platform (reels vs. posts vs. long videos). Publish according to the planned calendar, keeping timing consistent for each channel.

  </Step>

  <Step title="3. Engage and respond" icon="message-square">

  Monitor comments, DMs, mentions, and replies. Answer questions, clarify doubts, and route support issues to the right team while maintaining a clear, friendly brand tone.

  </Step>

  <Step title="4. Repurpose and retarget where relevant" icon="repeat">

  Turn strong posts into ads, reuse high-performing reels on multiple platforms, and adapt YouTube explainer videos into shorter cuts for Instagram and Facebook. Keep B2B content aligned across LinkedIn and other channels.

  </Step>

  <Step title="5. Measure and share learnings" icon="bar-chart">

  Track basic performance per platform: reach, engagement, leads, and key conversations. Share learnings with marketing and support so future content focuses on formats and topics that clearly work.

  </Step>

</Steps>
