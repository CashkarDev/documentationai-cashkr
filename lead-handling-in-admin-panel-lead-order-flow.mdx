## Overview

Use this module to understand how inbound leads move from lead to order in the Admin Panel and how to handle each lead to maximize conversions.

This guide is for the Sales and Follow-Up teams who work daily inside the Admin Panel on incoming leads.

Goals:

- Convert high-intent users into orders
- Reduce drop-offs after price check
- Maintain structured and documented follow-ups
- Ensure correct handoff to Operations
- Improve lead-to-order conversion rate

## What is a lead?

A lead is a customer who:

- Selected a device
- Checked the price
- Entered contact details
- Did not complete checkout

These users already have high buying intent and require timely follow-up.

## Where to work in the Admin Panel

Path:

- Admin Panel → Leads

Key tabs:

- All Leads
- Not Called
- Callbacks
- High Ends

Sales should work primarily from Not Called and High Ends.

## Lead → Order conversion flow

<Steps>

<Step title="New lead is created" icon="plus">

A new lead is created when a user shows intent but does not complete checkout.

- User drops off after viewing the price
- User exits during checkout
- User closes the app or website after entering contact details

The new lead appears in the Not Called tab.

</Step>

<Step title="Call the lead" icon="phone">

Call the lead from the Not Called or High Ends tab using the approved opening line.

> Hi, you checked the price for your device on Cashkr. Can I help you complete your order?

<Callout kind="info">

High Ends: call within 15 minutes.  
All other leads: call within the same day.

</Callout>

</Step>

<Step title="Identify customer intent" icon="help-circle">

During the call, identify the customer's intent and take the matching action.

**Ready to sell**

- Move directly to Create Order from the lead
- Confirm device details, price, and pickup basics

**Needs time**

- Mark the lead as Callback with the correct date and time
- Add clear notes on what the customer is waiting for

**Has doubts**

- Address objections using the objection handling guide below
- If they still need time, move to Callback with notes

**Not interested**

- Mark the correct disposition
- Add a short note explaining why
- Do not push for an order

</Step>

<Step title="Assist with order creation" icon="edit">

Help the customer complete the order in whichever way is easiest for them.

You can:

- Guide the customer to complete checkout on the website or app
- Or manually create an order from the Admin Panel

Path for manual creation:

- Leads → Convert to Order

Collect and confirm these required fields:

- Device model
- Condition
- Pincode
- Address
- Pickup time

</Step>

<Step title="Order is created and handed to Ops" icon="check-circle">

Once confirmed:

- The lead converts into an Order ID
- The order moves to Pending Vendor Assignment
- Sales responsibility ends
- Operations takes over from this stage

</Step>

</Steps>

## Lead statuses and dispositions

Every call must end with a disposition. Never leave a lead without an updated status.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Connected" icon="check">

For calls where you spoke to the customer:

- Create Order
- Customer Will Place Order
- Callback
- Waiting for Quote
- Already Placed an Order

</Tab>

<Tab title="Not connected" icon="x">

For calls where you did not speak to the customer:

- Not Reachable / Switched Off

</Tab>

</Tabs>

<Callout kind="alert">

Never leave a lead without a disposition. No blank dispositions are allowed.

</Callout>

## Handling each lead type

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Not Called" icon="alert-triangle">

Handle Not Called leads first because they are fresh and most likely to convert.

- Treat as highest priority
- Call immediately when leads appear
- Attempt to call at least 3 times per day
- Add clear call notes after every attempt

</Tab>

<Tab title="Callbacks" icon="clock">

Follow callback commitments strictly to maintain trust and conversion.

- Call at the exact scheduled time
- Treat missed callbacks as poor customer experience and avoid them
- If the customer is still not ready, reschedule with a new time and update notes

</Tab>

<Tab title="High Ends" icon="star">

High Ends leads usually have higher-value devices and need extra care.

- Assign to a senior sales agent
- Clearly explain Cashkr advantages and process
- Push for same-day order creation where possible
- Offer assisted checkout and stay on call until order is confirmed

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Objection handling (quick guide)

Use these standard responses when customers raise common objections.

| Objection        | Sales response                    |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Price too low    | Explain inspection-based pricing  |
| Pickup trust     | Explain verified vendors          |
| Payment delay    | Emphasize instant UPI payment     |
| Wants to compare | Ask for competitor quote and try to match |
| Not sure         | Offer callback and WhatsApp follow-up |

## When not to create an order

<Callout kind="alert">

Do not create an order in these cases:

- Pincode is not serviceable
- Customer is clearly not interested
- Customer wants a pickup date far in the future
- Device is not supported in the system

</Callout>

## Sales KPIs (tracked daily)

These metrics show how effectively you are converting leads into orders:

- Calls made
- Leads contacted
- Orders created
- Lead → Order conversion percentage
- High-end conversion percentage
- Callback success rate

## Common mistakes to avoid

<Callout kind="alert">

Avoid these mistakes that directly hurt conversions and customer experience:

- Delaying the first call after a lead is created
- Missing scheduled callbacks
- Not adding call notes
- Selecting wrong or incomplete dispositions
- Over-promising on pickup timelines or price

</Callout>

## Quick summary card

<Callout kind="tip">

**Lead → Order flow**

Lead Created → Call → Identify Intent → Assist Checkout → Order Created → Ops Takes Over

**Priority order**

High Ends → Not Called → Callbacks

**Where sales responsibility ends**

Sales ownership ends once the order is created and moves to Pending Vendor Assignment. Operations handles everything after that stage.

</Callout>
