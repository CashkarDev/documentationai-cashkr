## Overview

- **Team:** Vendor Operations / Vendor Support  
- **When used:**
  - During vendor onboarding to explain how credits work
  - When vendors contact support about credit deductions, reversals, or refunds
- **Goal:** Help vendors clearly understand the credit system: what credits are, when they are deducted, when they are refunded, and when they are not.
- **Tone:** Calm, transparent, and firm (since this involves money).

## Script by language

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English (official script)" icon="book-open">

<Steps>

<Step title="Opening" icon="play-circle">

> "Hi \[Vendor Name], this is \[Your Name] from Cashkr. I will quickly explain how your credits work and when refunds or reversals happen."

</Step>

<Step title="What are credits?" icon="info">

> "Credits are the commission you pay to Cashkr for accessing verified customer leads.  
> Whenever you unlock an order, a small number of credits are deducted from your account.  
> These credits are non-refundable once the order is completed successfully, because that customer was exclusively provided to you."

<Callout kind="alert">

Credits are non-refundable once an order is completed successfully. Do not promise or process credit refunds for completed orders.

</Callout>

</Step>

<Step title="When are credits deducted?" icon="minus">

> "Credits are deducted only when you unlock an order.  
> That means:
>
> - Viewing orders is free.  
> - But to see full customer details such as name, phone number, and address, you need to unlock the order using credits."

</Step>

<Step title="When are credits refunded?" icon="refresh-ccw">

> "Credits are refunded only if an order could not be completed due to a valid and verified reason, such as:
>
> - Customer not reachable, even after multiple attempts  
> - Wrong model or fake listing  
> - Locked or damaged device not matching the description  
> - Pickup cancelled by the customer
>
> In such cases, you must mark the order as `To Be Failed` in your app with the correct reason. Our team will verify it and, if valid, credits will be reversed automatically within 24–48 hours."

<Callout kind="alert">

Credits are refunded only after the vendor marks the order as `To Be Failed` with the correct reason and the case passes verification. The reversal happens within 24–48 hours after successful verification.

</Callout>

</Step>

<Step title="When credits are not refunded" icon="x-circle">

> "To avoid confusion, here are situations where credits are not refundable:
>
> - If the vendor delays contacting the customer  
> - If pickup is cancelled because of vendor unavailability  
> - If the vendor misbehaves or the customer complains  
> - If an incorrect reason is given for marking `To Be Failed`  
> - If the vendor or their agent violates SLA rules"

</Step>

<Step title="How to check reversals" icon="search">

> "You can check all your credit activity in `Transaction History` inside the side menu.  
> Every unlock, refund, or deduction is listed there clearly, with order ID, date, and status."

</Step>

<Step title="Example for clarity" icon="list-ordered">

> "Let us take an example.  
> Suppose you unlock an order using 20 credits:
>
> - If the pickup happens successfully, the credits remain deducted.  
> - If the order is cancelled by the customer or found invalid, and you mark `To Be Failed` correctly, credits are reversed after our verification."

</Step>

<Step title="Tip for vendors" icon="tip">

> "Try to confirm pickup within 1–2 hours of unlocking the order.  
> The faster you act, the lower the chance of loss or failed orders."

</Step>

<Step title="Closing" icon="check-circle">

> "\[Vendor Name], this credit system ensures that only serious vendors work on verified customers.  
> Keep your communication quick and transparent. That way, your credits will always work in your favor."

</Step>

</Steps>

<Tab title="Hinglish (real conversations)" icon="users">

<Steps>

<Step title="Opening" icon="play-circle">

> "Hello \[Vendor Name], main \[Your Name] bol raha/rahi hoon Cashkr se.  
> Main aapko batata/batati hoon credits ka system kaise kaam karta hai aur refund ya reversal kab milta hai."

</Step>

<Step title="Credits kya hain" icon="info">

> "Credits basically commission hai jo aap Cashkr ko dete ho verified customer leads ke liye.  
> Jab bhi aap order unlock karte ho, tab aapke account se thode credits deduct hote hain.  
> Ye credits non-refundable hote hain jab order successfully complete ho jata hai, kyunki woh customer sirf aapko hi diya gaya hota hai."

<Callout kind="alert">

Completed orders ke credits non-refundable hote hain. Completed order ke liye refund ya reversal ka commitment mat do.

</Callout>

</Step>

<Step title="Deduction kab hoti hai" icon="minus">

> "Credits tabhi deduct hote hain jab aap order unlock karte ho.  
> Iska matlab:
>
> - Sirf orders dekhne ke liye koi charge nahi lagta  
> - Customer ka full detail jaise naam, phone number, address dekhne ke liye aapko order unlock karke credits use karne padte hain"

</Step>

<Step title="Refund kab milta hai" icon="refresh-ccw">

> "Agar order kisi genuine reason ki wajah se complete nahi ho paya, tab hi credits refund hote hain. Jaise:
>
> - Customer reachable nahi hai, multiple attempts ke baad bhi  
> - Galat model ya fake listing  
> - Device locked ya damaged hai jo description se match nahi karta  
> - Pickup customer ki taraf se cancel ho gaya
>
> Aise cases me aapko order ko app me `To Be Failed` mark karna hota hai, sahi reason ke saath.  
> Hamari team verification karti hai aur agar reason valid hota hai, toh 24–48 hours ke andar credits wapas reverse ho jate hain."

<Callout kind="alert">

Credits tabhi refund hote hain jab vendor order ko `To Be Failed` sahi reason ke saath mark kare aur verification successful ho. Verification ke baad hi 24–48 hours ke andar reversal hota hai.

</Callout>

</Step>

<Step title="Refund kab nahi milta" icon="x-circle">

> "Confusion avoid karne ke liye, ye cases me refund nahi milta:
>
> - Agar customer ko call karne me delay aapki taraf se hua  
> - Agar pickup vendor ki unavailability ki wajah se cancel hua  
> - Agar vendor ne misbehave kiya ya customer ne complaint ki  
> - Agar `To Be Failed` ke liye galat reason dala gaya  
> - Agar vendor ya unka agent SLA rules violate karta hai"

</Step>

<Step title="Reversals kaise check kare" icon="search">

> "`Transaction History` me jaa kar aap saare credit activity dekh sakte ho.  
> Har unlock, refund, ya deduction clearly dikhta hai, order ID, date aur status ke saath."

</Step>

<Step title="Example (clarity ke liye)" icon="list-ordered">

> "Example lete hain.  
> Mano aapne 20 credits use karke ek order unlock kiya:
>
> - Agar pickup successfully complete ho gaya, toh ye credits deduct hi rehte hain.  
> - Agar order customer ki taraf se cancel ho gaya ya invalid nikla, aur aapne sahi reason ke saath `To Be Failed` mark kiya, toh verification ke baad credits reverse ho jate hain."

</Step>

<Step title="Tip for vendors" icon="tip">

> "Koshish karo ki order unlock karne ke 1–2 ghante ke andar customer se contact karke pickup confirm kar lo.  
> Jitni jaldi aap kaam karoge, utna kam chance rahega loss ya failed orders ka."

</Step>

<Step title="Closing" icon="check-circle">

> "Credits aapke business ka fuel hain, isliye unko smartly use karo.  
> Jab aap communication fast aur clear rakhte ho, toh credits hamesha aapke favor me kaam karte hain."

</Step>

</Steps>

## Internal notes (Cashkr team)

- Use this script during vendor onboarding and for any vendor credit refund or reversal disputes.
- After the call, send the vendor the Credit Policy PDF.
- Update internal tools or CRM with status: `Credit Policy Explained`.

<Callout kind="info">

If the vendor still disputes the decision after you explain the policy and share the Credit Policy PDF, escalate the case to the Ops Head for manual review and final approval.

</Callout>
