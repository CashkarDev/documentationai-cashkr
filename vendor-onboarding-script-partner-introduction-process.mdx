## Call context and usage

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  **Team / Used by:** Vendor Onboarding, Operations, and any team member introducing the Cashkr Vendor Partner Program on phone or WhatsApp calls.

  **When to use:** First-time introduction or early discussion with potential vendors who buy and sell used devices.

  **Goal:** Explain the partner model, clarify how earnings and payments work, and guide the vendor to complete registration and KYC on the Vendor App.

  **Tone:** Professional, respectful, and clear. Treat the vendor as a business partner. Be honest about availability and do not promise fixed earnings or guaranteed daily leads.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="danger" collapsed="false">
  Always follow policy:

  - Do not promise fixed earnings, daily lead counts, or exact timelines.

  - Do not allow or suggest cash, external UPI, or manual transfers to customers.

  - Vendor payments to customers must happen inside the Cashkr Vendor App using the wallet and OTP flow only.
</Callout>

## What you are offering (in 3 lines)

- Cashkr connects local resellers and traders with customers who want to sell used phones, laptops, tablets, and Macs.

- Vendors see customer sell leads in the Cashkr Vendor App and unlock the ones they want to work on.

- Vendors earn by buying devices from customers at their price, then selling or trading those devices in their own business.

## How Cashkr works (model summary)

Cashkr runs a partner model where vendors handle pickup and payment, and Cashkr focuses on customer acquisition and lead quality.

- Customer sell requests appear in the **Cashkr Vendor App** for approved vendors. Availability depends on the vendor’s service areas, device categories, and internal policy.

- Vendors use **credits** to unlock customer details. Credits are a virtual point system that represent Cashkr’s commission on each unlocked lead.

- Vendors must maintain a real money **wallet balance** in the Vendor App. The wallet balance is used to pay customers at pickup through an OTP-based payment flow.

At pickup, the correct payment flow is:

1. Vendor visits the customer, inspects the device, and confirms the final price with the customer.

2. Vendor initiates payment inside the **Cashkr Vendor App** from the wallet.

3. Customer receives an OTP, shares it with the vendor, and the vendor enters it in the app to confirm.

4. The app sends the payment from the vendor’s **wallet** to the customer’s **bank account**, and shows `Payment Successful`.

5. Only after the app shows payment success should the vendor take the device from the customer.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  All payments to customers must go through **wallet → OTP → bank transfer** inside the app.\
  No cash, no external UPI apps, and no offline or side payments are allowed.
</Callout>

Credits and wallet work together:

- **Credits:**

  - Virtual points used to unlock customer details.

  - Deducted when you unlock a lead.

  - Added when you buy more credits and admin approves your payment proof.

  - Not convertible back to cash.

- **Wallet:**

  - Real money balance used to pay customers.

  - Funded by depositing money to your assigned virtual account.

  - Payment to customers always uses wallet and OTP verification before the bank transfer.

For deep training on this, share or review: [Understanding Vendor Credits and Wallet System](/understanding-vendor-credits-and-wallet-system).

## Call flow (recommended for associates)

Use this flow as your internal structure. You can adjust words but keep the sequence and rules.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Greet and position Cashkr" icon="phone" title-type="p">
    - Confirm the vendor’s name and introduce yourself and Cashkr.

    - Position Cashkr as a platform that brings customers who want to sell devices to local resellers.

    - Confirm that the vendor currently buys and sells used devices.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Explain the partner model" icon="users" title-type="p">
    - Explain that Cashkr brings customer sell leads into the Vendor App.

    - Clarify that Cashkr is the bridge, not the buyer: the vendor buys directly from the customer.

    - Emphasize that vendors control their own pricing and margins after inspection.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Explain credits and wallet" icon="database" title-type="p">
    - Describe **credits** as the fee to unlock customer details (Cashkr commission).

    - Describe **wallet** as the real-money balance used to pay customers.

    - Make clear that both credits and wallet are required: credits to unlock, wallet to pay.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set expectations and compliance rules" icon="shield" title-type="p">
    - Clarify that lead volume depends on city, pincode coverage, categories, and policy.

    - State clearly that Cashkr does not guarantee fixed earnings or daily lead counts.

    - Reiterate that all customer payments must be done inside the app using wallet and OTP, with no cash or external UPI.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Guide registration and KYC" icon="rocket" title-type="p">
    - Ask if the vendor is interested to register and get started.

    - If yes, explain that registration and KYC happen in the Vendor App and that admin approval is required before they can see and unlock leads.

    - If they are unsure, offer to share a short document or video explaining the process and benefits.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Close with next steps" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    - Confirm what you will send after the call: app link, registration guide, credits information.

    - Remind them that once approved and active, they can start seeing available orders based on their area and availability.

    - Thank them and confirm any follow-up time if needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Copy-ready call scripts

Use these as your base script. You can adjust small phrases to match your style and the vendor’s language, but do not change rules about payments, credits, or expectations.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="English (Official call script)" icon="book-open">
    **Opening and qualification**

    “Hi \[Vendor Name], this is \[Your Name] from Cashkr.

    We run the **Cashkr Vendor Partner Program** where local resellers and traders earn by buying used phones, laptops, tablets, and Macs directly from customers in their own city.

    Do you currently buy used devices from customers for your business?”

    ---

    **Explain how Cashkr works**

    “On our side, customers place sell requests on Cashkr when they want to sell their devices.

    Based on your approved service areas and device categories, these customer leads can appear in your **Cashkr Vendor App**.

    From there, you choose which leads to work on, visit the customer, inspect the device, and buy it directly from them.”

    ---

    **Credits and wallet explanation**

    “To view full customer details, you **unlock the lead using credits**.

    Credits are our commission for connecting you to the customer. When you unlock, a few credits are deducted, and then the remaining profit from the deal is yours.

    For paying the customer, you use your **wallet balance** inside the app. The app sends the money from your wallet to the customer’s bank account after OTP verification.”

    ---

    **Payment compliance (very clear)**

    “At pickup, you must complete payment **inside the Cashkr Vendor App only**.

    You inspect the device, agree on the final amount, tap pay in the app, enter the OTP the customer gives you, and then the app transfers money from your wallet to their bank account.

    Once the app shows `Payment Successful`, you take the device.

    Cash or external UPI payments outside the app are **not allowed** under our policy.”

    ---

    **Benefits (without guarantees)**

    “This model gives you:

    - Direct, genuine customer leads in your target areas, depending on availability.

    - No middleman in the deal — you handle pricing and negotiation yourself.

    - Freedom to resell, refurbish, or trade the devices as per your business.”

    ---

    **Set expectations**

    “I want to be transparent — Cashkr does **not** promise fixed daily income or guaranteed number of leads.

    Lead flow depends on customer demand, your selected areas, categories, and your own activity in the app.”

    ---

    **Registration and next step (interested)**

    “If you are interested, the next step is:

    1. Download the **Cashkr Vendor App**.

    2. Complete your registration and KYC.

    3. Our team verifies your details and approves your profile.

    4. You add credits and maintain wallet balance so you can unlock and pay for orders.

    Once you are approved and active, you can start seeing available orders for your areas whenever they are available.”

    “Should I send you the app download link and a short registration guide on WhatsApp now?”

    ---

    **If vendor says maybe / wants time**

    “No problem at all.

    I will share a short information document and a video that show how other vendors use Cashkr — how credits and wallet work, and how payment happens with OTP.

    You can review it in your own time, and whenever you are ready, you can register on the app. If you want, I can also schedule a quick follow-up call.”
  </Tab>

    “Pickup ke time pe payment hamesha **Cashkr Vendor App ke through** hi karna hota hai.

    - Aap device inspect karte ho, customer se final amount fix karte ho.

    - App me payment option select karte ho, customer ke paas OTP aata hai.

    - Customer aapko OTP batata hai, aap app me OTP daalte ho, phir app aapke **wallet se seedha customer ke bank account** me paise bhej deta hai.

    - Jab app me `Payment Successful` dikh jaaye tabhi aap device le sakte ho.

    ---

    Leads ka volume depend karta hai customer demand, aapke selected pincode aur category par, aur aap kitna active rehte ho app par.”

    3. Hamari team aapke documents verify karke profile approve karegi.

    4. Uske baad aap credits add kar sakte ho aur wallet me balance rakh sakte ho, taaki aap leads unlock karke customer ko app se payment kar sako.

    Jab aap approve ho jaoge aur active rahoge, tab aapke area ke available orders app me dikhna start ho sakte hain, availability ke hisaab se.

    Kya main abhi aapko app ka link aur registration steps WhatsApp pe bhej du?”

    ---

    **Agar vendor bolta hai – soch ke batata hoon**

    Main aapko ek chhota document aur video bhejta hoon jisme pura process clearly dikhaya gaya hai — credits kaise kaam karte hain, wallet kya hota hai, aur OTP se payment kaise hoti hai.

    Aap aaram se dekh lo, jab aap ready ho jao tab app pe registration kar sakte ho. Zarurat ho to main ek short follow-up call bhi schedule kar sakta/sakti hoon.”
  </Tab>

## Common questions and objections (approved answers)

Use these answers without changing the policy part. You can adjust small words to sound natural but keep the meaning and rules.

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Can you guarantee how much I will earn per day or per month?" default-open="true">
    No, Cashkr does **not** guarantee fixed daily or monthly earnings.

    Your earnings depend on customer demand, your selected areas and categories, your buying price, and how actively you work on leads. Our role is to connect you with genuine sell leads; your profit comes from how you run your business.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Why do I need to pay credits to unlock leads?" default-open="false">
    Credits are the commission you pay to Cashkr for each customer lead you choose to work on.

    We invest in marketing, technology, and support to bring genuine customers. You only spend credits when you unlock a specific lead. After that, the full profit from the deal is your business income.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I pay the customer? Can I pay in cash or UPI directly?" default-open="false">
    All payments to customers must go **inside the Cashkr Vendor App** through wallet and OTP.

    You pay from your app wallet, the customer receives an OTP, you enter that OTP in the app, and the app sends money to the customer’s bank. Cash, external UPI, or manual transfers are not allowed under our policy.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="When will I start seeing leads in my app?" default-open="false">
    You start seeing available orders only **after**:

    - You register and complete KYC,

    - Our team approves your profile and vendor type, and

    - Your areas and categories are active as per our policy.

    Lead flow then depends on real customer demand in your service area, so it can vary day to day. We cannot promise exact counts.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What is the process after I register on the app?" default-open="false">
    After registration and document upload, you verify your phone via OTP and then wait for admin approval.

    Our team reviews your details, sets your vendor type, and activates your account if everything is correct. Only approved vendors can log in and start viewing or unlocking leads.

    For the full flow, see: [Vendor Onboarding Process](/vendor-onboarding-process).
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I send my agent to pick up instead of going myself?" default-open="false">
    Yes, as long as your account setup and our policy for your vendor type allow agents, you can use trained agents.

    However, the agent must follow all Cashkr rules: verify device properly, behave professionally, and complete payment **inside the app using OTP**. You remain responsible for any violation or issue caused by your agent.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What if I do not have wallet balance at pickup time?" default-open="false">
    If your wallet does not have enough balance, you cannot complete the OTP payment to the customer.

    In that case, you must either add money to your wallet first or reschedule the pickup. You should never promise or attempt to pay in cash or from an external UPI app to bypass the wallet requirement.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I add credits to my account?" default-open="false">
    You add credits using the **Buy Credits** option in the Vendor App.

    You typically pay via QR or other accepted method and upload the payment screenshot. Our admin team checks and approves it, and only then are the credits added to your account. Until then, credits remain pending and you may not be able to unlock new leads.

    For full details, see: [Buy Credits](/buy-credits).
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## After-call checklist (internal use)

Use this checklist after each call to keep the process consistent and traceable.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Update disposition and interest level" icon="check" title-type="p">
    - Mark the correct disposition in your calling tool or CRM: Interested, Maybe, Not Interested, Wrong Number, or Call Back.

    - Add brief notes on vendor type, volume, and any key concerns (for example, prefers cash, wants only high-end phones).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send resources on WhatsApp" icon="send" title-type="p">
    - Share the **Vendor App download link**.

    - Attach the **registration and KYC steps** (document or short video).

    - Send **credits and wallet information** or the standard PDF as applicable.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reinforce payment and policy rules" icon="shield" title-type="p">
    - If the vendor asked about cash or external UPI, send a short text template confirming that payments must be done via wallet and OTP in the app.

    - Remind them not to promise customers any payment method outside the app.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Schedule follow-up (if needed)" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    - For `Maybe` or `Interested but busy` vendors, set a clear date and time for follow-up.

    - Note any conditions they mentioned (for example, “will register after 7 pm”, “needs to discuss with partner”).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Monitor activation and escalate issues" icon="alert-triangle" title-type="p">
    - After a few days, check if the vendor has registered and completed KYC.

    - If inactive after 7 days, trigger the **Vendor Reactivation Script** or follow your team’s reactivation SOP.

    - Escalate to the onboarding or operations lead if you notice repeated confusion about payments or policy violations.
  </Step>
</Steps>
