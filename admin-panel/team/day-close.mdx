## Overview

**Sidebar:** B2C Sales → Team → **Day Close** (`/team/day-close`)

Day Close summarises a sales day. Pick a date at the top; for today a badge shows "Day still running · freezes at 8 PM" — the numbers are frozen at the 8 PM cutoff.

## What the screen shows

### The day — totals to the cutoff

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Leads arrived | New leads that came in. |
| First calls made | Leads that got their first call. |
| Same-day call rate | Share of the day's leads called the same day. |
| Orders placed | Orders created. |
| Closed as lost | Leads closed as lost. |
| Retention attempts | Retention calls logged on failed orders. |
| Escalations raised | New escalations. |

### Slipping — what tomorrow inherits

| Item | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Failed orders not called today | In retention, with no attempt logged today — called yesterday still counts as slipping. |
| Follow-ups past their time | A callback was promised and the time has passed. |
| Escalations still open | Raised and not yet resolved, from any day. See [Escalations](/admin-panel/team/escalations). |

<Callout kind="tip">

Use the Slipping card to plan the next morning: start with overdue follow-ups, then uncalled failed orders.

</Callout>
