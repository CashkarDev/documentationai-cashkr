## Adding a new tool

When you add a new analytics or marketing tool here, capture at least:

- **Tool name** – how it appears in the product or in finance/vendor records.
- **Primary use case** – what the team actually uses it for (for example, reporting, attribution, scheduling).
- **Example activity** – a concrete action or workflow that happens in this tool (for example, schedule Instagram posts, build a weekly ROAS report).
- **Owner or team** (optional but recommended) – who maintains access, configuration, and reporting for this tool.
