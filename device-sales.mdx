## Overview

The **Device Sales** page in the **Finance** module shows every device sold from the [All Inventory](/inventory/all-inventory) module, including sale price, margin, GST on margin, and buyer details for both customers and dealers.

<Callout kind="info">

Use Device Sales to reconcile revenue, track per-device margins, and monitor GST on margin for compliance and finance reporting.

</Callout>

---

## What it shows

### Summary cards

At the top of the page, four summary cards display real-time metrics for the currently applied filter or date range.

<ul>
  <li>
    <ParamField body="totalSales" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
      **Total Sales** card showing the total INR value of all devices sold in the current view, based on their final sale prices.
    </ParamField>
  </li>
  <li>
    <ParamField body="totalMargin" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
      **Total Margin** card showing the total profit earned across all displayed sales, calculated as sale price minus cost for each device.
    </ParamField>
  </li>
  <li>
    <ParamField body="gstOnMargin" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
      **GST on Margin** card showing the GST amount applicable on the total margin for the filtered set of sales.
    </ParamField>
  </li>
  <li>
    <ParamField body="itemsSold" param-type="integer" show-location="false">
      **Items Sold** card showing the total number of devices sold in the current filtered view.
    </ParamField>
  </li>
</ul>

## How sales entries are created

Device Sales entries are generated automatically from the All Inventory module when a sale is punched, so you do not need to add rows manually on this page.

```plaintext
Admin selects device(s) in All Inventory
        ↓
Punch Sale confirmed (Dealer / Customer selected)
        ↓
Device removed from All Inventory
        ↓
New row created in Device Sales
        ↓
Summary cards updated (Total Sales, Margin, GST, Items Sold)
```

---

## Margin & GST calculation

Device Sales calculates per-device profit and the GST on that profit, then aggregates these values in the summary cards.

<ul>
  <li>
    <ParamField body="margin" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
      **Margin** is calculated for each device as sale price minus cost price.
    </ParamField>
  </li>
  <li>
    <ParamField body="gstOnMargin" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
      **GST on Margin** applies an 18 percent GST rate on the margin amount for each sale.
    </ParamField>
  </li>
  <li>
    <ParamField body="totalSales" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
      **Total Sales** is the sum of all sale prices for the device rows in the current filtered view.
    </ParamField>
  </li>
  <li>
    <ParamField body="totalMargin" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
      **Total Margin** is the sum of all per-device margin values in the current filtered view.
    </ParamField>
  </li>
</ul>

## Customer types

Buyer information in the sales table distinguishes between individual customers and registered dealers.

<ul>
  <li>
    <ParamField body="customerTypeCustomer" param-type="string" show-location="false">
      **Customer** refers to an individual or walk-in buyer who is not registered as a dealer.
    </ParamField>
  </li>
  <li>
    <ParamField body="customerTypeDealer" param-type="string" show-location="false">
      **Dealer** refers to a registered reseller or bulk buyer whose record comes from the Customers (Dealers) module.
    </ParamField>
  </li>
</ul>

## Connections to other modules

Device Sales ties into other Admin Panel modules to keep inventory, cash flow, and reporting aligned.

<ul>
  <li>
    <ParamField body="allInventory" param-type="relation" show-location="false">
      **All Inventory** provides the devices; punching a sale here removes the device from inventory and creates a corresponding entry in Device Sales.
    </ParamField>
  </li>
  <li>
    <ParamField body="customersDealers" param-type="relation" show-location="false">
      **Customers (Dealers)** supplies dealer records that are used as buyers when you punch a sale.
    </ParamField>
  </li>
  <li>
    <ParamField body="reportsFinance" param-type="relation" show-location="false">
      **Reports** uses Device Sales data as an input to finance and performance reports.
    </ParamField>
  </li>
  <li>
    <ParamField body="cashInHand" param-type="relation" show-location="false">
      **Cash In Hand** reflects cash payment sales recorded in Device Sales as part of overall cash flow tracking.
    </ParamField>
  </li>
  <li>
    <ParamField body="gstFinance" param-type="relation" show-location="false">
      **GST / Finance** uses the GST on margin amounts from Device Sales for GST compliance and finance reporting.
    </ParamField>
  </li>
</ul>
