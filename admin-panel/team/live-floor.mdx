## Overview

**Sidebar:** B2C Sales → Team → **Live Floor** (`/team/live-floor`)

Live Floor shows what the sales and support floor is doing right now. It refreshes automatically every two minutes.

## What the screen shows

### Retention breaches

A red badge at the top right: "*n* retention breaches · past 60 min, uncalled" — failed orders in retention that have gone more than 60 minutes without a call.

### Executive table

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Executive | Sales executive name. |
| Open | Open leads assigned to them. |
| Untouched today | Assigned leads with no activity today. |
| Oldest untouched | Age of their oldest untouched lead (red when old). |
| Calls today | Calls logged today. |
| Last activity | Time since their last logged action ("never" if none). |

<Callout kind="info">

Calls today and Last activity read the actor recorded in the activity log, which only started being recorded recently — both look thin until the team has worked a full day with it.

</Callout>

### CX Support

Lists admins with the **CX** role. If nobody has it, the card says "No CX associates yet" — give someone the CX role and they appear here.

Below the card:

- **Callbacks pending** — floor total of callbacks waiting.
- **Missed during working hours today** — support calls missed while the desk was open.

<Callout kind="tip">

Missed calls during working hours appear only on this screen, never on an associate's own numbers. A rising miss rate means the coverage model needs resizing — it is not a coaching signal. Callbacks carry no assignee, so they are a floor total rather than a per-person column.

</Callout>
