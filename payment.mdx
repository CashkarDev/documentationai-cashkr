## What the Payment screen shows

The Payment screen shows the user's stored payout details that Cashkr uses to send money after a device pickup and order completion. Users can add or update either bank account details or a UPI ID. Customers can also choose Amazon Gift Card as a payment method during the Sell Device order flow (available only for orders under ₹10,000).

<Callout kind="info">

Payment details are stored on the user's profile and used during payout processing after an order is successfully completed. Any change to payment details affects future payouts for that user.

</Callout>

## Step-by-step user flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Payment section" icon="folder" title-type="p">

  - Open the **Profile** menu on the Cashkr Website.
  - Select the **Payment** option to view existing payout details (if any).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Add payout details" icon="plus" title-type="p">

  - On the Payment screen, add either **Bank Details** or a **UPI ID** for receiving payouts.
  - Save the details so they are stored against the user's profile.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Update payout details" icon="edit" title-type="p">

  - When the user edits Bank Details or UPI ID and saves, update the stored payout method on the profile.
  - Reflect the updated details anywhere payouts are calculated or displayed.

  </Step>
</Steps>

### Amazon Gift Card

Customers can choose Amazon Gift Card as their payment method during the Sell Device order flow on the website, alongside Bank, UPI, and Cash. When Amazon Gift Card is selected, no payout is processed at order placement — the system queues gift card issuance. Once the vendor completes the order, the system automatically issues Amazon Gift Card details (gift card code and redemption instructions) to the customer. The gift card is redeemable exclusively on Amazon.

<Callout kind="warning">

Amazon Gift Card is only available for orders with a total value less than ₹10,000 at the time of order creation. Orders of ₹10,000 or above will not show the gift card option — customers must choose Cash, UPI, or Bank transfer instead. The gift card is issued after the vendor completes the order, not at payment selection.

</Callout>

## System behavior and connections

Payment information is part of the user profile and feeds into payout processing and related surfaces.

- Store Bank Account and UPI details as fields on the **user profile**.
- Use these fields during **payout processing** after an order is marked as completed.
- Link the same payment details into:
  - The **admin/vendor payout flow** for calculating and executing payouts.
  - The **customer app**, so changes made on the website appear consistently across platforms.
- When Amazon Gift Card is selected as the payment method, the system does not process a payout at order placement. Upon vendor completion, the system automatically issues Amazon Gift Card details to the customer. See [Amazon Gift Card Payment Option](/amazon-gift-card-payment) for the full feature documentation.

<Callout kind="tip">

Align profile data models across Website, Admin Panel, Vendor flows, and Customer App so a single source of truth for Bank/UPI details drives all payout calculations and displays.

</Callout>

## Developer and tester notes

**For developers**

- Save payment details (Bank Account and UPI ID) securely with **encryption** in the database.
- Add backend validation to:
  - Reject malformed or incomplete bank details.
  - Reject invalid UPI IDs.
- Ensure payout logic uses the **current profile values** so recent updates are available for post-order payouts.
- Propagate profile changes so updated payment details reflect consistently across:
  - **Website**
  - **Admin Panel**
  - **Customer App**

<Callout kind="tip">

Treat payment detail changes as profile-level updates so all future payouts see the latest Bank/UPI details without extra configuration.

</Callout>

**For testers**

- Verify that adding **invalid Bank details** or an **invalid UPI ID** shows a clear error and does not save the record.
- Verify that **updates reflect across systems**:
  - Change existing Bank or UPI details on the Payment screen.
  - Confirm the updated values:
    - Display correctly on the Payment screen.
    - Are available in the Admin Panel and any payout-related views.
    - Appear correctly in the Customer App.
- During an **end-to-end order flow**, confirm that after order completion, payout processing is linked to the stored Bank/UPI details on the user's profile.
- Verify that Amazon Gift Card option appears only for orders with total value less than ₹10,000. Create an order above ₹10,000 and confirm the gift card option is not shown.
- Complete an order with Amazon Gift Card selected (via vendor app) and verify that gift card details are delivered to the customer after completion, not at order placement.
