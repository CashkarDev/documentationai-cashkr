## Purpose

<Callout kind="info">

Cashkr uses Slack as the primary communication tool, supported by a clear meeting rhythm and structured reporting. These norms keep teams aligned, responsive, and focused.

</Callout>

## Working hours and escalation

- **Standard working hours:** 10:00 AM – 6:30 PM, Monday to Saturday.
- **Primary tool:** Use Slack for all day-to-day work communication; minimize email.
- **Urgent issues after hours:** Use the escalation matrix in the Team Directory for critical issues that cannot wait.
- **Non-urgent issues after hours:** Post in the relevant Slack channel and expect a response in the next working day.

## Communication modes

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Slack">

Slack is the default channel for internal communication and updates.

### Channels

Use the correct channel so information is visible to everyone who needs it:

- **#announcements** — Company-wide updates from the CEO and management.
- **#marketing** — Campaigns, performance reports, creative approvals.
- **#ops** — Vendor communication, SLAs, order assignments, operational queries.
- **#tech** — Bugs, product updates, releases, testing coordination.
- **#general** — Informal, non-work conversations and cultural updates.

### Direct messages and response time

- Use DMs only for **sensitive** topics or **urgent** matters.
- For everything else, post in the appropriate channel for transparency and future reference.
- During working hours (10:00 AM – 6:30 PM), aim to respond within **1–2 hours**.
- Outside working hours, respond only to urgent escalations as per the escalation matrix.

<Callout kind="tip">

Prefer channels over DMs. Post in a channel first, then follow up in DM only if you need to share sensitive details or handle an urgent 1:1 clarification.

</Callout>

</Tab>

<Tab title="Meetings">

Meetings exist to unblock work, align priorities, and share context that is difficult to handle in Slack alone.

### Meeting rhythm

- **Daily standups (optional, per team):**  
  10–15 minutes to review yesterday, today, and blockers when a team needs tighter coordination.
- **Weekly department meetings:**  
  Review progress, key metrics, blockers, and priorities for the upcoming week.
- **Monthly all-hands (CEO-led):**  
  Company performance, key wins, challenges, and upcoming goals.

### Meeting etiquette

- Join on time and stay present; keep your camera on during remote calls when possible.
- Come prepared with a clear agenda and your updates.
- Capture key decisions, notes, and action items and post them in the relevant Slack channel after the meeting.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Reporting">

Reporting keeps leadership and teams aligned on performance and priorities across functions.

### Daily reports

- Teams such as **Operations, Marketing, Tech, and Sales** share daily reports in their respective Slack channels.
- Use bullet points, clear metrics, and links or screenshots where relevant.

### Weekly reports

- Share weekly summaries in Slack, then record the same data in **Zoho Sheets**.
- Focus on trends, key metrics, major work completed, and issues that require decisions.

### Monthly reports

- Managers consolidate team reports into a monthly view.
- Managers review these with the CEO, highlighting performance, key learnings, and next-month priorities.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Golden rules

1. Use **Slack first** for company communication; minimize the use of email.
2. Respect working hours: **10:00 AM – 6:30 PM, Monday to Saturday**.
3. Follow the **Team Directory and Escalation Matrix** for urgent issues and after-hours escalations.
4. Keep all communication **short, structured, and actionable** so others can respond and execute quickly.
