**Purpose:** Standardize how orders are created, vendors are assigned or reassigned, and SLAs are maintained for smooth pickups.

<Callout kind="info">

Fast and accurate vendor assignment is critical for pickup speed, order completion rate, and customer satisfaction. Follow this module as the single source of truth.

</Callout>

## 1. Module goals

You use this module to align all teams on how orders move from creation to successful vendor pickup.

Key outcomes:

- Faster pickups and fewer delays
- Accurate vendor assignment based on coverage, wallet, credits, and performance
- Higher order completion and lower cancellation rates
- Clear ownership across CX, Ops, Vendor Ops, and BD

## 2. How orders are created

Orders enter the system in two ways: directly by customers or manually by internal teams.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Customer self-creation" icon="users">

Customers create most orders themselves via the website or app.

The customer:

- Selects the device
- Selects the device condition
- Views the offered price
- Enters personal details
- Enters address and pincode
- Confirms the order

After confirmation:

- The order appears in the Admin Panel under `Orders → Pending Vendor Assignment`.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Manual creation (Admin Panel)" icon="terminal">

Sales and CX teams create orders manually when customers need help.

Use manual creation when:

- Customer calls and needs assistance placing an order
- Customer cannot complete checkout on their own
- Customer needs manual support to confirm details
- Lead conversion requires admin-side order creation

<Steps>

<Step title="Open order creation in Admin Panel" icon="monitor">

- Go to `Orders → Create Order` in the Admin Panel.

</Step>

<Step title="Capture customer and device details" icon="edit">

- Enter customer details.
- Select the device and model.
- Select the correct device condition.

</Step>

<Step title="Add address and confirm" icon="map">

- Enter the full address and pincode.
- Review the price with the customer.
- Confirm the order.

</Step>

<Step title="Verify order status" icon="check-circle">

- Confirm that the order appears under `Orders → Pending Vendor Assignment`.

</Step>

</Steps>

</Tab>

</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">

Always confirm the final price and pickup pincode with the customer before creating or confirming any order.

</Callout>

## 3. Vendor assignment process

Once an order is in `Pending Vendor Assignment`, a vendor must be assigned either by the system or by Ops.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Auto-assignment (default)" icon="zap">

The system auto-assigns vendors whenever possible. This is the default and preferred method.

The system selects a vendor based on:

- Pincode match with the order address
- Device type support (phone, tablet, laptop, Mac)
- Vendor wallet and credits balance
- Vendor SLA score and performance
- Distance and current availability
- Past performance and reliability

When auto-assignment succeeds:

- The selected vendor sees the order and can unlock it if they meet the unlock conditions.
- Once unlocked, the vendor is officially assigned and notifications are triggered.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Manual assignment (Ops / Vendor Ops)" icon="settings">

Ops or Vendor Ops assign vendors manually when system auto-assignment is not enough.

Use manual assignment when:

- No vendor is available automatically
- Assigned vendor is unresponsive
- Vendor needs replacement
- Order is high-value or urgent
- Customer has a special request that needs a specific vendor

<Steps>

<Step title="Open pending orders" icon="folder">

- Go to `Orders → Pending Assignment`.

</Step>

<Step title="Start vendor assignment" icon="plus">

- Select the target order.
- Click `Assign Vendor`.

</Step>

<Step title="Review eligible vendors" icon="bar-chart">

- View the list of eligible vendors.
- Check each vendor’s SLA score, credits, wallet balance, and device coverage.

</Step>

<Step title="Assign the best vendor" icon="check-circle">

- Select the vendor best suited for the order.
- Confirm the assignment and monitor vendor acceptance within SLA.

</Step>

</Steps>

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## 4. Vendor unlock logic

A vendor must meet specific conditions before unlocking and accepting an order.

Before accepting an order, the vendor must:

- Maintain the minimum wallet balance needed to pay the customer
- Have sufficient credits to unlock the order
- Cover the requested device type (phone, laptop, tablet, Mac, and so on)
- Be within the serviceable area range for the customer address

Only when all conditions are satisfied:

- The vendor can unlock the order.
- Once unlocked, the vendor is officially assigned.
- Both the customer and the vendor receive notifications about the assignment.

<Callout kind="info">

If a vendor fails unlock conditions due to wallet or credits, treat it as a supply or readiness issue and coordinate with Vendor Ops and BD as needed.

</Callout>

## 5. Reassignment rules

Reassignment ensures the customer is not stuck when the current vendor fails to progress.

Trigger reassignment when any of these apply:

- **Vendor not responding**
  - No call made to the customer within the acceptance SLA time (15 minutes)
  - Vendor is unreachable or phone is switched off
- **Vendor declines pickup**
  - Vendor is not available
  - Vendor does not support the device type
  - Vendor raises consistent delay issues
- **Customer requests a different time**
  - Current vendor is not available at the new time
  - You need to assign a new vendor who can meet the updated schedule
- **Vendor violates behaviour rules**
  - CX reports misbehaviour or policy violation
  - Ops must reassign immediately and flag the vendor
- **Vendor lacks wallet or credits**
  - Vendor does not have enough balance or credits to continue
  - Order must be moved to another eligible vendor

<Callout kind="alert">

Reassign within the SLA window to protect customer experience. Do not wait for the vendor to come back if they are clearly non-responsive or non-compliant.

</Callout>

## 6. Reassignment procedure (Ops / CX)

Use this standard flow whenever you reassign an order from one vendor to another.

<Steps>

<Step title="Check current vendor status" icon="info">

- Review the current vendor’s activity and timeline on the order.
- Confirm the actual reason for delay or failure (no call, unreachable, decline, behaviour issue, or low wallet or credits).

</Step>

<Step title="Document the reason" icon="edit">

- Add a clear internal note explaining the reason for reassignment.
- Mention any customer communication and vendor response (or lack of response).

</Step>

<Step title="Initiate reassignment" icon="settings">

- Click `Reassign Vendor` on the order.
- Open the list of eligible replacement vendors.

</Step>

<Step title="Select the next best vendor" icon="users">

- Choose the next best eligible vendor based on:
  - SLA score and performance
  - Credits and wallet balance
  - Device coverage and service area

</Step>

<Step title="Notify the customer" icon="phone">

- Inform the customer about the vendor change.
- Share updated expectations for call and pickup timing.

</Step>

<Step title="Monitor the new vendor" icon="check-circle">

- Track the new vendor’s response and actions.
- Ensure they accept, call, and reach the customer within SLA.

</Step>

</Steps>

## 7. SLA standards for vendor assignment and pickup

Use these SLAs as hard timelines for tracking vendor performance and deciding when to intervene.

- **Vendor acceptance after assignment:** within 15 minutes  
  Vendor must accept the order after assignment or unlocking within 15 minutes.

- **Vendor calling the customer:** within 20 minutes  
  After accepting the order, the vendor must call the customer within 20 minutes.

- **Vendor reaching the customer:**
  - Within the scheduled appointment time, or
  - Within 60 minutes if the customer selected ASAP pickup

- **Reassignment when vendor fails:**
  - Start reassignment within 10 minutes after detecting SLA failure or non-response

<Callout kind="tip">

CX and Ops should monitor these four checkpoints: assignment, acceptance, first call, and reach time. Escalate or reassign as soon as any SLA is missed.

</Callout>

## 8. Team responsibilities

Clear ownership across teams prevents orders from getting stuck.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="CX" icon="users">

CX owns customer communication and first-line monitoring.

CX must:

- Monitor order tracking for all active orders
- Ensure vendor calls the customer within the defined SLA
- Contact the vendor if there is no update or missed call SLA
- Communicate ETA and updates clearly to the customer
- Trigger reassignment if the vendor is unresponsive or non-compliant
- Add internal notes for every action and conversation
- Update the customer on vendor details, changes, and next steps

</Tab>

<Tab title="Operations" icon="settings">

Ops owns assignment logic, escalations, and overall flow.

Ops must:

- Monitor all pending and assigned orders in the system
- Ensure timely vendor acceptance and response
- Approve or execute vendor reassignment when needed
- Support CX in escalated or complex issues
- Coordinate with Vendor Ops when there are vendor shortages or repeated SLA issues

</Tab>

<Tab title="Vendor Ops" icon="database">

Vendor Ops owns vendor supply, quality, and readiness.

Vendor Ops must:

- Ensure enough active vendors across all areas
- Monitor vendor availability per city and device type
- Support reassignment when local supply is low
- Handle vendors who repeatedly miss orders, SLAs, or behaviour standards

</Tab>

<Tab title="Business Development" icon="bar-chart">

BD ensures overall serviceability so that incoming orders always have vendor coverage.

BD must:

- Identify pincode gaps where orders are created but no vendors are available
- Coordinate with Vendor Ops to onboard vendors for uncovered or weak areas
- Track high-value areas that need more vendor supply
- Share weekly coverage reports with Ops and management
- Support expansion into new cities to avoid unassigned orders

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## 9. Notifications during assignment

Timely notifications keep both customer and vendor aligned on the order status.

### Customer notifications

The customer receives:

- **Order created:** confirmation that the order is placed and under processing
- **Vendor assigned:** once a vendor unlocks and accepts the order
- **Vendor en route:** when the vendor taps `Start Trip` in the app

Clear and prompt communication builds trust and improves conversion to successful pickup.

### Vendor notifications

The vendor receives:

- **New order available:** when the system or Ops routes an order to the vendor
- **Unlock requirements:** wallet and credits conditions, device type, and other criteria
- **Customer details after unlock:** contact information and address after successful unlock
- **Route navigation:** access to the route and location for pickup
- **Payment process guidelines:** clear guidance on how to complete payment at pickup

## 10. One-page summary (training card)

Use this as a quick recap during training or daily stand-up.

**Order creation**

- Customer self-creates via website or app  
- CX or Sales creates manually in Admin Panel when needed  
- All new orders move to `Pending Vendor Assignment`

**Vendor assignment**

- Default: system auto-assignment based on pincode, device, wallet, credits, SLA score, distance, and performance  
- Manual: Ops or Vendor Ops assign when auto-assignment fails or special handling is required  
- Vendor must have enough wallet balance and credits and must cover the device type  
- Vendor unlocks the order, becomes officially assigned, and notifications go out

**Reassignment**

- Trigger reassignment when vendor is:
  - Unresponsive or not calling within SLA
  - Not available or not supporting device type
  - Violating SLA or behaviour rules
  - Out of coverage or lacking wallet or credits
- Follow the reassignment procedure and always log internal notes

**Team roles**

- **CX:** tracking orders and handling all customer communication
- **Ops:** assignment, reassignment, and escalation management
- **Vendor Ops:** vendor supply, quality control, and compliance
- **BD:** pincode coverage, vendor onboarding, and expansion planning

<Callout kind="info">

If you are unsure whether to wait longer or reassign, default to protecting the customer experience: check SLAs, document the situation, and escalate to Ops or Vendor Ops.

</Callout>
