## Overview

**Sidebar:** B2C Sales → Team → **Assignment** (`/team/assignment`)

Assignment is where a team lead hands unassigned B2C leads to sales executives. It has three parts: **Distribute a batch** (bulk split), **Unassigned** (the open queue) and **Executives** (current load per person, with Move leads).

<Callout kind="alert">

A banner at the top shows how many open leads are unassigned and how many arrived today. Auto-assignment exists as a scheduled job; if the unassigned number keeps growing, the job is not running. Assigning by hand at high volume is not a fix — raise it with tech.

</Callout>

## Distribute a batch

"Highest quote first, never called, split across everyone receiving leads."

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Give to | Chips for every executive receiving leads. All are selected by default — click a name to leave them out of this batch. |
| Leads per executive | How many leads each selected executive gets (default 60). |
| Quote from (₹) | Minimum quoted price. |
| To (₹) | Maximum quoted price — **0 for no cap**. |
| Device | Any, Mobile, Laptop, Tablet, Mac, Watch, Console, Camera, Headphone, Drone, Desktop, TV, AC. |
| Arrived | Today, Last 3 days, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, The whole backlog, Custom range… |

- **Check** previews what the batch would assign.
- **Distribute** assigns the batch.

How distribution works:

- Leads are taken highest quote first, and only leads that were never called.
- A customer who already has one of the chosen executives goes back to that executive; the rest is dealt evenly.
- Nothing already assigned is touched, so pressing Distribute twice tops the team up rather than doubling anyone.

## Unassigned

The open, unassigned queue — total count with the newest 25 shown. Each row shows customer name, device, lead ID, city, quoted price and age, with a checkbox.

Tick one or more leads and the Executives card switches to "Assign *n* selected to…", with an **Assign here** button on each executive.

## Executives

"Current load" for each executive receiving leads:

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Open | Open leads assigned to them. |
| Untouched | Assigned leads with no activity yet. |
| Today | Leads assigned today. |
| Oldest | Age of their oldest open lead. |

### Move leads

Click **Move leads** on an executive to open "Move *name*'s leads":

- **Which leads** — Promised callbacks, Never called, or Both.
- **How many** — number of leads to move (default 50).
- **Destination** — to the pool (unassigned) or to another named executive.
- **Move** applies it; **Cancel** closes the panel.

<Callout kind="info">

Promised callbacks move soonest-first — those are the ones that break if nobody picks them up. Leads already in conversation stay put: handing a live call to a stranger is usually worse for the customer than waiting.

</Callout>
