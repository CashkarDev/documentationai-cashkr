## Overview

**Sidebar path:** Dashboards → OPERATING → Inventory & Pricing → **Inventory** tab (`/dashboard/inventory`)

The **Inventory Dashboard** shows stock levels, sales performance and inventory health for devices Cashkr holds for resale. The second tab, **Pricing**, is documented on the Pricing page.

## Filters

| Control | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Date range** | Scopes period metrics (added, sold, revenue, profit). |
| **Refresh** | Reloads the data. |

## What the screen shows

### KPI cards

In Stock, Inventory Value, Added, Sold, Revenue, Profit, Margin %, Aging Items.

### Health

A score with a *Critical* → *Optimal* bar, summarising Turnover, Aging %, Margin % and Avg Hold (days).

### Panels

| Panel | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Stock Overview** | Total In Stock, Inventory Value, Avg Cost Price, All-Time Sold, Avg Hold Days. |
| **Sales Performance** | Items Sold, Revenue, Profit, Margin, Potential Revenue. |
| **Aging Analysis** | Units by holding time: 0–7d, 8–15d, 16–30d, 31–60d, 60+d, and a fresh vs aging bar. |
| **Supply Sources** | Units and value by source: Excess, B2B, Walk-in, B2C; plus a **By condition** split. |
| **Daily Inventory Flow** | Added vs sold per day. |

### Detail tabs

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Top Devices** | Top-selling devices: Device, Sold, Revenue, Cost, Profit, Margin. |
| **Aging Inventory** | Units in stock by age. |
| **Sales History** | Sold units. |
| **Vendor Supply** | Units supplied by vendor. |
| **B2B Deals** | Inventory from B2B buyback deals. |

## Actions

The dashboard is read-only. To edit stock records, use **Inventory & Catalog → All Inventory**.

<Callout kind="tip">
Watch the **60+d** aging bucket. Units held longer than 90 days count as dead stock on the Executive and CFO dashboards.
</Callout>
