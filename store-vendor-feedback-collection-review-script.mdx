<Callout kind="info">

**Used by:** Store Partnership / Channel Expansion / Onboarding Associates

**Purpose:** Train onboarding associates on how to collect, categorize, and report feedback from active B2Ch store vendors, covering app experience, store coordination, payment delays, and policy improvements.

**Objective:** Ensure associates:

- Regularly collect structured feedback from vendors
- Identify and escalate operational or technical issues early
- Track satisfaction trends and highlight recurring problem areas
- Maintain a feedback-to-action loop with Operations and Product teams

</Callout>

## Internal training script for onboarding associates

Vendor feedback collection is a recurring activity, not a one-time task. Use this script and structure to keep conversations consistent and actionable.

### Importance of vendor feedback

“Store vendors are our on-ground partners — they directly interact with customers and store staff daily.  

Their feedback helps us improve the app, speed up payments, simplify processes, and maintain smooth collaboration.  

Feedback is not a complaint — it is a health check of our network.”

### When and how to collect feedback

Use these three structured touchpoints for vendor feedback. At each touchpoint, confirm experience across app, store coordination, payments, and support.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Post-onboarding check (Day 3–5)" icon="download">

  Use this call to ensure smooth login, wallet setup, and the first few orders.

  - **Objective:** Confirm that the vendor has logged in, understood the app flow, and completed at least one order.
  - **Recommended channels:** WhatsApp call as primary, with a follow-up Google Form or in-app survey link if needed.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Monthly review" icon="calendar">

  Run a short check-in to understand operational issues or repetitive blockers.

  - **Objective:** Capture any friction in daily usage, store coordination, or payouts before it becomes serious.
  - **Recommended channels:** Scheduled WhatsApp call or phone call; share a Google Form for additional written feedback.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Quarterly quality audit" icon="check-circle">

  Conduct a deeper review focused on overall quality of collaboration.

  - **Objective:** Capture detailed feedback on Cashkr system, store coordination, and customer flow, plus improvement ideas.
  - **Recommended channels:** Longer WhatsApp or phone call, plus structured Google Form or in-app survey for documentation.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">

Treat these touchpoints as relationship check-ins, not surveys. Keep the tone conversational and focused on making the vendor’s work smoother.

</Callout>

## Starting the feedback conversation

<ExpandableGroup>

  <Expandable title="Guidance: tone and opening line" default-open="true">

  “Always keep tone friendly and open — vendors should feel heard, not interrogated.”

  Example opening:

  “Hi \[Vendor Name], I’m calling from Cashkr Ops Team. Just checking how your experience has been so far with the store orders — everything going smooth?”

  Start simple, then move into specific questions once the vendor is comfortable.

  </Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Core questions to ask (feedback template)

Use these structured question sets while talking to vendors or collecting written feedback. Choose the tab based on the area you are focusing on, but try to cover all four during a full review.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="App and tech feedback">

  “Use this as your standard format while talking to vendors or collecting written feedback.”

  - Is the app smooth and fast during order creation?
  - Any errors or issues with OTP or payment screens?
  - How easy is it to upload ID and verify customer info?

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Store coordination">

  - Are the assigned stores cooperative and responsive?
  - Any confusion on order ownership or payout flow?
  - Do you face pressure from store staff for higher offers?

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Payment and wallet">

  - Any delays in wallet top-up reflection?
  - Have all customer payments been smooth?
  - Any reversals or missing credits noticed?

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Operations and support">

  - Are you satisfied with Cashkr’s vendor support response time?
  - Have you received clarity on your SLA and reporting process?
  - What one improvement would make your work easier?

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Handling feedback, patterns, and escalation

<ExpandableGroup>

  <Expandable title="Handling negative feedback">

  “If vendor shares a complaint, acknowledge and reassure — do not justify or dismiss.”

  Example:

  “Got it sir, thank you for highlighting that. I’ll forward this to our Ops Manager right away. We’ll review and get back with a resolution.”

  Log such cases immediately in the Feedback Tracker Sheet or Ops Ticket Portal under category:

  - Tech Issue
  - Payment Delay
  - Store Conflict
  - App Bug
  - Support Delay

  <Callout kind="alert">

  - Always acknowledge and thank the vendor for sharing the issue.
  - Do not argue, blame the vendor, or make promises you cannot keep.
  - Log every negative feedback case on the same day in the Feedback Tracker or Ops Ticket Portal.

  </Callout>

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Identifying patterns (recurring concerns)">

  “If 3 or more vendors mention the same issue within a week (like app lag or delayed reversals), mark it as a recurring concern.  

  Tag these under a common label in the feedback sheet — this helps the Product or Tech team prioritize real-world problems faster.”

  <Callout kind="alert">

  - Treat any issue mentioned by 3 or more vendors in a week as a **recurring concern**.
  - Use a consistent label for the same issue in the feedback tracker to make patterns visible.

  </Callout>

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Feedback escalation flow">

  “The escalation process is straightforward:  

  1️⃣ Collect – Associate records vendor’s issue or suggestion.  

  2️⃣ Categorize – Classify it (App / Store / Wallet / Support).  

  3️⃣ Submit – Enter in Feedback Tracker Sheet (daily).  

  4️⃣ Review – Ops Manager reviews and assigns resolution to relevant team.  

  5️⃣ Close loop – Update vendor once resolved.

  Every piece of feedback must end with communication back to the vendor.”

  <Callout kind="alert">

  - Do not keep feedback only in personal notes or chats — always record it in the official tracker.
  - Always close the loop back to the vendor once there is an update or resolution.

  </Callout>

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Recognition of positive feedback">

  “Encourage vendors to share what is working too — not just issues.

  Positive mentions (like smooth app flow, on-time payments, or helpful support) should be logged under ‘Success Mentions’.  

  Every quarter, the Ops Manager highlights top-performing vendors in the Vendor Appreciation Board — this boosts morale and retention.”

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Quarterly review and report submission">

  “At the end of every quarter, associates must prepare a Vendor Feedback Summary Report with these metrics:

  - Total vendors active vs. responded
  - Percentage satisfied with app and payment
  - Common issues raised
  - Percentage of resolved vs. pending concerns
  - Top improvement suggestions

  This report helps management track network satisfaction and product improvement roadmap.”

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Closing feedback conversations">

  “Always close the conversation positively, even if there were complaints.”

  Example:

  “Thanks for sharing your feedback, sir. Your points really help us improve the system for everyone. We’ll make sure your concern is looked into and keep you posted.”

  Respectful closure builds long-term cooperation.

  </Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Responsibilities and cadence

Use this as a quick reference for who does what, how often, and what the expected output is.

### Post-onboarding check (Day 3–5)

- **Owner:** Onboarding Associate  
- **Frequency:** Once per new store vendor, between Day 3 and Day 5 after activation  
- **Output:** Confirmation that vendor is comfortable with login, wallet, and first orders; key notes added to Feedback Tracker

### Monthly vendor feedback

- **Owner:** Ops Associate  
- **Frequency:** Every 30 days for active vendors  
- **Output:** Updated issue log and feedback entries in the Feedback Tracker, with categories and priority

### Quarterly vendor review

- **Owner:** Ops plus Channel Team  
- **Frequency:** Every 90 days  
- **Output:** Consolidated Vendor Feedback Summary Report with metrics, recurring concerns, and improvement suggestions

### Escalation handling

- **Owner:** Ops Manager  
- **Frequency:** As and when escalations are submitted  
- **Output:** Assigned ticket or task for resolution, status updates, and documented closure with vendor update

### Vendor recognition

- **Owner:** HR or Ops (as per internal allocation)  
- **Frequency:** Quarterly  
- **Output:** Vendor Appreciation Board updates, recognition communication, and list of positive feedback or ‘Success Mentions’

## Key talking points and tone guidelines

<Callout kind="tip">

Use these reminders during every feedback conversation:

- “Feedback is insight — not a complaint.”
- “Always listen fully before suggesting solutions.”
- “Document every pattern; data helps prevent bigger problems.”
- “Keep vendors updated after their issue is resolved.”
- “Celebrate good feedback — it builds motivation.”

</Callout>
