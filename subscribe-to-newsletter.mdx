## Subscribe to the BigboldTech newsletter

Stay updated with new articles, product recommendations, and other curated content by subscribing from the website footer.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the newsletter signup form">

Scroll to the bottom of any BigboldTech page and find the **Newsletter Signup** form in the footer.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter your email address">

Type your **email address** into the newsletter field in the footer form.

</Step>

<Step title="Click Subscribe">

Select **Subscribe** to submit your email address and join the mailing list. A confirmation email is sent to the address you entered.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm your email">

Open the confirmation email, then click the **verification link** to activate your subscription and start receiving updates.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What to expect after subscribing

After you verify your email, you start receiving newsletter updates from BigboldTech at the address you used during signup.

<Callout kind="info">

If you do not see the confirmation email within a few minutes, check your **spam**, **junk**, or **promotions** folders and mark the message as not spam before clicking the verification link.

</Callout>
