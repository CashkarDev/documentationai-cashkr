This Help Center is for the **internal tech and ops teams**. It answers "why is this happening" questions about live Cashkr behaviour, and points you at the canonical page when the answer is longer than a paragraph.

<Callout kind="tip">
Before opening a ticket, search this site. Most "bugs" reported against Cashkr turn out to be documented business rules — tier gating, credit limits, or the reschedule window.
</Callout>

## Escalation paths

Match the problem to the owning team rather than broadcasting to everyone.

| Symptom | Owner | Where it is documented |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wrong quote, deduction, or dead price | Pricing | [Price evaluation logic](/in-depth-device-price-evaluation-logic) |
| Vendor cannot see or unlock an order | Vendor ops | [Tiers](/tiers-system), [Credits](/credit-system) |
| Pincode not serviceable | Expansion | [Area](/area) |
| Customer blocked or flagged | Trust & safety | [Customer blocking](/customer-account-blocking-feature) |
| Payout or settlement discrepancy | Finance | [Credits](/credits) |
| Notification not delivered | Platform | [Customer notifications](/customer-notification) |
| Analytics event missing | Data | [Event implementation guide](/event-implementation-guide-for-tech-team-web-app) |

<Callout kind="alert">
Anything involving a **stolen device**, a suspected fraudulent order, or customer data exposure goes to trust and safety immediately — do not sit on it while you investigate.
</Callout>
