## Overview

**Sidebar path:** Orders → CLOSED → **Final Price Edits** (`/orders/final-price-edits`)

Final Price Edits lists every correction made to a completed order's Final Price. It is a read-only log: corrections are made on the order itself, and each one is recorded here.

## What the table shows

The page shows the latest 200 corrections.

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Date** | When the correction was made |
| **Order** | The order that was corrected |
| **Was** | Final Price before the correction |
| **Now** | Final Price after the correction |
| **By** | Admin who made the correction |
| **Reason** | Reason entered for the correction |

When nothing has been corrected yet, the page shows *No corrections yet.*

## How to correct a Final Price

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the completed order" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    Find the order in any Orders view and click it to open the [order drawer](/all-orders#order-detail-drawer).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the pencil on the Final Price card" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    In the **Pricing** section, click the pencil on the **Final Price** card and enter the corrected amount and the reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the log" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    The correction appears at the top of Final Price Edits with your name and reason.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
The Final Price feeds finance reports, GST and the vendor's records. Correct it only with a clear reason, and write the reason so someone else can follow it later.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info">
Which roles can see the pencil, and whether a correction also adjusts vendor credits, is not shown on the screen. Confirm with the tech team before relying on either.
</Callout>
