### 1. Overview

Review Refund is a vendor-incentive feature. After a vendor completes a device order, they can upload a photo of the review the customer provided. Operations verifies that photo in the Admin panel. If approved, the vendor is rewarded with credit equal to 5% of the device's credit value, added to the vendor's total available credit. If rejected, no credit is granted.

The feature exists to encourage vendors to collect genuine customer reviews — improving review volume and social proof — while keeping a human verification step to prevent fraudulent or invalid submissions from earning credit.

Worked example: if a device carries a credit value of 30, an approved review earns the vendor 30 × 5% = 1.5 credit, added to their running total. Because this result is fractional, the exact rounding/precision behavior must be defined (see 5.2 and 11).

Goals and non-goals:

| In scope                                                 | Out of scope                                      |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Vendor upload of a customer-review photo post-completion | Customer-facing review submission on marketplaces |
| Ops verification (approve / reject) in Admin             | Payout / withdrawal of accumulated credit         |
| 5% credit award on approval                              | Device pricing / credit-value calculation itself  |
| Vendor credit ledger update & audit                      | The original order completion workflow            |

### 3. End-to-end flow

Happy path:

1. Vendor completes the device order. The Upload Review option becomes available for that order.
2. Vendor captures/uploads a photo of the customer's review and submits it. Review state → SUBMITTED.
3. The submission appears in Admin → Review Refund for the Operations team.
4. Ops opens the submission, verifies the review photo, and records a decision.
5. If Approved: system computes 5% of the device credit value and adds it to the vendor's total available credit. State → APPROVED.
6. If Rejected: no credit is granted. State → REJECTED (with reason, if captured).
7. Vendor sees the updated credit (on approval) and/or the decision status for the order.

Flow diagram:

```typescript
Vendor completes order
        |
        v
[Upload Review] --photo--> Review = SUBMITTED
        |
        v
Admin > Review Refund  (Ops sees pending submission)
        |
   Ops verifies
        |
    +---+-----------------------------+
    |                                 |
 APPROVED                          REJECTED
    |                                 |
 credit = 5% x device_credit      no credit
    |                             (reason logged)
    v
 vendor.total_credit += credit
    |
    v

```

### 4. Review lifecycle (state machine)

| State         | Meaning                                           | Terminal? |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| (none)        | Order completed; no review uploaded yet.          | —         |
| SUBMITTED     | Vendor uploaded a review photo; awaiting Ops.     | No        |
| UNDER\_REVIEW | Ops has opened/claimed the submission (optional). | No        |
| APPROVED      | Verified valid; 5% credit granted to vendor.      | Yes       |
| REJECTED      | Verified invalid; no credit granted.              | Yes       |

Valid transitions:

```typescript
(none)        -> SUBMITTED
SUBMITTED     -> UNDER_REVIEW | APPROVED | REJECTED
UNDER_REVIEW  -> APPROVED | REJECTED
APPROVED      -> (terminal)
REJECTED      -> (terminal)   [confirm: is re-upload allowed? see 11]
```

Invariant: credit is granted only on the transition to APPROVED, and exactly once per review. No other transition may modify vendor credit.

### 5. Business rules & credit calculation

5.1 The rule — Award = device\_credit\_value × 5%, granted to the vendor's total available credit on approval. The percentage (5%) should be configurable rather than hard-coded, so Ops/Product can change it without a release.

5.2 Rounding & precision (must be confirmed) — Because 5% of many credit values is fractional (e.g. 30 → 1.5), the system must define one consistent behavior across backend calculation, database storage, API response, and vendor-facing display:

| Behavior              | 30 × 5%      | Impact                                                  |
| --------------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Store exact decimal   | 1.5          | Most accurate; requires decimal-safe storage & display. |
| Round half up         | 2 (from 1.5) | Favors vendor; platform cost grows at scale.            |
| Round down / truncate | 1 (from 1.5) | Favors platform; low-value devices may earn 0.          |
| Round to 2 decimals   | 1.50         | Common for wallet balances; define tie-breaking.        |

Decision required: pick one rule and apply it identically everywhere. A frequent bug is the DB storing 1.5 while the UI shows 2 — the vendor's visible balance then drifts from the real one.

5.3 Boundary values:

| Device credit | 5% raw | What it probes                                 |
| ------------- | ------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 30            | 1.5    | The .5 rounding boundary (primary example)     |
| 20            | 1.0    | Clean whole number (control)                   |
| 10            | 0.5    | Rounds to 0 or 1? risk of zero reward          |
| 5             | 0.25   | Sub-half fraction                              |
| 1             | 0.05   | Minimum; likely rounds to 0                    |
| 0             | 0      | Zero-credit device — is approval even allowed? |
| 99            | 4.95   | Awkward near-whole decimal                     |
| 33            | 1.65   | Two-decimal precision                          |

5.4 Accumulation rule — The vendor's total must equal the exact sum of individual awards. Three approvals of 1.5 must total 4.5, not 4 or 5. Decide whether rounding happens per award or only on display of the total; mixing the two causes drift.

### 8. Edge cases & failure handling

| Scenario                              | Expected behavior                                          |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Upload before order completed         | Upload option unavailable; direct API attempt rejected.    |
| Duplicate upload for same order       | Blocked, or replaces prior SUBMITTED — confirm rule (11).  |
| Re-upload after rejection             | Confirm whether allowed; if yes, prevent multiple credits. |
| Approve double-click / retry          | Idempotent — credit granted exactly once.                  |
| Two Ops approve simultaneously        | Only one succeeds; no double credit (locking/idempotency). |
| Invalid / corrupt / oversized image   | Rejected at upload with a clear error; not submittable.    |
| Zero-credit device                    | Define: allow approval with 0 award, or block? (11)        |
| Order cancelled/reversed after credit | Define reversal: claw back the awarded credit or not.      |
| Vendor deactivated before approval    | Define: hold, reject, or credit on reactivation.           |

### 10. Non-functional checks

Performance: Admin Review Refund list loads within target even with a large pending backlog; photos load lazily. Reliability: if the credit write fails after approval, the whole approval rolls back (no APPROVED state without a matching ledger entry). Observability: metrics for submissions/day, approval rate, average verification time, total credit granted; alert on anomalies. Data integrity: vendor total\_credit always reconciles to the ledger sum. Usability: clear vendor feedback across submitted, pending, approved (credit shown), and rejected (reason shown) states.

### 11. Open questions & assumptions

1. Rounding rule: how is fractional 5% (e.g. 1.5) stored and displayed — exact decimal, round up, round down, or 2-dp?
2. Per-award vs total rounding: where does rounding happen, if any?
3. Percentage config: is 5% fixed, or configurable by Ops/Product?
4. Zero-credit devices: is approval allowed when the award would be 0?
5. Duplicate / re-upload: can a vendor upload again after rejection, or replace a pending submission?
6. Reversal: if an order is cancelled after credit is granted, is the credit clawed back?
7. Rejection reason: mandatory or optional?
8. Time window: is there a deadline after completion for uploading the review?
9. Image rules: accepted formats, max size, any duplicate-photo detection?
10. Credit usage: what can vendors do with accumulated credit?
