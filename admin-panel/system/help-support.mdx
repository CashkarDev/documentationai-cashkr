## Overview

Help & Support is where admins raise internal issues (bugs or problems) about Cashkr products and track their status.

Open it from **System → Admin → Help & Support** in the left sidebar (route `/help&support`).

## What the screen shows

| Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Open Tickets | Issues currently open |
| Close Tickets | Issues that have been closed |
| Pending Tickets | Issues awaiting action |

Below the cards, raised issues are listed. When there are none, the page shows "There are no Issues yet."

## Raising an issue

Click **Add Issue** (top right) to open the **Add Issue** dialog — "Please provide a detailed explanation of the problems you encountered and where they occurred."

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Short title for the issue |
| Epic | The module affected: **Admin Panel**, **Vendor App**, **Website** or **Customer App** |
| Description | What problem you faced, and where |
| Screenshots | Drag and drop or browse to attach screenshots |

Click **Add Issue** to submit, or **Reset** to clear the form. Close the dialog with **×**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Describe the problem" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
Give a clear title and pick the correct Epic so the issue reaches the right team.
</Step>
<Step title="Add context" icon="image" title-type="p">
Describe the steps to reproduce and attach screenshots. Hide any customer personal data in screenshots first.
</Step>
<Step title="Submit and track" icon="list-checks" title-type="p">
Submit the issue, then follow its progress through the ticket counts.
</Step>
</Steps>
