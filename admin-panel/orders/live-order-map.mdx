## Overview

Open **Orders → Active → Live Order Map**. The page shows *every in-play pickup, coloured by how much SLA time is left*, so you can see at a glance where pickups are running late.

The map refreshes automatically every minute; the footer shows the last update time (for example *Updated 3:18 PM · refreshes every minute*).

## What the screen shows

### SLA chips

| Chip | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every in-play pickup (with count) |
| **Late** (red) | Pickups past their SLA |
| **Due soon** (amber) | Pickups close to their SLA deadline |
| **On track** (green) | Pickups with comfortable SLA time left |

Each chip shows its count. Click a chip to show only those pickups on the map.

### Map markers

- Each marker is one open order, coloured by its SLA state.
- **Solid** markers are placed at the customer's coordinates. **Hollow** markers are placed at the pincode centroid — accurate to the area, not the doorstep.
- An info bar above the map states how many points are pincode centroids and how many open orders have no coordinates at all (those are not shown).
- Use **+ / −** to zoom.

### Marker popup

Click a marker to see:

- Order token
- Device type, city and pincode
- How late it is (or time left) and the SLA stage, for example `2d 4h late (pickup)`
- Assigned vendor
- Pickup date and time slot
- *Approximate — pincode centre* when the location is a centroid

## Filters and actions

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **All cities** dropdown | Limit the map to one city |
| **Refresh** | Reload the map immediately |

<Callout kind="tip">
  Use the map together with **Delayed** and **Coverage Gaps**: clusters of red markers in one area usually point to a vendor capacity or coverage problem rather than a single late vendor.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info">
  The map is read-only. To act on an order, find it by token in **All Orders** or **Delayed** and use the order detail drawer.
</Callout>
