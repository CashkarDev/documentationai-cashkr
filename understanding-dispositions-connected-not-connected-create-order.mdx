## Overview

Use this module to select the correct call disposition on every lead so data, follow-ups, and performance metrics stay reliable.

**Audience:** Inbound Sales and Follow-Up teams.  
**Purpose:** Explain what dispositions mean and how to apply **Connected**, **Not Connected**, and **Create Order** consistently after each call.

<Callout kind="alert">

**Golden Rule:** Every call must end with one correct disposition. No exceptions.

</Callout>

---

## Why dispositions exist

A disposition is the final status of a sales call that explains what happened with the lead.

Dispositions answer:

- Did you connect with the customer?
- What did the customer say?
- What should happen next?

Dispositions drive:

- Follow-ups and callbacks
- Reporting and sales KPIs
- Handoffs to CX and Ops teams

Correct dispositioning helps Cashkr:

- Track real lead quality
- Measure sales performance accurately
- Improve follow-up efficiency
- Reduce data confusion
- Increase lead → order conversion

---

## How to choose the right disposition

Use this quick decision flow before ending every call.

<Steps>
  <Step title="1. Did you speak to the customer?">

If you **spoke to the customer**, select **Connected**.  
If you **did not speak to the customer** (no answer, switched off, not reachable, call disconnected), select **Not Connected**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="2. Is the customer ready to sell now and needs help placing an order?">

If the customer **is ready to sell now**, **agrees on the price**, and **needs help placing the order**, select **Create Order** and ensure the order is actually created.  
If the customer is **not ready right now** or will **place the order themselves later**, stay under **Connected** and choose the correct sub-option.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disposition categories and rules

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Connected">

### Meaning

You spoke to the customer successfully, even if the conversation was short.

### When to select Connected

- Customer picked up the call.
- A conversation happened (any duration).

After selecting **Connected**, always choose the correct sub-option and add notes.

### Connected sub-options

#### A. Customer Will Place Order

- Customer agrees to sell.
- Wants to place the order themselves.
- Needs no further assistance.

**Example:**  
“I’ll place it in the evening.”

#### B. Callback

- Customer asked to be called later.
- Gave a specific time.

**Rule:** Write the callback time clearly in the notes.

#### C. Waiting for Quote

- Customer wants to compare prices.
- Needs time to check another platform.

#### D. Already Placed an Order

- Customer confirms the order is already created.
- Verify the order ID if possible.

#### E. Technical Issue

- Model not listed.
- App or website error.
- Price not loading.

CX or Tech escalation is required here.

### Mandatory rules for Connected

- Add clear call notes.
- Select the correct sub-option.
- Never leave **Connected** without explanation.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Not Connected">

### Meaning

You did **not** speak to the customer.

### When to select Not Connected

- Phone switched off.
- Not reachable.
- No answer.
- Call disconnected.

“Switched Off” and “Not Reachable” are merged under this category.

### Rules for Not Connected

- Try at least **3 attempts** at different times.
- Add a note for each call attempt.
- Retry later the same day or the next day.

### Example notes

- “Switched off – will retry at 6 PM.”
- “No answer – 2nd attempt.”

</Tab>

<Tab title="Create Order">

### Meaning

The customer is **ready to sell now** and needs assistance placing the order.

### When to select Create Order

- Customer clearly confirms intent to sell.
- Customer agrees on the price.
- Customer wants help placing the order.

### What Sales must do next

- Create the order via the Admin Panel,  
  **or**
- Guide the customer step by step to place the order.

### Important rule

- Never mark **Create Order** unless the order is actually created.

Once the order is created:

- The lead is converted.
- An order ID is generated.
- The Ops team takes over.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Examples: correct disposition selection

| Customer response                | Correct disposition                   |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| “Call me at 7 PM”               | Connected → Callback                  |
| “I will place it myself”        | Connected → Customer Will Place Order |
| “Price is low, checking others” | Connected → Waiting for Quote         |
| “Help me place the order”       | Create Order                          |
| Phone not reachable             | Not Connected                         |
| “Already placed order”          | Connected → Already Placed an Order   |

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Marking **Connected** without actually speaking to the customer.
- Using **Create Order** before the order is created.
- Missing callback notes or unclear follow-up times.
- Choosing a wrong disposition just to close the lead quickly.
- Leaving the disposition field blank.

---

## Why dispositions matter for management

Dispositions help management track:

- Real contact rate.
- Follow-up efficiency.
- Sales productivity.
- Lead quality.
- Conversion bottlenecks.

Wrong dispositions lead to wrong decisions.

## Sales KPIs linked to dispositions

- Connected percentage.
- Not Connected percentage.
- Create Order percentage.
- Callback success rate.
- Lead → Order conversion rate.

## One-page quick summary

**Three main dispositions**

- **Connected** → You spoke to the customer.
- **Not Connected** → You did not speak to the customer.
- **Create Order** → Order is created now with Sales assistance.

**Rules**

- One call = one disposition.
- Notes are mandatory for every call.
- Use **Create Order** only after the order is actually created.

**Goal**

- Clear data → Better follow-ups → Higher conversions.
