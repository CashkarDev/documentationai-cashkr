## Weekly Reports

### Cadence

- Every Monday before 12:00 PM

### Owners

- Data Analyst (Ashwini)
- Marketing Head (Pranjal) for marketing insights
- Ops Leads for SLA and vendor insights
- SEO Specialist (Rabina) for SEO insights

### Audience

- CEO (Ibrahim)
- Founders
- Marketing Team
- Operations Team
- Vendor Team
- SEO Team
- CX Team

### Deliverables

- 1 PDF deck (5–12 slides)
- Dashboard links (Looker Studio)

### Deck structure (minimum content)

1. Top metrics (past 7 days)
   - Leads
   - Orders
   - Conversion rate
   - CPA / cost per order
   - App installs
   - Traffic (web and app)
   - Revenue (if applicable)
2. Trends
   - WoW (week-over-week) changes
   - Highest performing campaigns
   - Lowest performing campaigns
   - Device trends (phones, tablets, Mac, laptop)
3. Top insights
   - What improved?
   - What dropped?
   - Why? (root cause)
4. Actions for next week
   - What changes will be made in ads, SEO, Ops and CX?
   - What experiments will be launched?

## Monthly Reports

### Cadence

- 1st–3rd of every month

### Owners

- Data Analyst: full performance dashboard
- Marketing Head: campaign review
- SEO Specialist: SEO progress
- Ops Head: SLA and vendor highlights
- CX Head: ticket performance

### Audience

- CEO
- Marketing
- SEO
- Ops
- Vendor Management
- Finance Team

### Deliverables

- Full deck (12–25 slides)
- Dashboard links
- CSV or sheets of raw data (if required)

### Deck structure (minimum content)

1. Overview (past 30 days)
   - Total leads
   - Total orders
   - Conversion rate
   - Total marketing spend
   - Revenue or payouts
   - CPA, ROAS
2. Channel breakdown
   - Google Ads
   - Meta Ads
   - SEO traffic
   - Direct or app
   - Referral or partner
3. City and device analysis
   - Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, Bangalore, Chennai, Ahmedabad
   - Device type performance
4. SEO report
   - Indexed pages
   - Rankings
   - Non-indexed pages
   - Top keywords
   - Blog performance
5. Ops and SLA report
   - Pickup success percentage
   - Vendor SLA violations
   - Order failure reasons
   - Vendor performance heatmap
6. CX performance
   - Total tickets
   - Ticket resolution rate
   - Escalations
   - Customer feedback
7. Insights and recommendations
   - What worked
   - What did not
   - What needs change
   - Experiments planned for next month

## Quarterly Reports

### Cadence

- 1st week of each quarter (Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct)

### Owners

- Data Analyst and all department heads

### Audience

- CEO
- Founders
- Directors
- All department heads

### Deliverables

- Senior leadership deck (25–40 slides)
- All major dashboard links
- Written summary (1–2 pages)

### Deck structure (minimum content)

1. Business scorecard
   - Total orders
   - Revenue
   - CAC (customer acquisition cost)
   - LTV (if available)
   - Growth percentage QoQ
2. Marketing growth
   - Spend vs ROI
   - Top performing campaigns
   - City-wise performance
   - Seasonality trends
3. SEO quarter summary
   - Indexed pages
   - Traffic growth
   - Blogs published
   - Keyword movements
   - Technical issues fixed
4. Product and app
   - Event performance (Firebase or GA4)
   - Key flows (checkout, lead, order)
   - Conversion funnel insights
5. Ops and vendor SLA
   - Vendor performance scorecard
   - SLA compliance
   - Pickup delay trends
   - Reassignments
   - Order-level quality issues
6. CX and support
   - Ticket volume trends
   - SLA compliance
   - Complaint categories
   - Resolution time
7. High-level insights
   - 5–10 major learnings
   - Biggest issues
   - Biggest improvements
8. Action plan for next quarter
   - Strategic priorities
   - Experiments
   - Budget reallocations
   - Ops improvements

## Minimum Content for All Reports

Every report (weekly, monthly, quarterly) must contain:

1. Metrics
   - Key KPIs
   - Channel performance
   - Conversions
2. Trends
   - WoW, MoM, or QoQ
   - City-wise
   - Device-wise
   - Channel-wise
3. Insights
   - Why numbers changed
   - What patterns appeared
4. Actions
   - What decisions must be taken
   - What optimizations will be made
   - What issues need fixing next

## Report Delivery Rules

### Rule 1: Timing

- Avoid delays unless pre-approved.
- Weekly reports: Monday before 12 PM.
- Monthly reports: 1st–3rd.
- Quarterly reports: 1st week.

### Rule 2: Format

- Always use PDF deck.
- Always attach dashboard links.
- Always add short insights (bullet points).

### Rule 3: Ownership

- Ashwini owns the data accuracy.
- Each department owns their insights.
- CEO receives a combined final version.
