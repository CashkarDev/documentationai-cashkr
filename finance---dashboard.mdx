## Overview

**Sidebar path:** Dashboards → OPERATING → **Finance** (`/dashboard/finance`)

Finance opens the **CFO** tab of the Executive dashboard. The console's subtitle summarises its scope: *P&L · GST · TDS · Cash · Working Capital · Inventory · Compliance*. The CEO, CMO and COO tabs stay available at the top.

## Filters

| Control | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **P&L period** | Date range for the P&L, GST and TDS sections. Defaults to the last 30 days. |
| **Refresh** | Reloads the console. |

<Callout kind="info">
**Cash & Treasury**, **Working Capital** and **Inventory & Asset Health** are snapshots. They do not follow the P&L period picker.
</Callout>

## What the screen shows

The console is split into eight numbered sections.

| # | Section | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Profitability snapshot** | Turnover (gross), Revenue (net of GST), Gross Profit (with margin), EBITDA (≈ Net Profit), Net Profit, Net Margin, Cash in Bank, Runway. |
| 2 | **Profit & Loss Statement** | 30-day period vs previous. Revenue (net of GST): B2C Commission (external vendors), Device Resale (B2C), B2B Deals (resale margin), Total Revenue. Costs: Marketing & Ads, Total OpEx. Bottom line: Revenue, Operating Expenses, Net Profit (EBITDA) with net margin. |
| 3 | **GST & Indirect Tax** | Output GST, Input Tax Credit (estimated), Net GST Payable. Output GST breakdown (on credit sales at 18/118, on commission, on resale B2C-origin and B2B-origin under margin scheme), CGST 9%, SGST 9% or IGST 18%. ITC estimate from GST-bearing expense categories. |
| 4 | **TDS & Direct Tax** | TDS 194O (1% on marketplace gross), TDS 194Q (0.1% on B2B procurement), total TDS estimate, and how each is computed. |
| 5 | **Cash & Treasury** | Cash in Bank, Receivables, Inventory, Working Capital; 30/60/90-day cash forecast and projected cash at +90 days. |
| 6 | **Working Capital — AR / AP** | Accounts receivable with DSO and aging buckets (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days); accounts payable with DPO (aggregate pending vendor credit refunds). |
| 7 | **Inventory & Asset Health** | Inventory value and units, dead stock over 90 days, estimated GST credit locked, inventory by source (B2C internal vendor, B2B paid net of sold, Excess / Walk-in / Direct). |
| 8 | **Compliance Calendar** | Total dues, Overdue, Due in 7 days, Filed; table of Filing, Period, Due, Days, Estimate and Status (for example TDS deposit, GSTR-1, PF, ESI, GSTR-3B, Advance Tax). |

## Actions

- **Compliance Calendar rows** — click a row to mark that filing as filed. Only do this once the filing is actually complete.
- Switch tabs (**CEO / CFO / CMO / COO**) at the top to move to other consoles.

<Callout kind="alert">
GST, ITC and TDS figures are **estimates only**. Reconcile against actual purchase invoices before claiming ITC on GSTR-3B, and have the CFO/CA confirm TDS before deduction.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip">
For transaction-level finance data, use the **Finance** sidebar group (Device Sales, Expenses, Pending Payments, Reports: Profit & Loss, GST, Money Flow, Credits).
</Callout>
