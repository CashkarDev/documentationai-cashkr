## Overview

- **Audience:** Inbound Sales and Follow-Up teams (with visibility for CX and Ops).
- **Goal:** Handle two specific outcomes correctly to:
  - Avoid duplicate orders and vendor issues
  - Prevent over-calling and pressure on customers
  - Keep dispositions, metrics, and handoffs clean and fair

<Callout kind="info">

**Golden Rule:** If the customer is acting independently, support them, do not interfere.

</Callout>

---

## Definitions

### Customer Will Place Order

Use **Customer Will Place Order** when the customer has intent and says they will complete the order themselves (now or later).

Examples:

- "I will place it tonight."
- "I will do it from the app."
- "I will ask someone and then place."

### Already Ordered

Use **Already Ordered** when the customer has already completed the order before or during the call.

Examples:

- "I already placed the order."
- "Pickup is scheduled."
- "I got an order ID."

---

## Decision guide

| Customer statement       | Correct handling              |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| "I will place it myself" | **Customer Will Place Order** |
| "I will do it later today" | **Customer Will Place Order** |
| "I already booked"       | **Already Ordered**           |
| "Pickup is fixed"        | **Already Ordered**           |
| "Help me place now"      | **Create Order**              |

---

## Handling: Customer Will Place Order

Use this flow when the customer clearly says they will complete the order on their own.

<Steps>

<Step title="Acknowledge and support" icon="check">

Acknowledge the customer's intent and make it clear you are available if they get stuck.

> "Perfect. If you need any help, I am here."

</Step>

<Step title="Set a light follow-up" icon="clock">

- Ask permission for a follow-up time (same day or next day).
- Do not push for manual order creation while the customer wants to act independently.

</Step>

<Step title="Offer help, not pressure" icon="help-circle">

Keep the tone supportive and low-pressure.

> "If anything blocks you, just reply or I can assist."

</Step>

</Steps>

### Admin Panel actions

- **Disposition:** Connected → *Customer Will Place Order*
- **Notes (mandatory):**

```text
Customer will place order themselves today.
Asked for follow-up tomorrow 11 AM (if not placed).
```

### Follow-up rules

- Make **one** gentle follow-up only (call or WhatsApp).
- If still not placed, move to **Waiting for Quote** or close politely.
- If the customer has placed the order, update disposition to **Already Ordered**.

---

## Handling: Already Ordered

Use this flow when the customer confirms the order is already created.

<Steps>

<Step title="Congratulate and confirm" icon="check-circle">

Thank the customer and acknowledge the completed action.

> "Great. Thanks for confirming."

</Step>

<Step title="Verify lightly" icon="info">

- Ask for the **Order ID** (if available), or
- Confirm pickup details (date, time, location) without interrogating.

</Step>

<Step title="Stop selling" icon="x-circle">

- Do not upsell or re-negotiate.
- Do not create a new order or re-create anything already confirmed.
- Do not add persuasion once the order exists.

</Step>

</Steps>

### Admin Panel actions

- **Disposition:** Connected → *Already Placed an Order*
- **Notes (mandatory):**

```text
Customer confirmed order placed.
Pickup scheduled.
```

### Next steps

- Sales hands off the customer at this point.
- Ops/CX owns the journey from here.
- Sales should not follow up again unless the customer explicitly asks.

---

## What not to do

<Callout kind="alert">

Avoid these actions in both Customer Will Place Order and Already Ordered outcomes:

- Do not create a new order "just to be safe".
- Do not re-pitch or renegotiate once the customer has decided.
- Do not mark **Create Order** without actually creating an order.
- Do not keep calling after the customer has confirmed an existing or planned order.

Duplicate orders create vendor issues, bad customer experience, and wrong performance metrics.

</Callout>

## Common edge cases and how to handle them

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Case 1: Customer says already ordered, but no order found" default-open="true">

- Ask for the Order ID and verify.
- Check the Admin Panel by customer number and other known details.
- If you still cannot find the order, ask the customer to wait and escalate to CX for verification before taking any further action.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Case 2: Customer Will Place Order keeps delaying">

- Make only one follow-up after the customer commits to placing the order.
- If there is still no progress after that follow-up, move the lead to **Waiting for Quote** or close politely as appropriate.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Case 3: Customer orders after your follow-up">

- When the customer confirms they have now placed the order, update the disposition to **Already Ordered**.
- Add a short confirmation note in Admin Panel.
- Stop any further calls or follow-ups.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## KPI and reporting impact

Correct handling improves:

- Accurate **Create Order %** (no fake "created" tags).
- True **Conversion %** from lead to order.
- Lower **Duplicate Order Rate**.
- Better **Agent Fairness** in performance evaluation.

Wrong handling inflates:

- Fake conversions and misleading success numbers.
- Ops workload to clean up duplicate or incorrect orders.
- CX complaints from over-calling or confused order status.

---

## Quick training summary

### Customer Will Place Order

- Customer intent is **yes**, action is **pending**.
- One gentle follow-up only (with permission).
- Do not push manual order creation while they want to self-serve.

### Already Ordered

- Action is **done** before or during the call.
- Verify lightly, then stop selling or re-pitching.
- Handoff to Ops/CX; no more follow-ups unless the customer asks.

**Golden Rule:** Support independence. Do not duplicate effort.
