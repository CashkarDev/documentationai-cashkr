## Objective

Schedule and publish social media content accurately and consistently across all relevant platforms using Robin Reach, aligned with the monthly content calendar.

## Roles and responsibilities

- **Social Media Executive**
  - Schedule content via Robin Reach.
  - Ensure caption formatting, platform fit, and creative dimensions are correct.
  - Track scheduled and live status for each post.

- **Marketing Manager**
  - Review and approve final captions and creatives before handoff to the Social Media Executive.

<Callout kind="info">

Final captions and creatives must be approved by the Marketing Manager before you schedule anything in Robin Reach.

</Callout>

## Tools required

- Robin Reach
- Canva (for approved creatives and captions)
- Google Drive or Notion (optional backup tracker)
- Access to BBT Gmail (for Robin Reach login)

## Platforms supported

- Instagram
- Facebook
- Twitter (X)
- LinkedIn
- Threads
- YouTube

## Step-by-step process

<Steps>

<Step title="Log in and select brand" icon="login">

- Go to [Robin Reach](https://robinreach.io).
- Click **Login with Google**.
- Select the **BBT Gmail account**.
- From the brand selector, choose **Cashkr**.

</Step>

<Step title="Compose a new post" icon="edit">

- Click **Compose**.
- Add the **final caption** (copy it from Canva or the approved document).
- Upload the correct **creative file** from Canva or Drive:
  - Square `1080x1080` → Instagram Feed, Facebook, Threads
  - Landscape `1200x628` → X, LinkedIn
  - Vertical `9:16` → Reels (may require manual upload)

<Callout kind="tip">

Use square creatives for most feed posts, landscape for X and LinkedIn, and vertical for Reels or story-style content.

</Callout>

</Step>

<Step title="Configure post settings" icon="settings">

- Select all **platforms** where the post should go (Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Threads, YouTube as relevant).
- Choose the **post format**:
  - Feed post
  - Story (if available)
  - Reel (manual upload might be needed separately)
- Select a **thumbnail** where applicable:
  - Recommended for Reels.
  - Recommended for LinkedIn videos.

<Callout kind="tip">

Set a clear, on-brand thumbnail for Reels and LinkedIn videos to improve click-through and avoid random frames being shown.

</Callout>

</Step>

<Step title="Set schedule time" icon="calendar">

- Click the **schedule time selector**.
- Choose the **date and time** based on the content type:
  - Default for regular posts: **12:00 AM IST**
  - Reels and Office Fun posts: **6:00 PM IST**
- Confirm that the **timezone** is set to **India Standard Time (IST)**.
- Click **Schedule**.

<Callout kind="info">

Before you confirm any time, double-check that the timezone is India Standard Time and that AM/PM is set correctly for time-sensitive posts.

</Callout>

</Step>

<Step title="Preview and verify content" icon="monitor">

Use Robin Reach preview to confirm the post is publication-ready:

- **Caption**
  - Check line breaks and spacing.
  - Confirm emojis render correctly.
  - Ensure tone matches brand guidelines.
- **Creative**
  - Verify the correct creative is attached.
  - Confirm the size suits the selected platforms.
- **CTA**
  - Ensure the call-to-action is clear and visible (for example, `Tap the link in bio`).
- **Hashtags**
  - Include 10–20 hashtags.
  - Use varied, relevant hashtags per post.
- **Platform layout**
  - Confirm no key visual or text is cropped.
  - Check that text is not too close to edges.

</Step>

<Step title="Update tracking log (optional but recommended)" icon="sheet">

Maintain a Google Sheet or Notion tracker for visibility and audits. Include at least:

- **Date** (for example, `2025-08-10`)
- **Platform** (for example, `Instagram`)
- **Caption status** (for example, `Approved`)
- **Creative link** (for example, direct Canva URL)
- **Scheduled time** (for example, `12:00 AM`)
- **Status** (for example, `Scheduled`, `Published`)

Use this log to cross-check that all planned content from the monthly calendar is scheduled and published.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Operational notes and tips

<Callout kind="info">

For campaigns, festivals, and other key moments, schedule posts at least 5–7 days in advance to avoid last-minute issues.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip">

If Robin Reach does not support Reels or Stories for a specific account, schedule a reminder and upload them manually using the Instagram or Facebook app.

</Callout>

- For any time-sensitive post (for example, festive offer expiry, bonus windows), triple-check:
  - Date
  - Time
  - AM/PM
  - Timezone (IST)
- Reels and Stories may still require manual handling even if planned in Robin Reach; keep them aligned with the same captions and creatives used in feed posts.
