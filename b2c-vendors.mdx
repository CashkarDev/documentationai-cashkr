## Overview

**Sidebar:** Vendors → Roster → **All Vendors** (`/vendors`)

All Vendors lists every registered B2C vendor with live status, performance indicators and key profile details. Operations teams use this view to monitor vendor activity across cities and devices, and to open any vendor for configuration or issue checks. Changes made from the vendor panel directly control which vendors receive leads and orders and under what limits.

## What the list shows

Each row is one registered vendor. Columns marked sortable show a sort arrow in the header.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Vendor ID | Short system identifier for the vendor. |
| Vendor Name | Vendor's name, star rating (when reviews exist) and shop / business name. |
| Contact | Vendor phone number with a quick-call button. |
| City | City where the vendor operates. |
| Health Score | Letter grade (A–D) with a percentage. |
| Avg. Drop | Average price drop on the vendor's orders (shown as a negative ₹ amount; "—" when no data). |
| In Progress | Number of orders currently active with the vendor. |
| Status | **Live**, **Hold** or **Barred**. |
| Pickup Rate, Trend, City Rank, Errors, Top Devices | Performance indicators; show "—" or 0 when the vendor has no data yet. |

The footer shows the page number, total results and Previous / Next pagination.

## Filters & search

- **Search vendors** — search box at the top right.
- **New Vendors** — checkbox to show only recently registered vendors.
- **Filters** — opens the filter row:
  - **Status** — Hold, Barred or Live.
  - **City** — limit to one city.
  - **From / To date** — date range.
  - **Reset** clears the filters; **Apply Filters** applies them.

## Vendor detail panel

Click any vendor row to open the side panel. It opens on the **Details** tab and has these tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| In Progress | The vendor's lead details: credit balance and count of active leads. |
| Reviews | Average rating, review count and a star breakdown with All / 5-Star … 1-Star filters. |
| Report | From / To date range with Orders Attempted, Completed, In Progress, Delayed, Failed, Requote Count, Completion %, Requote %, Delayed % and a Failed Disposition breakdown by reason. |
| Failed Rejected | Orders failed or rejected by the vendor. |
| Details | Profile and configuration (see below). |
| Transactions | Current balance, Purchased, Used on Orders, Refunded and Fines cards; date range and transaction-type filter; **Export Statement**; ledger with Date, Particulars, Status, Debit, Credit, Balance. |
| Attendance | Monthly calendar of attendance for the vendor and their cashboys (Present / Logged, Still in, No Log), with a date range, person filter (Vendor + All Cashboys) and Export. |
| Documents | Uploaded KYC documents: GSTIN Certificate (optional), Gumasta Certificate (optional), Aadhar Udhyog, Shop Photo, PAN Card, Aadhar Front, Aadhar Back, Vendor Photo. |
| Cashboy | Cashboys (field agents) under this vendor, with **Add cashboy**. |

### Details tab

- **Status and status actions** — the current status badge with **Hold** and **Barr** buttons. The panel explains the difference:
  - **Hold** — pincodes are kept, Max Leads is set to 0, and the vendor is auto-released on schedule.
  - **Barr** — pincodes are cleared and the vendor needs manual reactivation.
  - Going Live requires at least one pincode and Max Leads greater than 0.
- **Internal Vendor** — toggle to mark the vendor as internal.
- **Contact information** — phone, email and address, with **Edit details**, plus **GSTIN** (or "Not provided").
- **Devices Assigned** — device types the vendor receives orders for (for example Mobile, Tablet, Laptop, Mac). Use **Add Device** to add a type, or the × on a chip to remove it.
- **Credits** — current credit balance.
- **Max Leads** — maximum number of leads the vendor can hold at once.
- **Vendor Type** — the vendor's type number, which determines tier access.
- **Tier Access** — tiers the vendor can access, with **Tier System Info** showing each tier's base credits (for example Tier 1 = 100, Tier 2 = 70, Tier 3 = 30 credits).
- **Area Coverage** — count of covered pincodes, **Assigned Zones** with **Assign Zone…**, and **Add Pincodes** with the list of assigned pincodes (each pincode chip has an edit icon and a × to remove it).
- **Dedicated DID** — the vendor's dedicated Acefone number, or a note that the shared DID is used when none is assigned and whether spare numbers exist in the pool. See [DID Number Management](/did-number-management).
- **Created on** — registration date.
- **Activity History / Pincode History** — two switchable views at the bottom of the panel:
  - **Activity History** — log of changes such as Tier, Max Leads, Devices and Status (old → new value, date), with a count of changes. Paginated 10 per page with **← Prev** / **Next →**.
  - **Pincode History** — log of pincodes added to or removed from the vendor. History is kept only from 15 Oct 2026; changes made before that were not recorded (empty state: "No pincode changes recorded yet.").

## Important notes

<Callout kind="info">

- Changes to devices, pincodes / zones and Max Leads take effect immediately and sync to the Vendor App.
- A vendor on **Hold** keeps their pincodes but receives no leads (Max Leads 0) until released. A **Barred** vendor loses their pincodes and must be reactivated manually.
- To go Live a vendor needs at least one pincode and Max Leads greater than 0.
- Always verify documents in the Documents tab before activating a vendor or increasing their access.
- Health score is calculated by the system and cannot be edited manually.

</Callout>
