## Objective

Ensure all festive and event-based social media posts are scheduled in advance, correctly formatted for each platform, and published at **12:00 AM IST**, based on the annual content calendar using Canva and Robin Reach.

<Callout kind="info">

- Schedule every festive and event post for **12:00 AM IST** on the festival or event day.
- Use only **final approved creatives and captions** from Canva before scheduling.
- Complete scheduling for all **yearly festive and event posts in advance** as per the content calendar.

</Callout>

## Tools required

- **Robin Reach** – For scheduling posts
- **Canva** – Source of final approved creatives and captions
- **Slack / WhatsApp / Email** – For coordination

## Roles and responsibilities

- **Social Media Executive** – Schedules posts on all platforms, formats content as per platform norms, and logs final status.
- **Marketing Manager** – Approves creatives and captions inside Canva before scheduling begins.

## Platforms covered

- Instagram
- Facebook
- Twitter (X)
- LinkedIn
- Threads

## Step-by-step process

<Steps>

  <Step title="Access final creatives and captions in Canva" icon="folder">

  - Open the Canva folder:  
    `Cashkr > Social Media Creatives > 2025 > [Month] > [Festival Name]`.
  - Confirm platform-specific design formats are available:
    - Square
    - Landscape
    - Vertical
  - Confirm the final caption is added inside Canva (either in a comment or on a caption slide).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Log into Robin Reach" icon="monitor">

  - Open Robin Reach using official credentials.
  - Go to the **Content Scheduler Panel**.
  - Select the target platform: Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, or Threads.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload the correct creative format" icon="image">

  - Use these creative formats and dimensions:
    - **Instagram, Facebook, Threads**
      - Format: square
      - Dimensions: 1080x1080 px
    - **LinkedIn, Twitter (X)**
      - Format: landscape
      - Dimensions: 1200x628 px
    - **Reels, Stories**
      - Format: vertical
      - Dimensions: 1080x1920 px
  - Download required formats from Canva for the platforms you are scheduling.
  - Export assets in high resolution:
    - PNG or JPG for images
    - MP4 for video

  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy caption from Canva and format by platform" icon="edit">

  - Copy the **final approved caption** from Canva from the comment box or caption slide.
  - Format captions per platform:

    - **Instagram**
      - Use emojis where relevant.
      - Add line breaks for readability.
      - Place hashtags at the bottom.
    - **LinkedIn**
      - Avoid emojis.
      - Use paragraph spacing.
      - Maintain a formal tone.
    - **Twitter (X)**
      - Prepare a 280-character version.
    - **Facebook**
      - Keep it CTA-focused and mid-length.
    - **Threads**
      - Match Instagram style or use a shorter variation.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Set publishing time" icon="clock">

  - Schedule each post for **12:00 AM IST** on the festival or event day.
  - Double-check both the **date** and **timezone** before saving.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview and final QA" icon="check-circle">

  - Use **Robin Reach Preview** for each selected platform.
  - Validate:
    - Correct image or video format is attached.
    - Caption layout is correct, including spacing and emoji usage.
    - CTA is clearly visible.
    - Spelling, links, and any placeholders such as `[Insert Here]` are fully resolved.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Schedule post and mark status in Canva" icon="zap">

  - Click **Schedule** in Robin Reach for each platform.
  - Mark the post as `✅ Scheduled` inside the **Canva file name** or in a Canva comment so the status is visible to the team.

  </Step>

</Steps>

## Notes and tips

<Callout kind="info">

- Schedule **yearly festive and event posts in advance** according to the content calendar.
- Follow the **12:00 AM IST** publishing rule consistently for all festive and event posts.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip">

- For Reels, consider adding a custom thumbnail and trimming the caption if it feels too long for vertical video.
- Avoid reusing the exact same hashtag combinations to maintain freshness and organic reach across platforms.

</Callout>
