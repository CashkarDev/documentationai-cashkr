## Overview

Cashkr Wallet stores the money you earn from **referral rewards** in the Customer App. When someone uses your referral code, completes an order, and the reward is approved, that money appears in your wallet as balance you can withdraw.

<Callout kind="info">

- Wallet money comes **only from approved referral rewards**.
- Wallet is **not used** for selling devices or receiving order payouts.
- Wallet is created **automatically** when you earn your **first referral reward**.

</Callout>

## What the wallet shows

The wallet screen summarizes your total referral earnings and withdrawals.

### Total balance

Total balance shows how much money is **currently available to withdraw** from your wallet.

- This includes only **approved rewards** that have already been credited.
- Any reward that is still **pending approval** does **not** increase this balance.

You may sometimes see a small initial credit (for example, `₹50`) after you start using referrals. Treat this as part of your balance, but you can withdraw only when the balance meets the withdrawal rules.

### Total earned

Total earned shows the **total referral income credited to your wallet till date**.

- Includes money that is still in your wallet.
- Also includes any amount you have already withdrawn.

### Total withdrawn

Total withdrawn shows the **total amount you have already withdrawn** from the wallet to your UPI ID.

## How money gets into the wallet

Referral rewards follow a pending → approval → credit flow.

1. Your friend registers using your **referral code**.
2. Your friend completes their **device order**.
3. A **referrer reward** is generated for that order.
4. The reward appears in your referral section as **pending**.
5. After the admin approves the reward, the amount is **credited to your Cashkr Wallet**.

<Callout kind="info">

- Pending rewards **do not** increase wallet total balance.
- Only **approved and credited** rewards appear in total balance, total earned, and transaction history.

</Callout>

## Withdrawal eligibility and rules

You can withdraw your wallet balance only when basic conditions are met.

<Callout kind="info">

- **Minimum balance rule:** You can withdraw **only when your wallet balance is at least `₹100`**.
- **Amount rule:** The withdrawal amount must be **₹100 or more** and **cannot be higher than your available balance**.
- **UPI-only:** Withdrawals are paid **only to a UPI ID**.
- **Manual approval:** Every withdrawal request is **reviewed and processed manually**, so payouts can take time.

</Callout>

Key points:

- Example: If you see `₹50` in your wallet as an initial reward, you **must earn additional rewards** until the total balance is **₹100 or more** before requesting a withdrawal.
- If your wallet balance is `₹95`, you **cannot** withdraw yet.
- If your wallet balance is `₹400`, you can:
  - Withdraw any amount from `₹100` up to `₹400`, or
  - Withdraw the **full balance** in a single request.

## Step-by-step: withdraw to UPI

Use the wallet screen in the Customer App to withdraw your referral earnings.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the Cashkr Wallet" icon="wallet">

- Open the **Customer App**.
- Go to the **Cashkr Wallet** section from the main menu or profile.

</Step>

<Step title="Check your available balance" icon="info">

- Confirm that your **total balance is at least ₹100**.
- Decide how much you want to withdraw within your available balance.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter your UPI ID" icon="edit">

- In the withdrawal form, enter the **UPI ID** where you want to receive money.
- Use a valid UPI handle such as `rahul.yadav@upi` or `rahul@okhdfcbank`.
- Make sure the UPI ID belongs to **you** and is active.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter withdraw amount" icon="edit">

- Enter the **withdraw amount**.
- Keep the amount:
  - **≥ ₹100**, and
  - **≤ your total balance**.

Example: If your wallet balance is `₹1,000`, you might enter `₹600` as the withdraw amount.

</Step>

<Step title="Submit the request" icon="arrow-right">

- Review the UPI ID and amount.
- Tap **Submit** to send the withdrawal request.
- The app records this as a **pending withdrawal** and reduces your available balance only after approval and payout.

</Step>

<Step title="Wait for manual approval and payout" icon="clock">

- The Cashkr team **reviews** your withdrawal request.
- After approval:
  - The amount is sent to your **UPI ID**.
  - **Wallet balance** is updated.
  - **Total withdrawn** increases.
- If there is an issue (for example, invalid UPI), support may contact you or the payout may fail.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">

Withdrawals are processed **manually**, so there can be a delay between submitting the request and receiving money in your UPI account.

</Callout>

## Common states and errors

This section helps you understand typical wallet and withdrawal issues.

### Insufficient balance

- **Symptom:** You cannot submit the withdrawal form, or you see an error about low balance.
- **Cause:** Your total balance is **below ₹100**, or you entered an amount that is **less than ₹100**.
- **Fix:** Earn more referral rewards until your balance reaches **₹100 or more**, then try again.

### Amount greater than balance

- **Symptom:** Error when submitting the form saying the amount is more than available balance.
- **Cause:** Withdraw amount is **higher than your wallet total balance**.
- **Fix:** Enter an amount that is **equal to or less than** your current balance.

### Invalid or incorrect UPI ID

- **Symptom:** Payout fails or is delayed; you might see a failure status in support or get a message from the team.
- **Cause:** UPI ID is typed incorrectly or does not exist.
- **Fix:** Double-check the UPI ID format and spelling before submitting. Use a UPI ID that you actively use in your bank or UPI app.

<Callout kind="info">

Incorrect UPI IDs can cause **payment delays or failures**. Always verify the UPI handle in your UPI app first.

</Callout>

### Pending withdrawal

- **Symptom:** You see a withdrawal in a **pending** state and money has not reached your bank yet.
- **Cause:** The request is waiting for **manual review** by the Cashkr team.
- **Fix:** Wait for the team to process the request. If it stays pending for an unusually long time, contact support with your withdrawal details.

### Payout delay after approval

- **Symptom:** Status looks approved, but money has not yet arrived in your UPI-linked bank account.
- **Cause:** Bank or UPI network delay, or settlement taking extra time.
- **Fix:** Wait for a short period, then verify in your UPI app or bank statement. If the delay is long, share the transaction reference with support.

## Connections to other features

The Cashkr Wallet is tightly connected to referrals and is separate from device-selling payouts.

- **Refer & Earn (Customer App):**
  - Referral rewards are created when a referred friend completes an order.
  - Rewards stay **pending** until admin approval.
  - After approval, rewards are **credited to the Cashkr Wallet**, increasing:
    - **Total balance**
    - **Total earned**

- **Payment Method (device-selling payouts):**
  - When you **sell a device**, the order payout uses the **payment method** configured for that order (UPI, bank transfer, or cash).
  - These payouts go **directly to your bank/UPI or in cash**, and **do not pass through the Cashkr Wallet**.
  - Wallet withdrawals are **only for referral rewards**, not for device sale amounts.

If you are checking where your **device sale money** went, look at **Payment Method** and **Order details**, not the wallet.

## Developer and tester notes

<Callout kind="tip">

Use this section when validating the Cashkr Wallet implementation in staging or during QA.

</Callout>

- **Wallet creation**
  - Create the wallet record **only when a user earns the first approved referral reward**.
  - Do not show wallet UI for users with **no referral activity**, unless product explicitly requires a placeholder view.

- **Balances and aggregation**
  - **Total balance** = sum of all **approved, credited rewards** minus all **approved withdrawals**.
  - **Total earned** = sum of all **approved, credited rewards**, irrespective of withdrawals.
  - **Total withdrawn** = sum of all **approved and paid withdrawals**.
  - Ensure **pending rewards** do **not** affect any of these numbers.

- **Threshold and validations**
  - Enforce **min balance ≥ ₹100** to enable withdrawal.
  - Validate:
    - Withdraw amount `≥ 100`.
    - Withdraw amount `≤ current total balance`.
  - Block submission if validations fail and show clear error messages.

- **UPI handling**
  - Restrict withdrawal destination to **UPI only** (no bank account fields in this flow).
  - Validate basic UPI format on the client where possible, and always re-validate on the server.

- **Status and history**
  - Store and display:
    - Reward credit events (with order reference and approval time).
    - Withdrawal requests with status: `pending`, `approved`, `rejected`, `failed`.
  - Keep a clear audit trail so support can reconcile wallet, referral rewards, and payment logs.

- **Separation from order payouts**
  - Keep wallet logic **fully separate** from device order payout flows documented under **Payment Method**.
  - Never credit sale payouts into the wallet; they should follow the configured order payment mode only.
