## Overview

Payment Method in the Customer App lets customers store payout details (UPI ID or bank account) on their profile so vendors can pay them after a device pickup and order completion.

<Callout kind="info">

Payment details are stored at the **profile level**, used for **payouts after order completion**, and are **shared across Website, Customer App, and Admin Panel**.

</Callout>

## What the Payment Method screen shows

The Payment screen under the customer profile shows the payout information stored for that user.

- Current **UPI ID**, if set.
- Current **bank account details**, if set (bank name, IFSC code, account number).
- **Amazon Gift Card** option (available only for orders under ₹10,000) — when selected, the customer receives gift card details after the vendor completes the order, instead of a direct payout.
- Actions to **add** a new payment method or **edit** existing details.

If no payment method exists yet, the screen prompts the user to add either a UPI ID or a bank account.

## Step-by-step user flow

Use this flow to add or update payout details in the Customer App.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Open Payment from Profile" icon="user">

  - Open the Customer App.
  - Go to **My Profile**.
  - Tap the **Payment** option to open the Payment Method screen.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose UPI or Bank Account" icon="settings">

  - On the Payment screen, choose whether to manage **UPI ID** or **Bank Account**.
  - If both exist, the customer can edit either one as needed.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter UPI details" icon="terminal">

  - If the customer selects **UPI**:
    - Enter the **UPI ID** (for example, `rajiv@abcbank`).
  - Confirm the UPI ID matches the one registered with the customer's UPI app.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter bank account details" icon="database">

  - If the customer selects **Bank Account**:
    - Enter **Bank Name**.
    - Enter **IFSC Code**.
    - Enter **Account Number**.
  - Verify these fields before saving to avoid payout failures.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and confirm changes" icon="check-circle">

  - Tap **Save** to store the new or updated payment method.
  - The app updates the user's profile and syncs the changes with the website and admin tools.
  - On success, the Payment screen shows the latest saved details.

  </Step>

</Steps>

## Key rules and behaviors

<Callout kind="info">

- Payment methods are stored on the **user profile**, not per order.
- During the **Sell Device** order flow, each confirmed order stores a snapshot of the **selected payment method**.
- If the customer does not select UPI or Bank during the order flow, the **default payment mode is set to Cash** so every order has a non-empty payment mode.
- Changes to payment details affect **future payouts and new orders**, not already completed payouts.

</Callout>

Once an order is confirmed, the payment mode attached to that order stays fixed, even if the customer later edits their profile payment details.

### Amazon Gift Card

Customers can select Amazon Gift Card as their payment method during the Sell Device order flow, alongside Cash, UPI, and Bank. When Amazon Gift Card is selected, no payout is processed at order placement — instead, the system queues gift card issuance. Once the vendor completes the order, the system automatically issues Amazon Gift Card details (gift card code and redemption instructions) to the customer. The gift card is redeemable exclusively on Amazon.

<Callout kind="warning">

Amazon Gift Card is only available for orders with a total value less than ₹10,000 at the time of order creation. Orders of ₹10,000 or above will not show the gift card option — customers must choose Cash, UPI, or Bank transfer instead.

</Callout>

## System logic

System behavior around Payment Method is consistent across channels.

- Store payment details against the **user profile**, not against a single order.
- Keep payout details **editable in the Customer App until an order is completed**; after that, changes only apply to subsequent orders.
- Use the saved payment method during **payout processing** once the order is marked completed by the vendor.
- Use **input validation** to block malformed UPI IDs and incorrect bank details.
- Store sensitive fields with **backend encryption** and restrict visibility to systems that need them (Customer App display, Admin Panel, payout processing).
- When Amazon Gift Card is selected as the payment mode, no payout is processed at order placement. The system issues Amazon Gift Card details to the customer automatically after the vendor completes the order.

## Connections

Payment Method in the Customer App is tightly connected to the website and to order and payout workflows.

- **Website Payment page**  
  Changes on the Customer App Payment screen sync with the **Payment** page on the website and vice versa, so the user sees the same UPI/bank details on both platforms.

- **Orders and payment mode**  
  When a customer confirms a **Sell Device** order:
  - The system attaches the current **payment mode** (UPI, Bank, or Cash by default) to that order.
  - Both the Customer App and Admin Panel must always show a non-empty payment mode for the order.

- **Payout flow (vendor side)**  
  Vendors use the **payment method attached to the order** when processing payouts after pickup and order completion:
  - If the order has UPI or Bank as payment mode, the vendor pays to those details.
  - If the order defaulted to Cash, the vendor pays out in cash.

- **Amazon Gift Card**  
  When a customer selects Amazon Gift Card as the payment method, payout is deferred until vendor completion. Upon order completion, the system issues Amazon Gift Card details directly to the customer — the vendor does not handle gift card delivery. See [Amazon Gift Card Payment Option](/amazon-gift-card-payment) for the full feature documentation.

## Common states and errors

Handle these states to keep the Payment Method experience predictable.

### Invalid UPI ID

- When the user enters an incorrectly formatted UPI ID (missing handle, invalid characters, or fails backend validation):
  - Show an inline error on the UPI field.
  - Block the **Save** action until the customer corrects the value.

### Invalid IFSC or account number

- For bank accounts, validate:
  - **IFSC Code** format and length.
  - **Account Number** length and character pattern.
- If validation fails:
  - Highlight the problematic field(s).
  - Display a clear error, for example: "Enter a valid IFSC code" or "Enter a valid account number".
  - Do not update stored bank details until all fields pass validation.

### Network and backend failures

- If network calls to fetch or save payment details fail:
  - Show an error message and keep the last known saved values visible where possible.
  - Ensure the **Save** action does not falsely confirm success when the backend update did not complete.
  - On retry, re-validate input and reattempt the update.

## Developer and tester notes

<Callout kind="tip">

**For Developers**

- Encrypt sensitive fields (UPI ID, account number, IFSC) in storage and in transit.
- Use a shared validation layer with the website so UPI and bank checks are consistent across platforms.
- Ensure profile payment changes propagate to:
  
  - Customer App Payment screen
  - Website **Payment** page
  - Admin Panel views used for payout processing

- When creating or updating a Sell Device order:
  
  - Attach the **current payment mode** (UPI, Bank, or Cash default) to the order.
  - Ensure this stored value is used in payout logic, not the mutable profile fields.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip">

**For Testers**

- Verify adding and editing both **UPI** and **Bank** details from the Customer App and confirm they appear on the website Payment page.
- Attempt to save **invalid UPI IDs** and **invalid bank details** and confirm the app blocks saving with clear error messages.
- Create Sell Device orders in these scenarios:
  
  - With UPI selected as payment method.
  - With Bank selected as payment method.
  - Without selecting a method, and confirm the order shows **Cash** as payment mode.
- With Amazon Gift Card selected (for orders under ₹10,000), and confirm no payout is processed at order placement.

- Confirm the order's payment mode is visible and consistent in:
  
  - Customer App order details
  - Admin Panel order details

- Verify that Amazon Gift Card option appears only for orders with total value less than ₹10,000. Create an order above ₹10,000 and confirm the gift card option is not shown.
- Complete an order with Amazon Gift Card selected (via vendor app) and verify that gift card details are delivered to the customer after completion.

- After order completion, change profile payment details and confirm:
  
  - The completed order still shows the **original** payment mode.
  - New orders use the updated payment method.

</Callout>
