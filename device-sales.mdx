## Overview

**Sidebar path:** Finance → Device Sales (`/finance/sales`)

The **Device Sales** page shows every device sold from the [All Inventory](/inventory/all-inventory) module, **grouped by sale transaction**. Each sale shows the buyer (customer or dealer), number of devices, payment method, totals, amount received, pending balance and margin. Expanding a sale shows the individual devices with cost, sale price, margin, GST and profit.

<Callout kind="info">
Use Device Sales to reconcile revenue, track per-device margins and GST, and follow up on sales that are not yet fully paid.
</Callout>

---

## What the screen shows

### Summary cards

The cards at the top reflect the current filters and date range.

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Sales** | Total value of all sales in the current view |
| **Margin** | Total margin (sale price minus cost) across the sales |
| **Received** | Total amount already collected from buyers |
| **Pending** | Total amount still to be collected |
| **Sales / Items** | Number of sale transactions / number of devices sold |

### Sale cards (grouped by transaction)

Each sale appears as a collapsible card showing:

- **Buyer name and phone**, with a **Customer** or **Dealer** badge
- A **Pending** badge when the sale is not fully paid
- **Sale date · device count · payment method · sale reference ID** (e.g. `#AB12CD34`)
- **Total**, **Received**, **Pending** (shows **Paid** when nothing is pending) and **Margin**

### Expanded sale details

Click the arrow on a sale card to expand it. The device table shows:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Device** | Model name of the sold device |
| **Serial** | Serial / IMEI number |
| **Cost** | Cost price recorded in inventory |
| **Sale Price** | Price the device was sold at |
| **Margin** | Sale price minus cost |
| **GST** | GST on the margin |
| **Profit** | Margin after GST |

Below the table you see the payment **Ref** (if any), a progress bar showing the **% paid**, and an **Update Payment** button for sales with a pending balance.

## Filters & search

| Control | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Pick a date range** | Limit sales and summary cards to a date range |
| **Search** | Search by customer, device or serial (press **Enter** to search) |
| **Type** | **All Types**, **Customer** or **Dealer** |
| **Pending Only** | Show only sales that still have a pending balance |
| **Refresh** | Reload the data |
| **Export** | Export the current list |

---

## Actions

### Update Payment (Record Payment)

For a sale with a pending amount, expand it and click **Update Payment**. The **Record Payment** dialog shows the customer, **Total Amount**, **Already Received** and **Pending**, and has these fields:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Payment Amount** | Amount being received now (maximum = pending amount) |
| **Payment Method** \* | **Cash**, **UPI** or **Bank Transfer** |
| **Transaction ID** | Receipt / reference number (optional) |
| **Mark Fully Paid** | Marks the sale as fully paid |

Click **Save** to record the payment, or **Close** to cancel.

## How sales entries are created

Device Sales entries are created automatically when a sale is punched from the All Inventory module — you do not add sales on this page.

```plaintext
Admin selects device(s) in All Inventory
        ↓
Punch Sale confirmed (Dealer / Customer selected)
        ↓
Device removed from All Inventory
        ↓
New sale transaction created in Device Sales
        ↓
Pending payments collected later via Update Payment
```

---

## Margin & GST calculation

- **Margin** = sale price − cost price, per device.
- **GST** is calculated on the margin amount (18%, treated as included in the margin).
- **Profit** = margin − GST.
- **Total Sales** and **Margin** cards are the sums across the current filtered view.

---

## Customer types

- **Customer** — an individual or walk-in buyer.
- **Dealer** — a registered reseller or bulk buyer from the Customers (Dealers) module.

---

## Connections to other modules

- **All Inventory** — punching a sale removes the device from inventory and creates the sale here.
- **Customers (Dealers)** — supplies dealer records used as buyers.
- **Cash In Hand** — cash sales and cash payments feed overall cash tracking.
- **Reports** — sales, margin and GST feed the Profit & Loss, GST and Money Flow reports.
