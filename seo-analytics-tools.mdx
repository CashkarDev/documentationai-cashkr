## Why this matters

<Callout kind="info">

SEO brings qualified, free traffic to Cashkr by matching what users search with what we offer. Analytics tools then show which keywords, pages, and markets perform well, where we are losing visibility, and what needs fixing across content, technical SEO, and local presence.

</Callout>

## Tools we use

<Card title="Ubersuggest" icon="bar-chart">

Keyword research and competitor insights for organic search.

- Find search volume and difficulty for device-selling keywords.
- Discover content gaps compared to competitors.
- Prioritize new landing pages and blog topics.

*Example:* Ubersuggest shows that "sell old tablet" has strong volume and low difficulty, so the SEO and content teams plan a dedicated landing page.

</Card>

<Card title="Google Search Console (GSC)" icon="monitor">

Primary view of how Google sees the Cashkr website.

- Track impressions, clicks, and average position for key queries.
- See which pages are indexed or blocked.
- Identify search terms already driving conversions to Cashkr.

*Example:* GSC shows "sell laptop online" with rising clicks, so we expand related FAQs and internal links to strengthen that topic.

</Card>

<Card title="Google My Business (GMB)" icon="map">

Local SEO and Maps presence for Cashkr.

- Manage Cashkr listings for relevant cities and service areas.
- Keep address, phone, and timings accurate.
- Respond to reviews and monitor local search queries.

*Example:* When someone searches "sell phone near me" in Mumbai, a well-optimized GMB listing helps Cashkr appear with recent reviews and photos.

</Card>

<Card title="Bing Webmaster" icon="cloud">

Visibility tracking for Bing and Microsoft search.

- Submit sitemaps and monitor indexing on Bing.
- Check search performance and crawl errors.
- Ensure we capture additional traffic from Microsoft Edge users.

*Example:* Bing Webmaster flags indexing issues for a set of device-category pages, so tech and SEO teams adjust internal links and sitemap coverage.

</Card>

<Card title="AnswerThePublic" icon="help-circle">

Question and intent discovery for content planning.

- Collect real user questions around selling phones, laptops, and other devices.
- Group topics into FAQ sections, comparison guides, and how-to content.
- Feed the content backlog with high-intent ideas.

*Example:* AnswerThePublic surfaces "what to do before selling my old phone", which becomes a detailed blog and on-page checklist.

</Card>

<Card title="SEO audit tools (JupiterSEO, ClickRank, SiteGuru, Labrika)" icon="settings">

Technical and on-page SEO health checks.

- Crawl Cashkr pages for broken links, slow pages, and missing meta tags.
- Flag duplicate content or thin pages that need consolidation.
- Track improvements after tech and content changes.

*Example:* SiteGuru highlights that the "Sell Tablet" page is loading slowly, so tech focuses on image optimization and caching, then re-runs audits to confirm improvements.

</Card>

## How we use these daily

- **SEO team** → Runs weekly keyword and opportunity analysis in Ubersuggest and GSC, prioritizes new pages, and reviews SEO audit reports for critical issues.
- **Content team** → Uses AnswerThePublic and keyword inputs to plan articles, FAQs, and landing-page copy that match real search queries.
- **Marketing team** → Manages GMB listings, reviews, and photos, and aligns campaigns with high-performing organic queries from GSC and Bing.
- **Tech team** → Works from SEO audit tool reports and GSC or Bing errors to fix speed, indexing, and structured data issues across the site.

## Operational workflow

<Steps>

  <Step title="Research keywords and opportunities" icon="edit">

  SEO reviews Ubersuggest, GSC, and Bing data to find topics with good search volume and intent where Cashkr can rank or grow. This produces a prioritized list of landing pages, blog ideas, and optimizations.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish and optimize content" icon="code">

  Content and SEO teams create or update pages based on that list, including titles, headings, meta descriptions, and on-page FAQs targeted to the selected queries.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Monitor performance in GSC and Bing" icon="monitor">

  Once content is live, SEO tracks impressions, clicks, and positions in GSC and Bing Webmaster. If pages are not being indexed or are losing rankings, they log follow-up actions.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Fix technical and on-page issues" icon="settings">

  Tech uses audit tools like SiteGuru, Labrika, JupiterSEO, and ClickRank plus search-console errors to resolve slow pages, broken links, and missing tags, then confirms fixes with new crawls and performance checks.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Maintain local SEO presence" icon="map">

  Marketing and SEO keep GMB listings up to date, respond to reviews, and adjust descriptions and photos to reflect current services, especially when launching in new cities.

  </Step>

</Steps>
