## Price differs between the website and the app

It should not. The Website calculator, the Website sell flow, and the Customer App sell flow all call the same pricing engine over the same class data.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the exact same variant" icon="smartphone">
    Storage, RAM, and connectivity all resolve to different variants with different base prices. Most reported mismatches are two different variants.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare the condition answers" icon="list-checks">
    A single different answer changes the deduction set. Compare the full answer set, not just the headline price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check for a cached client" icon="refresh-cw">
    A stale app session can hold an old quote. Reproduce fresh on both surfaces before escalating.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Then escalate" icon="triangle-alert">
    If the variant and answers genuinely match and prices still differ, that is a real engine or data bug. Include both quotes, the variant ID, and the full condition set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The deduction looks wrong

<Expandable title="Percentage deductions are calculated on base price, not the running total" default-open="false">
Every percentage deduction resolves against the **base price**. They do not compound.

If someone has reimplemented pricing outside the engine — in a sheet, a dashboard, or a script — and applied percentages to a running total, their number will be wrong and the engine will be right.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Accessories deduct when missing, not when present" default-open="false">
A1–A4 cover bill, box, charger, and other. The deduction applies for what the customer does **not** have. This reads backwards to people seeing it for the first time.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Multiple physical conditions each charge independently" default-open="false">
P1–P4 can apply simultaneously and each is charged on its own. Two physical issues means two deductions.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="On laptops, S1–S4 is screen size, not screen condition" default-open="false">
On mobile, S1–S5 encodes screen condition. On laptops the same-looking codes encode screen **size**. Reading a laptop quote with the mobile mapping produces confusing results.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="A dead device returns dead price and skips everything else" default-open="false">
If the device does not power on, the engine returns `deadPrice` immediately and no other logic runs. Dead price also acts as the floor — if the computed price lands at or below zero, dead price is returned instead, or zero if none is configured.
</Expandable>

## Device is not appearing in search or the sell flow

Usually the variant has no valid class attached. Without a class there are no deduction values, so the device cannot be priced and is withheld from the flow entirely.

Check the variant in Admin Panel → Devices and confirm a class is assigned. See [Classes](/classes).

## Coupon will not apply

<Callout kind="alert">
Cashkr coupons are not retail discounts. A coupon adds a **flat amount to the device value**. It is never a percentage and it never stacks.
</Callout>

Work through these:

| Check | Why it fails |
| --- | --- |
| Platform targeting | Each coupon targets Mobile, Web, or Both. A valid app coupon can legitimately fail on the website. |
| Another coupon already applied | Coupons do not stack. The second will be rejected. |
| Validity window | Expired or not yet active. |
| Order status | Coupons apply during the sell flow, not after confirmation. |
| Customer eligibility | Some programs are restricted to new or referred customers. |

See [Coupon application](/coupon-application) and [How the coupon system works](/how-coupon-system-works).

## Referral reward did not arrive

Referee rewards approve instantly at checkout. Referrer rewards are only created **after the referee's order completes**, then wait on admin approval.

Also confirm the reward is being looked for in the right place: referral earnings sit in the Cashkr Wallet, which is separate from device payouts.
