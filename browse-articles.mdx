## Overview

Explore a curated collection of articles on the BigboldTech website, including trending highlights, additional news, and category-based content.

## How to browse articles

<Steps>

<Step title="Start from the homepage">

On the homepage, scroll through the main articles section to see what is currently available.

Your view typically includes a mix of highlighted and recent articles.

</Step>

<Step title="Use Trending News and More News sections">

Look for the **Trending News** section to see popular or highlighted articles.

Below or alongside it, use the **More News** section to discover additional articles beyond the top highlights.

</Step>

<Step title="Browse by category from the navigation bar">

If you want articles on a specific topic, use the navigation bar at the top of the page.

Select one of the available categories to open a dedicated page showing articles related to that category.

</Step>

<Step title="Open an article">

When you find something you want to read, select the article by clicking its **title** or **image**.

This action opens the full article details view on a separate page.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Navigate within an article

Use the in-article navigation tools to move between sections and control how you read:

- If available, use the **table of contents** to jump directly to specific sections of the article.
- If a **smooth scroll** option is available, use it for a more continuous, reading-focused experience.
