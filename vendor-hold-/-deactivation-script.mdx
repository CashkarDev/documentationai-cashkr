## Overview

**Used by:** Vendor Management / Operations Team

**Purpose:** Guide team members on how to inform vendors about Hold or Deactivation actions on their account, explain reasons, and ensure clear communication on what needs to happen for reactivation or closure.

**Objectives:**

- Maintain transparency and professionalism while taking disciplinary actions.
- Ensure vendors understand why the action happened and what they can do next.
- Reduce frustration, confusion, or escalation from vendors.

**Definitions:**

- **Hold (temporary):** The vendor account is paused from receiving new orders for a limited period, usually due to SLA violations, inactivity, repeated complaints, or document issues. Once the issue is reviewed and resolved, the account can be reactivated.
- **Deactivation (permanent):** The vendor profile is disabled from receiving or processing orders on Cashkr after repeated violations, fraud-related issues, or continued non-compliance. Deactivation is permanent unless senior leadership approves a fresh activation after review.

<Callout kind="info">

When speaking with vendors, stay transparent and factual, do not argue or blame, and never promise reactivation. Reactivation always depends on review and approval from the defined internal owners.

</Callout>

## Call / chat script (common questions)

Use this section when vendors contact you about account Holds or Deactivations. Select the relevant tab based on the vendor status.

<Tabs>

### Vendor says: Why cannot I receive any orders suddenly?

**Agent response:**

> I understand your concern.  
> Your account is currently on Hold, which means it is temporarily paused from receiving new orders. This can happen for reasons like SLA violations, inactivity, repeated customer complaints, or pending document issues.  
> It is not permanent — once the issue is reviewed and resolved, we can reactivate your account.

### Vendor says: Why was my account put on Hold?

**Agent response:**

> The Hold was applied due to repeated SLA or performance-related issues recorded over the last few days.  
> Our system automatically tracks these, and when there are multiple violations, your profile goes on temporary Hold for review.  
> Once you maintain proper performance consistency and we confirm that all service issues are cleared, we will reactivate your account immediately.

If a specific issue is known, add:

> In your case, it was mainly because of \[Delayed pickups / Improper failures / Missed payouts / No response to orders]. These were flagged under SLA breaches, which triggered the Hold.

### Vendor says: I did not get any warning before my account went on Hold.

**Agent response:**

> I understand. We always try to communicate in advance through WhatsApp or in-app notifications.  
> Sometimes, if the violations happen back-to-back or involve serious issues like false pickup, fraud, or unprofessional conduct, the system triggers the Hold immediately for safety reasons.  
> I can share the exact reason and the date when the flag was raised on your account.

### Vendor says: This is unfair — I want my account reactivated.

**Agent response:**

> I completely understand your concern, and I will help you with that.  
> Reactivation is possible once the reason for the Hold is cleared.  
> I will raise a review request for your account and share it with the Ops Manager.  
> If it is approved, your account can be reactivated within 24–48 hours. You will also receive a message once your account is active again.

### Vendor says: I want to work again — what can I do?

**Agent response:**

> That is good to hear. We value vendors who want to improve performance.  
> If you want to get reactivated, share a short confirmation that you will maintain SLA standards and complete orders as per policy.  
> Once that is submitted, I will forward your request to the Ops Manager.  
> If it is approved, your account will be reactivated within the next 1–2 working days.

### Vendor says: Can I know how many violations I have?

**Agent response:**

> Yes, you can view your SLA summary anytime in your Vendor App.  
> Go to Performance → SLA Summary. It shows the number of warnings, total violations, and your weekly record.  
> You can also ask me to send you the report — I can share the violation log directly from the Admin Panel.

### Vendor says: What does Deactivation mean? Is it permanent?

**Agent response:**

> Deactivation means your vendor profile has been permanently disabled from receiving or processing orders on Cashkr.  
> This usually happens only after repeated violations, fraud-related activity, or non-compliance even after multiple warnings.  
> Once deactivated, you cannot unlock new orders or use the Wallet or Credit system, unless it is approved again by the senior team after review.

### Vendor says: I did not get any warning before Deactivation.

**Agent response:**

> I understand. We always try to communicate in advance through WhatsApp or in-app notifications.  
> Sometimes, if the violations happen back-to-back or involve serious issues like false pickup, fraud, or unprofessional conduct, the system triggers a restriction immediately for safety reasons.  
> I can share the exact reason and the date when the flag was raised on your account.

### Vendor says: This is unfair — I want my account reactivated.

**Agent response:**

> I completely understand your concern, and I will help you with that.  
> Reactivation is possible once the reason for Deactivation is cleared and the review is passed.  
> I will raise a review request for your account and share it with the Ops Manager.  
> If it is approved, your account can be reactivated within 24–48 hours. You will also receive a message once your account is active again.

### Vendor says: Can I know the exact reason for my Deactivation?

**Agent response:**

> Yes, of course.  
> The system logs every reason for such actions. In your case, the deactivation reason was \[repeated SLA violations / misuse of credits / fake failures / fraud suspicion / unresponsive account / misconduct].  
> This was verified by the operations team before applying the final deactivation.

### Vendor says: I want to work again — what can I do?

**Agent response:**

> That is good to hear. We value vendors who want to improve performance.  
> If you want to get reactivated, share a short confirmation that you will maintain SLA standards and complete orders as per policy.  
> Once that is submitted, I will forward your request to the Ops Manager.  
> If it is approved, your account will be reactivated within the next 1–2 working days.

### Vendor says: Will I lose my Wallet or Credits after Deactivation?

**Agent response:**

> No, your Wallet and Credit balances remain safe in your account.  
> If the account stays deactivated permanently, we will process a final settlement to return any Wallet balance to your registered bank account.  
> While your account is on Hold or deactivated, you cannot use the Wallet or unlock new orders until reactivation.

## Preventing future holds (vendor guidance)

Use this guidance when vendors ask how to avoid future Holds or want to improve performance.

**Vendor says:** How can I avoid my account going on Hold again?

**Agent response (talk through these points and share as a message if needed):**

- Always respond to new leads within minutes.
- Avoid marking orders as failed without a valid reason.
- Reach the pickup location within the committed time window.
- Complete payouts on the spot after inspection.
- Communicate politely with customers and with our team.
- Maintain consistent service and communication to keep your profile active and preferred for future leads.

## Internal team notes (quick reference)

Use this table as a quick internal reference. Do not read it out to vendors word-for-word.

| Action                 | Trigger                                           | Approval needed     | Duration                     |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Hold (Temporary)       | 3–5 SLA violations, inactivity, unresponsive vendor | Ops Manager         | 24–72 hrs                    |
| Deactivation (Permanent) | Fraud, repeated violations, misconduct            | CEO + Ops Head      | Permanent                    |
| Reactivation           | Vendor appeal and review passed                   | Ops Manager         | 24–48 hrs post approval      |

## Key tone phrases

Use these phrases to keep your tone calm, supportive, and professional:

- I completely understand your concern.
- This is a temporary pause; we will review it together.
- Once you maintain consistency, your leads will resume normally.
- We truly value your partnership and want you back on track soon.
- I will raise your reactivation request immediately after this call.
