## Overview

The Leads page in the Buyback B2B section is the starting point of the corporate buyback journey for the Sales team. All corporate leads — whether generated from the Cashkr website corporate buyback form or added manually — are managed here.

Sales uses this page to track every lead, log all interactions, and convert interested leads into clients.

## How leads enter the system

Corporate leads enter the system from the website or through manual input by the Sales team.

### Website form

- When a corporate customer fills out the corporate buyback inquiry form on the Cashkr website, the system automatically creates a lead.
- The new lead appears in the **New** column of the pipeline.

### Manual entry

- The Sales team can manually add a lead by clicking **Add Lead** and entering the company details directly.
- Use this when you receive leads through direct outreach, referrals, or offline conversations.

### CSV import

Click **Import CSV** to bulk import multiple corporate leads at once from a CSV file. This is useful when adding leads from trade events, outreach campaigns, or external contact lists.

## Pipeline stages

The Leads page uses a horizontal Kanban board with seven pipeline stages. Each column represents one stage and shows the count of leads currently in that stage.

- **New** — Lead has just entered the system and has not yet been contacted.
- **Attempted** — Sales team has tried to make contact but has not yet received a response.
- **Connected** — Sales team has successfully spoken with the corporate client.
- **Interested** — Client has expressed genuine interest in the corporate buyback service.
- **Converted** — Lead has been converted into a client; a client record is created in the Clients page.
- **Lost** — Lead was engaged but ultimately did not proceed with any deal.
- **Disqualified** — Lead is not a suitable fit for the Cashkr B2B service.

Each lead card shows:

- Company name  
- City  
- Contact person name  
- Date of the last interaction or note  

## Sales team actions

For each lead, the Sales team can:

- Call or email the corporate contact to introduce Cashkr's buyback services.
- Log every interaction — calls, emails, meetings — as notes on the lead card.
- Update the lead stage after each interaction to reflect the current status.
- Move the lead to **Interested** once the client confirms they want to explore a deal.
- Convert the lead to a client once they are ready to proceed; this creates a new record in the Clients page.

## Lead conversion flow

Use this flow as a quick checklist from first contact to client creation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Lead is created" icon="plus">
    Lead is created from the Cashkr corporate buyback website form or added manually by the Sales team, and appears in the <strong>New</strong> column.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initial contact" icon="phone">
    Sales team contacts the corporate client and updates the stage to <strong>Attempted</strong> or <strong>Connected</strong> based on the outcome of the call or email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client shows interest" icon="help-circle">
    Once the client confirms they want to explore a corporate buyback deal, update the stage to <strong>Interested</strong>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Intent to sell confirmed" icon="check-circle">
    Sales team confirms the client's intent to sell devices and gathers necessary details for the deal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Convert lead to client" icon="arrow-right">
    Convert the lead; the system creates a new client record in the <strong>Clients</strong> page, and Sales begins managing the relationship from there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connections to other modules

The Leads page is connected to other parts of the Cashkr system:

- **Buyback B2B — Clients** — When you convert a lead, the system creates a new client record in the Clients page.
- **Cashkr Website** — Corporate buyback form submissions on the website automatically create leads on this page.

## Important notes

<Callout kind="info">
  Every interaction with a lead should be logged as a note on the lead card so any team member can understand the full history and continue the conversation smoothly.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Move leads through the pipeline stages promptly after each interaction. Keeping stages updated avoids stale leads and maintains a reliable view of the pipeline.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert">
  Convert a lead only after the client has clearly confirmed their intent to sell devices. Premature conversions create inaccurate client data and waste the pricing team's time.
</Callout>
