## Overview

Team: B2B Sales  
When used: After the initial contact (Corporate Introduction Script) to collect specific information on the client device disposal needs.  
Goal: Understand the corporate client requirements, volume of devices to be sold, and set up a proposal or plan for them.  
Tone: Professional, consultative, and inquisitive.

## Quick checklist (call flow)

Use this as a mental map while you speak:

1. **Opening** – Reconnect, set context, and ask for time.
2. **Quantify volume** – How many devices, which types, any common models.
3. **Current process** – How they dispose or sell today, and challenges.
4. **Special requirements** – Data wiping, grading, logistics, pricing expectations.
5. **Decision-making** – Who decides, and typical approval timelines.
6. **Next steps and follow-up** – Proposal format, meeting or email, and follow-up.

## Script variants

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="English">

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Opening">

    “Hi \[Corporate Lead Name], it is \[Your Name] from Cashkr again.

    Thanks for taking the time earlier. I would like to dive a bit deeper into your needs so that I can create a tailored solution for your company. Do you have a moment?”

    If yes:

    “Great, thank you. I have a few questions to make sure we provide the best service possible. This will also help us determine how many devices you need to offload and any special requirements you may have.”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Step 1 – Understand the need">

    “I would love to know more about your company process of managing old devices. Can you share how many devices you typically need to dispose of or sell per month?”

    Use these probing questions as needed:

    <ul>
      <li>How many old devices (laptops, phones, tablets) does your company replace each month?</li>
      <li>What types of devices are they (smartphones, laptops, tablets, etc.)?</li>
      <li>Do you have any specific models that you often need to sell?</li>
      <li>If yes to specific models: Do you prefer selling particular brands or models more often (like iPhones, Samsung, etc.)?</li>
    </ul>

    </Step>

    <Step title="Step 2 – Current solution (existing process)">

    “I understand that you might already have a process for handling this. Could you let me know:”

    <ul>
      <li>How do you currently dispose of or sell old devices?</li>
      <li>What challenges are you facing with your current solution? For example, delayed payments, limited reach, device pricing.</li>
    </ul>

    “This helps us see where we can provide a better solution.”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Step 3 – Clarify any special requirements">

    “Do you have any specific requirements regarding:”

    <ul>
      <li>Device condition and grading, for example do you need detailed reports or certificates of recycling?</li>
      <li>Device data wiping or secure destruction requirements?</li>
      <li>Shipping or pickup preferences, for example on-site pickup or shipping devices to a central location?</li>
      <li>Any special pricing expectations for bulk orders or recurring services?</li>
    </ul>

    </Step>

    <Step title="Step 4 – Understand their decision-making process">

    “I would also like to understand your decision-making process:”

    <ul>
      <li>Who in your company will be responsible for finalizing the partnership, for example procurement, IT, or finance?</li>
      <li>What is the usual turnaround time for approval?</li>
    </ul>

    “This helps us ensure we align with your internal timelines.”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Step 5 – Next steps">

    “Based on the details you have shared, I will prepare a custom proposal outlining:

    <ul>
      <li>The best pricing structure for your devices based on volume</li>
      <li>The steps in our device pickup and payment process</li>
      <li>Any special terms based on your specific requirements</li>
    </ul>

    When would be a good time to schedule a follow-up meeting where I can present the proposal to your team?”

    If yes to meeting:

    “Great. I will send over a calendar invite for \[date and time]. Looking forward to connecting again.”

    If no to meeting:

    “I understand. Would it be helpful if I send over a detailed proposal via email for your team to review at their convenience? We can set up a call once it has been reviewed.”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Closing">

    “Thank you again for your time, \[Corporate Lead Name]. I am confident Cashkr can offer a solution that works well with your needs and we are excited to help you offload your old devices. I will follow up with the proposal and will be happy to answer any questions once you have had a chance to review it.”

    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Tab title="Hinglish">

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Opening">

    “Hello \[Corporate Lead Name], main \[Your Name] bol raha/rahi hoon Cashkr se.

    Pehle aapne jo time diya uska dhanyawaad. Main aapke company ke requirements ko aur achche se samajhna chahta/chahti hoon taaki hum aapke liye best solution design kar sake. Kya aapke paas abhi thoda waqt hai?”

    If yes:

    “Thank you. Main aapse kuch questions puchunga/puchungi taaki hume clear idea mile ki kitne devices aapko offload karne hote hain aur koi special requirements hain kya.”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Step 1 – Need understanding">

    “Sabse pehle yeh batayein ki aapka company kitne purane devices (laptops, phones, tablets) har mahine bechne ya dispose karne ke liye ready karta hai?”

    Use these questions to go deeper:

    <ul>
      <li>Har mahine approx kitne devices aapko offload karna padta hai?</li>
      <li>Kaunse type ke devices aapke company mein zyada replace hote hain, jaise smartphones, laptops, tablets?</li>
      <li>Kya aapko frequently kuch specific brand ya model bechne hote hain?</li>
    </ul>

    Agar lead specific models mention kare:

    “Kya aap log kisi particular brand ya model ko zyada frequently replace karte hain, jaise iPhone series, Samsung phones, ya specific laptop models?”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Step 2 – Current solution">

    “Currently aap in devices ko manage kaise karte hain?”

    <ul>
      <li>Abhi aapke paas kya solution hai in devices ko dispose ya sell karne ka?</li>
      <li>Aap kis tarah se apne devices ko bechne ya dispose karte hain, for example direct buyer, vendor, auction, internal process?</li>
      <li>Aapko koi challenge feel ho raha hai apne current process mein, jaise delayed payment, limited buyers, ya pricing clear nahin hona?</li>
    </ul>

    “Yeh sab jaanne se hume samajh aata hai ki hum kaha better value add kar sakte hain.”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Step 3 – Special requirements">

    “Kya aapko koi special requirements hain, jaise:”

    <ul>
      <li>Device condition grading ya detailed reports chahiye hote hain, for example certification of recycling ya audit reports?</li>
      <li>Data wiping ya secure data destruction ki specific requirement, jaise particular standard follow karna?</li>
      <li>Pickup preferences, jaise on-site pickup chahiye ya devices ek central location se ship karna prefer karenge?</li>
      <li>Bulk orders ya regular buyback ke liye special pricing expectation ya structure?</li>
    </ul>

    </Step>

    <Step title="Step 4 – Decision-making process">

    “Final decision kaun karega aapke company mein is partnership ke liye?”

    <ul>
      <li>Decision making mein kaun log involved honge, jaise procurement team, IT team ya finance?</li>
      <li>Usually approval lene mein kitna time lagta hai aapke organisation mein?</li>
    </ul>

    “Isse hume aapki internal timeline ke hisaab se plan karna easy ho jata hai.”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Step 5 – Next steps">

    “Main aapke diye gaye details ke hisaab se ek custom proposal prepare karunga/karungi, jisme:

    <ul>
      <li>Volume ke hisaab se pricing structure</li>
      <li>Pickup process aur payment process ke steps</li>
      <li>Aapke special requirements ke hisaab se koi specific terms</li>
    </ul>

    cover honge.

    Aapke liye kab convenient hoga ek follow-up meeting schedule karna jahan main yeh proposal aapke team ko detail mein explain kar sakun?”

    Agar meeting ke liye haan bole:

    “Perfect, main \[date/time] ke liye calendar invite bhej deta/deti hoon. Phir detail mein discuss karenge.”

    Agar meeting abhi nahin rakhna chahte:

    “Theek hai, koi problem nahin. Main aapko detailed proposal email kar dunga/dungi taaki aapki team apne time se review kar sake. Review ke baad hum ek call schedule kar sakte hain.”

    </Step>

    <Step title="Closing">

    “Phir se time dene ke liye dhanyawaad, \[Corporate Lead Name]. Mujhe lagta hai Cashkr aapke liye ek aisa solution de sakta hai jo aapke current process ke saath smoothly fit ho jaye aur aapko purane devices offload karne mein madad kare.

    Main aapko proposal bhejunga/bhejungii aur jab aap review kar lenge, main aapke questions ka answer dene ke liye available rahunga/rahungi.”

    </Step>
  </Steps>

## Internal notes for Cashkr team

<Callout kind="tip">

<strong>Track in CRM</strong>  
Add the corporate lead to the Corporate Lead pipeline and mark the meeting as scheduled if it is confirmed.

<strong>Follow up with proposal</strong>  
Send an email with the detailed proposal and clear action items for the client.

<strong>Reminder for unresponsive leads</strong>  
If the lead is unresponsive, send a reminder email after 3–4 days to re-open the conversation.

</Callout>
