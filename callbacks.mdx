## Overview

Callbacks shows leads where the customer has requested a call and the disposition is marked as Connected with the reason set to callback.

<Callout kind="info">

Callbacks includes only leads with **disposition status = Connected** and **reason = Callback**, so you always see customers expecting a follow-up call.

</Callout>

## At a glance

<Columns cols={3}>

<Card title="What it shows" icon="list">

- Leads where the customer requested a callback
- Disposition status is **Connected**
- Reason is **Callback**
- Device, city, and contact details for each lead

</Card>

<Card title="When to use" icon="clock">

- During daily follow-ups to honor callback promises  
- When you need a focused list of leads expecting a call  
- To avoid missing time-bound customer requests

</Card>

<Card title="What happens next" icon="arrow-right">

- Call the customer at the requested time  
- Update disposition as Connected or Not Connected with the right reason  
- Convert interested customers to confirmed orders

</Card>

## Filters and search

Use filters to narrow down callback leads:

- **Disposition status**: Connected  
- **Device type**: Mobile, Laptop, Tablet, Mac  
- **City**: Filter leads by customer city  
- **Search**: Find leads by lead ID, device name, or customer phone number

## Callback workflow

<Steps>

<Step title="Open a callback lead" icon="list">

- Go to **Admin Panel → Leads → Callbacks**.
- Select a lead from the callbacks list to view its details.

</Step>

<Step title="Call the customer" icon="phone">

- Call the customer on the registered phone number.
- Confirm interest, timing, and any questions about the offer.

</Step>

<Step title="Update status and disposition" icon="edit">

- Set **status** as **Connected** or **Not Connected**.
- Select the appropriate **reason** based on the call outcome (see Dispositions below).

</Step>

<Step title="Add notes" icon="file-text">

- Add brief call notes (intent, objections, timing, special requests).
- Use clear notes to make future follow-ups faster and more effective.

</Step>

<Step title="Convert to order (if qualified)" icon="check-circle">

- If the customer wants to proceed, click **Create lead**.
- Enter pickup address, payment details, and appointment date and time.
- The lead moves to the **Orders** module as a confirmed order.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Dispositions

Use consistent dispositions so reporting and follow-ups stay accurate.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Connected dispositions" icon="check-circle">

When you reach the customer and speak to them, set **status = Connected** and choose one of these reasons:

- **Callback** – Customer asked to be called again later.  
- **Sold elsewhere** – Customer sold the device to another buyer.  
- **Order already placed** – Customer has already placed an order with Cashkr.  
- **Better price elsewhere** – Customer received a higher offer from another platform.  
- **Higher expectation** – Customer expects a higher price than the current quote.  
- **Was just checking** – Customer was only exploring and is not currently interested.  
- **Not selling anywhere** – Customer has decided not to sell the device.  
- **Out of town** – Customer is not currently available due to travel.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Not connected dispositions" icon="x-circle">

When you cannot connect to the customer, set **status = Not Connected** and choose one of these reasons:

- **Switched off** – Phone is switched off.  
- **Not reachable** – Network issue or number temporarily unreachable.  
- **Ringing** – Phone keeps ringing but no one answers.  
- **Wrong number** – Number is invalid or does not belong to the customer.

</Tab>

</Tabs>
