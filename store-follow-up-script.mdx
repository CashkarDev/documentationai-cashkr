## Overview

- **Team:** Store Onboarding / Channel Operations (B2CH)
- **Purpose:** Follow up with onboarded store partners who have not started creating orders or have been inactive for a few days.
- **When to use:** Call stores every 3–5 days if they are inactive or have not created any orders after onboarding.
- **Tone:** Friendly, encouraging, and supportive — focus on helping them start, not pressuring.

<Callout kind="info">

Keep the conversation supportive and focused on helping the store succeed, not on pressuring them. After every call, resend the training video and login link so they can easily start creating orders.

</Callout>

## Call scripts

<Tabs>

<Tab title="English (Training)" icon="book-open">

<Steps>

<Step title="Opening" icon="play-circle">

> "Hi \[Store Owner Name], this is \[Your Name] from Cashkr. I just wanted to check how your experience has been with the Cashkr Store App so far."

<Step title="If store hasn’t used the app yet" icon="help-circle">

> "No worries at all. Just wanted to remind you — you can start creating orders anytime when customers come to exchange their old phones. It is a quick process, and our associate at your store will help with inspection and valuation."

</Step>

<Step title="Encourage action" icon="zap">

> "The more customers you register, the higher your daily buyback volume — and it also helps you close more new phone sales."

</Step>

<Step title="If store says they forgot or were busy" icon="info">

> "That is totally fine, sir. I can schedule a short 5-minute refresher call or have our associate drop by again to help your staff with a live demo."

</Step>

<Step title="If store is unsure about the process" icon="info">

> "No problem — we will resend you the short training video and guide. Once you create your first order, the rest becomes easy."

</Step>

<Step title="Closing" icon="check-circle">

> "Thank you for your time. I will stay in touch — let us try creating your first order this week itself. It really helps boost store conversions."

</Step>

## Usage notes

- Call inactive stores every 3–5 days.
- Tag each store’s status after the call as one of: *Active*, *Pending*, *Need Demo*, *Not Responding*.
- Always resend the training video and login link after each call.
- Keep your tone encouraging — the goal is to reactivate and support the store, not to create pressure.

## After-call checklist

- Update the store’s status tag to one of: *Active*, *Pending*, *Need Demo*, *Not Responding*.
- Resend the training video and login link to the store.
- If you promised a follow-up (for example, reminder call or demo), schedule the next call in 3–5 days.
