## Overview

Open **B2C Sales → To Work → My Leads**. This is the logged-in sales executive's personal queue: leads arrive here through **auto-assignment**, shown as cards in the order the system recommends working them.

## What the screen shows

### Lead cards

- One card per lead assigned to you. **Open any card to log the call.**
- The order is a recommendation, not a lock — you can work leads out of order.
- When nothing is assigned the page shows *Nothing assigned to you.*

<Callout kind="alert">
  If My Leads stays empty while new leads are coming in, auto-assignment is not running. Report it to the tech team.
</Callout>

### My numbers today

A live rail that refreshes as the day runs:

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Calls made** | Calls you have logged today |
| **Orders placed** | Leads you converted to orders today |
| **Untouched today** | Assigned leads you have not called yet today |
| **Oldest untouched** | Age of the oldest lead you have not touched |

<Callout kind="tip">
  The rail is self-supervision: if you see untouched leads late in the day, clear them before picking up new work.
</Callout>

## Actions

Opening a card opens the lead drawer, where you can call the customer (**Click to call**), set the call outcome under **Update Disposition**, add remarks, and convert the lead with **Create Lead**. See [New Leads](/new-leads) for the drawer and dispositions.
