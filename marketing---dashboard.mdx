## Overview

**Sidebar path:** Dashboards → LEADERSHIP → **Marketing Report** (`/dashboard/marketing-report`)

The Marketing Report is the weekly marketing health report. It compares the selected period with the previous period of the same length. A similar report also appears as the **CMO** tab of the Executive dashboard.

## Filters

| Control | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Period** | Date range picker (defaults to the last 7 days). The header shows the comparison, for example *vs previous 6 days*, and the last updated time. |
| **Refresh** | Reloads the report. |

## What the screen shows

### 1. Marketing overview

Headline KPI tiles with % change vs the previous period:

| Tile | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Spend** | All marketing spend, online and offline. |
| **Revenue** | Revenue from marketing-attributed orders. |
| **ROAS** | Revenue ÷ spend. |
| **Sessions** | GA4 sessions. |
| **Leads** / **Orders** | Volume in the period. |
| **CPL (Paid)** | Cost per paid lead. |
| **CPA (Google/Meta)** | Platform-reported CPA (shows *awaiting sync* until ad data arrives). |
| **CPA (Our tracking)** | CPA using Cashkr's own attribution. |
| **CPA (Blended)** | Total spend ÷ all orders. |
| **Lead → Order CR** | Lead-to-order conversion. |

<Callout kind="info">
Cost KPIs use red for growth and green for shrinkage; revenue KPIs use the opposite.
</Callout>

### 2. Channel performance

Table of channels (SEO, Google Ads, Direct, Cross-network, Unassigned, Organic Social, Meta Ads, Email, AI Assistant, Referral and others) with Spend, Sessions, Leads, Orders, Conv (Google/Meta), CPA (Google/Meta), CPA (our tracking) and Lead → Order. A note shows what % of leads carry a channel tag.

### 3. Paid campaign performance

Callouts for **Top performer**, **Biggest waste** and **Needs attention**, then a campaign table. Rows above the target CPA (₹60) or with spend but no orders are marked red. Only paid campaigns are listed.

### 4. Funnel performance

Stage-to-stage rates with drop-off counts: Sessions → Leads, Leads → Orders booked, Booked → Pickup completed, and Completed → Payout disbursed (marked *Assumed* — the vendor pays the customer at pickup).

### 5. Geo / city performance

City bars (completed orders) and a **By state** table: State, Leads, Orders, Lead → Order, Completion %, Revenue. State is derived from the lead's pincode; there is no spend by state.

### 6. Device & platform

Three splits: **Web vs App** (with conversion), **Android vs iOS** (and other OS), and **Device type** (Mobile, Desktop, Tablet).

Each section ends with a short **Why this format** note explaining how to read it.

## Actions

The report is read-only.

<Callout kind="tip">
If the paid campaign table is empty while ads are running, the ads-sync integration may not be firing. Ask the tech team to check the ad-data sync before reading CPA figures.
</Callout>
