## Overview

Open **B2C Sales → To Work → Callbacks** (breadcrumb *Dashboard / B2C Sales / Callbacks*). The page lists leads that need another call: customers who asked to be called back (**Connected - Callback**) and leads whose last call did not connect (**Not Connected** — Ringing, Not Reachable, Switched Off).

<Callout kind="info">
  Use the status chips to switch between customer-requested callbacks and not-connected retries. Leads marked **Not Connected → Ringing** stay here and are not counted as lost in **Lost Leads**.
</Callout>

## Why calls are not connecting

A summary panel at the top shows how many calls did not connect in the selected period (for example *0 of 0 calls did not connect*). Switch the period with **Today**, **7 days** or **30 days**.

## Status chips and scope

| Control | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Status chips | **All**, **Connected - Callback**, **All Not Connected**, **Ringing**, **Not Reachable**, **Switched Off** |
| Scope toggle | **Mine**, **All**, **Unassigned** |

## What the table shows

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Order Date** | When the lead was created |
| **Call Back On** | Scheduled callback date and time. Past callbacks show an **Overdue** label with how long ago it was due |
| **Model** | Device model |
| **Assigned** | Sales executive the lead is assigned to, or *Unassigned* |
| **Type** | Device category badge |
| **Status** | Disposition, for example *Connected - Callback* or *Not Connected - Ringing* |
| **Notes** | Notes from the last call, or *No Notes* |

Click a row to open the lead drawer with device specifications, **Update Disposition** (Select Status, reason, remarks), **Create Lead**, customer information with **Click to call**, and activity logs (see [New Leads](/new-leads)).

## Filters and search

- **Search leads...** box in the header.
- Click **Filters** to open: **Type** (device type), **All Tiers** (High End, Mid End, Low End), **City**, and a from/to date range.
- Click **Apply Filters** to apply or **Reset** to clear.

## Callback workflow

<Steps>

<Step title="Open a callback lead" icon="list" title-type="p">

- Go to **B2C Sales → Callbacks**.
- Start with overdue callbacks, then pick a lead to open its drawer.

</Step>

<Step title="Call the customer" icon="phone" title-type="p">

- Use **Click to call** in the drawer.
- Confirm interest, timing, and any questions about the offer.

</Step>

<Step title="Update status and disposition" icon="edit" title-type="p">

- In **Update Disposition**, set **Select Status** to **Connected** or **Not Connected**.
- Select the appropriate reason based on the call outcome (see Dispositions below), add remarks and click **Update Disposition**.

</Step>

<Step title="Convert to order (if qualified)" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">

- If the customer wants to proceed, click **Create Lead**.
- Enter pickup address, payment details, and appointment date and time.
- The lead moves to **Orders** as a confirmed order and appears in **Converted Orders**.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Dispositions

Use consistent dispositions so reporting and follow-ups stay accurate.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Connected dispositions" icon="check-circle">

When you reach the customer and speak to them, set **status = Connected** and choose one of these reasons:

- **Callback** – Customer asked to be called again later.
- **Sold elsewhere** – Customer sold the device to another buyer.
- **Order already placed** – Customer has already placed an order with Cashkr.
- **Better price elsewhere** – Customer received a higher offer from another platform.
- **Higher expectation** – Customer expects a higher price than the current quote.
- **Was just checking** – Customer was only exploring and is not currently interested.
- **Not selling anywhere** – Customer has decided not to sell the device.
- **Out of town** – Customer is not currently available due to travel.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Not connected dispositions" icon="x-circle">

When you cannot connect to the customer, set **status = Not Connected** and choose one of these reasons:

- **Switched off** – Phone is switched off.
- **Not reachable** – Network issue or number temporarily unreachable.
- **Ringing** – Phone keeps ringing but no one answers.
- **Wrong number** – Number is invalid or does not belong to the customer.

</Tab>

</Tabs>
