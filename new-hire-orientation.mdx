Welcome to **Cashkr**. This orientation program helps every new employee feel confident, informed, and ready to contribute from day one.

<Callout kind="info">

Use this page to guide a new hire through their first two weeks: objectives, timeline, detailed flow, tools and access, HR checklist, and feedback.

</Callout>

## Objectives

The orientation program focuses on four main outcomes:

- Familiarize new hires with Cashkr vision, values, and operations.
- Ensure they understand their KRA, tools, and daily workflows.
- Introduce team members and company communication norms.
- Provide access to all platforms, trackers, and training materials.

## Orientation timeline

Use the tabs to see what happens at each stage of the first two weeks, who owns it, and what to cover.

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Day 1" icon="calendar">

**Timeframe:** First day  
**Focus:** Company introduction, system setup, HR policies overview  
**Owner:** HR

- Welcome the new hire and introduce Cashkr mission, vision, and values.
- Walk through company structure and key leadership roles.
- Set up company email, HRMS (Petpooja), Slack, and other base systems.
- Explain high-level HR policies and confirm documents to be completed.

</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 2" icon="calendar">

**Timeframe:** Next working day  
**Focus:** Department goals, tools, and daily tasks  
**Owner:** Department Head

- Introduce department structure, team members, and primary goals.
- Explain KRA and KPI expectations for the role.
- Walk through daily workflows, reporting format, and handoffs.
- Highlight key department-specific tools used regularly.

</Tab>
<Tab title="Week 1 (Days 3–5)" icon="calendar">

**Timeframe:** Week 1 (days 3–5)  
**Focus:** SOPs, KPIs, and internal workflows  
**Owner:** Manager

- Provide detailed SOPs for core processes the new hire will own.
- Deep dive into KPIs and how performance is measured.
- Demonstrate internal workflows, trackers, and reporting cadence.
- Assign simple, guided tasks to practice workflows.

</Tab>
<Tab title="Week 2" icon="calendar">

**Timeframe:** Week 2  
**Focus:** Shadowing and hands-on training  
**Owner:** Team Lead

- Schedule 1–2 days of shadowing with experienced team members.
- Assign training modules (videos, SOPs, scripts) to complete.
- Review sample tasks performed by the new hire and give feedback.
- Gradually move from observation to partial ownership of tasks.

</Tab>
<Tab title="End of Week 2" icon="check-circle">

**Timeframe:** End of onboarding period  
**Focus:** Review, clarification, and readiness check  
**Owner:** HR and Manager

- Confirm that all tools and logins are active and policies are acknowledged.
- Assess the new hire’s understanding of KRA, KPIs, and workflows.
- Address open questions or blockers before full ownership of tasks.
- Collect onboarding feedback and agree on next steps.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Orientation flow (detailed steps)

Use this flow to run the actual orientation sequence with each new hire.

<Steps>
<Step title="Welcome and introduction (HR)" icon="users">

- Share Cashkr mission, vision, and values.
- Give a brief overview of company structure and leadership.
- Provide company email, Slack access, and Petpooja HRMS login.
- Set expectations for the two-week orientation timeline.

</Step>

<Step title="Policy and compliance (HR)" icon="shield">

- Walk through core HR policies: leave, payroll, grievance, code of conduct.
- Explain working hours: **10:00 AM – 6:30 PM**, including break expectations.
- Clarify attendance process and reporting norms (who to report to and how).
- Confirm the new hire understands where to find policy documents.

</Step>

<Step title="Tools and access setup (HR + IT)" icon="settings">

- Share logins and ensure access to:
  - Petpooja HRMS
  - Slack
  - Eniston or Centerspace (knowledge base)
  - Zoho Sheets for reports and trackers
  - Department-specific tools as needed
- Verify the new hire can log in to each tool without issues.
- Note any pending accesses and track them to closure.

</Step>

<Step title="Department induction (Department Head)" icon="folder">

- Introduce the team, roles, and how the department supports Cashkr goals.
- Explain the new hire’s KRA and KPI in clear, concrete terms.
- Walk through daily workflows, reporting templates, and routines.
- Align on communication norms specific to the department (channels, meeting rhythm).

</Step>

<Step title="Training and shadowing (Team Lead)" icon="book-open">

- Assign relevant training modules: videos, SOPs, scripts, and reference docs.
- Schedule 1–2 days of shadowing with senior team members.
- Ask the new hire to perform sample tasks under supervision.
- Review outputs and give targeted feedback before increasing responsibility.

</Step>

<Step title="Onboarding review and confirmation (HR + Manager)" icon="check-circle">

- HR verifies all logins are active and mandatory documents are acknowledged.
- Manager confirms the new hire is ready to begin independent tasks.
- Capture any remaining questions, concerns, or support needed.
- Share the link to the New Employee Feedback Form and explain when to submit it.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Tools and platforms

Make sure every new hire has access to these core systems by the end of day one:

- **Petpooja HRMS** for HR records, attendance, and payroll.
- **Slack** for day-to-day communication and team channels.
- **Eniston or Centerspace** for documentation and knowledge sharing.
- **Zoho Sheets** for reports, trackers, and performance logs.
- **Department-specific tools** such as outreach, CX, or analytics dashboards, based on role.

## HR checklist

Use these grouped checklists to track completion of onboarding tasks. Keep owners consistent across hires.

### Before day 1

- [ ] Welcome email sent (**Owner:** HR)
- [ ] Laptop and basic access setup completed (**Owner:** HR)

### Day 1

- [ ] New hire introduced on Slack (**Owner:** HR)
- [ ] HR policies and compliance explained (**Owner:** HR)

### Week 1–2

- [ ] Department induction completed (**Owner:** Manager)
- [ ] Tools access verified across all required platforms (**Owner:** IT)
- [ ] Role-specific training completed (**Owner:** Manager)

### Wrap-up (end of week 2)

- [ ] New employee feedback collected via New Employee Feedback Form (**Owner:** HR)

## Feedback and support

After two weeks, ask the new hire to submit the **New Employee Feedback Form** so HR and the manager can improve the onboarding process.

Encourage them to share:

- How their overall onboarding experience has been.
- Whether their KRA and KPI are clear and actionable.
- Any suggestions to improve orientation, training content, or timing.
